机器人学习 · 模仿学习 · 强化学习Robot learning · Imitation learning · Reinforcement learning

从示范到试错From Demonstration to Trial and Error

模仿学习、强化学习、逆强化学习、离线强化学习、世界模型、VLA……机器人学习的名词很多，但它们都在解决同一个问题：给机器人找一个策略，也就是"看到什么，就做什么"。真正的区别只有一个：学习信号从哪里来。这一篇沿着一条光谱讲：最左边是有人手把手示范，中间是只告诉你做得好不好，最右边是谁也不告诉你，机器人自己摸索世界怎么运转。每一站都讲清楚它解决什么问题、核心概念是什么、好在哪、坑在哪。最重要的两站，模仿学习和强化学习，配了好几个交互，拖一拖就能看到问题出在哪。Imitation learning, reinforcement learning, inverse RL, offline RL, world models, VLAs… robot learning has a lot of names, but they all tackle the same problem: find the robot a policy, a rule for “given what I see, do this”. The one real difference between them is where the learning signal comes from. This page walks along a single spectrum: on the far left someone shows the robot exactly what to do; in the middle someone only says how well it did; on the far right nobody says anything and the robot works out on its own how the world behaves. For each stop we cover what problem it solves, its key ideas, what it is good at and where it breaks. The two most important stops, imitation learning and reinforcement learning, come with several interactives, so you can drag things around and see exactly where the trouble starts.

第 0 步 · 总览Step 0 · Overview

一条光谱：学习信号从哪里来One spectrum: where the learning signal comes from

不管叫什么名字，这些方法最后要学的都是同一个东西：策略。策略就是一个函数，输入是机器人此刻感知到的（相机画面、关节角度、力传感器读数），输出是它下一步该做的（每个电机转多少、夹爪开还是合）。区别只在于"老师"给了多少信息：Whatever they are called, these methods all end up learning the same thing: a policy. A policy is a function whose input is what the robot senses right now (camera images, joint angles, force readings) and whose output is what it should do next (how far to turn each motor, whether to open or close the gripper). The only difference is how much the “teacher” tells it:

  • 最左边，老师手把手示范，每一步都给出标准答案。这是模仿学习。On the far left, a teacher demonstrates by hand and gives the correct answer at every step. This is imitation learning.
  • 往右走，老师给的越来越少：只有视频没有动作，只说两段里哪段更好，或者只在最后打一个分。打分这一站就是强化学习。Moving right, the teacher says less and less: video without actions, only which of two attempts was better, or just one score at the end. The scoring stop is reinforcement learning.
  • 最右边，没有老师，也没有人给的任何标注，只有数据本身：机器人自己动来动去的经历，或者网上海量的视频和图文。可以让数据自己给自己出题，比如用这一帧去预测下一帧，答案就在数据里，这叫自监督学习；学到的"我这么做，世界会怎么变"，叫世界模型。At the far right there is no teacher and no human labelling at all, only the data itself: the robot’s own experience of moving around, or the huge amount of video, images and text online. The data can set its own exercises, such as predicting the next frame from this one, with the answer already in the data; this is called self-supervised learning. What it learns about “if I do this, how will the world change” is called a world model.

越往左，每条数据带的信息越多，但要人花时间；越往右，数据越便宜，机器人自己就能大量产生，但每条数据能教的东西越少，学起来越难。实际好用的系统，几乎都是把几站组合起来用。The further left, the more each piece of data tells you, but the more human time it costs; the further right, the cheaper the data, which the robot can produce in bulk by itself, but the less each piece teaches and the harder learning becomes. Systems that work well in practice almost always combine several stops.

第 1 步 · 基本概念Step 1 · Basics

所有方法共用的几个词A few words every method uses

把机器人和环境的互动切成一步一步。每一步：机器人看到状态 s，按策略 π 选一个动作 a；世界按自己的规律变到新的状态 s′（读作"s 撇"，表示"下一个状态"）；如果有人定义了什么叫好，还会给一个奖励 r。然后重复。这个循环有个正式的名字，叫马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）。"马尔可夫"的意思是：下一步会怎样，只取决于现在的状态和动作，和更早的历史无关。Cut the interaction between a robot and its environment into steps. At each step the robot sees a state s and, following its policy π, picks an action a; the world, by its own rules, moves to a new state s′ (read “s prime”, meaning “the next state”); and if someone has defined what counts as good, there is also a reward r. Then it repeats. This loop has a formal name, the Markov decision process (MDP). “Markov” means that what happens next depends only on the current state and action, not on any earlier history.

先用一个格子世界把这些词变具体：你来当策略。A grid world makes these words concrete. You get to be the policy.

  • 点老鼠旁边的格子，或者按方向键，让老鼠往上、下、左、右走一格。撞到墙（灰色格子）或边界就原地不动。Tap a cell next to the mouse, or use the arrow keys, to move the mouse one cell up, down, left or right. Bumping into a wall (grey cell) or the edge leaves it where it is.

  • 折扣 γ（滑块）：越晚拿到的奖励越不值钱。从第 0 步数起，第 k 步的奖励要先乘 k 次 γ，再加进这一局的总分；这个总分叫回报 G，下面会实时算出来。γ = 0.9 时，第 10 步（k = 9）才拿到的 +10 只算 10 × 0.99 ≈ 3.9。Discount γ (slider): the later a reward arrives, the less it is worth. Counting from step 0, the reward at step k is multiplied by γ k times before it is added to the episode’s total; that total is called the return G and is computed live below. With γ = 0.9, a +10 that only arrives at step 10 (k = 9) counts as just 10 × 0.99 ≈ 3.9.
  • 显示最优策略（按钮）：每个格子画出最好的走法（箭头），格子里的数是从这一格出发、按最好的走法走下去平均能拿到的总分。Show optimal policy (button): every cell shows the best move (an arrow), and the number in the cell is the average total you get from that cell if you keep making the best moves.

为什么会越开越偏：分布偏移Why it drifts further and further: distribution shift

训练时，策略只见过专家走过的那些状态，而专家总是开在赛道中间。一个策略会经过哪些状态、各经过多少次，叫它的状态分布。学生一旦犯了一个小错，就到了一个专家从来没去过的地方：那里没有训练数据，策略只能瞎猜；猜错了又走得更偏，误差像滚雪球一样越滚越大。Ross 和 Bagnell（2010）证明：如果克隆出来的策略在专家会去的那些状态上，每一步犯错的概率是 ε（这正是监督学习在压低的那个误差），那么走 T 步，最坏情况下它一局犯的错（总代价）会比专家多出 T²ε 量级；如果训练和使用时的状态分布一样，像普通的监督学习那样，只会多出 Tε 量级。直觉是：第 t 步一旦犯错走偏，后面剩下的 T − t 步可能全在陌生的地方瞎猜，错误一路被拖下去；每一步都有 ε 的可能开这个头，加起来大约是 ε × (T + (T − 1) + … + 1) ≈ εT² ∕ 2。比如 T = 100、ε = 1%：普通监督学习预计多错 1 次左右，最坏情况下却可能多错 50 次左右。During training the policy only sees the states the expert visited, and the expert always drives down the middle. Which states a policy visits, and how often, is called its state distribution. Once the student makes a small mistake, it is somewhere the expert has never been: there is no training data there, so the policy guesses; a wrong guess takes it even further off, and the error snowballs. Ross and Bagnell (2010) showed that if the cloned policy errs with probability ε per step on the states the expert visits (exactly the error supervised learning drives down), then over T steps its mistakes in an episode (its total cost) can, in the worst case, exceed the expert’s by an amount on the order of T²ε; if training and use saw the same state distribution, as in ordinary supervised learning, the excess would only be on the order of Tε. The intuition: once a mistake at step t throws the car off, the remaining T − t steps may all be guesses in unfamiliar places, so the error is dragged along; every step has a chance ε of starting this, which adds up to about ε × (T + (T − 1) + … + 1) ≈ εT² ∕ 2. With T = 100 and ε = 1%, for example, ordinary supervised learning predicts about 1 extra mistake, but the worst case is about 50.

训练误差小，不代表跑得好。训练数据来自专家的状态分布，执行时的状态来自学生自己的分布，两者不一样。这叫分布偏移（distribution shift），它造成的"越错越多"叫误差累积（compounding error），是模仿学习最核心的坑。A small training error does not mean the policy drives well. The training data come from the expert’s state distribution, while the states at run time come from the student’s own distribution, and the two differ. This is called distribution shift, and the “more and more mistakes” it causes is called compounding error; it is the central pitfall of imitation learning.

两条路都对，取平均就撞树：多模态Both ways are right, the average hits the tree: multimodality

第二个坑：同一个状态下，专家可能有好几种都对的做法。绕过一棵树，从这边绕、从那边绕都行；拿杯子，可以抓杯身，也可以抓把手。这种"一个输入对应好几个正确输出"的情况叫多模态：把示范动作画成分布，会有好几个峰，所以也叫多峰。（这里的"模态"指分布的峰，和"能看图又能读文字"的多模态大模型不是一回事。）如果用均方误差（平方误差的平均）训练，策略学到的是所有示范动作的平均：比如一半示范往左偏 30°、一半往右偏 30°，输出 c 的总误差是 (c − 30)² + (c + 30)²，c = 0 时最小，也就是直直地朝树飞过去。The second pitfall: in the same state, an expert may have several equally good ways to act. To get past a tree you can go round either side; to pick up a mug you can grab the body or the handle. This “one input, several correct outputs” situation is called multimodality: plot the demonstrated actions as a distribution and it has several peaks (modes), so it is also called multi-peaked. (Here a “mode” means a peak of the distribution; this has nothing to do with “multimodal” models that handle both images and text.) Train with the mean squared error (the average of squared errors) and the policy learns the average of all demonstrated actions: if half the demonstrations veer 30° left and half 30° right, an output c has total error (c − 30)² + (c + 30)², which is smallest at c = 0, flying straight at the tree.

解决办法是让策略输出一个分布（每个动作有多大可能），而不是一个平均数，见下图。常见的有三种：混合高斯，把几个钟形的分布叠在一起，就能有好几个峰；把动作切成一个个档位，每个档位当成一个 token（一个"词"），像做选择题一样给每个档位一个概率，RT-2 就是这么做的；或者用生成模型直接"生成"动作，比如 Diffusion Policy（Chi 等 2023）用扩散模型，π0（Black 等 2024，这是一个模型的名字，读作 pi-zero）用流匹配。这两种模型都是从一团随机数出发，一步步把它修整成一个像示范的动作；从不同的随机数出发，就会得到不同、但都像样的动作，自然就能从这边绕或者从那边绕，而不会取平均。The fix is to have the policy output a distribution (how likely each action is) rather than a single average; see the figure below. There are three common ways. A Gaussian mixture stacks several bell-shaped distributions, so it can have several peaks. Or cut the actions into bins and treat each bin as a token (a “word”), giving each bin a probability as in a multiple-choice question; RT-2 does this. Or “generate” actions with a generative model, such as the diffusion model in Diffusion Policy (Chi et al. 2023) or the flow matching in π0 (Black et al. 2024; this is the name of a model, read “pi-zero”). Both start from a handful of random numbers and refine them step by step into an action that looks like a demonstration; start from different random numbers and you get different but sensible actions, so the policy naturally goes round one side or the other instead of averaging.

示范不够理想的时候When the demonstrations are not ideal

行为克隆要求每一步都有"状态加上专家动作"。往光谱右边挪一点，信号变弱了，但更容易拿到。下面三种都还是"从人身上学"，区别在于人给的是什么。Behaviour cloning needs “a state plus the expert’s action” at every step. 只有视频，没有动作：从观察中学习Video without actions: learning from observation

网上有海量人做事的视频，但视频里没有动作标签：看不出每一帧手上用了多大力，更不知道机器人的电机该转多少。常用的思路分两步。先让机器人自己随便动一动，收集一批"这一帧、做的动作、下一帧"的三元组 (s, a, s′)，训练一个逆动力学模型：看前后两帧，猜中间做了什么动作。再用它给视频补上动作标签，然后照常做行为克隆。BCO（Torabi 等 2018）就是这个思路。OpenAI 的 VPT（Baker 等 2022）把它做到了很大规模：用约 2000 小时带键盘鼠标记录的 Minecraft 录像训练逆动力学模型，给约 7 万小时网络视频补上标签，再做行为克隆；再用强化学习微调（在已经学好的模型上接着训练一会儿）以后，成了第一个能在游戏里做出钻石工具的 AI。The internet has endless videos of people doing things, but no action labels: you cannot tell how much force the hand used in each frame, let alone how far a robot’s motors should turn. A common recipe has two steps. First let the robot move around at random and collect triples of “this frame, the action taken, the next frame”, (s, a, s′), to train an inverse dynamics model: look at two consecutive frames and guess the action in between. Then use it to fill in action labels for the videos, and do behaviour cloning as usual. BCO (Torabi et al. 2018) follows this recipe. OpenAI’s VPT (Baker et al. 2022) scaled it up: about 2,000 hours of Minecraft recordings with logged keyboard and mouse actions trained the inverse dynamics model, which labelled about 70,000 hours of online video for behaviour cloning; after fine-tuning with reinforcement learning (training an already trained model a little further), it became the first AI able to craft diamond tools in the game.

机器人还多一道坎：视频里是人手，机器人是夹爪，身体结构不一样（embodiment gap）。人手的动作不能直接照搬，要先换算成机器人能做的动作。Robots face one more hurdle: the videos show human hands, while the robot has a gripper, so the bodies differ (the embodiment gap). Human motions cannot be copied directly; they first have to be translated into motions the robot can make.

推断意图：逆强化学习Inferring intent: inverse reinforcement learning

换个思路：不学"他怎么做"，而学"他想要什么"。逆强化学习（IRL，Ng 和 Russell 2000）从示范里反推出一个奖励函数（给每一步打分的规则），使得专家的行为在这个奖励下是最优的；再用强化学习去找让这个奖励最大的策略。普通的强化学习是"给定奖励，求行为"，这里反过来，"给定行为，求奖励"，所以叫"逆"。Another angle: instead of learning “how he does it”, learn “what he wants”. Inverse reinforcement learning (IRL, Ng and Russell 2000) works backwards from demonstrations to a reward function (a rule that scores every step) under which the expert’s behaviour is optimal, then uses reinforcement learning to find the policy that maximizes that reward. Ordinary reinforcement learning goes “given a reward, find the behaviour”; this goes the other way, “given the behaviour, find the reward”, hence “inverse”.

这样做有两个好处。第一，意图比动作更容易迁移：环境变了、身体变了，"把盘子放进碗架"这个目标不变。第二，优化奖励有可能做得比示范者更好：斯坦福的自主直升机从专家飞行员的多次示范里学出目标轨迹和奖励，完成了一系列高难度特技，论文报告它"和专家飞得一样好，而且常常更好"（Abbeel、Coates、Ng 2010）。This has two advantages. First, intent transfers better than actions: if the environment or the body changes, the goal “put the plate in the dish rack” stays the same. Second, optimizing a reward can beat the demonstrator: Stanford’s autonomous helicopter learned a target trajectory and a reward from several demonstrations by an expert pilot and flew a series of difficult aerobatic manoeuvres, and the paper reports performance “as well as, and often even better than” the expert pilot (Abbeel, Coates and Ng 2010).

难点是歧义：同一段示范可以被无数个奖励解释，最极端的是"奖励恒为 0"，在它下面任何行为都是最优的。所以需要额外的假设，比如最大熵 IRL：假设专家不是每次都选最好的那条轨迹，而是"奖励越高的轨迹越可能被选中"，被选中的概率正比于 e奖励：总奖励每高 1 分，被选中的可能就大 e ≈ 2.7 倍。除此之外不再多做假设，挑最"不偏不倚"的那种解释，"最大熵"指的就是这个。然后调整奖励，让专家实际做出的那些示范在这个模型下出现的概率尽量大，这叫最大似然。在这个假设下，"奖励恒为 0"就不行了：所有轨迹一样可能，专家偏偏总选那几条就显得太巧，示范出现的概率很低。代价是计算很贵：外层每改一次奖励，内层都要先跑一遍强化学习，求出这个奖励下的最优策略，看它和专家像不像，再回到外层接着改。GAIL（Ho 和 Ermon 2016）换了个更直接的做法：训练一个判别器（一个二分类器，就像认猫，只是这里分的是"专家"还是"学生"），分辨每一步的状态和动作是来自专家还是学生；学生用强化学习去骗过判别器，判别器越认为它"像专家"，学生拿到的奖励越高。两边你追我赶，这叫对抗训练，训练过程常常不稳定。GAIL 最后得到的是策略本身，不会留下一个能单独拿去复用的奖励函数。The difficulty is ambiguity: the same demonstrations can be explained by countless rewards, the extreme case being “the reward is always 0”, under which every behaviour is optimal. So extra assumptions are needed, such as maximum-entropy IRL: assume the expert does not always pick the very best trajectory, but that “higher-reward trajectories are more likely to be chosen”, with probability proportional to ereward: each extra point of total reward makes a trajectory e ≈ 2.7 times more likely. Beyond that it assumes nothing more, choosing the most “even-handed” explanation, which is what “maximum entropy” refers to. Then the reward is adjusted so that the demonstrations the expert actually produced are as probable as possible under this model; this is called maximum likelihood. Under this assumption “the reward is always 0” no longer works: every trajectory would be equally likely, so the expert always picking those few would be too much of a coincidence, and the demonstrations would get a low probability. The price is computation: every time the outer loop changes the reward, an inner loop has to run reinforcement learning to find the optimal policy for that reward and see how much it looks like the expert before the outer loop changes the reward again. GAIL (Ho and Ermon 2016) takes a more direct route: train a discriminator (a two-way classifier, like the cat recognizer but sorting “expert” from “student”) to tell whether each state and action came from the expert or the student, and train the student with reinforcement learning to fool it; the more the discriminator thinks a step looks like the expert’s, the higher the student’s reward. The two sides chase each other, which is called adversarial training, and training is often unstable. What GAIL produces is the policy itself; it does not leave behind a reward function you can reuse on its own.

比较好坏：偏好学习Comparing attempts: preference learning

有些行为人既做不出来，也打不出准确的分，但把两段放在一起，能看出哪段更好。偏好学习让人反复比较两段录像，用这些比较训练一个奖励模型，再用强化学习去优化它。For some behaviours a person can neither perform them nor give an accurate score, yet can tell which of two attempts is better when they are side by side. Preference learning has people compare pairs of clips over and over, trains a reward model on those comparisons, and then optimizes it with reinforcement learning.

它解决的问题：示范拿不到（没人能遥操作四条腿在碎石上跑），或者想做得比示范者更好时，就只剩下奖励这条路。强化学习（RL）让机器人反复尝试：带来高回报的做法多做，带来低回报的少做。好处是不需要老师；难处在于奖励往往要很多步以后才出现，必须弄清楚"这个好结果要归功于之前的哪几步"，这叫信用分配；还得自己决定去哪里尝试，这叫探索。The problem it solves: when demonstrations are unavailable (nobody can teleoperate four legs running over rubble), or you want to do better than the demonstrator, a reward is all that is left. Reinforcement learning (RL) has the robot try again and again: do more of what brings a high return and less of what brings a low one. The upside is that no teacher is needed. The hard part is that rewards often arrive many steps later, so you must work out which earlier steps deserve the credit for a good outcome, which is called credit assignment; and you must decide for yourself where to try things, which is called exploration.

强化学习有两条主线：一条先学"每个状态、每个动作有多好"（价值），再据此选动作；另一条直接调整策略本身（策略梯度）。今天的大多数算法是这两条线的结合。Reinforcement learning has two main threads: one first learns how good each state and action is (values) and then picks actions accordingly; the other adjusts the policy itself directly (policy gradients). Most algorithms today combine the two.

The simplest remedy is ε-greedy: with probability ε, take a random step. But here it barely helps: the big cheese is 10 steps away, and a random walk only gets there if it happens to get many steps right in a row; even with ε at 0.3, a thousand episodes hardly ever reach it. Optimistic initial values work much better: start every Q very high, so that every untried action looks tempting, and an action’s Q only comes down once it has been tried and found wanting. The mouse then tries the untried actions systematically and finds the big cheese within about ten episodes.

But it cuts the noise dramatically. Without it every score is positive, every dart pulls μ towards itself, and the direction can only be told from which darts pull harder. Using a learned value function V(s) as the baseline is called REINFORCE with a baseline; going one step further and replacing G − b with r + γV(s′) − V(s) (the TD error), which can be computed after a single step without waiting for the episode to end (this is bootstrapping: updating the estimate for this step using your own estimate for the next state), gives actor-critic methods: the actor is the policy and the critic is V.

PPO (Schulman et al. 2017) keeps each update from changing the policy too much; it is stable and easy to tune, and it is the default for training quadrupeds and humanoids at scale in simulation. SAC (Haarnoja et al. 2018) can reuse old data again and again and adds an entropy bonus to encourage exploration (extra points the more “undecided” the policy is, that is, the more willing to try different actions); it is sample-efficient and better suited to real robots. For a systematic introduction, the textbook by Sutton and Barto is free online from the authors.

你奖励什么，就得到什么You get what you reward

强化学习只认奖励。奖励写得不对，策略会精确地去优化那个错误的目标，这叫奖励钻空子（reward hacking），也叫规格博弈（specification gaming）。两个经典例子：一个学骑自行车的智能体（agent，强化学习里那个做决定、去试错的程序），奖励是"朝终点前进"，结果它学会了在起点附近绕半径 20 到 50 米的圈，因为每一圈里总有一段是朝着终点的，背离终点却不扣分（Randløv 和 Alstrøm 1998）；OpenAI 的赛艇游戏智能体发现，在一小片水域里兜圈子、反复撞几个会重新出现的加分道具，比跑完全程得分更高（Faulty reward functions）。Reinforcement learning cares only about the reward. Write the reward wrong and the policy will optimize that wrong objective with great precision; this is called reward hacking, or specification gaming. Two classic examples: an agent (the program that makes decisions and learns by trial and error in reinforcement learning) learning to ride a bicycle was rewarded for “progress towards the goal” and learned to ride in circles with a radius of 20 to 50 metres around the start, because every circle includes a stretch heading towards the goal while heading away was never penalized (Randløv and Alstrøm 1998); and OpenAI’s boat-racing agent found that circling in a small lagoon, repeatedly hitting a few bonus targets that kept reappearing, scored more than finishing the race (Faulty reward functions).

With the “+1 per step closer” reward, on the other hand, going straight in earns nine +1s plus the +10 at the door, 17.88 after discounting, while walking up to the door and pacing earns +1 every two steps, adding up to 53.63 over a lifetime, far more than going in. 出问题的不是智能体，而是奖励：它完美地优化了你写下的东西。训练越久、模型越强，漏洞越容易被找到；γ 越大，智能体越看重长远的总账，来回踱步这种"细水长流"的刷分法也就越划算。写奖励时，要设想"一个只想刷分的对手会怎么做"。Nothing is wrong with the agent; the reward is the problem: the agent optimized exactly what you wrote. The longer the training and the stronger the model, the more likely a loophole is found; and the larger γ, the more the agent cares about the long-run total, so slow-but-steady point farming such as pacing pays off more. When writing a reward, imagine what an opponent who only wants points would do.

奖励太稀疏：探索不到Rewards too sparse: nothing to explore towards

另一个极端：只在成功时给 1，其余全是 0。如果随机尝试几乎不可能碰巧成功（把钥匙插进锁孔、把一堆积木搭成塔），智能体就永远收不到信号，什么也学不会。常见的办法：The other extreme: give 1 only on success and 0 everywhere else. If random attempts almost never succeed by chance (putting a key into a lock, stacking a pile of blocks into a tower), the agent never receives a signal and learns nothing. Common remedies:

  • 塑形：就是上面讲的，记得写成势能的形式。Shaping: as above, remembering to write it in potential form.
  • 示范：用示范把策略带到成功附近，再开始试（第 8 步）。Demonstrations: use them to bring the policy near success before trial and error begins (step 8).
  • 事后重标记：策略的输入里除了状态，还有"这次的目标是什么"。没把物块推到目标 A，但推到了 B，那就假装目标本来就是 B。这次失败对"推到 B"来说就是一次成功的经验。这就是 HER（Hindsight Experience Replay，Andrychowicz 等 2017）。Hindsight relabelling: besides the state, the policy also takes “what the goal is this time” as input. You failed to push the block to goal A but got it to B? Pretend the goal was B all along. For “push it to B” this failure is a successful experience. This is HER (Hindsight Experience Replay, Andrychowicz et al. 2017).
  • 好奇心：机器人一边走一边预测"做了这个动作，下一刻会怎样"（这就是第 7 步的世界模型），把"预测得有多不准"当作额外奖励，鼓励去没见过的地方（Pathak 等 2017）。Curiosity: as it moves, the robot predicts “if I do this, what happens next” (this is the world model of step 7), and uses “how wrong the prediction was” as an extra reward, which encourages going to unfamiliar places (Pathak et al. 2017).
  • 课程：先练简单版本，再逐步加难。Curricula: practise an easy version first, then make it harder step by step.
  • 让大语言模型写奖励：Eureka（Ma 等 2023）让 GPT-4 写、改奖励函数的代码，在仿真里教会了五指灵巧手转笔。Have a large language model write the reward: Eureka (Ma et al. 2023) had GPT-4 write and revise reward-function code, and taught a five-fingered dexterous hand to spin a pen in simulation.

第 6 步 · 仿真到现实Step 6 · Sim to real

在仿真里试一亿次，再搬到真机上A hundred million tries in simulation, then onto the real robot

强化学习要海量的试错。真机器人一秒钟只能走那么几步，摔了会坏，还得有人在旁边扶；仿真器可以几千个机器人同时跑，还比真实时间快得多。Rudin 等（2021）在一块 GPU 上同时仿真几千只四足机器人，平地行走的策略不到 4 分钟就训练好了，崎岖地形也只要 20 分钟。Reinforcement learning needs enormous amounts of trial and error. A real robot can only take so many steps per second, breaks when it falls and needs someone standing by; a simulator can run thousands of robots at once, much faster than real time. Rudin et al. (2021) simulated thousands of quadrupeds in parallel on a single GPU: a policy for flat ground trained in under 4 minutes, and one for rough terrain in 20.

问题是仿真和现实总有差距（reality gap）：摩擦系数、质量、电机的延迟和力矩极限、相机的光照和噪声，都不可能和真实世界一模一样。在仿真里练到"完美"的策略，可能恰恰利用了仿真器某个不真实的地方，一到真机上就摔。The catch is that simulation and reality always differ (the reality gap): friction, masses, motor delays and torque limits, camera lighting and noise can never match the real world exactly. A policy that is “perfect” in simulation may be exploiting some unrealistic quirk of the simulator, and fall over the moment it runs on the real robot.

域随机化：让现实变成"又一个仿真"Domain randomization: make reality “just another simulation”

"域"（domain）指数据来自的环境，这里就是仿真器的各种设置。与其把仿真做得完全准确（做不到），不如每一局都随机改一改仿真的参数：摩擦、质量、延迟、颜色、光照……策略被迫学会在所有这些情况下都能用。只要真实世界落在随机的范围里，在策略眼里它就只是又一种情况。Tobin 等（2017）随机化渲染的颜色和纹理，只用仿真图片训练的物体检测器（在图里找出物体在哪儿的模型），在真实图像上的定位误差在 1.5 厘米以内；Peng 等（2018）随机化动力学参数，只在仿真里训练的推物体策略直接搬上了真机械臂；OpenAI（2019）用自动扩大范围的随机化（ADR）训练机械手单手还原魔方，拧哪一面由现成的求解器算好，学的是怎么用手把魔方转对。“Domain” means the environment the data come from, here the settings of the simulator. Rather than making the simulator perfectly accurate (impossible), randomize its parameters every episode: friction, mass, delay, colours, lighting… The policy is forced to work under all of them. As long as the real world falls within the randomized range, to the policy it is just one more case. Tobin et al. (2017) randomized rendered colours and textures, and an object detector (a model that finds where objects are in an image) trained only on simulated images located objects in real images to within 1.5 cm; Peng et al. (2018) randomized dynamics parameters, and a pushing policy trained only in simulation went straight onto a real robot arm; OpenAI (2019) used automatically widening randomization (ADR) to train a robot hand to solve a Rubik’s cube one-handed, with an off-the-shelf solver choosing which face to turn and the learned policy doing the turning.

随机化管用有两种方式。一种是鲁棒：学到的做法在整个随机范围里都过得去，Tobin 的检测器就是这样，它只看一张图，没法边做边调。另一种是适应：有记忆的策略能"边做边看"，从刚才的反应推断出眼下的条件，再做调整。冰壶例子里，看冰壶减速得快不快、再决定刷不刷冰，就是这一种；只有训练时见过各种各样的冰面，这种调整才派得上用场，策略也才学得会。真实的机器人策略常常把最近一段时间的观测和动作一起输入，从中隐式地推断摩擦、负载、地形这些看不见的参数。Randomization works in two ways. One is robustness: what the policy learns works acceptably across the whole randomized range. Tobin’s detector is like this; it looks at a single image and cannot adjust as it goes. The other is adaptation: a policy with memory can “watch as it goes”, infer the current conditions from how things just responded, and adjust. In the curling example, watching how quickly the stone slows and then deciding whether to sweep is this second kind; only if training has shown it all kinds of ice does such adjusting pay off, and only then can the policy learn it. Real robot policies often take the recent history of observations and actions as input and infer hidden parameters such as friction, payload and terrain from it implicitly.

老师-学生：先作弊，再模仿Teacher and student: cheat first, then imitate

仿真里什么都知道：地形的高度、摩擦系数、物体的精确位置。先用强化学习训练一个能看到这些特权信息的"老师"策略，信息全，所以好学；再让一个只用真机传感器的"学生"策略去模仿老师（行为克隆或 DAgger），学生必须从自己的传感器历史里推断出那些看不到的信息。Lee 等（2020）这样训练的 ANYmal 四足机器人，在泥地、雪地、碎石、密草和流水里都能走，这些情况训练时一样都没见过。这是光谱左边和中间的组合：老师靠试错学，学生靠示范学。自动驾驶里的 Learning by Cheating（Chen 等 2019）也是先让能看到特权信息的老师学会，再让只看相机的学生模仿它，只是那里的老师本身也是模仿人类驾驶学出来的。In simulation everything is known: the terrain height, the friction, the exact positions of objects. First use reinforcement learning to train a “teacher” policy that can see this privileged information; with the full picture it is easy to learn. Then train a “student” policy that only uses the real robot’s sensors to imitate the teacher (by behaviour cloning or DAgger); the student has to infer the hidden information from its own sensor history. The ANYmal quadruped of Lee et al. (2020), trained this way, walked through mud, snow, rubble, thick vegetation and running water, none of which it had seen in training. It combines the left end and the middle of the spectrum: the teacher learns by trial and error, the student learns from demonstrations. Learning by Cheating (Chen et al. 2019) in autonomous driving also first trains a teacher that sees privileged information and then has a camera-only student imitate it, except that there the teacher itself learned by imitating human driving.

好在哪What it is good at

  • 试错便宜、安全，可以大规模并行Trial and error is cheap and safe, and can run massively in parallel
  • 仿真里什么都测得到，可以用特权信息，可以随时重置Everything is measurable in simulation, so privileged information and instant resets are available
  • 腿足运动、灵巧操作这类很难示范的技能，主要靠它取得了进展Skills that are hard to demonstrate, such as legged locomotion and dexterous manipulation, have advanced mainly this way

坑在哪Where it breaks

  • 现实差距永远存在，策略可能钻仿真器的空子The reality gap never disappears, and the policy may exploit flaws in the simulator
  • 仿真器要人搭：接触、摩擦和软的东西（布、绳子、液体）都很难仿准Someone has to build the simulator: contact, friction and soft things (cloth, rope, liquids) are all hard to simulate accurately
  • 随机化的范围要调：太窄搬不过去，太宽学不会或者变得过于保守The randomization range needs tuning: too narrow and it does not transfer, too wide and it fails to learn or becomes overly cautious

第 7 步 · 自监督与世界模型Step 7 · Self-supervision and world models

没有老师：先学"世界怎么运转"No teacher: first learn how the world works

光谱最右边，既没有示范，也没有奖励，只有机器人自己动来动去收集的 (s, a, s′)。这种数据不需要人来标注："下一刻发生了什么"本身就是答案，数据自己给自己出题，所以叫自监督。从这样的数据里能学到几样东西：At the far right of the spectrum there are neither demonstrations nor rewards, only the (s, a, s′) the robot collects by moving around on its own. Such data need no human labelling: “what happened next” is itself the answer, and the data set their own exercises, which is why this is called self-supervised. Several things can be learned from such data:

  • 前向模型，也就是世界模型：给定 s 和 a，预测 s′。它是第 1 步那个转移 P（也叫动力学）的学出来的版本；"前向"是说从原因往后推结果。Forward models, that is, world models: given s and a, predict s′. It is a learned version of the transition P (also called the dynamics) from step 1; “forward” means reasoning from cause to effect.
  • 逆向模型，也就是第 3 步的逆动力学模型：给定 s 和 s′，猜中间的 a。第 3 步给视频补动作用的就是它。Agrawal 等（2016）让机器人自己戳了大量物体，学会了"想把物体推到那里，该怎么戳"。An inverse model, which is the inverse dynamics model of step 3: given s and s′, guess the a in between. This is what step 3 uses to fill in actions for videos. Agrawal et al. (2016) had a robot poke lots of objects on its own and learn “how to poke to move an object over there”.
  • 成败的标签：让机器人自己判断成没成，比如抓完提起来以后，夹爪没有完全合拢，说明手里有东西。这样每一次尝试都自动带上了"成功"或"失败"的标签，可以用来训练一个预测"这样抓能不能成"的模型。Pinto 和 Gupta（2016）让机器人自己试抓了 5 万次、用了 700 小时，标签全部自动产生；Levine 等（2016）用 6 到 14 台机械臂，两个月里收集了 80 多万次抓取。Success labels: let the robot judge for itself whether it succeeded; for example, if the gripper has not fully closed after lifting, something is in its hand. Every attempt then comes with a “success” or “failure” label automatically, which can train a model that predicts “will this grasp work”. Pinto and Gupta (2016) had a robot attempt 50,000 grasps by itself over 700 hours, with every label generated automatically; Levine et al. (2016) collected more than 800,000 grasp attempts over two months with 6 to 14 robot arms.

推一个方块：模型学到"推 1 秒，方块走 2 厘米"。目标在 10 厘米外，规划器就想出"推 5 秒"。但如果桌子边缘附近的数据从来没收集过，模型不知道方块会掉下去，规划器可能会很自信地把它推下桌。Pushing a block: the model has learned “push for 1 second and the block moves 2 cm”. 规划器会专门找模型的漏洞：模型在没见过的区域里预测得很离谱，规划器偏偏会挑那些"模型以为特别好"的动作。所以要限制规划别离开数据覆盖的范围，或者一边用一边收集新数据修正模型。A planner hunts for the model’s flaws: where the model has seen no data its predictions can be wildly wrong, and the planner will pick exactly the actions the model wrongly thinks are excellent. So planning is kept within the range the data cover, or new data keep being collected to correct the model as it is used.

这条路上的代表作：World Models（Ha 和 Schmidhuber 2018）让智能体完全在自己学到的"梦境"里练习躲火球，再搬回真实的游戏里；Dreamer（Hafner 等 2019）在压缩后的隐空间里想象未来（把一张图压缩成几十个数，只留下要紧的信息，在这几十个数上往前推，比预测整张图省事得多），用来训练策略；DayDreamer（Wu 等 2022）把它用到真机上，四足机器人不用仿真器、从零开始，大约一小时学会了从仰躺翻身、站起来和走路；V-JEPA 2（Meta 2025）先在一百多万小时的网络视频上自监督预训练（预训练：先在海量的通用数据上把模型训练一遍，打好基础），再用不到 62 小时的机器人视频学一个"知道动作会带来什么"的世界模型，在两个新的实验室里不用再收集数据，靠规划完成了抓取和摆放。Landmarks on this road: World Models (Ha and Schmidhuber 2018) trained an agent to dodge fireballs entirely inside its own learned “dream” and then moved it back into the real game; Dreamer (Hafner et al. 2019) imagines the future in a compressed latent space (squeezing an image into a few dozen numbers that keep only what matters, and rolling those forward, which is far easier than predicting whole images) to train its policy; DayDreamer (Wu et al. 2022) took it to real hardware, where a quadruped with no simulator learned from scratch, in about an hour, to roll off its back, stand up and walk; V-JEPA 2 (Meta 2025) was pretrained self-supervised on over a million hours of internet video (pretraining: first training a model on huge amounts of general data to lay the groundwork), then learned an action-conditioned world model from less than 62 hours of robot video, and picked and placed objects by planning in two new labs without collecting any data there.

玩耍数据：不预设任务Play data: no task set in advance

还有一种折中：让人随便"玩"机器人，遥操作着开抽屉、推方块、按按钮，没有规定的任务；再把录下来的轨迹里任意两个时刻配成"从这里出发，目标是那里"的训练样本。到达过的任何状态，都可以看作一个已经达成的目标，这和 HER 是同一个想法，所以策略的输入里也要有"目标是什么"。Lynch 等（2019）这样用一份玩耍数据学会了很多任务。A middle way: let people “play” with the robot, teleoperating it to open drawers, push blocks and press buttons with no assigned task, then turn any two moments of the recorded trajectories into a training example of “start here, the goal is there”. Any state that was reached counts as a goal that was achieved, the same idea as HER, so here too the policy takes “what the goal is” as an input. Lynch et al. (2019) learned many tasks from one set of play data this way.

好在哪What it is good at

  • 数据便宜，不需要人来标注，可以大规模收集Data are cheap and need no human labelling, so they can be collected at scale
  • 学到的是通用的"物理常识"，换任务不用从头学What is learned is general “physical common sense”, so a new task does not mean starting from scratch
  • 有了模型就能规划，样本效率通常比无模型的强化学习高得多，不过模型的误差可能限制最终能达到的水平A model enables planning, which is usually far more sample-efficient than model-free reinforcement learning, although model errors can limit how good the final result gets

坑在哪Where it breaks

  • 本身不知道该做什么，最后还是要给目标或奖励It does not know what to do by itself; a goal or reward is still needed in the end
  • 模型误差会随着想象的步数累积，还会被规划器钻空子Model errors accumulate over imagined steps and get exploited by the planner
  • 直接预测像素又难又浪费，现在多在压缩后的隐空间里预测Predicting pixels directly is hard and wasteful, so prediction now mostly happens in a compressed latent space
  • 随便乱动收集的数据，往往覆盖不到精细操作需要的那些状态Data from random wandering often miss the states that fine manipulation needs

第 8 步 · 组合起来用Step 8 · Combining them

真实系统：几站一起上Real systems: several stops at once

光谱上的每一站都有明显的短板，所以真正好用的系统几乎都是组合。最常见的套路是：先用便宜、信息丰富的信号把策略带到"差不多会"的地方，再用更贵但更直接的信号去打磨。Every stop on the spectrum has obvious weaknesses, so systems that work well are almost always combinations. The most common pattern: use a cheap, information-rich signal to bring the policy to “roughly able to do it”, then polish it with a more expensive but more direct signal.

先模仿，再强化Imitate first, then reinforce

示范解决了强化学习最头疼的探索问题：策略一开始就在成功附近，不用指望瞎碰。强化学习反过来解决了行为克隆的两个问题：上限是老师，以及分布偏移（它会在自己的状态分布上练）。DAPG（Rajeswaran 等 2017）用少量人类示范加上强化学习，让 24 自由度（大致说，有 24 个能活动的关节）的五指手学会了开门、用锤子等任务，需要的经验只相当于几个小时的机器人时间。AlphaGo（2016）也是先用人类棋谱做行为克隆，再自我对弈。Demonstrations solve reinforcement learning’s biggest headache, exploration: the policy starts close to success instead of hoping to stumble on it. Reinforcement learning in turn fixes two problems of behaviour cloning: the teacher as a ceiling, and distribution shift (it practises on its own state distribution). DAPG (Rajeswaran et al. 2017) combined a small number of human demonstrations with reinforcement learning to teach a hand with 24 degrees of freedom (roughly, 24 joints that can move) tasks such as opening a door and using a hammer, with experience equivalent to only a few hours of robot time. AlphaGo (2016) likewise started with behaviour cloning on human games and then played itself.

离线强化学习：只用旧数据，但想比数据做得好Offline RL: only old data, but aiming to beat it

有时手里只有一大堆旧日志，好的坏的都有，还带着奖励，却不能再让机器人去试。行为克隆只会学到这些数据的"平均水平"；离线强化学习用上奖励，有可能把不同轨迹里的好片段拼起来，得到比任何一条都好的策略。难点又是分布偏移：Q 函数（就是第 4 步的 Q 值，只是用神经网络来算）对数据里没出现过的动作只能瞎猜，猜错有高有低，可策略取最大值时偏偏挑中被高估的那些。比如某个状态下有 5 个数据里没见过的动作，它们真实的价值都是 0，Q 函数对每个都猜错一点（误差大约 ±1）；取最大值时挑中的往往是猜得最高的那个，平均下来要高估约 1.16。自举（用 Q 去估 Q）又把这些高估一路传开。CQL（Kumar 等 2020）的办法是故意压低没见过的动作的 Q 值，宁可保守。入门可以看 Levine 等（2020）的教程综述。Sometimes all you have is a big pile of old logs, good and bad mixed, with rewards attached, and the robot cannot try anything new. Behaviour cloning would only learn the data’s “average level”; offline reinforcement learning uses the rewards and may stitch good pieces from different trajectories into a policy better than any one of them. The difficulty is, once again, distribution shift: for actions that never appear in the data the Q function (the Q values of step 4, computed by a neural network) can only guess, and its errors go both ways, but taking the maximum picks out exactly the overestimated ones. Say a state has 5 actions never seen in the data, all truly worth 0, and the Q function gets each a little wrong (errors of about ±1); the maximum tends to pick whichever was guessed highest, and on average it overestimates by about 1.16. Bootstrapping (estimating Q from Q) then spreads these overestimates further. CQL (Kumar et al. 2020) deliberately pushes down the Q values of unseen actions, preferring to be conservative. A good starting point is the tutorial and review by Levine et al. (2020).

网络数据加机器人示范：视觉-语言-动作模型Web data plus robot demonstrations: vision-language-action models

把一个在网络图文数据上预训练好的视觉-语言模型（能看图、能读写文字的大模型），接着用大量机器人示范微调，让它直接输出动作，这类模型叫 VLA（vision-language-action）。网络数据（光谱最右边）提供常识和语义，机器人示范（最左边）提供动作。RT-2（2023）把动作写成文字 token，和语言一起训练；Open X-Embodiment 汇集了 21 家机构、22 种机器人的一百多万条轨迹；OpenVLA（2024）是一个开源的 70 亿参数模型；π0（2024）用流匹配生成连续的动作块，能叠衣服、收拾桌子。它的后续版本 π*0.6（2025，名字借用了最优策略的记号 π*）又在 VLA 上加入了强化学习，从机器人自己的尝试和人的纠正里继续提高，绕了一圈又回到光谱中间。Take a vision-language model (a large model that can look at images and read and write text) pretrained on web images and text, fine-tune it on lots of robot demonstrations, and have it output actions directly: such models are called VLAs (vision-language-action). The web data (far right of the spectrum) supply common sense and semantics; the robot demonstrations (far left) supply the actions. RT-2 (2023) writes actions as text tokens and trains them together with language; Open X-Embodiment pooled more than a million trajectories from 22 kinds of robots at 21 institutions; OpenVLA (2024) is an open 7-billion-parameter model; π0 (2024) generates chunks of continuous actions with flow matching and can fold laundry and clear tables. Its successor π*0.6 (2025, whose name borrows the optimal-policy symbol π*) added reinforcement learning on top of a VLA, improving from the robot’s own attempts and from human corrections, coming full circle back to the middle of the spectrum.

先玩：点几个真实系统，看它们各用了光谱上的哪几站、按什么顺序，以及为什么这样组合。Play first: pick a few real systems and see which stops on the spectrum each one uses, in what order, and why.

    规律是：信号从哪来，取决于什么便宜、什么可靠。能示范、难写奖励（叠衣服），就从示范出发；不能示范，但有好的仿真和清楚的奖励（腿足运动、下棋），就以试错为主；两样都缺，但数据海量（网络视频和文本），就先自监督学通用的知识，再用少量示范或奖励把它引到具体任务上。The pattern: where the signal comes from depends on what is cheap and what is reliable. If a task is easy to demonstrate but hard to reward (folding clothes), start from demonstrations; if it cannot be demonstrated but there is a good simulator and a clear reward (legged locomotion, board games), rely mainly on trial and error; if both are missing but data are plentiful (web video and text), first learn general knowledge by self-supervision, then steer it to a specific task with a few demonstrations or a reward.

    几条经验A few rules of thumb

    • 先问信号从哪来。有示范就先用示范；能写出奖励、又有仿真，就用强化学习；两样都有，就组合起来。Ask first where the signal comes from. If there are demonstrations, start with them; if you can write a reward and have a simulator, use reinforcement learning; if you have both, combine them.
    • 模仿学习先查两件事：分布偏移（加 DAgger、加噪声示范、加纠偏数据）和多模态（用能表示分布的策略，用动作块）。For imitation learning, check two things first: distribution shift (add DAgger, noisy demonstrations or recovery data) and multimodality (use a policy that can represent a distribution, use action chunks).
    • 强化学习先查两件事：奖励有没有漏洞（想象一个只想刷分的对手），探索够不够（奖励是不是太稀疏）。For reinforcement learning, check two things first: whether the reward has loopholes (imagine an opponent who only wants points) and whether exploration is sufficient (is the reward too sparse?).
    • 训练指标好，不等于真机上好。行为克隆的训练损失、强化学习在仿真里的回报，都可能骗人，最终要看真机上的表现。Good training numbers do not mean a good real robot. A behaviour-cloning training loss and a return in simulation can both mislead; in the end it is the real robot’s performance that counts.
    • 先试最简单的基线。很多操作任务里，几十条高质量示范，加上好的动作表示（扩散模型、动作块），就已经够用了。Try the simplest baseline first. For many manipulation tasks, a few dozen high-quality demonstrations plus a good action representation (diffusion models, action chunks) are already enough.

    检查一下Check yourself

    行为克隆在训练集上误差很小，为什么一跑就出问题？Behaviour cloning has a tiny error on the training set. Why does it go wrong as soon as it runs?

    训练用的状态来自专家，执行时的状态来自学生自己。学生一偏离，就到了训练数据没有覆盖的地方，误差越积越大，这就是分布偏移。DAgger 让专家在学生实际到过的状态上补标签，直接修正这一点。The training states come from the expert, but at run time the states come from the student. Once the student deviates it is somewhere the training data do not cover, and errors pile up: that is distribution shift. DAgger has the expert label the states the student actually visits, which fixes exactly this.

    "离目标越近，奖励越多"这种写法有什么风险？怎么改？What is risky about “the closer to the goal, the more reward”? How would you fix it?

    如果只奖励靠近、不惩罚远离，来回走就能反复刷分；γ 越大，智能体越看重长远的总账，这种细水长流的刷分就越划算。改成势能塑形 F = γΦ(s′) − Φ(s)，并让终点的势能为 0：一整局的塑形奖励总账就是 −Φ(起点)，和中间怎么走无关，最优策略不会变。If you reward approaching without penalizing retreating, going back and forth farms points over and over; and the larger γ, the more the agent cares about the long-run total, so this slow-but-steady farming pays off more. Use potential-based shaping F = γΦ(s′) − Φ(s) instead, with the potential at the goal set to 0: an episode’s total shaping reward is then just −Φ(start), whatever the route in between, and the optimal policy does not change.

    为什么 AlphaGo Zero 不用人类棋谱，反而比 AlphaGo 更强？Why is AlphaGo Zero, which uses no human games, stronger than AlphaGo?

    围棋有完美的仿真器（规则）和清楚的奖励（输赢），自我对弈的数据要多少有多少。条件这么好时，强化学习不再需要示范来帮忙探索，从零开始的自我对弈也不受人类下法的局限。机器人很少有这样的条件，所以通常还是要示范帮忙。Go has a perfect simulator (the rules) and a clear reward (win or lose), and self-play supplies as much data as you want. Under such conditions reinforcement learning no longer needs demonstrations to help it explore, and self-play from scratch is not limited by the way humans play. Robots rarely enjoy such conditions, which is why they usually still need demonstrations.

    离线强化学习和行为克隆都只用现成的数据，区别在哪？Offline RL and behaviour cloning both use only existing data. What is the difference?

    行为克隆不看奖励，学的是数据里的平均行为，上限就是数据。离线强化学习用上了奖励，有可能挑出、拼接数据里的好片段，学到比数据里任何一条都好的策略；代价是要小心它高估没见过的动作。Behaviour cloning ignores rewards and learns the average behaviour in the data, so the data are its ceiling. Offline RL uses the rewards and may pick out and stitch together the good pieces of the data, learning a policy better than any single trajectory in it; the price is guarding against its overestimates for unseen actions.

    一半示范从左边绕过障碍、一半从右边。用均方误差训练会怎样？Half the demonstrations pass an obstacle on the left, half on the right. What happens if you train with a squared error?

    策略会输出两种做法的平均，也就是直直撞上障碍。要用能表示多峰分布的策略（混合高斯、离散 token、扩散或流匹配），最好一次生成一整段动作，免得每一步都在两种做法之间摇摆。The policy outputs the average of the two, which drives straight into the obstacle. Use a policy that can represent a multi-peaked distribution (a Gaussian mixture, discrete tokens, diffusion or flow matching), ideally generating a whole chunk of actions at a time, so it does not waver between the two options at every step.
