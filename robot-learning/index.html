从示范到试错From Demonstration to Trial and Error

模仿学习、强化学习、逆强化学习、离线强化学习、世界模型、VLA……机器人学习的名词很多，但它们都在解决同一个问题：给机器人找一个策略，也就是"看到什么，就做什么"。真正的区别只有一个：学习信号从哪里来。这一篇沿着一条光谱讲：最左边是有人手把手示范，中间是只告诉你做得好不好，最右边是谁也不告诉你，机器人自己摸索世界怎么运转。每一站都讲清楚它解决什么问题、核心概念是什么、好在哪、坑在哪。最重要的两站，模仿学习和强化学习，配了好几个交互，拖一拖就能看到问题出在哪。Imitation learning, reinforcement learning, inverse RL, offline RL, world models, VLAs… robot learning has a lot of names, but they all tackle the same problem: find the robot a policy, a rule for “given what I see, do this”. The one real difference between them is where the learning signal comes from. This page walks along a single spectrum: on the far left someone shows the robot exactly what to do; in the middle someone only says how well it did; on the far right nobody says anything and the robot works out on its own how the world behaves. For each stop we cover what problem it solves, its key ideas, what it is good at and where it breaks. The two most important stops, imitation learning and reinforcement learning, come with several interactives, so you can drag things around and see exactly where the trouble starts.

第 0 步 · 总览Step 0 · Overview

一条光谱：学习信号从哪里来One spectrum: where the learning signal comes from

不管叫什么名字，这些方法最后要学的都是同一个东西：策略。策略就是一个函数，输入是机器人此刻感知到的（相机画面、关节角度、力传感器读数），输出是它下一步该做的（每个电机转多少、夹爪开还是合）。区别只在于"老师"给了多少信息：Whatever they are called, these methods all end up learning the same thing: a policy. A policy is a function whose input is what the robot senses right now (camera images, joint angles, force readings) and whose output is what it should do next (how far to turn each motor, whether to open or close the gripper). The only difference is how much the “teacher” tells it:

  • 最左边，老师手把手示范，每一步都给出标准答案。这是模仿学习。On the far left, a teacher demonstrates by hand and gives the correct answer at every step. This is imitation learning.
  • 往右走，老师给的越来越少：只有视频没有动作，只说两段里哪段更好，或者只在最后打一个分。打分这一站就是强化学习。Moving right, the teacher says less and less: video without actions, only which of two attempts was better, or just one score at the end. The scoring stop is reinforcement learning.
  • 最右边，没有老师，机器人只能自己观察"我这么做，世界会怎么变"。这是自监督学习和世界模型。On the far right, there is no teacher, and the robot can only watch “if I do this, how does the world change”. This is self-supervised learning and world models.

越往左，每条数据带的信息越多，但要人花时间；越往右，数据越便宜，机器人自己就能大量产生，但每条数据能教的东西越少，学起来越难。实际好用的系统，几乎都是把几站组合起来用。The further left, the more each piece of data tells you, but the more human time it costs; the further right, the cheaper the data, which the robot can produce in bulk by itself, but the less each piece teaches and the harder learning becomes. Systems that work well in practice almost always combine several stops.

第 1 步 · 基本概念Step 1 · Basics

所有方法共用的几个词A few words every method uses

把机器人和环境的互动切成一步一步。每一步：机器人看到状态 s，按策略 π 选一个动作 a；世界按自己的规律变到新的状态 s′；如果有人定义了什么叫好，还会给一个奖励 r。然后重复。这个循环有个正式的名字，叫马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）。Cut the interaction between the robot and its environment into steps. At each step the robot sees a state s and picks an action a according to its policy π; the world moves to a new state s′ by its own rules; and if someone has defined what “good” means, it also hands out a reward r. Then it repeats. This loop has a formal name: a Markov decision process (MDP).

状态和观测不一样。理论上的状态要包含决定未来的全部信息；真机器人只拿得到观测 o，一张照片看不出物体多重、正在以多快的速度运动。所以很多机器人策略会把最近几帧一起输入，补上单帧看不到的信息。A state is not the same as an observation. In theory the state contains everything that determines the future; a real robot only gets an observation o, and a single photo does not tell you how heavy an object is or how fast it is moving. That is why many robot policies take the last few frames as input, to recover what a single frame cannot show.

γ 决定"有多耐心"。γ 小，眼前的小奶酪更香；γ 大，值得为大奶酪多走几步。γ 还有一个技术上的用处：就算一局无限长，回报也是一个有限的数。γ sets how patient the agent is. With a small γ the small cheese right in front looks better; with a large γ the big cheese is worth a few extra steps. γ also has a technical job: even an endless episode has a finite return.

奖励和回报不是一回事：奖励是每一步的分，回报是一整局的总账。所有方法追求的都是回报大（准确地说是期望回报大），而不是某一步的奖励大。为了长远的回报暂时吃点亏，比如绕远路，是常有的事。Reward and return are different things: a reward is the score of one step, the return is the bill for a whole episode. Every method is after a large return (more precisely, a large expected return), not a large reward at any single step. Taking a small loss now for a better return later, such as a detour, is perfectly normal.

第 2 步 · 模仿学习Step 2 · Imitation learning

行为克隆：把"开车"变成监督学习Behaviour cloning: turning “driving” into supervised learning

它解决的问题：很多任务里，"做得好"很难写成公式。叠衣服，什么叫叠好了？倒水，什么叫倒得好？但人可以直接做给机器人看。模仿学习的想法很朴素：把专家的示范记下来，让机器人照着做。The problem it solves: for many tasks, “doing it well” is hard to write down as a formula. When is a shirt folded properly? What counts as pouring well? But a person can simply show the robot. The idea of imitation learning is simple: record an expert’s demonstrations and have the robot do the same.

示范怎么来？常见的有四种：遥操作，人用一套主手、VR 头显或手柄实时控制机器人，比如 ALOHA；动觉示教，人直接扶着机械臂走一遍；手持夹爪，人拿着和机器人同款的夹爪去做，相机记录下来，比如 UMI；或者直接拍人做事的视频（第 3 步）。Where do demonstrations come from? Four common ways: teleoperation, where a person drives the robot live with leader arms, a VR headset or a gamepad, as in ALOHA; kinesthetic teaching, where a person physically guides the arm; handheld grippers, where a person does the task holding the same gripper the robot uses while a camera records it, as in UMI; or plain videos of people doing things (step 3).

为什么会越开越偏：分布偏移Why it drifts further and further: distribution shift

训练时，策略只见过专家走过的那些状态，而专家总是开在赛道中间。学生一旦犯了一个小错，就到了一个专家从来没去过的地方：那里没有训练数据，策略只能瞎猜；猜错了又走得更偏，误差像滚雪球一样越滚越大。Ross 和 Bagnell（2010）证明：如果克隆出来的策略在专家会去的那些状态上犯错的概率是 ε（也就是训练误差），走 T 步，最坏情况下它的总代价会比专家多出 T²ε 量级；如果训练和使用时的状态分布一样，像普通的监督学习那样，只会多出 Tε 量级。During training the policy only sees the states the expert visited, and the expert always drives down the middle. Once the student makes a small mistake, it is somewhere the expert has never been: there is no training data there, so the policy guesses; a wrong guess takes it even further off, and the error snowballs. Ross and Bagnell (2010) showed that if the cloned policy errs with probability ε on the states the expert visits (its training error), then over T steps its total cost can, in the worst case, exceed the expert’s by an amount on the order of T²ε; if training and use saw the same state distribution, as in ordinary supervised learning, the excess would only be on the order of Tε.

训练误差小，不代表跑得好。训练数据来自专家的状态分布，执行时的状态来自学生自己的分布，两者不一样。这叫分布偏移（distribution shift），也叫误差累积（compounding error），是模仿学习最核心的坑。A small training error does not mean good driving. The training data come from the expert’s state distribution, but at run time the states come from the student’s own distribution, and the two differ. This is called distribution shift, or compounding error, and it is the central pitfall of imitation learning.

DAgger：让专家给"犯错以后的状态"打标签DAgger: have the expert label the states you reach after a mistake

D ← D ∪ { (s, π*(s)) : s 是学生 πθ 到过的状态 }D ← D ∪ { (s, π*(s)) : s visited by the student πθ }

办法很直接：让学生自己开，把它实际到过的状态记下来，请专家补标"这时候该怎么做"（π* 是专家），并进数据集重新训练，循环几轮。这样数据里就有了"偏了以后怎么回来"。这就是 DAgger（Dataset Aggregation，Ross 等 2011），在一定假设下（学习算法是"无悔"的，犯下的错也能纠正回来），理论上把误差的增长从 T² 量级降回了 T 量级。交互里点"DAgger：专家补标一轮"就是这一步。The fix is direct: let the student drive, record the states it actually reaches, ask the expert (π*) to label “what should be done here”, add them to the dataset, retrain, and repeat for a few rounds. Now the data contain “how to get back after drifting”. This is DAgger (Dataset Aggregation, Ross et al. 2011), which under certain assumptions (a “no-regret” learner, and mistakes that can be recovered from) brings the error growth back from order T² to order T in theory. Tapping “DAgger: one round of expert labels” in the interactive does exactly this.

代价是专家要随叫随到地给标签，而且是给"学生开到的奇怪位置"标。开车还好说，很多任务里人很难只看一个状态就说出正确动作。The price is an expert on call to provide labels, and labels for the odd places the student wanders into. For driving that is manageable; for many tasks a person finds it hard to name the right action from a single state.

不想请专家在线标注，也可以在收集示范时就想办法制造"偏离"。NVIDIA 的端到端自动驾驶（Bojarski 等 2016）在车上装了左右两个偏置的相机，把它们拍到的画面当作"车开偏了"的样本，标上往回打的方向。DART（Laskey 等 2017）则在示范时故意给专家的动作加噪声：专家被推偏了会往回开，这些往回开的动作就被记进了数据。交互里打开"示范时故意加噪声"试试。If you cannot ask an expert for online labels, you can try to create “drift” while recording the demonstrations. NVIDIA’s end-to-end self-driving car (Bojarski et al. 2016) carried two extra cameras offset to the left and right, treated their images as samples of “the car has drifted”, and labelled them with a steering correction back to the centre. DART (Laskey et al. 2017) instead adds noise to the expert’s actions while recording: when the expert gets pushed off line it steers back, and those recoveries end up in the data. Try turning on “Noisy demos” in the interactive.

两条路都对，取平均就撞树：多模态Both ways are right, the average hits the tree: multimodality

第二个坑：同一个状态下，专家可能有好几种都对的做法。绕过一棵树，从左绕、从右绕都行；拿杯子，可以抓杯身，也可以抓把手。这种"一个输入对应好几个正确输出"的情况叫多模态。如果用均方误差训练，策略学到的是所有示范动作的平均：左绕和右绕平均下来，正好是直直地朝树飞过去。The second pitfall: in the same state an expert may have several equally correct ways to act. You can pass a tree on the left or on the right; you can grab a cup by its body or by its handle. One input with several correct outputs is called multimodality. Trained with a squared error, a policy learns the average of all demonstrated actions, and the average of “left” and “right” is flying straight into the tree.

解决办法是让策略输出一个分布，而不是一个平均数：可以用混合高斯；可以把动作离散成一个个 token，像分类一样输出，RT-2 就是这么做的；也可以用生成模型直接生成动作，比如 Diffusion Policy（Chi 等 2023）用扩散模型，π0（Black 等 2024）用流匹配。另一个关键技巧是一次预测一整段动作（动作块），ACT（Zhao 等 2023）和 Diffusion Policy 都这么做：既不会每一步都在两个选择之间摇摆，需要做决定的次数也少了，误差累积得更慢。The fix is to have the policy output a distribution rather than a single average: a Gaussian mixture; discretizing actions into tokens and outputting them like a classifier, as RT-2 does; or generating actions with a generative model, such as the diffusion model in Diffusion Policy (Chi et al. 2023) or the flow matching in π0 (Black et al. 2024). Another key trick is to predict a whole sequence of actions at once (an action chunk), as both ACT (Zhao et al. 2023) and Diffusion Policy do: the policy no longer wavers between two choices at every step, and with fewer decisions to make, errors compound more slowly.

好在哪What it is good at

  • 简单、稳定，本质就是监督学习，好训练Simple and stable: it is just supervised learning, so it is easy to train
  • 不需要写奖励函数No reward function to write
  • 数据效率高：一个具体任务，几十到几百条示范就可能学会，ACT 论文里每个任务只用了约 10 分钟的示范Data-efficient: a specific task can be learned from tens to hundreds of demonstrations; the ACT paper used only about 10 minutes of demonstrations per task
  • 不需要机器人在真机上冒险试错No risky trial and error on the real robot

坑在哪Where it breaks

  • 分布偏移：偏了就不知道怎么回来Distribution shift: once off course, it does not know how to get back
  • 示范要人亲手做，贵、慢，很难扩展到大量任务Demonstrations are made by hand: expensive, slow, and hard to scale to many tasks
  • 上限是老师：学不出比示范更好的做法，示范里的失误也会一并学走The teacher is the ceiling: it cannot learn to do better than the demonstrations, and it copies their mistakes too
  • 多模态处理不好，就会"取平均"Mishandle multimodality and it averages

第 3 步 · 示范的变体Step 3 · Variants of demonstration

示范不够理想的时候When the demonstrations are not ideal

行为克隆要求每一步都有"状态加上专家动作"。往光谱右边挪一点，信号变弱了，但更容易拿到。下面三种都还是"从人身上学"，区别在于人给的是什么。Behaviour cloning needs “a state plus the expert’s action” at every step. Move a little to the right on the spectrum and the signal gets weaker but easier to obtain. The three ideas below still learn from people; they differ in what people provide.

只有视频，没有动作：从观察中学习Video without actions: learning from observation

网上有海量人做事的视频，但视频里没有动作标签：看不出每一帧手上用了多大力，更不知道机器人的电机该转多少。常用的思路分两步。先让机器人自己动一动，收集一批 (s, a, s′)，训练一个逆动力学模型：看前后两帧，猜中间做了什么动作。再用它给视频补上动作标签，然后照常做行为克隆。BCO（Torabi 等 2018）就是这个思路。OpenAI 的 VPT（Baker 等 2022）把它做到了很大规模：用约 2000 小时带键盘鼠标记录的 Minecraft 录像训练逆动力学模型，给约 7 万小时网络视频补上标签，再做行为克隆；用强化学习进一步微调后，成了第一个能做出钻石工具的游戏智能体。The internet has endless videos of people doing things, but no action labels: you cannot tell how much force the hand used in each frame, let alone how far a robot’s motors should turn. A common recipe has two steps. First let the robot move around a bit, collect some (s, a, s′), and train an inverse dynamics model: look at two consecutive frames and guess the action in between. Then use it to fill in action labels for the videos, and do behaviour cloning as usual. BCO (Torabi et al. 2018) follows this recipe. OpenAI’s VPT (Baker et al. 2022) scaled it up: about 2,000 hours of Minecraft recordings with logged keyboard and mouse actions trained the inverse dynamics model, which labelled about 70,000 hours of online video for behaviour cloning; after further fine-tuning with reinforcement learning it became the first game agent able to craft diamond tools.

机器人还多一道坎：视频里是人手，机器人是夹爪，身体结构不一样（embodiment gap）。人手的动作不能直接照搬，要先换算成机器人能做的动作。Robots face one more hurdle: the videos show human hands, while the robot has a gripper, so the bodies differ (the embodiment gap). Human motions cannot be copied directly; they first have to be translated into motions the robot can make.

推断意图：逆强化学习Inferring intent: inverse reinforcement learning

换个思路：不学"他怎么做"，而学"他想要什么"。逆强化学习（IRL，Ng 和 Russell 2000）从示范里反推出一个奖励函数，使得专家的行为在这个奖励下是最优的；再用强化学习去优化这个奖励。A different idea: learn not “how they did it” but “what they wanted”. Inverse reinforcement learning (IRL, Ng and Russell 2000) works backwards from demonstrations to a reward function under which the expert’s behaviour is optimal, then uses reinforcement learning to optimize that reward.

这样做有两个好处。第一，意图比动作更容易迁移：环境变了、身体变了，"把盘子放进碗架"这个目标不变。第二，优化奖励有可能做得比示范者更好：斯坦福的自主直升机从专家飞行员的多次示范里学出目标轨迹和奖励，完成了一系列高难度特技，论文报告它"和专家飞得一样好，而且常常更好"（Abbeel、Coates、Ng 2010）。This has two advantages. First, intent transfers better than actions: if the environment or the body changes, the goal “put the plate in the dish rack” stays the same. Second, optimizing a reward can beat the demonstrator: Stanford’s autonomous helicopter learned a target trajectory and a reward from several demonstrations by an expert pilot and flew a series of difficult aerobatic manoeuvres, and the paper reports performance “as well as, and often even better than” the expert pilot (Abbeel, Coates and Ng 2010).

难点是歧义：同一段示范可以被无数个奖励解释，最极端的是"奖励恒为 0"，在它下面任何行为都是最优的。所以需要额外的假设，比如最大熵 IRL：把专家看成按"奖励越高的轨迹越可能被选中"（概率正比于 e 的奖励次方）的方式行动，再用最大似然去拟合奖励。而且每猜一次奖励，内循环都要跑一遍强化学习，很贵。GAIL（Ho 和 Ermon 2016）换了个更直接的做法：训练一个判别器，分辨每一步的状态和动作是来自专家还是学生；学生用强化学习去骗过判别器，判别器越认为它"像专家"，学生拿到的奖励越高。GAIL 最后得到的是策略本身，不会留下一个能单独拿去复用的奖励函数。The difficulty is ambiguity: the same demonstrations can be explained by countless rewards, the extreme case being “the reward is always 0”, under which every behaviour is optimal. So extra assumptions are needed, such as maximum-entropy IRL: model the expert as picking trajectories with probability proportional to e raised to their reward, so higher-reward trajectories are more likely, and fit the reward by maximum likelihood. And every guess of the reward requires running reinforcement learning in an inner loop, which is expensive. GAIL (Ho and Ermon 2016) takes a more direct route: train a discriminator to tell whether each state–action pair came from the expert or the student, and train the student with reinforcement learning to fool it; the more the discriminator thinks a step looks like the expert’s, the higher the student’s reward. What GAIL produces is the policy itself; it does not leave behind a reward function you can reuse on its own.

比较好坏：偏好学习Comparing attempts: preference learning

有些行为人既做不出来，也打不出准确的分，但把两段放在一起，能看出哪段更好。偏好学习让人反复比较两段录像，用这些比较训练一个奖励模型，再用强化学习去优化它。For some behaviours a person can neither perform them nor give an accurate score, yet can tell which of two attempts is better when they are side by side. Preference learning has people compare pairs of clips over and over, trains a reward model on those comparisons, and then optimizes it with reinforcement learning.

奖励模型也会被钻空子：策略可能找到某种让 R̂ 很高、人却并不喜欢的行为。所以要不断补充新的比较，或者限制策略别偏离太远。The reward model can be gamed too: the policy may find behaviour that gets a high R̂ but that people do not actually like. So new comparisons keep being added, or the policy is kept from drifting too far.

The same method, applied later to language models, is RLHF (InstructGPT, Ouyang et al. 2022).

好在哪What it is good at

  • 视频几乎免费，数量比机器人示范多好几个数量级Video is almost free, and there is orders of magnitude more of it than robot demonstrations
  • 逆强化学习学到的是意图，更容易迁移，还可能超过示范者Inverse RL learns intent, which transfers better and can surpass the demonstrator
  • 偏好比示范、比打分都容易给：人只要会评判，不用自己会做Preferences are easier to give than demonstrations or scores: people only need to judge, not to do it themselves

坑在哪Where it breaks

  • 视频没有动作，身体也和机器人不一样，补出来的标签有噪声Video has no actions and the body differs from the robot’s, so the inferred labels are noisy
  • 逆强化学习有歧义，内循环还要跑强化学习，计算很贵Inverse RL is ambiguous and runs reinforcement learning in its inner loop, which is computationally expensive

第 4 步 · 强化学习Step 4 · Reinforcement learning

只告诉你好不好，怎么做自己试Only told how well you did, the how is up to you

它解决的问题：示范拿不到（没人能遥操作四条腿在碎石上跑），或者想做得比示范者更好时，就只剩下奖励这条路。强化学习（RL）让机器人反复尝试：带来高回报的做法多做，带来低回报的少做。好处是不需要老师；难处在于奖励往往要很多步以后才出现，必须弄清楚"这个好结果要归功于之前的哪几步"，这叫信用分配；还得自己决定去哪里尝试，这叫探索。The problem it solves: when demonstrations are unavailable (nobody can teleoperate four legs running over rubble), or you want to do better than the demonstrator, a reward is all that is left. Reinforcement learning (RL) has the robot try again and again: do more of what brings a high return and less of what brings a low one. The upside is that no teacher is needed. The hard part is that rewards often arrive many steps later, so you must work out which earlier steps deserve the credit for a good outcome, which is called credit assignment; and you must decide for yourself where to try things, which is called exploration.

强化学习有两条主线：一条先学"每个状态、每个动作有多好"（价值），再据此选动作；另一条直接调整策略本身（策略梯度）。今天的大多数算法是这两条线的结合。Reinforcement learning has two main threads: one first learns how good each state and action is (values) and then picks actions accordingly; the other adjusts the policy itself directly (policy gradients). Most algorithms today combine the two.

In the interactive, with ε = 0.1 and Q starting at 0, the mouse soon finds the small cheese nearby and keeps going there, never once reaching the big cheese. Two simple remedies: ε-greedy, which takes a random step with probability ε; and optimistic initial values, which start every Q high so that every untried action looks tempting and gets tried.

格子世界可以给每个 (s, a) 存一个数。状态换成相机画面，格子就多到存不下了，要用神经网络来近似 Q：DQN（Mnih 等 2015）这样用同一套网络学玩 49 个 Atari 游戏，很多达到了人类水平。A grid world can store one number per (s, a). 策略梯度：直接调策略Policy gradients: adjust the policy directly

The other route writes the policy directly as a parameterized probability distribution πθ(a | s), for example “action = a mean μθ(s) computed by a network, plus a bit of Gaussian noise”, and adjusts θ so that actions that bring high returns become more likely.

减去基线不改变梯度的期望方向，但能大大减小噪声。不减时所有得分都是正的，每支镖都在把 μ 往自己那边拉，方向只能靠"谁拉得更用力"来分辨。用学出来的价值函数 V(s) 当基线，叫带基线的 REINFORCE；再进一步，用 r + γV(s′) − V(s) 代替整段回报（自举），就是 actor-critic（演员-评论家）方法：演员是策略，评论家是 V。Subtracting a baseline does not change the expected direction of the gradient, but it cuts the noise dramatically. Without it every score is positive, every dart pulls μ towards itself, and the direction can only be told from which darts pull harder. Using a learned value function V(s) as the baseline is called REINFORCE with a baseline; going one step further and replacing the whole return with r + γV(s′) − V(s) (bootstrapping) gives actor-critic methods: the actor is the policy and the critic is V.

今天常用的深度强化学习算法都在这两条主线上。PPO（Schulman 等 2017）限制每次更新别把策略改得太多，稳定、好调，是在仿真里大规模训练四足和人形机器人的首选。SAC（Haarnoja 等 2018）能反复利用旧数据，还加了熵奖励鼓励探索，样本效率高，更适合真机。想系统入门，推荐 Sutton 和 Barto 的教材，作者免费公开。The deep reinforcement learning algorithms in common use today all sit on these two threads. PPO (Schulman et al. 2017) keeps each update from changing the policy too much; it is stable and easy to tune, and it is the default for training quadrupeds and humanoids at scale in simulation. SAC (Haarnoja et al. 2018) can reuse old data again and again and adds an entropy bonus to encourage exploration; it is sample-efficient and better suited to real robots. For a systematic introduction, the textbook by Sutton and Barto is free online from the authors.

第 5 步 · 奖励与探索Step 5 · Rewards and exploration

你奖励什么，就得到什么You get what you reward

强化学习只认奖励。奖励写得不对，策略会精确地去优化那个错误的目标，这叫奖励钻空子（reward hacking），也叫规格博弈（specification gaming）。两个经典例子：一个学骑自行车的智能体，奖励是"朝终点前进"，结果它学会了在起点附近绕半径 20 到 50 米的圈，因为每一圈里总有一段是朝着终点的，背离终点却不扣分（Randløv 和 Alstrøm 1998）；OpenAI 的赛艇游戏智能体发现，在一小片水域里兜圈子、反复撞几个会重新出现的加分道具，比跑完全程得分更高（Faulty reward functions）。Reinforcement learning cares only about the reward. Write the reward wrong and the policy will optimize that wrong objective with great precision; this is called reward hacking, or specification gaming. Two classic examples: an agent learning to ride a bicycle was rewarded for “progress towards the goal” and learned to ride in circles with a radius of 20 to 50 metres around the start, because every circle includes a stretch heading towards the goal while heading away was never penalized (Randløv and Alstrøm 1998); and OpenAI’s boat-racing agent found that circling in a small lagoon, repeatedly hitting a few bonus targets that kept reappearing, scored more than finishing the race (Faulty reward functions).

只奖励"势能涨了多少"：往前走势能涨，得正分；退回去势能跌，把刚拿到的扣回去。把一整局的塑形奖励打折后加起来，中间的项两两抵消，只剩 γTΦ(终点) − Φ(起点)。约定终点的势能为 0（一局无限长时 γT 也趋于 0），总账就是 −Φ(起点)：不管中间怎么走都一样，所以兜圈子刷不到分。Ng、Harada、Russell（1999）证明：塑形奖励写成这种形式，最优策略和不加塑形时完全一样；Φ 选得好，每一步都有反馈，通常能学得更快。反过来，不是这种形式的塑形，都有可能改变最优策略。Only reward how much the potential went up: stepping forward raises it and earns a positive amount; stepping back lowers it and takes back what you just earned. Add up an episode’s discounted shaping rewards and the terms in the middle cancel in pairs, leaving only γTΦ(end) − Φ(start). With the potential at the end set to 0 (and in an endless episode γT goes to 0 anyway), the total is just −Φ(start): the same whatever route you take, so circling earns nothing. Ng, Harada and Russell (1999) proved that shaping of this form leaves the optimal policy exactly as it was without shaping; with a well-chosen Φ there is feedback at every step, which usually speeds up learning. Conversely, any shaping not of this form can change the optimal policy.

Φ = −到门的格数，γ = 0.99，起点离门 9 格。直奔门口：塑形奖励加起来是 0.999 × 0 − (−9) = 9。在门前来回踱步一辈子：加起来也趋近于 0 − (−9) = 9。两种走法的塑形总账一样，谁更好还是由原来的奖励（门口的 +10）决定。而"靠近一步 +1"的写法，来回踱步每两步就有 +1，一辈子的总账约 1 ∕ (1 − γ²) ≈ 50，远远超过进门那一次 +10。Φ = −(cells to the door), γ = 0.99, and the start is 9 cells from the door. Going straight in: the shaping rewards add up to 0.999 × 0 − (−9) = 9. Pacing in front of the door forever: they add up to a limit of 0 − (−9) = 9 as well. Both ways of walking get the same shaping total, so which is better is still decided by the original reward (the +10 at the door). With “+1 per step closer”, pacing earns +1 every two steps, and over a lifetime that totals about 1 ∕ (1 − γ²) ≈ 50, far more than the single +10 for going in.

出问题的不是智能体，而是奖励：它完美地优化了你写下的东西。优化得越狠（γ 越大、训练越久、模型越强），漏洞越容易被找到。写奖励时，要设想"一个只想刷分的对手会怎么做"。The fault lies not with the agent but with the reward: it optimized exactly what you wrote. The harder you optimize (larger γ, longer training, stronger models), the more likely a loophole gets found. When writing a reward, imagine what an opponent who only wants points would do.

奖励太稀疏：探索不到Rewards too sparse: nothing to explore towards

另一个极端：只在成功时给 1，其余全是 0。如果随机尝试几乎不可能碰巧成功（把钥匙插进锁孔、把一堆积木搭成塔），智能体就永远收不到信号，什么也学不会。常见的办法：The other extreme: give 1 only on success and 0 everywhere else. If random attempts almost never succeed by chance (putting a key into a lock, stacking a pile of blocks into a tower), the agent never receives a signal and learns nothing. Common remedies:

  • 塑形：就是上面讲的，记得写成势能的形式。Shaping: as above, remembering to write it in potential form.
  • 示范：用示范把策略带到成功附近，再开始试（第 8 步）。Demonstrations: use them to bring the policy near success before trial and error begins (step 8).
  • 事后重标记：没把物块推到目标 A，但推到了 B，那就假装目标本来就是 B。这次失败对"推到 B"来说就是一次成功的经验。这就是 HER（Hindsight Experience Replay，Andrychowicz 等 2017）。Hindsight relabelling: you failed to push the block to goal A but pushed it to B, so pretend B was the goal all along. That failure becomes a successful experience for “push it to B”. This is HER (Hindsight Experience Replay, Andrychowicz et al. 2017).
  • 好奇心：把"自己预测得有多不准"当作额外奖励，鼓励去没见过的地方（Pathak 等 2017）。Curiosity: use how badly you predicted something as an extra reward, which encourages visiting unfamiliar places (Pathak et al. 2017).
  • 课程：先练简单版本，再逐步加难。Curricula: practise an easy version first, then make it harder step by step.
  • 让大语言模型写奖励：Eureka（Ma 等 2023）让 GPT-4 写、改奖励函数的代码，在仿真里教会了五指灵巧手转笔。Have a large language model write the reward: Eureka (Ma et al. 2023) had GPT-4 write and revise reward-function code, and taught a five-fingered dexterous hand to spin a pen in simulation.

第 6 步 · 仿真到现实Step 6 · Sim to real

在仿真里试一亿次，再搬到真机上A hundred million tries in simulation, then onto the real robot

强化学习要海量的试错。真机器人一秒钟只能走那么几步，摔了会坏，还得有人在旁边扶；仿真器可以几千个机器人同时跑，还比真实时间快得多。Rudin 等（2021）在一块 GPU 上同时仿真几千只四足机器人，平地行走的策略不到 4 分钟就训练好了，崎岖地形也只要 20 分钟。Reinforcement learning needs enormous amounts of trial and error. A real robot can only take so many steps per second, breaks when it falls and needs someone standing by; a simulator can run thousands of robots at once, much faster than real time. Rudin et al. (2021) simulated thousands of quadrupeds in parallel on a single GPU: a policy for flat ground trained in under 4 minutes, and one for rough terrain in 20.

问题是仿真和现实总有差距（reality gap）：摩擦系数、质量、电机的延迟和力矩极限、相机的光照和噪声，都不可能和真实世界一模一样。在仿真里练到"完美"的策略，可能恰恰利用了仿真器某个不真实的地方，一到真机上就摔。The catch is that simulation and reality always differ (the reality gap): friction, masses, motor delays and torque limits, camera lighting and noise can never match the real world exactly. A policy that is “perfect” in simulation may be exploiting some unrealistic quirk of the simulator, and fall over the moment it runs on the real robot.

域随机化：让现实变成"又一个仿真"Domain randomization: make reality “just another simulation”

与其把仿真做得完全准确（做不到），不如每一局都随机改一改仿真的参数：摩擦、质量、延迟、颜色、光照……策略被迫学会在所有这些情况下都能用。只要真实世界落在随机的范围里，在策略眼里它就只是又一种情况。Tobin 等（2017）随机化渲染的颜色和纹理，只用仿真图片训练的物体检测器，在真实图像上的定位误差在 1.5 厘米以内；Peng 等（2018）随机化动力学参数，只在仿真里训练的推物体策略直接搬上了真机械臂；OpenAI（2019）用自动扩大范围的随机化（ADR）训练机械手单手还原魔方，拧哪一面由现成的求解器算好，学的是怎么用手把魔方转对。Rather than making the simulator perfectly accurate (impossible), randomize its parameters every episode: friction, mass, delay, colours, lighting… The policy is forced to work under all of them. As long as the real world falls within the randomized range, to the policy it is just one more case. Tobin et al. (2017) randomized rendered colours and textures, and an object detector trained only on simulated images located objects in real images to within 1.5 cm; Peng et al. (2018) randomized dynamics parameters, and a pushing policy trained only in simulation went straight onto a real robot arm; OpenAI (2019) used automatically widening randomization (ADR) to train a robot hand to solve a Rubik’s cube one-handed, with an off-the-shelf solver choosing which face to turn and the learned policy doing the turning.

先玩：冰壶。真实冰面有多涩，策略事先不知道。先看"只在一个仿真里练"在各种冰面上的表现，再换成"每局随机换摩擦来练"，调一调随机化范围，最后把"真实冰面"拖到范围外面。Play first: curling. The policy does not know in advance how sticky the real ice is. First see how “Train in one simulator” does on different ice, then switch to “Randomize friction every episode”, adjust the randomization range, and finally drag the “real ice” outside the range.

随机化管用有两种方式。一种是鲁棒：学到的做法在整个随机范围里都过得去，Tobin 的检测器就是这样，它只看一张图，没法边做边调。另一种是适应：有记忆的策略能"边做边看"，从刚才的反应推断出眼下的条件，再做调整。冰壶例子里，看冰壶减速得快不快、再决定刷不刷冰，就是这一种；只有训练时见过各种各样的冰面，这种调整才派得上用场，策略也才学得会。真实的机器人策略常常把最近一段时间的观测和动作一起输入，从中隐式地推断摩擦、负载、地形这些看不见的参数。Randomization works in two ways. One is robustness: what the policy learns works acceptably across the whole randomized range. Tobin’s detector is like this; it looks at a single image and cannot adjust as it goes. The other is adaptation: a policy with memory can “watch as it goes”, infer the current conditions from how things just responded, and adjust. In the curling example, watching how quickly the stone slows and then deciding whether to sweep is this second kind; only if training has shown it all kinds of ice does such adjusting pay off, and only then can the policy learn it. Real robot policies often take the recent history of observations and actions as input and infer hidden parameters such as friction, payload and terrain from it implicitly.

老师-学生：先作弊，再模仿Teacher and student: cheat first, then imitate

仿真里什么都知道：地形的高度、摩擦系数、物体的精确位置。先用强化学习训练一个能看到这些特权信息的"老师"策略，信息全，所以好学；再让一个只用真机传感器的"学生"策略去模仿老师（行为克隆或 DAgger），学生必须从自己的传感器历史里推断出那些看不到的信息。Lee 等（2020）这样训练的 ANYmal 四足机器人，在泥地、雪地、碎石、密草和流水里都能走，这些情况训练时一样都没见过。这是光谱两端的组合：老师靠试错学，学生靠示范学。自动驾驶里的 Learning by Cheating（Chen 等 2019）也是先让能看到特权信息的老师学会，再让只看相机的学生模仿它，只是那里的老师本身也是模仿人类驾驶学出来的。In simulation everything is known: the terrain height, the friction, the exact positions of objects. First use reinforcement learning to train a “teacher” policy that can see this privileged information; with the full picture it is easy to learn. Then train a “student” policy that only uses the real robot’s sensors to imitate the teacher (by behaviour cloning or DAgger); the student has to infer the hidden information from its own sensor history. The ANYmal quadruped of Lee et al. (2020), trained this way, walked through mud, snow, rubble, thick vegetation and running water, none of which it had seen in training. It combines both ends of the spectrum: the teacher learns by trial and error, the student learns from demonstrations. Learning by Cheating (Chen et al. 2019) in autonomous driving also first trains a teacher that sees privileged information and then has a camera-only student imitate it, except that there the teacher itself learned by imitating human driving.

好在哪What it is good at

  • 试错便宜、安全，可以大规模并行Trial and error is cheap and safe, and can run massively in parallel
  • 仿真里什么都测得到，可以用特权信息，可以随时重置Everything is measurable in simulation, so privileged information and instant resets are available
  • 腿足运动、灵巧操作这类很难示范的技能，主要靠它取得了进展Skills that are hard to demonstrate, such as legged locomotion and dexterous manipulation, have advanced mainly this way

坑在哪Where it breaks

  • 现实差距永远存在，策略可能钻仿真器的空子The reality gap never disappears, and the policy may exploit flaws in the simulator
  • 仿真器要人搭：接触、摩擦和软的东西（布、绳子、液体）都很难仿准Someone has to build the simulator: contact, friction and soft things (cloth, rope, liquids) are all hard to simulate accurately
  • 随机化的范围要调：太窄搬不过去，太宽学不会或者变得过于保守The randomization range needs tuning: too narrow and it does not transfer, too wide and it fails to learn or becomes overly cautious

第 7 步 · 自监督与世界模型Step 7 · Self-supervision and world models

没有老师：先学"世界怎么运转"No teacher: first learn how the world works

光谱最右边，既没有示范，也没有奖励，只有机器人自己动来动去收集的 (s, a, s′)。这种数据的标签是自动产生的："下一刻发生了什么"本身就是答案，所以叫自监督。从这样的数据里能学到几样东西：At the far right of the spectrum there are neither demonstrations nor rewards, only the (s, a, s′) the robot collects by moving around on its own. The labels in such data come for free: “what happened next” is itself the answer, which is why this is called self-supervised. Several things can be learned from such data:

  • 前向模型，也就是世界模型：给定 s 和 a，预测 s′。A forward model, that is, a world model: given s and a, predict s′.
  • 逆向模型：给定 s 和 s′，猜中间的 a。第 3 步给视频补动作用的就是它。Agrawal 等（2016）让机器人自己戳了大量物体，学会了"想把物体推到那里，该怎么戳"。An inverse model: given s and s′, guess the a in between. This is what step 3 uses to fill in actions for videos. Agrawal et al. (2016) had a robot poke lots of objects on its own and learn “how to poke to move an object over there”.
  • 自动判断成败：比如抓完提起来以后，夹爪没有完全合拢，说明手里有东西。Pinto 和 Gupta（2016）让机器人自己试抓了 5 万次、用了 700 小时，标签全部自动产生；Levine 等（2016）用 6 到 14 台机械臂，两个月里收集了 80 多万次抓取。Judging success automatically: for example, if the gripper has not fully closed after lifting, something is in its hand. Pinto and Gupta (2016) had a robot attempt 50,000 grasps by itself over 700 hours, with every label generated automatically; Levine et al. (2016) collected more than 800,000 grasp attempts over two months with 6 to 14 robot arms.

学会了"推一下会怎样"，就可以先在脑子里试：想象几十种动作序列，挑预测结果最好的那个，只执行第一步，再根据新的观测重新想，这叫模型预测控制（MPC）。也可以在模型里"做梦"：用想象出来的经历训练强化学习策略，真机只需要很少的交互。Once you have learned what a push does, you can try things in your head first: imagine dozens of action sequences, pick the one whose predicted outcome is best, execute only its first step, then think again from the new observation. This is called model predictive control (MPC). Or you can “dream” inside the model: train a reinforcement learning policy on imagined experience, so the real robot needs very little interaction.

推一个方块：模型学到"推 1 秒，方块走 2 厘米"。目标在 10 厘米外，规划器就想出"推 5 秒"。但如果桌子边缘附近的数据从来没收集过，模型不知道方块会掉下去，规划器可能会很自信地把它推下桌。Pushing a block: the model has learned “push for 1 second and the block moves 2 cm”. The goal is 10 cm away, so the planner comes up with “push for 5 seconds”. But if no data were ever collected near the edge of the table, the model does not know the block can fall off, and the planner may confidently push it off the table.

规划器会专门找模型的漏洞：模型在没见过的区域里预测得很离谱，规划器偏偏会挑那些"模型以为特别好"的动作。所以要限制规划别离开数据覆盖的范围，或者一边用一边收集新数据修正模型。A planner hunts for the model’s flaws: where the model has seen no data its predictions can be wildly wrong, and the planner will pick exactly the actions the model wrongly thinks are excellent. So planning is kept within the range the data cover, or new data keep being collected to correct the model as it is used.

这条路上的代表作：World Models（Ha 和 Schmidhuber 2018）让智能体完全在自己学到的"梦境"里练习躲火球，再搬回真实的游戏里；Dreamer（Hafner 等 2019）在压缩后的隐空间里想象未来，用来训练策略；DayDreamer（Wu 等 2022）把它用到真机上，四足机器人不用仿真器、从零开始，大约一小时学会了从仰躺翻身、站起来和走路；V-JEPA 2（Meta 2025）先在一百多万小时的网络视频上自监督预训练，再用不到 62 小时的机器人视频学一个"知道动作会带来什么"的世界模型，在两个新的实验室里不用再收集数据，靠规划完成了抓取和摆放。Landmarks on this road: World Models (Ha and Schmidhuber 2018) trained an agent to dodge fireballs entirely inside its own learned “dream” and then moved it back into the real game; Dreamer (Hafner et al. 2019) imagines the future in a compressed latent space to train its policy; DayDreamer (Wu et al. 2022) took it to real hardware, where a quadruped with no simulator learned from scratch, in about an hour, to roll off its back, stand up and walk; V-JEPA 2 (Meta 2025) was pretrained self-supervised on over a million hours of internet video, then learned an action-conditioned world model from less than 62 hours of robot video, and picked and placed objects by planning in two new labs without collecting any data there.

玩耍数据：不预设任务Play data: no task set in advance

还有一种折中：让人随便"玩"机器人，遥操作着开抽屉、推方块、按按钮，没有规定的任务；再把录下来的轨迹里任意两个时刻配成"从这里出发，目标是那里"的训练样本。到达过的任何状态，都可以看作一个已经达成的目标，这和 HER 是同一个想法。Lynch 等（2019）这样用一份玩耍数据学会了很多任务。A middle way: let people “play” with the robot, teleoperating it to open drawers, push blocks and press buttons with no assigned task, then turn any two moments of the recorded trajectories into a training example of “start here, the goal is there”. Any state that was reached counts as a goal that was achieved, the same idea as HER. Lynch et al. (2019) learned many tasks from one set of play data this way.

好在哪What it is good at

  • 数据便宜，标签自动产生，可以大规模收集The data are cheap and the labels automatic, so collection scales
  • 学到的是通用的"物理常识"，换任务不用从头学What is learned is general “physical common sense”, so a new task does not mean starting from scratch
  • 有了模型就能规划，样本效率通常比无模型的强化学习高得多，不过模型的误差可能限制最终能达到的水平A model enables planning, which is usually far more sample-efficient than model-free reinforcement learning, although model errors can limit how good the final result gets

坑在哪Where it breaks

  • 本身不知道该做什么，最后还是要给目标或奖励It does not know what to do by itself; a goal or reward is still needed in the end
  • 模型误差会随着想象的步数累积，还会被规划器钻空子Model errors accumulate over imagined steps and get exploited by the planner
  • 直接预测像素又难又浪费，现在多在压缩后的隐空间里预测Predicting pixels directly is hard and wasteful, so prediction now mostly happens in a compressed latent space
  • 随便乱动收集的数据，往往覆盖不到精细操作需要的那些状态Data from random wandering often miss the states that fine manipulation needs

第 8 步 · 组合起来用Step 8 · Combining them

真实系统：几站一起上Real systems: several stops at once

光谱上的每一站都有明显的短板，所以真正好用的系统几乎都是组合。最常见的套路是：先用便宜、信息丰富的信号把策略带到"差不多会"的地方，再用更贵但更直接的信号去打磨。Every stop on the spectrum has obvious weaknesses, so systems that work well are almost always combinations. The most common pattern: use a cheap, information-rich signal to bring the policy to “roughly able to do it”, then polish it with a more expensive but more direct signal.

先模仿，再强化Imitate first, then reinforce

示范解决了强化学习最头疼的探索问题：策略一开始就在成功附近，不用指望瞎碰。强化学习反过来解决了行为克隆的两个问题：上限是老师，以及分布偏移（它会在自己的状态分布上练）。DAPG（Rajeswaran 等 2017）用少量人类示范加上强化学习，让 24 自由度的五指手学会了开门、用锤子等任务，需要的经验只相当于几个小时的机器人时间。AlphaGo（2016）也是先用人类棋谱做行为克隆，再自我对弈。Demonstrations solve reinforcement learning’s biggest headache, exploration: the policy starts close to success instead of hoping to stumble on it. Reinforcement learning in turn fixes two problems of behaviour cloning: the teacher as a ceiling, and distribution shift (it practises on its own state distribution). DAPG (Rajeswaran et al. 2017) combined a small number of human demonstrations with reinforcement learning to teach a 24-degree-of-freedom hand tasks such as opening a door and using a hammer, with experience equivalent to only a few hours of robot time. AlphaGo (2016) likewise started with behaviour cloning on human games and then played itself.

离线强化学习：只用旧数据，但想比数据做得好Offline RL: only old data, but aiming to beat it

有时手里只有一大堆旧日志，好的坏的都有，还带着奖励，却不能再让机器人去试。行为克隆只会学到这些数据的"平均水平"；离线强化学习用上奖励，有可能把不同轨迹里的好片段拼起来，得到比任何一条都好的策略。难点又是分布偏移：Q 函数对数据里没出现过的动作只能瞎猜，猜错有高有低，可策略取最大值时偏偏挑中被高估的那些，自举（用 Q 去估 Q）又把高估一路传开。CQL（Kumar 等 2020）的办法是故意压低没见过的动作的 Q 值，宁可保守。入门可以看 Levine 等（2020）的教程综述。Sometimes all you have is a big pile of old logs, good and bad mixed, with rewards attached, and the robot cannot try anything new. Behaviour cloning would only learn the data’s “average level”; offline reinforcement learning uses the rewards and may stitch good pieces from different trajectories into a policy better than any one of them. The difficulty is, once again, distribution shift: for actions that never appear in the data the Q function can only guess, and its errors go both ways, but taking the maximum picks out exactly the overestimated ones, and bootstrapping (estimating Q from Q) spreads the overestimate further. CQL (Kumar et al. 2020) deliberately pushes down the Q values of unseen actions, preferring to be conservative. A good starting point is the tutorial and review by Levine et al. (2020).

网络数据加机器人示范：视觉-语言-动作模型Web data plus robot demonstrations: vision-language-action models

把一个在网络图文数据上预训练好的视觉-语言模型，接着用大量机器人示范微调，让它直接输出动作，这类模型叫 VLA（vision-language-action）。网络数据（光谱最右边）提供常识和语义，机器人示范（最左边）提供动作。RT-2（2023）把动作写成文字 token，和语言一起训练；Open X-Embodiment 汇集了 21 家机构、22 种机器人的一百多万条轨迹；OpenVLA（2024）是一个开源的 70 亿参数模型；π0（2024）用流匹配生成连续的动作块，能叠衣服、收拾桌子。π*0.6（2025）又在 VLA 上加入了强化学习，从机器人自己的尝试和人的纠正里继续提高，绕了一圈又回到光谱中间。Take a vision-language model pretrained on web images and text, fine-tune it on lots of robot demonstrations, and have it output actions directly: such models are called VLAs (vision-language-action). The web data (far right of the spectrum) supply common sense and semantics; the robot demonstrations (far left) supply the actions. RT-2 (2023) writes actions as text tokens and trains them together with language; Open X-Embodiment pooled more than a million trajectories from 22 kinds of robots at 21 institutions; OpenVLA (2024) is an open 7-billion-parameter model; π0 (2024) generates chunks of continuous actions with flow matching and can fold laundry and clear tables. π*0.6 (2025) added reinforcement learning on top of a VLA, improving from the robot’s own attempts and from human corrections, coming full circle back to the middle of the spectrum.

先玩：点几个真实系统，看它们各用了光谱上的哪几站、按什么顺序，以及为什么这样组合。Play first: pick a few real systems and see which stops on the spectrum each one uses, in what order, and why.

    规律是：信号从哪来，取决于什么便宜、什么可靠。能示范、难写奖励（叠衣服），就从示范出发；不能示范，但有好的仿真和清楚的奖励（腿足运动、下棋），就以试错为主；两样都缺，但数据海量（网络视频和文本），就先自监督学通用的知识，再用少量示范或奖励把它引到具体任务上。The pattern: where the signal comes from depends on what is cheap and what is reliable. If a task is easy to demonstrate but hard to reward (folding clothes), start from demonstrations; if it cannot be demonstrated but there is a good simulator and a clear reward (legged locomotion, board games), rely mainly on trial and error; if both are missing but data are plentiful (web video and text), first learn general knowledge by self-supervision, then steer it to a specific task with a few demonstrations or a reward.

    几条经验A few rules of thumb

    • 先问信号从哪来。有示范就先用示范；能写出奖励、又有仿真，就用强化学习；两样都有，就组合起来。Ask first where the signal comes from. If there are demonstrations, start with them; if you can write a reward and have a simulator, use reinforcement learning; if you have both, combine them.
    • 模仿学习先查两件事：分布偏移（加 DAgger、加噪声示范、加纠偏数据）和多模态（用能表示分布的策略，用动作块）。For imitation learning, check two things first: distribution shift (add DAgger, noisy demonstrations or recovery data) and multimodality (use a policy that can represent a distribution, use action chunks).
    • 强化学习先查两件事：奖励有没有漏洞（想象一个只想刷分的对手），探索够不够（奖励是不是太稀疏）。For reinforcement learning, check two things first: whether the reward has loopholes (imagine an opponent who only wants points) and whether exploration is sufficient (is the reward too sparse?).
    • 训练指标好，不等于真机上好。行为克隆的训练损失、强化学习在仿真里的回报，都可能骗人，最终要看真机上的表现。Good training numbers do not mean a good real robot. A behaviour-cloning training loss and a return in simulation can both mislead; in the end it is the real robot’s performance that counts.
    • 先试最简单的基线。很多操作任务里，几十条高质量示范，加上好的动作表示（扩散模型、动作块），就已经够用了。Try the simplest baseline first. For many manipulation tasks, a few dozen high-quality demonstrations plus a good action representation (diffusion models, action chunks) are already enough.

    检查一下Check yourself

    行为克隆在训练集上误差很小，为什么一跑就出问题？Behaviour cloning has a tiny error on the training set. Why does it go wrong as soon as it runs?

    训练用的状态来自专家，执行时的状态来自学生自己。学生一偏离，就到了训练数据没有覆盖的地方，误差越积越大，这就是分布偏移。DAgger 让专家在学生实际到过的状态上补标签，直接修正这一点。The training states come from the expert, but at run time the states come from the student. Once the student deviates it is somewhere the training data do not cover, and errors pile up: that is distribution shift. DAgger has the expert label the states the student actually visits, which fixes exactly this.

    "离目标越近，奖励越多"这种写法有什么风险？怎么改？What is risky about “the closer to the goal, the more reward”? How would you fix it?

    如果只奖励靠近、不惩罚远离，来回走就能反复刷分，γ 越大越容易被发现。改成势能塑形 F = γΦ(s′) − Φ(s)，并让终点的势能为 0：一整局的塑形奖励总账就是 −Φ(起点)，和中间怎么走无关，最优策略不会变。If you reward approaching without penalizing retreating, going back and forth farms points over and over, and the larger γ is, the more likely this is discovered. Use potential-based shaping F = γΦ(s′) − Φ(s) instead, with the potential at the goal set to 0: an episode’s total shaping reward is then just −Φ(start), whatever the route in between, and the optimal policy does not change.

    为什么 AlphaGo Zero 不用人类棋谱，反而比 AlphaGo 更强？Why is AlphaGo Zero, which uses no human games, stronger than AlphaGo?

    围棋有完美的仿真器（规则）和清楚的奖励（输赢），自我对弈的数据要多少有多少。条件这么好时，强化学习不再需要示范来帮忙探索，从零开始的自我对弈也不受人类下法的局限。机器人很少有这样的条件，所以通常还是要示范帮忙。Go has a perfect simulator (the rules) and a clear reward (win or lose), and self-play supplies as much data as you want. Under such conditions reinforcement learning no longer needs demonstrations to help it explore, and self-play from scratch is not limited by the way humans play. Robots rarely enjoy such conditions, which is why they usually still need demonstrations.

    离线强化学习和行为克隆都只用现成的数据，区别在哪？Offline RL and behaviour cloning both use only existing data. What is the difference?

    行为克隆不看奖励，学的是数据里的平均行为，上限就是数据。离线强化学习用上了奖励，有可能挑出、拼接数据里的好片段，学到比数据里任何一条都好的策略；代价是要小心它高估没见过的动作。Behaviour cloning ignores rewards and learns the average behaviour in the data, so the data are its ceiling. Offline RL uses the rewards and may pick out and stitch together the good pieces of the data, learning a policy better than any single trajectory in it; the price is guarding against its overestimates for unseen actions.

    一半示范从左边绕过障碍、一半从右边。用均方误差训练会怎样？Half the demonstrations pass an obstacle on the left, half on the right. What happens if you train with a squared error?

    策略会输出两种做法的平均，也就是直直撞上障碍。要用能表示多峰分布的策略（混合模型、离散 token、扩散或流匹配），最好一次生成一整段动作，免得每一步都在两种做法之间摇摆。The policy outputs the average of the two, which drives straight into the obstacle. Use a policy that can represent a multi-peaked distribution (mixture models, discrete tokens, diffusion or flow matching), ideally generating a whole chunk of actions at a time, so it does not waver between the two options at every step.
