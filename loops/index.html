环环相扣Loops Within Loops

机械臂、四足机器人、自动驾驶汽车、无人机、扫地机器人，长得天差地别，里面却是同一副骨架：测量世界，弄清现状，决定做什么，让身体动起来，然后再测量。这条链不是一个环，而是一层套一层的好几个环：最里面的每秒跑几万次，最外面的几分钟才动一次。这一篇用一个个能推、能拔线、能调慢的小模拟，把这些层和环拆开来看：一个环怎么闭上，它要跑多快，环为什么要套着环，最里面的电流怎么控制，一台机器人从任务到电机每层传什么，五种机器人怎么装进同一副骨架，硬件之间又怎么连。Robot arms, legged robots, self-driving cars, drones and robot vacuums look nothing alike, yet inside they share one skeleton: measure the world, work out what is going on, decide what to do, move the body, then measure again. That chain is not one loop but several, nested one inside another: the innermost runs tens of thousands of times a second, the outermost moves once every few minutes. This piece takes the layers and loops apart with small simulations you can push, unplug and slow down: how a loop closes, how fast it has to run, why loops sit inside loops, how the current at the very bottom is controlled, what each layer of a robot hands to the next from task to motor, how five kinds of robot fit the same skeleton, and how the hardware is wired together.

第 0 步 · 总览Step 0 · Overview

从"去扫厨房"到轮子转起来From “clean the kitchen” to turning wheels

你在手机上点一下"扫厨房"。十几秒后，扫地机器人离开充电座，绕过扶手椅，又绕过路上的一只袜子，开进厨房，一条一条地来回扫。从一句话到轮子转起来，机器人里的程序分成六道工序，像一条流水线：每道工序吃进上一道交来的东西，加工一下，再交给下一道。You tap “clean the kitchen” on your phone. A dozen seconds later the robot vacuum leaves its dock, goes round the armchair, then round a sock lying in its way, drives into the kitchen and sweeps it in back-and-forth passes. Between that one sentence and the wheels turning, the robot’s software works in six stages, like an assembly line: each stage takes in what the one before handed over, works on it, and passes the result to the next.

传感Sensing测到了什么？What has been measured?

把光、距离、转动、受力、电流这些物理量变成数字，每个数字都记下测量的时刻。Turns physical quantities such as light, distance, rotation, force and current into numbers, each stamped with the moment it was measured.

常用：相机、激光雷达、IMU（测转动和加速度）、关节编码器、力传感器、电流传感器Typical: cameras, lidar, IMU (measures rotation and acceleration), joint encoders, force sensors, current sensors

↓ 原始数据：一帧帧图像、一圈圈激光测距、关节角度、加速度、电流↓ Raw data: camera frames, lidar sweeps of distances, joint angles, accelerations, currents

感知与估计Perception and estimation这些数字说明了什么？What do these numbers mean?

把一堆数字变成对世界的描述：我在哪、朝哪、在怎么动，周围有什么、它们在怎么动。Turns a heap of numbers into a description of the world: where am I, which way am I facing, how am I moving, what is around me, and how is it moving.

常用：卡尔曼滤波（按可信程度把预测和测量合起来）、SLAM（边建图边定位）、目标检测（上一篇的卷积网络和 Transformer 主要用在这里）Typical: Kalman filters (which blend predictions and measurements by how far each can be trusted), SLAM (mapping while localising), object detection (where the convolutional networks and Transformers of the previous piece mostly live)

↓ 世界状态：我的位置和速度、地图、物体列表（是什么、在哪、多快）＋ 人给的任务↓ World state: my position and velocity, the map, a list of objects (what, where, how fast)+ the task from a person

决策Decision现在该做什么？What should I do now?

结合任务和现状，选出眼下的目标或模式。大部分时候不变，到了关键时刻才切换。Combines the task and the current situation to choose the goal or mode for now. Most of the time it stays the same, switching only at key moments.

常用：有限状态机（几种模式加上切换条件）、行为树（把任务拆成一棵按顺序和条件执行的树）、任务规划Typical: finite state machines (a few modes plus the conditions for switching between them), behaviour trees (a task broken into a tree that runs in order and on conditions), task planning

↓ 目标：去哪个点、抓哪个东西、用哪种模式↓ Goal: which point to go to, which object to pick up, which mode to use

规划Planning怎么做到？How do I get it done?

算出一条路线，或者一段带时间的轨迹：第几秒该在哪、什么姿态，同时绕开障碍。Works out a route, or a timed trajectory (where to be, and in what pose, at each moment) that also avoids obstacles.

常用：A* 这类路径搜索、轨迹优化、MPC（往前预测一小段，算出最好的一串动作，只执行第一步）Typical: path search such as A*, trajectory optimisation, MPC (it predicts a short way ahead, works out the best sequence of actions and carries out only the first)

↓ 计划：一串路点，或者每个时刻的目标位置、速度、姿态↓ Plan: a string of waypoints, or target positions, velocities and poses for every moment

控制Control此刻用多大的劲？How hard should I push right now?

每秒几十到几千次，比较"计划要的"和"实际测到的"，算出给执行器的命令。Tens to thousands of times a second, compares what the plan wants with what is actually measured and works out the commands for the actuators.

常用：PID（第 1 步）、全身控制（把身体要的动作分配成每个关节的力矩）、学到的策略Typical: PID (step 1), whole-body control (shares out the motion the body needs as torques for each joint), learned policies

↓ 命令：轮速、关节力矩、推力、转向角↓ Commands: wheel speeds, joint torques, thrusts, steering angles

执行Actuation怎么把命令变成力？How do commands become force?

驱动器调节电机里的电流，电流产生力矩，力矩让身体动起来。The drives regulate the current in the motors; current makes torque, and torque moves the body.

常用：驱动器、电机、减速器（第 4 步）Typical: drives, motors, gearboxes (step 4)

↓ 力和运动↓ Force and motion

物理世界：机器人自己的身体，和它周围的一切The physical world: the robot’s own body, and everything around it

左边的灰线：执行改变了世界，传感器再测到这个变化，环就闭上了。右边的青线：感知的结果也直接交给规划和控制，它们不用等决策层。The grey line on the left: actuation changes the world, the sensors measure the change, and the loop is closed. The teal line on the right: perception’s results also go straight to planning and control, which do not wait for the decision layer.

读这张图，记住三件事：To read this chart, keep three things in mind:

  • 方框是工序，箭头是交接的东西。每一层只认上一层交来的东西，不管它是怎么算出来的。换一个更好的激光雷达，只要感知层交出的"世界状态"格式不变，下面几层一行代码都不用改。Boxes are stages; arrows are what gets handed over. Each layer only relies on what the layer above hands it, not on how it was worked out. Swap in a better lidar, and as long as perception hands on its “world state” in the same format, not a line of code below needs to change.
  • 左边那条线让它成了环。执行改变了世界，传感器再测到变化，下一轮算出来的就不一样了。这就是第 1 步要讲的"闭环"。The line on the left makes it a loop. Actuation changes the world, the sensors measure the change, and the next round comes out differently. This is the “closed loop” of step 1.
  • 不是每件事都要走完整条链。控制层每秒要比较几十到几千次"计划要的"和"实际测到的"，它直接拿感知的最新结果，不等决策层点头。所以一台机器人里不止一个环：任务的大环里套着规划的环，规划的环里套着控制的环，最里面还有驱动器里的电流环。这就是这一篇的题目"环环相扣"。Not everything has to go down the whole chain. Control compares “what the plan wants” with “what is actually measured” tens to thousands of times a second, so it takes perception’s latest result directly instead of waiting for the decision layer’s nod. That is why a robot has more than one loop: the planning loop sits inside the big task loop, the control loop sits inside the planning loop, and innermost of all is the current loop inside each drive. Hence the title of this piece, “Loops Within Loops”.

五个例子放在一起，能看出三条规律：Put the five examples side by side and three patterns appear:

  • 事情从哪一层进来，就从哪一层往下传。人给的任务从决策层进来；路上多了东西，从传感进来，到规划为止就处理完了，决策层不用管；被推一下、被风吹一下，只在最下面几层里转一圈。Each event comes in at a particular layer and travels down from there. A task from a person enters at the decision layer; something new in the way enters at sensing and is dealt with by the time it reaches planning, without the decision layer getting involved; a push or a gust only goes round the bottom few layers.
  • 决策层大部分时候不动。它像一个开关，只在关键时刻切一下：到了、抓住了、看到人了、电量低了。这些时刻几乎都是由一个测量引发的。Most of the time the decision layer does nothing. Like a switch, it flips only at key moments: arrived, gripped, a person spotted, battery low. Nearly every one of those moments is set off by a measurement.
  • 越要紧的反应，走的路越短。撞到椅子腿，碰撞开关直接让底盘停下；汽车看到行人，整条链走下来要 0.3 秒左右，这段时间里车已经开出去 3 m 多。快的事交给下面，慢的事交给上面。The more urgent the reaction, the shorter its path. Bump into a chair leg and the bumper stops the base directly; for the car to react to the pedestrian, the news has to travel the whole chain, which takes about 0.3 s, by which time the car has covered more than 3 m. Fast things go to the bottom, slow things to the top.

这些层同时在跑：被推了一下的 0.3 秒The layers all run at once: the 0.3 s after a push

上面的流程图容易让人以为各层排着队，一个算完再交给下一个。实际上并不是这样。一台四足机器人站在原地，你从侧面推它一把。它晃了一下，脚挪了半步，站稳了，前后不到半秒。这半秒里，机器人里面发生了什么？The flowchart above can give the impression that the layers wait in line, each finishing before handing over to the next. That is not how it works. A quadruped is standing still and you push it from the side. It sways, shifts a foot half a step and steadies itself, all in under half a second. What went on inside the robot in that half second?

不是一个程序在反应，而是好几层程序同时在跑，各有各的节拍。最里面的电流环每秒跑几万次，你的手刚碰到它，它已经调整了几十次；全身控制每秒 500 次；规划落脚点的 MPC 每秒几十次；相机每秒 30 帧；决定往哪走的导航规划一秒才一次；至于"今天要去哪"的任务层，压根不知道你推过它。MPC 和全身控制的频率取自 MIT 的四足机器人 Mini Cheetah（Kim 等 2019），别的四足机器人量级相近。关节 PD 在下面画成每秒 1 万次，好和电流环分开看；Mini Cheetah 上它和电流环一样，每秒 4 万次。It is not one program reacting but several layers running at the same time, each to its own beat. The current loop at the very bottom runs tens of thousands of times a second, and has adjusted dozens of times before your hand has even settled; whole-body control runs 500 times a second; MPC, which plans the footholds, runs dozens of times a second; the camera delivers 30 frames a second; navigation, which decides where to go, runs once a second; and the task layer, which knows where the robot is supposed to be today, never finds out you pushed it. 第 1 步 · 闭环Step 1 · Closing the loop

一个环怎么闭上How a loop closes

先只看最小的一个环：一个关节，一个电机，手臂末端挂着一个重物。目标是让手臂停在 30°。Start with the smallest loop there is: one joint, one motor, and a weight hanging from the end of the arm. The goal is to hold the arm at 30°.

最直接的想法叫开环（open loop）：事先算好"托住这个重物、停在 30° 要多大的力矩"，让电机一直出这么大的力矩，完事。很多 3D 打印机就是这么干的：步进电机发多少步，就当自己转了多少，从不检查。喷头被卡一下，它会"丢步"，打出来的件从那一层开始整体错位，它自己毫不知情。The most direct idea is called open loop: work out in advance how much torque it takes to hold this weight at 30°, have the motor put out that torque forever, and be done. Many 3D printers work this way: the stepper motor assumes it has turned exactly as many steps as it was sent, and never checks. If the nozzle catches on something, it “skips steps”, and from that layer on the whole print is shifted sideways without the printer knowing.

闭环（closed loop）多做了一件事：一直测量，一直纠正。关节上装一个编码器（测转角的传感器），每个周期读一次角度，和目标比一比，差多少就补多少。电机转了，编码器的读数就变了，下一个周期的误差也跟着变：测量、计算、出力、物理世界变化、再测量，这个圈是穿过物理世界闭上的。最常见的补法是 PD 控制：A closed loop does one more thing: it keeps measuring and keeps correcting. An encoder on the joint (a sensor that measures rotation) reads the angle every cycle, compares it with the target, and makes up whatever is missing. When the motor turns, the encoder reading changes, and so does the error in the next cycle: measure, compute, push, the physical world changes, measure again. The loop closes through the physical world. The most common way to make up the difference is PD control:

τ = Kp (q* − q) − Kd q̇

电机这一刻的力矩，等于"离目标还差多少"乘 Kp，再减去"转得多快"乘 Kd。The motor’s torque right now equals “how far from the target” times Kp, minus “how fast it is turning” times Kd.

开环全凭事先算好，算错了、被推了，它都不知道。闭环把测量接了回来：只要离目标还有差距，就一直有力矩往回拉。只有这一项还不够：手臂被拉回目标时还带着速度，会冲过头、来回晃，所以再减去一项和速度成正比的力矩，提前刹车。Open loop relies entirely on the calculation made in advance: if it was wrong, or the arm gets pushed, it never knows. A closed loop feeds the measurement back in: as long as there is any gap to the target, there is torque pulling the arm back. That term alone is not enough: the arm arrives back at the target still moving, overshoots and swings back and forth, so a second term proportional to the speed is subtracted to brake in advance.

在关节和目标之间装一根看不见的弹簧，再并一个阻尼器，就像门上防止门"砰"地关上的那个缓冲器。弹簧负责往回拉，阻尼器负责别冲过头。Put an invisible spring between the joint and the target, with a damper alongside it, like the closer that stops a door from slamming. The spring pulls the arm back; the damper keeps it from overshooting.

Kp = 50 N·m/rad，关节离目标还差 0.1 rad（约 6°），弹簧给 5 N·m 往目标拉。如果它正以每秒 1 rad 冲向目标，Kd = 4 N·m·s/rad，再减掉 4 N·m，实际只出 1 N·m：提前点刹车。Kp = 50 N·m/rad and the joint is still 0.1 rad (about 6°) from the target: the spring gives 5 N·m toward the target. If the joint is rushing toward the target at 1 rad per second and Kd = 4 N·m·s/rad, another 4 N·m is taken off, leaving just 1 N·m: braking early.

这行公式里没有"重物有多重"：闭环不需要知道负载，只要能测到结果。代价是：要托住重物，弹簧得先被拉开一点才有力气，所以纯 PD 控制托重物时总会下垂一点。下垂多少？下垂角（弧度）≈ 重力力矩 ÷ Kp。There is nothing in this formula about how heavy the weight is: a closed loop does not need to know the load, only to measure the result. The price: to hold the weight up, the spring must first be stretched a little before it has the strength, so plain PD control always sags slightly under a load. By how much? Sag angle (in radians) ≈ gravity torque ÷ Kp.

这个小实验里有闭环最重要的三件事：This small experiment holds the three most important things about closed loops:

  1. 闭环不怕"不知道"：负载变了、被推了，它都能拉回来，因为它看的是结果，不是事先的假设。A closed loop does not mind not knowing: if the load changes or the arm is pushed, it pulls back anyway, because it looks at the result rather than at assumptions made in advance.
  2. 纯反馈有代价：误差先出现，力矩才出现。托 1 kg 的重物停在 30°：手臂自重 0.5 kg，重心在中点 0.2 m 处，重力力矩 ≈ (0.5 × 0.2 + 1 × 0.4) × 9.8 × cos 30° ≈ 4.2 N·m；Kp = 50 时，下垂 ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 ≈ 0.085 rad，约 5°。Kp 调大，下垂变小，可阻尼不变时，刚度越大越容易晃；下一步还会看到另一个限制：环跑得慢、测量来得晚时，增益只能调得更软。另一个常见的办法是再加一项积分（I）：把误差随时间累加起来，只要还下垂，这一项就越攒越大，直到把下垂顶回去。P、I、D 三项合起来，就是最常用的 PID 控制。积分项反应慢，攒过头还会冲过目标，所以能算出来的重力，最好直接算好了给（下一条）。Pure feedback has a price: the error has to appear before the torque does. Holding 1 kg at 30°: the arm itself weighs 0.5 kg with its centre of mass halfway along, at 0.2 m, so the gravity torque ≈ (0.5 × 0.2 + 1 × 0.4) × 9.8 × cos 30° ≈ 4.2 N·m; with Kp = 50 the sag ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 ≈ 0.085 rad, about 5°. Turn Kp up and the sag shrinks, but with the same damping a stiffer spring swings more easily; the next step shows another limit: when the loop runs slowly or its measurements arrive late, the gains have to be softer. Another common fix is to add an integral term (I): it adds up the error over time, so as long as the arm still sags, this term keeps growing until it pushes the sag away. P, I and D together make the most widely used controller, PID. The integral term is slow, and once it has built up too much it can carry the arm past the target, so gravity, which can be calculated, is better worked out and supplied directly (next point).
  3. 已知的交给前馈，意外的交给反馈：重力、惯性这些能算出来的，事先算好直接给；算不准的部分和突发的扰动，交给反馈去补。真实的机械臂控制器几乎都是这么搭的。Hand the known part to feedforward, the surprises to feedback: whatever can be calculated, such as gravity and inertia, is worked out in advance and applied directly; whatever cannot be calculated well, and any sudden disturbance, is left for feedback to make up. Almost every real robot arm controller is built this way.

开环的好处What open loop is good at

  • 简单，不需要传感器Simple, no sensors needed
  • 不会因为反馈而振荡Never oscillates because of feedback
  • 模型准、扰动小的场合够用，比如步进电机、风扇Good enough when the model is accurate and disturbances are small, as in stepper motors and fans

开环的问题Where open loop falls down

  • 模型算错了、负载变了，它都不知道If the model is wrong or the load changes, it never finds out
  • 被推一下就回不去One push and it cannot get back
  • 误差会一点点累积Errors pile up bit by bit

闭环也不是免费的：它需要传感器，而且一旦测量来得太晚、太稀，"纠正"本身就会把系统推向振荡。这就引出了下一个问题：一个环要跑多快才够？Closed loops are not free either: they need sensors, and if the measurements arrive too late or too seldom, the “correction” itself pushes the system into oscillation. Which raises the next question: how fast does a loop have to run?

第 2 步 · 频率和延迟Step 2 · Rate and delay

立扫把容易，立铅笔难A broom is easy to balance, a pencil is not

把一把扫把倒过来立在手心，多数人练一会儿就能做到；换成一支铅笔，几乎没人做得到。手还是那只手，差在哪？差在东西倒下的快慢。Turn a broom upside down and balance it on your palm: most people manage after a little practice. Try the same with a pencil and almost nobody can. It is the same hand, so what is the difference? How fast the thing falls.

θ̈ = (g ∕ l) · θT = √(l ∕ g)

杆偏离竖直的角度 θ，它的"角加速度"和角度本身成正比：偏得越多，倒得越快。倒起来以后，每过时间 T，偏角大约放大 2.7 倍。The angle θ by which the stick leans from vertical has an “angular acceleration” proportional to the angle itself: the further it leans, the faster it falls. Once it is falling, every time T passes, the lean grows by a factor of about 2.7.

竖直立着的杆是不稳定的：一点点偏差，重力就会把它越放越大。放大得多快，只看杆有多长：短杆一下子就被重力带倒了，长杆倒得慢，给人留出了反应的时间。T 就是这只"倒下时钟"走一格的时间。A stick standing upright is unstable: gravity amplifies the slightest lean, more and more. How fast it amplifies depends only on the stick’s length: a short stick is toppled by gravity in an instant, a long one falls slowly and leaves time to react. T is one tick of this “falling clock”.

从这根杆可以读出两条经验，它们几乎适用于所有控制环：Two rules of thumb can be read off this stick, and they hold for almost every control loop:

  • 环要比它管的东西快一个数量级左右。工程上常说：采样频率至少是闭环带宽的 10 倍。带宽就是一个环能跟得上的最快变化，单位也是 Hz。A loop should be about an order of magnitude faster than what it controls. Engineers often say the sampling rate should be at least 10 times the loop’s closed-loop bandwidth. Bandwidth is the fastest change a loop can keep up with, also measured in Hz.
  • 延迟比频率更要命。每秒算 1000 次，可每次用的都是 0.1 秒前的数据，照样扶不住短杆。延迟包括传感器曝光、传输、排队、计算、电机响应，环里的每一段都算。Delay hurts more than rate. Compute 1000 times a second but always use data from 0.1 s ago, and the short stick still falls. Delay includes sensor exposure, transmission, queueing, computation and motor response: every part of the loop counts.

第 0 步那些频率就是这么来的。电机线圈里的电流，变化的时间尺度是零点几到几毫秒（大约等于线圈的电感除以电阻），所以电流环要每秒几万次；关节转起来是几十毫秒，伺服环每秒几千次；身体摔倒、侧滑是零点几秒，全身控制每秒几百次；走廊里的人走过来是几秒，感知和规划每秒十几次就够了。人的身体也是这么分的：膝跳反射只要几十毫秒，走脊髓，不经过大脑；看到偏差再修正动作，要 0.1 到 0.2 秒以上；决定去哪，是几秒钟的事。That is where the rates in step 0 come from. The current in a motor coil changes on a time scale of a fraction of a millisecond to a few milliseconds (roughly the coil’s inductance divided by its resistance), so the current loop runs tens of thousands of times a second; a joint takes tens of milliseconds to get moving, so servo loops run thousands of times a second; a body falls or slips in fractions of a second, so whole-body control runs hundreds of times a second; a person walking down the corridor takes seconds, so perception and planning at ten-odd times a second is enough. The human body divides the work the same way: the knee-jerk reflex takes only tens of milliseconds, through the spinal cord without involving the brain; correcting a movement after seeing an error takes 0.1 to 0.2 s or more; deciding where to go takes seconds.

这也解释了为什么最快的环都放在离电机最近的芯片上，而不是绕到网络另一头的大电脑上：每多绕一段路，就多一段延迟。工程上说的实时（real-time），讲的也不是"算得快"，而是"保证每次都按时算完"。每次运行的时刻忽早忽晚，叫抖动（jitter）；偶尔整整晚了一拍，叫超时（overrun）。对控制环来说，这两样都像延迟突然变大，比平均慢一点更危险。It also explains why the fastest loops sit on the chips closest to the motors rather than on the big computer at the far end of a network: every extra stretch of road adds delay. What engineers call real-time does not mean “computes fast” either, but “always finishes on time, guaranteed”. When the moment each run starts wanders early and late, that is called jitter; when a run occasionally misses its slot altogether, that is an overrun. For a control loop, both are like a sudden jump in delay, and more dangerous than being a little slow on average.

第 3 步 · 级联Step 3 · Cascades

环套环：无人机的四级串联Loops inside loops: a drone’s four-stage cascade

四旋翼无人机只能做一件事：改变几个螺旋桨的转速。可你给它的命令是"飞到那棵树旁边"。从"位置"到"转速"，中间隔着好几层：要往右飞，机身得先向右倾斜，让推力有一部分朝右；要倾斜，机身得先转起来；要转起来，左右两边的推力就得不一样。A quadrotor drone can do exactly one thing: change how fast its propellers spin. Yet the command you give it is “fly over to that tree”. Between “position” and “propeller speed” there are several layers: to fly right, the body must first lean right so that part of the thrust points right; to lean, the body must first start turning; to turn, the left and right propellers must push differently.

于是控制器被拆成一串环，每个环只管一件事，它算出的"要多少"，就是下一个环的目标：So the controller is split into a chain of loops, each looking after one thing, and the “how much” that one loop works out is the next loop’s target:

每一行都是同一个句式："要的 = 系数 ×（上一层要的 − 实际测到的）"。每一行算出来的"要"，交给下一行当目标：位置环要一个速度，速度环要一个倾角，姿态环要一个角速度，角速度环要一个推力差。Every line follows the same pattern: “what I want = gain × (what the layer above wants − what was actually measured)”. What each line wants becomes the target of the next: the position loop wants a velocity, the velocity loop a tilt, the attitude loop a turning rate, the rate loop a difference in thrust.

位置没法直接控制，只能通过速度；速度要靠倾斜机身；倾斜要靠转动；转动要靠两边推力不一样。拆成一串以后，每一层都只是一个简单的比例控制，就像第 1 步 PD 里的 P 那一项。还有一个好处：内层能就地消灭扰动。一阵风把机身吹歪了，角速度环几毫秒内就开始纠正；等慢得多的位置环察觉到，机身早已被扶住，它只需要把漂开的一小段拉回来。Position cannot be controlled directly, only through velocity; velocity takes tilting the body; tilting takes turning; turning takes the two sides pushing differently. Once split into a chain, every layer is just a simple proportional controller, like the P term of the PD controller in step 1. There is another bonus: inner loops deal with disturbances on the spot. When a gust knocks the body askew, the rate loop starts correcting within milliseconds; by the time the much slower position loop notices, the body has long been caught, and all it has to do is pull back the short distance the drone has drifted.

像一家公司：老板只说"去那儿"（位置），经理换算成"多快走"（速度），组长换算成"身子歪多少"（倾角），工人换算成"转多快"（角速度），最后落到"两边各使多大劲"（推力）。越往下，命令越具体，反应越快。Like a company: the boss only says “go over there” (position), the manager turns that into “how fast to walk” (velocity), the team leader into “how far to lean” (tilt), the worker into “how fast to turn” (turning rate), and it all ends up as “how hard each side pushes” (thrust). The further down, the more concrete the orders and the faster the reactions.

目标在右边 2 m，kx = 1 /s：要的速度 2 m/s。现在悬停不动，kv = 2.5 /s：要的加速度 5 m/s²，要的倾角约 5 ∕ 9.8 ≈ 0.5 rad，将近 30°。现在机身是平的，kθ = 8 /s：要的角速度约 4 rad/s，每秒转 230° 左右。角速度环再把"要转这么快"变成左右的推力差。The target is 2 m to the right and kx = 1 /s: the velocity asked for is 2 m/s. The drone is hovering still and kv = 2.5 /s: the acceleration asked for is 5 m/s², so the tilt asked for is about 5 ∕ 9.8 ≈ 0.5 rad, nearly 30°. The body is level and kθ = 8 /s: the turning rate asked for is about 4 rad/s, roughly 230° a second. The rate loop then turns “turn this fast” into a difference between left and right thrust.

内层要比外层快得多：内层的带宽（跟得上的最快变化）通常是外层的几倍到十倍。这里的"快"指反应快，不一定是每秒跑的次数多：下面表里的位置环和速度环都是每秒 50 次，速度环的增益更大，反应就更快。外层下一次发命令之前，内层已经把上一个命令执行到位了，外层就可以把内层当成"说到做到"的执行者。两层快慢差不多时，就会互相"打架"：外层还没看到上一次命令的效果，又加码了。Inner loops must be much faster than outer ones: an inner loop’s bandwidth (the fastest change it can follow) is usually several to ten times the outer loop’s. “Faster” here means quicker to respond, not necessarily more runs per second: in the table below, the position and velocity loops both run 50 times a second, but the velocity loop has the larger gain, so it responds faster. Before the outer loop sends its next command, the inner loop has already carried out the last one, so the outer loop can treat the inner loop as an executor that does exactly what it is told. When two layers run at similar speeds they start to fight: the outer loop has not yet seen the effect of its last command and piles on more.

几件事值得注意：A few things are worth noticing:

  • 扰动在最近的那一层被消灭。风一吹，最先动起来的是角速度环：机身刚开始歪，它下一次运行就加大两边的推力差顶回去，机身只歪了 3° 左右。风还在把机身往旁边推，速度环稍后发现，让机身迎着风倾斜；被吹偏的那一小段位置，要靠慢得多的位置环花两三秒拉回来。A disturbance is dealt with by the nearest layer. When the wind blows, the first to move is the rate loop: as soon as the body starts to tip, its next run raises the thrust difference to push back, and the body tips by only about 3°. The wind is still pushing the drone sideways; the velocity loop notices a little later and leans the body into the wind; the short distance the drone has been blown off course is pulled back by the much slower position loop over two or three seconds.
  • 最内层的环最怕慢。角速度环降到每秒 20 次左右，两次纠正之间机身已经转过了头，一直大幅摇晃；降到每秒 15 次以下，就会翻：它管的东西变得最快，所以它最需要快。真实的飞控里，PX4 的角速度环也是最内的一环；航拍和科研用的飞控每秒跑几百次起，竞速穿越机用的 Betaflight 常跑每秒 2000 到 8000 次。The innermost loop suffers most from being slow. Drop the rate loop to about 20 runs a second and the body turns too far between two corrections, so it keeps rocking widely; below about 15 runs a second it flips: it controls the thing that changes fastest, so it needs speed most. In real flight controllers the rate loop is also the innermost one, as in PX4; camera and research drones run it from a few hundred times a second, and racing quads on Betaflight often run it 2,000 to 8,000 times a second.
  • 外层太急，内层跟不上。位置环的增益太大，它要的速度变得比里面几层能跟上的还快，于是冲过目标再折回来。When the outer loop is in a hurry, the inner loops cannot keep up. Make the position-loop gain too high and the velocity it asks for changes faster than the layers inside can follow, so it overshoots the target and comes back.
  • 少一层，就少一种"刹车"。关掉角速度环，没人专门管"转得多快"，姿态环只能按角度差给推力差，就像第 1 步里把 Kd 拖到 0，只剩弹簧、没有阻尼器。第 1 步的手臂只是来回晃个不停；这里电机的推力要 20 毫秒左右才跟上命令，每次都推晚了一点，晃动就越来越大。One layer fewer, one brake fewer. Turn off the rate loop and nobody looks after how fast the body is turning; the attitude loop can only turn the angle error straight into a thrust difference, like dragging Kd to 0 in step 1, which leaves a spring with no damper. In step 1 the arm just keeps swinging; here the motors take about 20 ms to deliver the thrust they are told to, so every push comes a little late, and the swinging grows.

这种环套环在机器人里到处都是，叫级联控制（cascade control）。机械臂每个关节的驱动器里，是位置环套速度环、速度环套电流环；四足机器人是 MPC 套全身控制、全身控制套关节 PD、关节 PD 套电流环；汽车是轨迹跟踪套转向角、转向角套转向电机的电流。最里面那一环，几乎永远是电流。Loops inside loops are everywhere in robots, and are called cascade control. Inside each joint drive of a robot arm, a position loop wraps a velocity loop, which wraps a current loop; in a quadruped, MPC wraps whole-body control, which wraps joint PD, which wraps the current loop; in a car, trajectory tracking wraps the steering angle, which wraps the current of the steering motor. The innermost loop is nearly always current.

第 4 步 · 执行Step 4 · Actuation

最里面一环：力矩就是电流The innermost loop: torque is current

所有电动机器人的最后一站都一样：把电变成力。电机的力矩和流过线圈的电流成正比，τ = Kt · i，所以"控制力矩"说到底是"控制电流"。可驱动器手里只有开关：电源要么接通，要么断开，没有"接通一半"。怎么用开关得到想要的电流？Every electric robot ends at the same place: turning electricity into force. A motor’s torque is proportional to the current through its coils, τ = Kt · i, so “controlling torque” really means “controlling current”. But all a drive has in its hands is a switch: the supply is either connected or disconnected, never “half connected”. How do you get the current you want out of a switch?

V̄ = D · VbusΔi ≈ Vbus · D (1 − D) ∕ (L · f)

平均电压等于占空比乘电源电压；电流的波动（纹波），和电源电压成正比，和线圈的电感、开关频率成反比。The average voltage equals the duty cycle times the supply voltage; the current’s fluctuation (the ripple) is proportional to the supply voltage and inversely proportional to the coil’s inductance and the switching frequency.

开关得足够快，线圈来不及跟着每一次开关变化，只能感受到平均值，这就是 PWM（脉宽调制）：用"接通时间占多少比例"来调平均电压。开关越快，电流越平；可每开关一次都有一点损耗，开关管也会发热，所以不能无限快。常见的折中是每秒两万到几万次。If the switching is fast enough, the coil cannot follow every switch and only feels the average. That is PWM (pulse-width modulation): set the average voltage through the fraction of time the switch is on. The faster the switching, the smoother the current; but every switch costs a little energy and heats the transistors, so it cannot be infinitely fast. The usual compromise is twenty to several tens of thousands of times a second.

电流环是整台机器人里最快、也最"低调"的一环：它把电池电压的波动、线圈的电阻和电感、电机转起来以后产生的反电动势统统挡在里面，让上面的每一层都可以假装"我要多少力矩，就有多少力矩"。第 3 步说内层把下面"理想化"，最彻底的就是这一层。The current loop is the fastest and the most self-effacing loop in the whole robot: it walls off the sagging battery voltage, the coil’s resistance and inductance, and the back-EMF a motor generates once it spins, so that every layer above can pretend “whatever torque I ask for, I get”. Step 3 said inner loops make what lies below look ideal; this layer does it most thoroughly.

真实的无刷电机有三组线圈。驱动器要按转子此刻的角度（编码器测），算出三组线圈各该通多少电流，让线圈产生的磁场始终和转子的磁场垂直：就像推门时总是垂直门板用力，同样的力气，门转得最动。这叫磁场定向控制（FOC）。它先把三组线圈的电流换算到跟着转子一起转的坐标系里，变成出力矩的 q 轴电流和不出力矩的 d 轴电流（通常控制为 0），各用一个上面这样的电流环；算出的电压再换算回三组线圈的占空比。A real brushless motor has three sets of coils. Using the rotor’s angle at this instant (measured by the encoder), the drive works out how much current each set should carry so that the magnetic field of the coils always stays at right angles to the rotor’s field: like always pushing a door at right angles to the door, which turns it most for the same effort. This is called field-oriented control (FOC). It first converts the three coil currents into a frame that turns with the rotor, giving a q-axis current, which makes torque, and a d-axis current, which does not (usually held at 0); each gets a current loop like the one above, and the resulting voltages are converted back into duty cycles for the three sets of coils.

电机转得快、力矩小，关节要的是转得慢、力矩大，中间靠减速器换：机械臂的关节常用上百比一的谐波减速器，力大、精确、几乎没有间隙；MIT Cheetah 系列和不少四足机器人却只用 6 到 10 比 1，好让腿落地时能被地面推回去、顺势"让一下"，这叫反向可驱动（backdrivable）。减速比小，地面传回来的力也能直接从电流里读出来，电机本身就成了力传感器。A motor spins fast with little torque, while a joint needs to turn slowly with a lot of torque; a gearbox trades one for the other. Robot arm joints usually have harmonic gears with ratios over 100 to 1: strong, precise and almost free of backlash. The MIT Cheetah robots and many other quadrupeds use only 6 to 10 to 1, so that when a leg lands, the ground can push it back and it can “give” a little. This is called being backdrivable. With a low gear ratio, the force coming back from the ground can be read straight off the current, and the motor itself becomes a force sensor.

一台机器人，从任务到电机One robot, from task to motor

前面几步把环一个个拆开看。现在把它们装回一台完整的机器人：一台扫地机器人。它简单，可第 0 步的六层一层不少：任务层是决策；全局规划和局部规划是规划；激光雷达扫描、更新地图是传感和感知；底盘控制和轮速环是控制；轮子的电机是执行。The previous steps took the loops apart one by one. Now put them back into a complete robot: a robot vacuum. It is simple, but all six layers from step 0 are there: the task layer is decision; global and local planning are planning; the lidar sweeps and map updates are sensing and perception; base control and the wheel speed loops are control; the wheel motors are actuation.

从上往下，每层把自己的输出交给下一层：任务层给出"下一个要去的点"；全局规划在地图上搜出一条路线（一串路点；常用 A* 算法，在网格地图上一格一格地找最短的路）；局部规划沿着这条路线，结合激光雷达刚看到的东西，算出此刻该走多快、转多快；底盘控制把"走多快、转多快"换算成左右两个轮子的速度；轮速环让轮子真的转到这个速度。从下往上，测量一路传回来：轮子的编码器、激光雷达的扫描、碰撞开关。From the top down, each layer hands its output to the next: the task layer names the next point to go to; global planning searches the map for a route (a string of waypoints, often found with the A* algorithm, which searches a grid map cell by cell for the shortest way); local planning follows that route, takes into account what the lidar has just seen, and works out how fast to drive and turn right now; the base controller turns “how fast to drive and turn” into speeds for the left and right wheels; the wheel speed loops make the wheels actually turn at those speeds. From the bottom up, measurements travel back: the wheel encoders, the lidar sweeps, the bumper switch.

局部规划只关心"整台机器人怎么动"，不关心它有几个、什么样的轮子；把意图翻译成具体执行器的语言，是底盘控制的事。换成四个麦克纳姆轮（能横着走的轮子），只要换掉这一层，上面一行代码都不用改。这是分层的另一个好处：每层只和相邻的层说话，说的内容（接口）是约定好的。Local planning only cares how the robot as a whole should move, not how many wheels it has or what kind; translating that intention into the language of particular actuators is the base controller’s job. Swap in four mecanum wheels (wheels that can drive sideways) and only this layer changes; not a line of code above it has to. That is another benefit of layering: each layer only talks to its neighbours, in an agreed form (the interface).

有几层环，看有几种快慢As many loops as there are speeds

数一数，每种机器人身体里的环都在 4 到 7 层之间。这个数不是规定出来的：机器人要同时应付几种相差约一个数量级的时间尺度，就有几层环。无人机的机身天生不稳，零点几秒就会翻，所以最内的控制环必须极快；扫地机器人停下来想一会儿也没关系，它的环可以更少、更慢；汽车的转向和刹车本身就有零点几秒的惯性，控制层每秒几十到一百次就够了。Count them: every kind of robot has between 4 and 7 loops in its body. The number is not a rule: a robot has as many loops as there are time scales, roughly an order of magnitude apart, that it has to handle at once. A drone’s body is unstable by nature and flips within a fraction of a second, so its innermost control loop must be extremely fast; a robot vacuum can stop and think for a moment, so its loops can be fewer and slower; a car’s steering and brakes have a fraction of a second of inertia of their own, so a control layer running dozens to a hundred times a second is enough.

机器人该怎么分层，上世纪八九十年代就有了两种经典的说法。Albus 在美国国家标准与技术研究院提出的 RCS 参考架构，按时间尺度分层：伺服层只规划 0.03 秒，往上依次是 0.3 秒、3 秒、30 秒，每层差十倍，而且每一层都闭环。今天用 ROS 2 搭起来的系统，大体还是这副"快环在下、慢环在上"的骨架。By the 1980s and 1990s there were already two classic answers to how a robot should be layered. The RCS reference architecture Albus proposed at the US National Institute of Standards and Technology layers by time scale: the servo level plans only 0.03 s ahead, and the levels above plan 0.3 s, 3 s and 30 s: a factor of ten per level, with feedback closed at every level. Systems built with ROS 2 today still follow much the same skeleton, with fast loops below and slow loops above.

Brooks 的包容架构（1986）换了一种分法：按能力分层。最底层只管"别撞上东西"，往上叠"四处走走"，再叠"去探索"；每一层都是从传感器直接到动作的反应式行为，每加一层就多一种能力，下面的层照常运行，上层需要时可以压过下层的输出。它当时是为了反对"先感知、再规划、后行动"一条长链提出的。今天的机器人里也常能看到它的影子：上一步的碰撞开关一压下就后退，不等上层慢慢想。Brooks’s subsumption architecture (1986) divides things another way: by ability. The bottom layer only makes sure the robot does not run into things; on top of it comes “wander about”, then “explore”. Each layer is a reactive behaviour that goes straight from sensors to action; each new layer adds an ability, the layers beneath keep running, and a higher layer can override a lower one’s outputs when it needs to. It was put forward as a rejection of the long chain of “sense, then plan, then act”. Its traces are common in robots today: the bumper in the previous step makes the robot back off the moment it is pressed, without waiting for the upper layers to think it over.

学习方法改变的是中间几层Learning changes the middle layers

从示范到试错那一篇讲的学习方法，在这副骨架里坐在哪？大多坐在中间。强化学习训练出来的行走策略，常见每秒运行 50 次，直接输出各个关节的目标角度，交给关节里的 PD 控制（Rudin 等 2021），它替掉的是 MPC 和全身控制；VLA（视觉-语言-动作模型）从图像和一句指令直接输出一段动作，相当于把感知、规划和一部分控制并成了一层。但它们输出的仍然是末端位姿或关节的目标，下面还要经过逆运动学或控制器，伺服环和电流环一个不少：电机的物理没有变，最快的那几个环也就省不掉。最外面还多出一个环：数据环。部署，收集失败的例子，重新训练，再部署，转一圈要几天到几个月。Where do the learning methods from From Demonstration to Trial and Error sit in this skeleton? Mostly in the middle. A walking policy trained with reinforcement learning typically runs 50 times a second and outputs target angles for every joint, which are handed to PD control in the joints (Rudin et al. 2021); it replaces MPC and whole-body control. A VLA (vision-language-action model) goes straight from images and a one-sentence language instruction to a stretch of actions, merging perception, planning and part of control into a single layer. But what they output is still a target for the end effector’s pose or for the joints, which then passes through inverse kinematics or a controller, and every servo loop and current loop below stays: the physics of the motors has not changed, so the fastest loops cannot be dropped. And one more loop appears on the outside: the data loop. Deploy, collect the failures, retrain, deploy again; one turn takes days to months.

  • 设备之间（机器人身体里）：用现场总线（fieldbus）。最常见的两种是 CAN 和 EtherCAT，下面的交互比较它们；还有更简单的 RS-485，一些四足机器人的关节在用。总线上跑的"语言"也有标准：CANopen 里的 CiA 402 规定了驱动器的状态机，和位置、速度、力矩几种工作模式，所以不同厂家的驱动器能互换；这套规定在 EtherCAT 上也能用。Between devices (inside the robot’s body): a fieldbus. What runs over the bus is standardised too: CiA 402 in CANopen defines a drive’s state machine and its position, velocity and torque modes, so drives from different makers can be swapped; the same definitions also work over EtherCAT.

  • 程序之间（主计算机里）：用中间件。ROS 2 建在 DDS（一种发布、订阅的通信标准）上：感知程序发布"障碍物列表"，规划程序订阅它，谁也不用知道对方在哪个进程、哪台电脑上。传感器数据、位姿这类消息带一个消息头：测量的时间戳，和数据所在坐标系的名字；tf2 记录各个坐标系之间随时间变化的变换。真正的硬实时环不走这些消息，而是放在同一个进程里（ros2_control 就是这么做的），或者走共享内存，免得排队带来不确定的延迟。Between programs (inside the main computer): middleware. ROS 2 is built on DDS (a publish-and-subscribe communication standard): the perception program publishes an “obstacle list”, the planning program subscribes to it, and neither needs to know which process or which computer the other runs on. Messages such as sensor data and poses carry a header: the timestamp of the measurement and the name of the coordinate frame the data is in; tf2 keeps track of how the transforms between frames change over time. Truly hard real-time loops do not go through these messages; they run inside a single process (that is what ros2_control does) or over shared memory, to avoid the unpredictable delays of queueing.
  • 两种模式。CAN：四个节点同时想说话。每个报文以一位 0 开头，接着是 11 位的 ID，从最高位发起。总线的规矩是"0 能盖过 1"：只要有一个节点发 0，线上就是 0（0 叫显性，1 叫隐性）。每个节点一边发一边听：发的是 1、听到的却是 0，说明有更优先的节点在说话，它马上闭嘴，等下次。Two modes. CAN: four nodes want to talk at the same time. Every message starts with a single 0, followed by an 11-bit ID, highest bit first. The rule on the bus is “a 0 overrides a 1”: if any node sends a 0, the line reads 0 (0 is called dominant, 1 recessive). Each node listens while it sends: if it sent a 1 but hears a 0, a node with higher priority is talking, so it stops at once and waits for the next chance.

CAN 的仲裁不浪费时间：赢家的报文从头到尾没被打断，输家只是晚一点再发。代价是带宽小：经典 CAN 最高每秒 1 兆位，一帧最多 8 字节数据，新一些的 CAN FD 能装 64 字节、跑得更快。它便宜、抗干扰，汽车里各个电子控制单元之间、很多小型机器人的关节都用它。EtherCAT 走以太网的硬件，一帧"边走边读写"，控制周期能短到 100 微秒以下；各个驱动器的时钟还会用"分布式时钟"对齐，大家在同一时刻采样，误差远小于 1 微秒（EtherCAT）。工业机械臂和很多高端研究平台用的是它。CAN arbitration wastes no time: the winner’s message is never interrupted, and the losers simply send a little later. The price is low bandwidth: classic CAN tops out at 1 megabit a second with at most 8 bytes of data per frame; the newer CAN FD carries 64 bytes and runs faster. It is cheap and robust against interference, and links the electronic control units in cars and the joints of many small robots. EtherCAT runs on Ethernet hardware, each frame is “read and written on the fly”, and control cycles can be shorter than 100 microseconds; the drives’ clocks are aligned with “distributed clocks” so that they all sample at the same moment, with errors far below 1 microsecond (EtherCAT). Industrial robot arms and many high-end research platforms use it.

时间对不齐会怎样When clocks disagree

把相机和激光雷达的数据融合起来，前提是知道每份数据是在哪一刻测的。可每个传感器都有自己的时钟，时钟靠晶振计时，晶振的频率总有一点偏差。Fusing camera and lidar data assumes you know the moment each piece of data was measured. But every sensor keeps its own clock, each clock counts with a crystal oscillator, and no crystal runs at exactly the right rate.

Δx = v · Δt

两份数据的时间戳差了 Δt，运动的物体在两份数据里就错开 v · Δt。If the two pieces of data are time-stamped Δt apart, a moving object appears shifted by v · Δt between them.

融合程序默认"时间戳一样，就是同一时刻的世界"。时间戳一错，它就把两个不同时刻的世界拼在了一起；物体越快，错得越多。Fusion software assumes that the same timestamp means the same moment in the world. Once the timestamps are off, it stitches together the world at two different moments; the faster the object, the bigger the error.

最后是两件不起眼、但一件都不能少的事。Finally, two things that are easy to overlook but cannot be left out.

计算平台按"能不能保证准时"分三档。单片机裸跑程序或者跑 RTOS，时间精确到微秒，跑电流环和关节环；实时 Linux 跑每秒一千次左右的控制，让 Linux 能按时响应的 PREEMPT_RT 补丁，2024 年并入了 Linux 6.12 主线；普通 Linux 加 GPU，毫秒级、不保证准时，跑感知、规划和大模型。第 0 步那一叠层，最后就是这样分到三类芯片上的。Computers come in three grades of “guaranteed on time”. A microcontroller running bare code or an RTOS keeps time to the microsecond, and runs the current and joint loops; real-time Linux runs control at around a thousand times a second (PREEMPT_RT, the patches that let Linux respond on time, were merged into mainline Linux 6.12 in 2024); ordinary Linux with a GPU works in milliseconds with no guarantee of punctuality, and runs perception, planning and large models. That is how the stack of layers from step 0 is finally divided among three kinds of chip.

安全回路不在任何一层里，而是和整条链并联。急停按钮通过硬线直接触发驱动器的 STO（安全转矩关断），让电机不再出力，不经过应用软件，因为软件可能卡死。STO 只管"不再出力"：转着的关节会靠惯性继续转，受重力的手臂会掉下来，所以还要配合抱闸（一断电就刹住的机械刹车），或者先受控地停下、再切断力矩。急停回路本身也按"线会断"来设计：用常闭、双通道的接线，线断了就等于按下急停。看门狗则让下层定时等上层的"心跳"，超时就自动进入安全状态，就是第 5 步里那只让机器人停车的看门狗。The safety circuit is not inside any layer; it runs in parallel with the whole chain. An emergency stop button triggers the drives’ STO (safe torque off) directly over a hard-wired line, so the motors stop producing torque without going through the application software, which might hang. STO only stops the torque: a turning joint keeps coasting, and an arm held up against gravity falls, so STO is combined with holding brakes (mechanical brakes that clamp as soon as power is lost), or the machine is first brought to a controlled stop and the torque cut afterwards. The e-stop circuit itself is designed on the assumption that wires break: it uses normally closed, dual-channel wiring, so a broken wire counts as pressing the e-stop. A watchdog, in turn, has a lower layer wait for a regular “heartbeat” from the layer above and switch to a safe state when it stops: the same watchdog that stopped the robot in step 5.

回到开头的问题Back to the opening questions

  • 六层之间传的是什么？传感交出原始数据，感知交出世界状态，决策交出目标，规划交出路线或轨迹，控制交出命令，执行交出力和运动；动作改变了世界，再被传感测到。事情从哪一层进来，就从哪一层往下传：任务从决策层进来，路上的障碍从传感进来、到规划为止，一阵风只在最下面几层里转一圈。What passes between the six layers? Sensing hands on raw data, perception the world state, decision a goal, planning a route or trajectory, control commands, and actuation force and motion; the action changes the world, which sensing then measures again. Each event enters at a particular layer and travels down from there: a task enters at decision, an obstacle in the way enters at sensing and stops at planning, and a gust only goes round the bottom few layers.
  • 被推的那 0.3 秒里发生了什么？最下面的电流环和关节 PD 先动，几十微秒到几毫秒里就开始顶住；状态估计和全身控制几毫秒后接上；MPC 几十毫秒后重新安排落脚点；相机和导航可能根本没参与。越快的扰动，由越靠下的环处理。What happened in the 0.3 s after the push? The current loops and joint PD at the bottom move first, starting to push back within tens of microseconds to a few milliseconds; state estimation and whole-body control join a few milliseconds later; MPC replans the footholds after tens of milliseconds; the camera and navigation may not take part at all. The faster the disturbance, the lower the loop that handles it.
  • 为什么要分这么多层？每一层管的物理过程快慢不同，环要比它管的东西快一个数量级左右；要"想"的事算得慢，"反射"简单算得快，两条趋势刚好对上。分层还让每一层都简单、扰动能就地消灭、上层出事时下层能兜底。Why so many layers? Each layer looks after physics that changes at its own speed, and a loop must be about an order of magnitude faster than what it controls; things that need “thinking” are slow to compute, while “reflexes” are simple and fast, and the two trends line up neatly. Layering also keeps each layer simple, lets disturbances be dealt with on the spot, and lets a lower layer catch the robot when an upper one fails.
  • 闭环一共有几层？身体里通常 4 到 7 层，看要同时应付几种时间尺度。外面还有重新规划的环、人在旁边遥控或监督的环、部署和训练之间的数据环；安全回路和整条链并联。How many loops are there? Usually 4 to 7 inside the body, depending on how many time scales it has to handle at once. Outside there are more: the replanning loop, the loop of a person teleoperating or supervising, and the data loop between deployment and training; the safety circuit runs in parallel with the whole chain.
  • 机器人那么多，为什么都一样？因为它们面对的是同样的事：电机的电流以微秒变化，身体以零点几秒变化，环境以秒变化，任务以分钟变化。层的名字各不相同，排法是同一个。With so many kinds of robot, why are they all alike? Because they all face the same facts: motor current changes in microseconds, bodies in fractions of a second, surroundings in seconds, tasks in minutes. The layers go by different names, but they are arranged the same way.

检查一下Check yourself

第 1 步的手臂停在 30° 时，关节托着约 4.2 N·m 的重力力矩。Kp = 50 N·m/rad，大约下垂多少？想让下垂不到 1°，Kp 至少要多大？With the arm from step 1 held at 30°, the joint carries about 4.2 N·m of gravity torque. With Kp = 50 N·m/rad, roughly how much does it sag? How large must Kp be to keep the sag under 1°?

下垂 ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 = 0.084 rad ≈ 4.8°。1° ≈ 0.0175 rad，所以 Kp 至少要 4.2 ÷ 0.0175 ≈ 241 N·m/rad，留点余量，取 250 左右。另外两个办法：加重力补偿（前馈），或者加积分项，Kp 就不用这么大。Sag ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 = 0.084 rad ≈ 4.8°. 1° ≈ 0.0175 rad, so Kp must be at least 4.2 ÷ 0.0175 ≈ 241 N·m/rad; with a little margin, about 250. Two other ways out: add gravity compensation (feedforward), or add an integral term, and then Kp does not need to be nearly as large.

按第 2 步的交互，总滞后超过杆的 T 的一半左右，就很难扶住了。人从看见到动手大约 0.15 秒，大约多长的杆还扶得住？In the interactive of step 2, once the total lag is more than about half of the stick’s T, it becomes very hard to hold. A person takes about 0.15 s from seeing to moving the hand: roughly how long must a stick be to be held?

That is only the rule of thumb for the controller in the interactive; people anticipate, so in practice they can hold a somewhat shorter stick, but certainly not a pencil.

为什么无人机的角速度环要比位置环快得多？反过来，让位置环每秒跑 1000 次、角速度环每秒跑 20 次，会怎样？Why must a drone’s rate loop be so much faster than its position loop? And the other way round: what happens if the position loop runs 1,000 times a second and the rate loop 20?

角速度环管的是机身转动，零点几秒就能翻过去；位置变化慢得多。反过来，位置环再快也没用：它每次都在要一个新的速度，可下面负责让机身转到位的环太慢，执行不出来。按第 3 步的交互，角速度环每秒 20 次时，机身一直大幅摇晃；再慢一点就会翻。快慢要按"管的东西变得多快"来分配。The rate loop looks after how the body turns, and it can flip in a fraction of a second; position changes far more slowly. Turn it round and a fast position loop is no use: it keeps asking for new velocities, but the loop below, which has to get the body turned into place, is too slow to deliver. In the interactive in step 3, with the rate loop at 20 times a second the body keeps rocking widely; a little slower and it flips. Speed has to be allotted according to how fast the controlled thing changes.

开关频率从 20 kHz 降到 2 kHz，电流纹波大约变成多少倍？为什么很多驱动器不用 2 kHz？If the switching frequency drops from 20 kHz to 2 kHz, by roughly what factor does the current ripple change? Why do so many drives avoid 2 kHz?

纹波和开关频率成反比，大约变成 10 倍。纹波大，力矩跟着抖；2 kHz 在人耳能听到的范围里，电机会发出刺耳的尖叫；电流环也只能每 0.5 毫秒改一次占空比，反应变慢。Ripple is inversely proportional to switching frequency, so it grows about tenfold. Larger ripple makes the torque shudder; 2 kHz is well within human hearing, so the motor gives off a piercing whine; and the current loop can only change the duty once every 0.5 ms, so it reacts more slowly.

相机和激光雷达的时间戳差了 30 ms，横穿过来的车速 15 m/s，两份数据里的车错开多少？The camera and lidar timestamps are 30 ms apart, and a car crossing in front moves at 15 m/s. How far apart does the car appear in the two pieces of data?

Δx = v · Δt = 15 × 0.03 = 0.45 m。Δx = v · Δt = 15 × 0.03 = 0.45 m.

扫地机器人的主电脑死机了，为什么它不会一直往前冲？If a robot vacuum’s main computer crashes, why does it not keep charging ahead?

底盘控制器上有看门狗：一段时间（交互里是 0.3 秒，具体数字因机器而异）没收到新的速度命令，就主动停车。更严重的情况，还有急停按钮通过硬线触发驱动器的 STO，让电机不再出力，不经过应用软件；受重力的关节同时靠抱闸刹住。The base controller has a watchdog: if no new speed command arrives for a while (0.3 s in the interactive; the exact figure varies from machine to machine), it stops the robot. For anything more serious, an emergency stop button triggers the drives’ STO over a hard-wired line, so the motors stop producing torque without going through the application software; joints held up against gravity are clamped by their holding brakes at the same time.
