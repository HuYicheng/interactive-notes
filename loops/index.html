环环相扣Loops Within Loops

机械臂、四足机器人、自动驾驶汽车、无人机、扫地机器人，长得天差地别，里面却是同一副骨架。按分工看，都是六层：传感、感知与估计、决策、规划、控制、执行；测量从一头进去，变成力从另一头出来，作用到世界上，再被测量。按快慢看，是一级套一级的好几个环：最里面的每秒跑几万次，最外面的几分钟才动一次。两种看法说的是同一台机器人；机器人之间的区别，全在每一层、每一级用什么硬件、跑多快、用什么算法。这一篇用一个个能推、能拔线、能调慢的小模拟，把这些层和环拆开来看：一个环怎么闭上，它要跑多快，环为什么要套着环，最里面的电流怎么控制，一台机器人从任务到电机各个程序传什么，五种机器人怎么装进同一副骨架，硬件之间又怎么连。Robot arms, legged robots, self-driving cars, drones and robot vacuums look nothing alike, yet inside they share one skeleton. Sliced by job, they all have six layers: sensing, perception and estimation, decision, planning, control, actuation; measurements go in at one end, force comes out at the other, acts on the world, and is measured again. Sliced by speed, they are several loops nested level within level: the innermost runs tens of thousands of times a second, the outermost moves once every few minutes. Both views describe the same robot; what sets one robot apart from another lies in the hardware, the speed and the algorithms used at each layer and each level. This piece takes the layers and loops apart with small simulations you can push, unplug and slow down: how a loop closes, how fast it has to run, why loops sit inside loops, how the current in the innermost loop is controlled, what a robot’s programs hand to one another from task to motor, how five kinds of robot fit the same skeleton, and how the hardware is wired together.

第 0 步 · 总览Step 0 · Overview

六层和几级环：所有机器人的同一副骨架Six layers and nested loops: the skeleton every robot shares

机器人种类那么多，拆开来看，都能用两把尺子来量。一把按分工：从测量到出力，中间要做哪几件事。不管什么机器人，都是这六件：传感、感知与估计、决策、规划、控制、执行，这一篇叫它们六层（功能层）。另一把按快慢：机器人身体里有好几个"测量、比较、纠正"的环，一个套一个，外面一级给里面一级定目标，越往里越快，从最外面的任务环一直到最里面的电流环，这一篇说它们是几级环。However many kinds of robot there are, take any one apart and you can measure it with two rulers. One goes by job: what has to happen between measuring and pushing. Whatever the robot, it is the same six things: sensing, perception and estimation, decision, planning, control, actuation; this page calls them the six layers (functional layers). The other goes by speed: inside a robot there are several loops of “measure, compare, correct”, one inside another, each outer loop setting the target for the one inside it, faster the further in, from the task loop on the outside all the way to the current loop at the centre; this page calls these loop levels.

这两套层次说的是同一台机器人，又常用同样的词（"规划层"和"规划环"，"控制层"和"内环控制"），所以特别容易混。可它们回答的是两个问题：六层回答"从测量到出力，信息要经过哪几道工序"；几级环回答"谁给谁定目标、各自多快"。机器人之间的区别，也都落在两者的交叉点上：同一层、同一级，这台机器人用什么硬件、每秒跑几次、用什么算法，那台又是什么。这一步先看六层，再把一个环拆开，然后看几级环怎么套，最后把两者放进同一张图。Both hierarchies describe the same robot and often share the same words (“planning layer” and “planning loop”, “control layer” and “inner-loop control”), which makes them especially easy to mix up. Yet they answer two different questions: the six layers answer “which stages does information pass through between measuring and pushing?”; the loop levels answer “who sets whose target, and how fast does each run?”. The differences between robots, too, all sit where the two cross: at a given layer and level, which hardware this robot uses, how many times a second it runs, which algorithm, and what another robot uses instead. This step looks at the six layers first, then takes one loop apart, then shows how loop levels nest, and finally puts the two into a single picture.

六层：每台机器人都要做的六件事The six layers: six jobs every robot must do

要把测量变成动作，不管是扫地还是飞行，都得做完这六件事。按信息流过的顺序：To turn measurements into action, whether sweeping a floor or flying, a robot has to get through these six jobs. In the order information flows through them:

传感测到了什么？SensingWhat was measured?

做什么What it does
把光、距离、转动、受力、电流这些物理量变成数字，每个数字都记下测量的时刻。Turns physical quantities such as light, distance, rotation, force and current into numbers, each stamped with the time it was measured.
吃进 → 交出In → out
物理世界 → 原始数据：一帧帧图像、一圈圈激光测距、关节角、加速度、电流。The physical world → raw data: camera frames, lidar sweeps, joint angles, accelerations, currents.
用什么Tools
相机、激光雷达、IMU（测转动和加速度）、编码器（测转角）、力传感器、电流传感器。Cameras, lidar, IMUs (measuring rotation and acceleration), encoders (measuring how far something has turned), force sensors, current sensors.
多快How fast
从深度相机每秒几十帧，到驱动器里的电流每秒采样几万次。From a depth camera’s few dozen frames a second to a drive sampling its current tens of thousands of times a second.

感知与估计这些数字说明了什么？Perception and estimationWhat do the numbers mean?

做什么What it does
把一堆数字变成对世界的描述：我在哪、朝哪、在怎么动，周围有什么、它们在怎么动。Turns a heap of numbers into a description of the world: where I am, which way I face, how I am moving, what is around me and how it is moving.
吃进 → 交出In → out
原始数据 → 世界状态：自己的位置、速度、姿态，地图，周围物体的清单。Raw data → the world state: its own position, velocity and attitude, a map, a list of the objects nearby.
用什么Tools
卡尔曼滤波（按可信程度把预测和测量合起来）、SLAM（边建图边定位）、目标检测和跟踪（上一篇的卷积网络和 Transformer 主要用在这里）。Kalman filters (combining prediction and measurement according to how far each can be trusted), SLAM (mapping while localising), object detection and tracking (where the convolutional networks and Transformers of the previous page mostly work).
多快How fast
建图每秒一次到几次，估计机身姿态每秒几百次。Maps are updated once to a few times a second; the body’s attitude is estimated hundreds of times a second.

决策现在该做什么？DecisionWhat should I do now?

做什么What it does
结合任务和现状，选出眼下的目标或模式。大部分时候不变，到了关键时刻才切换。Combines the task with the current situation to choose the goal or mode for now. Most of the time it stays put, switching only at key moments.
吃进 → 交出In → out
任务和世界状态 → 目标：去哪个点、抓哪个东西、用哪种步态。The task and the world state → a goal: which point to reach, which object to grasp, which gait to use.
用什么Tools
有限状态机（几种模式，加上在它们之间切换的条件）、行为树（把任务拆成一棵按顺序和条件执行的树）、任务规划。Finite-state machines (a few modes plus the conditions for switching between them), behaviour trees (a task broken down into a tree that runs by sequence and condition), task planning.
多快How fast
多半是有事才动：到了、被挡住了、电不够了。Mostly acts only when something happens: arrived, blocked, battery low.

规划怎么做到？PlanningHow do I get there?

做什么What it does
算出一条路线，或者一段带时间的轨迹：每一刻该在哪、什么姿态，同时绕开障碍。Works out a route, or a timed trajectory: where to be and in what pose at each moment, while avoiding obstacles.
吃进 → 交出In → out
目标和世界状态 → 计划：一串路点，或者每个时刻的目标位置、速度、姿态。A goal and the world state → a plan: a string of waypoints, or a target position, velocity and pose for each moment.
用什么Tools
A* 这类在地图上搜路的算法、RRT（随机撒点，长出一棵树去找路）、轨迹优化、MPC（往前预测一小段，算出最好的一串动作，只执行第一步）。Map-search algorithms such as A*, RRT (scatters random points and grows a tree to find a way), trajectory optimisation, MPC (looking a little way ahead, working out the best sequence of actions and carrying out only the first).
多快How fast
每秒一次到几十次。Once to a few dozen times a second.

控制此刻用多大的劲？ControlHow hard should I push right now?

做什么What it does
按此刻的数值误差，把目标（一个设定值，或者轨迹上的一个点）变成具体的命令：差多少、往哪边、用多大的劲。Turns the target (a setpoint, or a point on a trajectory) into a concrete command from the numerical error right now: how far off, which way, how hard.
吃进 → 交出In → out
目标和测到的状态 → 命令：轮速、关节力矩、电流、推力、转向角。A target and the measured state → commands: wheel speeds, joint torques, currents, thrusts, steering angles.
用什么Tools
PID（第 1 步）、MPC（就是规划卡片里那个，每个周期重算一次，拿来直接出命令）、全身控制（把身体要的动作分配成每个关节的力矩）、学到的策略（训练出来的神经网络）。PID (step 1), MPC (the same one as on the planning card, recomputed every cycle and used to issue commands directly), whole-body control (sharing out the motion the body needs as torques at each joint), learned policies (trained neural networks).
多快How fast
每秒几十次到几万次：这一层里叠着好几级环。From tens to tens of thousands of times a second: several loop levels are stacked in this one layer.

执行怎么把命令变成力？ActuationHow do commands become force?

做什么What it does
把命令变成力：功率管按命令把电池的电压接到电机上，电流产生力矩，再经过减速器让身体动起来。Turns commands into force: power transistors connect the battery voltage to the motor as the command says, the current produces torque, and through a gearbox the torque moves the body.
吃进 → 交出In → out
命令 → 力和运动。Commands → force and motion.
用什么Tools
功率级（一组功率管，第 4 步）、电机、减速器；汽车的刹车还要用液压。驱动器这个盒子里装着两层：芯片上算的电流环属于控制，功率管才是执行。A power stage (a set of power transistors, step 4), motors, gearboxes; a car’s brakes also use hydraulics. A drive is a box holding two layers: the current loop computed on its chip belongs to control; only the power transistors are actuation.
多快How fast
功率管每秒开关几万次（PWM，第 4 步）；身体最终动多快，受电机和身体的惯性限制。The power transistors switch tens of thousands of times a second (PWM, step 4); how fast the body finally moves is limited by the inertia of the motors and the body.

六层排成一条链：测量从传感进来，一层层加工，最后变成力，作用到物理世界上；世界变了，又被测量。有的机器人某一层很薄（扫地机器人的决策就是几条规则），有的新方法把几层并进一个神经网络（第 6 步），但这六件事一件也省不掉。The six layers form a chain: measurements come in at sensing and are processed layer by layer until they become force acting on the physical world; the world changes and is measured again. In some robots a layer is very thin (a robot vacuum’s decision layer is a handful of rules), and some newer methods fold several layers into one neural network (step 6), but none of the six jobs can be skipped.

这条链不是新说法。Brooks 在 1986 年描述当时的经典做法时，把它切成五块（Brooks 1986 第二节和图 1）：传感，把传感数据变成对世界的描述，规划，执行任务，控制电机，和这里的六层大同小异。信息从环境经过传感流进机器人，再经过动作回到环境，"闭合了反馈环"。他还在括号里补了一句：这些单元的大多数实现，内部也都有反馈环。前半句是六层；括号里那半句，就是这一步后面要讲的几级环。Brooks 画这条链，其实是为了反对它、提出另一种分法，第 6 步再讲。The chain is not a new idea. When Brooks described the classic approach of his day in 1986, he cut it into five pieces (section II and figure 1 of Brooks 1986): sensing, mapping sensor data into a description of the world, planning, task execution, motor control, much like the six layers here. Information flows from the environment into the robot through sensing and back to the environment through action, “closing the feedback loop”. He added in parentheses that most implementations of these units have internal feedback loops of their own. The first half is the six layers; the remark in parentheses is the loop levels this step comes to later. Brooks drew this chain in order to argue against it and propose another way of dividing a robot, which step 6 returns to.

一个完整的环：扫地机器人的一个轮子A complete loop: one wheel of a robot vacuum

环（loop）是这一篇的另一个主角。说机器人在"控制"一样东西，意思总是同一件事：心里有一个想要的值，手里有一个测到的值，两者之差叫误差；机器人的每一个动作，都是为了让这个误差变小。测到的值是从传感器一路送回来的，这条往回送的路叫反馈（feedback）。有了它，"动作 → 世界变了 → 测量 → 新的动作"才连成一个圈，这叫闭环（closed loop）；不看测量、只按事先算好的命令去做，叫开环（open loop）。闭环控制离不开这两样：没有误差，就不知道该纠正什么；没有反馈，就算不出误差。The loop is this page’s other main subject. When a robot “controls” something, it always means the same thing: there is a value it wants and a value it measures, and the difference between them is the error; every move the robot makes is meant to shrink that error. The measured value is carried back from a sensor, and that path back is called feedback. With it, “act → the world changes → measure → act again” closes into a circle, called a closed loop; acting on commands worked out in advance, without looking at any measurement, is called open loop. Closed-loop control cannot do without either: without an error it does not know what to correct; without feedback there is no way to compute the error.

先看一个最小、最完整的环。扫地机器人往前走，靠两个轮子各自转到规定的速度。拿右轮来说，外面一级的环要它转出 0.3 m/s。可地板有硬有软，电池有满有亏，同样的电压在不同时候转出来的速度并不一样。所以它不靠"事先算好电压给上去"，而是一直在比：想要多少，测到多少，差多少补多少。教科书把这件事画成一张标准的方框图（下面交互里的那张），图上每个零件都有名字：Start with the smallest complete loop. A robot vacuum moves by making each of its two wheels turn at a set speed. Take the right wheel: the loop one level out wants 0.3 m/s from it. But floors are hard or soft and the battery is full or flat, so the same voltage gives different speeds at different times. That is why the vacuum does not “work out the voltage and apply it”; it keeps comparing: how much it wants, how much it measures, and makes up the difference. 误差等于想要的减去测到的。电压分成两份：一份和眼下的误差成正比，一份和到目前为止所有误差的累加成正比。The error is what you want minus what you measure. The voltage comes in two parts: one proportional to the error right now, one proportional to the sum of all the errors so far.

只看眼下的误差（只有 Kp 那一项）有个毛病：轮子要转出 0.3 m/s，电机上得一直有大约 7.5 V；可这一项给的电压和误差成正比，误差一旦变成 0，电压也变成 0。所以它只能停在"还差一点、才有一点电压"的地方：这里会停在 0.24 m/s，差的 0.06 m/s 乘上 Kp 正好是 6 V，而 6 V 只够转出 0.24 m/s。第二项把过去的误差一点点攒起来：只要还差一点，它就一直往上加，直到误差真的归零；归零以后它不再变，正好"记住"了需要的那 7.5 V。压上地毯以后需要的电压变大，它就再往上攒。Looking only at the error right now (the Kp term alone) has a flaw: to turn at 0.3 m/s the motor needs about 7.5 V all the time, but this term’s voltage is proportional to the error, so once the error is 0 the voltage is 0 too. So it can only settle where “a little short gives a little voltage”: here it settles at 0.24 m/s, where the 0.06 m/s shortfall times Kp is exactly 6 V, and 6 V is only enough for 0.24 m/s. The second term saves up past errors bit by bit: as long as anything is left over, it keeps adding, until the error really is zero; then it stops changing, having “remembered” exactly the 7.5 V needed. Roll onto the carpet and more voltage is needed, so it saves up some more.

像开车想保持 60 km/h：比 60 慢一点，就多踩一点油门，这是比例项；要是上坡一直慢，你会越踩越深，直到速度回到 60，脚就停在那个深度，这是积分项。Like driving at a steady 60 km/h: a little under 60, you press the accelerator a little more, which is the proportional term; going uphill and staying slow, you press deeper and deeper until you are back at 60, and your foot then stays at that depth, which is the integral term.

r = 0.30 m/s，测到 ym = 0.27 m/s，所以 e = 0.03 m/s。Kp = 100 V/(m/s)，比例项给 3 V；积分项还记着平常的约 7.5 V，加起来约 10.5 V，把轮子往上推。等误差回到 0，比例项归零，电压只剩积分项那约 7.5 V：正好是硬地板上维持 0.3 m/s 要的电压。With r = 0.30 m/s and a measured ym = 0.27 m/s, e = 0.03 m/s. 控制器从来看不到真实的轮速 y，只看得到 ym。测量差多少，控制就跟着错多少：编码器的换算写错了 10%，轮子就会被稳稳地控制在错 10% 的速度上，而误差显示为 0。The controller never sees the true wheel speed y, only ym. However far off the measurement is, the control is off by as much: get the encoder conversion 10% wrong and the wheel is held steadily at a speed 10% wrong, while the error reads 0.

反馈是怎么建立的How feedback is built

反馈是闭环的精髓，可它不是随手接一根线就有了。要把现实送回比较器、和想要的放在一起减，得做好五件事：Feedback is the heart of a closed loop, but it does not appear just by connecting a wire. To carry reality back to the comparator and subtract it from what you want, five things have to be right:

  1. 测对的东西。要管轮速，就得测轮速。电压代替不了轮速：同样 7.5 V，在地毯上只转得出 0.18 m/s。测的必须是你想控制的那个量，或者能可靠地换算成它的量。Measure the right thing. To control wheel speed you have to measure wheel speed. Voltage is no substitute: the same 7.5 V gives only 0.18 m/s on a carpet. What you measure must be the quantity you want to control, or something that converts reliably into it.
  2. 换算成同一种量、同一个单位。编码器交出来的不是速度，是一串脉冲。轮子周长 0.22 m，一圈 360 个脉冲，一个脉冲就是 0.61 mm；数一数 20 毫秒里来了几个，乘上 0.61 mm，再除以 20 毫秒，才得到 m/s，才能和 r 相减。外面几级的环更费事：要比较"在不在路线上"，得先用激光雷达和地图算出位置，还得和路线放进同一个坐标系。Convert it into the same quantity, in the same units. The encoder hands over pulses, not a speed. The wheel has a circumference of 0.22 m and gives 360 pulses a turn, so one pulse is 0.61 mm; count the pulses that arrive in 20 ms, multiply by 0.61 mm and divide by 20 ms, and only then do you have m/s that can be subtracted from r. Loops further out have more work to do: to ask “am I on the route?”, the position must first be worked out from the lidar and the map, and put in the same frame of reference as the route.
  3. 按固定的节拍比较。比较器每 1 毫秒做一次 r − ym。环就是在这一次次减法里闭上的：电压改了，轮速变了，下一次数到的脉冲变了，误差也跟着变。Compare on a fixed beat. The comparator computes r − ym once every millisecond. The loop closes through these repeated subtractions: the voltage changes, the speed changes, the next pulse count changes, and the error changes with it.
  4. 认清测量的毛病。脉冲只能一个一个地数：窗口太短，测到的速度一跳一跳，这是噪声；窗口太长，测到的是过去一段时间的平均，这是延迟。噪声和延迟都会原封不动地混进误差，控制器分不出哪一部分是真的。Know the measurement’s flaws. Pulses can only be counted whole: with too short a window the measured speed jumps around, which is noise; with too long a window it is the average over a stretch of the past, which is delay. Noise and delay both pass straight into the error, and the controller cannot tell which part is real.
  5. 符号必须是负的。是 r 减 ym，不是加。这叫负反馈：测到的越大，误差越小，命令越小，把系统往回拉。接反了就成了正反馈：轮子越快，"误差"越大，电压越加，轮子越快，直到电压顶到头。注意"负"说的是绕环一圈的总效果：电机的两根线接反、编码器的方向装反，比较器里写的虽然还是减号，整个环也成了正反馈。The sign must be negative. It is r minus ym, not plus. This is negative feedback: the larger the measurement, the smaller the error and the smaller the command, pulling the system back. Wire it the other way and it becomes positive feedback: the faster the wheel, the bigger the “error”, the more voltage, the faster the wheel, until the voltage hits its limit. Note that “negative” describes the effect all the way round the loop: swap the motor’s two wires or mount the encoder backwards, and even with a minus sign still in the comparator the whole loop has become positive feedback.

这个小环里有三件事，后面每一个环都会再遇到：This small loop holds three lessons that every later loop repeats:

  • 控制器只认误差。它不知道地毯有多厚、电池还剩多少电，也不需要知道：这些都会变成误差，误差一出现，它就去消灭。The controller cares only about the error. It does not know how thick the carpet is or how much charge is left, and does not need to: all of it turns into error, and as soon as an error appears it sets about removing it.
  • 它看到的是测量，不是现实。测得慢，它就纠正得晚；测得糙，它就跟着抖。20 毫秒左右的窗口是个折中：再短太抖，再长太晚。It sees the measurement, not reality. Measure late and it corrects late; measure coarsely and it jitters along. A window of about 20 ms is a compromise: shorter is too jittery, longer is too late.
  • 执行器有极限。目标定得太高、地毯又太厚时，电压顶到 14.4 V 也追不上。反馈能发现误差，却变不出力气来。Actuators have limits. With the target set too high and the carpet too thick, even 14.4 V cannot catch up. Feedback can find an error, but it cannot conjure up strength.

一台机器人里不止一个环A robot has more than one loop

轮速环只管轮速。它能把两个轮子都稳稳地转到 0.3 m/s，可它不知道机器人是不是走在路线上：轮子在水渍上打滑、机身被撞歪，编码器照样数到 0.3 m/s 的脉冲，它的误差一直是 0。要发现"走歪了"，得测另一样东西：机身在房间里的位置和朝向。要发现"厨房还没扫完"，又得测另一样东西：哪些地方已经扫过。The wheel loop only looks after wheel speed. It can hold both wheels steadily at 0.3 m/s, yet it has no idea whether the robot is on its route: if the wheels skid on a wet patch or the body is knocked crooked, the encoders still count the pulses of 0.3 m/s and its error stays at 0. To find out the robot has drifted off course, something else must be measured: the body’s position and heading in the room. To find out the kitchen is not finished, something else again: which parts have been cleaned.

每多一样要管的东西，就多一个环。这些环不是平起平坐的，而是一个套一个：外面那个环的输出，正好是里面那个环的目标。这种结构叫级联（cascade），套在一起的每个环叫一级，从最外面数起。同一级上也可以并排好几个环，比如左右两个轮子各有一个轮速环。扫地机器人身体里有三级环：Every extra thing to look after means another loop. These loops are not equals sitting side by side; they nest one inside another: the output of the outer loop is exactly the target of the inner one. This structure is called a cascade, and each loop in the nest is one level, counted from the outside in. 第 1 步 · 闭环Step 1 · Closing the loop

一个环怎么闭上How a loop closes

第 0 步的轮速环让你看了一个完整的环长什么样。这一步换一个更常见的环，看控制器拿到误差以后，力矩到底该怎么算：一个关节，一个电机，手臂末端挂着一个重物，目标是让手臂停在 30°。Step 0’s wheel loop showed what a complete loop looks like. This step takes a more common loop and looks at what the controller should actually do with the error to get a torque: one joint, one motor, a weight hanging from the end of the arm, and the goal of holding the arm at 30°.

最直接的想法是开环：事先算好"托住这个重物、停在 30° 要多大的力矩"，让电机一直出这么大的力矩，完事。很多 3D 打印机就是这么干的：步进电机发多少步，就当自己转了多少，从不检查。喷头被卡一下，它会"丢步"，打出来的件从那一层开始整体错位，它自己毫不知情。The most direct idea is open loop: work out in advance how much torque holds this weight at 30°, have the motor produce that torque all the time, and that is that. Many 3D printers do exactly this: however many steps the stepper motor is sent, it assumes it has turned that far, and never checks. If the nozzle catches, it “skips steps”, and everything printed from that layer up is shifted, without the printer knowing.

闭环（closed loop）多做了一件事：一直测量，一直纠正。关节上装一个编码器（测转角的传感器），每个周期读一次角度，和目标比一比，差多少就补多少。电机转了，编码器的读数就变了，下一个周期的误差也跟着变：测量、计算、出力、物理世界变化、再测量，这个圈是穿过物理世界闭上的。最常见的补法是 PD 控制：A closed loop does one more thing: it keeps measuring and keeps correcting. An encoder on the joint (a sensor that measures rotation) reads the angle every cycle, compares it with the target, and makes up whatever is missing. When the motor turns, the encoder reading changes, and so does the error in the next cycle: measure, compute, push, the physical world changes, measure again. The loop closes through the physical world. The most common way to make up the difference is PD control:

τ = Kp (q* − q) − Kd q̇

电机这一刻的力矩，等于"离目标还差多少"乘 Kp，再减去"转得多快"乘 Kd。The motor’s torque right now equals “how far from the target” times Kp, minus “how fast it is turning” times Kd.

开环全凭事先算好，算错了、被推了，它都不知道。闭环把测量接了回来：只要离目标还有差距，就一直有力矩往回拉。只有这一项还不够：手臂被拉回目标时还带着速度，会冲过头、来回晃，所以再减去一项和速度成正比的力矩，提前刹车。Open loop relies entirely on the calculation made in advance: if it was wrong, or the arm gets pushed, it never knows. A closed loop feeds the measurement back in: as long as there is any gap to the target, there is torque pulling the arm back. That term alone is not enough: the arm arrives back at the target still moving, overshoots and swings back and forth, so a second term proportional to the speed is subtracted to brake in advance.

这行公式里没有"重物有多重"：闭环不需要知道负载，只要能测到结果。代价是：要托住重物，弹簧得先被拉开一点才有力气，所以纯 PD 控制托重物时总会下垂一点。下垂多少？下垂角（弧度）≈ 重力力矩 ÷ Kp。There is nothing in this formula about how heavy the weight is: a closed loop does not need to know the load, only to measure the result. The price: to hold the weight up, the spring must first be stretched a little before it has the strength, so plain PD control always sags slightly under a load. By how much? 这个小实验里有闭环最重要的三件事：This small experiment holds the three most important things about closed loops:

  1. 闭环不怕"不知道"：负载变了、被推了，它都能拉回来，因为它看的是结果，不是事先的假设。A closed loop does not mind not knowing: if the load changes or the arm is pushed, it pulls back anyway, because it looks at the result rather than at assumptions made in advance.
  2. 纯反馈有代价：误差先出现，力矩才出现。托 1 kg 的重物停在 30°：手臂自重 0.5 kg，重心在中点 0.2 m 处，重力力矩 ≈ (0.5 × 0.2 + 1 × 0.4) × 9.8 × cos 30° ≈ 4.2 N·m；Kp = 50 时，下垂 ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 ≈ 0.085 rad，约 5°。Kp 调大，下垂变小，可阻尼不变时，刚度越大越容易晃；下一步还会看到另一个限制：环跑得慢、测量来得晚时，增益只能调得更软。另一个常见的办法是再加一项积分（I），第 0 步的轮子用的就是它：把误差随时间累加起来，只要还下垂，这一项就越攒越大，直到把下垂顶回去。P、I、D 三项合起来，就是最常用的 PID 控制。积分项反应慢，攒过头还会冲过目标，所以能算出来的重力，最好直接算好了给（下一条）。Pure feedback has a price: the error has to appear before the torque does. Holding 1 kg at 30°: the arm itself weighs 0.5 kg with its centre of mass halfway along, at 0.2 m, so the gravity torque ≈ (0.5 × 0.2 + 1 × 0.4) × 9.8 × cos 30° ≈ 4.2 N·m; with Kp = 50 the sag ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 ≈ 0.085 rad, about 5°. Turn Kp up and the sag shrinks, but with the same damping a stiffer spring swings more easily; the next step shows another limit: when the loop runs slowly or its measurements arrive late, the gains have to be softer. Another common fix is to add an integral term (I), the one the wheel in step 0 uses: it adds up the error over time, so as long as the arm still sags, this term keeps growing until it pushes the sag away. P, I and D together make the most widely used controller, PID. The integral term is slow, and once it has built up too much it can carry the arm past the target, so gravity, which can be calculated, is better worked out and supplied directly (next point).
  3. 已知的交给前馈，意外的交给反馈：重力、惯性这些能算出来的，事先算好直接给；算不准的部分和突发的扰动，交给反馈去补。真实的机械臂控制器几乎都是这么搭的。Hand the known part to feedforward, the surprises to feedback: whatever can be calculated, such as gravity and inertia, is worked out in advance and applied directly; whatever cannot be calculated well, and any sudden disturbance, is left for feedback to make up. Almost every real robot arm controller is built this way.

开环的好处What open loop is good at

  • 简单，不需要传感器Simple, no sensors needed
  • 不会因为反馈而振荡Never oscillates because of feedback
  • 模型准、扰动小的场合够用，比如步进电机、风扇Good enough when the model is accurate and disturbances are small, as in stepper motors and fans

开环的问题Where open loop falls down

  • 模型算错了、负载变了，它都不知道If the model is wrong or the load changes, it never finds out
  • 被推一下就回不去One push and it cannot get back
  • 误差会一点点累积Errors pile up bit by bit

闭环也不是免费的：它需要传感器，而且一旦测量来得太晚、太稀，"纠正"本身就会把系统推向振荡。这就引出了下一个问题：一个环要跑多快才够？Closed loops are not free either: they need sensors, and if the measurements arrive too late or too seldom, the “correction” itself pushes the system into oscillation. Which raises the next question: how fast does a loop have to run?

  • 环要比它管的东西快一个数量级左右。工程上常说：采样频率至少是闭环带宽的 10 倍。带宽就是一个环能跟得上的最快变化，单位也是 Hz。A loop should be about an order of magnitude faster than what it controls. The current in a motor coil changes on a time scale of a fraction of a millisecond to a few milliseconds (roughly the coil’s inductance divided by its resistance), so the current loop runs tens of thousands of times a second; a joint takes tens of milliseconds to get moving, so servo loops run thousands of times a second; a body falls or slips in fractions of a second, so whole-body control runs hundreds of times a second; a person walking down the corridor takes seconds, so perception and planning at ten-odd times a second is enough. 这也解释了为什么最快的环都放在离电机最近的芯片上，而不是绕到网络另一头的大电脑上：每多绕一段路，就多一段延迟。工程上说的实时（real-time），讲的也不是"算得快"，而是"保证每次都按时算完"。每次运行的时刻忽早忽晚，叫抖动（jitter）；偶尔整整晚了一拍，叫超时（overrun）。对控制环来说，这两样都像延迟突然变大，比平均慢一点更危险。It also explains why the fastest loops sit on the chips closest to the motors rather than on the big computer at the far end of a network: every extra stretch of road adds delay. What engineers call real-time does not mean “computes fast” either, but “always finishes on time, guaranteed”. When the moment each run starts wanders early and late, that is called jitter; when a run occasionally misses its slot altogether, that is an overrun. For a control loop, both are like a sudden jump in delay, and more dangerous than being a little slow on average.

第 3 步 · 级联Step 3 · Cascades

环套环：无人机的四级串联Loops inside loops: a drone’s four-level cascade

第 0 步已经看到，扫地机器人是三级环一个套一个。这一步把环套环的道理讲透，例子换成一个离了它就飞不起来的机器人：四旋翼。这里只看它身体里的四级，也就是第 0 步大表里的 ② 到 ⑤；最外面的任务环先不管。Step 0 showed the robot vacuum’s three loop levels nested one inside another. This step works through why loops are nested, using a robot that could not fly at all without this nesting: the quadrotor. Only the four levels inside its body are covered here, ② to ⑤ in step 0’s big table; the task loop outside them is left aside.

四旋翼无人机只能做一件事：改变几个螺旋桨的转速。可你给它的命令是"飞到那棵树旁边"。从"位置"到"转速"，中间隔着好几级：要往右飞，机身得先向右倾斜，让推力有一部分朝右；要倾斜，机身得先转起来；要转起来，左右两边的推力就得不一样。A quadrotor drone can do exactly one thing: change how fast its propellers spin. Yet the command you give it is “fly over to that tree”. Between “position” and “propeller speed” there are several levels: to fly right, the body must first lean right so that part of the thrust points right; to lean, the body must first start turning; to turn, the left and right propellers must push differently.

于是控制器被拆成一串环，每个环只管一件事，它算出的"要多少"，就是下一个环的目标：So the controller is split into a chain of loops, each looking after one thing, and the “how much” that one loop works out is the next loop’s target:

v* = kx (x* − x)a* = kv (v* − v)， θ* ≈ a* ∕ ga* = kv (v* − v), θ* ≈ a* ∕ gω* = kθ (θ* − θ)ΔF = kω (ω* − ω)

每一行都是同一个句式："要的 = 系数 ×（外面一级要的 − 实际测到的）"。每一行算出来的"要"，交给下一行当目标：位置环要一个速度，速度环要一个倾角，姿态环要一个角速度，角速度环要一个推力差。Every line follows the same pattern: “what I want = gain × (what the level outside wants − what was actually measured)”. What each line wants becomes the target of the next: the position loop wants a velocity, the velocity loop a tilt, the attitude loop a turning rate, the rate loop a difference in thrust.

位置没法直接控制，只能通过速度；速度要靠倾斜机身；倾斜要靠转动；转动要靠两边推力不一样。拆成一串以后，每一级都只是一个简单的比例控制，就像第 1 步 PD 里的 P 那一项。还有一个好处：内环能就地消灭扰动。一阵风把机身吹歪了，角速度环几毫秒内就开始纠正；等慢得多的位置环察觉到，机身早已被扶住，它只需要把漂开的一小段拉回来。Position cannot be controlled directly, only through velocity; velocity takes tilting the body; tilting takes turning; turning takes the two sides pushing differently. Once split into a chain, every level is just a simple proportional controller, like the P term of the PD controller in step 1. There is another bonus: inner loops deal with disturbances on the spot. When a gust knocks the body askew, the rate loop starts correcting within milliseconds; by the time the much slower position loop notices, the body has long been caught, and all it has to do is pull back the short distance the drone has drifted.

像一家公司：老板只说"去那儿"（位置），经理换算成"多快走"（速度），组长换算成"身子歪多少"（倾角），工人换算成"转多快"（角速度），最后落到"两边各使多大劲"（推力）。越往下，命令越具体，反应越快。Like a company: the boss only says “go over there” (position), the manager turns that into “how fast to walk” (velocity), the team leader into “how far to lean” (tilt), the worker into “how fast to turn” (turning rate), and it all ends up as “how hard each side pushes” (thrust). The further down, the more concrete the orders and the faster the reactions.

目标在右边 2 m，kx = 1 /s：要的速度 2 m/s。现在悬停不动，kv = 2.5 /s：要的加速度 5 m/s²，要的倾角约 5 ∕ 9.8 ≈ 0.5 rad，将近 30°。现在机身是平的，kθ = 8 /s：要的角速度约 4 rad/s，每秒转 230° 左右。角速度环再把"要转这么快"变成左右的推力差。The target is 2 m to the right and kx = 1 /s: the velocity asked for is 2 m/s. The drone is hovering still and kv = 2.5 /s: the acceleration asked for is 5 m/s², so the tilt asked for is about 5 ∕ 9.8 ≈ 0.5 rad, nearly 30°. The body is level and kθ = 8 /s: the turning rate asked for is about 4 rad/s, roughly 230° a second. The rate loop then turns “turn this fast” into a difference between left and right thrust.

内环要比外环快得多：内环的带宽（跟得上的最快变化）通常是外环的几倍到十倍。这里的"快"指反应快，不一定是每秒跑的次数多：下面表里的位置环和速度环都是每秒 50 次，速度环的增益更大，反应就更快。外环要的变化比内环能跟上的慢得多，内环总能很快把外环的命令执行到位，外环就可以把内环当成"说到做到"的执行器。两级快慢差不多时，就会互相"打架"：外环还没看到上一次命令的效果，又加码了。Inner loops must be much faster than outer ones: an inner loop’s bandwidth (the fastest change it can follow) is usually several to ten times the outer loop’s. “Faster” here means quicker to respond, not necessarily more runs per second: in the table below, the position and velocity loops both run 50 times a second, but the velocity loop has the larger gain, so it responds faster. The changes the outer loop asks for are much slower than what the inner loop can follow, so the inner loop always carries out the outer loop’s command quickly, and the outer loop can treat the inner loop as an actuator that does exactly what it is told. When two levels run at similar speeds they start to fight: the outer loop has not yet seen the effect of its last command and piles on more.

几件事值得注意：A few things are worth noticing:

  • 扰动在最近的那一级被消灭。风一吹，最先动起来的是角速度环：机身刚开始歪，它下一次运行就加大两边的推力差顶回去，机身只歪了 3° 左右。风还在把机身往旁边推，速度环稍后发现，让机身迎着风倾斜；被吹偏的那一小段位置，要靠慢得多的位置环花两三秒拉回来。A disturbance is dealt with by the nearest level. When the wind blows, the first to move is the rate loop: as soon as the body starts to tip, its next run raises the thrust difference to push back, and the body tips by only about 3°. The wind is still pushing the drone sideways; the velocity loop notices a little later and leans the body into the wind; the short distance the drone has been blown off course is pulled back by the much slower position loop over two or three seconds.
  • 最里面的环最怕慢。角速度环降到每秒 20 次左右，两次纠正之间机身已经转过了头，一直大幅摇晃；降到每秒 15 次以下，就会翻：它管的东西变得最快，所以它最需要快。真实的飞控里，PX4 的角速度环也是最内的一环；航拍和科研用的飞控每秒跑几百次起，竞速穿越机用的 Betaflight 常跑每秒 2000 到 8000 次。The innermost loop suffers most from being slow. Drop the rate loop to about 20 runs a second and the body turns too far between two corrections, so it keeps rocking widely; below about 15 runs a second it flips: it controls the thing that changes fastest, so it needs speed most. In real flight controllers the rate loop is also the innermost one, as in PX4; camera and research drones run it from a few hundred times a second, and racing quads on Betaflight often run it 2,000 to 8,000 times a second.
  • 外环太急，内环跟不上。位置环的增益太大，它要的速度变得比里面几级能跟上的还快，于是冲过目标再折回来。When the outer loop is in a hurry, the inner loops cannot keep up. Make the position-loop gain too high and the velocity it asks for changes faster than the levels inside can follow, so it overshoots the target and comes back.
  • 少一级，就少一种"刹车"。关掉角速度环，没人专门管"转得多快"，姿态环只能按角度差给推力差，就像第 1 步里把 Kd 拖到 0，只剩弹簧、没有阻尼器。第 1 步的手臂只是来回晃个不停；这里电机的推力要 20 毫秒左右才跟上命令，每次都推晚了一点，晃动就越来越大。One level fewer, one brake fewer. Turn off the rate loop and nobody looks after how fast the body is turning; the attitude loop can only turn the angle error straight into a thrust difference, like dragging Kd to 0 in step 1, which leaves a spring with no damper. In step 1 the arm just keeps swinging; here the motors take about 20 ms to deliver the thrust they are told to, so every push comes a little late, and the swinging grows.

这种环套环就是第 0 步说的级联控制（cascade control），在机器人里到处都是。机械臂每个关节的驱动器里，是位置环套速度环、速度环套电流环；四足机器人是 MPC 套全身控制、全身控制套关节 PD、关节 PD 套电流环；汽车是轨迹跟踪套转向角、转向角套转向电机的电流。最里面那一环，几乎永远是电流。These loops within loops are the cascade control of step 0, and they are everywhere in robots. In each joint drive of an arm, a position loop wraps a velocity loop and the velocity loop wraps a current loop; in a quadruped, MPC wraps whole-body control, which wraps joint PD, which wraps the current loop; in a car, trajectory tracking wraps the steering angle, which wraps the steering motor’s current. The innermost loop is almost always current.

第 4 步 · 执行Step 4 · Actuation

最里面一环：力矩就是电流The innermost loop: torque is current

所有电动机器人的最后一站都一样：把电变成力。电机的力矩和流过线圈的电流成正比，τ = Kt · i，所以"控制力矩"说到底是"控制电流"。可驱动器手里只有开关：电源要么接通，要么断开，没有"接通一半"。怎么用开关得到想要的电流？Every electric robot ends at the same place: turning electricity into force. A motor’s torque is proportional to the current through its coils, τ = Kt · i, so “controlling torque” really means “controlling current”. But all a drive has in its hands is a switch: the supply is either connected or disconnected, never “half connected”. How do you get the current you want out of a switch?

V̄ = D · VbusΔi ≈ Vbus · D (1 − D) ∕ (L · f)

平均电压等于占空比乘电源电压；电流的波动（纹波），和电源电压成正比，和线圈的电感、开关频率成反比。The average voltage equals the duty cycle times the supply voltage; the current’s fluctuation (the ripple) is proportional to the supply voltage and inversely proportional to the coil’s inductance and the switching frequency.

开关得足够快，线圈来不及跟着每一次开关变化，只能感受到平均值，这就是 PWM（脉宽调制）：用"接通时间占多少比例"来调平均电压。开关越快，电流越平；可每开关一次都有一点损耗，开关管也会发热，所以不能无限快。常见的折中是每秒两万到几万次。If the switching is fast enough, the coil cannot follow every switch and only feels the average. That is PWM (pulse-width modulation): set the average voltage through the fraction of time the switch is on. The faster the switching, the smoother the current; but every switch costs a little energy and heats the transistors, so it cannot be infinitely fast. The usual compromise is twenty to several tens of thousands of times a second.

像用手指飞快地按水龙头：按得足够快，水管里的水不会一冲一停，而是平均出一个中等的流量。按得越快，水流越平稳。Like flicking a tap on and off very fast with a finger: fast enough, and the water in the pipe no longer surges and stops but settles into a medium flow. The faster you flick, the steadier the flow.

24 V 电源、占空比 50%：平均 12 V。线圈电阻 1 Ω、电感 1 mH，电机不转时，平均电流约 12 A（转起来以后，电机像发电机一样产生一个顶着电源的电压，叫反电动势，电流会小一些）；Kt = 0.1 N·m/A 时，力矩 1.2 N·m。开关频率 20 kHz 时纹波约 24 × 0.5 × 0.5 ∕ (0.001 × 20000) = 0.3 A；降到 1 kHz，纹波约 6 A，力矩跟着一抖一抖，电机还会发出 1 kHz 的尖叫。A 24 V supply at 50% duty: 12 V on average. A coil with 1 Ω resistance and 1 mH inductance carries about 12 A on average while the motor is not turning (once it turns, the motor acts like a generator and produces a voltage that opposes the supply, called back-EMF, and the current is smaller); with Kt = 0.1 N·m/A, that is 1.2 N·m of torque. Switching at 20 kHz, the ripple is about 24 × 0.5 × 0.5 ∕ (0.001 × 20000) = 0.3 A; drop to 1 kHz and the ripple is about 6 A, the torque shudders with it, and the motor whines at 1 kHz.

人耳能听到大约 20 Hz 到 20 kHz 的声音，所以很多驱动器把开关频率放在 20 kHz 以上。电流环每个开关周期测一次电流、改一次占空比，所以电流环也是每秒几万次。People can hear sounds from about 20 Hz to 20 kHz, so many drives switch above 20 kHz. The current loop measures the current and updates the duty once every switching period, so it too runs tens of thousands of times a second.

电流环是整台机器人里最快、也最"低调"的一环：它把电池电压的波动、线圈的电阻和电感、电机转起来以后产生的反电动势统统挡在里面，让外面的每一级都可以假装"我要多少力矩，就有多少力矩"。第 3 步说外环可以把内环当成"说到做到"的执行器，做得最彻底的就是这一环。The current loop is the fastest and the most self-effacing loop in the whole robot: it walls off the sagging battery voltage, the coil’s resistance and inductance, and the back-EMF a motor generates once it spins, so that every level outside it can pretend “whatever torque I ask for, I get”. Step 3 said an outer loop can treat the inner loop as an actuator that does what it is told; this loop does that most thoroughly.

真实的无刷电机有三组线圈。驱动器要按转子此刻的角度（编码器测），算出三组线圈各该通多少电流，让线圈产生的磁场始终和转子的磁场垂直：就像推门时总是垂直门板用力，同样的力气，门转得最动。这叫磁场定向控制（FOC）。它先把三组线圈的电流换算到跟着转子一起转的坐标系里，变成出力矩的 q 轴电流和不出力矩的 d 轴电流（通常控制为 0），各用一个上面这样的电流环；算出的电压再换算回三组线圈的占空比。A real brushless motor has three sets of coils. Using the rotor’s angle at this instant (measured by the encoder), the drive works out how much current each set should carry so that the magnetic field of the coils always stays at right angles to the rotor’s field: like always pushing a door at right angles to the door, which turns it most for the same effort. This is called field-oriented control (FOC). It first converts the three coil currents into a frame that turns with the rotor, giving a q-axis current, which makes torque, and a d-axis current, which does not (usually held at 0); each gets a current loop like the one above, and the resulting voltages are converted back into duty cycles for the three sets of coils.

电机转得快、力矩小，关节要的是转得慢、力矩大，中间靠减速器换：机械臂的关节常用上百比一的谐波减速器，力大、精确、几乎没有间隙；MIT Cheetah 系列和不少四足机器人却只用 6 到 10 比 1，好让腿落地时能被地面推回去、顺势"让一下"，这叫反向可驱动（backdrivable）。减速比小，地面传回来的力也能直接从电流里读出来，电机本身就成了力传感器。A motor spins fast with little torque, while a joint needs to turn slowly with a lot of torque; a gearbox trades one for the other. Robot arm joints usually have harmonic gears with ratios over 100 to 1: strong, precise and almost free of backlash. The MIT Cheetah robots and many other quadrupeds use only 6 to 10 to 1, so that when a leg lands, the ground can push it back and it can “give” a little. This is called being backdrivable. With a low gear ratio, the force coming back from the ground can be read straight off the current, and the motor itself becomes a force sensor.

从上往下，每个程序把自己的输出交给下一个：任务程序给出"下一个要去的点"；全局规划在地图上搜出一条路线（一串路点；常用 A* 算法，在网格地图上一格一格地找最短的路）；局部规划沿着这条路线，结合激光雷达刚看到的东西，算出此刻该走多快、转多快；底盘控制把"走多快、转多快"换算成左右两个轮子的速度；轮速环让轮子真的转到这个速度。从下往上，测量一路传回来：轮子的编码器、激光雷达的扫描、碰撞开关。From the top down, each program hands its output to the next: the task program names the next point to go to; global planning searches the map for a route (a string of waypoints, often found with the A* algorithm, which searches a grid map cell by cell for the shortest way); local planning follows that route, takes into account what the lidar has just seen, and works out how fast to drive and turn right now; the base controller turns “how fast to drive and turn” into speeds for the left and right wheels; the wheel speed loops make the wheels actually turn at those speeds. From the bottom up, measurements travel back: the wheel encoders, the lidar sweeps, the bumper switch.

局部规划只关心"整台机器人怎么动"，不关心它有几个、什么样的轮子；把意图翻译成具体执行器的语言，是底盘控制的事。换成四个麦克纳姆轮（能横着走的轮子），只要换掉底盘控制这个程序，上面一行代码都不用改。这是按分工拆开的好处：每个程序只和相邻的程序说话，说的内容（接口）是约定好的。Local planning only cares how the robot as a whole should move, not how many wheels it has or what kind; translating that intention into the language of particular actuators is the base controller’s job. 数一数第 0 步里的几级环：扫地机器人 3 级，机械臂和汽车 4 级，四旋翼和四足各 5 级；再加上感知、规划这些按自己节拍跑的程序，上面的图里每种机器人有 6 到 7 行。这个数不是规定出来的：机器人要同时应付几种快慢不同的时间尺度，就有几级环。无人机的机身天生不稳，零点几秒就会翻，所以最内的控制环必须极快；扫地机器人停下来想一会儿也没关系，它的环可以更少、更慢；汽车的转向和刹车本身就有零点几秒的惯性，跟踪轨迹的那一级每秒几十到一百次就够了。Count the loop levels from step 0: three in the robot vacuum, four in the arm and the car, five each in the quadrotor and the quadruped; add the programs that run to their own beat, such as perception and planning, and each robot has six or seven rows in the chart above. That number is not decreed: a robot has as many levels of loops as it has time scales of different speeds to cope with at once. A drone’s body is unstable and can flip within a fraction of a second, so its innermost control loop must be extremely fast; a robot vacuum can stop and think for a while, so its loops can be fewer and slower; a car’s steering and brakes have a fraction of a second of inertia of their own, so the level that tracks the trajectory needs to run only tens to a hundred times a second.

机器人该怎么分，上世纪八九十年代就有了几种经典的说法。Albus 在美国国家标准与技术研究院提出的 RCS 参考架构，按时间尺度分级：最里面的伺服级只规划 0.03 秒，往外依次是 0.3 秒、3 秒、30 秒，大约每级差十倍，"每一级都闭合反馈环"。每一级里又都有同样的几个模块：感知处理和世界建模（相当于感知与估计）、价值判断（给各个方案算代价和好处）、行为生成（决策、规划加控制）。这正是第 0 步那张大表：级是行，模块是列。今天用 ROS 2 搭起来的系统，大体还是这副"快环在里、慢环在外"的骨架。By the 1980s and 1990s there were already several classic answers to how a robot should be divided up. The RCS reference architecture Albus proposed at the US National Institute of Standards and Technology divides by time scale: the innermost servo level plans only 0.03 s ahead, and the levels outside it plan 0.3 s, 3 s and 30 s, roughly a factor of ten per level, with “reactive feedback loops closed at every level”. And every level contains the same modules: sensory processing and world modelling (much like perception and estimation), value judgement (weighing the costs and benefits of candidate plans) and behaviour generation (decision, planning and control). That is exactly step 0’s big table: the levels are the rows and the modules the columns. Systems built with ROS 2 today still follow much the same skeleton, with fast loops inside and slow loops outside.

Brooks 的包容架构（1986）又是另一种分法：按能力分层。这里的"层"既不是六层，也不是几级环，而是一种种行为：最底层只管"别撞上东西"，往上叠"四处走走"，再叠"去探索"；每一层都是从传感器直接到动作的一个短环，每加一层就多一种能力，下面的层照常运行，上层需要时可以压过下层的输出。它当时是为了反对"先感知、再规划、后行动"一条长链提出的，也就是第 0 步那六层一层不落地串成一条长链。今天的机器人里也常能看到它的影子：上一步的碰撞开关一压下就后退，不等上面的程序慢慢想。Brooks’s subsumption architecture (1986) is yet another way to divide things: layers by ability. Its “layers” are neither the six layers nor loop levels but behaviours: the bottom layer only makes sure the robot does not run into things; on top of it comes “wander about”, then “explore”. Each layer is a short loop going straight from sensors to action; each new layer adds an ability, the layers beneath keep running, and a higher layer can override a lower one’s outputs when it needs to. It was put forward as a rejection of the long chain of “sense, then plan, then act”, that is, of step 0’s six layers strung one after another into a single long chain. Its traces are common in robots today: the bumper in the previous step makes the robot back off the moment it is pressed, without waiting for the programs above to think it over.

学习方法改变的是中间几层Learning changes the middle layers

从示范到试错那一篇讲的学习方法，在这副骨架里坐在哪？大多坐在中间。强化学习训练出来的行走策略，常见每秒运行 50 次，直接输出各个关节的目标角度，交给关节里的 PD 控制（Rudin 等 2021），它替掉的是 MPC 和全身控制；VLA（视觉-语言-动作模型）从图像和一句指令直接输出一段动作，相当于把感知、规划和一部分控制并进了一个网络。但它们输出的仍然是末端位姿或关节的目标，下面还要经过逆运动学或控制器，伺服环和电流环一个不少：电机的物理没有变，最快的那几个环也就省不掉。最外面还多出一个环：数据环。部署，收集失败的例子，重新训练，再部署，转一圈要几天到几个月。Where do the learning methods from From Demonstration to Trial and Error sit in this skeleton? Mostly in the middle. A walking policy trained with reinforcement learning typically runs 50 times a second and outputs target angles for every joint, which are handed to PD control in the joints (Rudin et al. 2021); it replaces MPC and whole-body control. A VLA (vision-language-action model) goes straight from images and a one-sentence language instruction to a stretch of actions, merging perception, planning and part of control into a single network. But what they output is still a target for the end effector’s pose or for the joints, which then passes through inverse kinematics or a controller, and every servo loop and current loop below stays: the physics of the motors has not changed, so the fastest loops cannot be dropped. And one more loop appears on the outside: the data loop. Deploy, collect the failures, retrain, deploy again; one turn takes days to months.

Truly hard real-time loops do not go through these messages; they run inside a single process (that is what ros2_control does) or over shared memory, to avoid the unpredictable delays of queueing.
  • 两种模式。CAN：四个节点同时想说话。每个报文以一位 0 开头，接着是 11 位的 ID，从最高位发起。总线的规矩是"0 能盖过 1"：只要有一个节点发 0，线上就是 0（0 叫显性，1 叫隐性）。每个节点一边发一边听：发的是 1、听到的却是 0，说明有更优先的节点在说话，它马上闭嘴，等下次。Two modes. CAN: four nodes want to talk at the same time. Every message starts with a single 0, followed by an 11-bit ID, highest bit first. The rule on the bus is “a 0 overrides a 1”: if any node sends a 0, the line reads 0 (0 is called dominant, 1 recessive). Each node listens while it sends: if it sent a 1 but hears a 0, a node with higher priority is talking, so it stops at once and waits for the next chance.

CAN 的仲裁不浪费时间：赢家的报文从头到尾没被打断，输家只是晚一点再发。代价是带宽小：经典 CAN 最高每秒 1 兆位，一帧最多 8 字节数据，新一些的 CAN FD 能装 64 字节、跑得更快。它便宜、抗干扰，汽车里各个电子控制单元之间、很多小型机器人的关节都用它。EtherCAT 走以太网的硬件，一帧"边走边读写"，控制周期能短到 100 微秒以下；各个驱动器的时钟还会用"分布式时钟"对齐，大家在同一时刻采样，误差远小于 1 微秒（EtherCAT）。工业机械臂和很多高端研究平台用的是它。CAN arbitration wastes no time: the winner’s message is never interrupted, and the losers simply send a little later. The price is low bandwidth: classic CAN tops out at 1 megabit a second with at most 8 bytes of data per frame; the newer CAN FD carries 64 bytes and runs faster. It is cheap and robust against interference, and links the electronic control units in cars and the joints of many small robots. EtherCAT runs on Ethernet hardware, each frame is “read and written on the fly”, and control cycles can be shorter than 100 microseconds; the drives’ clocks are aligned with “distributed clocks” so that they all sample at the same moment, with errors far below 1 microsecond (EtherCAT). Industrial robot arms and many high-end research platforms use it.

时间对不齐会怎样When clocks disagree

把相机和激光雷达的数据融合起来，前提是知道每份数据是在哪一刻测的。可每个传感器都有自己的时钟，时钟靠晶振计时，晶振的频率总有一点偏差。Fusing camera and lidar data assumes you know the moment each piece of data was measured. But every sensor keeps its own clock, each clock counts with a crystal oscillator, and no crystal runs at exactly the right rate.

Δx = v · Δt

两份数据的时间戳差了 Δt，运动的物体在两份数据里就错开 v · Δt。If the two pieces of data are time-stamped Δt apart, a moving object appears shifted by v · Δt between them.

融合程序默认"时间戳一样，就是同一时刻的世界"。时间戳一错，它就把两个不同时刻的世界拼在了一起；物体越快，错得越多。Fusion software assumes that the same timestamp means the same moment in the world. Once the timestamps are off, it stitches together the world at two different moments; the faster the object, the bigger the error.

计算平台按"能不能保证准时"分三档。单片机裸跑程序或者跑 RTOS，时间精确到微秒，跑电流环和关节环；实时 Linux 跑每秒一千次左右的控制，让 Linux 能按时响应的 PREEMPT_RT 补丁，2024 年并入了 Linux 6.12 主线；普通 Linux 加 GPU，毫秒级、不保证准时，跑感知、规划和大模型。第 0 步那些环，最后就是这样分到三类芯片上的：越里面的环，越靠近电机，越要保证准时。Computers come in three grades of “guaranteed on time”. A microcontroller running bare code or an RTOS keeps time to the microsecond, and runs the current and joint loops; real-time Linux runs control at around a thousand times a second (PREEMPT_RT, the patches that let Linux respond on time, were merged into mainline Linux 6.12 in 2024); ordinary Linux with a GPU works in milliseconds with no guarantee of punctuality, and runs perception, planning and large models. That is how the loops of step 0 are finally divided among three kinds of chip: the further in a loop sits, the closer to the motor it runs and the more its timing must be guaranteed.

安全回路不属于哪一级环，也不经过哪一层软件，而是和整条链并联。急停按钮通过硬线直接触发驱动器的 STO（安全转矩关断），让电机不再出力，不经过应用软件，因为软件可能卡死。STO 只管"不再出力"：转着的关节会靠惯性继续转，受重力的手臂会掉下来，所以还要配合抱闸（一断电就刹住的机械刹车），或者先受控地停下、再切断力矩。急停回路本身也按"线会断"来设计：用常闭、双通道的接线，线断了就等于按下急停。看门狗则让下面的程序定时等上面的程序发来"心跳"，超时就自动进入安全状态，就是第 5 步里那只让机器人停车的看门狗。The safety circuit belongs to no loop level and passes through no software layer; it runs in parallel with the whole chain. An emergency stop button triggers the drives’ STO (safe torque off) directly over a hard-wired line, so the motors stop producing torque without going through the application software, which might hang. STO only stops the torque: a turning joint keeps coasting, and an arm held up against gravity falls, so STO is combined with holding brakes (mechanical brakes that clamp as soon as power is lost), or the machine is first brought to a controlled stop and the torque cut afterwards. The e-stop circuit itself is designed on the assumption that wires break: it uses normally closed, dual-channel wiring, so a broken wire counts as pressing the e-stop. A watchdog, in turn, has a program below wait for a regular “heartbeat” from the program above and switch to a safe state when it stops: the same watchdog that stopped the robot in step 5.

回到开头的问题Back to the opening questions

  • 一个环由什么组成？目标 r、比较器、误差 e = r − ym、控制器、命令 u、执行器、被控对象、输出 y、传感器和测量值 ym。反馈要测对的量，换算成和目标同一种量，按固定的节拍去比较，而且必须是负的；测量的延迟和噪声会原封不动地混进误差。What is a loop made of? A target r, a comparator, the error e = r − ym, a controller, a command u, an actuator, a plant, the output y, a sensor and the measurement ym. Feedback must measure the right quantity, convert it into the same quantity as the target, be compared on a fixed beat, and be negative; the delay and noise in the measurement pass straight into the error.
  • 层和级是什么关系？同一张表的两个方向：一级环是一行，一层是一列。一级环横穿好几层：传感、感知与估计坐在它的反馈通路上，它在决策、规划或控制那一层做比较，里面几级合起来是它的执行器。一层里也可以叠好几级：控制层从每秒几十次一直叠到每秒几万次。外面一级的输出，就是里面一级的目标：新目标从外往里传，扰动由最先测得到它的那一级接住，每一级只纠正得了它测得到的误差。How do layers and levels relate? They are two directions of one table: a loop level is a row, a layer is a column. A loop level runs across several layers: sensing, perception and estimation sit on its feedback path, it makes its comparison in the decision, planning or control layer, and the levels inside it together are its actuator. One layer can also stack several levels: the control layer stacks from tens of times a second up to tens of thousands. The output of an outer level is the target of the level inside it: new targets travel inward, disturbances are caught by whichever level measures them first, and each level can only correct the errors it can measure.
  • 被推的那 0.3 秒里发生了什么？最里面的电流环和关节 PD 先动，几十微秒到几毫秒里就开始顶住；状态估计和全身控制几毫秒后接上；MPC 几十毫秒后重新分配脚力，落脚点也跟着挪；相机和导航可能根本没参与。越快的扰动，由越靠里的环处理。What happened in the 0.3 s after the push? The innermost current loops and joint PD move first, starting to push back within tens of microseconds to a few milliseconds; state estimation and whole-body control join a few milliseconds later; MPC reshares the foot forces after tens of milliseconds, and the footholds move with them; the camera and navigation may not take part at all. The faster the disturbance, the further in the loop that handles it.
  • 为什么要分六层？从测量到出力，这六件事一件也省不掉。把它们分开，每一层只和相邻的层约定好交接的东西（接口）：换一个检测网络，只动感知那一格；换一种轮子，只动执行和底盘控制那两格，别的都不用改。Why six layers? Between measuring and pushing, none of the six jobs can be skipped. Keeping them separate means each layer only has to agree with its neighbours on what gets handed over (the interface): a new detection network changes only the perception cell; a new kind of wheel changes only the actuation and base-control cells, and nothing else has to change.
  • 为什么要分好几级？每一级管的物理过程快慢不同，环要比它管的东西快一个数量级左右；要"想"的事算得慢，"反射"简单算得快，两条趋势刚好对上。分级还让每一级都简单、扰动能就地消灭、外面出事时里面能兜底。Why several levels? Each level looks after physics that changes at its own speed, and a loop must be about an order of magnitude faster than what it controls; things that need “thinking” are slow to compute, while “reflexes” are simple and fast, and the two trends line up neatly. Levels also keep each loop simple, let disturbances be dealt with on the spot, and let the inner levels catch the robot when something further out fails.
  • 一台机器人有几级环？身体里的环通常套 3 到 5 级：扫地机器人 3 级，机械臂和汽车 4 级，四旋翼和四足 5 级，看要同时应付几种时间尺度。外面还有重新规划的环、人在旁边遥控或监督的环、部署和训练之间的数据环；安全回路和整条链并联。How many levels of loop does a robot have? The body usually nests 3 to 5 levels of loop: 3 in the robot vacuum, 4 in the arm and the car, 5 in the quadrotor and the quadruped, depending on how many time scales it must cope with at once. Outside the body there are more: loops that replan, loops with a person supervising or teleoperating, and the data loop between deployment and training; the safety circuit runs in parallel with the whole chain.
  • 机器人那么多，为什么都一样？因为它们面对的是同样的事：电机的电流以微秒变化，身体以零点几秒变化，环境以秒变化，任务以分钟变化。所以都是这六层，都是一级套一级；不同的是有几级，每一格里填什么硬件、什么算法。With so many kinds of robot, why are they all alike? Because they all face the same facts: motor current changes in microseconds, bodies in fractions of a second, surroundings in seconds, tasks in minutes. So they all have the six layers and all nest their loops level within level; what differs is how many levels there are and which hardware and algorithm fill each cell.

检查一下Check yourself

扫地机器人的编码器换算里，把轮子周长误写成了 0.20 m（实际是 0.22 m）。目标 0.30 m/s 时，轮子实际转多快？轮速环显示的误差是多少？In the robot vacuum’s encoder conversion, the wheel circumference was written down as 0.20 m (it is really 0.22 m). With a target of 0.30 m/s, how fast does the wheel really turn? What error does the wheel loop show?

每个脉冲被当成 0.20 ÷ 360 m，比实际的 0.22 ÷ 360 m 少算了一截，所以测到的 ym 只有真实轮速的 0.20 ÷ 0.22 ≈ 0.91 倍。轮速环把 ym 控制到 0.30，真实轮速就是 0.30 ÷ 0.91 ≈ 0.33 m/s，快了 10%；而误差显示为 0。环只对它测到的东西负责，所以外面一级的路线环才要用激光雷达再测一次位置。Each pulse is counted as 0.20 ÷ 360 m instead of the real 0.22 ÷ 360 m, so the measured ym is only 0.20 ÷ 0.22 ≈ 0.91 of the true speed. The wheel loop holds ym at 0.30, so the true speed is 0.30 ÷ 0.91 ≈ 0.33 m/s, 10% fast, while the error reads 0. A loop answers only for what it measures, which is why the route loop, one level out, measures the position again with the lidar.

第 1 步的手臂停在 30° 时，关节托着约 4.2 N·m 的重力力矩。Kp = 50 N·m/rad，大约下垂多少？想让下垂不到 1°，Kp 至少要多大？With the arm from step 1 held at 30°, the joint carries about 4.2 N·m of gravity torque. With Kp = 50 N·m/rad, roughly how much does it sag? How large must Kp be to keep the sag under 1°?

下垂 ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 = 0.084 rad ≈ 4.8°。1° ≈ 0.0175 rad，所以 Kp 至少要 4.2 ÷ 0.0175 ≈ 241 N·m/rad，留点余量，取 250 左右。另外两个办法：加重力补偿（前馈），或者加积分项，Kp 就不用这么大。Sag ≈ 4.2 ÷ 50 = 0.084 rad ≈ 4.8°. 1° ≈ 0.0175 rad, so Kp must be at least 4.2 ÷ 0.0175 ≈ 241 N·m/rad; with a little margin, about 250. Two other ways out: add gravity compensation (feedforward), or add an integral term, and then Kp does not need to be nearly as large.

按第 2 步的交互，总滞后超过杆的 T 的一半左右，就很难扶住了。人从看见到动手大约 0.15 秒，大约多长的杆还扶得住？In the interactive of step 2, once the total lag is more than about half of the stick’s T, it becomes very hard to hold. A person takes about 0.15 s from seeing to moving the hand: roughly how long must a stick be to be held?

T 至少要 0.15 ÷ 0.5 = 0.3 s，l = g · T² ≈ 9.8 × 0.09 ≈ 0.9 m。这只是交互里那个控制器的经验界限；人会预判，实际能扶住更短一些的杆，但铅笔肯定不行。T must be at least 0.15 ÷ 0.5 = 0.3 s, so l = g · T² ≈ 9.8 × 0.09 ≈ 0.9 m. That is only the rule of thumb for the controller in the interactive; people anticipate, so in practice they can hold a somewhat shorter stick, but certainly not a pencil.

为什么无人机的角速度环要比位置环快得多？反过来，让位置环每秒跑 1000 次、角速度环每秒跑 20 次，会怎样？Why must a drone’s rate loop be so much faster than its position loop? And the other way round: what happens if the position loop runs 1,000 times a second and the rate loop 20?

角速度环管的是机身转动，零点几秒就能翻过去；位置变化慢得多。反过来，位置环再快也没用：它每次都在要一个新的速度，可下面负责让机身转到位的环太慢，执行不出来。按第 3 步的交互，角速度环每秒 20 次时，机身一直大幅摇晃；再慢一点就会翻。快慢要按"管的东西变得多快"来分配。The rate loop looks after how the body turns, and it can flip in a fraction of a second; position changes far more slowly. Turn it round and a fast position loop is no use: it keeps asking for new velocities, but the loop below, which has to get the body turned into place, is too slow to deliver. In the interactive in step 3, with the rate loop at 20 times a second the body keeps rocking widely; a little slower and it flips. Speed has to be allotted according to how fast the controlled thing changes.

开关频率从 20 kHz 降到 2 kHz，电流纹波大约变成多少倍？为什么很多驱动器不用 2 kHz？If the switching frequency drops from 20 kHz to 2 kHz, by roughly what factor does the current ripple change? Why do so many drives avoid 2 kHz?

纹波和开关频率成反比，大约变成 10 倍。纹波大，力矩跟着抖；2 kHz 在人耳能听到的范围里，电机会发出刺耳的尖叫；电流环也只能每 0.5 毫秒改一次占空比，反应变慢。Ripple is inversely proportional to switching frequency, so it grows about tenfold. Larger ripple makes the torque shudder; 2 kHz is well within human hearing, so the motor gives off a piercing whine; and the current loop can only change the duty once every 0.5 ms, so it reacts more slowly.

相机和激光雷达的时间戳差了 30 ms，横穿过来的车速 15 m/s，两份数据里的车错开多少？The camera and lidar timestamps are 30 ms apart, and a car crossing in front moves at 15 m/s. How far apart does the car appear in the two pieces of data?

Δx = v · Δt = 15 × 0.03 = 0.45 m。Δx = v · Δt = 15 × 0.03 = 0.45 m.

扫地机器人的主电脑死机了，为什么它不会一直往前冲？If a robot vacuum’s main computer crashes, why does it not keep charging ahead?

底盘控制器上有看门狗：一段时间（交互里是 0.3 秒，具体数字因机器而异）没收到新的速度命令，就主动停车。更严重的情况，还有急停按钮通过硬线触发驱动器的 STO，让电机不再出力，不经过应用软件；受重力的关节同时靠抱闸刹住。The base controller has a watchdog: if no new speed command arrives for a while (0.3 s in the interactive; the exact figure varies from machine to machine), it stops the robot. For anything more serious, an emergency stop button triggers the drives’ STO over a hard-wired line, so the motors stop producing torque without going through the application software; joints held up against gravity are clamped by their holding brakes at the same time.
