从 PID 到世界模型From PID to World Models

PID、LQR、滑模、MPC、强化学习、世界模型，名字五花八门，看上去像一堆互不相干的发明。其实它们都在回答同一个问题：知道了现在的情况，这一刻该出多大的力，才能让以后的结果最好？区别只在四件事上：手里有多少关于被控对象的知识（模型），这道题什么时候算（事先算好，还是每一步现算），往前看多远，怎么对付算不准的那部分。这一篇用一根电机带动的摆杆贯穿全篇，从 PID 一路走到世界模型：每种方法解决了前一种的什么问题，又带来了什么新问题；它们坐在上一篇那"六层、几级环"里的什么位置；最后看人形机器人一轮又一轮的高潮，为什么是强化学习把它带到了今天，世界模型又在里面扮演什么角色。PID, LQR, sliding mode, MPC, reinforcement learning, world models: a motley collection of names that look like a pile of unrelated inventions. In fact they all answer the same question: knowing the situation now, how much force should be applied at this moment so that what follows turns out best? They differ in just four things: how much is known about the thing being controlled (the model); when the problem is solved (worked out in advance, or afresh at every step); how far ahead it looks; and how it handles the part it cannot calculate accurately. This page follows one motor-driven pendulum arm from start to finish, from PID all the way to world models: what problem each method solves that the one before it could not, and what new problems it brings; where each sits among the previous page’s “six layers and loop levels”; and finally, looking at wave after wave of excitement about humanoid robots, why it was reinforcement learning that brought them to where they are today, and what role world models play in all this.

第 0 步 · 总览Step 0 · Overview

所有控制器都在解同一道题Every controller is solving the same problem

全篇用同一个例子：一根杆，一头装在电机轴上，另一头挂着 1 kg 的重物，杆长 0.5 m，就是上一篇第 1 步那个关节。电机出力矩 τ，杆转到角度 θ；θ = 0 是自然垂下，θ = 180° 是倒立在正上方。垂着的时候它是稳的，倒立的时候一碰就倒。几乎所有控制问题的味道，这根杆上都有：要托住重力，要快又不能振，要同时管角度和角速度，大角度时不再是线性的，重物换了没人告诉你，碰到墙要能软下来，力矩不够时得先荡起来，模型写不出来时只能学。The whole page uses one example: an arm 0.5 m long, with one end mounted on a motor shaft and a 1 kg weight at the other; it is the joint from step 1 of the previous page. The motor applies a torque τ and the arm turns to an angle θ; θ = 0 is hanging straight down, and θ = 180° is upright, balanced directly above the shaft. Hanging down, it is stable; upright, the slightest touch makes it fall. Nearly every flavour of control problem shows up in this one arm: it has to hold the weight up against gravity; it has to be fast without oscillating; it has to look after angle and angular velocity at once; at large angles it is no longer linear; the weight gets swapped without anyone telling you; when it hits a wall it has to go soft; when the torque is not enough, it first has to swing itself up; and when the model cannot be written down, the only option is to learn.

不管用哪种方法，控制器做的事都一样：每个周期拿到测量 y 和目标 r，交出一个命令 u。它就是一个函数 u = π(y, r)。各种方法的差别，在于这个函数是怎么得到的。把"想要什么"写清楚，就是下面这道题：Whatever the method, a controller does the same job: every cycle it takes a measurement y and a target r, and hands out a command u. It is simply a function, u = π(y, r). What sets the methods apart is how that function is obtained. Write down clearly “what we want”, and you get the following problem:

选一串命令 u，让总代价 J 最小。总代价是每一步"离目标多远、用了多大力"的加总，再加上最后停在哪的代价。同时要服从物理规律 f；状态只能通过带噪声的测量 y 知道；命令和状态不能超出允许的范围。Choose a sequence of commands u that makes the total cost J as small as possible. The total cost adds up, over every step, “how far from the target, and how much force was used”, plus a cost for where it finally ends up. All the while it must obey the laws of physics, f; the state can only be known through the noisy measurement y; and the commands and the state must not go beyond what is allowed.

几乎所有"我想让它怎样"都能写成代价：想准，就罚误差；想省电、想柔和，就罚力矩；想安全，就把危险的地方写成约束。物理规律不由你挑，所以它是约束，不是愿望。现实里模型总有误差、测量总有噪声，所以式子里留着 d 和 n。Almost any “I want it to do this” can be written as a cost: to be accurate, penalise the error; to save power or be gentle, penalise the torque; to be safe, write the dangerous regions in as constraints. The laws of physics are not yours to choose, so they are a constraint, not a wish. In reality the model always has errors and the measurements always have noise, which is why the equations keep d and n.

控制就是"在规则之内，花最少的代价把事办成"。每种控制方法，都是用某种办法近似地解这道题，因为没有谁能把它完整解出来：模型不全知道，状态测不全，算力有限，时间也有限。Control means “getting the job done within the rules, at the least cost”. Every control method solves this problem approximately, one way or another, because nobody can solve it in full: the model is never entirely known, the state cannot all be measured, computing power is limited, and so is time.

让摆杆停在 60°，可以取 ℓ = (θ − 60°)² + 0.01 τ²（角度按弧度算）：差 10°（0.17 rad）每一步罚 0.03，用 3 N·m 罚 0.09。0.01 这个权重，决定了"多在乎准、多在乎省力"。To hold the arm at 60°, you could take ℓ = (θ − 60°)² + 0.01 τ² (angles in radians): being 10° (0.17 rad) off costs 0.03 per step, and using 3 N·m costs 0.09. The weight 0.01 decides “how much to care about accuracy, and how much about saving effort”.

后面每一种方法，都可以问四个问题：它用了多少模型？这道题什么时候算？往前看多远？怎么对付模型和测量的误差？答案不同，就是不同的方法。Of every method that follows, you can ask four questions: how much of a model does it use? When is the problem solved? How far ahead does it look? How does it handle errors in the model and the measurements? Different answers make different methods.

控制在六层和几级环里坐在哪Where control sits among the six layers and the loop levels

上一篇把机器人拆成两套层次：按分工的六层（传感、感知与估计、决策、规划、控制、执行），和按快慢的几级环（从每秒几万次的电流环，到几秒一次的任务环）。在那张"层 × 级"的大表里，控制是一列：每一级环都有一个控制格子，它拿到"目标减去估计"的误差，交出给下一级的命令。所以机器人里没有"一个"控制器，而是每一级都有一个，各自快慢不同、方法不同：电流环里常是 PI，关节环里是 PD 加重力补偿或阻抗控制，全身那一级是二次规划或者一个强化学习策略，再往上是 MPC，最上面的"控制"已经是决策了。The previous page took a robot apart in two ways: the six layers, divided by job (sensing, perception and estimation, decision, planning, control, actuation), and the loop levels, divided by speed (from the current loop, running tens of thousands of times a second, to the task loop, running once every few seconds). In that big “layers × levels” table, control is a column: every loop level has a control cell, which takes the error, “target minus estimate”, and hands a command to the next level in. So a robot does not have “one” controller; every level has its own, each with its own speed and its own method: the current loop is usually PI; the joint loop is PD plus gravity compensation, or impedance control; the whole-body level is a quadratic program or a reinforcement learning policy; above that comes MPC; and at the very top, “control” has already become decision.

很多方法会同时占好几层。LQG 把"估计"（卡尔曼滤波）和"控制"（LQR）连在一起设计；MPC 把"规划"和"控制"合成一道优化；一个强化学习的步行策略，从关节角和陀螺仪的历史直接算出关节目标，等于把估计、规划、控制压进了一个网络；VLA 更是从看图听话一直管到出动作。可以这样合，是因为六层本来就是为了方便分工而切开的，它们解的是同一道题：估计和控制在数学上是一对"对偶"问题（第 ③ 步），规划就是看得更远、模型更粗的控制（第 ⑦ 步）。分开有分开的好处（各自好调、好测、好换），代价是接口上会丢东西：规划不知道控制做不做得到，估计不知道控制最在乎哪个误差。只要一次计算能在这一级的周期里把合起来的题解完，合起来就能少丢一点。Many methods occupy several layers at once. LQG designs “estimation” (the Kalman filter) and “control” (LQR) together; MPC merges “planning” and “control” into one optimisation; a reinforcement learning walking policy computes joint targets directly from the history of joint angles and gyroscope readings, which amounts to squeezing estimation, planning and control into one network; a VLA goes further still, handling everything from seeing images and hearing instructions all the way to producing actions. They can be merged like this because the six layers were only ever cut apart to make the division of labour convenient; they are solving the same problem: estimation and control are, mathematically, a pair of “dual” problems (step ③), and planning is just control that looks further ahead with a rougher model (step ⑦). Keeping them apart has its benefits (each is easy to tune, test and swap on its own); the price is that things get lost at the interfaces: planning does not know whether control can actually do what it asks, and estimation does not know which error control cares about most. As long as a single computation can solve the merged problem within that level’s period, merging means losing a little less.

但方法很少跨级。每一级的周期给计算定了预算：每秒 50 次的策略网络，替不了每秒两万次的电流环；每秒 5 次的 VLA，也接不住摔倒。所以几十年来变的，是每一级里用什么方法，以及级数怎么排：算力变快了，MPC 和全身控制可以合成一级；强化学习用一个每秒 50 次的网络，替掉了"MPC + 全身控制"这两级；但网络下面的关节 PD 和电流环一直都在。第 ⑩ 步会看到，人形机器人一代又一代的控制，就是这张表里的格子一格一格换掉的过程。But methods rarely span levels. The period of each level sets a budget for computation: a policy network running 50 times a second cannot replace a current loop running 20,000 times a second, and a VLA running 5 times a second cannot catch a fall. So what has changed over the decades is which method is used within each level, and how the levels are arranged: as computing got faster, MPC and whole-body control could merge into one level; reinforcement learning replaced the two levels of “MPC + whole-body control” with a single network running 50 times a second; but the joint PD and the current loop beneath the network have been there all along. Step ⑩ will show that each generation of humanoid robot control has been a matter of swapping out the cells of this table, one at a time.

第 ① 步 · PIDStep ① · PID

PID：三个数，为什么够用一百年PID: why three numbers have been enough for a hundred years

先让摆杆停在 60°。最直接的办法：差多少，就往回推多少，差得越多推得越狠。这就是比例控制（P）。可这根杆挂着重物，要停在 60°，电机必须一直出力托着它；而 P 的力矩和误差成正比，误差是 0，力矩也是 0。所以杆一定会停在比 60° 低一点的地方，正好让"误差 × Kp"等于重力力矩。这个差，叫稳态误差。First, hold the arm at 60°. The most direct way: push back by however much it is off, and the further off, the harder the push. This is proportional control (P). But this arm carries a weight, and to stay at 60° the motor has to keep pushing to hold it up; P’s torque is proportional to the error, so zero error means zero torque. The arm will therefore always settle a little below 60°, exactly where “error × Kp” equals the gravity torque. This gap is called the steady-state error.

办法有两个。一是把误差"攒"起来：只要还差一点，就让力矩一点点往上加，直到误差真的消失，这是积分（I）。二是干脆用模型算出托住杆要多大的力矩，直接加上，这是前馈。另外，只有 P 的话，杆像挂在一根弹簧上，会来回晃；加一项"往回转得越快，越要刹住"，就是微分（D），它的作用像阻尼器。三项加在一起：There are two ways out. One is to “save up” the error: as long as some error remains, keep raising the torque bit by bit until the error really is gone; this is the integral (I). The other is simply to use the model to work out how much torque it takes to hold the arm up, and add that in directly; this is feedforward. Besides, with P alone the arm behaves as if it hangs on a spring and swings back and forth; add a term that says “the faster it swings back, the harder to brake”, and you have the derivative (D), which works like a damper. 三项各管一件事，正好对应时间的三个方向：P 看现在，I 看过去，D 看趋势（马上要变成什么样）。从物理上说，P 是一根把杆拉向目标的弹簧，D 是一个阻尼器，I 是一只"记账"的手，只要账上还欠着，就一直加力。弹簧加阻尼，就能让一个有惯性的东西又快又稳地停下；记账，就能抵消任何一直存在的扰动，比如重力、摩擦、负载。Each term looks after one thing, and together they cover the three directions of time: P looks at the present, I at the past, D at the trend (what it is about to become). Physically, P is a spring pulling the arm towards the target, D is a damper, and I is a “bookkeeping” hand that keeps adding force for as long as anything is still owed. A spring plus a damper brings anything with inertia to rest quickly and steadily; the bookkeeping cancels any disturbance that is always there, such as gravity, friction or a load.

积分有两个毛病。一是慢：要攒一会儿。二是会"饱和"：力矩已经顶到电机上限了，误差还在，积分还在攒，等杆终于到了目标，攒下的力矩一时放不掉，就冲过头。工程上的办法叫抗积分饱和：力矩顶到上限、而且误差还在往同一边推的时候，暂停积分。微分也有毛病：测量里的噪声变化很快，D 会把它放大成力矩的抖动，所以 D 前面总要加一个低通滤波器（只让慢的变化通过，把快的抖动削掉）。The integral has two weaknesses. First, it is slow: it takes time to build up. Second, it can “wind up”: the torque has already hit the motor’s limit, but the error is still there and the integral keeps building; when the arm finally reaches the target, the stored-up torque cannot be released at once, and the arm overshoots. The engineering fix is called anti-windup: pause the integral while the torque is at its limit and the error is still pushing the same way. The derivative has a weakness too: noise in the measurement changes very fast, and D amplifies it into jitter in the torque, so a low-pass filter is always put in front of D (it lets slow changes through and shaves off the fast jitter).

为什么 PID 依然是"神"Why PID is still “king”

在过程控制（化工、造纸、电力）里，Åström 和 Hägglund 在他们的 PID 教科书开头写道，超过 95% 的回路是 PID，而且其中大多数其实只用了 PI（Åström & Hägglund 1995）。机器人里也一样：每个电机的电流环几乎都是 PI，关节的伺服不是 PD，就是"位置 P + 速度 PI"的级联。一百年前用来给船自动掌舵的东西（Minorsky 1922），为什么到今天还在最里面那几级环里？In process control (chemical plants, paper mills, power stations), Åström and Hägglund write at the start of their PID textbook that more than 95% of loops are PID, and that most of those actually use only PI (Åström & Hägglund 1995). Robots are no different: almost every motor’s current loop is PI, and a joint’s servo is either PD or a cascade of “position P + velocity PI”. Why is something first used a hundred years ago to steer ships automatically (Minorsky 1922) still sitting in the innermost loop levels today?

  • 它管的正是物理上必需的三件事。任何有惯性的东西要停得又快又稳，都需要一个恢复力和一个阻尼，这就是 P 和 D；任何一直存在的扰动都需要被抵消，这就是 I。第 ③ 步会看到，对"力矩 → 角速度 → 角度"这种对象，用最优控制理论算出来的最优控制器，恰好就是一个 PD。It looks after exactly the three things physics demands. Anything with inertia needs a restoring force and damping to stop quickly and steadily: those are P and D; any disturbance that is always there has to be cancelled: that is I. Step ③ will show that for a plant like “torque → angular velocity → angle”, the optimal controller worked out with optimal control theory turns out to be exactly a PD.
  • 它几乎不要模型。三个数，对着真机调一调就能用；做个小实验就能按规则整定（Ziegler & Nichols 1942），用继电实验还能让控制器自己整定（Åström & Hägglund 1984）。对大多数稳定、单输入单输出、近似线性、没有长延迟的对象，一个调好的 PID 已经离"能做到的最好"不远。It needs almost no model. Three numbers, adjusted on the real machine, and it works; a small experiment lets you tune it by rule (Ziegler & Nichols 1942), and a relay experiment even lets the controller tune itself (Åström & Hägglund 1984). For most plants that are stable, single-input single-output, roughly linear and free of long delays, a well-tuned PID is not far from “the best that can be done”.
  • 它便宜、快、看得懂。每一步就几次乘法和加法，微秒级，能放进每秒几万次的电流环；出了问题，工程师一眼就能看出是哪一项不对。越靠近电机，这一点越要紧：那里周期短、芯片小，而且一旦出错就是真的电流和真的力。It is cheap, fast and easy to understand. Each step is a few multiplications and additions, taking microseconds, so it fits into a current loop running tens of thousands of times a second; when something goes wrong, an engineer can see at a glance which term is at fault. The closer to the motor, the more this matters: periods there are short, chips are small, and any mistake means real current and real force.
  • 一百年的工程积累。抗饱和、微分滤波、前馈、自整定、增益调度，所有的坑都有人踩过、都有现成的补法。A hundred years of engineering experience. Anti-windup, derivative filtering, feedforward, auto-tuning, gain scheduling: someone has already fallen into every pitfall, and every one has a ready-made fix.

那为什么常常只用 PI 或者 PD？看被控对象有几个"积分"。So why is it so often just PI, or just PD? Look at how many “integrators” the plant has.

  • PI 用在一阶的对象上：电压 → 电流（线圈），力矩 → 转速，阀门开度 → 流量。这种对象自己就"有阻尼"，不会来回晃，D 帮不上什么忙，反而把噪声放大；可它们总有恒定的负载（反电动势、摩擦、背压），需要 I 来消掉稳态误差。所以电流环、速度环、过程控制里，几乎都是 PI。PI is used on first-order plants: voltage → current (in a coil), torque → speed, valve opening → flow. Such plants are “damped” by nature and do not swing back and forth, so D does little to help and only amplifies noise; but they always carry a constant load (back-EMF, friction, back pressure), so they need I to remove the steady-state error. That is why current loops, velocity loops and process control are almost all PI.
  • PD 用在二阶的对象上：力矩 → 角速度 → 角度，中间有两个积分，像一个质量块挂在弹簧上，没有 D 就会晃很久（交互里的 P 模式）；只用 PI 更糟，积分的滞后让它越晃越大（交互里选 PI 试试）。至于稳态误差，机器人更愿意用模型的前馈（重力补偿）去消，而不是靠 I：积分在位置环里会饱和、会让动作变慢；更麻烦的是碰到东西时，被挡住的误差会让积分一直攒，力越推越大。强化学习控制的机器人也是这样：网络每秒 50 次给出"关节目标角"，下面每个关节一个 PD，把目标角变成力矩。这个 PD 其实是一根可调软硬的弹簧，第 ⑥ 步会看到它就是阻抗控制。PD is used on second-order plants: torque → angular velocity → angle, with two integrators in between, like a mass hanging on a spring: without D it swings for a long time (the P mode in the interactive); with PI alone it is worse still, as the lag of the integral makes the swinging grow (try PI in the interactive). As for the steady-state error, robots prefer to remove it with model feedforward (gravity compensation) rather than with I: in a position loop the integral winds up and slows motion down; worse, when the robot touches something, the blocked error makes the integral keep building, so it pushes harder and harder. Robots controlled by reinforcement learning are no exception: the network gives “target joint angles” 50 times a second, and below it each joint has a PD that turns its target angle into torque. This PD is really a spring whose stiffness can be adjusted; step ⑥ will show that it is exactly impedance control.
  • 积分常常由别人代劳。外面一级环会慢慢挪动给里面的目标；模型前馈直接给出托住重力的力矩；第 ⑤ 步的扰动观测器把"一直存在的扰动"估出来再抵消，比积分快得多。The integral’s job is often done by someone else. The loop one level out slowly shifts the target it hands inward; model feedforward directly supplies the torque that holds the arm up against gravity; and the disturbance observer of step ⑤ estimates the “always-present disturbance” and then cancels it, much faster than an integral.

PID 管不了的事也很清楚，后面每一步都在补其中一块：它只看一个误差，不懂几个变量之间的耦合（③）；它不知道力矩有上限，也不会往前看（⑦）；对象一旦非线性、或者参数变了，调好的三个数就不再合适（④⑤）；碰到环境时，"位置要准"这个目标本身就不对了（⑥）。What PID cannot handle is just as clear, and each of the following steps fills in one piece: it looks at only one error and knows nothing of the coupling between several variables (③); it does not know the torque has a limit, and it does not look ahead (⑦); once the plant is nonlinear or its parameters change, the three tuned numbers no longer fit (④, ⑤); and once it touches the environment, the very goal of “getting the position exactly right” is the wrong one (⑥).

第 ② 步 · 频率Step ② · Frequency

换一个角度看：频率、带宽和延迟Another angle: frequency, bandwidth and delay

调 PID 时，总有一个让人不安的现象：增益调大，反应变快，可再大一点，它就开始振，再大就发散。延迟让这件事更糟：同样的增益，测量晚到 40 毫秒，本来稳稳的回路就开始晃。为什么？最清楚的答案来自另一种看法：不看一次阶跃怎么响应，而看回路对每一个频率的正弦波怎么反应。这个看法来自 1930 年代贝尔实验室做电话放大器的工程师（Nyquist 1932，Bode 1940），至今仍是调伺服、调电源的主要工具。Tuning PID, you always run into an unsettling effect: turn the gain up and the response gets faster; a little higher and it starts to oscillate; higher still and it diverges. Delay makes this worse: with the same gains, if the measurement arrives 40 milliseconds late, a loop that was perfectly steady starts to wobble. Why? The clearest answer comes from a different way of looking: instead of how the loop responds to a single step, look at how it reacts to a sine wave at every frequency. This view comes from the engineers at Bell Labs who built telephone amplifiers in the 1930s (Nyquist 1932, Bode 1940), and it is still the main tool for tuning servos and power supplies.

思路是这样：把回路在某处断开，从一头输入一个频率为 ω 的正弦，看它绕一圈回来，幅值放大了多少倍，相位落后了多少。这就是开环频率响应 L(jω)。如果在某个频率上，信号绕一圈回来"一样大、而且正好反了个向"，负反馈就变成了正反馈，回路会在这个频率上自己振起来。The idea is this: break the loop at some point, feed a sine wave of frequency ω in at one end, and see, once it has come back round the loop, by what factor its amplitude has changed and how far its phase has fallen behind. This is the open-loop frequency response, L(jω). If at some frequency the signal comes back round “the same size, and turned exactly the other way”, negative feedback becomes positive feedback, and the loop starts oscillating by itself at that frequency.

At the frequency ωc where the amplification is exactly 1, see how many degrees the phase still is from −180°: that is the phase margin, PM.

−180° 意味着信号绕一圈回来正好反向，负反馈变成正反馈；放大倍数 1 意味着它不衰减。两样同时满足，回路就会自己振下去。所以离这一点有多远，就是离不稳定有多远。相位裕度小于 30° 左右，阶跃响应就会明显来回晃；等于 0，就会一直振。−180° means the signal comes back round exactly reversed, so negative feedback becomes positive feedback; an amplification of 1 means it does not shrink. When both hold at once, the loop keeps oscillating by itself. So how far you are from this point is how far you are from instability. With a phase margin below about 30°, the step response visibly swings back and forth; at 0, it oscillates forever.

频率的看法还给出了一条更深的限制：把扰动在某些频率上压下去，它就会在别的频率上冒出来，像压水床，压下一边，别处就鼓起来（Bode 的灵敏度积分）。这说明反馈不是万能的：它能把误差"挪"到你不在乎的频率上，却不能让它凭空消失。后来的 H∞ 鲁棒控制，就是把这种频率上的取舍做成了可以证明的多变量设计（第 ⑤ 步）。The frequency view also exposes a deeper limit: push a disturbance down at some frequencies and it pops up at others, like a waterbed, where pressing down on one side makes another part bulge (Bode’s sensitivity integral). So feedback is not all-powerful: it can “move” errors to frequencies you do not care about, but it cannot make them vanish into thin air. The H∞ robust control that came later turned this trade-off across frequencies into multivariable designs with provable guarantees (step ⑤).

PID 只看一个误差。可要预测杆接下来怎么动，光知道角度不够：同样在 10°，往上转和往下转，结局完全不同。能完整决定"以后会怎样"的那组数，叫状态。摆杆的状态是两个数：角度和角速度。1960 年前后，Kalman 把控制问题改写成状态的语言（状态空间），一下子把"多个变量互相耦合"和"最优"这两件事都变得可以算了。PID looks at only one error. But to predict how the arm will move next, knowing the angle is not enough: at the same 10°, turning upward and turning downward end in completely different ways. The set of numbers that fully determines “what happens next” is called the state. The pendulum arm’s state is two numbers: the angle and the angular velocity. Around 1960, Kalman rewrote control problems in the language of the state (state space), and at a stroke made two things computable: “many variables coupled to one another” and “optimal”.

倒立时，偏得越多，重力就把它往外推得越狠（第一项是正的）；摩擦让它慢下来；电机力矩除以转动惯量，就是它给的角加速度。写成矩阵，就是"状态的变化率 = A × 状态 + B × 命令"。Upside down, the further the arm leans, the harder gravity pushes it further over (the first term is positive); friction slows it down; the motor torque divided by the moment of inertia is the angular acceleration the motor provides. In matrix form: “rate of change of the state = A × state + B × command”.

垂着的时候，重力是把杆拉回去的弹簧；倒过来以后，这根弹簧变成了"负的"：偏一点，就推得更偏。所以倒立是不稳定的，必须有人一直在下面扶着。When the arm hangs down, gravity is a spring pulling it back; once it is turned upside down, that spring becomes “negative”: lean a little, and it pushes the arm further over. That is why balancing upside down is unstable: something has to keep holding it up from below.

LQR（线性二次型调节器）不让你直接挑增益，而是让你回答一个更自然的问题：偏离一点有多贵，用力一点有多贵？然后它替你算出最划算的反馈律：LQR (the linear quadratic regulator) does not ask you to pick gains directly. Instead, it asks you a more natural question: how costly is being a little off, and how costly is pushing a little harder? Then it works out the most cost-effective feedback law for you:

min ∫ (xᵀQx + uᵀRu) dt⇒　u = −K x，K = R⁻¹BᵀP⇒ u = −K x, K = R⁻¹BᵀPAᵀP + PA − PBR⁻¹BᵀP + Q = 0

给"偏离"定价 Q，给"用力"定价 R，求让总价最低的控制。答案恰好是状态的线性组合 u = −K₁ φ − K₂ ω，而 K 由一个只要解一次的矩阵方程（Riccati 方程）给出。Put a price Q on “being off” and a price R on “effort”, and find the control that makes the total price lowest. The answer turns out to be a linear combination of the state, u = −K₁ φ − K₂ ω, with K given by a matrix equation that only has to be solved once (the Riccati equation).

对象是线性的、代价是二次的，这种组合下，最优控制一定是状态的线性函数，这是可以证明的。更重要的是那个 P：xᵀPx 恰好等于"从状态 x 出发，用最优控制，以后最少还要付多少总代价"。这个"剩余代价"叫价值函数 V(x)。很多强化学习算法（值函数方法、PPO 里的"评论家"）学的就是它；第 ⑧ 步的随机搜索则跳过它，直接改策略。With a linear plant and a quadratic cost, it can be proved that the optimal control is always a linear function of the state. More important still is P: xᵀPx equals exactly “the least total cost still to be paid, starting from state x and using the optimal control”. This “cost-to-go” is called the value function V(x). Many reinforcement learning algorithms (value-based methods, the “critic” in PPO) learn exactly this; the random search of step ⑧ skips it and improves the policy directly.

Yet however expensive torque gets (very large r), K₁ does not drop to 4.9: if it were only a little above 4.9, the net “positive spring” K₁ − mgl would be almost 0, the arm would take a very long time to come back, and the force used along the way would add up to more. The most economical way is to flip the unstable pole +4.3 over to −4.3, which takes exactly K₁ − mgl ≈ mgl, that is, K₁ ≈ 2mgl ≈ 9.8 (the other pole, −4.5, barely moves). This is the mathematical answer to “holding it steady with the least force”.

LQR 有一个前提：要知道全部状态。可编码器只测角度，角速度得自己算。最直接的办法是差分：这次的角度减上次的，除以时间间隔。问题在于，测量噪声也被除以了那个很小的时间间隔。卡尔曼滤波（Kalman 1960）换了个思路：手里既然有模型，就让模型一直往前推算状态，每来一次测量，按"模型和测量各有多可信"把推算值往测量那边拉一点。LQR has a precondition: it needs to know the whole state. But the encoder measures only the angle; the angular velocity has to be worked out. The most direct way is differencing: this angle minus the previous one, divided by the time between them. The trouble is that the measurement noise gets divided by that tiny time interval too. The Kalman filter (Kalman 1960) takes a different approach: since there is a model to hand, let the model keep projecting the state forward, and each time a measurement arrives, pull the projection a little towards it, according to “how far the model and the measurement can each be trusted”.

测量和预测之间的差（叫新息），一部分来自噪声，一部分来自模型的误差。L 由两者的大小决定：测量越准，越信测量；模型越准，越信预测。卡尔曼滤波求的 L，让估计误差的方差最小；它的方程和 LQR 的 Riccati 方程长得一模一样，只是转了个方向，所以说估计和控制是一对对偶问题。The difference between measurement and prediction (called the innovation) comes partly from noise and partly from errors in the model. L is set by the sizes of the two: the more accurate the measurement, the more it is trusted; the more accurate the model, the more the prediction is trusted. The L that the Kalman filter finds makes the variance of the estimation error as small as possible; its equation looks exactly like LQR’s Riccati equation, only turned the other way round, which is why estimation and control are said to be a pair of dual problems.

  • LQR 解出来就是 PD。对"力矩 → 角速度 → 角度"这种对象，最优的线性反馈就是角度和角速度的加权和，正是 PD。第 ① 步说"PD 是物理上必需的"，这里得到了证明。不同的是，LQR 的两个数是从"多在乎什么"算出来的；而且对象一复杂（几十个关节、互相耦合），手调 PID 无从下手，LQR 照样一行公式。LQR’s solution is a PD. Step ① said “PD is what physics requires”; here that is proved. The difference is that LQR’s two numbers are computed from “what matters, and how much”; and once the plant gets complicated (dozens of joints, all coupled to one another), you would not know where to begin tuning PIDs by hand, while LQR is still a one-line formula.
  • K 里藏着"往后的全部"。LQR 优化的是从现在到无穷远的总代价，答案却只是两个数。因为对线性对象，最优策略不随时间变，"往后怎么走"全被压进了 K 和 P 里。这是"离线算好、在线只算公式"的极致。K hides “everything from here on”. LQR optimises the total cost from now to infinity, yet the answer is just two numbers. That is because, for a linear plant, the optimal policy does not change over time, so “how to go on from here” is entirely compressed into K and P. This is “compute it offline, just evaluate a formula online” taken to its extreme.
  • 估计和控制要一起考虑。差分的力矩在乱抖，并不是 LQR 设计得不好，而是它以为的状态是错的。机器人里的状态估计（上一篇的"感知与估计"层）因此和控制紧紧连在一起，有时干脆被合进同一个网络（第 ⑧ 步）。Estimation and control have to be considered together. When the torque jitters wildly with differencing, it is not because LQR was badly designed, but because the state it thinks it is in is wrong. LQR 也有三个明显的短处：它只在线性化的那个点附近好用；它不知道力矩有上限，给出的命令可能根本做不到；它要全部状态。下面三步分别处理离开平衡点（④）、模型不准（⑤）和接触（⑥），第 ⑦ 步再回来处理约束。LQR also has three obvious weaknesses: it works well only near the point where it was linearised; it does not know that torque has a limit, so the commands it gives may be impossible to carry out; and it needs the whole state. Lifting it directly, the motor can raise it only to about 18° (where sin θ = 1.5 ∕ 4.9), and no further.

LQR 在这里会怎样？它的增益是在竖直附近按线性模型算的，对它来说，杆在最下面只是"偏了 180°"，于是它把力矩顶在上限上，一直往一个方向推，推不上去，也不会想到先往反方向荡。这是所有线性控制器的共同局限：它们只认识平衡点附近那一小块地方。离开这块地方，有几种办法：What would LQR do here? Its gains were computed from the linear model near upright, so to LQR the arm at the bottom is simply “180° off”: it pins the torque at the limit and keeps pushing in one direction; it cannot push the arm up, and it never occurs to it to swing the other way first. This is a limitation shared by every linear controller: they know only the small patch around the equilibrium. To get beyond that patch, there are several approaches:

  • 增益调度：在很多个工作点上各做一个线性设计，运行时按当前工况插值。飞机的飞控就是这么做的：不同高度、不同速度，用不同的增益。Gain scheduling: make a separate linear design at each of many operating points, and at run time interpolate between them according to the current conditions. This is how aircraft flight control works: different altitudes and different speeds use different gains.

这一步展示了非线性控制的典型做法：远处用非线性的方法（能量、李雅普诺夫函数）保证"往对的方向走"，近处用线性的方法（LQR）把它稳稳接住，中间用一个有保证的区域来切换。真实的机器人到处都是这种"混合"结构：步态在"脚着地"和"脚离地"之间切换，机械臂在"自由运动"和"接触"之间切换。上面每一种局部的控制器都不难，难的是切换的逻辑，和"在哪个区域里它一定管用"的保证。This step shows the typical recipe of nonlinear control: far away, use nonlinear methods (energy, Lyapunov functions) to guarantee “heading in the right direction”; close in, use linear methods (LQR) to catch it firmly, and switch between them through a region that comes with a guarantee. Real robots are full of this kind of “hybrid” structure: gaits switch between “foot on the ground” and “foot in the air”, robot arms between “free motion” and “contact”. None of the local controllers is hard on its own; what is hard is the switching logic, and the guarantee of “the region in which it is sure to work”.

换一个思路，也可以用模型直接把非线性抵消掉。机械臂的动力学可以写成 M(q)q̈ + C(q, q̇)q̇ + g(q) = τ：惯性矩阵 M 随姿态变，科氏力和离心力 C 随速度变，重力 g 随角度变。计算力矩控制取 τ = M(q)(q̈d + Kdė + Kpe) + Cq̇ + g，把这些全部补掉，每个关节就剩下一个干净的线性对象，再用 PD 去管。它在任务空间的版本叫操作空间控制（Khatib 1987），直接控制手的位置和力，后来成了全身控制的内核（第 ⑥ 步）。代价是：模型要准。模型一不准，抵消不干净，剩下的部分就得靠反馈去扛。怎么扛，是下一步的事。A different approach is to use the model to cancel the nonlinearity outright. A robot arm’s dynamics can be written as M(q)q̈ + C(q, q̇)q̇ + g(q) = τ: the inertia matrix M changes with the pose, the Coriolis and centrifugal terms C change with the velocity, and gravity g changes with the angles. Computed-torque control takes τ = M(q)(q̈d + Kdė + Kpe) + Cq̇ + g, compensating for all of these, so that each joint is left with a clean linear plant, which a PD then controls. Its task-space version is called operational-space control (Khatib 1987): it controls the hand’s position and force directly, and later became the core of whole-body control (step ⑥). The price: the model has to be accurate. If it is off, the cancellation is not clean, and whatever is left over has to be borne by feedback. How to bear it is the business of the next step.

第 ⑤ 步 · 不确定Step ⑤ · Uncertainty

模型不准怎么办：硬扛、估计、学习When the model is wrong: tough it out, estimate, learn

前面几步都默认模型是对的。现实里，模型永远不完全对：负载换了，摩擦随温度变，齿轮有间隙，电机老化。面对"不知道的那部分"，控制理论发展出三类办法：The previous steps all assumed the model was right. In reality, a model is never entirely right: the load changes, friction varies with temperature, gears have backlash, motors age. To deal with “the part you do not know”, control theory has developed three kinds of approach:

  • 硬扛：事先估一个"最多错多少"的范围，按最坏情况设计，保证在范围内都行。代表是 H∞ 鲁棒控制和滑模控制。Tough it out: estimate in advance a range for “how wrong it can be at most”, design for the worst case, and guarantee that everything within that range works. Typical examples are H∞ robust control and sliding mode control.
  • 边用边估计：运行时把不知道的东西估出来，再抵消掉或者把模型改过来。代表是扰动观测器、ADRC、自适应控制。积分项其实也是最简单的一种：它在"估计"一直存在的扰动有多大。Estimate as you go: while running, estimate what is unknown, then cancel it out or correct the model. Typical examples are the disturbance observer, ADRC and adaptive control. The integral term is in fact the simplest of these: it is “estimating” how large a persistent disturbance is.
  • 从重复和多样里学：同一件事做很多遍，把上一遍的误差记下来改下一遍（迭代学习控制）；或者训练时就让它见过各种各样的情况（第 ⑧ 步的域随机化）。Learn from repetition and variety: do the same thing many times, recording the error from the last run to correct the next one (iterative learning control); or let it see all kinds of situations during training (the domain randomisation of step ⑧).

硬扛：滑模控制Tough it out: sliding mode control

滑模控制的想法很直接：先挑一条"走上去就一定会回到目标"的路，再用一个足够大的开关，把状态强行按到这条路上（Utkin 1977）。The idea of sliding mode control is very direct: first pick a path that “is sure to lead back to the target once you are on it”, then use a large enough switch to force the state onto that path (Utkin 1977).

滑模和 H∞ 是"硬扛"的两种风格。滑模在时间里硬扛：一个够大的开关。H∞（Zames 1981，Doyle 等 1989）在频率里硬扛：把"模型在哪些频率上、最多错多少"写进设计，求一个在最坏情况下也稳、也够好的控制器。两者共同的代价是保守：为了最坏的情况，平时也要付出性能（滑模付出颤振，H∞ 付出带宽）。而且最坏情况的范围要事先知道：交互里 2 倍质量加摩擦时，模型误差最多约 4 N·m，k 取 3 就压不住了（误差约 7°），取 4 以上才行。Sliding mode and H∞ are two styles of “toughing it out”. Sliding mode toughs it out in the time domain, with one big enough switch. The price both pay is being conservative: to be ready for the worst case, they give up performance the rest of the time as well (sliding mode pays in chattering, H∞ in bandwidth). And the extent of the worst case has to be known in advance: in the interactive, with 2× the mass plus friction, the model error reaches about 4 N·m at most; set k to 3 and it can no longer hold the state down (the error is about 7°); it takes 4 or more.

另一种思路不去硬压，而是把"模型以外的一切"当成一个看不见的力，边运行边把它估出来，再原样抵消掉。扰动观测器（DOB）用名义模型反算"按理说该用多少力矩"，和实际用的一比，差值就是扰动（Ohnishi 等 1996）。韩京清提出的自抗扰控制（ADRC）走得更远：几乎不要模型，只要知道"力矩对加速度的影响大约有多大"（b₀），其余的一切，包括重力、摩擦、负载、模型误差，统统合成一个"总扰动"f，由一个观测器和状态一起估出来（Han 2009）。Another approach is not to force it down, but to treat “everything outside the model” as an invisible force, estimate it while running, and cancel it out exactly. A disturbance observer (DOB) uses the nominal model to work backwards from the motion to “how much torque this should have taken”, compares that with the torque actually applied, and the difference is the disturbance (Ohnishi et al. 1996). Active disturbance rejection control (ADRC), proposed by Han Jingqing, goes further: it needs almost no model, only a rough idea of “how strongly torque affects acceleration” (b₀). Everything else, including gravity, friction, the payload and model errors, is lumped together into one “total disturbance” f, which an observer estimates together with the state (Han 2009).

θ̈ = f + b₀ u　（f：模型以外的一切）θ̈ = f + b₀ u (f: everything outside the model)u = (u₀ − f̂) ∕ b₀u₀ = Kp(θ目标 − θ̂) − Kdθ̇̂u₀ = Kp(θtarget − θ̂) − Kdθ̇̂

The extended state observer treats f as an extra state and estimates it together with the angle and the angular velocity. Once it is estimated and cancelled, the plant “becomes” the nominal model. The observer bandwidth ωo decides how quickly it estimates: faster rejects disturbances better, but lets in more noise too.

"边用边估计"还有一支历史更长的家族：自适应控制。它不估一个笼统的力，而是估模型里的参数（这里是负载质量 m̂），把控制律跟着改过来（Kokotović 1992 是很好的入门综述）。好处是估出来的东西有物理意义，换了负载能"记住"；麻烦是要知道模型的结构；γ 太大时 m̂ 会来回过冲（交互里 γ = 5、负载 3 kg 时）；运动不够"丰富"、又有噪声时，参数还会慢慢漂走。自校正调节器（Åström & Wittenmark 1973）是另一种做法：边运行边用最小二乘辨识一个小模型，每次辨识完重新设计控制器。“Estimating as you go” has another branch with a longer history: adaptive control. Rather than estimating a catch-all force, it estimates the parameters in the model (here the payload mass m̂) and changes the control law to match (Kokotović 1992 is a good introductory survey). The advantage is that what it estimates has a physical meaning, so it can “remember” a new payload; the trouble is that it needs to know the structure of the model; with γ too large, m̂ overshoots back and forth (in the interactive, with γ = 5 and a 3 kg payload); and when the motion is not “rich” enough and there is noise, the parameters also slowly drift away. The self-tuning regulator (Åström & Wittenmark 1973) is another approach: while running, it identifies a small model by least squares, and redesigns the controller after each identification.

如果同一个动作要重复成千上万遍（产线上的机械臂、晶圆台），还有一种更朴素的办法：迭代学习控制（Arimoto 等 1984）。每做完一遍，把这一遍每个时刻的误差记下来，按时间对齐加进下一遍的前馈里。几遍以后，所有"每次都一样"的误差（模型误差、重复的扰动）都被学掉了，剩下的只有每次都不一样的噪声。积分是在时间上累积误差，迭代学习是在"遍数"上累积，这是"从经验里学"在控制里最早、最干净的版本。If the same motion has to be repeated thousands upon thousands of times (robot arms on a production line, wafer stages), there is an even plainer method: iterative learning control (Arimoto et al. 1984). After each run, record the error at every moment of that run, and add it, aligned in time, into the feedforward of the next run. After a few runs, every error that is “the same every time” (model errors, repeating disturbances) has been learned away, and all that is left is the noise that differs from run to run. An integral accumulates error over time; iterative learning accumulates it over “runs”. This is the earliest and cleanest version of “learning from experience” in control.

第 ⑥ 步 · 接触Step ⑥ · Contact

碰到东西：位置、阻抗和力Touching things: position, impedance and force

到目前为止，目标都是"到某个位置"。可机器人一碰到东西，这个目标本身就成了问题。设想机械臂要把一个零件压进孔里，零件的位置差了 1 毫米，而墙和零件都很硬：一个"必须到达目标位置"的控制器会用尽全力去消灭这 1 毫米误差，力可能大到压坏零件，或者顶到电机上限。人不是这样做事的：手碰到桌面，会自然地"软"下来，按着它、贴着它滑。机器人要做的，是控制碰到时有多软，或者直接控制用多大的力。So far, the goal has always been “get to a certain position”. But the moment a robot touches something, that goal itself becomes a problem. Imagine a robot arm pressing a part into a hole, with the part’s position 1 millimetre off and both the walls of the hole and the part very stiff: a controller that “must reach the target position” will use all its strength to wipe out that 1 millimetre of error, and the force may grow large enough to crush the part, or push the motor to its limit. People don’t work that way: when a hand touches a table, it naturally goes “soft”, pressing on it and sliding along it. What a robot needs to do is control how soft it is when it touches, or directly control how much force it uses.

When it touches the wall, it and the wall are like two springs joined in series, and the pushing force is set by the softer of the two.

位置控制其实也是一根弹簧，只不过刚度极大（Kp = 20 000 N/m）。目标在墙里 3 cm，它就要用 20 000 × 0.03 = 600 N 去推；墙越硬，越接近这个数，直到电机顶到上限。把 K 降到 800 N/m，同样 3 cm 的"误差"只换来约 24 N。软硬不是好坏，而是一个可以按任务选择的量：插销要软，钻孔要硬。Position control is really a spring too, just an extremely stiff one (Kp = 20 000 N/m). Lower K to 800 N/m, and the same 3 cm of “error” buys only about 24 N. Soft or stiff is not a matter of good or bad but a quantity you choose to suit the task: inserting a peg wants soft, drilling wants stiff.

阻抗控制需要能控力矩的关节：电机的力要能"透明"地传到末端。高减速比的工业关节做不到，就反过来：用力传感器量出接触力，算出"该让多少"，再交给很硬的位置环去执行，这叫导纳控制。两者是一对：阻抗是"给位移、出力"，导纳是"量到力、出位移"。直接控制力本身（力控）也可以，但它只在接触时才有意义，碰到之前要先慢慢靠近。Impedance control needs joints whose torque can be controlled: the motor’s force has to reach the tip “transparently”. Industrial joints with high gear ratios cannot do this, so it is done the other way round: a force sensor measures the contact force, the controller works out “how far to give way”, and hands that to a very stiff position loop to carry out. This is called admittance control. The two are a pair: impedance is “given a displacement, produce a force”; admittance is “measure a force, produce a displacement”. Controlling the force itself directly (force control) also works, but it only means something in contact, so before touching, the robot has to approach slowly.

全身控制：很多个接触、很多个任务Whole-body control: many contacts, many tasks

一台足式机器人同时有几只脚着地，手还可能拿着东西。每一处接触都有自己的规矩：脚底的力只能往上推，不能把地面往下拉；水平方向的力不能太大，否则会打滑（摩擦锥）。还要同时照顾很多任务：身体别倒，手伸到那里，脚别滑，关节别超限。全身控制（WBC）最早用任务空间投影，按优先级把这些任务一层层叠起来（Sentis & Khatib 2005）；今天多半写成一道大约每毫秒解一次的二次规划：A legged robot has several feet on the ground at once, and its hands may be holding something as well. Every contact has its own rules: the force under a foot can only push up, never pull the ground down; the horizontal force must not be too large, or the foot slips (the friction cone). And many tasks have to be looked after at the same time: the body must not fall, the hand must get where it is going, the feet must not slip, the joints must stay within their limits. The advantage of a quadratic program is that it is convex, with no local optima to get stuck in, and can be solved reliably in under a millisecond.

全身控制只看"这一毫秒"，不往前看：它不知道下一步脚该落在哪、身体该怎么倾斜才能在 0.3 秒后接住自己。所以它上面总还要有一层往前看的东西，这就是下一步的 MPC。Whole-body control looks only at “this millisecond”, never ahead: it does not know where the next footstep should land, or how the body should lean to catch itself 0.3 seconds from now. 第 ⑦ 步 · 往前看Step ⑦ · Looking ahead

往前看：模型预测控制Looking ahead: model predictive control

回到摆杆。力矩上限 2.5 N·m，只有重力最大力矩的一半，要从最下面甩到竖直。第 ④ 步用能量泵加 LQR 解决了它，可那是专门为摆杆想出来的技巧。有没有一种不需要专门技巧、换一个问题也能用的办法？Back to the pendulum arm. The torque limit is 2.5 N·m, only half of gravity’s largest torque, and the arm has to be swung from the very bottom up to upright. Step ④ solved this with an energy pump plus LQR, but that was a trick thought up specially for the pendulum. Is there a method that needs no special tricks and still works when the problem changes?

有：把第 0 步那道题，在每一个控制周期里现场解一遍。从刚测到的状态出发，用模型往前预测一段时间，在力矩上限之内找出让总代价最小的一串力矩；只执行第一个；下一个周期，从新的测量出发，再解一遍。这就是模型预测控制（MPC），也叫滚动时域控制。There is: solve the problem from step 0 on the spot, once in every control cycle. Starting from the state just measured, use the model to predict a stretch of time ahead, and within the torque limit find the sequence of torques with the smallest total cost; carry out only the first; in the next cycle, start from the new measurement and solve it again. This is model predictive control (MPC), also called receding horizon control.

从当前测到的状态出发，挑一串未来 N 步的命令，让预测出来的总代价最小，而且每个命令都不超过上限。只执行这串命令的第一个，下一个周期丢掉剩下的，从新的测量重新算。Starting from the state measured now, pick a string of commands for the next N steps that makes the predicted total cost smallest, with no command exceeding the limit. Carry out only the first command of the string; in the next cycle, throw away the rest and recompute from the new measurement.

"只执行第一个、每步重算"是 MPC 的关键：计划里后面那些命令，用的是越来越不准的预测；而每一步重算都从实际测到的状态出发，模型的误差、意外的扰动，都会在下一次计划里被看见、被纠正。所以 MPC 虽然每一步都在做开环的规划，整体上却是一个闭环的反馈控制器。约束（力矩上限、安全区域）直接写在题目里，计划天生就满足它们，这是 LQR、PID 都做不到的。“Carry out only the first, recompute at every step” is the key to MPC: the later commands in the plan rest on predictions that get less and less accurate, while each recomputation starts from the actually measured state, so model errors and unexpected disturbances are seen, and corrected, in the next plan. So although MPC makes an open-loop plan at every step, as a whole it is a closed-loop feedback controller. Constraints (torque limits, safe regions) are written straight into the problem, so the plan satisfies them by its very nature, something neither LQR nor PID can do.

摆杆的自然摆动周期约 2π ∕ 4.4 ≈ 1.4 s。只看 0.5 s 的话，"一直往上推"看起来最划算，它就一直顶在上限上、上不去；要先往反方向荡、攒能量，这个好处要大半个摆动（约 0.7 s）以后才显出来。交互里 iLQR 求解时，预测时长短于约 0.8 s 就上不去，1.2 s 时约 1.7 秒到顶。这是 iLQR（梯度法）的表现：从静止出发，"一直顶着"是一个局部最优，短视让"先往回荡"的好处看不见。换成 MPPI，随机的试探先把摆晃了起来，之后"顺着运动方向推、下一刻更高"这种短视的选择本身就在泵能量，0.3 s 的预测也能上去。The arm’s natural swing period is about 2π ∕ 4.4 ≈ 1.4 s. 如果模型是线性的、代价是二次的、没有约束、预测时长无限，MPC 解出来的就是 LQR。所以 LQR 是"在线算好一切"的 MPC 的特例；反过来，MPC 也常拿 LQR 的 xᵀPx 当终端代价 ℓf，用"无穷远以后的估价"来补偿"只看了有限远"。If the model is linear, the cost quadratic, there are no constraints and the horizon is infinite, then what MPC solves for is exactly LQR. So LQR is a special case of MPC, the approach that “works everything out online”; conversely, MPC often uses LQR’s xᵀPx as its terminal cost ℓf, using “an estimate of everything from there out to infinity” to make up for “having looked only a finite way ahead”.

每一步怎么解这道题？两类主流的求解方法：How is this problem solved at each step? There are two main families of solvers:

  • 梯度类（iLQR、DDP）：拿着当前的计划，沿着它把模型线性化，从终点往回推，算出"每一步该改多少"，再往前试一遍，反复几次（Mayne 1966，Li & Todorov 2004）。每一次迭代解的其实就是一个随时间变化的 LQR。快、准，但要模型可导，还可能陷在局部最优里；上一步的解拿来做下一步的初值（热启动），会大大加快。Gradient-based (iLQR, DDP): take the current plan, linearise the model along it, work backwards from the end to find “how much to change each step”, try it forwards again, and repeat a few times (Mayne 1966, Li & Todorov 2004). Each iteration is in fact solving a time-varying LQR. Fast and accurate, but the model has to be differentiable, and it can get stuck in a local optimum; using the previous step’s solution as the starting point for the next (a warm start) speeds it up a great deal.
  • 采样类（MPPI，还有 CEM 交叉熵方法：反复采样、留下最好的一批、再在它们附近采样）：随机试几百上千条力矩序列，每条都用模型推一遍、算出代价，按 exp(−代价 ∕ λ) 加权平均成新的计划（Williams 等 2016）。不需要模型可导，代价可以不光滑（碰撞、离散事件），天然适合 GPU 并行；代价是样本少时动作发抖，预测越长越难采到好的。Sampling-based (MPPI, and also CEM, the cross-entropy method: sample repeatedly, keep the best batch, then sample again around them): try hundreds or thousands of random torque sequences, roll each one out through the model and work out its cost, and average them, weighted by exp(−cost ∕ λ), into a new plan (Williams et al. 2016). The model need not be differentiable, the cost can be non-smooth (collisions, discrete events), and it suits parallel computation on a GPU naturally; the price is jittery motion when there are few samples, and the longer the horizon, the harder it is to sample good ones.

  • 没人教，它自己找到了"先往回荡"。能量泵是人想出来的技巧；MPC 只知道代价和模型，荡秋千的动作是优化自己找出来的。换一个问题（双摆、机械臂、汽车），只要换模型和代价，方法不用变。Nobody taught it; it found “swing back first” on its own. The energy pump is a trick a person thought up; MPC knows only the cost and the model, and the swinging motion was found by the optimisation itself. For a different problem (a double pendulum, a robot arm, a car), only the model and the cost need changing; the method stays the same.
  • 约束是题目的一部分。计划里的力矩一直贴着上限，却从不超过：它知道自己只有这么点力气，所以计划里早早就开始荡。LQR 给出的命令则可能是电机根本做不到的。Constraints are part of the problem. The torques in the plan keep hugging the limit yet never go past it: it knows it only has this much strength, so the plan starts swinging early. LQR, by contrast, may give commands the motor simply cannot carry out.
  • 反馈来自重新规划。"只规划一次"时，计划本身没错，可真实的杆一受到扰动，就和模型以为的分道扬镳，到了顶上停不住，一两秒就掉下来；模型有误差时，干脆到不了顶。每一步从实测重算，这两件事都消失了。Feedback comes from re-planning. With “Plan once”, the plan itself is fine, but as soon as the real arm is disturbed it parts ways with what the model believes, cannot stay put at the top, and falls within a second or two; with a wrong model, it does not even reach the top. Recompute from the measurement at every step, and both problems disappear.
  • 代价是算力和模型。这里每步要算零点几到几毫秒，控制周期是 20 ms，来得及。可一台人形机器人有几十个关节，完整模型的 MPC 很难在 1 ms 里算完，所以工程上常用简化模型（单刚体、质心动力学）做 MPC，每秒几十到一百次，下面再接每秒 500 到 1000 次的全身控制（第 ⑥ 步）。MIT 的四足 Cheetah 3 用的就是这种简化模型的 MPC：凸二次规划每秒几十次算出每只脚该用多大的力，再用腿的雅可比矩阵直接换成关节力矩（Di Carlo 等 2018）；后来的 Mini Cheetah 在这两者之间又加了一层全身控制（Kim 等 2019）。The price is computing power and a model. MPC 并不新。1970 年代末，化工厂和炼油厂就开始用它（Richalet 等 1978）：那里的过程很慢，几分钟算一次也来得及，而"贴着约束运行"直接意味着多赚钱（Qin & Badgwell 2003 统计了它在工业里的应用）。它进入机器人，是算力一路变快的结果：同样的算法，从几分钟一次，到每秒几百次。到这里，"模型"在控制器里的位置变了：在 LQR 里，模型只在设计时用一次，之后就"蒸发"进了增益 K；在 MPC 里，模型每一步都在线上被用一遍。第 ⑨ 步会把"模型放在哪"这件事完整地理一遍。MPC is not new. In the late 1970s, chemical plants and oil refineries were already using it (Richalet et al. 1978): their processes are slow, so computing once every few minutes is fast enough, and “running right up against the constraints” translates directly into more profit (Qin & Badgwell 2003 surveys its industrial applications). It reached robotics because computing kept getting faster: the same algorithm went from once every few minutes to hundreds of times a second. At this point the model’s place in the controller has changed: in LQR, the model is used only once, at design time, and then “evaporates” into the gain K; in MPC, the model is used online at every single step. Step ⑨ will sort out the whole question of “where the model goes”.

第 ⑧ 步 · 学出来Step ⑧ · Learning

学出来：强化学习、模仿学习，和给它们兜底的安全滤波Learning the controller: reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and the safety filter that backs them up

到第 ⑦ 步为止，所有方法都需要有人写下模型：LQR 要 A 和 B，MPC 要 f，滑模要误差的上界。可有些东西的模型极难写准：脚和碎石的接触，手指和软布的摩擦，一辆车在冰面上打滑。强化学习（RL）换了一条路：不写模型，让控制器自己试，按结果的好坏去改。Up to step ⑦, every method needed someone to write down a model: LQR needs A and B, MPC needs f, sliding mode needs an upper bound on the error. But some things are extremely hard to model accurately: a foot’s contact with loose gravel, the friction between fingers and soft cloth, a car skidding on ice. Reinforcement learning (RL) takes a different road: write no model, let the controller try things for itself, and change it according to how good or bad the results are.

它解的仍然是第 0 步那道题，只是换了说法：代价换成"回报"（奖励的总和，越大越好），控制器叫"策略" π。关键的工具是第 ③ 步出现过的价值函数：It still solves the problem from step 0, only put differently: cost is replaced by “return” (the sum of the rewards, the bigger the better), and the controller is called a “policy”, π. The key tool is the value function we met in step ③:

V(x) = minu [ ℓ(x, u) + γ · V(f(x, u)) ]π(x) = 让上式括号最小的那个 uπ(x) = the u that minimises the bracket above

从状态 x 出发、以后最少要付的代价，等于"这一步付的"加上"走到下一个状态以后、最少还要付的"（远处的打一点折扣 γ）。只要知道了 V，每一步挑让括号最小的 u，就是最优策略。The least cost still to be paid from state x onwards equals “what this step costs” plus “the least still to be paid after reaching the next state” (costs further in the future are discounted a little, by γ). Once V is known, picking at every step the u that minimises the bracket is the optimal policy.

这是 Bellman 方程，动态规划的核心：一条最优路线的后半段，自己也一定是最优的。LQR 是它在"线性对象 + 二次代价"下的精确解，V(x) = xᵀPx；MPC 是只往前展开 N 步、后面用 ℓf 近似 V 的版本。强化学习则是不知道 f 的时候，用试出来的样本去近似 V 或者直接近似 π。所以强化学习就是近似的动态规划（Bertsekas 2019，Recht 2019）。This is the Bellman equation, the heart of dynamic programming: the second half of an optimal route must itself be optimal. LQR is its exact solution for a “linear plant + quadratic cost”, V(x) = xᵀPx; MPC is the version that unrolls only N steps ahead and approximates V beyond them with ℓf. Reinforcement learning is what you do when f is not known: it uses samples gathered by trial to approximate V, or to approximate π directly. So reinforcement learning is approximate dynamic programming (Bertsekas 2019, Recht 2019).

强化学习的代价是样本：它要试很多次。在真机上试，机器会磨损、会摔坏，所以今天的做法几乎都是在仿真器里试。那样的话，模型并没有消失，只是从控制器里搬到了仿真器里（第 ⑨ 步）。The price of reinforcement learning is samples: it has to try a great many times. Trying on a real machine wears it out and can break it in a fall, so today it is nearly always done in a simulator. In that case the model has not disappeared; it has only moved from the controller into the simulator (step ⑨).

最简单的强化学习是随机搜索（ARS，Mania 等 2018）：策略只有两个数 u = −k₁φ − k₂ω，每一轮朝几个随机方向各往正、反两边试一试，哪边回报高，就往哪边挪一步。它不用求导、不用模型，却和更复杂的算法一样能学会很多控制任务。The simplest reinforcement learning is random search (ARS, Mania et al. 2018): the policy is just two numbers, u = −k₁φ − k₂ω; each iteration tries a few random directions, each one forwards and backwards, and moves a step towards whichever side scored the higher return. It needs no derivatives and no model, yet it learns many control tasks as well as more complex algorithms do.

k ← k + α ∕ (N σR) · Σi [R(k + νδi) − R(k − νδi)] · δi

新的增益 = 旧的增益 + 步长 × 每个随机方向上"正着试的回报减反着试的回报"乘上这个方向，加起来再平均。New gains = old gains + step size × the average, over the random directions, of “the return from trying it forwards minus the return from trying it backwards” times that direction.

两边一比，就知道沿这个方向走，回报是变好还是变坏、变得多快：这是用试验估计出的梯度。除以回报的标准差 σR，让步长不受回报大小的影响。策略梯度、PPO 这些更复杂的算法，骨子里也是"多试、按回报加权、往好的方向挪"。Comparing the two sides tells you whether moving along this direction makes the return better or worse, and how quickly: this is a gradient estimated from trials. Dividing by the standard deviation of the returns, σR, keeps the step size from depending on how big the returns are. More complex algorithms such as policy gradient and PPO are, at heart, also “try a lot, weight by return, move in the better direction”.

它找到的就是 LQR 的答案：同一道题，一个用模型一次算出，一个用试验慢慢摸出来。在这么简单的问题上，有模型当然更省事；可当模型写不出来（接触、软体、视觉）时，"试"是唯一的路。What it finds is LQR’s answer: the same problem, one method working it out in one go from a model, the other feeling its way there slowly by trial. On a problem this simple it is of course easier to have a model; but when the model cannot be written down (contact, soft bodies, vision), “trying” is the only way.

Its goal is the same as the robust control of step ⑤: to work across a whole range of masses. The method differs: instead of solving equations for the worst case, it sees every case in the range and optimises the average performance; the range of the randomisation (here 0.5–2.5 kg) still has to be chosen by people.

深度学习在控制里做什么What deep learning does in control

把两个增益换成一个有几百万个参数的神经网络，就是深度强化学习。网络在这里扮演几种角色，常常同时出现：Replace the two gains with a neural network of several million parameters, and you have deep reinforcement learning. Here the network plays several roles, often at the same time:

  • 当策略：从一大堆输入（几十个关节角、陀螺仪、几帧历史，甚至图像）直接算出命令。它能表示 PD 表示不了的东西：随地形、随姿态、随速度变化的"增益"，相当于一张连续的、自动学出来的增益调度表。常用 PPO（Schulman 等 2017）一类的算法来训练。As the policy: it computes the command directly from a large pile of inputs (dozens of joint angles, the gyroscope, a few frames of history, even images). It can represent things PD cannot: “gains” that change with the terrain, the posture and the speed, in effect a continuous gain-scheduling table that is learned automatically. It is usually trained with algorithms of the PPO kind (Schulman et al. 2017).
  • 当估计器：从一段本体感觉的历史里，猜出看不见的东西（地面多滑、负载多重、脚有没有踩稳）。RMA 在仿真里练一个这样的小网络，上线后几分之一秒就能适应新地面（Kumar 等 2021）。这就是"学出来的自适应控制"。As the estimator: from a short history of proprioception (the robot’s sense of its own body), it guesses things that cannot be seen (how slippery the ground is, how heavy the load is, whether a foot is planted firmly). RMA trains a small network like this in simulation, and once deployed it adapts to new ground within a fraction of a second (Kumar et al. 2021). This is “learned adaptive control”.
  • 当模型：从数据里学出动力学，交给 MPC 或者拿来"想象"（第 ⑨ 步）。As the model: it learns the dynamics from data, to be handed to MPC or used for “imagining” (step ⑨).
  • 模仿示范：有些任务的奖励极难写（叠衣服、穿针），却很容易示范。模仿学习把人的示范当标准答案，直接监督学习一个"看到什么 → 做什么"的网络；它"一步错、步步错"的毛病，有 DAgger 这类办法来补（Ross 等 2011）。近几年的扩散策略（Chi 等 2023）和 ACT（Zhao 等 2023）一次输出一小段动作；ACT 只用约 10 分钟的示范，就学会了打开调料盒、插电池这类精细的双手操作。把它接到在海量图文上预训练过的大模型上，就是 VLA（RT-2，π0）。Imitating demonstrations: for some tasks the reward is extremely hard to write (folding clothes, threading a needle), yet they are easy to demonstrate. Imitation learning takes human demonstrations as the correct answers and trains a “what it sees → what it does” network directly by supervised learning; its weakness, that one wrong step leads to more wrong steps, is patched by methods such as DAgger (Ross et al. 2011). The diffusion policies of recent years (Chi et al. 2023) and ACT (Zhao et al. 2023) output a short stretch of actions at a time; ACT learned delicate two-handed tasks such as opening a condiment cup and slotting a battery from only about 10 minutes of demonstrations. Connect this to a large model pretrained on vast amounts of images and text, and you have a VLA (RT-2, π0).

一句话总结两者的分工：强化学习回答"怎么做最好"，靠奖励和试错，适合"好坏容易判断、动作难以示范"的事，比如在碎石上不摔倒；模仿学习回答"像人那样做"，靠示范，适合"动作容易示范、好坏难以写成奖励"的事，比如把杯子放进洗碗机。今天的机器人常常两者都用：先模仿，再用强化学习打磨。The division of labour in a nutshell: reinforcement learning answers “what is the best way to do it”, relying on reward and trial and error, and suits jobs where “good and bad are easy to judge but the motion is hard to demonstrate”, such as not falling over on rubble; imitation learning answers “do it the way a person does”, relying on demonstrations, and suits jobs where “the motion is easy to demonstrate but good and bad are hard to write as a reward”, such as putting a cup in the dishwasher. Robots today often use both: imitate first, then polish with reinforcement learning.

给学出来的策略兜底：安全滤波A safety net for learned policies: the safety filter

神经网络策略有一个让工程师不安的地方：说不清它在想什么，也无法证明它不会做出危险的动作。一种越来越常见的做法，是在它外面套一层很薄的安全滤波：平时原样放行，只有当它的命令会让系统在接下来"刹不住"时，才做最小的修改（Ames 等 2017；综述见 Hsu 等 2024）。Neural network policies have one thing about them that makes engineers uneasy: nobody can say what they are thinking, and there is no way to prove they will never do something dangerous. An increasingly common answer is to wrap them in a thin safety filter: most of the time it passes the command through untouched, and only when a command would leave the system “unable to stop” in what follows does it make the smallest possible change (Ames et al. 2017; for a survey, see Hsu et al. 2024).

在所有"不会让安全余量 h 掉得太快"的命令里，挑一个离原来命令最近的。h 是"现在开始全力刹车，还剩多少距离"：h ≥ 0 就还来得及。Of all the commands that “do not make the safety margin h fall too fast”, pick the one closest to the original command. h is “how much distance would be left if you started braking as hard as you can right now”: if h ≥ 0, there is still time.

要求 ḣ ≥ −γh，意思是：余量越小，就越不许它继续变小，于是 h 永远不会穿过 0。这和第 ④ 步的李雅普诺夫函数是同一种数学：一个让"能量"只降不升，一个让"余量"不变成负数。对这辆小车，这道优化有现成的解：给命令加一个上限，原来的命令没超过上限就原样放行。Requiring ḣ ≥ −γh means: the smaller the margin, the less it is allowed to keep shrinking, so h never crosses 0. This is the same kind of mathematics as the Lyapunov function in step ④: one makes the “energy” only fall and never rise, the other keeps the “margin” from going negative. For this cart the optimisation has a ready-made solution: put an upper limit on the command, and let the original command through unchanged if it stays below the limit.

Switch the floor to slippery (it can actually brake at only 2.5 m/s², while the filter still thinks 3.5) and raise γ above 6, and it crashes again.

滤波器的保证只和它的模型一样准：它需要知道"最快能刹多快"。安全不是凭空来的，它是被搬到了一个小而可验证的模型上。这也是"学出来的大网络 + 有模型的小安全层"这种组合越来越常见的原因。The filter’s guarantee is only as good as its model: it needs to know “how hard it can brake at most”. Safety does not come from nowhere; it has been moved onto a small model that can be verified. That is also why the combination of “a large learned network + a small model-based safety layer” is becoming more and more common.

第 ⑨ 步 · 模型Step ⑨ · Models

模型到底放在哪：model-based、model-free 和世界模型Where the model actually sits: model-based, model-free and world models

"基于模型"（model-based）和"无模型"（model-free）是控制和强化学习里最常听到的一对词，也最容易让人糊涂：sim-to-real 的强化学习被叫作无模型，可它明明离不开一个精心搭建的仿真器；LQR 是基于模型的，可它在线运行时根本不碰模型。问题出在"模型"这个词上：它说的是一个能预测"做了这个动作，接下来会怎样"的东西，也就是第 0 步的 f。关键不在于有没有用到它，而在于它放在哪、什么时候用。数一数，一共有五个位置：“Model-based” and “model-free” are the pair of terms you hear most often in control and reinforcement learning, and also the pair most likely to confuse: sim-to-real reinforcement learning is called model-free, yet it plainly cannot do without a carefully built simulator; LQR is model-based, yet when it runs online it never touches the model at all. The trouble lies in the word “model”: it means something that can predict “what happens next if I take this action”, that is, the f of step 0. What matters is not whether it is used, but where it sits and when it is used. Count them up, and there are five places in all:

所以"sim-to-real 的强化学习是 model-free"这句话，说的是策略这一层不用模型：网络不知道动力学方程，只是在仿真器里一次次试出来的。但整个系统的模型都在仿真器里，只是从"在线、在控制器里"挪到了"离线、在训练时"。真机上跑的时候，模型确实不再需要了，这是它在线极快、极鲁棒的原因之一。So the saying “sim-to-real reinforcement learning is model-free” means that the policy layer uses no model: the network does not know the equations of motion; it was simply arrived at by trying again and again in the simulator. But the model of the whole system is all there in the simulator; it has only moved from “online, in the controller” to “offline, at training time”. When it runs on the real machine, the model really is no longer needed, which is one reason it is so fast and so robust online.

反过来，"基于模型"也有好几种用法：模型可以用来预测（MPC 往前推演），用来估计（卡尔曼滤波用它去猜看不见的状态），用来设计（LQR 解出增益），用来生成数据（仿真器），用来想象（在模型里练策略）。同一个模型，放在不同的位置，就是不同的方法。第 0 步那张地图的竖轴是"模型从哪来"，横轴其实就是"模型在什么时候、以什么方式被用到"。Conversely, “model-based” also covers several uses: a model can be used to predict (MPC rolls it forward), to estimate (a Kalman filter uses it to guess the states it cannot see), to design (LQR solves for the gains), to generate data (a simulator) and to imagine (training a policy inside the model). The same model, put in different places, makes different methods. In the map of step 0, the vertical axis is “where the model comes from”; the horizontal axis is really “when, and in what way, the model is used”.

有模型和没模型，各自换来了什么？What does having a model buy you, and what does doing without one buy you?

  • 有模型，换来的是"没见过也能算"。MPC 第一次见到这个摆杆，就能自己找出"先往回荡"；换一个目标、加一个约束，不用重新训练。代价是模型要有人写、要写准，而且每一步都要算。With a model, what you gain is “it can work things out without having seen them before”. The very first time MPC meets this arm, it can find “swing back first” by itself; change the goal or add a constraint, and nothing needs retraining. The price is that someone has to write the model, and write it accurately, and that it has to be computed at every step.
  • 没有模型（或模型只在训练时用），换来的是"在线便宜、对写不清的东西鲁棒"。网络在线只做一次前向计算；接触、打滑这些不必写成公式。代价是要大量试验或示范，而且只会做训练时见过的那一类事：换一个任务，往往要重新训练。Without a model (or with the model used only in training), what you gain is “cheap online, and robust to things nobody can write down clearly”. Online, the network does just one forward pass; contact and slipping need not be written as formulas. The price is a great many trials or demonstrations, and it can only do the kind of thing it saw in training: a different task often means retraining.
  • 学出来的模型，想两头都占：从数据里得到模型（不用人写），再拿它去预测、规划或想象（不用在真机上大量试）。这就是世界模型。A learned model tries to have it both ways: get the model from data (no one has to write it), then use it to predict, plan or imagine (no need for lots of trials on the real machine). This is the world model.

世界模型World models

"世界模型"就是学出来的 f，只不过学的对象可以很大：不是一个摆杆的两个状态，而是相机看到的画面、一整个房间、一条街。它通常先把高维的观测（图像）压成一个小的"潜状态" z，再在 z 上学"动作会让世界怎么变"（Ha & Schmidhuber 2018）。A “world model” is simply a learned f, except that what it learns about can be very large: not the two state variables of one arm, but the picture a camera sees, a whole room, a street. It usually first squeezes the high-dimensional observations (images) into a small “latent state” z, then learns, in terms of z, “how actions will change the world” (Ha & Schmidhuber 2018).

With these, you can roll forward “in your head” instead of having to try things in the real world.

在真实世界里试一次很贵（时间、磨损、危险），在模型里试一次几乎免费，还能同时试几千次。只要模型够准，在模型里找到的好动作，在真实世界里也是好的。问题就在"够准"：模型的误差会随着往前推的步数累积，推得越远，越不可信。One try in the real world is expensive (time, wear, danger); one try inside the model is almost free, and thousands can run at once. As long as the model is accurate enough, a good action found in the model is good in the real world too. The catch is “accurate enough”: the model’s errors pile up with every step it rolls forward, and the further ahead it goes, the less it can be trusted.

为什么是强化学习Why reinforcement learning

MPC 加全身控制已经能让机器人跑酷、后空翻，为什么最后普及开来的是强化学习？原因不只是"神经网络更强"，而是好几件事同时凑齐了：MPC plus whole-body control could already make robots do parkour and backflips, so why was it reinforcement learning that finally caught on everywhere? The reason is not just that “neural networks are more powerful”; several things came together at the same time:

  • 最难的部分恰好是最难建模的部分。行走的难点在接触：脚什么时候着地、会不会滑、踩到的是石头还是草。接触是不光滑的、一会儿有一会儿没有的，基于模型的方法要么把它简化掉（ZMP 平脚、单刚体），要么由人事先排好"哪只脚什么时候着地"的状态机。强化学习把这些全扔进仿真器，让策略自己摸索出什么时候迈步、往哪里迈。The hardest part happens to be the part that is hardest to model. What makes walking difficult is contact: when a foot touches down, whether it will slip, whether it has stepped on stone or on grass. Contact is non-smooth, now there and now gone; model-based methods either simplify it away (ZMP’s flat feet, a single rigid body) or have people lay out in advance a state machine of “which foot touches down when”. Reinforcement learning throws all of this into the simulator and lets the policy work out for itself when to step and where to put its feet.
  • 鲁棒来自"见得多"。域随机化让策略在训练时就见过成千上万种地面、质量、摩擦和延迟（第 ⑧ 步的交互里，质量从 0.5 到 2.5 kg 随便变）。目标和第 ⑤ 步的鲁棒控制一样，却不用按最坏情况去解 H∞ 那样的方程，只要定好随机的范围，在里面多见、多练。Robustness comes from “having seen a lot”. With domain randomisation, the policy has already met thousands of different terrains, masses, frictions and delays during training (in the interactive in step ⑧, the mass varies at random from 0.5 to 2.5 kg). The goal is the same as the robust control of step ⑤, but there is no need to solve H∞-style equations for the worst case: you only set the range of the randomness, then see plenty and practise plenty within it.
  • 算力和仿真器变了。GPU 上的并行物理仿真能同时跑几千台机器人，几分钟到几小时就能练出一个步态（Makoviychuk 等 2021；Rudin 等 2021 的标题就叫"几分钟学会走路"）。在这之前，同样的训练要在 CPU 上跑很久，改一次奖励就要等一轮。Computing power and simulators changed. Parallel physics simulation on a GPU can run thousands of robots at once and train a gait in minutes to hours (Makoviychuk et al. 2021; Rudin et al. 2021 is even titled “Learning to Walk in Minutes”). Before that, the same training had to run for a long time on CPUs, and every change to the reward meant waiting through another round.
  • 硬件变得"好仿真"了。新一代电驱关节用大扭矩电机、低减速比，摩擦小、力矩可控，仿真里的电机模型和真机接近得多；液压和高减速比的关节则很难仿真得准。这一点常被忽略，却是 sim-to-real 能成功的前提之一。仿真搬不过去的那部分，还可以用真机数据补：ANYmal 2019 年的工作就专门用真机数据学了一个"电机模型"网络，放进仿真器里。The hardware became “easy to simulate”. A new generation of electrically driven joints uses high-torque motors with low gear ratios, so friction is low and torque is controllable, and the motor model in simulation is much closer to the real machine; hydraulic joints and joints with high gear ratios are hard to simulate accurately. This point is often overlooked, yet it is one of the preconditions for sim-to-real to succeed. What the simulation cannot carry over can be filled in with data from the real machine: the 2019 work on ANYmal learned a dedicated “motor model” network from real-robot data and put it inside the simulator.
  • 在线极其便宜。训练很贵，但部署时只是一次网络前向计算，比在线解一个 MPC 便宜得多，每秒 50 次甚至更快都不成问题，而且计算时间是固定的，不会偶尔超时。Online, it is extremely cheap. Training is expensive, but once deployed it is just one forward pass through the network, far cheaper than solving an MPC problem online: 50 times a second or even faster is no problem, and the computing time is fixed, so there are no occasional overruns.
  • 它把几层合成了一层。估计（地面多滑、身体在往哪倒）、规划（脚落在哪）、控制（关节目标）被压进一个网络，接口上不再丢东西（第 0 步）。It merges several layers into one. Estimation (how slippery the ground is, which way the body is falling), planning (where the feet land) and control (the joint targets) are squeezed into one network, and nothing is lost at the interfaces any more (step 0).

但强化学习并没有替掉所有东西。它下面的关节 PD 和电流环一直都在（第 ① 步），那是网络不该管、也管不过来的快环；它上面，理解任务、操作物体的部分，靠的主要是模仿学习和预训练的大模型（VLA），而不是强化学习；安全的那一层，越来越多地交给有模型、可验证的安全滤波（第 ⑧ 步）。MPC 也没有消失：在很多研究和产品里，它和学习方法结合在一起，或者在需要精确满足约束的地方继续工作。But reinforcement learning has not replaced everything. Below it, the joint PD and the current loop are still there (step ①): fast loops that the network should not handle and could not keep up with. Above it, the parts that understand the task and manipulate objects rely mainly on imitation learning and large pretrained models (VLAs), not on reinforcement learning; and the safety layer is increasingly handed to safety filters that have a model and can be verified (step ⑧). MPC has not disappeared either: in much research and many products it is combined with learning methods, or carries on working where constraints must be met exactly.

最新的方向，和为什么会走到这里The latest direction, and how we got here

把五十年连起来看，人形控制的每一次跃进，都是把表格里的一格从"人写的"换成"在线优化的"，再换成"学出来的"。今天的典型结构是一个分层的"大脑 + 小脑"：最上面一个 VLA，每秒几次地看图、听指令、决定做什么；中间一个快的动作网络，把意图变成连续的动作；下面一个用动作跟踪（让策略在仿真里模仿人的动捕动作）的强化学习练出来的全身控制器；最下面是关节 PD 和电流环。Figure 公开的 Helix 就是这样分的：慢的部分约每秒 7 到 9 次，快的部分每秒 200 次（Figure 2025）；NVIDIA 的 GR00T N1 也是类似的两层（NVIDIA 2025）；底下的全身控制器，常从人的动捕数据学起（HOVER，ASAP，BeyondMimic）。Looking at the fifty years as a whole, every leap in humanoid control has turned a cell of the table from “written by people” into “optimised online”, and then into “learned”. The typical structure today is a layered “brain plus cerebellum”: at the top, a VLA that looks at images, listens to instructions and decides what to do a few times a second; in the middle, a fast action network that turns intentions into continuous motion; below that, a whole-body controller trained by reinforcement learning with motion tracking (having the policy imitate human motion-capture movements in simulation); and at the very bottom, joint PD and the current loop. Figure’s published Helix is divided exactly this way: the slow part runs about 7 to 9 times a second, the fast part 200 times a second (Figure 2025); NVIDIA’s GR00T N1 has a similar two-part structure (NVIDIA 2025); and the whole-body controllers underneath often learn from human motion-capture data (HOVER, ASAP, BeyondMimic).

为什么会走上这条路？因为每一代方法的瓶颈，最后都落在"人写不过来"上：ZMP 时代要人写好步态，DARPA 时代要人写好接触的状态机和几十个任务权重，MPC 时代每加一个新动作都要大量工程。而数据和算力的增长没有上限：仿真可以并行，动捕和遥操作可以规模化，互联网上的图文视频几乎是无穷的。哪种方法能把"更多的数据和算力"直接变成"更好的行为"，哪种方法就会胜出。强化学习和模仿学习恰好是这样的方法。Why did things go this way? Because each generation of methods ended up with the same bottleneck: “more than people can write by hand”. In the ZMP era people had to write the gaits; in the DARPA era, the contact state machines and dozens of task weights; in the MPC era, every new move took a great deal of engineering. The growth of data and computing power, on the other hand, has no ceiling: simulation can run in parallel, motion capture and teleoperation can be scaled up, and the text, images and video on the internet are practically endless. Whichever method can turn “more data and computing power” directly into “better behaviour” will win. Reinforcement learning and imitation learning happen to be such methods.

世界模型扮演什么角色The role of world models

这条路的下一个瓶颈是数据：真机数据又贵又慢，物理仿真器又搭不出真实世界的千变万化。世界模型正好补在这里。第 ⑨ 步说过它的三种用法，放到人形机器人上：The next bottleneck on this road is data: data from real robots is expensive and slow to collect, and physics simulators cannot build the endless variety of the real world. That is exactly the gap world models fill. Step ⑨ described their three uses; on humanoid robots they become:

  • 当数据工厂和训练场：生成仿真器搭不出来的家庭、工厂、街道场景，给 VLA 和策略造训练数据；a data factory and a training ground: generating the homes, factories and street scenes that simulators cannot build, to make training data for VLAs and policies;
  • 当考场：在里面评测一个策略，而不用每次都上真机（Google DeepMind 已经这样评测 Gemini Robotics 的策略，2025）；an exam hall: evaluating a policy inside it, without putting it on a real robot every time (Google DeepMind already evaluates Gemini Robotics policies this way, 2025);
  • 当脑子里的预演：在潜空间里往前推演，挑出更好的动作（规划），或者让策略在"想象"里练习。a rehearsal in the head: rolling forward in latent space to pick out better actions (planning), or letting a policy practise “in imagination”.

从控制的角度看，世界模型意味着"模型"在兜了一圈之后又回来了：从人写的物理方程，到藏进仿真器，到现在从视频和交互里学出来。它最终会更多地出现在离线一侧（造数据、评测、训练），还是走进在线的控制回路（实时规划），现在还没有定论；但它的位置，就在第 0 步那张地图的最下面一行。From the point of view of control, world models mean that “the model” has come back after going full circle: from physics equations written by people, to being hidden inside a simulator, to being learned today from video and interaction. Whether it will end up mostly on the offline side (making data, evaluating, training) or move into the online control loop (real-time planning) is not yet settled; but its place is the bottom row of the map in step 0.

回到开头的问题Back to the opening questions

  • PID、LQR、滑模、MPC 和最新的方法之间是什么关系？它们都在做一件什么事？都在近似地解同一道题：在物理规律和各种限制之内，选出让以后的总代价最小的命令（第 0 步）。不同的是四件事：用了多少模型、什么时候算、往前看多远、怎么对付不准的部分。它们之间有很多直接的亲缘：LQR 用在"力矩 → 角速度 → 角度"的对象上解出来就是 PD；不加约束、预测无限长的 MPC 就是 LQR；滑模面 s = ė + λe 是 PD 的形状；ADRC 的扰动估计干的是积分的活；LQR 的 xᵀPx 是价值函数，强化学习在不知道模型时，要么去学它（值函数方法、PPO 里的"评论家"），要么跳过它、直接改策略（第 ⑧ 步的随机搜索）；MPPI 的加权平均和策略梯度同源；TD-MPC2 是"MPC + 学出来的价值函数"。How are PID, LQR, sliding mode, MPC and the newest methods related? What is the one thing they are all doing? They are all approximately solving the same problem: within the laws of physics and all the various limits, choose the command that makes the total cost from now on as small as possible (step 0). They differ in four things: how much model they use, when they compute, how far ahead they look, and how they deal with what is inaccurate. Many of them are directly related: apply LQR to a “torque → angular velocity → angle” plant and the solution is PD; MPC without constraints and with an infinite horizon is LQR; the sliding surface s = ė + λe has the form of PD; ADRC’s disturbance estimate does the integral’s job; LQR’s xᵀPx is a value function; when the model is unknown, reinforcement learning either learns it (value-based methods, the “critic” in PPO) or skips it and improves the policy directly (the random search of step ⑧); MPPI’s weighted average has the same roots as the policy gradient; TD-MPC2 is “MPC + a learned value function”.
  • 一个机器人有六层、好几级环，控制在其中扮演什么角色？控制是那张"层 × 级"大表里的一列：每一级环里都有一个控制格子，把"目标减估计"的误差变成交给下一级的命令。所以机器人里不是一个控制器，而是每一级一个，各有各的快慢和方法：电流环 PI，关节 PD 或阻抗，全身那一级是二次规划或强化学习策略，再往上是 MPC，最上面已经是决策。A robot has six layers and several loop levels: what role does control play among them? Control is one column in that big “layer × level” table: every loop level has a control cell that turns the error, “target minus estimate”, into a command for the next level. So a robot has not one controller but one per level, each with its own speed and method: PI in the current loop, PD or impedance at the joints, a quadratic program or a reinforcement learning policy at the whole-body level, MPC above that, and at the very top it has already become decision.
  • 不同的控制方法会同时涉及好几层吗？为什么可以？会。LQG 连着估计和控制，MPC 连着规划和控制，强化学习策略把估计、规划、控制压进一个网络，VLA 从感知一直管到动作。可以这样做，是因为六层原本是为了分工方便才切开的，它们解的是同一道题（估计和控制互为对偶，规划就是看得更远、模型更粗的控制）；合起来能减少接口上的损失，只要合起来的题能在这一级的周期里算完。但方法很少跨"级"：每一级的周期给了计算一个硬预算，慢的网络替不了快的电流环。Can different control methods span several layers at once? Why is that possible? Yes. LQG links estimation and control, MPC links planning and control, a reinforcement learning policy squeezes estimation, planning and control into one network, and a VLA covers everything from perception to action. This is possible because the six layers were only cut apart in the first place for a convenient division of labour, and they are solving the same problem (estimation and control are duals of each other, and planning is control that looks further ahead with a coarser model); merging them cuts the losses at the interfaces, as long as the merged problem can be solved within that level’s cycle. But methods rarely cross “levels”: each level’s cycle sets a hard budget for computation, and a slow network cannot stand in for a fast current loop.
  • 深度学习和强化学习在里面起什么作用？强化学习是"近似的动态规划"：在写不出模型（或者模型太复杂、没法在线算）的地方，用试错去找最优策略，模型被挪到了训练用的仿真器里。深度学习提供了能表示复杂策略、价值函数、估计器和模型的函数，还让机器人能直接从图像和示范里学（模仿学习、VLA）。今天在人形机器人上：全身控制多半是强化学习练出来的，任务和操作多半是模仿学习加预训练大模型，最底下的快环仍然是 PD 和 PI。What part do deep learning and reinforcement learning play in all this? Reinforcement learning is “approximate dynamic programming”: where the model cannot be written down (or is too complex to compute online), it uses trial and error to find the optimal policy, and the model moves into the simulator used for training. Deep learning supplies functions that can represent complex policies, value functions, estimators and models, and lets robots learn directly from images and demonstrations (imitation learning, VLAs). On today’s humanoid robots, whole-body control is mostly trained with reinforcement learning, tasks and manipulation are mostly imitation learning plus large pretrained models, and the fast loops at the very bottom are still PD and PI.
  • 最新的发展方向是什么？为什么会走上这条路？方向是"分层的学习系统"：VLA 在上面理解任务，快的动作网络和强化学习练出的全身控制器在中间，PD 和电流环在下面，外面再套安全滤波；世界模型正在成为造数据、评测和规划的工具。原因是每一代方法的瓶颈最后都落在"人写不过来"上（步态、状态机、任务权重、每个新动作的工程），而数据和算力可以不断增长。能把更多数据和算力直接变成更好行为的方法，就会胜出。What is the latest direction, and why did things go this way? The direction is “layered learning systems”: a VLA on top understands the task, a fast action network and a whole-body controller trained with reinforcement learning sit in the middle, PD and the current loop sit at the bottom, and a safety filter wraps around the outside; world models are becoming a tool for making data, evaluating and planning. The reason is that each generation of methods ended up with the same bottleneck, “more than people can write by hand” (gaits, state machines, task weights, the engineering for every new move), while data and computing power can keep growing. Whichever method can turn more data and computing power directly into better behaviour will win.
  • 人形机器人一轮又一轮的高潮，为什么最后是强化学习让它走到了今天？因为最难的部分（接触、摔倒、地形）恰好最难建模，而强化学习可以把它交给仿真器；域随机化让策略靠"见得多"得到鲁棒性；GPU 并行仿真让训练从以天计变成以分钟计；新的电驱关节让仿真和真机更接近；部署时只要一次前向计算，便宜、稳定。几个条件在 2019 年前后才同时具备（第 ⑩ 步）。Humanoid robots have had boom after boom: why was it reinforcement learning that finally brought them to where they are today? Because the hardest parts (contact, falls, terrain) happen to be the hardest to model, and reinforcement learning can hand them over to the simulator; domain randomisation gives the policy robustness from “having seen a lot”; parallel simulation on GPUs cut training from days to minutes; new electrically driven joints brought simulation closer to the real machine; and once deployed it needs just one forward pass, which is cheap and dependable. Only around 2019 were all these conditions in place at once (step ⑩).
  • 世界模型在里面起什么作用？它是学出来的模型 f：能在脑子里往前推演"做了这个动作会怎样"。在机器人里有三种用法：放进 MPC 里规划（TD-MPC2、V-JEPA 2）；让策略在想象里练（Dreamer）；当数据工厂、训练场和考场（Cosmos、Genie 3、Veo）。它要解决的是下一个瓶颈：数据。What part do world models play? A world model is a learned model f: it can play forward in its head “what happens if I take this action”. In robots it has three uses: inside MPC for planning (TD-MPC2, V-JEPA 2); letting a policy practise in imagination (Dreamer); and serving as a data factory, training ground and exam hall (Cosmos, Genie 3, Veo). What it is meant to solve is the next bottleneck: data.
  • 为什么在这么多方法里，PID 依然是"神"？为什么很多时候只用 PD 或 PI？它管的正好是物理上必需的三件事（恢复力、阻尼、抵消恒定扰动）；几乎不要模型；每步几次乘加，能放进每秒几万次的最快的环；看得懂、好验证；有一百年的工程积累。用 PI 还是 PD 看对象：一阶的对象（电压 → 电流、力矩 → 转速、过程控制）自己有阻尼，D 没用还放大噪声，要 I 消稳态误差，所以用 PI；二阶的对象（力矩 → 角度）没有 D 会晃很久，只用 PI 甚至越晃越大，而稳态误差用模型前馈（重力补偿）消更好，积分在位置环里会饱和、碰到东西会越推越狠，所以用 PD（第 ①、② 步）。Among so many methods, why is PID still “king”? Why is it so often only PD or PI? It handles exactly the three things physics demands (a restoring force, damping, cancelling a constant disturbance); it needs almost no model; each step is a few multiply-adds, so it fits into the fastest loops, which run tens of thousands of times a second; it is easy to understand and easy to verify; and it has a hundred years of engineering experience behind it. Whether to use PI or PD depends on the plant. A first-order plant (voltage → current, torque → speed, process control) has damping of its own, so D is useless and only amplifies noise, while I is needed to remove steady-state error: hence PI. A second-order plant (torque → angle) swings for a long time without D, and with PI alone the swinging even grows, while steady-state error is better removed with model feedforward (gravity compensation), and an integral in a position loop winds up and pushes harder and harder when it runs into something: hence PD (steps ① and ②).
  • model-based 和 model-free：模型放在哪？做什么？各有什么好处？各需要什么？模型可以放在五个地方：在控制器里每步在线用（MPC，换任务容易、能处理约束，要准确的模型和算力）；只在设计时用一次（LQR，在线极快、能证明，只在设计范围里好）；在训练用的仿真器里（sim-to-real 强化学习，能处理写不成公式的接触，要够像的仿真器和大量 GPU）；从数据里学（世界模型，不用人写，但数据之外会出错）；或者根本不用（PID、模仿学习，最省事，但能力有限或需要大量示范）。所谓 model-free，通常只是说"策略这一层不用模型"（第 ⑨ 步）。Model-based and model-free: where does the model go, and what does it do? What does each gain, and what does each need? The model can sit in five places: in the controller, used online at every step (MPC: easy to change tasks and handles constraints, but needs an accurate model and computing power); used only once, at design time (LQR: extremely fast online and provable, but good only within the range it was designed for); in the simulator used for training (sim-to-real reinforcement learning: handles contact that cannot be put into a formula, but needs a simulator that is realistic enough and lots of GPUs); learned from data (world models: nobody has to write it, but it goes wrong outside its data); or not used at all (PID, imitation learning: the least effort, but limited in ability or needing many demonstrations). “Model-free” usually just means “the policy itself uses no model” (step ⑨).

检查一下Check yourself

只用 P 控制，Kp = 40 N·m/rad，杆要停在 60°。大约会差多少度？要让误差消失，有哪两种办法？P control only, Kp = 40 N·m/rad, and the arm is to stop at 60°. Roughly how many degrees short will it be? What are two ways to make the error go away?

停住时 Kp·e ≈ mgl·sin θ ≈ 4.9 × sin 55° ≈ 4.0 N·m，e ≈ 4.0 ÷ 40 = 0.1 rad，约 6°（比 Kp = 20 时的 11° 左右少了约一半）。办法一：加积分，让力矩慢慢攒到正好托住重力；办法二：用模型做重力前馈，直接加上 mgl·sin θ*。Once it settles, Kp·e ≈ mgl·sin θ ≈ 4.9 × sin 55° ≈ 4.0 N·m, so e ≈ 4.0 ÷ 40 = 0.1 rad, about 6° (roughly half of the 11° or so at Kp = 20). One way: add an integral, so the torque slowly builds up until it exactly holds up gravity. The other: use the model for gravity feedforward, adding mgl·sin θ* directly.

一个回路的穿越频率是 20 rad/s，相位裕度 50°。测量多了 30 ms 的延迟，相位裕度变成多少？会怎样？A loop has a crossover frequency of 20 rad/s and a phase margin of 50°. If the measurement picks up an extra 30 ms of delay, what does the phase margin become? What happens?

延迟在穿越频率上吃掉 ωc·τ = 20 × 0.03 = 0.6 rad ≈ 34°，裕度剩约 16°：阶跃响应会明显来回晃。要么把带宽降下来，要么想办法减小延迟。At the crossover frequency the delay eats up ωc·τ = 20 × 0.03 = 0.6 rad ≈ 34°, leaving a margin of about 16°: the step response will clearly swing back and forth. Either bring the bandwidth down or find a way to cut the delay.

倒立摆的 LQR，力矩的代价 r 越来越大，增益 K₁ 会一直降到 0 吗？In LQR for the inverted pendulum, if the torque cost r keeps growing, will the gain K₁ keep falling all the way to 0?

不会。K₁ 必须大于 mgl ≈ 4.9 N·m/rad，否则压不过重力那根"负弹簧"。但它也不会停在 4.9 附近：那样杆要很久才回得来，一路上一直在出力，总的力气反而花得更多。r 很大时，LQR 找的是最省力的稳法：把不稳定的极点 +4.3 翻到它的镜像 −4.3，另一个极点 −4.5 不动，这时 K₁ ≈ 2mgl ≈ 9.8。No. K₁ must be larger than mgl ≈ 4.9 N·m/rad, or it cannot overcome gravity’s “negative spring”. But it does not stop near 4.9 either: then the arm would take a very long time to come back, with force applied all the way, so the total effort would actually be greater. When r is very large, LQR finds the most economical way to stabilise it: flip the unstable pole +4.3 over to its mirror image −4.3, leaving the other pole at −4.5, and then K₁ ≈ 2mgl ≈ 9.8.

用差分算角速度：角度噪声 0.3°，每 5 ms 测一次。速度估计的噪声大约多大？为什么卡尔曼滤波能好那么多？Working out angular velocity by differencing: the angle noise is 0.3°, measured every 5 ms. Roughly how large is the noise in the velocity estimate? Why can a Kalman filter do so much better?

两次测量的噪声相减再除以 0.005 s：约 √2 × 0.3° ÷ 0.005 ≈ 85°/s。卡尔曼滤波不是只看最近两次测量，而是用模型把过去所有测量串起来：模型说"速度不可能突然跳 80°/s"，这些跳动就被当成噪声滤掉了。The noise of two measurements is subtracted and then divided by 0.005 s: about √2 × 0.3° ÷ 0.005 ≈ 85°/s. A Kalman filter does not look only at the last two measurements; it uses the model to link all past measurements together: the model says “the speed cannot suddenly jump by 80°/s”, so those jumps are filtered out as noise.

用 iLQR 求解的 MPC，预测时长只有 0.5 s，为什么甩不上去？如果不想把预测拉长，还有什么办法？An MPC solved with iLQR has a horizon of only 0.5 s: why can it not swing the pendulum up? Without lengthening the horizon, what else could you do?

0.5 s 里，任何"先往回荡"的计划都还没来得及换回好处，看起来不如一直往上推；iLQR 只在当前计划附近顺着梯度改，就困在"一直顶着"这个局部最优里。（换成 MPPI 反而能上去：随机的试探先把摆晃起来，之后"顺着运动方向推"这种短视的选择本身就在泵能量。）办法之一是给预测的终点一个更聪明的代价：比如按能量差打分（能量够了就算好），或者用学出来的价值函数当终端代价，这正是 TD-MPC2 的做法。Within 0.5 s, no plan that “swings back first” has had time to pay off yet, so it looks worse than simply pushing upward all the way; iLQR only adjusts the current plan locally, following the gradient, so it gets stuck in the local optimum of “keep pushing”. (Switch to MPPI and it does get up: random probing first gets the pendulum swinging, and after that the short-sighted choice of “push along the motion” itself pumps in energy.) One fix is to give the end of the prediction a smarter cost: for example, scoring it by the energy difference (enough energy counts as good), or using a learned value function as the terminal cost, which is exactly what TD-MPC2 does.

ADRC 的观测器带宽从 60 提到 150，下垂变小了，为什么不一直提高？Raising ADRC’s observer bandwidth from 60 to 150 makes the sag smaller. Why not keep raising it?

观测器越快，就越相信每一次测量，测量里的噪声也被当成"扰动"去抵消：交互里下垂从 2.2° 降到 0.77°，力矩噪声却大了约 11 倍，回到了 PD、PID 的水平，ADRC 原本在噪声上的优势就没了；而且真实系统里还有延迟和没建模的高频动态（比如齿轮和结构的弹性），太快的观测器会把它们激起来，甚至失稳。The faster the observer, the more it trusts each individual measurement, so the noise in the measurements is also treated as “disturbance” and cancelled: in the interactive the sag falls from 2.2° to 0.77°, but the torque noise becomes about 11 times larger, back to the level of PD and PID, so ADRC’s original advantage on noise is gone. A real system also has delay and unmodelled high-frequency dynamics (such as the elasticity of the gears and the structure), which an observer that is too fast will excite, possibly to the point of going unstable.

只在 1 kg 上训练的平衡策略，为什么挂上 2.5 kg 就倒？域随机化付出了什么代价？Why does a balancing policy trained only with 1 kg fall over as soon as 2.5 kg is hung on it? What price does domain randomisation pay?

2.5 kg 时重力"负弹簧"变成 12.3 N·m/rad，比学到的 k₁ ≈ 11 还大，弹簧压不住了。随机化以后，策略挪到 k₁ ≈ 18 附近，所有质量都稳得住，但在 1 kg 时代价高了约 25%：鲁棒是用平时的性能换来的，和第 ⑤ 步的鲁棒控制一样。At 2.5 kg, gravity’s “negative spring” becomes 12.3 N·m/rad, more than the learned k₁ ≈ 11, so the spring can no longer hold it. After randomisation the policy moves to around k₁ ≈ 18 and stays up at every mass, but at 1 kg its cost is about 25% higher: robustness is paid for with everyday performance, just like the robust control of step ⑤.

"sim-to-real 强化学习是 model-free"这句话对吗？Is it right to say “sim-to-real reinforcement learning is model-free”?

对一半。策略本身不用模型：网络里没有动力学方程，它是在仿真器里试出来的。但整个系统离不开模型：模型就是那个仿真器，只是从"在线、在控制器里"挪到了"离线、在训练时"。Half right. The policy itself uses no model: the network contains no dynamics equations; it was found by trial and error in the simulator. But the system as a whole cannot do without a model: the model is the simulator, only moved from “online, in the controller” to “offline, during training”.
