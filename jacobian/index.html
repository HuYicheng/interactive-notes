机器人学 · 雅可比、奇异值和动力学Robotics · Jacobians, singular values and dynamics

关节和手之间Between the Joints and the Hand

机械臂的电机管的是关节：每个关节转多少度、转多快、出多大的力矩。可活是手干的：手要到哪、走多快、往桌上压多大的劲。把这两边连起来的，是一个矩阵：雅可比矩阵 J。它在这一篇里出现三次：关节的转速经过 J 变成手的速度；手上要的力经过 J 的转置 Jᵀ 变成关节的力矩；关节的质量矩阵经过 J 变成"手上感到的质量"。奇异值分解（SVD）是读懂这个矩阵的办法：手往哪个方向好动、往哪个方向使得上劲、在哪里会卡死，都写在它的奇异值和奇异向量里；往哪个方向轻，还要把质量矩阵也放进来（第 ⑦ 步）。前半篇是运动学和静力学：手怎么动、怎么用力、多出来的关节怎么用；后半篇是动力学：质量矩阵、拉格朗日方程、牛顿–欧拉递推，最后用这些模型去控制。全篇只用一个任务：一只三节的平面机械臂，拿海绵擦桌子。A robot arm’s motors look after the joints: how many degrees each joint turns, how fast, and how much torque it produces. But the work is done by the hand: where it has to go, how fast it moves, and how hard it presses on the table. What links the two sides is a single matrix: the Jacobian J. It appears three times on this page: joint rates pass through J to become the hand’s velocity; the force wanted at the hand passes through J’s transpose Jᵀ to become joint torques; and the joints’ mass matrix passes through J to become "the mass felt at the hand". The singular value decomposition (SVD) is how to read this matrix: which way the hand moves easily, which way it can push hard and where it gets stuck are all written in its singular values and singular vectors; which way it feels light needs the mass matrix as well (step ⑦). The first half is kinematics and statics: how the hand moves, how it applies force, and how to use the extra joint. The second half is dynamics: the mass matrix, the Lagrange equations and the Newton–Euler recursion, and finally using these models for control. The whole page uses a single task: a three-link planar arm wiping a table with a sponge.

The arm has three links, all treated as uniform thin rods: the upper arm is 40 cm long and weighs 2.0 kg; the forearm is 35 cm long and weighs 1.5 kg, with the elbow between it and the upper arm; the last link is short, 15 cm long and 0.5 kg, with the wrist between it and the forearm. 两边：关节和手Two sides: the joints and the hand

电机能直接管的，是三个关节角 q = (q₁, q₂, q₃)：q₁ 是上臂和水平线的夹角，q₂ 是前臂相对上臂又转了多少，q₃ 是最后一节相对前臂又转了多少，逆时针为正。活要的却是手的位置 h = (x, y)。从 q 算 h，叫正运动学，几行三角函数就够（第 ① 步）；反过来，从想要的 h 求 q，叫逆运动学，难得多（第 ④ 步）。What the motors control directly are the three joint angles q = (q₁, q₂, q₃): q₁ is the angle between the upper arm and the horizontal, q₂ how far the forearm is turned relative to the upper arm, and q₃ how far the last link is turned relative to the forearm, positive anticlockwise. What the job needs, though, is the hand’s position h = (x, y). Computing h from q is called forward kinematics, and a few lines of trigonometry are enough (step ①); going the other way, finding q from a desired h, is called inverse kinematics, and is much harder (step ④).

这只手臂还有一个特点：关节有三个，手只有两个数要管（x 和 y）。多出来的这一个自由度，意思是同一个手的位置可以摆出无数种姿势：手压着不动，肘还能上下挪。这叫冗余（第 ⑤ 步）。它让逆运动学没有唯一的解，也给了我们挑姿势的余地。This arm has one more feature: there are three joints, but the hand has only two numbers to control (x and y). The one extra degree of freedom means the same hand position can be held in infinitely many postures: with the hand pressed in place, the elbow can still move up and down. This is called redundancy (step ⑤). It leaves inverse kinematics without a unique solution, and it also gives us room to choose the posture.

同一个 J，用三次One J, used three times

  • 速度：v = J q̇。关节的转速乘以 J，就是手的速度（第 ①—⑤ 步）。Velocity: v = J q̇. The joint rates multiplied by J give the hand’s velocity (steps ① to ⑤).
  • 力：τ = Jᵀ F。手上要的力乘以 J 的转置，就是关节要出的力矩（第 ⑥ 步）。Force: τ = Jᵀ F. The force wanted at the hand multiplied by J’s transpose gives the torques the joints must produce (step ⑥).
  • 惯性：Λ⁻¹ = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ。关节空间的质量矩阵 M 经过 J，变成手上感到的质量 Λ（第 ⑦ 步）。Inertia: Λ⁻¹ = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ. Passing through J, the joint-space mass matrix M becomes the mass felt at the hand, Λ (step ⑦).

J 不是一个固定的矩阵，它随姿势变；手臂快伸直时，它会"退化"，手往某个方向怎么也动不了。SVD 把 J 拆成"转、拉伸、再转"三步，拉伸的倍数叫奇异值，这三件事都能从它读出来：手往哪个方向好动（奇异值大的方向），往哪个方向使得上劲（奇异值小的方向），在哪里卡死（奇异值等于 0）。后半篇从质量矩阵出发，推出完整的运动方程，再看怎么用它控制擦桌子。J is not a fixed matrix; it changes with the posture. When the arm is nearly straight, J "degenerates": there is a direction in which the hand cannot move however the joints turn. The SVD breaks J into three steps, "rotate, stretch, rotate again", and the stretch factors are called singular values. Three things can be read from them: which way the hand moves easily (the direction with the large singular value), which way it can push hard (the direction with the small singular value), and where it gets stuck (where a singular value equals 0). The second half starts from the mass matrix, derives the full equations of motion, and then looks at how to use them to control the wiping.

从 q 算 h 很容易，一个 q 只给出一个 h。反过来就难了：给定 h，三个未知数只有两个方程，有无数组解（第 ⑤ 步）。Computing h from q is easy, and each q gives only one h. The other way round is hard: given h, there are three unknowns but only two equations, so there are infinitely many solutions (step ⑤).

正运动学回答"手在哪"。擦桌子要的却是"手怎么动"：关节以某个速度转，手以什么速度走？把上面两个式子对时间求导，ẋ 和 ẏ 都是三个关节转速的线性组合，系数排成一个 2 行 3 列的矩阵，这就是雅可比矩阵 J。名字来自 Jacobi 1841 年一篇讲"函数行列式"的长文（Jacobi 1841），"Jacobian"这个词是后来 Sylvester 起的。Forward kinematics answers "where is the hand". Wiping, though, needs "how does the hand move": if the joints turn at certain rates, how fast does the hand go? Differentiate the two formulas above with respect to time, and ẋ and ẏ each become a linear combination of the three joint rates; arrange the coefficients in a matrix with 2 rows and 3 columns, and this is the Jacobian matrix J. The name comes from a long 1841 paper by Jacobi on "functional determinants" (Jacobi 1841); the word "Jacobian" was coined later by Sylvester.

只转一个关节时，它后面的整段手臂像一根硬杆绕它转，手在一个以这个关节为圆心的圆上走：速度垂直于从关节到手的连线，大小是转速乘以这段距离。把连线 (Δx, Δy) 逆时针转 90°，就是 (−Δy, Δx)。几个关节同时转，速度直接相加：对很小的转动来说，效果是线性的。When only one joint turns, the whole arm beyond it swings round it like a rigid rod, and the hand moves on a circle centred on that joint: its velocity is perpendicular to the line from the joint to the hand, and its size is the rate times that distance. Turning the line (Δx, Δy) anticlockwise by 90° gives (−Δy, Δx). When several joints turn at once, the velocities simply add up: for very small turns, the effect is linear.

J 是一个线性的近似：它把手走的那段圆弧，换成了圆在起点的切线。转得少，切线和圆弧几乎重合；转得多，就分开了。不管转的是哪个关节，相对误差只看转了多少度：转 1° 差 0.9%，转 5° 差 4.4%，转 10° 差 8.7%，转 30° 差 26%。比如肩转 10°：J 预测手走 105.4 mm，真走 105.3 mm，长度几乎一样，方向却偏了 5°，两个落点相距 9.2 mm。所以 J 用在"很短的时间、很小的转动"上：控制器每毫秒左右算一次，这段时间里关节只转零点零几度，这个近似非常准。J is a linear approximation: it replaces the arc the hand travels along with the circle’s tangent at the starting point. For small turns the tangent and the arc almost coincide; for large ones they come apart. Whichever joint turns, the relative error depends only on how many degrees it turns: 0.9% off for 1°, 4.4% for 5°, 8.7% for 10° and 26% for 30°. For example, turn the shoulder by 10°: J predicts that the hand moves 105.4 mm, and it really moves 105.3 mm; the lengths are almost the same, but the direction is 5° off, and the two end points are 9.2 mm apart. So J is used for "very short times and very small turns": a controller recomputes it roughly every millisecond, in which time the joints turn only a few hundredths of a degree, and there the approximation is very accurate.

J 还可以一行一行地看：每一行说的是，手的一个坐标对三个关节各有多"敏感"。手在 0.55 m 时，第二行是 (0.550, 0.205, 0.036)：手的高度对肩最敏感，肩转 0.01 rad（约 0.6°）手升 5.5 mm，腕转同样多只升 0.36 mm。擦桌子要手的高度不变，在这个姿势下，三个关节的转速就得满足 0.550 q̇₁ + 0.205 q̇₂ + 0.036 q̇₃ = 0；姿势一变，这一行的系数也跟着变。J can also be read row by row: each row says how "sensitive" one coordinate of the hand is to each of the three joints. 三维的手臂道理一样，只是正运动学要把每个关节的 4 × 4 变换矩阵一节节乘起来，最常用的约定是 Denavit–Hartenberg 参数（Denavit & Hartenberg 1955），也可以用指数积公式（Lynch & Park 2017）。J 的每一列仍是"只转这一个关节时手的速度"：对转动关节，手的线速度那三行是 zi × (h − oi)，zi 是关节的转轴；手的角速度那三行就是 zi。用 J 把想要的手速度换成关节转速、一小步一小步地控制手臂，这就是 Whitney 提出的"分解运动速率控制"（Whitney 1969）。A 3D arm works the same way, except that forward kinematics multiplies together the 4 × 4 transformation matrices of the joints, link by link. The most common convention is the Denavit–Hartenberg parameters (Denavit & Hartenberg 1955); the product of exponentials formula can also be used (Lynch & Park 2017). Each column of J is still "the hand’s velocity when only this one joint turns": for a revolute joint, the three rows for the hand’s linear velocity are zi × (h − oi), where zi is the joint’s axis, and the three rows for the hand’s angular velocity are simply zi. Using J to convert a desired hand velocity into joint rates, and so controlling the arm in small steps, is what Whitney called "resolved motion rate control" (Whitney 1969).

这一步的要点：J 的第 i 列是"只转第 i 个关节时手的速度"，垂直于关节到手的连线，长度等于这段距离；J 随姿势变，只在小转动里准。下一步把三列合起来看：J 整体把关节的转速"变成"了什么样的手速度？The key points of this step: column i of J is "the hand’s velocity when only joint i turns"; it is perpendicular to the line from the joint to the hand, and its length equals that distance; J changes with the posture and is accurate only for small turns. The next step looks at the three columns together: what kind of hand velocity does J as a whole "turn" the joint rates into?

To see what it does as a whole, first look at a simpler case: a 2 × 2 matrix A, which turns arrows in the plane into arrows in the plane. A’s first column is where the arrow (1, 0) is sent; its second column is where (0, 1) is sent; every other arrow is a combination of these two and goes along with the same combination. Send every arrow of length 1 (their tips trace out a unit circle), and the result is always an ellipse.

A 把单位圆变成椭圆。椭圆最长和最短的两根半轴互相垂直，方向记作 u₁、u₂，长度记作 σ₁、σ₂。圆上被送到这两根半轴端点的两个方向 v₁、v₂，也恰好互相垂直（这是这个定理的关键）。于是 A 做的事可以拆成三步：先把 v₁、v₂ 转到两根坐标轴上（Vᵀ），沿坐标轴各拉伸 σ₁、σ₂ 倍（Σ），再把两根坐标轴转到 u₁、u₂ 上（U）。U 和 V 只转（或翻面），不改长度；所有的拉伸都在 Σ 里。A turns the unit circle into an ellipse. The ellipse’s longest and shortest semi-axes are perpendicular to each other; call their directions u₁, u₂ and their lengths σ₁, σ₂. The two directions on the circle that are sent to the ends of these semi-axes, v₁ and v₂, also turn out to be perpendicular (this is the key to the theorem). So what A does can be split into three steps: rotate v₁ and v₂ onto the two coordinate axes (Vᵀ), stretch along the axes by σ₁ and σ₂ (Σ), and then rotate the two axes onto u₁ and u₂ (U). U and V only rotate (or flip) and never change lengths; all the stretching is in Σ.

奇异值总是大于等于 0，而且对任何形状的矩阵都有，长方形的也行（特征值只对方阵有，还可能是复数）。U、Σ、V 各管一件事：V 说"从哪个方向进"，Σ 说"放大多少倍"，U 说"往哪个方向出"。Singular values are always greater than or equal to 0, and every matrix has them, whatever its shape, rectangular ones included (only square matrices have eigenvalues, and those can be complex). U, Σ and V each have one job: V says "which direction it comes in from", Σ says "how much it is magnified", and U says "which direction it goes out in".

几个特例。旋转的两个奇异值都是 1：它不改长度，只转。拉伸本来就沿着坐标轴，U 和 V 都不用转。剪切看起来没有转，其实是"先顺时针转 57°，沿两根轴拉伸成 1.55 倍和 0.65 倍，再逆时针转 33°"；σ₁σ₂ 正好是 1，面积不变。"压扁成线"的两列在同一条直线上，σ₂ = 0：整个平面被压到一条线上，有一个方向的信息丢了，这时 A 的秩是 1。"带镜像"的那个把 F 翻了过来，行列式是负的（−1.35）；翻面藏在 U 里，奇异值照样是正的，1.52 和 0.89。A few special cases. A rotation has both singular values equal to 1: it changes no lengths and only rotates. A stretch already lies along the coordinate axes, so neither U nor V needs to rotate. A shear looks as if it does not rotate, but it is really "rotate 57° clockwise, stretch along the two axes by factors of 1.55 and 0.65, then rotate 33° anticlockwise"; σ₁σ₂ is exactly 1, so the area is unchanged. In "Flattened" the two columns lie on the same straight line and σ₂ = 0: the whole plane is squashed onto a line, the information in one direction is lost, and the rank of A is then 1. "With a mirror" flips the F over, and its determinant is negative (−1.35); the flip is hidden in U, and the singular values are still positive, 1.52 and 0.89.

J 是 2 × 3 的：三个关节的转速进，两个手速度分量出。SVD 照样成立：J = U Σ Vᵀ，只是 V 是 3 × 3 的，有三个互相垂直的方向 v₁、v₂、v₃；U 是 2 × 2 的，只有 u₁、u₂；奇异值只有两个。v₁、v₂ 被送到 u₁、u₂ 上，各乘 σ₁、σ₂；第三个方向 v₃ 被送到 0。J is 2 × 3: three joint rates go in, two components of the hand’s velocity come out. The SVD still holds: J = U Σ Vᵀ, except that V is 3 × 3, with three perpendicular directions v₁, v₂, v₃; U is 2 × 2, with only u₁ and u₂; and there are only two singular values. v₁ and v₂ are sent onto u₁ and u₂, multiplied by σ₁ and σ₂ respectively; the third direction, v₃, is sent to 0.

J = σ₁ u₁ v₁ᵀ + σ₂ u₂ v₂ᵀJ v₃ = 0

J 是两条"一进一出"的通道之和：沿 v₁ 转关节，手沿 u₁ 走，放大 σ₁ 倍；沿 v₂ 转关节，手沿 u₂ 走，放大 σ₂ 倍。沿 v₃ 转关节，手不动。J is the sum of two "one in, one out" channels: turn the joints along v₁ and the hand moves along u₁, scaled by σ₁; turn the joints along v₂ and the hand moves along u₂, scaled by σ₂. Turn the joints along v₃ and the hand does not move.

把 U Σ Vᵀ 按列乘开就是这样。三个关节的任何一组转速，都能拆成沿 v₁、v₂、v₃ 的三个分量；J 对每个分量分别起作用，再加起来。This is what you get by multiplying out U Σ Vᵀ column by column. Any set of rates for the three joints can be split into three components along v₁, v₂ and v₃; J acts on each component separately, and the results add up.

奇异值还有一种算法：J Jᵀ 是一个 2 × 2 的对称矩阵，它的两个特征值正好是 σ₁² 和 σ₂²，特征向量是 u₁、u₂；同样，JᵀJ 的特征向量是 v₁、v₂、v₃，特征值是 σ₁²、σ₂² 和 0。所以"det(J Jᵀ)"这类式子，说的其实是奇异值（第 ③ 步）。线性代数里"四个基本子空间"（列空间、行空间、零空间、左零空间）彼此的关系，用 SVD 看最清楚：v₁、v₂ 张成行空间，v₃ 张成零空间，u₁、u₂ 张成列空间（Strang 1993）。There is another way to compute the singular values: J Jᵀ is a 2 × 2 symmetric matrix whose two eigenvalues are exactly σ₁² and σ₂², with eigenvectors u₁ and u₂; likewise, JᵀJ has eigenvectors v₁, v₂, v₃ and eigenvalues σ₁², σ₂² and 0. For the 2 × 3 J, one SVD takes just a few microseconds.

这一步的要点：任何矩阵都是"转、拉伸、再转"；奇异值是拉伸的倍数，V 的列是输入方向，U 的列是输出方向；被压成 0 的输入方向，组成零空间。下一步把 J 的 SVD 画出来：它就是"手往哪个方向好动"。The key points of this step: every matrix is "rotate, stretch, rotate again"; the singular values are the stretch factors, the columns of V are the input directions and the columns of U the output directions; the input directions squashed to 0 make up the null space. 第 ③ 步 · 速度椭圆和奇异Step ③ · Velocity ellipses and singularities

奇异：σ₂ = 0Singularity: σ₂ = 0

设想手到了最远处（交互里最远只能拖到差 2.6 mm 的地方，那里 σ₂ = 0.026）：三节杆排成一条直线，从肩到手。这时三个关节到手的连线都在同一条直线上，J 的三列都垂直于它，三列互相平行，J 的秩从 2 掉到 1，σ₂ = 0，椭圆压成一条线段。手只能沿垂直于手臂的方向动；沿着手臂往外，不管三个关节怎么转，一点也动不了。这样的姿势叫奇异位形。Imagine the hand at its farthest reach (in the widget you can drag it only to 2.6 mm short of it, where σ₂ = 0.026): the three links line up in one straight line from the shoulder to the hand. Now the lines from the three joints to the hand all lie on the same straight line, and the three columns of J are all perpendicular to it, so they are parallel to each other; the rank of J drops from 2 to 1, σ₂ = 0, and the ellipse collapses into a line segment. The hand can move only at right angles to the arm; outwards along the arm it cannot move at all, however the three joints turn. Such a posture is called a singular configuration.

麻烦不只在正好奇异的那一点，而在它附近。σ₂ 很小但不是 0 时，手沿 u₂ 还能动，只是要关节转得很多：沿 u₂ 走 1 m/s，关节得转 1/σ₂ rad/s。0.80 m 处 1/σ₂ = 6.9，0.85 m 处是 14.8，0.86 m 处是 28。下一步会看到，这正是"从手的速度反求关节转速"在奇异附近会疯转的原因。The trouble is not just at the exact singular point but also near it. When σ₂ is small but not 0, the hand can still move along u₂, but only with a lot of joint motion: to move at 1 m/s along u₂, the joints have to turn at 1/σ₂ rad/s. At 0.80 m, 1/σ₂ = 6.9; at 0.85 m it is 14.8; at 0.86 m, 28. As the next step shows, this is exactly why "working back from the hand velocity to the joint rates" sends the joints spinning wildly near a singularity.

奇异也不只在够得着的边界上。只要三节和手排在同一条直线上，比如肘完全折回去，J 的三列就都平行，σ₂ = 0；这只手臂上，手离肩 0.6 m、0.2 m、0.1 m 处都有这样的姿势。好在这只手臂多一个关节，在这些地方总能换一个不奇异的姿势；关节没有富余的手臂，就只能绕开它们走。三维的六关节手臂有几种典型的奇异：手腕的两根转轴转到一条线上、手臂伸直、手腕中心落到第一根转轴上。并联机构（几条腿一起撑着一个平台）的奇异分三类：一类和串联手臂一样，平台在某个方向动不了；一类是电机全锁住，平台却还能晃；第三类是两者同时发生（Gosselin & Angeles 1990）。Nor are singularities found only at the edge of the reach. Whenever the three links and the hand line up on one straight line, for example with the elbow folded right back, the three columns of J are all parallel and σ₂ = 0; for this arm there are such postures with the hand 0.6 m, 0.2 m and 0.1 m from the shoulder. Luckily this arm has one joint to spare, so at these places it can always switch to a posture that is not singular; an arm with no spare joints can only steer around them. A six-joint arm in three dimensions has a few typical singularities: two of the wrist’s rotation axes swinging into line, the arm stretched straight, and the wrist centre landing on the first rotation axis. Singularities of parallel mechanisms (several legs holding up one platform together) come in three kinds: one as in serial arms, where the platform cannot move in some direction; one where, with all the motors locked, the platform can still wobble; and a third where both happen at once (Gosselin & Angeles 1990).

这一步的要点：速度椭圆的两根半轴是 σ₁u₁ 和 σ₂u₂；σ₁σ₂ 说手总体上有多好动，σ₁/σ₂ 说各个方向有多不均匀；σ₂ = 0 是奇异，手有一个方向动不了，σ₂ 很小时，往那个方向动要关节转得非常多。The key points of this step: the two semi-axes of the velocity ellipse are σ₁u₁ and σ₂u₂; σ₁σ₂ says how easily the hand moves overall, and σ₁/σ₂ how uneven the directions are; σ₂ = 0 is a singularity, where there is one direction the hand cannot move in, and when σ₂ is small, moving in that direction takes a great deal of joint motion.

第 ④ 步 · 反过来求Step ④ · Working backwards

想要手这样走，关节该怎么转To move the hand like this, how should the joints turn?

控制器真正要解的是反过来的问题：已知手想要的速度 v，求关节转速 q̇。J 是 2 × 3 的，没有普通的逆；v = J q̇ 是两个方程、三个未知数，有无数组解：随便一组解，加上任意多的 v₃ 方向的转动，手的速度不变。总得挑一个。最自然的挑法是：在所有能让手这样走的关节转速里，挑合起来最小的那组，|q̇| 最小。这组解由伪逆 J⁺ 给出。What the controller really has to solve is the reverse problem: given the velocity v we want at the hand, find the joint rates q̇. J is 2 × 3 and has no ordinary inverse; v = J q̇ is two equations in three unknowns, with infinitely many solutions: take any one of them, add any amount of turning in the v₃ direction, and the hand velocity is unchanged. We have to pick one. The most natural choice: of all the joint rates that move the hand this way, pick the set that is smallest overall, the one with the smallest |q̇|. That solution is given by the pseudoinverse J⁺.

1/σ₂ 是危险的那一项。手臂快伸直时 σ₂ → 0，1/σ₂ → ∞：哪怕 v 在 u₂ 方向上只有一点点分量，算出来的关节转速也会大得离谱。1/σ₂ is the dangerous term. As the arm nears full stretch, σ₂ → 0 and 1/σ₂ → ∞: even if v has only the tiniest component along u₂, the joint rates that come out are absurdly large.

伪逆比机器人老：E. H. Moore 1920 年就定义过它，Penrose 1955 年又独立地给出了它，用四个等式唯一地确定（Penrose 1955），所以也叫 Moore–Penrose 逆。对任何形状的矩阵都有，而用 SVD 算它最稳。The pseudoinverse is older than robotics: E. H. Moore defined it as early as 1920, and Penrose arrived at it independently in 1955, pinning it down uniquely with four equations (Penrose 1955), which is why it is also called the Moore–Penrose inverse. Every matrix has one, whatever its shape, and computing it through the SVD is the most stable way.

阻尼：宁可差一点，也别疯转Damping: better slightly off than spinning wildly

阻尼最小二乘把伪逆里的 1/σ 换成 σ / (σ² + λ²)：σ 比 λ 大得多时，两者几乎一样；σ 小到和 λ 差不多时，放大倍数不再往上涨；σ → 0 时，放大倍数反而回到 0。Damped least squares replaces the 1/σ in the pseudoinverse with σ / (σ² + λ²): when σ is much larger than λ, the two are almost the same; when σ shrinks to about λ, the gain stops growing; and as σ → 0, the gain actually falls back to 0.

它求的不再是"手的速度正好是 v"，而是在"手的速度离 v 差多少"和"关节转得多猛"之间取舍：让 |J q̇ − v|² + λ²|q̇|² 最小。λ 是"转得猛"的价格。σ / (σ² + λ²) 在 σ = λ 时最大，是 1/(2λ)，所以不管多靠近奇异，关节转速都有上限。It no longer looks for "the hand velocity is exactly v"; instead it trades off "how far the hand velocity is from v" against "how hard the joints turn", by minimising |J q̇ − v|² + λ²|q̇|². λ is the price of "turning hard". σ / (σ² + λ²) is largest at σ = λ, where it equals 1/(2λ), so however close to a singularity the arm gets, the joint rates have an upper limit.

代价是一路上都跟得没那么紧：哪怕离奇异还远，每个方向都被打了一点折扣。λ 越大越稳，也越"懒"。实际系统常让 λ 随 σ₂ 变：离奇异远时取 0，靠近了才加上去。The price is that it follows less closely all the way: even far from a singularity, every direction is discounted a little. The larger λ, the steadier, and also the "lazier". Real systems often let λ vary with σ₂: 0 when far from a singularity, added only as it gets close.

阻尼最小二乘是 Nakamura 和 Hanafusa（Nakamura & Hanafusa 1986，叫"奇异鲁棒逆"）和 Wampler（Wampler 1986）各自提出的。Maciejewski 和 Klein 用 SVD 估计离奇异多近，只对最小的那个奇异值方向加阻尼，别的方向原样不动（Maciejewski & Klein 1988）；Buss 和 Kim 更进一步，给每个奇异值方向各配一个阻尼（Buss & Kim 2005）。Damped least squares was proposed independently by Nakamura and Hanafusa (Nakamura & Hanafusa 1986, who called it the "singularity-robust inverse") and by Wampler (Wampler 1986). Maciejewski and Klein used the SVD to estimate how close the arm is to a singularity, and added damping only along the direction of the smallest singular value, leaving the other directions as they were (Maciejewski & Klein 1988); Buss and Kim went a step further and gave each singular direction its own damping (Buss & Kim 2005).

截断小奇异值（截断 SVD）是更直接的办法：σ 小于门槛 ε 的方向干脆不管，放大倍数从 1/ε 一下跳到 0。它不会一直抖，可跨过门槛的那一刻，关节转速一下子掉下来；而且跨过之前，倍数已经涨到 1/ε（ε = 3 cm 时是 33），交互里最快的关节照样顶到了 3 rad/s。阻尼的倍数是连续地升上去、再连续地降下来的，所以更常用。Truncating small singular values (truncated SVD) is a more direct fix: any direction whose σ is below the threshold ε is simply ignored, and the gain jumps straight from 1/ε to 0. It does not jitter endlessly, but the moment the threshold is crossed, the joint rate drops all at once; and before it is crossed, the gain has already climbed to 1/ε (33 for ε = 3 cm), so in the widget the fastest joint still hits 3 rad/s. With damping, the gain rises and then falls again continuously, which is why it is used more often.

还有一种连逆都不求的办法：q̇ = α Jᵀ e，直接用 J 的转置把手的误差 e 变成关节的转动（Wolovich & Elliott 1984）。它在奇异附近不会疯转，代价是收敛慢。第 ⑥ 步会看到，Jᵀ 在力里有一个很实在的意思：这就像在手上挂一根弹簧，把手往目标拉。几种办法的比较，见 Buss 2004 的讲义。There is also a method that does not compute any inverse at all: q̇ = α Jᵀ e, which uses the transpose of J directly to turn the hand’s error e into joint motion (Wolovich & Elliott 1984). It does not spin wildly near singularities; the price is slow convergence. As step ⑥ will show, Jᵀ has a very concrete meaning for forces: this is like hanging a spring on the hand that pulls it towards the target. For a comparison of these methods, see the notes by Buss 2004.

这一步的要点：伪逆给出关节转得最少的解，但它的放大倍数是 1/σ，在奇异附近会疯转；阻尼最小二乘把倍数换成 σ / (σ² + λ²)，宁可手差一点，也不让关节转得太猛。The key points of this step: the pseudoinverse gives the solution with the least joint motion, but its gain is 1/σ, so it spins wildly near singularities; damped least squares replaces the gain with σ / (σ² + λ²), accepting a hand that is slightly off rather than letting the joints turn too hard.

第 ② 步里，J 的 SVD 有一个被送到 0 的方向 v₃。三个关节按 v₃ 的比例一起转，手纹丝不动，手臂的姿势却在变。手压在桌上同一个点，姿势有一整串：最后一节可以竖着，可以斜着，肘跟着上下。这一串姿势之间的挪动叫自运动，它的方向就是 J 的零空间。In step ②, the SVD of J had one direction, v₃, that is sent to 0. When the three joints turn together in the proportions of v₃, the hand does not move at all, yet the arm’s posture changes. With the hand pressed on the same spot of the table, there is a whole family of postures: the last link can be vertical or slanted, and the elbow moves up and down with it. Moving within this family of postures is called self-motion, and its direction is the null space of J.

只用伪逆，手沿桌面擦得一点不差，可擦的时候肘几乎一直顶在搁板里（89% 的时间）。加上零空间那一项，z 由两个高度的梯度组成：肘离搁板不到 10 cm 时，z 往"肘的高度变小"的方向推；腕离桌面不到 10 cm 时，z 往"腕的高度变大"的方向推（不然肘一降，腕会戳到桌面）。乘上 N 以后，这些推力只剩零空间里的那部分，不碰手。结果：开始时肘顶在搁板里，0.4 秒后就退了出来；之后手照样擦得一点不差，擦第二遍时肘离搁板最近也有 4.5 cm。With the pseudoinverse only, the hand wipes along the table perfectly, but the elbow is jammed into the shelf nearly all the time (89% of it). With the null-space term added, z is made of two height gradients: when the elbow is less than 10 cm from the shelf, z pushes towards "a lower elbow"; when the wrist is less than 10 cm above the table, z pushes towards "a higher wrist" (otherwise, as the elbow comes down, the wrist would jab into the table). After multiplying by N, only the null-space part of these pushes is left, and it does not touch the hand. The result: the elbow starts inside the shelf and is out of it after 0.4 seconds; from then on the hand wipes just as perfectly, and on the second pass the elbow never comes closer than 4.5 cm to the shelf.

这个办法叫梯度投影：写一个代价（这里是"肘别太高、腕别太低"），沿让代价变小的方向（负梯度）给 z，再投到零空间里。它最早被用来让关节躲开转角的极限（Liégeois 1977），后来被用来躲障碍（Maciejewski & Klein 1985）。This method is called gradient projection: write down a cost (here, "elbow not too high, wrist not too low"), set z in the direction that lowers the cost (the negative gradient), then project it onto the null space. It was first used to keep the joints away from their angle limits (Liégeois 1977), and later to avoid obstacles (Maciejewski & Klein 1985).

更多的任务，排个先后More tasks, in order of priority

要是第二件事也有一个"手"一样的目标，比如"肘的高度要跟着某条曲线走"，就把它写成第二个雅可比 Je（肘的雅可比），只许它用第一个任务剩下的零空间：第一个任务做足，第二个尽量做。这叫任务优先级（Nakamura, Hanafusa & Yoshikawa 1987，最早的版本是 1981 年）。任务可以一层层往下排（Siciliano & Slotine 1991）；人形机器人就是这样同时管重心、双脚、双手和头的（Sentis & Khatib 2005）。这里有一种新的奇异：两个任务本身都不奇异，可第二个任务在第一个的零空间里使不上劲（Je N 掉秩），两个任务在打架。这叫算法奇异。可以像第 ④ 步那样加阻尼；Chiaverini 给了另一种任务优先级的写法，两个任务打架时也不会疯转，代价是第二个任务总有一点误差（Chiaverini 1997）。If a second job also has a target like the hand’s, say "the elbow height should follow a certain curve", write it as a second Jacobian Je (the elbow’s Jacobian) and let it use only the null space that the first task leaves over: the first task is done in full, the second as far as possible. This is called task priority (Nakamura, Hanafusa & Yoshikawa 1987; the first version dates from 1981). Tasks can be stacked level by level (Siciliano & Slotine 1991); this is how humanoid robots manage the centre of mass, both feet, both hands and the head at the same time (Sentis & Khatib 2005). Here a new kind of singularity appears: neither task is singular on its own, but the second task gets no purchase within the null space of the first (Je N loses rank), and the two tasks fight each other. This is called an algorithmic singularity. It can be damped as in step ④; Chiaverini gave another way of writing task priority that stays well behaved when the two tasks conflict, at the cost of a small, permanent error in the second task (Chiaverini 1997).

伪逆还有一个不太明显的毛病：手沿一条围出面积的闭合路线走一圈回到原处，关节不一定回到原来的角度，一圈圈地往一边漂（Klein & Huang 1983）。要是擦的是一圈一圈的圆，这就麻烦了：手在竖直平面里画半径 8 cm 的圆，最后一节每圈偏 0.3°，一百圈就偏了 29°。像这里沿一条直线来回擦，回程正好原路退回，倒不会漂。一个补法是给 J 再加一行，把"零空间里的姿势"也定死，让 J 变成方的，这叫扩展雅可比（Baillieul 1985）；本页的默认姿势是事先沿桌面算好的一张表，手在同一个位置，姿势总是同一个。The pseudoinverse has another, less obvious flaw: when the hand goes once round a closed path that encloses an area and comes back to where it started, the joints do not necessarily return to their original angles; they drift to one side, loop after loop (Klein & Huang 1983). If the wiping went round and round in circles, this would be a problem: with the hand drawing a circle of radius 8 cm in the vertical plane, the last link shifts by 0.3° each lap, 29° after a hundred laps. Wiping back and forth along a straight line, as here, the return stroke retraces the outgoing one exactly, so nothing drifts. One fix is to add another row to J that also pins down "the posture within the null space", making J square; this is called the extended Jacobian (Baillieul 1985). The default postures on this page come from a table computed in advance along the tabletop, so the same hand position always gets the same posture.

这一步的要点：多出来的关节给了一个零空间：沿 v₃ 转，手不动；q̇ = J⁺v + (I − J⁺J) z 把"手的活"和"姿势的活"分开，第二件事只用第一件事剩下的自由。The key points of this step: the extra joint gives a null space: turning along v₃ leaves the hand still; q̇ = J⁺v + (I − J⁺J) z separates "the hand’s job" from "the posture’s job", and the second job uses only the freedom the first one leaves over.

手要推这么大的力，关节各出多少For the hand to push this hard, how much must each joint give?

擦桌子要往下压 10 N。电机只能出力矩，三个关节各该出多少，手上才正好有这 10 N？答案又是 J，只是转置了一下。理由是能量守恒：手臂静止不动、没有摩擦时，电机在关节上做的功，全部变成手在外界上做的功。Wiping the table means pressing down with 10 N. The motors can only produce torque: how much should each of the three joints give for the hand to deliver exactly this 10 N? The answer is J again, just transposed. The reason is conservation of energy: when the arm is at rest and there is no friction, all the work the motors do at the joints becomes work done by the hand on the outside world.

τ = Jᵀ Fτi = Ji · F

每个关节要出的力矩，是 J 的那一列和手上的力的点积，也就是这个力绕这个关节的力矩：力乘以力臂。The torque each joint must give is the dot product of its column of J with the force at the hand, which is just the moment of that force about the joint: force times lever arm.

让关节转一点点 δq，手就挪 δh = J δq。电机做的功是 τᵀδq，手推外界做的功是 Fᵀδh = Fᵀ J δq。两者对任何 δq 都相等，只能是 τ = Jᵀ F。这叫虚功原理。另一个看法：Ji 垂直于关节到手的连线，长度等于这段距离，所以 Ji · F 正好是"力臂 × 力"。Turn the joints by a tiny δq and the hand moves by δh = J δq. The work done by the motors is τᵀδq; the work done by the hand pushing on the world is Fᵀδh = Fᵀ J δq. The two are equal for every δq, which is only possible if τ = Jᵀ F. This is the principle of virtual work. Another way to see it: Ji is perpendicular to the line from the joint to the hand and as long as that distance, so Ji · F is exactly "lever arm × force".

手上感到的质量，随方向变The mass felt at the hand changes with direction

前面几步都当手臂没有质量：手要什么速度，关节就立刻转出什么速度。可手臂一共 4 kg，要它加速、减速，都得用力。动力学从动能开始。每一节的动能是"平动的 ½ m v²"加"转动的 ½ I ω²"，而每一节质心的速度，又是一个雅可比乘 q̇：J 又出现了。The previous steps treated the arm as massless: whatever velocity the hand needed, the joints produced it instantly. But the arm weighs 4 kg in all, and speeding it up or slowing it down takes force. Dynamics starts from kinetic energy. The kinetic energy of each link is "½ m v² of translation" plus "½ I ω² of rotation", and the velocity of each link’s centre of mass is in turn a Jacobian times q̇: J turns up again.

整只手臂的动能，是关节转速的一个二次式；系数排成一个 3 × 3 的矩阵 M，叫质量矩阵（也叫惯性矩阵）。它把每一节的质量和转动惯量，经过"这一节的质心怎么随关节动"的雅可比，加到一起。The kinetic energy of the whole arm is a quadratic in the joint rates; its coefficients form a 3 × 3 matrix M, called the mass matrix (also the inertia matrix). It adds up the mass and moment of inertia of every link, each passed through the Jacobian of "how that link’s centre of mass moves with the joints".

第 i 节质心的速度是 Jc,i q̇，平动动能 ½ mi |Jc,i q̇|² = ½ q̇ᵀ (mi Jc,iᵀ Jc,i) q̇。它的角速度是前 i 个关节转速之和，写成 Jω,i q̇，转动动能同理。所有节加起来，就是上式。M 是对称的，而且只要手臂在动，动能就大于 0，所以 M 是正定的。The velocity of link i’s centre of mass is Jc,i q̇, so its translational kinetic energy is ½ mi |Jc,i q̇|² = ½ q̇ᵀ (mi Jc,iᵀ Jc,i) q̇. Its angular velocity is the sum of the first i joint rates, written Jω,i q̇, and the rotational kinetic energy works the same way. Adding up all the links gives the formula above. M is symmetric, and whenever the arm is moving the kinetic energy is greater than 0, so M is positive definite.

It belongs to the same family as the velocity ellipse and the force ellipse: the impulse at the hand is carried to the joints by Jᵀ, turned into joint rates by M⁻¹, and brought back to the hand by J, so J M⁻¹ Jᵀ turns a circle at the hand into this ellipse. Khatib studied the inertia at the hand systematically and gave a way of drawing it that shows the size of the mass directly (Khatib 1995). Yoshikawa also extended manipulability to dynamics: all the accelerations the hand can have when, after the torques for gravity and velocity have been cancelled, the joints get another 1 N·m in total (Yoshikawa 1985). At 0.55 m the two semi-axes of this ellipse are 49.8 and 0.9 m/s²: by swinging that short, light rod, the wrist motor can send the hand lunging almost horizontally in an instant.

关节多出一个时，还有一个细节：同一个手的速度，可以对应很多种关节转速，它们的动能不一样；½ vᵀ Λ v 是其中最小的那个。本页的默认姿势是按"关节转得最少"挑的，不是按"动能最小"挑的：擦得最快的那一刻，手臂的动能是 0.082 焦耳，而同样的手速度最少只要 0.032 焦耳，多出来的那些都花在零空间里的转动上了。With one joint to spare, there is one more detail: the same hand velocity can come from many different joint rates, and their kinetic energies differ; ½ vᵀ Λ v is the smallest of them. This page’s default posture was chosen so that "the joints turn the least", not so that "the kinetic energy is least": at the moment of fastest wiping the arm’s kinetic energy is 0.082 joules, while the same hand velocity needs as little as 0.032 joules; all the extra goes into turning in the null space.

手上的质量和安全直接有关：手撞上人时，撞击有多狠，看它的速度和这个方向上的有效质量。对自由的撞击（被撞的东西能被撞开），Haddadin 等人的实验发现速度是主要因素，质量大到一定程度后，伤害指标就不再随它增加；被夹住的撞击（人被压在机器人和桌子之间）里，质量的影响就大得多（Haddadin 等 2009）。协作机器人的安全规范 ISO/TS 15066 估算碰撞时，用的是一个粗略的有效质量（机器人运动部分的一半加上负载），而不是这里按方向算的 Λ。The mass at the hand bears directly on safety: when the hand hits a person, how hard the impact is depends on its speed and on the effective mass in that direction. For free impacts (where whatever is hit can be knocked away), experiments by Haddadin and colleagues found speed to be the main factor: once the mass passes a certain size, the injury measures stop growing with it; in clamped impacts (a person pinned between the robot and a table), mass matters far more (Haddadin et al. 2009). The safety specification for collaborative robots, ISO/TS 15066, estimates collisions with a rough effective mass (half the mass of the robot’s moving parts plus the payload), rather than the direction-dependent Λ computed here.

这一步的要点：动能是 ½ q̇ᵀ M q̇，M 由各节的质量经过各自的雅可比加起来；手上感到的质量是 Λ，Λ⁻¹ = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ，它随方向变，推的方向和手跑的方向一般不一样。下一步从动能出发，写出完整的运动方程。The key points of this step: kinetic energy is ½ q̇ᵀ M q̇, where M adds up the masses of the links, each through its own Jacobian; the mass felt at the hand is Λ, with Λ⁻¹ = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ; it changes with direction, and the direction of the push and the direction the hand moves are generally not the same. The next step starts from kinetic energy and writes down the full equations of motion.

第 ⑧ 步 · 拉格朗日方程Step ⑧ · The Lagrange equations

从能量写出运动方程Equations of motion from energy

有了动能 T，再加上势能 V，就能写出手臂的运动方程：给定电机的力矩，关节怎么加速。对一个质点，这是牛顿的 F = m a；对三节连在一起的杆，直接用牛顿定律要处理关节里互相拉扯的力，很繁。拉格朗日的办法绕开了它们：只看整只手臂的能量，用关节角当坐标。With the kinetic energy T, plus the potential energy V, we can write down the arm’s equations of motion: given the motor torques, how the joints accelerate. For a point mass this is Newton’s F = m a; for three rods linked together, applying Newton’s laws directly means handling the forces with which the links tug on each other at the joints, which is tedious. Lagrange’s method sidesteps them: it looks only at the energy of the whole arm, and uses the joint angles as coordinates.

d/dt (∂𝓛/∂q̇i) − ∂𝓛/∂qi = τi − (Jᵀ F)i𝓛 = T − V

对每个关节：（动能对这个关节转速的导数）随时间的变化率，减去拉格朗日量对这个关节角的导数，等于电机的力矩减去桌子顶回来的那份。For each joint: the rate of change over time of (the derivative of the kinetic energy with respect to this joint’s rate), minus the derivative of the Lagrangian with respect to this joint’s angle, equals the motor torque minus the part taken up by the table pushing back.

这就是换了坐标的牛顿定律。∂T/∂q̇i 是"这个关节方向上的动量"，它的变化率就是"质量 × 加速度"那一项；∂V/∂qi 是势能沿这个关节的坡度，也就是重力在这个关节上的力矩。关节销子里的力不做功（它们只管"连着"），在能量里根本不出现，这正是这个办法省事的地方。外力 F 按第 ⑥ 步的转置换到关节上。This is Newton’s law in different coordinates. ∂T/∂q̇i is "the momentum in this joint’s direction", and its rate of change is the "mass × acceleration" term; ∂V/∂qi is the slope of the potential energy along this joint, that is, the torque gravity exerts at this joint. The forces in the joint pins do no work (their only job is "keeping things connected"), so they never appear in the energy at all, and that is exactly what makes this method economical. 把 T = ½ q̇ᵀ M q̇ 代进去，三个方程可以整理成一个矩阵式。它是机器人动力学最常用的形式：Substitute T = ½ q̇ᵀ M q̇ and the three equations can be gathered into a single matrix equation. This is the most commonly used form of robot dynamics:

M(q) q̈ + C(q, q̇) q̇ + g(q) = τ − Jᵀ FCkj = Σi ½ (∂Mkj/∂qi + ∂Mki/∂qj − ∂Mij/∂qk) q̇i

每个关节上有三种力矩：惯性 M q̈（加速要的力）、科氏力和离心力 C q̇（转起来以后彼此甩出来的力）、重力 g。三者加起来，等于电机的力矩减去桌子吸走的那份。At each joint there are three kinds of torque: inertia M q̈ (what it takes to accelerate), the Coriolis and centrifugal terms C q̇ (the forces the links fling at each other once they are turning) and gravity g. Together they equal the motor torque minus the part the table absorbs.

∂T/∂q̇ = M q̇，对时间求导得 M q̈ + Ṁ q̇；∂T/∂q = ½ q̇ᵀ (∂M/∂q) q̇。M 随姿势变，所以这两项都带着 q̇ 的平方：手臂转着转着，M 在变，"动量"就跟着变，这就是 C q̇。∂V/∂q 就是 g(q)。能量的账总对得上：T + V 的变化率正好是 q̇ᵀ(τ − Jᵀ F)，电机输入的功率减去桌子吸走的功率。C 的这种写法（来自克里斯托费尔符号）还多一个好性质：Ṁ − 2C 是反对称的，对任何 x 都有 xᵀ(Ṁ − 2C)x = 0；能量的账只要 x = q̇ 这一种情形，第 ⑩ 步的自适应控制要的是任何 x 都成立。∂T/∂q̇ = M q̇, whose time derivative is M q̈ + Ṁ q̇; ∂T/∂q = ½ q̇ᵀ (∂M/∂q) q̇. M changes with posture, so both of these terms carry the square of q̇: as the arm turns, M changes, and the "momentum" changes with it; that is C q̇. ∂V/∂q is simply g(q). The energy books always balance: the rate of change of T + V is exactly q̇ᵀ(τ − Jᵀ F), the power the motors put in minus the power the table absorbs. This way of writing C (from the Christoffel symbols) gives one more nice property: Ṁ − 2C is skew-symmetric, so xᵀ(Ṁ − 2C)x = 0 for every x; the energy books need only the case x = q̇, while the adaptive control of step ⑩ needs it for every x.

拉格朗日方程有几个在控制里很有用的性质。M 是对称正定的；Ṁ − 2C 是反对称的；而且整个方程对质量、"质量 × 质心位置"（一阶矩）和绕关节的转动惯量这些参数是线性的：τ = Y(q, q̇, q̈) θ，θ 是一列参数，Y 只和运动有关。前两条用来证明控制器稳定（第 ⑩ 步），第三条让我们能从测到的运动和力矩，用最小二乘把参数辨识出来（Atkeson, An & Hollerbach 1986）。用拉格朗日方程算机构的动力学，早在 1960 年代就有了（Uicker 1967 把约束也写进拉格朗日方程，算出单回路连杆机构轴承上的动态力）；它的缺点是算起来慢：照字面去算，计算量随关节数的四次方涨（Hollerbach 1980）。下一步换一个办法。The Lagrange equations have several properties that are very useful in control. M is symmetric and positive definite; Ṁ − 2C is skew-symmetric; and the whole equation is linear in parameters such as the masses, "mass × position of the centre of mass" (the first moments) and the moments of inertia about the joints: τ = Y(q, q̇, q̈) θ, where θ is a column of parameters and Y depends only on the motion. The first two are used to prove that controllers are stable (step ⑩); the third lets us identify the parameters by least squares from measured motions and torques (Atkeson, An & Hollerbach 1986). Using the Lagrange equations to compute the dynamics of mechanisms goes back to the 1960s (Uicker 1967 wrote the constraints into the Lagrange equations as well, to compute the dynamic forces on the bearings of single-loop linkages); their drawback is that they are slow to compute: done literally, the work grows with the fourth power of the number of joints (Hollerbach 1980). The next step takes another approach.

这个结构也被用来让神经网络学动力学。Deep Lagrangian Networks 不直接学 τ，而是学 M（写成一个对角线为正的下三角矩阵乘它的转置，保证正定）和重力项，C 由拉格朗日方程自己算出来（Lutter 等 2019）；哈密顿神经网络学的是能量本身，于是学出来的系统自动守恒它（Greydanus 等 2019）。This structure has also been used to let neural networks learn dynamics. Deep Lagrangian Networks do not learn τ directly; they learn M (written as a lower-triangular matrix with a positive diagonal times its transpose, which guarantees it is positive definite) and the gravity term, and C is worked out by the Lagrange equations themselves (Lutter et al. 2019); Hamiltonian neural networks learn the energy itself, so the learned system conserves it automatically (Greydanus et al. 2019).

这一步的要点：运动方程是 M q̈ + C q̇ + g = τ − Jᵀ F：惯性、科氏力和离心力、重力，加起来等于电机的力矩减去外界吸走的那份；电机不出力时能量守恒，这是检查模型的好办法。The key points of this step: the equation of motion is M q̈ + C q̇ + g = τ − Jᵀ F: inertia, the Coriolis and centrifugal terms and gravity add up to the motor torque minus the part the outside world takes up; with the motors applying no torque, energy is conserved, which is a good way to check a model.

第 ⑨ 步 · 牛顿–欧拉Step ⑨ · Newton–Euler

一节一节往外推运动，往里推力Link by link: motion outwards, force inwards

拉格朗日方程把关节里的力藏了起来，换来的是对 q 求导。另一个办法正好相反：老老实实地对每一节用牛顿定律（力 = 质量 × 加速度）和欧拉定律（力矩 = 转动惯量 × 角加速度），把关节里的力当成要求的东西，一节一节地传。给定 q、q̇、q̈，求 τ，这叫逆动力学；这个办法叫递推牛顿–欧拉算法（RNEA）。它只要两趟：The Lagrange equations hide the forces in the joints, in exchange for differentiating with respect to q. The other approach is the exact opposite: it dutifully applies Newton’s law (force = mass × acceleration) and Euler’s law (torque = moment of inertia × angular acceleration) to every link, treats the forces in the joints as unknowns to be found, and passes them along link by link. 牛顿–欧拉和拉格朗日算的是同一个东西，只是路子不同。一个从受力出发，顺带算出了关节里的力（设计轴承、选电机时要用）；一个从能量出发，给出了 M、C、g 这些整体的结构（分析、控制时要用）。Newton–Euler and Lagrange compute the same thing by different routes. 为什么快Why it is fast

牛顿–欧拉每一节只算一次往外、一次往里，计算量跟关节数成正比。照字面去算拉格朗日方程，计算量随关节数的四次方涨。1980 年，Luh、Walker 和 Paul 用它在一台 PDP 11/45 小型机上算斯坦福机械臂的逆动力学，平均每次 4.5 毫秒，控制器每秒至少能算 60 次（Luh, Walker & Paul 1980）；这让"在控制里实时用完整的动力学模型"变得可行。同一年，Hollerbach 把拉格朗日方程也改写成了递推的形式，计算量同样跟关节数成正比（Hollerbach 1980）。Newton–Euler goes over each link just once outwards and once inwards, so the work is proportional to the number of joints. Computed literally, the Lagrange equations take work that grows with the fourth power of the number of joints. In 1980, Luh, Walker and Paul used Newton–Euler to compute the inverse dynamics of the Stanford arm on a PDP 11/45 minicomputer, taking 4.5 milliseconds per computation on average, and the controller could compute it at least 60 times a second (Luh, Walker & Paul 1980); this made "using the full dynamics model in control, in real time" feasible. In the same year, Hollerbach rewrote the Lagrange equations in recursive form too, with the work likewise proportional to the number of joints (Hollerbach 1980).

反过来的问题，给定 τ 求 q̈（正动力学，仿真要用），有两条路。一条是先把 M 算出来，再解 M q̈ = τ − C q̇ − g − Jᵀ F：算 M 有专门的组合刚体算法（CRBA，Walker & Orin 1982），C q̇ + g 就是把 q̈ 设成 0 跑一遍 RNEA。本页的交互走的是这条路：M 按第 ⑦ 步的式子算，C q̇ + g 用 RNEA 算，再解一个 3 × 3 的方程组。另一条是 Featherstone 的铰接体算法（ABA），不显式地算 M，计算量也跟关节数成正比（Featherstone 1983），关节很多时更快。他后来把这些算法用六维的"空间向量"统一写在一本书里（Featherstone 2008）。The reverse problem, finding q̈ given τ (forward dynamics, needed for simulation), has two routes. One is to compute M first and then solve M q̈ = τ − C q̇ − g − Jᵀ F: there is a dedicated algorithm for M, the composite rigid body algorithm (CRBA, Walker & Orin 1982), and C q̇ + g is just one run of RNEA with q̈ set to 0. The widgets on this page take this route: M from the formula of step ⑦, C q̇ + g from RNEA, then a 3 × 3 system of equations is solved. The other is Featherstone’s articulated-body algorithm (ABA), which does not form M explicitly and whose work is also proportional to the number of joints (Featherstone 1983); with many joints it is faster. He later brought these algorithms together in one book, written in six-dimensional "spatial vectors" (Featherstone 2008).

今天的机器人软件里，这些算法都是现成的。Pinocchio 实现了 RNEA、CRBA、ABA，还能直接算出 RNEA 和 ABA 的解析导数，给优化和学习用（Carpentier 等 2019）；MuJoCo 这样的物理仿真器，也是在关节坐标里用递推算法算动力学，再处理接触（Todorov 等 2012）。In today’s robot software these algorithms come ready-made. Pinocchio implements RNEA, CRBA and ABA, and can also compute the analytical derivatives of RNEA and ABA directly, for use in optimisation and learning (Carpentier et al. 2019); physics simulators such as MuJoCo likewise compute the dynamics in joint coordinates with recursive algorithms, then deal with contact (Todorov et al. 2012).

这一步的要点：牛顿–欧拉往外推运动、往里推力，计算量跟关节数成正比，顺带算出了关节里的力；它和拉格朗日方程给出同一个 τ。The key points of this step: Newton–Euler propagates motion outwards and force inwards, with work proportional to the number of joints, and works out the joint forces along the way; it gives the same τ as the Lagrange equations.

g 抵掉了重力，误差不用再撑着手臂。更强的结论是：只要目标不动，从任何姿势出发，PD 加重力补偿都会让手臂停到目标上。证明用的是能量：把"动能 + PD 的弹簧势能"当成一个碗，阻尼让它只降不升，第 ⑧ 步那个反对称的性质保证科氏力不往碗里添能量（Takegaki & Arimoto 1981）。g cancels gravity, so the error no longer has to hold the arm up. A stronger result: as long as the target stays still, PD plus gravity compensation brings the arm to rest on the target from any starting posture. The proof uses energy: treat "kinetic energy + the potential energy of the PD springs" as a bowl; the damping only lets it go down, never up, and the skew-symmetry property of step ⑧ guarantees that the Coriolis terms add no energy to the bowl (Takegaki & Arimoto 1981).

重力补偿要模型准。海绵吸了水，多出 0.3 kg，控制器不知道：差的 0.3 × 9.81 ≈ 2.9 N 又只能靠误差撑着，高度差回到 5.2 mm。Gravity compensation needs an accurate model. If the model is accurate, the three joints become independent of each other, and each turns into an identical, clean spring–damper system.

把这个 τ 代进第 ⑧ 步的方程 M q̈ + C q̇ + g = τ，C q̇ 和 g 正好消掉，两边再除以 M（M 可逆），剩下 ë + 2ω ė + ω² e = 0，e = qd − q。这是一个临界阻尼的二阶系统：误差不来回晃，大约 4/ω 秒（这里是 0.13 秒）就衰减到十分之一。所以叫"反馈线性化"：用模型把非线性的手臂变成线性的。Substitute this τ into the equation of step ⑧, M q̈ + C q̇ + g = τ, and C q̇ and g cancel exactly; divide both sides by M (M is invertible), and what is left is ë + 2ω ė + ω² e = 0, with e = qd − q. Experiments on the MIT direct-drive arm found that the more complete the set of model terms added, the smaller the tracking error, and that adding the model only as feedforward was not much different from full computed torque (An et al. 1989).

别的路：让手像弹簧，或者自己学Other routes: make the hand behave like a spring, or let it learn

还有一种不分"管位置"和"管力"的想法：阻抗控制。Hogan 认为，机械臂一碰到东西，就和环境连成了一个系统，只管位置或只管力都不够，要管的是手的动态"脾气"：被推开时像多硬的弹簧、多大的阻尼、多重的质量（Hogan 1985）。第 ⑥ 步的 Kq = Jᵀ Kx J 是它最简单的情形。比如让手竖着像 1000 N/m 的弹簧，把目标放在桌面以下 1 cm：手和桌子是两根串起来的弹簧，合起来约 952 N/m，压力大约 9.5 N，桌面高低差一点、硬一点软一点，力都只变一点（桌面高 1 mm，力只多 1 N 左右），不会像位置控制那样乱跳。Another idea does not separate "controlling position" from "controlling force" at all: impedance control. Hogan argued that as soon as the arm touches something, it forms a single system with its environment, so controlling only position or only force is not enough; what must be controlled is the hand’s dynamic "temperament": when pushed away, how stiff a spring, how much damping and how heavy a mass it behaves like (Hogan 1985). The Kq = Jᵀ Kx J of step ⑥ is its simplest case. For example, make the hand behave vertically like a 1000 N/m spring and put its target 1 cm below the table surface: the hand and the table are two springs in series, about 952 N/m combined, and the force is about 9.5 N; if the table is a little higher or lower, a little harder or softer, the force changes only a little (raise the table by 1 mm and the force grows by only about 1 N), instead of jumping about as it does under position control.

模型不准，还可以让控制器自己改模型。第 ⑧ 步说过，运动方程对质量这些参数是线性的；自适应控制一边控制，一边按跟踪误差在线修正这些参数，能证明的是跟踪误差收敛（Slotine & Li 1987），参数本身不一定收敛到真值：要把湿海绵那 0.3 kg 真的"学"出来，手的运动还得足够"丰富"。更一般的是用数据去学正、逆动力学模型（Nguyen-Tuong & Peters 2011 的综述），第 ⑧ 步的 Deep Lagrangian Networks 就是其中一种。多出来的关节也可以按动力学去用，比如挑关节力矩最小的解；可 Hollerbach 和 Suh 发现，只按"每一刻力矩最小"去挑，长时间以后会出现"甩鞭子"一样的失稳，需要极大的力矩才拉得回来（Hollerbach & Suh 1987）。If the model is wrong, the controller can also be left to correct the model itself. As step ⑧ noted, the equations of motion are linear in parameters such as the masses; adaptive control corrects these parameters online from the tracking error as it controls. What can be proved is that the tracking error converges (Slotine & Li 1987); the parameters themselves need not converge to their true values: to really "learn" the wet sponge’s 0.3 kg, the hand’s motion must also be "rich" enough. More generally, forward and inverse dynamics models can be learned from data (the survey by Nguyen-Tuong & Peters 2011); the Deep Lagrangian Networks of step ⑧ are one example. Extra joints can also be used with dynamics in mind, for instance by choosing the solution with the smallest joint torques; but Hollerbach and Suh found that choosing only by "smallest torque at each instant" leads, after a while, to a "whip-cracking" instability that takes enormous torques to pull back from (Hollerbach & Suh 1987).

这一步的要点：模型用得越全，空中的轨迹跟得越准，可也越依赖模型准不准；一碰到东西，就得直接管手上的力：操作空间控制用 Λ 把想要的加速度变成力，用 Jᵀ 变成力矩，再用零空间安顿多出来的关节。The key points of this step: the more of the model you use, the more closely the arm follows its path in the air, but the more it depends on the model being right; once it touches something, you must control the force at the hand directly: operational space control uses Λ to turn the desired acceleration into a force and Jᵀ to turn that into torques, and lets the null space take care of the extra joint.

SVD 在机器人以外The SVD beyond robotics

SVD 本身是一件更大的工具，机器人只用了它的一角。它最有用的性质，是给出"最好的低秩近似"：The SVD itself is a much bigger tool, and robotics uses only a corner of it. Its most useful property is that it gives "the best low-rank approximation":

Ak = σ₁ u₁ v₁ᵀ + … + σk uk vkᵀminrank B = k ‖A − B‖ = ‖A − Ak‖ = σk+1

只留最大的 k 个奇异值和它们的奇异向量，得到的 Ak 是所有秩为 k 的矩阵里离 A 最近的；差多少，就是扔掉的第一个奇异值。Keep only the k largest singular values and their singular vectors, and the resulting Ak is, of all matrices of rank k, the closest to A; how far off it is equals the first singular value thrown away.

A 是一层层"一进一出"的通道叠起来的，每一层的强度是 σi。扔掉最弱的那几层，损失最小。这里的 ‖·‖ 是矩阵的谱范数：它把长度为 1 的输入最多拉长多少倍。按每个元素平方和算的范数也有同样的结论，那时差的是扔掉的奇异值的平方和开方。A is a stack of layers, each a "one in, one out" channel, and the strength of each layer is σi. Throwing away the weakest few layers loses the least. Here ‖·‖ is the spectral norm of a matrix: the largest factor by which it stretches an input of length 1. The norm built from the sum of the squares of all the entries leads to the same result; there, the difference is the square root of the sum of the squares of the discarded singular values.

  • 主成分分析：一堆数据点，减去平均以后一个点占一行，排成一个矩阵，它的第一个右奇异向量就是数据散得最开的方向，前 k 个张成最能代表数据的 k 维平面（Pearson 1901 找"离一堆点最近的直线和平面"）。Principal component analysis: take a set of data points, subtract the mean and stack them into a matrix, one point per row; its first right singular vector is the direction in which the data are most spread out, and the first k span the k-dimensional plane that best represents the data (Pearson 1901 sought "lines and planes of closest fit to systems of points").
  • 两组点之间的最佳旋转：同一个物体在两个坐标系里各量了一组点，把它们的"协方差"矩阵做 SVD，就能读出把一组转到另一组的最佳旋转。Kabsch 1976 年用特征分解给出了这个解（Kabsch 1976），Arun 等人写成了 3 × 3 矩阵的 SVD（Arun 等 1987），Umeyama 补上了它偶尔会给出镜像而不是旋转的漏洞，还推广到了任意维、带缩放（Umeyama 1991）。最经典的点云对齐办法（点到点的 ICP）每一轮都要解一次这个问题。The best rotation between two point sets: the same object is measured as a set of points in each of two coordinate frames; take the SVD of their "covariance" matrix and you can read off the best rotation taking one set onto the other. Kabsch gave this solution in 1976 using an eigendecomposition (Kabsch 1976), Arun and colleagues wrote it as the SVD of a 3 × 3 matrix (Arun et al. 1987), and Umeyama closed the loophole whereby it occasionally gives a reflection instead of a rotation, and generalised it to any dimension, with scaling (Umeyama 1991). The classic method for aligning point clouds (point-to-point ICP) solves this problem once in every iteration.
  • 两张照片之间的几何：八点法用 SVD 从八对匹配点里解出基础矩阵，再用一次 SVD 把它最小的奇异值置 0，让它的秩变成 2；先把点的坐标归一化，结果就稳得多（Hartley 1997）。The geometry between two photos: the eight-point algorithm uses the SVD to solve for the fundamental matrix from eight pairs of matched points, then uses the SVD again to set its smallest singular value to 0, so that its rank becomes 2; normalising the point coordinates first makes the result much more stable (Hartley 1997).
  • 解方程有多可靠："条件数"这个词来自 Turing，他用它分析解线性方程组时舍入误差会放大多少（Turing 1948）；今天最常用的定义，是最大和最小奇异值之比。第 ③ 步的 κ 是同一个东西：J 的条件数大，从手的误差反推关节就不可靠。How reliably equations can be solved: the term "condition number" comes from Turing, who used it to analyse how much rounding errors are amplified when solving systems of linear equations (Turing 1948); today the most common definition is the ratio of the largest to the smallest singular value. The κ of step ③ is the same thing: when J’s condition number is large, working back from errors at the hand to the joints is unreliable.
  • 大矩阵：矩阵大到放不进内存时，先用随机的向量去"探"它，找出它主要作用的那个子空间，再在这个小子空间里做精确的 SVD（Halko, Martinsson & Tropp 2011）。Huge matrices: when a matrix is too big to fit in memory, first "probe" it with random vectors to find the subspace where it mainly acts, then do an exact SVD within that small subspace (Halko, Martinsson & Tropp 2011).
  • 大模型：LoRA 微调一个大语言模型时，冻结原来的权重矩阵，只学一个低秩的修正 BA；对 GPT-3 175B，要训练的参数少了一万倍（Hu 等 2021）。它不直接算 SVD，可"一个矩阵的改动只需要几层通道"这个想法，正是上面的低秩近似。Large models: when LoRA fine-tunes a large language model, it freezes the original weight matrices and learns only a low-rank correction BA; for GPT-3 175B, the number of parameters to train falls by a factor of ten thousand (Hu et al. 2021). It does not compute an SVD directly, but the idea that "a change to a matrix needs only a few layers of channels" is exactly the low-rank approximation above.

这一步的要点：机器人里的 J、Jᵀ、M、Λ，是同一套线性代数在三件事（速度、力、惯性）上的三次应用；SVD 把每一次都拆成"沿哪个方向、放大多少"，它在数据、几何和学习里也做同样的事。The key points of this step: J, Jᵀ, M and Λ in robotics are one body of linear algebra applied three times, to three things (velocity, force, inertia); the SVD breaks each application down into "along which direction, and amplified by how much", and it does the same in data, geometry and learning.

其他几个问题A few other questions

  • 奇异值和特征值有什么不同？特征值只对方阵有，它问的是"哪个方向被 A 送回自己的方向上"，可能是负数、复数；奇异值对任何形状的矩阵都有，问的是"哪个方向被拉长多少"，总是大于等于 0。J 是 2 × 3 的，根本没有特征值；J Jᵀ 是对称的，它的特征值正好是 σ²。How do singular values differ from eigenvalues? Eigenvalues exist only for square matrices; they ask "which direction does A send back along itself", and can be negative or complex. Singular values exist for matrices of any shape; they ask "which direction is stretched, and by how much", and are always greater than or equal to 0. J is 2 × 3 and has no eigenvalues at all; J Jᵀ is symmetric, and its eigenvalues are exactly σ².
  • 为什么不直接用 J⁻¹？这只手臂的 J 不是方的，没有逆；就算是方的（比如锁住腕），手臂接近伸直时 J 也接近不可逆，算出来的关节转速大得离谱。伪逆处理"不是方的"，阻尼处理"接近不可逆"（④）。Why not just use J⁻¹? This arm’s J is not square, so it has no inverse; even if it were square (with the wrist locked, say), J would become nearly non-invertible as the arm approaches straight, and the computed joint rates would be absurdly large. The pseudoinverse deals with "not square", damping with "nearly non-invertible" (④).
  • 为什么同一个 J 既管速度又管力？因为功率在两边相等：电机给的 τᵀ q̇ 等于手做的 Fᵀ v（⑥）。速度用 J 从关节送到手，力就用 Jᵀ 从手送回关节；于是好动的方向推不动，推得动的方向动不了。Why does the same J govern both velocity and force? Because power is equal on both sides: the τᵀ q̇ put in by the motors equals the Fᵀ v delivered by the hand (⑥). Velocity goes from the joints to the hand through J, so force goes back from the hand to the joints through Jᵀ; and so in the directions where the hand moves easily it cannot push hard, and in the directions where it can push hard it can barely move.
  • 动力学模型非要那么准吗？看干什么。慢慢擦，重力比惯性大二十倍以上，PD 加重力补偿就够（⑧、⑩）；擦得快，惯性和科氏力跟速度的平方涨，就得把它们也补上；一碰到东西，模型再准也不够，要直接管力。Does the dynamics model really need to be that accurate? It depends on the job. Wiping slowly, gravity is more than twenty times larger than inertia, and PD plus gravity compensation is enough (⑧, ⑩); wiping fast, inertia and the Coriolis terms grow with the square of the speed, and they must be compensated too; once the arm touches something, no model, however accurate, is enough, and the force must be controlled directly.
  • 三维的六轴、七轴手臂也一样吗？一样，只是 J 变成 6 × n：上面三行管手的线速度，下面三行管手的角速度（①）。这时 SVD 有一个麻烦：线速度（m/s）和角速度（rad/s）单位不同，"合起来"本身就要先约定怎么加权，椭球的形状跟着这个约定变。Is it the same for six- and seven-axis arms in 3D? Yes, except that J becomes 6 × n: the top three rows give the hand’s linear velocity, the bottom three its angular velocity (①). The SVD then runs into a snag: linear velocity (m/s) and angular velocity (rad/s) have different units, so "combining" them first requires agreeing on how to weight them, and the shape of the ellipsoid changes with that choice.

小测验Quiz

J 的第 i 列是什么？为什么它总是垂直于从第 i 个关节到手的连线？What is column i of J? Why is it always perpendicular to the line from joint i to the hand?

是只让第 i 个关节以 1 rad/s 转、别的都不动时手的速度。这时它后面的整段手臂像一根硬杆绕它转，手在以这个关节为圆心的圆上走，速度沿圆的切线，所以垂直于半径；大小是转速乘半径，所以长度等于关节到手的距离（第 ① 步）。It is the hand’s velocity when only joint i turns, at 1 rad/s, and all the others stay still. The whole arm beyond that joint then turns about it like a rigid rod, and the hand moves on a circle centred on the joint; the velocity is along the tangent to the circle, so it is perpendicular to the radius; its size is the rate times the radius, so its length equals the distance from the joint to the hand (step ①).

手在 0.55 m 时 σ₁ = 0.744、σ₂ = 0.283（单位 m/rad）。要手沿 u₂ 以 0.1 m/s 走，关节转速合起来至少要多少？With the hand at 0.55 m, σ₁ = 0.744 and σ₂ = 0.283 (in m/rad). For the hand to move along u₂ at 0.1 m/s, what is the least combined joint rate needed?

沿 u₂ 每 1 rad/s 只有 0.283 m/s，所以至少 0.1 / 0.283 = 0.35 rad/s，而且要按 v₂ 的比例转（第 ③、④ 步）。沿 u₁ 走同样快只要 0.1 / 0.744 = 0.13 rad/s。Along u₂, each 1 rad/s gives only 0.283 m/s, so it takes at least 0.1 / 0.283 = 0.35 rad/s, with the joints turning in the proportions of v₂ (steps ③ and ④). Moving just as fast along u₁ takes only 0.1 / 0.744 = 0.13 rad/s.

手臂快伸直时，伪逆为什么会让关节疯转？阻尼最小二乘怎么避免它？Why does the pseudoinverse make the joints spin wildly when the arm is nearly straight? How does damped least squares avoid this?

伪逆沿每个方向把手的速度放大 1/σ 倍，伸直时 σ₂ → 0，1/σ₂ → ∞。阻尼最小二乘把倍数换成 σ / (σ² + λ²)，最大只有 1/(2λ)；代价是手跟得没那么紧，够不着的时候停在离目标最近的地方（第 ④ 步）。Along each direction the pseudoinverse multiplies the hand’s velocity by 1/σ; when the arm is straight, σ₂ → 0 and 1/σ₂ → ∞. Damped least squares replaces the factor with σ / (σ² + λ²), which is at most 1/(2λ); the price is that the hand follows less tightly, and when it cannot reach, it stops at the point closest to the target (step ④).

手压在桌上不动，三个关节还能怎么转？这个方向和 SVD 有什么关系？With the hand pressed on the table and not moving, how can the three joints still turn? How is this direction related to the SVD?

按 v₃ 的比例一起转：J v₃ = 0，手的速度是 0，只是姿势在变。v₃ 是 J 的第三个右奇异向量，它张成 J 的零空间。q̇ = J⁺v + (I − J⁺J) z 里，第二项就只沿 v₃（第 ②、⑤ 步）。All together, in the proportions of v₃: J v₃ = 0, so the hand’s velocity is 0 and only the posture changes. v₃ is J’s third right singular vector, and it spans the null space of J. In q̇ = J⁺v + (I − J⁺J) z, the second term lies only along v₃ (steps ② and ⑤).

手在 0.55 m 往下压 10 N，肩为什么要出 −5.5 N·m？加上重力以后，为什么肩反而轻松了？With the hand at 0.55 m pressing down with 10 N, why must the shoulder supply −5.5 N·m? And once gravity is included, why does the shoulder have it easier?

τ = Jᵀ F，肩的那份是这个力绕肩的力矩：力竖直向下，力臂是手离肩的水平距离 0.55 m，0.55 × 10 = 5.5 N·m，顺时针，所以是负的。托住手臂要逆时针的 12.3 N·m，两者相加只要 6.8 N·m：桌子往上顶手，替手臂扛了一部分重量（第 ⑥ 步）。τ = Jᵀ F, and the shoulder’s share is the moment of this force about the shoulder: the force points straight down and its lever arm is the horizontal distance from the shoulder to the hand, 0.55 m, so 0.55 × 10 = 5.5 N·m, clockwise, hence negative. Holding the arm up takes 12.3 N·m anticlockwise, so together only 6.8 N·m is needed: the table, pushing up on the hand, carries part of the arm’s weight (step ⑥).

为什么速度椭圆长的方向，力椭圆反而短？Why is the force ellipse short in the direction where the velocity ellipse is long?

速度椭圆的半轴是 σi，力椭圆的半轴是 1/σi，方向都是 ui。原因是功率相等：沿一个方向，关节的一点转动能让手走得多，同样的力矩在那个方向上就只能推出小的力（第 ⑥ 步）。The semi-axes of the velocity ellipse are σi, those of the force ellipse 1/σi, both along ui. The reason is equal power: in a direction where a little joint rotation moves the hand a long way, the same torque can push only weakly in that direction (step ⑥).

沿桌面推手，它只有 0.16 kg；往上推，有 1.4 kg，手还往旁边跑。为什么？Push the hand along the table and it feels like only 0.16 kg; push it upwards and it feels like 1.4 kg, and the hand runs off sideways as well. Why?

手上感到的质量是 Λ，Λ⁻¹ = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ，它是一个随方向变的矩阵。沿桌面推，甩一下又轻又短的最后一节就够了；往上推，得带着前臂甚至整条胳膊动。Λ⁻¹ 不是一个数乘单位矩阵，所以冲量 p 和速度变化 Λ⁻¹ p 一般不在同一个方向上，只有沿它的两个特征方向推才一致（第 ⑦ 步）。The mass felt at the hand is Λ, with Λ⁻¹ = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ, a matrix that varies with direction. Pushing along the table, a flick of the light, short last link is enough; pushing upwards, the forearm, or even the whole arm, has to move. Λ⁻¹ is not a number times the identity matrix, so the impulse p and the velocity change Λ⁻¹ p generally point in different directions; they agree only when you push along one of its two eigen-directions (step ⑦).

计算力矩在空中几乎没有误差，为什么在桌上反而最差？Computed torque has almost no error in the air, so why is it the worst on the table?

它只管位置，而且让手的硬度变成 ω²Λ：轻的方向软。手在 0.55 m 时，最轻的方向只有 0.157 kg，硬度才 141 N/m，桌面的摩擦一拽，手就被拽开一两厘米；压力也没人管，在 0.7 到 38 N 之间跳。要压得准，得直接管力（第 ⑩ 步）。It controls only position, and it makes the hand’s stiffness ω²Λ: soft in the light directions. With the hand at 0.55 m, the mass in the lightest direction is only 0.157 kg, so the stiffness is a mere 141 N/m, and a tug from the table’s friction drags the hand a centimetre or two off; nothing controls the force either, which jumps between 0.7 and 38 N. To press accurately, you have to control the force directly (step ⑩).

拉格朗日方程和牛顿–欧拉算出来的力矩，为什么一模一样？Why are the torques computed by the Lagrange equations and by Newton–Euler exactly the same?

它们是同一套力学的两种写法：一个从能量出发，关节里的力不做功、不出现；一个从每一节的受力出发，把关节里的力一节节算出来再消掉。交互里两者只差 10⁻⁸ N·m 上下，那是拉格朗日这边对 M 数值求导带来的误差（第 ⑧、⑨ 步）。They are two ways of writing the same mechanics: one starts from energy, where the forces in the joints do no work and never appear; the other starts from the forces on each link, working out the forces in the joints link by link and then eliminating them. In the widget the two differ by only around 10⁻⁸ N·m, the error from differentiating M numerically on the Lagrange side (steps ⑧ and ⑨).
