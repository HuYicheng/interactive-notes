从照片到场景From Photos to Scenes

NeRF 和高斯泼溅（3DGS）总被拿来比较，可它们到底在做什么？是一种新的"模态"，还是一种算法？它们其实是一长串方法里最新的两个，这串方法都在回答同一个问题：手上有一堆照片（有时还有深度），怎样还原出拍的是什么，好从没拍过的角度去看它，或者知道东西在哪、多大、什么形状。这个问题比深度学习老得多：三角测量、多视图立体、深度相机的融合、光场，然后才是辐射场、高斯泼溅，以及从成千上万个场景里学来的先验、一次就给出三维的大模型。这一篇用一个机器人的任务把它们串起来：机械臂绕着一个马克杯拍一圈照片，要回答两个问题：从一个没拍过的角度看，杯子是什么样？把手在哪，好去抓？一步一步看每一类方法怎么做、吃什么、吐什么、强在哪、弱在哪、彼此是什么关系。最后把一百多种方法按五个问题放在一起，回到开头：它们算不算一种模态。NeRF and Gaussian splatting (3DGS) are forever being compared, but what do they actually do? Are they a new "modality", or an algorithm? They are in fact the two newest members of a long line of methods that all answer one question: given a pile of photos (sometimes with depth), how do you recover what was photographed, so that you can look at it from angles nobody shot, or know where things are, how big and what shape? The question is far older than deep learning: triangulation, multi-view stereo, fusion of depth-camera frames and light fields came first, then radiance fields and Gaussian splatting, and then priors learned from thousands of other scenes and large models that output 3D in one go. This page ties them together with one robot task: an arm circles a mug taking photos and must answer two questions. What does the mug look like from an angle that was never photographed? Where is the handle, so the arm can grasp it? Step by step we look at how each family of methods works, what goes in and what comes out, where it is strong and weak, and how the families relate. Finally, over a hundred methods are laid side by side along five questions, and we return to the opening one: do they count as a modality?

所有方法背后的同一个问题The one problem behind every method

一张照片是三维世界在一个平面上的投影。每个像素记下的是沿一条射线射进相机的光；这光来自射线上多远的地方，照片不记。深度在按快门的那一刻就丢了，所以"从照片到场景"是在把投影反过来，而只靠一张照片，这件事没有唯一的答案。补回深度的办法，主要有三种：A photo is a projection of the 3D world onto a plane. Each pixel records the light arriving along one ray; how far along the ray that light came from, the photo does not record. Depth is lost the moment the shutter clicks, so "from photos to scenes" means running the projection backwards, and from a single photo that has no unique answer. There are three main ways to get depth back:

  • 换个位置再看。同一个点在两张照片里落在不同的位置，两条射线一交，就是它在哪（第 ①、② 步）；或者把整个场景当成未知数，调到所有照片都对得上（第 ④—⑥ 步）。Look again from somewhere else. The same point lands in different places in two photos, and where the two rays cross is where it is (steps ① and ②); or treat the whole scene as the unknown and adjust it until every photo matches (steps ④ to ⑥).
  • 自己发光去量。深度相机、激光雷达打出光再收回来，直接测出距离（第 ③ 步）。Send out light and measure. Depth cameras and LiDAR emit light and catch it again, measuring distance directly (step ③).
  • 靠见过的别的东西。一个在成千上万个场景上学过的模型，知道杯子一般是圆的、背面大概什么样（第 ⑧ 步）。Rely on other things you have seen. A model trained on thousands of scenes knows that mugs are usually round and roughly what their backs look like (step ⑧).

单张照片里也有一些线索，比如明暗、对焦的虚实；明暗在第 ② 步末尾会提到。A single photo also holds some cues, such as shading and how sharp or blurred things are; shading comes up at the end of step ②.

用五个问题给方法分类Sorting methods with five questions

方法很多，被拿来比的维度也很多。这一篇用五个问题去问每一个方法：There are many methods and many dimensions along which they get compared. This page asks every method five questions:

  • 吃什么（输入）：带位姿的照片、不带位姿的照片、视频、深度图、激光点云、一张照片，甚至一句话。What goes in (input): posed photos, unposed photos, video, depth maps, laser point clouds, a single photo, even a sentence.
  • 存成什么（场景的表示）：点、网格、体素格子、有符号距离、光场、辐射场（存在网络里或格子里）、一堆高斯，或者一个直接预测这些东西的网络。What it is stored as (the scene representation): points, meshes, voxel grids, signed distance, light fields, radiance fields (in a network or a grid), a crowd of Gaussians, or a network that predicts any of these directly.
  • 怎么比（拿什么判断对不对）：几何上的（点和射线、深度之差）、画出来和照片比颜色，还是"像不像见过的东西"。What it compares (what decides right and wrong): geometry (points and rays, differences in depth), colours of a rendering against the photos, or "does it look like things seen before".
  • 怎么求：一个式子直接解、一帧一帧地平均、对这一个场景反复优化、一个训练好的网络一次给出，还是生成。How it is solved: directly by a formula, by averaging frame after frame, by optimising this one scene over and over, in one pass by a trained network, or by generation.
  • 吐什么（输出）：新视角的照片、表面网格、深度图或点云、相机位姿、语义，还是会动的场景。What comes out (output): photos from new views, surface meshes, depth maps or point clouds, camera poses, semantics, or scenes that move.

拿 NeRF 和 3DGS 来说：它们吃带位姿的照片；存成辐射场，一个存在网络里，一个存在一堆高斯里；把场景画出来和照片比颜色；对每个场景反复优化；吐出新视角，几何只是副产品（第 ⑦ 步）。"它们算不算一种模态"，要先分清"模态"的几种意思，总结里回答。下面一步一步看：Take NeRF and 3DGS: they take in posed photos; they store a radiance field, one in a network and the other in a crowd of Gaussians; they render the scene and compare colours with the photos; they optimise each scene over and over; and they put out new views, with geometry only a by-product (step ⑦). 第 ① 步 · 相机和射线Step ① · Cameras and rays

一个像素是一条射线A pixel is a ray

先看照片是怎么来的。最简单的相机模型叫针孔相机：所有光线都穿过一个点，相机中心 o，再落到后面的成像平面上。倒过来看，每个像素对应一条从 o 出发的射线；射线上的任何一点，都落在同一个像素上。First, where photos come from. The simplest camera model is the pinhole camera: every ray of light passes through one point, the camera centre o, and lands on the image plane behind it. Seen the other way round, each pixel corresponds to a ray leaving o, and every point along that ray lands on the same pixel.

u = cx + f · xc / zc

一个点落在第几个像素：它在相机右边多远，除以它在相机前面多远，乘以焦距 f，再从光轴穿过的那个像素 cx 算起。Which pixel a point lands on: how far it is to the camera’s right, divided by how far it is in front of the camera, times the focal length f, counted from the pixel cx where the optical axis passes.

相似三角形：点偏离光轴的距离和它的深度之比，等于它的像偏离中心的距离和焦距之比。所以远处的东西小、近处的东西大。把 xc 和 zc 同时放大一倍，也就是沿着射线往外挪，u 不变：深度丢了。Similar triangles: a point’s distance from the optical axis divided by its depth equals its image’s distance from the centre divided by the focal length. That is why far things look small and near things large. Double both xc and zc, that is, slide the point outwards along its ray, and u does not change: the depth is lost.

相机在哪：位姿Where is the camera: poses

要用射线，先得知道每张照片拍的时候相机在哪（位置 o）、朝哪（朝向），合起来叫位姿；还得知道 f、cx 和镜头的畸变这些相机本身的数，叫内参。内参标一次就够：对着一块棋盘格拍几张，从格子的角点算出来（张正友 2000）。位姿有两种来法：To use rays, you first need to know, for every photo, where the camera was (position o) and which way it faced (orientation), together called its pose; you also need the camera’s own numbers, f, cx and the lens distortion, called its intrinsics. Intrinsics are calibrated once: photograph a checkerboard a few times and compute them from the corners of the squares (Zhang 2000). Poses come from one of two places:

  • 机器人自己知道。关节转角经过正运动学，给出手腕在哪、朝哪；相机和手腕之间那段固定的变换，用手眼标定求一次（Tsai & Lenz 1989）。本篇就是这样，24 张照片的位姿都已知。The robot knows. The joint angles, through forward kinematics, give where the wrist is and which way it faces; the fixed transform between camera and wrist is found once by hand–eye calibration (Tsai & Lenz 1989). That is the case on this page: the poses of all 24 photos are known.
  • 从照片里算。手机随手拍的照片，不知道相机在哪。SfM 在照片之间找同一个特征点，把相机的位姿和这些点的位置一起求出来（第 ② 步）。NeRF 和 3DGS 的论文，几乎都先跑一遍 COLMAP 拿位姿。Compute them from the photos. For photos snapped with a phone, nobody knows where the camera was. SfM finds the same keypoints across photos and solves for the camera poses and the positions of those points together (step ②). Almost every NeRF and 3DGS paper first runs COLMAP to get its poses.

两条射线交出一个点Two rays cross at a point

同一个点在两张照片里都看得到，就有两条射线，交点就是这个点。这叫三角测量，是所有"从照片算几何"的方法的根。可像素有误差：特征点的位置找偏一个像素很平常，射线跟着偏一点，交点就挪了。If the same point is visible in two photos, there are two rays, and where they cross is the point. This is triangulation, the root of every method that computes geometry from photos. But pixels have errors: finding a keypoint one pixel off is common, the ray tilts a little, and the crossing moves.

Where there are stripes, every change of colour is a landmark; on plain glaze, several pixels in B’s photo have nearly the same colour (in the widget, three to five differ by less than 2%), and any of them could be it. The whole pipeline in the next step is about finding correspondences, and what it fears most is a surface without texture.

这一步的两件事是后面所有方法的地基：一个像素是一条射线，所以要知道每张照片的位姿；深度要靠不同位置的射线交出来，准不准取决于夹角、距离、焦距和像素误差。The two ideas of this step underlie every method that follows: a pixel is a ray, so every photo’s pose must be known; depth comes from crossing rays seen from different places, and its precision depends on the angle, the distance, the focal length and the pixel error.

第 ② 步 · 从照片算几何Step ② · Geometry from photos

经典的流水线：找对应、交出点、连成面The classic pipeline: find correspondences, triangulate, connect into a surface

从 1990 年代到今天，从照片做三维的标准做法是一条流水线，每一站都是第 ① 步那两件事的放大：From the 1990s to today, the standard way to get 3D from photos has been a pipeline, each stage a scaled-up version of the two ideas of step ①:

  1. 找特征点。在每张照片里找出有特点的小块（角点、纹理），给每一块算一个描述它长相的向量。最有名的是 SIFT（Lowe 2004）：照片放大缩小、转一转，同一块的向量都差不多。Find keypoints. In every photo, find small distinctive patches (corners, texture) and compute for each a vector describing what it looks like. The best known is SIFT (Lowe 2004): enlarge, shrink or rotate the photo and the same patch keeps nearly the same vector.
  2. 匹配。在两张照片之间，把向量最像的特征点配成对。后来有了学来的匹配：SuperGlue（Sarlin 等 2020）用图神经网络一起看两张照片的特征点再配对，LoFTR（Sun 等 2021）连特征点都不用先找，LightGlue（Lindenberger 等 2023）更快；很多重建流水线用它们代替手工的配对。Matching. Between two photos, pair up the keypoints whose vectors are most alike. Later came learned matching: SuperGlue (Sarlin et al. 2020) looks at both photos’ keypoints together with a graph neural network before pairing them, LoFTR (Sun et al. 2021) does not even need to find keypoints first, and LightGlue (Lindenberger et al. 2023) is faster; many reconstruction pipelines use them in place of hand-made matching.
  3. SfM（运动恢复结构）。从配好的点，同时求出每台相机的位姿和这些点的三维位置，最后用光束法平差（BA，Triggs 等 2000）把所有相机和点一起调一遍。Photo Tourism（Snavely 等 2006）用它把网上的游客照片拼成三维；Agarwal 等 2009 一天之内重建了罗马；COLMAP（Schönberger & Frahm 2016）是今天最常用的开源实现。SfM 的产物是相机位姿和稀疏的点：每个点都是一个匹配上的特征。一边走一边做的 SfM 叫视觉 SLAM：ORB-SLAM（Mur-Artal 等 2015）只用一个普通相机，实时地跟踪相机、建出特征点的地图。SfM (structure from motion). From the matched points, solve for every camera’s pose and the points’ 3D positions together, finishing with bundle adjustment (BA, Triggs et al. 2000), which adjusts all cameras and points at once. Photo Tourism (Snavely et al. 2006) used it to assemble tourists’ Internet photos into 3D; Agarwal et al. 2009 rebuilt Rome within a day; COLMAP (Schönberger & Frahm 2016) is today’s most widely used open-source implementation. SfM produces camera poses and sparse points: each point is a matched keypoint. SfM done on the move is called visual SLAM: ORB-SLAM (Mur-Artal et al. 2015) uses just one ordinary camera to track the camera and build a map of keypoints in real time.
  4. MVS（多视图立体）。相机已知以后，给每个像素都找深度，得到稠密的点。MVS (multi-view stereo). Once the cameras are known, find a depth for every pixel, giving dense points.
  5. 表面和颜色。把稠密的点连成网格，常用泊松重建（Kazhdan 等 2006），再把照片的颜色贴上去。Surface and colour. Connect the dense points into a mesh, often with Poisson reconstruction (Kazhdan et al. 2006), then paste on colours from the photos.

多视图立体：沿着射线，找颜色对得上的地方Multi-view stereo: along the ray, find where the colours agree

有了相机，给参考照片的一个像素找深度：沿着它的射线试一串距离，每个距离对应空间里一个点；把这个点投到邻近的几张照片里，看那里是什么颜色。猜对了，大家看到的是同一个点，颜色应该一样，因为不发亮的表面从哪个方向看颜色都差不多，这叫光度一致性。颜色差最小的那个距离就是答案。一个距离一个距离地扫过去，叫平面扫描。With the cameras known, find the depth of one pixel of a reference photo: try a series of distances along its ray, each one a point in space; project that point into a few neighbouring photos and see what colour is there. At the right distance everyone is looking at the same point, so the colours should agree, because a non-shiny surface looks about the same colour from any direction. This is called photo-consistency. The distance with the smallest colour difference is the answer. Sweeping through the distances one by one is called a plane sweep.

But "no texture, no correspondence" cannot be got around. 只用轮廓：视觉外壳Silhouettes only: the visual hull

还有一种不用找对应的办法：把每张照片里杯子的轮廓抠出来，凡是有一张照片看成"背景"的地方，杯子一定不在那里，削掉；剩下的叫视觉外壳（Laurentini 1994）。它不怕素色，可它永远比真东西胖：凹进去的地方，哪个角度的轮廓都看不出来，比如杯子里面的空心。空间雕刻（Kutulakos & Seitz 2000）在削的时候再检查颜色是否一致，能削掉一些凹处。There is a way that needs no correspondences: cut out the mug’s silhouette in every photo; wherever any photo sees "background", the mug cannot be, so carve it away. What remains is the visual hull (Laurentini 1994). It does not mind plain surfaces, but it is always fatter than the real thing: a concavity never shows in any silhouette, such as the hollow inside the mug. Space carving (Kutulakos & Seitz 2000) also checks colour consistency while carving and can remove some concavities.

照片里还有另一种线索：明暗。光度立体（Woodham 1980）固定相机、换几个方向的灯拍几张，从明暗算出每个像素的表面朝向；从明暗恢复形状（Horn 1970）只用一张。它们都在第 ⑨ 步的光谱里。Photos hold another cue as well: shading. Photometric stereo (Woodham 1980) keeps the camera fixed, takes a few photos lit from different directions, and computes each pixel’s surface orientation from the shading; shape from shading (Horn 1970) uses just one photo. Both are in the spectrum of step ⑨.

这一步的方法吃有纹理、带位姿（或由 SfM 求出位姿）的照片，存成点和网格，比几何（重投影误差）和颜色（光度一致性），求法是匹配、三角测量和最小二乘，吐出相机位姿、点云和网格。强在：几何明确、准，软件成熟，结果能直接拿去量、去抓。弱在：素色、反光、透明和很细的东西（头发、树叶）都难；贴了颜色的网格也画不出照片那样的真实感，尤其是高光和半透明。The methods of this step take in textured photos with poses (or with poses found by SfM), store points and meshes, compare geometry (reprojection error) and colour (photo-consistency), are solved by matching, triangulation and least squares, and put out camera poses, point clouds and meshes. Strengths: the geometry is explicit and accurate, the software is mature, and the results can be measured and grasped directly. Weaknesses: plain, shiny, transparent and very fine things (hair, leaves) are all hard, and a mesh with pasted-on colour cannot look as real as a photo, especially for highlights and semi-transparency.

第 ③ 步 · 主动测距Step ③ · Active depth

自己发光去量：深度相机和融合Send out light and measure: depth cameras and fusion

第 ② 步卡在素色上，是因为它要从颜色里找对应。换一种传感器就不用找：自己打出光，再看光回来的样子，直接测出距离。Step ② got stuck on plain surfaces because it has to find correspondences in colours. A different sensor needs none: it sends out its own light, looks at how the light comes back, and measures distance directly.

  • 结构光：投出一个已知的图案（条纹或散斑），从图案在照片里的变形算深度（综述见 Geng 2011）。第一代 Kinect 就是这样。它等于自己给物体画上花纹，再做三角测量。Structured light: project a known pattern (stripes or speckle) and compute depth from how the pattern deforms in the image (see the review by Geng 2011). The first Kinect worked this way. It amounts to painting texture onto the object yourself and then triangulating.
  • 飞行时间（ToF）：测光打出去再回来用了多久，或者调制过的光回来时相位落后了多少（Hansard 等 2012）。Time of flight (ToF): measure how long light takes to go out and come back, or how far the phase of modulated light lags when it returns (Hansard et al. 2012).
  • 激光雷达（LiDAR）：扫描的激光测距，自动驾驶车顶上转的那种；测得远、测得准，但点比较稀。LiDAR: scanning laser range-finding, the kind spinning on the roofs of self-driving cars; long range and accurate, but the points are rather sparse.
  • 主动双目：两台相机加一个投射器打散斑，给素色的东西画上花纹，再做双目立体；RealSense D400 系列就是这种。机械臂的腕部相机常常就是它。Active stereo: two cameras plus a projector casting speckle, which paints texture onto plain things before binocular stereo; RealSense D400-series cameras work this way. A robot arm’s wrist camera is often one of these.

这里先回答"模态"的第一层意思。传感器模态说的是测的是什么物理量：普通相机测每个方向射来的光，也就是颜色；深度相机测每个方向上的距离；两者合起来叫 RGB-D。在这个意义上，NeRF 和 3DGS 吃的是和 SfM、MVS 一样的普通照片，不是一种新的模态。Here is the first sense of "modality". A sensor modality is about which physical quantity is measured: an ordinary camera measures the light arriving from each direction, that is, colour; a depth camera measures the distance in each direction; together they are called RGB-D. In this sense NeRF and 3DGS take in the same ordinary photos as SfM and MVS: they are not a new modality.

深度相机也有盲区：透明的玻璃、镜子一样的表面、很黑的东西、斜着看的表面（光打上去，散不回来），以及强烈的阳光。每一帧还都有噪声，而且只看得到朝着相机的那一面。Depth cameras have blind spots too: transparent glass, mirror-like surfaces, very dark things, surfaces seen at a grazing angle (the light hits them and does not scatter back), and strong sunlight. Every frame is noisy, and each sees only the side facing the camera.

把一帧帧深度融合起来Fusing depth frames together

要得到完整、干净的表面，就把很多帧平均起来。最常用的办法是 TSDF 融合（Curless & Levoy 1996）：把空间切成小格子，每格存两个数：一个是它沿视线离测到的表面有多远（近似它到最近表面的距离；表面前面为正、后面为负，离得太远的截断在 1），另一个是已经平均了几帧。每来一帧，就用这一帧的深度给每个格子一个新的距离，和以前的取平均。最后，距离等于 0 的地方就是表面，用行进立方体（Lorensen & Cline 1987）抽成网格。KinectFusion（Newcombe 等 2011）把这一套做到了实时：一边用 ICP（迭代最近点，Besl & McKay 1992）把新来的一帧对齐到已经融合出的表面上，求出相机的位姿，一边融合。For a complete, clean surface, average many frames. The most common way is TSDF fusion (Curless & Levoy 1996): cut space into small cells, each storing two numbers: how far it is from the measured surface along the line of sight (roughly its distance to the nearest surface; positive in front of the surface and negative behind it, truncated at 1 when far away), and how many frames have been averaged so far. Each new frame gives every cell a new distance from its depths, averaged with the earlier ones. In the end, the surface is where the distance is 0, extracted as a mesh with marching cubes (Lorensen & Cline 1987). KinectFusion (Newcombe et al. 2011) made this real time: it aligns each new frame to the surface fused so far with ICP (iterative closest point, Besl & McKay 1992) to find the camera pose, while fusing.

s = min(1, (zm − z) / τ)D ← (W·D + s) / (W + 1), W ← W + 1

一个格子在这条视线上比测到的表面近多少，以截断距离 τ 为单位、最多算到 1，就是这一帧给它的距离 s；格子里存的 D 是所有帧给它的距离的平均。How much nearer a cell is than the measured surface along this line of sight, in units of the truncation distance τ and capped at 1, is the distance s this frame gives it; the D stored in the cell is the average of the distances all the frames gave it.

平均让噪声变小：n 帧独立的噪声平均以后，大约只剩原来的 1/√n。截断，是因为离表面很远的格子，这一帧只知道"这里是空的"，不知道它离表面多远。在表面后面超过 τ 的格子（s < −1）这一帧不更新：那里可能是物体内部，也可能根本看不见。Averaging reduces noise: after averaging n frames of independent noise, only about 1/√n of it remains. The truncation is there because, for a cell far from the surface, this frame knows only that "it is empty here", not how far away the surface is. Cells more than τ behind the surface (s < −1) are not updated by this frame: they may be inside an object, or simply not visible.

这一步的方法吃深度图（RGB-D 还带颜色），存成体素格子里的截断距离，比几何（测到的深度和格子的位置），求法是一帧帧加权平均，吐出网格和相机的轨迹。强在：快，能实时；不怕素色；几何准，拿来抓东西最直接。弱在：要专门的传感器；透明、反光、很黑的东西测不到；格子越细越占内存；颜色一般，画不出照片那样的真实感。The methods of this step take in depth maps (RGB-D adds colour), store truncated distances in a voxel grid, compare geometry (measured depth against cell positions), are solved by weighted averaging frame after frame, and put out meshes and the camera’s trajectory. Strengths: fast, real time, unbothered by plain surfaces, accurate geometry, the most direct route to grasping. Weaknesses: it needs a special sensor; transparent, shiny and very dark things go unmeasured; finer grids eat more memory; colour is mediocre and cannot look as real as a photo.

第 ④ 步 · 从光场到辐射场Step ④ · From light fields to radiance fields

不求表面，只存"光往哪走"No surfaces, just store "where the light goes"

前三步都先求几何（点、面），再把颜色贴上去。还有另一条路。如果要的只是"从一个新角度看是什么样"，何必先求表面？照片记下的就是一条条射线的颜色；只要知道空间里每一条射线是什么颜色，新视角就是把新相机的那些射线挑出来。这叫光场（Levoy & Hanrahan 1996）：用非常多的照片把射线密密地采一遍，新视角就是查表加插值，完全不求几何。同年的 Lumigraph（Gortler 等 1996）做的是同一件事，只是还从轮廓里造一个粗糙的几何，用来修正插值。代价是照片要非常密。基于图像的渲染（IBR）走中间路线：有一个粗糙的几何，从最近的几张照片里取颜色；Debevec 等 1996 用它从几张照片渲染出建筑，颜色随视角在几张照片之间过渡。The first three steps all compute geometry first (points, surfaces) and paste colour on afterwards. There is another road. If all you want is "what it looks like from a new angle", why find surfaces at all? A photo records the colours of rays; if you knew the colour of every ray in space, a new view would just mean picking out the new camera’s rays. This is the light field (Levoy & Hanrahan 1996): sample the rays densely with a great many photos, and a new view is a table lookup plus interpolation, with no geometry at all. The Lumigraph of the same year (Gortler et al. 1996) does the same thing but also builds a rough geometry from silhouettes to correct the interpolation. The price is that the photos must be very dense. Image-based rendering (IBR) takes a middle road: with a rough geometry, take colours from the nearest few photos; Debevec et al. 1996 rendered buildings this way from a few photos, the colour shifting between photos as the view changes.

有了深度网络以后，这条路上出现了多平面图像（MPI）：网络从两张或几张照片预测一叠前后排开、半透明的彩色平面，新视角就是从新的角度把这一叠平面叠起来看（Zhou 等 2018）。LLFF（Mildenhall 等 2019）把附近每张照片都变成这样一叠平面，画新视角时混合最近的几叠。一叠半透明的层，已经很像下面要讲的体渲染；NeRF 的第一作者正是 LLFF 的第一作者，NeRF 测试真实场景用的也是 LLFF 的数据。Once deep networks arrived, this road led to multiplane images (MPI): from two or a few photos, a network predicts a stack of semi-transparent coloured planes at different depths, and a new view is that stack composited as seen from the new angle (Zhou et al. 2018). LLFF (Mildenhall et al. 2019) turns each nearby photo into such a stack and, for a new view, blends the nearest few. A stack of semi-transparent layers is already very like the volume rendering described next; NeRF’s first author is LLFF’s first author, and NeRF’s tests on real scenes use LLFF’s data.

辐射场把光场"收进"三维空间：不再存每条射线的颜色，而存空间里每一点的两样东西：A radiance field folds the light field into 3D space: instead of the colour of every ray, it stores two things at every point in space:

  • 密度 σ：这一点有多挡光。空气是 0，杯壁里很大，一团雾介于两者之间。Density σ: how strongly this point blocks light. Zero in air, very large inside the mug’s wall, in between in a patch of fog.
  • 颜色 c：这一点朝某个方向发出（或反射）的光是什么颜色。它可以随方向变，这样才表示得了跟着相机走的高光。Colour c: the colour of the light this point emits (or reflects) in a given direction. It may change with direction, which is what lets it represent a highlight that moves with the camera.

一个像素的颜色，就是沿它的射线把这些点的颜色加起来，但每一点都被它前面的东西挡掉一部分。这叫体渲染，最早用来画烟、云和 CT 数据（Kajiya & Von Herzen 1984；Max 1995 整理了从"只吸收"到"带散射"的几种光学模型，NeRF 用的是其中"发光加吸收"的那一种）。A pixel’s colour is the sum of these points’ colours along its ray, each point partly blocked by what lies in front of it. This is volume rendering, first used to draw smoke, clouds and CT data (Kajiya & Von Herzen 1984; Max 1995 surveyed several optical models, from absorption only to models with scattering, and NeRF uses the emission–absorption one among them).

C = Σi Ti · αi · ciαi = 1 − exp(−σi · δi), Ti = Πj<i (1 − αj)

沿射线取一串采样点。每个点挡掉 α 那么多的光，同时按这个比例把自己的颜色加进来；它前面的点已经挡掉了一部分，只剩 T 的光看得到它。像素的颜色是所有点的颜色按 T·α 加权的和。Take a series of samples along the ray. Each blocks a fraction α of the light and adds its own colour in that same proportion; the samples in front have already blocked some of it, so only a fraction T of the light sees it. The pixel’s colour is the sum of all the samples’ colours weighted by T·α.

光穿过厚 δ、密度 σ 的一小段，被挡掉的比例是 1 − e−σδ：越密、越厚挡得越多，而且永远不超过 1。T 是"光走到这里还剩多少"，每经过一个点就乘一次 (1 − α)。wi = Tiαi 是第 i 个点在像素颜色里占的份额；所有点的份额，加上最后透过去、落到背景上的那部分 T，正好是 1。Light crossing a short stretch of thickness δ and density σ loses a fraction 1 − e−σδ: denser and thicker blocks more, and never more than all of it. 这个交互里的场，是从真实的杯子直接造出来的。真正的问题是反过来的：只有照片，不知道场。可是这个式子处处可导，于是可以让场从一个随便的样子出发，按"画出来的和照片差多少"的梯度一点点改，直到所有照片都对得上。网格也能这样反推：把光栅化变"软"（Soft Rasterizer，Liu 等 2019），或者专门写好它的梯度（nvdiffrast，Laine 等 2020），就能按照片的误差去挪顶点。可网格的连接关系是事先定死的：从一个球出发，长不出把手那个洞。体渲染的场没有这个限制。剩下的问题是：场存在哪里？存进一个网络，就是 NeRF（第 ⑤ 步）；存成一堆高斯，就是 3DGS（第 ⑥ 步）。The field in this widget is built straight from the real mug. The real problem runs the other way: there are only photos and the field is unknown. But the expression is differentiable everywhere, so the field can start from anything and be nudged along the gradient of "how far the rendering is from the photos" until every photo matches. A mesh can be worked backwards the same way: make rasterisation "soft" (Soft Rasterizer, Liu et al. 2019) or work out its gradients specially (nvdiffrast, Laine et al. 2020), and the vertices can be moved by the error against the photos. But a mesh’s connectivity is fixed in advance: starting from a sphere, it cannot grow the hole of the handle. A volume-rendered field has no such limit. What remains is where to store the field. In a network, it is NeRF (step ⑤); as a crowd of Gaussians, it is 3DGS (step ⑥).

第 ⑤ 步 · NeRFStep ⑤ · NeRF

把辐射场存进一个网络A radiance field stored in a network

NeRF（神经辐射场，Mildenhall 等 2020）的做法：用一个多层感知机（MLP）当这个场，输入一个点的位置和看它的方向，输出那里的密度和颜色。训练时，从照片里随机挑一批像素，对每个像素沿射线采样、逐点问网络、按第 ④ 步的式子算出颜色，和照片比，再把差反向传播回网络的每个权重。网络就是场；训练好了，从任何角度都能渲染。NeRF (neural radiance fields, Mildenhall et al. 2020) makes a multilayer perceptron (MLP) the field: it takes a point’s position and the direction it is viewed from and returns the density and colour there. In training, a random batch of pixels is drawn from the photos; for each pixel, samples are taken along its ray, the network is asked about each one, the colour is computed with the formula of step ④ and compared with the photo, and the error is backpropagated into every weight of the network. The network is the field; once trained, it can be rendered from any angle.

网络是处处可导的函数，渲染也处处可导，所以整条链"权重 → 场 → 渲染 → 和照片比"可以端到端地求梯度。密度只由位置决定，颜色还取决于方向：一点"在不在"对所有方向都一样，几何因此在各个视角之间一致；高光又可以随方向变。The network is differentiable everywhere and so is rendering, so the whole chain "weights → field → rendering → comparison with the photo" can be differentiated end to end. Density depends only on position while colour also depends on direction: whether something is "there" is the same from every direction, which keeps the geometry consistent across views, while highlights can still change with direction.

网络只记住了这一个场景：它不是一个"会重建的模型"，换一个场景要从头再训。NeRF 里的"学习"，是对一个场景的优化，网络只是存场的容器。The network remembers this one scene only: it is not a "model that can reconstruct", and a new scene means training from scratch. The "learning" in NeRF is optimisation of one scene; the network is just the container for the field.

位置编码：让网络画得出锐利的边Positional encoding: letting the network draw sharp edges

直接把坐标喂进网络，学出来的场总是糊的：网络天生偏爱平滑的函数，越小越明显。NeRF 先把每个坐标变成一串频率越来越高的正弦和余弦，再喂进去：Feed raw coordinates to the network and the learned field always comes out blurry: networks are biased towards smooth functions, the smaller the more so. NeRF first turns each coordinate into a series of sines and cosines of ever higher frequency:

γ(p) = (sin(2⁰πp), cos(2⁰πp), sin(2¹πp), cos(2¹πp), …, sin(2L−1πp), cos(2L−1πp))

每个坐标 p 换成 L 对正弦和余弦，频率一个比一个高一倍。Each coordinate p is replaced by L pairs of sines and cosines, each frequency double the one before.

高频的正弦在 p 挪一点点时就变很多。网络拿到它们，用简单的组合就能拼出急剧的变化，比如杯壁的边缘、条纹的边；没有它们，网络得自己从平滑的输入里"造"出急剧的变化，很难学会。A high-frequency sine changes a lot when p moves a little. Given these, the network can build abrupt changes, such as the edge of the mug wall or of a stripe, from simple combinations; without them it would have to manufacture abrupt changes from smooth inputs on its own, which is hard to learn.

网络太慢：换成格子和哈希表Networks are too slow: switching to grids and hash tables

2022 年前后的一批工作，把场从网络换成显式的格子：A group of papers around 2022 moved the field out of the network into explicit grids:

  • Plenoxels（Fridovich-Keil、Yu 等 2022）：根本不用网络。稀疏的体素格子，每格存密度和球谐系数（颜色随方向的变化），直接用梯度下降调格子里的数，比 NeRF 快两个数量级。交互里的"格子"就是这种：64 × 64 个格子、每格 10 个数。Plenoxels (Fridovich-Keil, Yu et al. 2022): no network at all. A sparse voxel grid stores density and spherical-harmonic coefficients (how colour changes with direction) in each cell, and gradient descent adjusts the numbers in the grid directly, two orders of magnitude faster than NeRF. The widget’s "grid" is of this kind: 64 × 64 cells with 10 numbers each.
  • DVGO（Sun 等 2022）直接优化体素格子；TensoRF（Chen 等 2022）把格子分解成几个低维的因子，省内存。DVGO (Sun et al. 2022) optimises a voxel grid directly; TensoRF (Chen et al. 2022) factorises the grid into a few low-dimensional factors to save memory.
  • Instant-NGP（Müller 等 2022）：多个分辨率的哈希表里存特征向量，再接一个很小的网络；一个场景几秒到几分钟就训练好。Instant-NGP (Müller et al. 2022): feature vectors stored in hash tables at several resolutions, followed by a very small network; a scene trains in seconds to minutes.

快的代价是参数多：交互里的格子有 40 960 个数，网络只有 2476 个；而且格子之间互不相干，训久了容易把照片"背下来"，在没有照片管的地方长出杂点。网络的平滑偏好在这里反倒成了一种约束。The price of speed is parameters: the widget’s grid holds 40,960 numbers against the network’s 2476; and since cells are independent of each other, long training tends to "memorise" the photos and grow stray specks where no photo constrains them. Here the network’s preference for smoothness turns out to be a constraint in its own right.

Block-NeRF (Tancik et al. 2022) cuts a city district into many blocks and trains one for each.
  • 野外的照片：NeRF-W（Martin-Brualla 等 2021）对付网上的游客照片：给每张照片一个外观向量，吸收不同时间、天气、曝光带来的差别；再用另一个分支把行人、车这些只在个别照片里出现的东西分出去。Photos from the wild: NeRF-W (Martin-Brualla et al. 2021) tackles tourists’ Internet photos: an appearance vector per photo absorbs differences of time, weather and exposure, and a separate branch separates out people and cars that appear in only some photos.
  • 会动的场景：D-NeRF（Pumarola 等 2021）、Nerfies（Park 等 2021）、HyperNeRF（Park 等 2021）、K-Planes（Fridovich-Keil 等 2023）。Scenes that move: D-NeRF (Pumarola et al. 2021), Nerfies (Park et al. 2021), HyperNeRF (Park et al. 2021), K-Planes (Fridovich-Keil et al. 2023).
  • 在 NeRF 之前：Neural Volumes（Lombardi 等 2019）已经用可微的体渲染从照片学一个体素格子；SRN（Sitzmann 等 2019）用网络表示场景，再沿射线找表面。Before NeRF: Neural Volumes (Lombardi et al. 2019) already learned a voxel grid from photos through differentiable volume rendering; SRN (Sitzmann et al. 2019) represented a scene with a network and searched for the surface along each ray.

NeRF 吃带位姿的照片（位姿一般来自 COLMAP），存成辐射场（网络的权重），比的是画出来的颜色和照片，求法是对每个场景反复优化，吐出新视角。强在：真实感好，半透明和细小的东西也表示得了，网络版存储很小（一个场景几 MB）。弱在：训练和渲染慢（格子版快得多）；要很多张照片和准的位姿；几何是副产品（第 ⑦ 步）；场藏在权重里，不好挪、不好删、不好编辑。NeRF takes in posed photos (poses usually from COLMAP), stores a radiance field (the network’s weights), compares rendered colours with the photos, is solved by optimising each scene over and over, and puts out new views. Strengths: very realistic, represents semi-transparent and fine things too, and the network version is small (a few MB per scene). Weaknesses: slow to train and render (the grid versions much less so); needs many photos and accurate poses; geometry is a by-product (step ⑦); the field is hidden in the weights, hard to move, delete or edit.

第 ⑥ 步 · 高斯泼溅Step ⑥ · Gaussian splatting

把辐射场存成一堆半透明的高斯A radiance field stored as a crowd of semi-transparent Gaussians

3DGS（三维高斯泼溅，Kerbl 等 2023）换了一种存法：场景是几百万个半透明的三维高斯，每个像一个边缘模糊的椭球。每个高斯有一个中心 μ；一个形状 Σ，由三个轴的长短加一个旋转给出；一个不透明度 o；以及颜色，用球谐系数表示它随方向的变化。渲染不再沿射线一点点采样，而是反过来：把每个高斯投到照片上，变成一个二维的高斯色斑，这叫泼溅；按远近排好，从前往后一层层叠。3DGS (3D Gaussian splatting, Kerbl et al. 2023) stores the field another way: the scene is millions of semi-transparent 3D Gaussians, each like an ellipsoid with blurry edges. Each Gaussian has a centre μ; a shape Σ, given by the lengths of its three axes plus a rotation; an opacity o; and a colour, with spherical-harmonic coefficients describing how it changes with direction. Rendering no longer samples along rays step by step but works the other way round: each Gaussian is projected onto the photo as a 2D Gaussian blob, which is called splatting; the blobs are sorted by distance and blended front to back, layer by layer.

像素的颜色还是"前面挡掉多少、自己贡献多少"的叠加，和第 ④ 步的体渲染是同一个式子；只是现在每一层是一个高斯色斑，它在这个像素上有多不透明，取决于像素离色斑中心多远。The pixel colour is still a blend of "how much is blocked in front, how much each contributes", the same formula as step ④’s volume rendering; only now each layer is a Gaussian blob, and how opaque it is at this pixel depends on how far the pixel is from the blob’s centre.

一个三维高斯投到照片上，近似还是一个高斯：中心落在 ui，形状被投影的雅可比 J 拉伸成 Σ′i。这是 EWA 泼溅（Zwicker 等 2001）的办法：在高斯中心附近把投影近似成线性的。于是不用沿射线采样，空的地方一点计算都不花，只有高斯在的地方才有东西。Ti 还是前面所有色斑挡剩下的光，所以要先按深度排序。A 3D Gaussian projected onto a photo is, approximately, still a Gaussian: its centre lands at ui and its shape is stretched by the projection’s Jacobian J into Σ′i. This is the method of EWA splatting (Zwicker et al. 2001): treat the projection as linear near the Gaussian’s centre. So there is no sampling along rays, empty space costs nothing, and only where Gaussians are is there anything to compute. Ti is still the light left by all the blobs in front, so they must first be sorted by depth.

和以前的点渲染是什么关系How it relates to earlier point rendering

泼溅不是新东西。Westover 1989 年提出泼溅来画体数据（Westover 1989；1990 年的论文细化了"脚印"怎么算）：把每个体素的脚印投到屏幕上，叠起来。Pfister 等 2000 的面元（surfel）把表面存成一堆带朝向的小圆片。Zwicker 等 2001 的 EWA 泼溅解决了投影后的形状和抗锯齿。深度学习来了以后，点也开始从照片里学：DSS（Wang Yifan 等 2019）给点的位置和法向设计了梯度，让点云能按渲染出的误差来调；NPBG（Aliev 等 2020）给每个点存一个可学的特征，投到屏幕上以后，再交给一个网络画成照片；ADOP（Rückert 等 2022）把点画成一个像素大小，用网络补洞、上色，连相机也一起优化。3DGS 新在：每个高斯是一个可以拉长、转动的椭球，自己带着颜色和不透明度，按 NeRF 的体渲染式子直接叠起来，不用网络来补洞、上色；高斯的个数在训练中自适应地增减；再加上显卡上又快又可微的分块光栅化。它的渲染方式来自图形学的点渲染，它的训练方式来自 NeRF。Splatting is not new. Westover introduced splatting to draw volume data in 1989 (Westover 1989; his 1990 paper refined how the "footprints" are computed): project every voxel’s footprint onto the screen and stack them up. The surfels of Pfister et al. 2000 store a surface as many small oriented discs. EWA splatting by Zwicker et al. 2001 worked out the projected shape and the anti-aliasing. Once deep learning arrived, points too began to be learned from photos: DSS (Wang Yifan et al. 2019) designed gradients for point positions and normals so that a point cloud can be adjusted by the rendering error; NPBG (Aliev et al. 2020) stores a learnable feature in each point and, after projecting the points onto the screen, hands the result to a network that paints the photo; ADOP (Rückert et al. 2022) draws each point as a single pixel, fills holes and shades with a network, and optimises the cameras too. What is new in 3DGS: each Gaussian is an ellipsoid that can stretch and rotate, carrying its own colour and opacity, and they are blended directly with NeRF’s volume-rendering formula, with no network to fill holes or shade; the number of Gaussians grows and shrinks adaptively during training; and the tiled rasterisation on the GPU is both fast and differentiable. Its rendering comes from point rendering in graphics, its training from NeRF.

之后的分支（第 ⑨ 步有更多）：Branches that followed (step ⑨ has more):

  • 抗锯齿：Mip-Splatting（Yu 等 2024）给高斯加一个三维的平滑滤波和一个二维的、像素大小的滤波，放大缩小不再出问题。交互里的渲染器也用了它的二维滤波。Anti-aliasing: Mip-Splatting (Yu et al. 2024) gives the Gaussians a 3D smoothing filter and a pixel-sized 2D filter, so zooming in and out no longer causes trouble. The renderer in the widget uses its 2D filter too.
  • 更省、更稳：Scaffold-GS（Lu 等 2024）用一些锚点按需生成高斯；LightGaussian（Fan 等 2024）剪掉不重要的高斯、压缩颜色，存储平均缩小到原来的 1/15；3DGS-MCMC（Kheradmand 等 2024）把增减高斯写成一种有原则的重新安放，对起点不那么敏感。Leaner and steadier: Scaffold-GS (Lu et al. 2024) has anchor points spawn Gaussians on demand; LightGaussian (Fan et al. 2024) prunes unimportant Gaussians and compresses the colour, cutting storage to 1/15 on average; 3DGS-MCMC (Kheradmand et al. 2024) recasts adding and removing Gaussians as a principled relocation, less sensitive to the starting point.

  • 会动的场景：4D 高斯泼溅（Wu 等 2024）、Dynamic 3D Gaussians（Luiten 等 2024），后者顺带跟踪了每个点的运动。Scenes that move: 4D Gaussian splatting (Wu et al. 2024) and Dynamic 3D Gaussians (Luiten et al. 2024), the latter also tracking how every point moves.

3DGS 吃带位姿的照片（加上 SfM 点当起点），存成一堆显式的高斯，比的是画出来的颜色和照片，求法是对每个场景反复优化，吐出新视角。强在：训练快、渲染实时；场景是显式的，能直接挪、删、拼高斯，便于编辑。弱在：存储大；几何不精确（第 ⑦ 步）；照片少时容易在空中长出漂浮的色斑；要好的位姿和起点。3DGS takes in posed photos (with SfM points as the starting point), stores a crowd of explicit Gaussians, compares rendered colours with the photos, is solved by optimising each scene over and over, and puts out new views. Strengths: fast training and real-time rendering; the scene is explicit, so Gaussians can be moved, deleted and combined directly, which makes editing easy. Weaknesses: large storage; imprecise geometry (step ⑦); with few photos it easily grows blobs floating in mid-air; it needs good poses and a good start.

第 ⑦ 步 · 表面和深度Step ⑦ · Surfaces and depth

画得像，不等于形状对Looking right is not the same as the right shape

机器人要抓把手，要的是几何：表面在哪，准到几毫米。NeRF 和 3DGS 优化的是"照片对不对得上"，几何只是副产品。要从一个训练好的场里取出几何，常见的办法有两种：To grasp the handle, the robot needs geometry: where the surface is, to a few millimetres. NeRF and 3DGS optimise "do the photos match", and geometry is only a by-product. There are two common ways to take geometry out of a trained field:

  • 给密度定一个门槛，高于它的算"实"，再用行进立方体抽出表面。门槛定多少没有标准答案，半透明的地方会糊。Set a threshold on density, count everything above it as "solid", and extract a surface with marching cubes. There is no standard answer for the threshold, and semi-transparent regions come out blurred.
  • 渲染深度：沿每个像素的射线，把每一点的深度按它的贡献 w 加权平均（平均深度）；或者取光被挡掉一半的那一点的深度（中位深度）。Rendered depth: along each pixel’s ray, average the depths of the samples weighted by their shares w (mean depth); or take the depth of the point where half the light has been blocked (median depth).

平均深度：每一点的深度按它在像素颜色里的份额加权平均。中位深度：从近往远走，光第一次被挡掉一半的那一点的深度。Mean depth: each sample’s depth weighted by its share of the pixel’s colour. Median depth: walking from near to far, the depth of the point where half the light is first blocked.

贡献集中在一点时，两者一样。贡献分在两处时，比如一个像素一半落在把手上、一半落在后面的杯身上，平均深度会落在两者之间的空中，那里什么也没有；中位深度总是落在某个真的挡光的东西上。When the shares are concentrated at one point, the two agree. When they are split between two places, say a pixel half on the handle and half on the body behind it, the mean depth lands in the air between them where there is nothing; the median depth always lands on something that really blocks light.

教训是：照片的损失只管"看起来对"，要几何，就得在训练里加进管几何的东西。办法有三类。The lesson: a photo loss only cares about "looking right"; for geometry, something that cares about geometry must be added to training. There are three kinds of remedy.

办法一：换一种以表面为中心的表示Remedy one: a representation built around the surface

有符号距离场（SDF）给每一点存它到最近表面的距离，物体外面为正、里面为负，表面就是 0 的地方。第 ③ 步的 TSDF 就是一个存在格子里的 SDF。把它搬进网络：DeepSDF（Park 等 2019）用网络表示一类形状的 SDF；占据网络（Mescheder 等 2019）用网络表示"这一点在不在物体里面"。可它们训练时要三维的数据。要从照片学，就得让 SDF 也能体渲染：把 SDF 变成密度，再用第 ④ 步的式子。A signed distance field (SDF) stores at every point its distance to the nearest surface, positive outside the object and negative inside, so the surface is where it is 0. The TSDF of step ③ is an SDF stored in a grid. Move it into a network: DeepSDF (Park et al. 2019) represents the SDF of a class of shapes with a network; occupancy networks (Mescheder et al. 2019) use a network to say whether a point is inside the object. But they need 3D data to train. To learn from photos, the SDF must be volume-rendered too: turn it into a density, then use the formula of step ④.

σ(P) = a · Ψβ(−s(P))

密度是有符号距离的一个平滑的台阶：物体外面离表面远的地方是 0，里面是 a；台阶的中点正好在表面上，从 0 升到 a 要用掉表面两侧各几个 β。Density is a smooth step of the signed distance: zero outside the object away from the surface, a inside; the middle of the step sits exactly on the surface, and the rise from 0 to a takes a few β on either side of it.

这样一来，体渲染的贡献 w = T·α 就集中在表面附近很窄的一段里：表面前面几乎没有密度，光全透过来；一过表面，光几乎全被挡住。几何由 s 定义，是明确的：表面就是 s = 0 的地方。训练时 β 也跟着学，越学越小，表面越来越锐。Then the volume-rendering shares w = T·α concentrate in a narrow band around the surface: in front of it there is almost no density and the light passes through; just past it, nearly all the light is blocked. The geometry is defined by s and is unambiguous: the surface is where s = 0. β is learned during training and shrinks, so the surface gets sharper and sharper.

β = 1 mm。离表面外侧 3 mm 的点，s = 3 mm：Ψβ(−3 mm) = ½ e−3 ≈ 0.025，密度只有里面的 2.5%；正在表面上，Ψ = 0.5；表面里面 3 mm，Ψ ≈ 0.975。β = 1 mm. For a point 3 mm outside the surface, s = 3 mm: Ψβ(−3 mm) = ½ e−3 ≈ 0.025, so the density is only 2.5% of that inside; exactly on the surface, Ψ = 0.5; 3 mm inside, Ψ ≈ 0.975.

这是 VolSDF（Yariv 等 2021）的写法。同一年的 NeuS（Wang 等 2021）从 SDF 推出不透明度的方式不同，目的一样：让贡献的峰正好落在表面上。Neuralangelo（Li 等 2023）把 SDF 存进 Instant-NGP 那样的哈希格子，从视频里重建出很细的表面。代价是比纯辐射场慢，透明和很细的东西（头发、烟）不好表示。This is how VolSDF (Yariv et al. 2021) writes it. NeuS (Wang et al. 2021), the same year, derives opacity from the SDF in a different way with the same aim: to put the peak of the shares exactly on the surface. Neuralangelo (Li et al. 2023) stores the SDF in hash grids like Instant-NGP’s and reconstructs very fine surfaces from video. The price: slower than plain radiance fields, and transparent or very fine things (hair, smoke) are hard to represent.

办法二：把高斯压扁，贴到表面上Remedy two: flatten the Gaussians and lay them on the surface

2DGS（Huang 等 2024）把三维椭球换成扁平的二维圆盘，也就是面元：从哪个方向看，它和射线的交点都是确定的，几何在各个视角之间一致；再加上两项损失，让每条射线上的贡献集中在一处、让法向和深度一致，表面就准得多。SuGaR（Guédon & Lepetit 2024）让高斯贴到表面上，再抽出一个网格；Gaussian Opacity Fields（Yu 等 2024）从高斯定义出一个处处都有的不透明度场，再从里面抽表面。2DGS (Huang et al. 2024) replaces 3D ellipsoids with flat 2D discs, that is, surfels: from any direction their intersection with a ray is well defined, so the geometry agrees across views; adding two losses, one concentrating the shares along each ray in one place and one making normals agree with depth, makes the surfaces far more accurate. SuGaR (Guédon & Lepetit 2024) makes the Gaussians lie on the surface and then extracts a mesh; Gaussian Opacity Fields (Yu et al. 2024) define an opacity field everywhere from the Gaussians and extract the surface from it.

办法三：加一个测几何的传感器，或几何的先验Remedy three: add a sensor that measures geometry, or a prior on it

上面交互里的"照片 + 深度相机"就是这种。这在机器人上很常见，腕部相机常常就是 RGB-D 相机：SplaTAM（Keetha 等 2024）用 RGB-D 一边跟踪相机，一边建高斯地图。没有深度相机时，SfM 留下的稀疏点也有深度：DS-NeRF（Deng 等 2022）拿它们当监督，照片少时也能训，还训得更快。再不然，第 ⑧ 步的单目深度网络可以当这个"传感器"。The widget’s "Photos + depth camera" is of this kind. It is common on robots, whose wrist cameras are often RGB-D cameras: SplaTAM (Keetha et al. 2024) uses RGB-D to track the camera while building a Gaussian map. Without a depth camera, the sparse points left by SfM have depths too: DS-NeRF (Deng et al. 2022) uses them as supervision, so training works with fewer photos and goes faster. Failing that, the monocular depth networks of step ⑧ can serve as this "sensor".

反过来，什么时候偏偏要用 NeRF，而不是深度相机？透明的东西。深度相机打在玻璃上，光要么穿过去，要么乱反射，很难测准。Dex-NeRF（Ichnowski 等 2021）从多张照片训练 NeRF，用密度找出透明物体的表面，让机械臂去抓；GraspNeRF（Dai 等 2023）把它做成了不用逐场景训练的版本。Conversely, when would you choose NeRF over a depth camera? For transparent things. A depth camera’s light either passes through glass or reflects off it erratically, so it struggles to measure it. Dex-NeRF (Ichnowski et al. 2021) trains a NeRF from many photos and uses its density to find the surfaces of transparent objects for the arm to grasp; GraspNeRF (Dai et al. 2023) made a version with no per-scene training.

这一步的方法吃的和 NeRF、3DGS 一样（有时加上深度），存成有符号距离、扁平的面元或贴着表面的高斯，比颜色，也比几何（深度、法向、贡献集中不集中），求法还是对每个场景反复优化，吐出网格和深度，新视角往往稍差一点。它们处在第 ③ 步（几何为先）和第 ⑤、⑥ 步（画得像为先）之间。The methods of this step take in the same as NeRF and 3DGS (sometimes plus depth), store signed distances, flat surfels or Gaussians laid on the surface, compare colour and also geometry (depth, normals, how concentrated the shares are), are still solved by optimising each scene, and put out meshes and depth, with new views often slightly worse. They sit between step ③ (geometry first) and steps ⑤ and ⑥ (looking right first).

第 ⑧ 步 · 学来的先验Step ⑧ · Learned priors

照片不够时，信息从哪来When photos run short, where does the information come from?

到这里为止的每个方法，都只用这一个场景自己的数据：照片、位姿、深度。数据够，它们都做得很好；数据不够，比如照片少、角度窄、背面没拍到、表面是素色，它们只能乱猜，或者干脆什么都说不出来。人看一张照片就能大致想出杯子的样子，因为人见过无数个杯子。学来的先验就是这个：在成千上万个别的场景上训练一个模型，让它知道"世界一般长什么样"，再用它补这一个场景缺的信息。Every method so far uses only this one scene’s own data: photos, poses, depth. With enough data they all do well; with too little, such as few photos, a narrow range of angles, an unphotographed back or a plain surface, they can only guess wildly or say nothing at all. A person can roughly picture a mug from one photo because they have seen countless mugs. That is what a learned prior is: train a model on thousands of other scenes so it knows "what the world usually looks like", then use it to fill in what this scene is missing.

先看信息不够时会发生什么。假设杯子靠着墙，机械臂只够得着它前面 90° 的一段弧。First, what happens when information runs short. Suppose the mug stands against a wall and the arm can only reach a 90° arc in front of it.

先玩：3 张照片：拍过的角度 PSNR 多少？两张照片中间的角度呢？杯子背面呢？打开"加上网络估的深度"：中间的角度提高了多少？背面呢？再换成 7 张，开、关先验比一比：为什么这次先验反而碍事？Try first: with 3 photos, what is the PSNR at the photographed angles? Between two photos? At the back of the mug? Turn on "Add depths from a network": how much do the in-between angles improve? And the back? Then switch to 7 photos and compare with the prior on and off: why does the prior get in the way this time?

3 张照片时，拍过的角度在 48 dB 上下，几乎一模一样；两张之间的角度只有 21 dB 左右。照片太少，有很多种摆法都能把这 3 张凑对，大多数是错的，这叫过拟合。加上网络估的深度，两张之间的角度提高 4 dB 左右。可照片有 7 张时，同样的先验反而让中间的角度降了 5 dB 多：照片已经够了，一个差几毫米的先验在跟照片较劲。背面无论如何都只有 11 dB 左右：深度先验说的是东西在哪，说不出没拍到的那一面是什么颜色。With 3 photos, the photographed angles score around 48 dB, almost perfect; the angles between two photos only about 21 dB. With so few photos, many arrangements fit all 3 and most of them are wrong; this is called overfitting. Adding the network’s depths raises the in-between angles by about 4 dB. But with 7 photos the same prior lowers them by over 5 dB: the photos are already enough, and a prior that is a few millimetres off is fighting them. The back stays at about 11 dB whatever you do: a depth prior says where things are, not what colour the unphotographed side is.

交互里的"网络估的深度"是模拟的：真深度加上几毫米的平滑误差。真实的单目深度网络常常只给相对的远近，差一个比例和一个偏移，用它的方法会换成一种不在乎比例的损失。The widget’s "depths from a network" are simulated: the true depth plus a smooth error of a few millimetres. Real single-image depth networks often give only relative depth, off by an unknown scale and offset, and methods that use them switch to a loss that ignores scale.

先验的三种用法Three ways to use a prior

交互里是第一种。三种用法，从"给优化帮个忙"，到"直接给出答案"，再到"把没看到的编出来"：The widget shows the first. The three ways range from "helping the optimisation along", to "giving the answer outright", to "inventing what was never seen":

一、当约束。每个场景还是从头优化，只是多加一项来自先验的损失。最常用的先验是单目深度：从一张照片估每个像素的深度。这件事很早就有人从数据里学：Make3D（Saxena 等 2009）用马尔可夫随机场，从图像特征推出场景的三维结构；Eigen 等 2014 换成深度网络，一个粗的网络看全局，一个细的网络看局部。之后，MiDaS（Ranftl 等 2020）把许多数据集混在一起训练相对深度；DPT（Ranftl 等 2021）换成视觉 Transformer；Depth Anything（Yang 等 2024）用了大量没有标注的照片；Depth Pro（Bochkovskii 等 2025）不到一秒给出带绝对尺度的深度。One: as a constraint. Each scene is still optimised from scratch, with one more loss term from the prior. The most common prior is monocular depth: estimating every pixel’s depth from a single photo. People began learning this from data long ago: Make3D (Saxena et al. 2009) inferred a scene’s 3D structure from image features with a Markov random field; Eigen et al. 2014 switched to deep networks, a coarse one looking at the whole image and a fine one looking locally. Then MiDaS (Ranftl et al. 2020) trained relative depth on a mixture of many datasets; DPT (Ranftl et al. 2021) switched to a vision transformer; Depth Anything (Yang et al. 2024) used a large number of unlabelled photos; Depth Pro (Bochkovskii et al. 2025) gives depth with absolute scale in under a second.

二、直接给出答案（前馈）。一个在大量场景上训练好的网络，吃几张照片，一次前向就吐出三维，不再为每个场景优化。Two: giving the answer outright (feed-forward). A network trained on a great many scenes takes a few photos and outputs 3D in one forward pass, with no more optimisation per scene.

  • 学来的多视图立体：MVSNet（Yao 等 2018）把第 ② 步手工的"颜色差"换成学来的特征，在一个代价体里找深度。Learned multi-view stereo: MVSNet (Yao et al. 2018) replaces step ②’s hand-made "colour difference" with learned features and finds depth in a cost volume.
  • 前馈的辐射场：pixelNeRF（Yu 等 2021）、IBRNet（Wang 等 2021）、MVSNeRF（Chen 等 2021）从一张或几张照片直接给出辐射场；pixelSplat（Charatan 等 2024）、MVSplat（Chen 等 2024）从两张照片一次前向给出三维高斯。LRM（Hong 等 2024）从一张照片，约 5 秒给出一个用三个平面存的 NeRF。Feed-forward radiance fields: pixelNeRF (Yu et al. 2021), IBRNet (Wang et al. 2021) and MVSNeRF (Chen et al. 2021) give a radiance field straight from one or a few photos; pixelSplat (Charatan et al. 2024) and MVSplat (Chen et al. 2024) give 3D Gaussians from two photos in one forward pass. LRM (Hong et al. 2024) gives, from one photo in about 5 seconds, a NeRF stored on three planes.
  • 三维基础模型：DUSt3R（Wang 等 2024）不需要相机的位姿，也不需要内参：吃两张照片，直接回归每个像素对应的三维点（叫点图），相机位姿、深度、对应关系都从点图里读出来。MASt3R（Leroy 等 2024）在它上面加了匹配。VGGT（Wang 等 2025）一次能吃一张到几百张照片，直接给出相机参数、深度图、点图和点的跟踪，照片不多时不到一秒；它拿了 CVPR 2025 的最佳论文。3D foundation models: DUSt3R (Wang et al. 2024) needs neither camera poses nor intrinsics: it takes two photos and directly regresses the 3D point behind every pixel (a point map), from which camera poses, depth and correspondences are all read off. MASt3R (Leroy et al. 2024) adds matching on top. VGGT (Wang et al. 2025) can take from one to hundreds of photos at once and directly gives camera parameters, depth maps, point maps and point tracks, in under a second when the photos are few; it won the CVPR 2025 best paper award.
  • 2025 年以后，这条线走得很快。π³（Wang 等 2026）去掉了"以第一张照片为准"的参考系，结果不再依赖照片的顺序；MapAnything（Keetha 等 2026）还能吃可有可无的内参、位姿和深度，直接给出带真实尺度的三维；Depth Anything 3（Lin 等 2026）用一个普通的 Transformer，从任意多张照片预测深度和每个像素的射线，报告的相机位姿精度比 VGGT 平均高 44%；D4RT（Zhang 等 2026，CVPR 2026 最佳论文）把这一套带到会动的场景，从一段视频给出深度、点在时空里的对应和相机；VGGT 的后继 VGGT-Ω（Wang 等 2026）把模型和数据继续放大，论文报告质量跟着可以预期地提高。Since 2025 this line has moved fast. π³ (Wang et al. 2026) drops the "first photo as reference" frame, so results no longer depend on the order of the photos; MapAnything (Keetha et al. 2026) can also take optional intrinsics, poses and depth and gives 3D at true scale directly; Depth Anything 3 (Lin et al. 2026) uses a plain transformer to predict depth and every pixel’s ray from any number of photos, reporting camera-pose accuracy 44% better than VGGT on average; D4RT (Zhang et al. 2026, CVPR 2026 best paper) brings the approach to moving scenes, giving depth, correspondences of points through space and time, and cameras from a single video; VGGT’s successor VGGT-Ω (Wang et al. 2026) scales model and data further, and the paper reports quality improving predictably.

在照片少、没有位姿、SfM 容易失败的时候，它们已经开始替代 SfM。InstantSplat（Fan 等 2024）拿这类模型的输出当 3DGS 的起点，几张没有位姿的照片也能训练。也可以不先求位姿，把相机位姿当成参数，和场景一起优化：BARF（Lin 等 2021）只靠颜色误差，由粗到细地打开位置编码；NoPe-NeRF（Bian 等 2023）再借单目深度这个先验，约束相邻两帧的相对位姿；CF-3DGS（Fu 等 2024）用单目深度给高斯定起点，沿着视频一帧帧往下接。With few photos and no poses, where SfM tends to fail, they are already starting to replace SfM. InstantSplat (Fan et al. 2024) uses such a model’s output to start 3DGS, so a few unposed photos can be trained on. One can also skip solving for poses first and optimise the camera poses as parameters together with the scene: BARF (Lin et al. 2021) relies on colour errors alone, switching the positional encoding on from coarse to fine; NoPe-NeRF (Bian et al. 2023) also borrows monocular depth as a prior to constrain the relative pose between neighbouring frames; CF-3DGS (Fu et al. 2024) uses monocular depth to place the starting Gaussians and works along the video frame by frame.

三、把没看到的编出来（生成）。背面没拍到，只能猜，而猜得像样，要靠见过很多东西的生成模型。Zero-1-to-3（Liu 等 2023）用扩散模型从一张照片生成别的角度的照片；CAT3D（Gao 等 2024）用多视图扩散模型，从一张或几张照片一次生成一批彼此一致的新视角，再交给第 ⑤、⑥ 步那样的方法去重建，一个场景最快一分钟。DreamFusion（Poole 等 2023）拿一个二维的"文字生成图片"扩散模型当评委（分数蒸馏采样，SDS），从一句话优化出一个 NeRF；Magic3D（Lin 等 2023）先粗后细，快了一倍、分辨率更高，最后给出带贴图的网格；DreamGaussian（Tang 等 2024）换成高斯，从一张照片两分钟左右就出一个带贴图的网格。TRELLIS（Xiang 等 2025）直接生成一种结构化的三维表示，可以解码成高斯、辐射场或网格。生成的东西看起来合理，但不保证是这个物体真实的样子。Three: inventing what was never seen (generation). If the back was never photographed, it can only be guessed, and guessing well takes a generative model that has seen a great many things. Zero-1-to-3 (Liu et al. 2023) uses a diffusion model to generate views from other angles out of one photo; CAT3D (Gao et al. 2024) uses a multi-view diffusion model to generate, from one or a few photos, a batch of mutually consistent new views at once, then hands them to methods like those of steps ⑤ and ⑥ for reconstruction, in as little as a minute per scene. DreamFusion (Poole et al. 2023) uses a 2D text-to-image diffusion model as a judge (score distillation sampling, SDS) to optimise a NeRF from a sentence; Magic3D (Lin et al. 2023) works coarse to fine, twice as fast and at higher resolution, ending with a textured mesh; DreamGaussian (Tang et al. 2024) switches to Gaussians and turns one photo into a textured mesh in about two minutes. TRELLIS (Xiang et al. 2025) generates a structured 3D representation directly, decodable into Gaussians, a radiance field or a mesh. What is generated looks plausible but is not guaranteed to be how this object really looks.

更早的时候，从单张照片学三维的方法直接输出体素（3D-R2N2，Choy 等 2016）、变形一个网格（Pixel2Mesh，Wang 等 2018），或者先检测物体再给出网格（Mesh R-CNN，Gkioxari 等 2019）。它们要三维的训练数据，类别也有限。今天的模型大多也要三维监督（合成的三维物体库，或用深度传感器、SfM 重建出的真实场景），只是数据多了几个数量级，场景的种类多得多；单目深度这类模型还能用海量没有标注的照片。Earlier, methods that learned 3D from a single photo output voxels directly (3D-R2N2, Choy et al. 2016), deformed a mesh (Pixel2Mesh, Wang et al. 2018), or detected objects first and then gave a mesh for each (Mesh R-CNN, Gkioxari et al. 2019). They needed 3D training data and covered few categories. Most of today’s models also need 3D supervision (libraries of synthetic 3D objects, or real scenes reconstructed with depth sensors or SfM), only with orders of magnitude more data and far more kinds of scene; models such as monocular depth networks can also use vast numbers of unlabelled photos.

这一类方法吃得少：一张或几张照片、没有位姿的照片，甚至一句话。它们比的是"像不像见过的东西"，一次前向或者生成，吐出点图、位姿、深度、高斯、网格。强在：快，照片少也行，不需要 SfM。弱在：准不准取决于训练数据，没见过的东西会猜错；细节和精度常常不如逐场景优化；生成的部分是编的。所以常见的组合是：先验先给一个快的初值，再用这一个场景的照片去优化。This family takes in little: one or a few photos, unposed photos, even a sentence. It compares against "what things seen before look like", works in one forward pass or by generation, and puts out point maps, poses, depth, Gaussians, meshes. Strengths: fast, works with few photos, needs no SfM. Weaknesses: accuracy depends on the training data, and unfamiliar things get guessed wrong; detail and precision often fall short of per-scene optimisation; the generated parts are invented. So a common combination is: let the prior give a fast starting point, then optimise with this scene’s photos.

总结Summary

回到开头：NeRF 和 3DGS 在做什么，算不算一种模态Back to the start: what NeRF and 3DGS do, and whether they are a modality

它们做的是同一件事：把场景存成一个辐射场（空间里每一点有多挡光、朝各个方向发什么颜色的光），从带位姿的照片里，用"画出来、和照片比、按梯度改"的办法把它求出来，再从新的角度画出来。不同的只是场存在哪、怎么画：They do the same thing: store the scene as a radiance field (how strongly every point in space blocks light and what colour it sends in each direction), solve for it from posed photos by "render, compare with the photos, follow the gradient", and then render it from new angles. "模态"的三层意思Three senses of "modality"

  • 传感器模态：测的是什么物理量。颜色（普通相机）、距离（深度相机、激光雷达）、两者都有（RGB-D）、热、声……在这个意义上，NeRF 和 3DGS 不是模态：它们吃的是普通照片，和 SfM、多视图立体一样（第 ③ 步）。Sensor modality: which physical quantity is measured. Colour (ordinary cameras), distance (depth cameras, LiDAR), both (RGB-D), heat, sound… In this sense NeRF and 3DGS are not a modality: they take in ordinary photos, just like SfM and multi-view stereo (step ③).
  • 表示：场景存成什么格式。点云、网格、体素、有符号距离、辐射场、高斯，都是"表示"。一种表示用得多了，就成了一种数据类型，又可以当别的模型的输入，在这个意义上像一种"模态"：PointNet（Qi 等 2017）直接吃点云；nf2vec（Zama Ramirez 等 2024）直接吃 NeRF 的网络权重，做检索和部件分割；3D 高斯有了 Khronos 批准的 glTF 扩展 KHR_gaussian_splatting（2026 年），成了一种可以交换的三维文件格式。说"3DGS 是一种新的三维数据格式"是对的。Representation: the format the scene is stored in. Point clouds, meshes, voxels, signed distances, radiance fields and Gaussians are all "representations". Once a representation is widely used it becomes a data type that can in turn be the input to other models, and in that sense it is like a "modality": PointNet (Qi et al. 2017) consumes point clouds directly; nf2vec (Zama Ramirez et al. 2024) consumes NeRF network weights directly, for retrieval and part segmentation; 3D Gaussians now have a Khronos-ratified glTF extension, KHR_gaussian_splatting (2026), making them an interchangeable 3D file format. Calling 3DGS "a new 3D data format" is correct.
  • 方法：怎么从数据求出这个表示。"NeRF"和"3DGS"这两个词，同时指一种表示和一套求它的办法（体渲染或泼溅，加上逐场景的梯度优化）。同一种表示可以有别的求法：pixelSplat 一次前向就给出高斯（第 ⑧ 步）；同一种求法也可以换表示：VolSDF 用体渲染去求有符号距离（第 ⑦ 步）。Method: how the representation is computed from data. The words "NeRF" and "3DGS" name both a representation and a way of solving for it (volume rendering or splatting, plus per-scene gradient optimisation). The same representation can be solved for in other ways: pixelSplat outputs Gaussians in one forward pass (step ⑧); the same solving method can also switch representations: VolSDF uses volume rendering to solve for a signed distance (step ⑦).

一句话：NeRF 和 3DGS 是"场景的表示 + 从照片求它的方法"，不是一种新的传感器模态；但它们求出来的场景（尤其是已经有标准文件格式的 3DGS），正在成为下游模型的一种输入。In a sentence: NeRF and 3DGS are "a scene representation plus a method for computing it from photos", not a new sensor modality; but the scenes they compute (above all 3DGS, which already has a standard file format) are becoming an input for downstream models.

机器人该用哪个Which one should a robot use?

  • 要抓东西，要几何：手上有深度相机，先用它，TSDF 融合又快又准（③）；要更细，用表面类的方法（NeuS、2DGS），或者给 3DGS 加深度监督（⑦）。For grasping, you need geometry: if you have a depth camera, use it first; TSDF fusion is fast and accurate (③); for finer detail, use the surface methods (NeuS, 2DGS), or add depth supervision to 3DGS (⑦).
  • 要从新角度看，要快：3DGS（⑥）。For new views, fast: 3DGS (⑥).
  • 要边走边知道自己在哪：基于特征点的视觉 SLAM（ORB-SLAM，②）能实时跑，可地图只是稀疏的点；要地图也能画出照片，用高斯 SLAM（SplaTAM、Gaussian Splatting SLAM，⑥），不过目前每帧还要零点几秒到两秒多，离实时还差一些。To know where you are while moving: feature-based visual SLAM (ORB-SLAM, ②) runs in real time, but its map is only sparse points; for a map that can also render photos, use Gaussian SLAM (SplaTAM, Gaussian Splatting SLAM, ⑥), though for now each frame still takes from a few tenths of a second to over two seconds, short of real time.
  • 照片很少，没有位姿：三维基础模型（DUSt3R、VGGT）先给出位姿和点，再用 3DGS 细化（⑧）。Very few photos, no poses: a 3D foundation model (DUSt3R, VGGT) gives poses and points first, then 3DGS refines (⑧).
  • 透明的东西：深度相机测不准，从照片训练的 NeRF 反而行（Dex-NeRF，⑦）。Transparent things: depth cameras cannot measure them well, while NeRF trained from photos can (Dex-NeRF, ⑦).
  • 要知道"那是什么"：把语言或视觉特征也存进场里，比如 LERF（Kerr 等 2023）、LangSplat（Qin 等 2024），F3RM（Shen 等 2023）用它按一句话去抓东西。To know "what that is": store language or visual features in the field too, as LERF (Kerr et al. 2023) and LangSplat (Qin et al. 2024) do; F3RM (Shen et al. 2023) uses this to grasp things on request.

其他几个问题A few other questions

  • NeRF 和 3DGS，谁更好？看你要什么。3DGS 训练快、渲染实时、好编辑，所以 2023 年以后用得最多；NeRF 类的方法存储小，连续的场更适合做抗锯齿和一些需要平滑的问题，质量上最好的 NeRF 类方法和 3DGS 不相上下。两者的几何都不精确，要几何得加约束（⑦）。Which is better, NeRF or 3DGS? It depends on what you need. 3DGS trains fast, renders in real time and is easy to edit, which is why it has been used most since 2023; NeRF-style methods are compact, and a continuous field suits anti-aliasing and problems that need smoothness; in quality, the best NeRF-style methods and 3DGS are on a par. Neither gives precise geometry; for geometry, add constraints (⑦).
  • NeRF 是在"学习"吗？原版 NeRF 只是对一个场景做优化，网络是存场的容器，不会推广到别的场景。真正"学"了先验、能推广的，是第 ⑧ 步的前馈模型和基础模型。Is NeRF "learning"? The original NeRF just optimises one scene; the network is a container for the field and does not generalise to other scenes. What truly "learns" a prior and generalises are the feed-forward and foundation models of step ⑧.
  • 为什么它们都要相机位姿？因为每个像素是一条射线，不知道相机在哪，就不知道射线在哪（①）。位姿错了，画出来的就对不上，所以要么用 SfM 先求，要么把位姿当参数一起优化（BARF），要么用基础模型直接给出（VGGT）。Why do they all need camera poses? Because each pixel is a ray, and without knowing where the camera is you do not know where the ray is (①). With wrong poses the renderings cannot match, so either SfM finds them first, or the poses are optimised as parameters too (BARF), or a foundation model gives them directly (VGGT).
  • 传统方法过时了吗？没有。COLMAP 还是大多数 NeRF 和 3DGS 的第一步；TSDF 融合还是机器人建图的主力；光束法平差和三角测量藏在几乎每个系统里。新方法改变的主要是"存成什么"和"怎么画"，以及让先验进来。Are the classical methods obsolete? No. COLMAP is still the first step of most NeRF and 3DGS work; TSDF fusion is still the workhorse of robot mapping; bundle adjustment and triangulation hide inside almost every system. What the new methods have changed is mainly "what it is stored as" and "how it is drawn", and letting priors in.

小测验Quiz

只有一张照片，为什么从它里面原则上求不出深度？Why is depth impossible, in principle, to recover from a single photo?

每个像素只记下沿一条射线射进来的光，射线上任何一点都落在同一个像素上（第 ① 步的式子里，xc 和 zc 同时放大，u 不变）。要补回深度，得换个位置再拍、自己发光去量，或者用见过的别的东西去猜。Each pixel records only the light arriving along one ray, and every point on that ray lands on the same pixel (in step ①’s formula, scaling xc and zc together leaves u unchanged). To get depth back you must photograph again from elsewhere, send out light and measure, or guess from other things you have seen.

三角测量时，两台相机隔多远最好？为什么不是越远越好？In triangulation, how far apart should the two cameras be? Why is farther not always better?

夹角小时，两条射线几乎平行，误差沿视线方向拉得很长；夹角越接近 90° 越准，这时纵深的误差和横着的一样小；过了 90° 又变差。何况隔得远，同一块地方在两张照片里的样子差得多（角度、遮挡、高光都变了），特征就难匹配，还可能被物体自己挡住。实际上要在"交得准"和"对得上"之间取舍（第 ①、② 步）。When the angle is small, the two rays are nearly parallel and the error is stretched far along the line of sight; the closer the angle gets to 90°, the more precise it is, and there the depth error is as small as the sideways one; past 90° it gets worse again. Besides, far apart, the same spot looks very different in the two photos (angle, occlusion and highlights all change), so features are hard to match and may even be hidden by the object itself. In practice you trade "crossing precisely" against "matching reliably" (steps ① and ②).

多视图立体在素色的釉面上为什么会失败？深度相机为什么不会？Why does multi-view stereo fail on plain glaze, and why does a depth camera not?

多视图立体靠颜色找对应：素色的地方沿射线挪一挪还是同样的颜色，颜色差的谷又宽又平，找不出一个明确的深度。深度相机自己打光、直接测距离，不需要找对应（结构光是自己打上花纹）（第 ②、③ 步）。Multi-view stereo finds correspondences through colour: on plain glaze, moving along the ray keeps the same colour, so the dip in colour difference is wide and flat and no definite depth emerges. A depth camera sends out its own light and measures distance directly, with no correspondences to find (structured light paints on its own texture) (steps ② and ③).

NeRF 和 3DGS 的渲染式子是同一个，为什么 3DGS 快那么多？NeRF and 3DGS share one rendering formula, so why is 3DGS so much faster?

NeRF 要沿每条射线采很多点，每个点都跑一遍网络，空的地方也算；3DGS 把每个高斯投到照片上一次，排序后只在高斯落到的像素上叠加，空的地方不花计算；这套分块排序、叠加的光栅化又很适合在显卡上并行（第 ⑤、⑥ 步）。NeRF samples many points along every ray and runs the network once per point, empty space included; 3DGS projects each Gaussian onto the photo once and, after sorting, blends only at the pixels the Gaussians cover, so empty space costs nothing; and this tiled sort-and-blend rasterisation suits parallel computing on the GPU well (steps ⑤ and ⑥).

NeRF 里，为什么密度只由位置决定，颜色却还要看方向？In NeRF, why does density depend only on position while colour also depends on direction?

一点"在不在"不该随你从哪看而变，这样几何在各个视角之间才一致；颜色却可以变，比如高光跟着相机走。要是密度也能随方向变，网络可以给每个视角编一套不同的几何，把照片背下来，新视角就错了（第 ⑤ 步）。Whether a point is "there" should not change with where you look from, which keeps the geometry consistent across views; colour may change, as a highlight follows the camera. If density could change with direction too, the network could invent a different geometry for each view and memorise the photos, and new views would be wrong (step ⑤).

一个训练好的 3DGS 场景，新视角的 PSNR 很高，为什么不能直接拿它的深度去抓把手？A trained 3DGS scene scores a high PSNR at new views. Why not use its depth directly to grasp the handle?

照片的损失只管"看起来对"。好几层半透明的高斯叠在表面后面，一样能凑出正确的颜色，深度却不对，甚至落在本来空着的地方。要准的几何，得加管几何的约束：深度监督、扁平的高斯、有符号距离（第 ⑦ 步）。A photo loss only cares about "looking right". Several layers of semi-transparent Gaussians stacked behind the surface can produce the right colour just as well while the depth is wrong, even landing where there is really empty space. For accurate geometry, add constraints that care about geometry: depth supervision, flat Gaussians, signed distance (step ⑦).

照片很少时，加一个学来的深度先验有用；照片很多时，为什么它反而碍事？With few photos, a learned depth prior helps; with many, why does it get in the way?

先验是用来补缺的信息的。照片少时，很多种几何都能凑对照片，先验帮它挑一个大致对的；照片多时，照片本身已经把几何钉住了，先验那几毫米的误差反而在跟照片较劲（第 ⑧ 步）。A prior fills in missing information. With few photos, many geometries fit them, and the prior helps pick one that is roughly right; with many photos, the photos themselves already pin the geometry down, and the prior’s few millimetres of error end up fighting them (step ⑧).

"3DGS 是一种新的模态吗？"怎么回答最准确？What is the most accurate answer to "Is 3DGS a new modality?"

不是新的传感器模态：它吃的是普通照片。它是一种场景的表示，加上一套从照片求它的办法；作为表示，它已经成了一种可以交换的数据格式（glTF 扩展），可以当别的模型的输入，在这个意义上可以说"像一种模态"（总结）。Not a new sensor modality: it takes in ordinary photos. It is a scene representation plus a way of computing it from photos; as a representation it has become an interchangeable data format (a glTF extension) that can be the input to other models, and in that sense it can be called "like a modality" (summary).
