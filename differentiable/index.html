机器学习 · 可微Machine learning · Differentiability

每个旋钮往哪拧Which Way to Turn Each Knob

机器人把球扔向两米外的筐，远了 25 厘米。下一次该扔得慢一点，可慢多少？要是能调的不止一个旋钮，而是一个神经网络里的几百万个权重、一个三维场景里几百万个小色斑的位置和颜色呢？"可微"让这件事有了办法：对每一个旋钮，都能算出该往哪拧、大约拧多少；再加上反向传播，几百万个旋钮也能一起算。神经网络能训练，可微渲染能从照片还原三维场景，高斯泼溅能从照片重建场景、再实时渲染出来，可微仿真器能帮机器人规划，靠的都是它。可是在不同的地方说"可微"，图的东西并不完全一样。这一篇的前七步只用一个任务：机器人扔球进筐（第 0 步先把它说清楚）。先从一个旋钮讲起，再看为什么几百万个旋钮也只要多算一遍；然后看梯度在哪些地方会失灵：撞上筐沿、只看得到画面、要做离散的选择、面对的是真实世界，以及人们怎样把这些地方变得可微，或者绕过去。最后两步换个角度：看机器人里怎样用可微，再把几十种"可微 X"放在一起，回答开头的问题：在不同的地方说"可微"，动机和好处一样吗？A robot throws a ball towards a bin two metres away, and it goes 25 cm too far. Next time it should throw a little slower, but how much slower? And what if there is not just one knob to adjust, but millions of weights in a neural network, or the positions and colours of millions of small blobs in a 3D scene? Being "differentiable" is what makes this possible: for every knob, you can compute which way to turn it and roughly by how much; add backpropagation, and this can be done for millions of knobs at once. It is what lets neural networks be trained, differentiable rendering recover 3D scenes from photos, Gaussian splatting reconstruct a scene from photos and then render it in real time, and differentiable simulators help robots plan. But when people say "differentiable" in different places, they are not after quite the same thing. In its first seven steps, this page uses a single task: a robot throwing a ball into a bin (step 0 sets it out first). It starts with one knob, then shows why even millions of knobs need only one extra pass of computation. Then it looks at where the gradient breaks down: when the ball hits the rim, when all you can see is an image, when a discrete choice has to be made, and when you are dealing with the real world; and at how people make these cases differentiable, or get around them. The last two steps take a different angle: they look at how robots use differentiability, then put dozens of kinds of "differentiable X" side by side to answer the opening question: when people say "differentiable" in different places, are the motivations and the benefits the same?

一个具体的任务：把球扔进筐里A concrete task: throwing a ball into a bin

前七步只用一个任务，先把它说清楚。机械臂要把一个球扔进两米外的筐里。筐放在地上，口宽 30 cm、高 30 cm；球的半径是 3.5 cm。机械臂把手臂从身后往下、再往前上方一甩，在离地 1.0 m 的地方松手，这一刻叫出手；球带着出手那一刻的速度飞出去。这个速度有两个要素：多快，叫出手速度 v（单位 m/s，米每秒）；朝哪个方向，叫仰角 α，是出手方向和水平方向的夹角。出手以后就没有机器人什么事了，只有重力在管它，球沿着一条抛物线飞，落下来。空气阻力先不管，第 ⑦ 步再加上。The first seven steps use just one task, so here it is in detail first. A robot arm has to throw a ball into a bin two metres away. The bin stands on the floor; its opening is 30 cm wide, and it is 30 cm high. The ball’s radius is 3.5 cm. The arm swings down from behind and then forward and up, and lets go 1.0 m above the floor; this moment is called the release. The ball flies off with the velocity it had at the moment of release. That velocity has two parts: how fast, called the release speed v (in m/s, metres per second), and in which direction, called the angle α, measured between the direction of release and the horizontal. After release the robot has no more say: only gravity acts on the ball, which flies along a parabola and comes back down. Air resistance is left out for now and added in step ⑦.

这一扔远了 25 cm。下一次怎么办？显然要扔得慢一点，或者换个角度。可是慢多少？慢 0.1 m/s 还是 0.5 m/s？这就是整篇要回答的问题：差了一点，每个旋钮该往哪拧、拧多少？"旋钮"指一切能调、又会影响结果的数。这里是出手速度和仰角；往后还会有神经网络里成千上万的权重、三维场景里每个小色斑的位置和颜色、仿真器里的摩擦系数、机械臂每个关节的转角、机器人走过的每一个位置和朝向。This throw went 25 cm too far. What should happen next time? Clearly the throw should be a little slower, or at a different angle. But how much slower? By 0.1 m/s or by 0.5 m/s? This is the question the whole page sets out to answer: when we miss by a bit, which way should each knob turn, and by how much? A "knob" is any number that can be adjusted and that affects the result. Here, the knobs are the release speed and the angle; later there will be thousands of weights in a neural network, the position and colour of every small blob in a 3D scene, the friction coefficients in a simulator, the angle of every joint of a robot arm, and the position and heading of a robot at every point along its path.

为什么是扔？扔是机器人"把东西送到够不着的地方"的办法，也是一个研究得很多的任务：TossingBot 把杂乱的小物件抓起来，直接扔进机械臂够不到的箱子里（Zeng 等 2019，第 ⑦ 步还会讲它）。对讲"可微"来说，扔的好处是物理清楚：出手以后只剩重力，落点完全由出手的速度和角度决定，一个式子就能算出来。这让我们能把"可微"的每一层看得清清楚楚；等球撞上筐沿（第 ④ 步），物理就不那么规矩了。Why throwing? Throwing is how a robot "gets things to places it cannot reach", and it is a much-studied task: TossingBot picks up small objects from a cluttered pile and throws them straight into boxes outside the arm’s reach (Zeng et al. 2019; step ⑦ comes back to it). For explaining "differentiable", throwing has the advantage that the physics is clear: after release only gravity is left, so the landing point is fully determined by the release speed and angle, and a single formula gives it. This lets us see every layer of "differentiable" clearly; once the ball hits the rim (step ④), the physics stops being so well behaved.

这个式子是这样来的：水平方向没有力，球以不变的水平速度往前走；竖直方向从向上的速度出发，被重力一点点拉回来。落点就是水平速度乘以飞行时间。Here is where that formula comes from: horizontally there is no force, so the ball keeps moving forward at the same horizontal speed; vertically it starts with an upward speed and is gradually pulled back by gravity. The landing point is simply the horizontal speed times the flight time.

x = v cos α · tt = (v sin α + √(v² sin²α + 2gh)) / g

落点等于水平速度乘以飞行时间；飞行时间是球心从出手点升上去、再落回筐口高度所用的时间。The landing point equals the horizontal speed times the flight time; the flight time is how long the ball’s centre takes to rise from the release point and come back down to rim height.

以筐口高度为 0，球心的高度是 h + v sin α · t − ½ g t²。让它等于 0，解这个一元二次方程，取正的那个根，就是第二行。出手点越高（h 越大），球往下落时多飞一段，t 越长。Taking the rim height as 0, the height of the ball’s centre is h + v sin α · t − ½ g t². Set it equal to 0, solve this quadratic equation and take the positive root: that is the second line. The higher the release point (the larger h), the further the ball flies on its way down, and the longer t is.

什么是可微What it means to be differentiable

落点 x 随出手速度 v 变：v 大一点，x 远一点。把这个关系画成一条曲线，在曲线上任挑一点，放大，再放大。如果越放大越像一条直线，就说 x 在这一点可微。那条直线的斜率叫导数，记作 x′(v) 或 dx/dv：v 变一点点，x 变它的多少倍。The landing point x changes with the release speed v: raise v a little and x moves a little further. Draw this relationship as a curve, pick any point on it, and zoom in, then zoom in again. If it looks more and more like a straight line as you zoom in, x is said to be differentiable at that point. The slope of that line is called the derivative, written x′(v) or dx/dv: when v changes a tiny bit, x changes by this many times as much.

x(v + δ) ≈ x(v) + x′(v) · δ

速度从 v 变到 v + δ，落点大约变 x′(v) 乘以 δ；δ 越小，误差缩得比 δ 本身还快（误差 ÷ δ 趋于 0），这就是"可微"严格的意思。When the speed changes from v to v + δ, the landing point changes by about x′(v) times δ; as δ gets smaller, the error shrinks even faster than δ itself (error ÷ δ tends to 0), and that is the strict meaning of "differentiable".

有了这条直线，就不用真的再扔一次，也能知道改了速度以后会怎样：直线告诉你改多少会得到多少。这正好是"往哪拧、拧多少"需要的东西。只要放大以后像直线就够了，不需要整条曲线都是直线。只有一个旋钮时，"可微"和"可导"说的是同一件事。With this line, you can tell what changing the speed will do without actually throwing again: the line tells you how much the result changes for a given change. That is exactly what "which way to turn, and by how much" needs. The curve only has to look like a line once you zoom in; it does not have to be straight all the way along. With only one knob, "differentiable" and "having a derivative" mean the same thing.

可微只说"这一点附近像直线"，是局部的。走远了，直线就不准了；碰到"跳一下"的地方，比如球刚好擦过筐沿，放大多少倍都不像直线，那里就不可微（第 ④ 步）。Being differentiable only means "near this point it looks like a straight line"; it is local. Go further away and the line is no longer accurate. Where something "jumps", for example when the ball just grazes the rim, no amount of zooming makes it look like a straight line, and it is not differentiable there (step ④).

机器学习里说一个东西"可微"，意思通常更具体：从旋钮到最后那个"差多少"的数，整条计算链上的每一步都能求导，于是每个旋钮的导数都能自动算出来。算出来的导数有没有用，即方向对不对、大小靠不靠得住，是另一回事。为什么前一件事这么重要，后一件事又常常不成立，就是这一篇的内容。下面一步一步看：In machine learning, calling something "differentiable" usually means something more specific: every step in the chain of computation, from the knobs to that final "how much did it miss by" number, can be differentiated, so the derivative for every knob can be computed automatically. Whether the computed derivatives are any use, that is, whether they point the right way and whether their size can be trusted, is another matter. 第 ① 步 · 一个旋钮Step ① · One knob

一个旋钮：导数就是灵敏度One knob: the derivative is the sensitivity

先只调一个旋钮：仰角固定在 45°，只改出手速度。不懂物理也有办法：扔一次，远了就慢一点，近了就快一点，再扔。可每次改多少只能猜。比较稳的猜法是"二分"：先找到一个太近的速度和一个太远的速度，每次扔它们正中间的速度，按结果扔掉一半区间。已知 3.6 m/s 太近、4.1 m/s 太远，从这个区间出发，要把落点压到离筐中心 1 cm 以内，还得再扔 4 次。Start by adjusting just one knob: the angle is fixed at 45° and only the release speed changes. Even without knowing any physics, there is a way: throw once; if it is too far, go a little slower; if it is too short, a little faster; then throw again. But how much to change each time is guesswork. A safer way to guess is "bisection": first find one speed that is too short and one that is too far, then each time throw at the speed exactly halfway between them and, depending on the result, discard half of the interval. Here 3.6 m/s is known to be too short and 4.1 m/s too far; starting from this interval, it still takes another 4 throws to get the landing point within 1 cm of the bin centre.

如果知道导数，也就是灵敏度，一次就够了。远了 25 cm，速度每快 1 m/s 落点远 0.89 m，那就该慢 0.25 ÷ 0.89 ≈ 0.28 m/s。导数从哪来？有两个办法：If you know the derivative, that is, the sensitivity, one throw is enough. The ball went 25 cm too far, and every 1 m/s faster puts the landing point 0.89 m further, so the speed should come down by 0.25 ÷ 0.89 ≈ 0.28 m/s. Where does the derivative come from? There are two ways:

  • 量：在 v 和稍大一点的 v + δ 各扔一次，用落点之差除以 δ。这叫有限差分。代价是每量一次要多扔一次；真实的扔还有噪声，δ 取得太小，噪声会盖过差别，取得太大，量出来的又不是这一点的斜率。Measure: throw once at v and once at a slightly larger v + δ, and divide the difference in landing points by δ. This is called finite differences. The cost is one extra throw for every measurement. Real throws are also noisy: if δ is too small, the noise swamps the difference; if it is too large, what you measure is no longer the slope at this point.
  • 算：落点有公式，直接对公式求导，一次都不用多扔。"可微"给了第二条路：算落点的那个过程，可以顺便把导数也算出来。第 ② 步会看到它是怎么算的。Compute: there is a formula for the landing point, so differentiate the formula directly, without a single extra throw. Being "differentiable" opens up this second route: the process that computes the landing point can compute the derivative along the way too. Step ② shows how it is done.
vk+1 = vk − ek / x′(vk)

下一次的速度 = 这一次的速度 − 这一次差了多少 ÷ 灵敏度。Next throw’s speed = this throw’s speed − this throw’s miss ÷ the sensitivity.

在 vk 附近，落点近似是一条直线：x ≈ x(vk) + x′(vk)·(v − vk)。让它等于筐中心 D，解出来的 v 就是这个式子。画在图上，就是沿切线走到筐中心那条横线上。这叫牛顿法。Near vk, the landing point is approximately a straight line: x ≈ x(vk) + x′(vk)·(v − vk). Setting it equal to the bin centre D and solving for v gives exactly this formula. On the graph, it means following the tangent until it meets the horizontal line at the bin centre. This is called Newton’s method.

牛顿法要"解出交点"，一个旋钮、一个目标时好办。旋钮一多，"正好落在 D"的组合就不止一个（下一步会看到是一整条线），解交点得先决定挑哪个；要同时满足的目标一多（比如第 ③ 步里，同一组权重要应付很多个不同的距离），又往往没有一组旋钮能让每一次都正好落在筐中心。所以换一个更通用的说法：先把"差多少"变成一个越小越好的数，再顺着它下坡。Newton’s method has to "solve for the crossing point", which is easy with one knob and one target. With more knobs, more than one combination "lands exactly on D" (the next step shows that they form a whole line), so to solve for the crossing point you first have to decide which one to pick. With more targets to meet at once (in step ③, for example, one set of weights has to cope with many different distances), there is often no single setting of the knobs that puts every throw exactly at the bin centre. So the next idea is more general: first turn "how much it missed by" into a number where smaller is better, then follow it downhill.

L = ½ e²dL/dv = e · x′(v)vk+1 = vk − η · dL/dv

损失是差的平方的一半；损失对速度的导数，等于"差了多少"乘以灵敏度；下一次的速度，顺着这个导数的反方向走一步。The loss is half the square of the miss; the derivative of the loss with respect to speed is "how much it missed by" times the sensitivity; the next speed is one step in the opposite direction to this derivative.

为什么用平方：e 有正有负，我们要的是它接近 0；平方把"远了"和"近了"都变成正数，最小值正好在 e = 0；前面的 ½ 只是为了求导以后干净。中间那个式子是第 ② 步要讲的链式法则最简单的样子：L 通过 e 依赖 x，x 又依赖 v，所以把两段的导数乘起来。Why the square: e can be positive or negative, and what we want is for it to be close to 0. Squaring makes both "too far" and "too short" positive, with the minimum exactly at e = 0; the ½ in front is only there so that the derivative comes out clean. The middle formula is the simplest case of the chain rule, which step ② covers: L depends on x through e, and x in turn depends on v, so the derivatives of the two stages are multiplied together.

这个"顺着导数下坡"的办法叫梯度下降。后面大多数方法，从训练神经网络到拟合高斯泼溅，骨架都是它，或者它的近亲（牛顿法、高斯–牛顿法），区别只在导数从哪来、靠不靠得住。This "follow the derivative downhill" approach is called gradient descent. Most of the methods later on, from training neural networks to fitting a scene with Gaussian splatting, are built on it or on one of its close relatives (Newton’s method, the Gauss–Newton method); they differ only in where the derivative comes from and whether it can be trusted.

两件事要记住。第一，导数同时给出方向和大小：符号说往哪拧，大小说这里有多灵敏。第二，它只在附近准，所以一般要一步一步走，每走一步重新算一次。仰角那条曲线在 36.5° 附近最高：那是这个速度下扔得最远的角度（出手点比筐口高 0.7 m，球往下落时还能多飞一段，所以它比平地上的 45° 低），在那里稍微改一点角度，落点几乎不变，导数接近 0，按导数一步会迈到很远的地方去。导数小，不等于该大步走；实际中人们为此给步子设上限（比如把太长的梯度截短，叫梯度裁剪），或者换别的办法。Two things to remember. First, the derivative gives both a direction and a size: the sign says which way to turn, and the size says how sensitive things are here. Second, it is only accurate nearby, so you usually have to go step by step, recomputing it after every step. The angle curve is highest near 36.5°: that is the angle that throws furthest at this speed (the release point is 0.7 m higher than the rim, so the ball can fly an extra stretch on its way down; that is why the angle is lower than the 45° you would get on level ground). 还有代价的问题。量一个导数，要多扔一次；只有一个旋钮时，这不算什么。旋钮一多，两件事会变：导数变成一个方向，叫梯度；量导数的代价也跟着旋钮的个数涨。下一步先看前一件，再看后一件。Then there is the question of cost. Measuring a derivative takes one extra throw; with only one knob, that is no big deal. With many knobs, two things change: the derivative becomes a direction, called the gradient, and the cost of measuring derivatives grows with the number of knobs. The next step looks at the first of these, then at the second.

第 ② 步 · 旋钮一多Step ② · Many knobs

旋钮一多：反向传播一遍算出所有的导数Many knobs: one pass of backpropagation gives all the derivatives

现在两个旋钮一起调：出手速度 v 和仰角 α。每个旋钮各有自己的导数：只动这一个、另一个不动时，损失变多少。这叫偏导数，写作 ∂L/∂v 和 ∂L/∂α（∂ 读作"偏"）。把它们排在一起，就是梯度。Now both knobs are turned together: the release speed v and the angle α. Each knob has its own derivative: how much the loss changes when you move only that knob and leave the other alone. This is called a partial derivative, written ∂L/∂v and ∂L/∂α (∂ is read as "partial"). Line them up and you have the gradient.

∇L = (∂L/∂v, ∂L/∂α)θk+1 = θk − η · ∇L(θk)

梯度是两个偏导数拼成的箭头；每一步，所有旋钮一起顺着这个箭头的反方向走 η 倍。The gradient is an arrow made of the two partial derivatives; at each step, all the knobs move together, by η times this arrow, in the opposite direction.

把每一种（v, α）的组合看成地图上的一点，损失是这一点的海拔，梯度就是这里最陡的上坡方向，它总是和等高线垂直。往反方向走，下降得最快。旋钮再多也一样，只是地图从两维变成了很多维，箭头有很多个分量。两个旋钮时，"可微"就是放大以后像一块斜面；梯度指向这块斜面最陡的方向。光有两个偏导数还不够，得朝每个方向放大都像这块斜面。Think of each (v, α) combination as a point on a map, and the loss as the altitude at that point. The gradient is then the steepest uphill direction there, and it is always perpendicular to the contour lines. Going the opposite way is the fastest way down. With more knobs it is just the same, except that the map has many dimensions instead of two, and the arrow has many components. With two knobs, "differentiable" means that the surface looks like a tilted plane once you zoom in; the gradient points in the steepest direction of this plane. Having the two partial derivatives is not enough on its own: zoomed in, the surface has to look like this plane in every direction.

The partial derivative for the speed is positive: throw a little slower. The one for the angle is negative: raise the angle a little. Why does raising the angle bring the ball closer? Because 45° is already higher than "the angle that throws furthest": raise it more and the ball flies higher and travels less far forward.

梯度说的是"最陡"，这和旋钮用什么单位有关：角度用度还是用弧度，箭头的方向就不一样。所以实际训练时，常让每个旋钮自己调步长（比如 Adam 优化器），或者先把旋钮缩放到差不多的大小。The gradient gives the "steepest" direction, and what is steepest depends on the units the knobs are measured in: whether the angle is in degrees or in radians changes the direction of the arrow. So in real training, each knob is often left to adjust its own step size (the Adam optimiser does this, for example), or the knobs are first rescaled to roughly similar sizes.

导数怎么算：一遍一遍试太贵How to compute the derivatives: trying run after run is too expensive

最直接的办法还是第 ① 步的"量"：每个旋钮单独改一点，各算一遍落点。两个旋钮要算 3 遍（原样一遍，每个旋钮各改一遍）。旋钮有 N 个，就要算 N + 1 遍。在仿真里"算一遍"就是扔一次。N = 2 时这不算什么；可第 ③ 步的小网络有 321 个旋钮，每改一次就要扔 322 次；大模型有几千亿个旋钮，每改一次就要把整个模型跑几千亿遍，根本不可能。The most direct way is still the "measuring" of step ①: change each knob a little on its own and compute the landing point again for each. Two knobs take 3 passes (one as it is, plus one with each knob changed). N knobs take N + 1 passes. In a simulation, "one pass" means one throw. For N = 2 this is nothing much; but the small network in step ③ has 321 knobs, so every update takes 322 throws, and a large model has hundreds of billions of knobs, so every update would mean running the whole model hundreds of billions of times. That is simply impossible.

出路藏在"计算是一串小步骤"这件事里。算落点的那个式子，其实是几步简单的计算：先算水平速度和竖直速度，再算球落回筐口高度要多久，再乘出落点，最后减去 D、平方。每一小步都很简单，它们各自的导数（叫局部导数）人人会算。整串的导数，等于把局部导数沿路乘起来：The way out lies in the fact that "a computation is a chain of small steps". The formula for the landing point is really a few simple calculations: first the horizontal and vertical speeds, then how long the ball takes to come back down to rim height, then multiply to get the landing point, and finally subtract D and square. Each small step is simple, and anyone can work out the derivative of each one (called its local derivative). The derivative of the whole chain is the local derivatives multiplied together along the way:

∂L/∂v = ∂L/∂x · ∂x/∂v∂x/∂v = ∂x/∂vx · ∂vx/∂v + ∂x/∂t · ∂t/∂vy · ∂vy/∂v

速度通过两条路影响落点：一条经过水平速度（扔得快，往前走得也快），一条经过竖直速度（往上扔得快，在空中待得久）。每条路上把每一小步的导数乘起来，两条路的再加起来。The speed affects the landing point along two paths: one through the horizontal speed (throw faster and the ball also moves forward faster) and one through the vertical speed (throw upward faster and it stays in the air longer). Multiply the derivatives of the small steps along each path, then add the two paths together.

这就是链式法则：一串计算的导数，等于每一步的导数相乘；一个量有几条路影响结果，就把几条路的加起来。它把"对整个复杂过程求导"拆成了"对每一小步求导"。This is the chain rule: the derivative of a chain of calculations is the product of the derivatives of its steps; if a quantity affects the result along several paths, add the paths together. It breaks "differentiating the whole complicated process" down into "differentiating each small step".

一般的计算里，一步常有好几个输入、好几个输出，局部导数是一张表，整串导数是这些表连乘。结果不管先乘哪头都一样；可从旋钮那头乘，每个旋钮要单独乘一遍，从损失那头乘，因为损失只有一个数，乘一遍就够。下面的交互里，两个旋钮已经能看出这个差别：正向模式要算两遍，反向只要一遍。In a general computation, a step often has several inputs and several outputs; its local derivative is a table, and the derivative of the whole chain is all these tables multiplied together. The result is the same whichever end you start multiplying from. But if you start from the knob end, you have to multiply through once for every knob; if you start from the loss end, once is enough, because the loss is a single number. 沿着链往下乘，有两种顺序：There are two orders in which to multiply along the chain:

  • 正向模式：从一个旋钮出发，顺着计算的方向，把"它变一点，这一格变多少"和值一起往前带，到终点就得到损失对这一个旋钮的导数。一遍只管一个旋钮，N 个旋钮要 N 遍。Forward mode: start from one knob and follow the direction of the computation, carrying "how much this box changes when the knob changes a little" forward along with the values; at the end you have the derivative of the loss with respect to that one knob. Each pass handles only one knob, so N knobs need N passes.
  • 反向模式：先正向把每一格的值算出来存好；再从损失出发往回走，把"这一格变一点，损失变多少"一格一格往回传。传到起点时，所有旋钮的导数一下子全有了。不管有多少个旋钮，都只要往回走一遍。Reverse mode: first go forward, computing and storing the value in every box; then start from the loss and go back, passing "how much the loss changes when this box changes a little" back box by box. When it reaches the start, you have the derivatives for all the knobs at once. However many knobs there are, it takes only one pass back.

反向模式用在神经网络上，就叫反向传播（Rumelhart、Hinton、Williams 1986）；反向模式本身更早，常被追溯到 Linnainmaa 1970 年的硕士论文（1976 年发表成期刊论文；同一时期也有人在别的领域独立用过）；正向模式还要早，见 Wengert 1964。Reverse mode applied to neural networks is called backpropagation (Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams 1986). Reverse mode itself is older: it is often traced back to Linnainmaa’s 1970 master’s thesis (published as a journal paper in 1976; others used it independently in other fields around the same time). Forward mode is older still: see Wengert 1964.

这就是"可微"值钱的地方：反向模式求出所有 N 个导数的代价，和 N 无关，只是算一遍损失的一个小倍数。这个结论常被叫作"梯度很便宜"原则：对由加减乘除组成的计算，所有偏导数连同函数值一起算出来，计算量不超过只算函数值的 4 倍左右（只数乘除的话不超过 3 倍；Baur & Strassen 1983，Griewank & Walther 2008），实际中一般是 2 到 3 倍。代价在内存：反向要用到正向时的每一个中间值，得先存着。训练大网络时，这些中间值（激活）常常占去一大块显存，和参数、梯度、优化器的状态一起把显存占满；存不下就少存一些，用到时再重算一遍（这叫检查点），用时间换空间。This is where "differentiable" pays off: the cost of getting all N derivatives in reverse mode does not depend on N; it is only a small multiple of computing the loss once. This result is often called the "cheap gradient" principle: for a computation made of additions, subtractions, multiplications and divisions, computing all the partial derivatives together with the function value takes no more than about 4 times the work of computing the function value alone (no more than 3 times if you count only multiplications and divisions; Baur & Strassen 1983, Griewank & Walther 2008), and in practice it is usually 2 to 3 times. The price is memory: the reverse pass needs every intermediate value from the forward pass, so they have to be stored. When training large networks, these intermediate values (the activations) often take up a large share of the GPU memory, filling it up together with the parameters, the gradients and the optimiser state. If they do not all fit, store fewer of them and recompute the rest when they are needed (this is called checkpointing), trading time for space.

几点说明。第一，这里算出来的导数是精确的（到计算机的舍入误差为止），不是用"改一点点"去近似；它也不是把整个公式写出来再推导（那样式子会越推越长），而是一边算值、一边按每一小步的规则算导数，这叫自动微分（AD）。今天的 PyTorch、JAX 都是自动微分的工具（Baydin 等 2018 是一篇好的综述）。第二，它对程序也成立：有循环、有判断的程序，自动微分对"这一次实际走过的那条路"求导。第三，只要一个零件的每一步都能求导，它就能接进这条链，和别的零件一起，被同一个损失训练。这就是人们到处想把东西"做成可微"的原因：可微的零件可以拼起来，端到端地一起训练。A few remarks. First, the derivatives computed here are exact (up to the computer’s rounding error), not approximated by "changing things a tiny bit". Nor are they found by writing out the whole formula and then differentiating it (the expressions would grow longer and longer); instead, the derivatives are computed alongside the values, following the rule for each small step. This is called automatic differentiation (AD). Today’s PyTorch and JAX are both automatic differentiation tools (Baydin et al. 2018 is a good survey). Second, it works for programs too: for a program with loops and branches, automatic differentiation differentiates "the path actually taken this time". Third, as long as every step of a component can be differentiated, the component can be plugged into this chain and trained by the same loss together with the other components. That is why people everywhere want to "make things differentiable": differentiable components can be put together and trained end to end.

反过来，旋钮少的时候，正向模式、甚至有限差分都完全够用；只有一个损失、很多旋钮，才是反向模式的主场，而训练神经网络正是这种情况。On the other hand, when there are only a few knobs, forward mode or even finite differences are perfectly good enough. Reverse mode comes into its own only with one loss and many knobs, and training a neural network is exactly that case.

神经网络：把网络和物理串成一条链Neural networks: linking the network and the physics into one chain

现在筐不固定了：每次扔之前，它被放在 1.4 m 到 2.6 m 之间的一个随机位置。机器人需要一条"规则"（强化学习里叫策略：看到什么，就做什么）：给它筐的距离 D，它给出该用的出手速度 v（仰角还是 45°）。有公式时，当然可以每次都按第 ① 步现解；可在真实的机器人上，输入往往不是一个干净的 D，而是一张相机画面，这条规则只能学出来。这里让输入就是 D，好让学得对不对一眼就能看出来。Now the bin no longer stays put: before each throw, it is placed at a random position between 1.4 m and 2.6 m. The robot needs a "rule" (in reinforcement learning this is called a policy: what it sees decides what it does): give it the bin distance D, and it gives back the release speed v to use (the angle stays at 45°). When there is a formula, you can of course solve for v on the spot each time, as in step ①; but on a real robot the input is usually not a clean D but a camera image, and the rule can only be learned. Here the input is simply D, so that you can see at a glance whether it has been learned correctly.

让一个小神经网络来当这条规则：输入 D，经过两层各 16 个神经元（每个神经元把输入乘上权重、加起来、再加一个偏置，然后过一个平滑的函数 tanh，它把任意数压到 −1 到 1 之间），输出 v。网络里一共有 321 个可调的数（288 个权重和 33 个偏置，下面统称权重）：它们就是 321 个旋钮。训练，就是把这 321 个旋钮调到"对每个 D，扔出去都正好落在 D"。Let a small neural network be this rule: D goes in, passes through two layers with 16 neurons each (every neuron multiplies its inputs by weights, sums them, adds a bias and then passes the result through a smooth function, tanh, which squeezes any number into the range between −1 and 1), and v comes out. The network has 321 adjustable numbers in all (288 weights and 33 biases, from here on all simply called weights): these are the 321 knobs. Training means turning these 321 knobs until "for every D, the throw lands exactly at D".

网络本身也是一串小计算：乘、加、tanh，每一步都能求导。把它和物理接起来，从 D 到网络、到速度、到落点、到损失，整条链都可微。于是第 ② 步的反向模式可以一路往回传：从损失传到落点，穿过物理传到速度，再穿过网络传到每一个权重。扔一次（在仿真里），321 个导数就都有了。The network itself is also a chain of small calculations: multiply, add, tanh, and every step can be differentiated. Connect it to the physics, and the whole chain, from D through the network to the speed, the landing point and the loss, is differentiable. So the reverse mode of step ② can pass all the way back: from the loss to the landing point, through the physics to the speed, and then through the network to every weight. One throw (in simulation) gives all 321 derivatives.

筐在 2.0 m，网络给出 4.1 m/s，远了 0.249 m，x′ = 0.887，于是传回网络的信号是"损失对输出速度的导数是 0.249 × 0.887 = 0.221，往小里调"；网络再把它分给每个权重。The bin is at 2.0 m and the network gives 4.1 m/s: 0.249 m too far, with x′ = 0.887. So the signal passed back to the network is "the derivative of the loss with respect to the output speed is 0.249 × 0.887 = 0.221: make it smaller"; the network then shares this out among its weights.

链上每一环都得能求导。物理那一环要是不知道、或者求不了导，链就在 x′(v) 那里断了。Every link in the chain has to be differentiable. If the physics link is unknown, or cannot be differentiated, the chain breaks at x′(v).

链断在物理这一环时，标准的办法是试错学习，也就是强化学习的做法：每次扔，都给网络输出的速度加一点随机的抖动，看结果好不好；比平均好的那几次，抖动往哪边，就把网络的输出往哪边挪。网络那一段照样用反向传播，只是传给网络的那个信号，"往快里调还是往慢里调、调多少"，不再穿过物理算出来，而是从一次次随机抖动的结果里估出来：估的是"带着抖动扔，平均奖励"随速度变化的斜率（这个办法叫 REINFORCE，第 ⑥ 步讲它为什么成立）。这里的奖励是给每次扔打的分，怎么打又分两种：知道差了多少厘米，或者只知道进没进。When the chain breaks at the physics link, the standard approach is trial-and-error learning, which is what reinforcement learning does: on every throw, add a little random jitter to the speed the network outputs and see whether the result is good; for the throws that did better than average, move the network’s output in the direction their jitter went. The network part still uses backpropagation; it is just that the signal passed to the network, "faster or slower, and by how much", is no longer computed through the physics but estimated from the results of many randomly jittered throws. What is estimated is the slope of "the average reward when throwing with jitter" with respect to the speed (this method is called REINFORCE; step ⑥ explains why it works). The reward here is the score given to each throw, and there are two ways of scoring: you know how many centimetres it missed by, or you only know whether it went in.

三种都学会了大概：几百次以后，大部分距离都能进筐。差别在于"细不细"：穿过物理求导的那条线一直往下走，扔到一两万次时平均只差几毫米；两种试错的到后来停在几厘米上下抖。原因是它们的梯度是从带噪声的试探里估出来的，每一步都很散（方差大），步长不变时，就在最好的位置附近抖；想更细，就得把步子调小、扔得更多。All three learn roughly what to do: after a few hundred throws, most distances go in. The difference is "how fine" they get: the line for the gradient through the physics keeps going down, and by 10 000 to 20 000 throws its average miss is only a few millimetres; the two trial-and-error lines level off at a few centimetres and wobble around there. The reason is that their gradients are estimated from noisy tries, so their steps scatter widely (high variance), and with a fixed step size they keep wobbling around the best setting; to get finer, you have to make the steps smaller and throw more.

要说公道话：这里每次只需要定一个数（速度），试错学习吃的亏还不大。真实的机器人常常要一次定很多个数，比如一整段挥臂动作里每个关节在每个时刻的力矩，几十上百个数一起抖，试错估出来的梯度会越来越散；而反向传播求导的代价，不随这些数的个数变。这正是"可微仿真器"在机器人学习里的主要卖点：在动力学光滑的任务上，穿过仿真器求导，往往比强化学习少用很多样本、快很多。比如 SHAC（短视界演员–评论家，Xu 等 2022）在仿真里一个由 152 条肌肉–肌腱驱动的人形模型上，训练到同样的成绩，比最常用的强化学习算法之一 PPO（近端策略优化）快 17 倍以上（按实际花的时间算）。To be fair: here only one number (the speed) has to be chosen each time, so trial-and-error learning is not at much of a disadvantage. A real robot often has to choose many numbers at once, such as the torque at every joint at every moment of a whole arm swing; with dozens or even hundreds of numbers jittered together, the gradient estimated by trial and error becomes more and more scattered, whereas the cost of getting derivatives by backpropagation does not change with how many numbers there are. This is the main selling point of "differentiable simulators" in robot learning: on tasks with smooth dynamics, differentiating through the simulator often needs far fewer samples than reinforcement learning and is much faster. For example, on a simulated humanoid model driven by 152 muscle–tendon units, SHAC (Short-Horizon Actor-Critic, Xu et al. 2022) reached the same performance more than 17 times faster (measured in wall-clock time) than PPO (Proximal Policy Optimisation), one of the most widely used reinforcement learning algorithms.

对神经网络本身来说，可微不是锦上添花，而是地基。网络里的每一种零件，线性层、ReLU、注意力、归一化，都是挑过的，保证整个网络对每一个权重都能求导（ReLU 只在 0 这一点有个折角，几乎处处可导，框架约定那里的导数取 0）；几十亿个权重，实际上只能靠反向传播来训练。就算每一步都可导，网络很深时，几十层的局部导数连乘起来，梯度也可能小到几乎为 0，或者大到爆掉（叫梯度消失、梯度爆炸）；ReLU、残差连接、归一化这些零件，很大程度上就是为了让梯度一路传回去还有用。上面这个例子里，损失来自物理；更常见的是监督学习，损失直接来自数据：网络的输出和标注差多少。动机最单纯：从数据里学，旋钮是权重。顺带一提，旋钮也可以是网络的输入：权重不动，对输入的图片求导，可以找出让网络认错的微小扰动（对抗样本），也可以把一张照片一点点改成某种画风（风格迁移），这些在第 ⑨ 步的光谱里都有。For neural networks themselves, differentiability is not icing on the cake but the foundation. Every kind of component in a network (linear layers, ReLU, attention, normalisation) has been chosen to make sure the whole network can be differentiated with respect to every weight (ReLU has a kink only at 0, so it is differentiable almost everywhere, and frameworks by convention take its derivative there to be 0); with billions of weights, backpropagation is in practice the only way to train them. Even when every step is differentiable, in a deep network the local derivatives of dozens of layers are multiplied together, and the gradient can shrink to almost 0 or grow until it blows up (known as vanishing gradients and exploding gradients); components such as ReLU, residual connections and normalisation are there largely to keep the gradient useful all the way back. In the example above, the loss comes from physics; more common is supervised learning, where the loss comes straight from data: how far the network’s output is from the labels. The motivation is the simplest of all: learning from data, where the knobs are the weights. Incidentally, the knobs can also be the network’s inputs: keep the weights fixed and differentiate with respect to the input image, and you can find tiny perturbations that make the network get it wrong (adversarial examples), or turn a photo, little by little, into a particular painting style (style transfer). Both appear in the spectrum in step ⑨.

回到物理：可微仿真器的那些好处，前提是动力学光滑。下一步就会看到，它不总成立。Back to physics: all those advantages of differentiable simulators depend on the dynamics being smooth. The next step shows that this does not always hold.

第 ④ 步 · 撞上筐沿Step ④ · Hitting the rim

撞上筐沿：梯度会骗人Hitting the rim: gradients can lie

到目前为止，球在空中走的都是一条光滑的抛物线，落点随速度平滑地变。可真的筐有筐壁，筐壁顶上是一圈筐沿；球一碰到它们，就会弹开。把仰角固定在 45°、筐放在 2.0 m，从慢到快把出手速度过一遍，球的下场是这样的：So far the ball has always flown along a smooth parabola, and the landing point has changed smoothly with the speed. But a real bin has walls, with a rim running all the way round the top; as soon as the ball touches them, it bounces off. Fix the angle at 45°, put the bin at 2.0 m and sweep the release speed from slow to fast. Here is what happens to the ball:

  • 慢于 3.56 m/s：打在近处那面筐壁的外侧，掉在筐前面。Slower than 3.56 m/s: it hits the outside of the bin’s near wall and drops in front of the bin.
  • 3.56 到 3.68 m/s：碰到近处的筐沿，大多弹出去，偶尔弹进来；在其中几个很窄的速度段里，球会在两边的筐沿之间来回撞。3.56–3.68 m/s: it hits the near rim and mostly bounces out, occasionally in; in a few very narrow speed bands within this range, the ball bounces back and forth between the two rims.
  • 3.68 到 3.95 m/s：干净地落进筐里。3.68–3.95 m/s: it drops cleanly into the bin.
  • 3.95 到 4.05 m/s：碰到远处的筐沿，先是还能弹进去，再快一点就弹出去了。3.95–4.05 m/s: it hits the far rim; at first it still bounces in, but a little faster and it bounces out.
  • 快于 4.05 m/s：从远处的筐沿上方飞过去。Faster than 4.05 m/s: it flies over the far rim.

碰撞把光滑变成了跳变。梯度在这里会用三种方式骗人：Collisions turn smooth change into jumps. Here the gradient can lie in three ways:

  • 给你 0：如果只看"进没进"，它是一级一级的台阶，除了台阶边上，到处都是平的，导数是 0。离筐只差一点点，梯度也说"不用改"。It gives you 0: if you look only at "in or out", it is a staircase. Apart from the edges of the steps it is flat everywhere, and the derivative is 0. Even when the ball misses the bin by a hair, the gradient says "no change needed".
  • 指错方向：3.55 m/s 时，球打在近处筐壁的外侧掉下来。稍微慢一点，打得低一点，掉下来离筐中心反而近了一点点。于是这一点上的导数（只有一个旋钮时，它就是局部梯度）说"再慢一点"，正好背离了筐。It points the wrong way: at 3.55 m/s the ball hits the outside of the near wall and drops down. A little slower, and it hits a little lower and actually ends up a touch closer to the bin centre. So the derivative at this point (with only one knob, this is the local gradient) says "slower still", which points straight away from the bin.
  • 大得没有意义：在筐沿附近，速度变 0.001 m/s，球最后停的位置能变 10 cm，局部导数到了 100 左右，而在空中飞时只有 0.9。来回撞几次，每次都把小差别放大，这就是混沌。跟着这种梯度走，就像在暴风里看风向标。It is too big to mean anything: near the rims, changing the speed by 0.001 m/s can move the ball’s final resting place by 10 cm. The local derivative reaches about 100, against only 0.9 in free flight. The ball bounces back and forth a few times, and each bounce magnifies small differences: this is chaos. Following a gradient like this is like going by a weathervane in a storm.

注意，"球最后停在哪"这个函数也是"几乎处处可导"的：除了那些跳的点，每一点都能求出导数。可在筐沿和筐壁附近，求出来的导数没用。可微和梯度有用，是两回事。Note that the function "where the ball ends up" is also "differentiable almost everywhere": apart from the points where it jumps, a derivative can be computed at every point. But near the rims and walls of the bin, the derivatives you get are useless. Being differentiable and having a useful gradient are two different things.

怎么办？最朴素的想法是：既然一个点上的导数只看得到脚下，那就看一片。真实的扔本来就有误差，同一个指令，每次出手速度都会抖一点；我们关心的其实不是某一次进没进，而是带着这点抖动，平均能进几成。这个平均是光滑的。What can be done? The simplest idea is this: since the derivative at a single point sees only what is underfoot, look at a whole stretch instead. Real throws are never exact anyway: with the same command, the release speed jitters a little every time. What we really care about is not whether one particular throw goes in, but with that jitter, what fraction go in on average. That average is smooth.

Its slope can be estimated from n jittered throws: in the throws that went in, the side the jitter leaned towards on average is the uphill side.

对抖动取平均，等于把台阶抹成斜坡；抹开的宽度是 σ，所以从筐外面也能"看见"筐。第二个式子（估计式）一点导数都不用：只要扔、看结果。第 ⑥ 步会看到它就是 REINFORCE。Averaging over the jitter smooths the step out into a slope; the width of the smoothing is σ, so the bin can be "seen" even from outside it. 研究者对付碰撞，大致也是这几招：Researchers tackle collisions with much the same few tricks:

  • 平滑：一种是上面那样，对噪声取平均；另一种是把物理本身改软，把"碰到 / 没碰到"换成一个随穿透深度平滑增长的力。很多可微仿真器的接触都是这样软化过的，代价是碰撞不那么真实。Smoothing: one way is the one above, averaging over noise; the other is to make the physics itself soft, replacing "touching / not touching" with a force that grows smoothly with the penetration depth. Contact in many differentiable simulators is softened in this way, at the cost of less realistic collisions.
  • 抽样：干脆不对碰撞求导，像上面那样从一批带噪声的结果里估梯度，这就是强化学习的做法。Suh 等 2022 专门比较过：穿过仿真器算出的梯度，在碰撞这类跳变附近会有系统性的偏差，在又硬又敏感的系统里方差还可能比抽样更大；抽样估计不偏，但通常更散。他们提出把两种按比例混起来用。Sampling: don’t differentiate through the collision at all; as above, estimate the gradient from a batch of noisy results. This is what reinforcement learning does. Suh et al. 2022 compared the two directly: gradients computed through a simulator are systematically biased near jumps such as collisions, and in stiff, sensitive systems their variance can even be larger than that of sampling; sampled estimates are unbiased but usually more scattered. They propose a weighted mix of the two.
  • 截断：只对一小段求导，比如只到碰撞之前，或者只往前推几步，剩下的部分用一个光滑的量去概括（常用学出来的价值函数：从某个状态出发，往后平均还能拿到多少奖励）。SHAC 就是这样处理长时间的运动的。Truncation: differentiate only over a short span, say up to just before the collision, or just a few steps ahead, and summarise the rest with a smooth quantity (often a learned value function: how much reward can still be expected, on average, from a given state onwards). This is how SHAC copes with motion over long time spans.

Metz 等 2021 的论文题目说得更直接："梯度不是你需要的全部"：在混沌的系统里，穿过很长的过程求导，梯度和它的方差会随过程的长度指数地变大，这时不求导的黑箱估计反而可能更好。The paper by Metz et al. 2021 puts it more bluntly in its title, "Gradients are Not All You Need": in chaotic systems, when you differentiate through a long process, the gradients and their variance grow exponentially with the length of the process, and then black-box estimates that take no derivatives can actually do better.

到这里能看出第一处差别了。神经网络是人设计的，每个零件都特意挑成能求导、大多光滑的；物理不是人设计的，碰撞、摩擦、开关本来就是跳的。在仿真器里说"可微"，图的是和第 ③ 步一样的东西：少试几次、学得更准；可碰撞和混沌让这些梯度很脆。所以在机器人领域，"穿过仿真器求导"和"强化学习"至今各有地盘。This is where the first difference shows up. Neural networks are designed by people, and every part is deliberately chosen to be differentiable and mostly smooth; physics was designed by nobody, and collisions, friction and on/off switching are full of jumps by nature. In a simulator, "differentiable" is wanted for the same reasons as in step ③: fewer tries and more accurate learning. But collisions and chaos make these gradients brittle. That is why, in robotics, "differentiating through the simulator" and "reinforcement learning" still each have their own territory.

第 ⑤ 步 · 从画面反推Step ⑤ · Working back from images

从画面反推：让渲染变软Working back from images: making rendering soft

前面一直假设机器人知道筐在 2.0 m。可真实的机器人只有相机：它看到的是一张画面，得从画面反推出筐在哪。从结果反推原因，这类问题叫逆问题。有一个很通用的解法，叫"渲染再比较"：先猜一个场景（筐在哪、多大），按这个猜测画出相机应该看到的画面，和真拍到的画面逐个像素比较，再顺着"像素差"的梯度去改猜测。从场景画出画面的过程叫渲染，它是光的物理；渲染可微，像素差对场景参数的梯度就有了。Until now we have assumed that the robot knows the bin is at 2.0 m. But a real robot has only a camera: what it sees is a picture, and it has to work back from the picture to where the bin is. Problems like this, working back from effects to their causes, are called inverse problems. One very general way to solve them is "render and compare": guess a scene (where the bin is, how big it is), draw the picture the camera ought to see under that guess, compare it pixel by pixel with the picture actually taken, and then change the guess by following the gradient of the "pixel difference". The process of drawing a picture from a scene is called rendering, and it is the physics of light. If rendering is differentiable, you have the gradient of the pixel difference with respect to the scene parameters.

麻烦出在边缘上。最普通的渲染叫光栅化：每个像素只问一句：像素中心落没落在物体里？落在里面就是物体的颜色，不在就是背景。把猜的筐挪一点点，只要没有一条边跨过某个像素中心，画面一个像素都不变；跨过的那一下，又突然变一整格。像素差是一级一级的台阶，导数几乎处处是 0，和第 ④ 步"进没进"的毛病一模一样。The trouble is at the edges. The most ordinary kind of rendering, called rasterisation, asks each pixel just one question: does the pixel centre fall inside the object? If it does, the pixel gets the object’s colour; if not, the background colour. Move the guessed bin a tiny bit, and as long as no edge crosses a pixel centre, not a single pixel of the picture changes; the moment one does, a whole pixel changes at once. The pixel difference is a staircase whose derivative is 0 almost everywhere: exactly the same trouble as "in or out" in step ④.

每个像素的值不再是非 0 即 1，而是随它离边缘的距离，从 0 平滑地过渡到 1；过渡的宽度是 β。损失是画出来的和拍到的、每个像素之差的平方和。Each pixel’s value is no longer strictly 0 or 1: it goes smoothly from 0 to 1 depending on its distance from the edge, over a width of β. The loss is the sum, over all pixels, of the squared difference between the drawn value and the captured one.

平滑的过渡让"挪一点，画面就变一点"重新成立，像素差对位置的导数不再是 0。Soft Rasterizer 就是这个思路：每个三角形对每个像素给出一个"覆盖它的概率"，用的也是到边缘距离的 S 形函数（论文里用的是距离的平方），再用一个参数控制有多锐（Liu 等 2019）。The smooth transition makes "move a little, the picture changes a little" true again, so the derivative of the pixel difference with respect to position is no longer 0. This is the idea behind Soft Rasterizer (Liu et al. 2019): each triangle gives every pixel a "probability of covering it", computed, as here, with a sigmoid of the distance to the edge (the paper uses the squared distance), and a parameter sets how sharp it is.

软有代价：太软，画面是糊的，猜的位置会被带偏；太硬，离得远了就没有梯度。常用的办法和第 ④ 步一样，先软后硬，叫由粗到细。Softness has a price. Too soft, and the picture is blurred and the guessed position can be pulled off target; too hard, and once the guess is far off there is no gradient. The usual remedy is the same as in step ④: soft first, then hard. This is called coarse to fine.

真正的可微渲染器，就是在和这些边缘打交道。OpenDR（2014）给普通渲染配上近似的导数；Neural 3D Mesh Renderer（2018）明说"光栅化这一步挡住了反向传播"，于是把边缘跨过像素时的突变换成线性的渐变，当作近似梯度；Soft Rasterizer 把整个画面都画软。另一条路不把画面画软，而是把导数算对：一个像素的颜色是它覆盖的那一小块面积上的积分，物体挪动时，积分的边界跟着动，导数里就多出一项"边缘上的贡献"。Li 等 2018 专门在边缘上采样，把这一项也算进去，做出了（据作者所知）第一个能对相机、形状、材质、光照给出正确、无偏的导数估计的通用光线追踪器。今天常用的工具有 Mitsuba 3、nvdiffrast、PyTorch3D 等。Real differentiable renderers are all about dealing with these edges. OpenDR (2014) gives ordinary rendering approximate derivatives. Neural 3D Mesh Renderer (2018) says plainly that the rasterisation step "prevents back-propagation", so it replaces the sudden jump when an edge crosses a pixel with a linear ramp and uses that as an approximate gradient. Soft Rasterizer draws the whole picture soft. The other route does not draw the picture soft but gets the derivative right: a pixel’s colour is an integral over the small area it covers, and when an object moves, the boundary of that integral moves with it, so the derivative gains an extra term, the "contribution from the edges". Li et al. 2018 sample points on the edges specifically to include this term as well, and built what was, to the authors’ knowledge, the first general-purpose ray tracer that gives correct, unbiased estimates of the derivatives with respect to camera, shape, materials and lighting. Common tools today include Mitsuba 3, nvdiffrast and PyTorch3D.

NeRF 和高斯泼溅：换一种天生就软的表示NeRF and Gaussian splatting: switching to a representation that is soft by nature

还有一个更彻底的办法：别用有硬边缘的三角形表示场景，换一种天生就软的表示。There is a more radical way still: don’t represent the scene with hard-edged triangles at all, but switch to a representation that is soft by nature.

NeRF（Mildenhall 等 2020）把场景表示成一个函数：给它空间中的一个点和一个观看方向，它给出这里有多"浓"、是什么颜色。一个像素的颜色，是沿着从相机射出的那条光线，把一路上的颜色按浓度加权累加起来：前面越浓，后面的颜色透过来得越少。这叫体渲染。没有哪一处是"突然被挡住"的，每个像素都是这个函数的光滑的累加，论文里说"体渲染天然可微"。于是只要有一组知道相机位姿（位置加朝向）的照片，就能用梯度下降把场景拟合出来。代价是慢：原始的 NeRF 在一张 V100 上训练一个场景要 1 到 2 天，渲染一帧要 30 秒左右。后来的 Plenoxels 干脆不要神经网络，在一个三维格子里直接存浓度和颜色，同样用梯度下降去拟合，11 分钟就能达到相近的效果；Instant NGP 训练 15 秒就赶上了原始 NeRF。Plenoxels 的作者由此说：关键的零件不是神经网络，而是可微的体渲染。NeRF (Mildenhall et al. 2020) represents a scene as a function: give it a point in space and a viewing direction, and it tells you how "dense" things are there and what colour they are. A pixel’s colour comes from following its ray out from the camera and adding up the colours along the way, weighted by density: the denser things are in front, the less of the colour behind shows through. This is called volume rendering. Nothing is ever "suddenly blocked"; every pixel is a smooth accumulation of this function, and the paper says that "volume rendering is naturally differentiable". So, given a set of photos with known camera poses (position plus orientation), the scene can be fitted by gradient descent. The price is speed: the original NeRF takes 1–2 days to train one scene on a single V100 and about 30 seconds to render one frame. The later Plenoxels drops the neural network altogether, storing density and colour directly in a 3D grid and fitting them by gradient descent in the same way; it reaches similar results in 11 minutes. Instant NGP catches up with the original NeRF after 15 seconds of training. From this, the authors of Plenoxels conclude that the key part is not the neural network but differentiable volume rendering.

三维高斯泼溅（3DGS，Kerbl 等 2023）走得更远：把场景表示成几百万个半透明的三维高斯色斑（一团椭球形的颜色，中心最浓、往外按正态分布的钟形渐淡），每个有位置、形状和朝向（用缩放和旋转表示）、不透明度和颜色。渲染时，把它们投到画面上，按远近排好，从前往后一层层叠，前面的挡住后面的一部分。每个色斑的边缘都是渐淡的，所以除了前后次序互换这类少数时刻，每个像素都随所有参数平滑地变；求导时把次序当作不变，整个过程就能反向传播；而且叠色斑比沿光线一点点采样快得多。在 Mip-NeRF360 论文发布的那组场景上，3DGS 训练 41 分钟，渲染每秒 134 帧；同一组场景上，当时质量最好的方法 Mip-NeRF360 要训练 48 小时，每秒 0.06 帧。有一处不是梯度在管：色斑的个数。哪里该分裂出更多色斑、哪里该删掉透明的，是按规则做的（某个色斑位置的梯度长期很大，就复制或分裂它）。"个数"是离散的，正是第 ⑥ 步要讲的那类问题。3D Gaussian splatting (3DGS, Kerbl et al. 2023) goes further. It represents the scene as millions of semi-transparent 3D Gaussian blobs (ellipsoid-shaped clouds of colour, densest at the centre and fading outwards along the bell curve of a normal distribution), each with a position, a shape and orientation (given by a scale and a rotation), an opacity and a colour. To render, it projects them onto the picture, sorts them by distance and blends them layer by layer from front to back, those in front partly hiding those behind. Every blob fades out towards its edges, so apart from a few moments, such as when two blobs swap places in the front-to-back order, every pixel changes smoothly with all the parameters; if the order is treated as fixed when differentiating, the whole process can be backpropagated through. And blending blobs is much faster than sampling bit by bit along rays. On the set of scenes released with the Mip-NeRF360 paper, 3DGS trains in 41 minutes and renders at 134 frames per second; on the same scenes, Mip-NeRF360, the best method in quality at the time, takes 48 hours to train and renders 0.06 frames per second. One thing is not left to gradients: how many blobs there are. Where to split off more blobs and where to delete transparent ones is decided by rules (if the gradient of a blob’s position stays large for a long time, the blob is copied or split). "How many" is discrete: exactly the kind of problem step ⑥ is about.

这一步的动机和前面都不一样。训练神经网络，是从数据里学一个规律；可微渲染要解的是逆问题：旋钮是场景本身的参数（形状、材质、光照、相机位置），模型是固定的光学，"学习"其实是在反推。最大的障碍是可见性带来的跳变：谁挡住谁、边缘落在哪个像素。人们的办法，要么把画面画软，要么把边缘上的导数算对，要么干脆换成一种天生就软的表示。高斯泼溅流行起来，是因为它挑中了一种既可微、渲染又快的表示：可微让它能从照片里拟合出来，快让它能实时显示。The motivation in this step is different from all the earlier ones. Training a neural network means learning a pattern from data; differentiable rendering solves an inverse problem: the knobs are the parameters of the scene itself (shape, materials, lighting, camera position), the model is fixed optics, and "learning" really means working backwards. The biggest obstacle is the jumps caused by visibility: what hides what, and which pixel an edge falls in. People’s answers are to draw the picture soft, to get the derivative at the edges right, or to switch altogether to a representation that is soft by nature. Gaussian splatting caught on because it hit on a representation that is both differentiable and fast to render: being differentiable lets it be fitted from photos, and being fast lets it be shown in real time.

第 ⑥ 步 · 离散和随机Step ⑥ · Discrete and random

离散和随机：选一个、抽一个，怎么求导Discrete and random: how to differentiate a choice or a draw

上一步留下一个尾巴：高斯色斑的个数是离散的，梯度管不了。AI 系统里到处是这种"选择"：下一个字输出哪个，这一步该看句子里的哪个词，网络的这一层用哪种结构，三个筐扔哪一个。选择通常是：给每个选项打一个分，取分最高的那个。比如地上有 A、B、C 三个筐，机器人给它们各打一个分：1.0、1.4、0.6，于是选分最高的 B。把 C 的分数提高一点点，只要还没超过 1.4，结果就不变；一旦超过，结果突然从 B 跳到 C。又是台阶，导数又几乎处处是 0。The previous step left a loose end: the number of Gaussian blobs is discrete, and gradients cannot handle it. AI systems are full of "choices" like this: which word to output next, which word in the sentence this step should look at, which structure to use for this layer of the network, which of three bins to throw into. A choice is usually made like this: give each option a score and take the one with the highest score. For example, with three bins A, B and C on the floor, the robot gives them scores of 1.0, 1.4 and 0.6, so it picks B, which has the highest. Raise C’s score a tiny bit, and as long as it has not yet passed 1.4, the result stays the same; once it does, the result suddenly jumps from B to C. Once again it is a staircase, and once again the derivative is 0 almost everywhere.

第一个办法是把选择变软：不再只选一个，而是按分数给每个选项一个权重，分数越高权重越大，权重加起来是 1。这就是 softmax。The first fix is to make the choice soft: instead of picking just one, give each option a weight according to its score; the higher the score, the larger the weight, and the weights add up to 1. This is softmax.

pk = exp(sk / τ) / Σj exp(sj / τ)

选项 k 的权重，等于它的分数除以温度再取指数，再除以所有选项这个指数的总和。The weight of option k is its score divided by the temperature, then exponentiated, and finally divided by the sum of these exponentials over all the options.

取指数，让所有权重都是正的，而且分数高的权重也高；除以总和，让它们加起来是 1。权重随分数平滑地变，所以可以求导：∂pk/∂sk = pk(1 − pk)/τ。Taking the exponential makes every weight positive and gives a higher score a higher weight; dividing by the sum makes the weights add up to 1. The weights change smoothly with the scores, so they can be differentiated: ∂pk/∂sk = pk(1 − pk)/τ.

软的选择是"混合"，不是"选择"：一半扔向 A、一半扔向 B，意思是瞄着两个筐的中间扔，球会落在两个筐之间。温度越低，越像真的选择，可导数只在分数接近时才大，别处几乎是 0。软一点，梯度有用，可离真的选择偏得远；硬一点，偏差小，可梯度几乎处处是 0：又是此消彼长。A soft choice is a "mix", not a "choice": throwing half towards A and half towards B means aiming midway between the two bins, so the ball lands between them. The lower the temperature, the more it acts like a real choice, but then the derivative is large only where the scores are close, and almost 0 everywhere else. Softer, and the gradient is useful, but the result is far from a real choice; harder, and the bias is small, but the gradient is 0 almost everywhere. Once again, bias and gradient trade off against each other.

这个"变软"用处极广。注意力就是软的查找：不是只挑一个词去看，而是按 softmax 权重把所有词加权平均，于是"该看哪里"本身也能用梯度学出来（Bahdanau 等 2015；后来 Transformer 的核心就是它）。DARTS（可微结构搜索）把"这一层用哪种结构"也变成 softmax 权重，用梯度一起学，学完再保留权重最大的那个，搜索网络结构的花费，从 NASNet、AmoebaNet（分别用强化学习、进化算法去搜）的两三千 GPU 天（一块 GPU 算一天）降到了 1.5 到 4 GPU 天。This "softening" is used very widely. Attention is a soft lookup: instead of picking just one word to look at, it takes a weighted average of all the words using softmax weights, so "where to look" can itself be learned by gradients (Bahdanau et al. 2015; it later became the core of the Transformer). DARTS (Differentiable Architecture Search) also turns "which structure to use for this layer" into softmax weights, learns them by gradients together with the network, and when training is done keeps the one with the largest weight. This cut the cost of searching for a network architecture from 2 000–3 000 GPU days (a GPU day is one GPU computing for a day) for NASNet and AmoebaNet, which searched with reinforcement learning and an evolutionary algorithm respectively, to 1.5–4 GPU days.

有时我们要的不是混合，而是按概率随机抽一个：探索时要随机试，生成文字时要抽一个字，有的模型里有离散的随机变量。抽样本身也不能求导。有一个漂亮的技巧：给每个分数各加一个独立的 Gumbel 噪声（取 0 到 1 之间的均匀随机数 U，算 −log(−log U)），再取最大，抽到每个选项的概率正好就是 softmax(s) 给出的概率（Gumbel-max 技巧）。Gumbel-softmax（Jang 等 2017；同时期的 Maddison 等 2017 叫它 Concrete 分布）再把最后那个"取最大"换成低温的 softmax：抽出来的几乎是一个选项，又能对分数求导。还有更粗暴的直通估计（这个名字来自 Bengio 等 2013）：往前算时用硬的选择，往回传梯度时假装它是软的。它有偏差，可在实践中常常管用，量化网络时的"取整"就常这样处理。Sometimes what we want is not a mix but to draw one at random according to the probabilities: exploration means trying things at random, generating text means drawing a word, and some models contain discrete random variables. Sampling itself cannot be differentiated either. There is a neat trick: add independent Gumbel noise to each score (draw a uniform random number U between 0 and 1 and compute −log(−log U)) and then take the maximum; the probability of drawing each option is then exactly the probability given by softmax(s) (the Gumbel-max trick). Gumbel-softmax (Jang et al. 2017; Maddison et al. 2017, working at the same time, called it the Concrete distribution) then replaces that final "take the maximum" with a low-temperature softmax: what comes out is almost a single option, yet it can be differentiated with respect to the scores. Cruder still is the straight-through estimator (the name comes from Bengio et al. 2013): on the way forward it uses the hard choice, and when passing the gradient back it pretends the choice was soft. It is biased, but it often works in practice: the "rounding" in quantised networks is commonly handled this way.

随机：对一个平均值求导Random: differentiating an average

回到扔球。机械臂的手会抖：同一个指令速度 μ，实际出手的是 v = μ + σε，ε 每次都不一样（第 ④ 步的指令速度 v，这里改叫 μ，好和实际出手的 v 分开）。我们能调的是指令 μ，关心的是平均下来好不好：平均差多少，或者平均能进几成。要对一个"平均值"求导，有两个办法：Back to throwing the ball. The robot arm’s hand shakes: for the same commanded speed μ, the actual release speed is v = μ + σε, with a different ε each time (the commanded speed called v in step ④ is called μ here, to keep it apart from the actual release speed v). What we can adjust is the command μ, and what we care about is how well it does on average: how much it misses by on average, or what share of throws go in. There are two ways to differentiate an "average":

d/dμ E[L(v)] = E[L′(v)]d/dμ E[f(v)] = E[f(v) · ε / σ]

第一行：抖动当作外来的、和 μ 无关的随机数，对每一次带抖动的扔各求一次导，再取平均。第二行：不对结果求导，只看结果和抖动的关系：结果好的那几次，抖动偏向哪边，梯度就指向哪边。First line: treat the jitter as a random number from outside that has nothing to do with μ, differentiate each jittered throw separately, then take the average. Second line: don’t differentiate the result; look only at how the result relates to the jitter: on the throws that turned out well, whichever way the jitter leaned is the way the gradient points.

第一行叫重参数化：把"随机抽一个 v"改写成"μ 加上一个和 μ 无关的噪声"，随机性被挪到了链的外面，剩下的部分就能照常求导（Kingma & Welling 2013 用它训练变分自编码器（VAE），第七篇第 ③ 步讲过）。第二行叫得分函数估计（得分函数指概率密度取对数以后对参数的导数，这里正好是 ε/σ；它和奖励、分数都无关），强化学习里叫 REINFORCE（Williams 1992）：它来自对正态分布的密度求导，f 本身完全不用可导。第 ④ 步的估计式，就是这里的第二行用 n 次扔来估，再减去基线 ȳ。The first line is called reparameterisation: rewrite "draw a random v" as "μ plus noise that has nothing to do with μ". The randomness is moved outside the chain, and the rest can be differentiated as usual (Kingma & Welling 2013 used it to train the variational autoencoder (VAE), covered in step ③ of page 7). The second line is called the score function estimator (the score function is the derivative of the log of the probability density with respect to the parameter, here exactly ε/σ; it has nothing to do with rewards or scores), known in reinforcement learning as REINFORCE (Williams 1992): it comes from differentiating the density of the normal distribution, and f itself does not need to be differentiable at all. The estimate in step ④ is just the second line estimated from n throws, with the baseline ȳ subtracted.

μ = 3.95 m/s、σ = 0.06 m/s，每批扔 16 次，各估 200 批：对"平均差的平方"，重参数化估出来的 200 个值散开（标准差）0.022，REINFORCE 散开 0.111，是它的 5 倍。对"进筐率"，重参数化永远给 0：对每一次扔求导，"进没进"的导数几乎处处是 0，唯一不是 0 的是跳的那一点，那里是一个无穷高的尖峰，一次次抽样正好全漏掉（第 ⑤ 步的边缘采样，补的就是这类"跳变处的贡献"）。真正的梯度却是每 m/s −6.24（3.95 m/s 时平均落点比筐中心远约 12 cm，刚出"进"（差不到 10 cm）的范围：再快，进筐率掉得很快）。REINFORCE 平均下来是对的，只是很散。With μ = 3.95 m/s and σ = 0.06 m/s, 16 throws per batch, and one estimate from each of 200 batches: for the "mean squared miss", the 200 reparameterisation estimates have a spread (standard deviation) of 0.022 and the REINFORCE ones 0.111, 5 times as much. For the "hit rate", reparameterisation always gives 0: differentiated throw by throw, "in or not" has a derivative of 0 almost everywhere; the only place it is not 0 is the point where it jumps, and there it is an infinitely tall spike, which every single sample misses (the edge sampling in step ⑤ is exactly what adds back this kind of "contribution from the jumps"). Yet the true gradient is −6.24 per m/s (at 3.95 m/s the average landing point is about 12 cm beyond the bin centre, just outside the "in" range of a miss under 10 cm: any faster, and the hit rate drops quickly). REINFORCE is right on average, just widely spread.

两个办法各有代价：重参数化要求结果可导，可导时它又稳又准；REINFORCE 什么都能用，代价是方差大。减去一个"基线"（同一批里别的几次的平均），能明显减小方差，又不改变平均值。第 ④ 步的写法（减整批的平均，再除以 n − 1）和它完全等价。Each method has its price: reparameterisation needs the result to be differentiable, and when it is, it is both steady and accurate; REINFORCE works on anything, at the price of high variance. Subtracting a "baseline" (the average of the other throws in the same batch) reduces the variance noticeably without changing the average. The form used in step ④ (subtracting the average of the whole batch, then dividing by n − 1) is exactly equivalent.

这一对估计，把前面几步串起来了。第 ③ 步的试错学习、第 ④ 步对带噪声的进筐率求斜率，用的都是第二行：世界不给导数，只能从结果和抖动的关系里估梯度，所以强化学习要扔很多很多次。生成模型里，采样的过程是我们自己写的、可导的，于是变分自编码器用的是第一行，又稳又准。扩散模型训练时更简单：加噪声写成"原图加上一个独立的噪声"，那一步本身没有要学的参数，梯度只穿过去噪的网络。This pair of estimators ties the earlier steps together. The trial-and-error learning in step ③ and the slope of the noisy hit rate in step ④ both use the second line: the world gives no derivatives, so the gradient can only be estimated from how the result relates to the jitter, which is why reinforcement learning needs so very many throws. In generative models, the sampling process is something we write ourselves and is differentiable, so the variational autoencoder uses the first line, and its gradient is steady and accurate. Training a diffusion model is simpler still: adding the noise is written as "the original image plus independent noise"; that step itself has no parameters to learn, and the gradient passes only through the denoising network.

这一步的动机又不一样：AI 系统里离散的、随机的零件到处都是：注意力要挑看哪里，结构搜索要挑用哪种层，变分自编码器要抽一个隐变量，智能体要做一个个决定。把它们变得可微，或者给它们估一个梯度，整套系统才能被同一个损失端到端地训练。（语言模型训练时只对每个字的概率求导，softmax 本身可微；要给抽出来的整段话打分、再据此改模型，比如用人类反馈做强化学习，才要用第二行。）代价要么是偏差（软化、直通），要么是方差（得分函数）。The motivation in this step is different again. AI systems are full of discrete and random parts: attention has to pick where to look, architecture search has to pick which kind of layer to use, a variational autoencoder has to draw a latent variable, and an agent has to make one decision after another. Only by making these parts differentiable, or by estimating a gradient for them, can the whole system be trained end to end by a single loss. (Training a language model only needs the derivative of each word’s probability, and softmax itself is differentiable; the second line is needed only when a whole sampled passage is scored and the model is then changed according to that score, as in reinforcement learning from human feedback.) The price is either bias (softening, straight-through) or variance (the score function).

第 ⑦ 步 · 替身Step ⑦ · Stand-ins

真实世界不给导数：学一个可微的替身The real world gives no derivatives: learn a differentiable stand-in

到目前为止，"穿过物理求导"都有一个前提：手里有物理的式子。真实的机器人没有。手松开时，球的速度可能比指令慢一点（手指打滑）；空气在拖着球；球还会转。真扔一次，只有一个落点，没有一张计算图可以往回传：真实世界不给导数。So far, "differentiating through the physics" has always rested on one assumption: that we have a formula for the physics. A real robot does not. When the hand lets go, the ball may be a little slower than commanded (the fingers slip); the air drags on the ball; and the ball spins too. A real throw leaves only a landing point, with no computational graph to pass back through: the real world gives no derivatives.

办法大致有四种，它们的区别在于：真实世界和梯度之间，隔着一个什么样的"替身"。There are roughly four ways round this. What sets them apart is what kind of "stand-in" sits between the real world and the gradient.

  • 不要替身，直接试：在真实世界里试错（强化学习、黑箱优化）。什么模型都不需要，代价是每一次试都是真扔，费时、磨损，还可能出事。No stand-in, just try: trial and error in the real world (reinforcement learning, black-box optimisation). No model of any kind is needed; the price is that every try is a real throw, which takes time, causes wear and tear, and can even lead to accidents.
  • 学一个替身：用真扔的记录，学一个"我这样扔，会落在哪"的模型。学出来的模型是一个神经网络或别的光滑函数，天生可微，于是可以对着它求导、瞄准。这就是上一篇的世界模型。Learn a stand-in: use the record of real throws to learn a model that answers "if I throw like this, where will it land?" The learned model is a neural network or some other smooth function, differentiable by nature, so you can differentiate it and aim with it. This is the world model of the previous page.
  • 书本物理加残差：保留书本上的公式，只让一个小模型去学"公式算的和真实的差多少"。TossingBot（Zeng 等 2019）用的是这个思路的"反方向"版本：不学"这样扔会落在哪"，而是直接学"想扔到 p，出手速度要在公式的基础上加多少"。仰角固定 45°，抛体公式按目标给出一个出手速度，网络再给它加一个修正量。修正量是自己标出来的：头顶的相机看到物体实际落在 p̂，公式算出"扔到 p̂ 本该用多快"，实际用的速度减去它，就是"想扔到 p̂"时该加的修正量。瞄准时它不用求导，修正量是直接回归出来的。它把杂乱的物体扔进机械臂够不到的箱子，每小时能处理 500 多个，扔得准的比例约 85%。Textbook physics plus a residual: keep the formula from the textbook, and let a small model learn only "how far the formula’s answer is from the real one". TossingBot (Zeng et al. 2019) uses a "reversed" version of this idea: instead of learning "where a throw like this will land", it learns directly "how much to add to the formula’s release speed to throw to p". With the angle fixed at 45°, the projectile formula gives a release speed for the target, and a network then adds a correction to it. The robot labels the corrections itself: an overhead camera sees that the object actually landed at p̂, the formula works out "how fast it should have been thrown to reach p̂", and the speed actually used minus that is the correction to add "to throw to p̂". When aiming, it does not differentiate anything; the correction is obtained directly by regression. It throws a jumble of objects into boxes beyond the arm’s reach, handling over 500 an hour, with about 85% of its throws on target.
  • 系统辨识：保留一个可微的仿真器，只是里面有几个不知道的参数（打滑多少、空气阻力多大），用真扔的记录去拟合它们，拟合靠的正是穿过仿真器的梯度。gradSim 甚至直接从视频像素出发，把可微的物理仿真和可微的渲染接在一起，反推出物体的质量、摩擦和弹性。System identification: keep a differentiable simulator that simply has a few unknown parameters in it (how much the hand slips, how strong the air drag is), and fit them to the record of real throws; what the fitting relies on is precisely the gradient through the simulator. gradSim even starts directly from video pixels, joining differentiable physics simulation and differentiable rendering together to work back to an object’s mass, friction and elasticity.

替身的作用是把不给导数的真实世界，换成一个可微的近似，好让梯度有地方可走。保留的物理要是对的，要学的东西就少，需要的真扔也少；可要是学的那一部分形状不对（这里残差只用一条直线，真正的空气阻力却随速度的平方涨），离开数据照样会偏。A stand-in’s job is to replace the real world, which gives no derivatives, with a differentiable approximation, so that the gradient has somewhere to go. 这正是很多机器人系统的做法。Dreamer（Hafner 等 2020）从画面学一个世界模型，然后在模型的想象里训练策略：价值的梯度穿过学出来的模型，一路传回策略。PILCO（Deisenroth & Rasmussen 2011）用高斯过程（一种会同时给出预测和"有多没把握"的回归方法）当替身，对策略直接求解析梯度，在真实的小车倒立摆上，只用了几次尝试、一共 17.5 秒的交互，就学会了把摆甩起来并稳住。强化学习里的 DDPG（深度确定性策略梯度，Lillicrap 等 2016）也是这个思路：评论家 Q 学的是"在这个状态做这个动作，往后能拿到多少奖励"，它是一个可微的替身；演员顺着 Q 对动作的梯度去改自己。SuperMimic（Liao 等 2026）更有意思：它在仿真里训练人形机器人跟着人的动作做，手边本来就有仿真器，可它还是学了一个可微的世界模型去模仿这个仿真器，再让策略的梯度穿过这个模型往前想 32 步。作者给的理由是：梯度来自学出来的模型，就不必对仿真器求导，所以哪怕仿真器里的接触不光滑、程序本身根本不能求导（MuJoCo 的文档说，他们用的 MuJoCo Warp 目前就不支持求导），也照样能用；而且这个模型跑得比仿真器还快 10 倍以上。This is exactly what many robot systems do. Dreamer (Hafner et al. 2020) learns a world model from images, then trains a policy in the model’s imagination: the gradient of the value passes through the learned model, all the way back to the policy. PILCO (Deisenroth & Rasmussen 2011) uses a Gaussian process (a regression method that gives both a prediction and "how unsure it is") as the stand-in and computes analytic gradients for the policy directly; on a real cart-pole, with only a few trials and 17.5 seconds of interaction in all, it learned to swing the pendulum up and keep it balanced. In reinforcement learning, DDPG (deep deterministic policy gradient, Lillicrap et al. 2016) follows the same idea: the critic Q learns "how much reward I will get from now on if I take this action in this state", and it is a differentiable stand-in; the actor adjusts itself by following the gradient of Q with respect to the action. SuperMimic (Liao et al. 2026) is more interesting still: it trains humanoid robots in simulation to follow human motions. It already has a simulator to hand, yet it still learns a differentiable world model to imitate that simulator, then lets the policy’s gradient pass through this model as it thinks 32 steps ahead. The reason the authors give: because the gradient comes from the learned model, there is no need to differentiate the simulator, so the method still works even when the contacts in the simulator are not smooth, or the program itself cannot be differentiated at all (according to MuJoCo’s documentation, the MuJoCo Warp they use does not currently support differentiation); and the model even runs more than 10 times faster than the simulator.

所以在世界模型里说"可微"，图的和仿真器一样：少试几次、学得更准；不一样的是梯度从哪来：真实世界（有时连仿真器也一样）不给导数，学出来的模型天生可微，梯度是"白送的"。白送的东西也有毛病：它只和模型一样准；往前想得太远，第 ④ 步的那些毛病会回来。这也是为什么很多世界模型的方法，规划时宁可在模型里随机试一批动作再挑最好的（上一篇第 ⑤ 步），也不一路求导。So in world models, "differentiable" is wanted for the same reasons as in simulators: fewer tries and more accurate learning. What differs is where the gradient comes from: the real world (and sometimes even the simulator) gives no derivatives, but a learned model is differentiable by nature, so the gradient comes "for free". What comes free has its flaws too: the gradient is only as accurate as the model, and if you think too far ahead, the problems of step ④ come back. This is also why many world-model methods, when planning, would rather try a batch of random actions in the model and pick the best (step ⑤ of the previous page) than differentiate all the way through.

第 ⑧ 步 · 机器人Step ⑧ · Robots

机器人里的可微：同一招，不同的旋钮Differentiability in robotics: the same trick, different knobs

在机器人领域，"可微"出现在很多地方。招式到处都一样：用一个可微的模型做预测，和测量或目标比较，把差别反向传回旋钮。变的是旋钮是什么。In robotics, "differentiable" turns up in many places. The trick is the same everywhere: make a prediction with a differentiable model, compare it with a measurement or a goal, and pass the difference back to the knobs. What changes is what the knobs are.

  • 可微仿真器：把物理仿真写在能自动求导的框架里，比如 Brax、MuJoCo MJX（MuJoCo 的 JAX 版）、NVIDIA Warp、DiffTaichi。旋钮是策略的参数或物理参数：穿过仿真器求导来训练策略，比如第 ③ 步的 SHAC；Song 等 2024 让四足机器人在几分钟里学会走路（前向用不可微的高保真仿真器，梯度走一个简化的可微替身）；Zhang 等 2025 用一个简单的可微质点模型，训练无人机凭深度图在树林里高速穿行，实机飞到每秒 20 米。也可以从数据里辨识参数（第 ⑦ 步）。接触让梯度变脆（第 ④ 步），所以它们大多用软化的接触，有的版本干脆只"大致"支持求导：MJX 的文档就说它的 JAX 实现"大部分支持"自动微分，更快的 Warp 实现则不支持。Differentiable simulators: physics simulation written in a framework that can differentiate automatically, such as Brax, MuJoCo MJX (the JAX version of MuJoCo), NVIDIA Warp and DiffTaichi. The knobs are a policy’s parameters or physical parameters. A policy can be trained by differentiating through the simulator, for example SHAC from step ③; Song et al. 2024 taught a quadruped robot to walk within minutes (the forward pass uses a high-fidelity simulator that is not differentiable, while the gradient goes through a simplified differentiable stand-in); Zhang et al. 2025 used a simple differentiable point-mass model to train a drone to fly through a forest at high speed using depth images, reaching 20 m/s in real flights. Parameters can also be identified from data (step ⑦). Contact makes the gradients brittle (step ④), so most of these simulators use softened contact, and some versions support differentiation only "roughly": MJX’s documentation says its JAX implementation "mostly" supports automatic differentiation, while the faster Warp implementation does not.
  • 轨迹优化：旋钮是一整段动作序列。DDP（微分动态规划，1966）和 iLQR（迭代线性二次调节器，2004）沿着轨迹用动力学的导数，一次改进整段动作：在深度学习之前，机器人就已经在"对动力学求导"了。可微 MPC（2018）又把这样的规划器本身变成可以求导的一层，让规划器里的代价和动力学也能从演示里端到端地学出来，原则上还能接在感知网络后面一起训练。Trajectory optimisation: the knob is a whole sequence of actions. DDP (differential dynamic programming, 1966) and iLQR (iterative linear quadratic regulator, 2004) use the derivatives of the dynamics along the trajectory to improve the whole sequence at once: before deep learning, robots were already "differentiating the dynamics". Differentiable MPC (2018) then turned such a planner itself into a layer that can be differentiated, so that the cost and the dynamics inside the planner can themselves be learned end to end from demonstrations; in principle, it can also be placed after a perception network and trained together with it.
  • 定位和建图：旋钮是相机的位姿（位置加朝向）和地图。iNeRF（2021）对一个训练好的 NeRF 做"渲染再比较"，用梯度下降找出相机在哪；Gaussian Splatting SLAM 和 SplaTAM（2024）用三维高斯当地图，边走边对着高斯地图直接优化相机位姿、边往地图里加高斯；DROID-SLAM（2021）把经典的光束法平差（同时调所有相机的位姿和所有三维点，让它们投到照片上的位置和实际看到的最吻合）做成网络里一个可微的层。定位和建图里最经典的"对误差求导"，是更早的位姿图优化，下面第二个例子。Localisation and mapping: the knobs are the camera’s pose (position plus orientation) and the map. iNeRF (2021) does "render and compare" against a trained NeRF, using gradient descent to find where the camera is; Gaussian Splatting SLAM and SplaTAM (2024) use 3D Gaussians as the map, and as the camera moves they optimise its pose directly against the Gaussian map while adding Gaussians to it; DROID-SLAM (2021) turns classical bundle adjustment (adjusting all the camera poses and all the 3D points together, so that their projections onto the photos match what was actually seen as closely as possible) into a differentiable layer in a network. The most classic case of "differentiating the error" in localisation and mapping is the older pose-graph optimisation, the second example below.
  • 标定和配准：旋钮是一个刚体变换。这是最老的"可微"：相机标定、手眼标定（求相机和机械手之间那个固定的变换）、光束法平差，都是最小二乘问题，靠对误差求导一步步解：每个旋钮动一点、每条误差变多少，排成一张表，叫雅可比矩阵。把两组点云对齐的经典办法 ICP（1992）交替做两件事：给每个点找最近的对应点，再求最好的刚体变换。也可以直接对"点到表面的距离"求导；DiffDRR（2022）则从 CT 可微地渲染出 X 光片，用梯度下降把术中的 X 光片和术前的 CT 对齐。Calibration and registration: the knob is a rigid transformation. This is the oldest kind of "differentiable": camera calibration, hand–eye calibration (finding the fixed transformation between the camera and the robot hand) and bundle adjustment are all least-squares problems, solved step by step using the derivatives of the error: how much each error changes when each knob moves a little, laid out as a table called the Jacobian matrix. ICP (1992), the classic way of aligning two point clouds, alternates between two things: finding the nearest corresponding point for each point, then solving for the best rigid transformation. You can also differentiate the "distance from a point to the surface" directly; DiffDRR (2022) renders X-ray images from CT differentiably and uses gradient descent to align X-rays taken during surgery with the pre-operative CT.

下面细看两个最经典的问题。几乎每台机械臂、每个自己认路的机器人都要解它们，而且它们都比深度学习早几十年：手要到那里，每个关节该转多少；绕了一圈回来，走过的路到底是什么样。Below, we look closely at two of the most classic problems. Almost every robot arm, and every robot that finds its own way around, has to solve them, and both are decades older than deep learning: how far should each joint turn for the hand to get there; and after driving a loop and coming back, what did the path really look like?

逆运动学：手要到那里，每个关节该转多少Inverse kinematics: how far each joint must turn for the hand to get there

机械臂要去抓桌上的一个杯子。知道每个关节转了多少度，算手在哪很容易：从底座出发，把一节节连杆首尾接起来就是，这叫正运动学。反过来，要让手到杯子那里，每个关节该转多少度？这叫逆运动学（IK），难得多：只有一些特别设计的手臂才有现成的公式可套；关节比要满足的条件多时，答案还不止一个。A robot arm is to pick up a cup on the table. If you know how many degrees each joint has turned, working out where the hand is is easy: start from the base and join the links end to end; this is called forward kinematics. The other way round, how many degrees should each joint turn for the hand to get to the cup? This is called inverse kinematics (IK), and it is much harder: only some specially designed arms have a ready-made formula; and when there are more joints than conditions to meet, there is more than one answer.

通用的办法就是这一篇的招：把关节角当旋钮，把"手离目标多远"当损失，一步步拧。要的导数又是一张雅可比矩阵 J：每个关节转一点点，手往横、竖两个方向各动多少。有了它，有三种走法：第 ① 步的梯度下降和牛顿法，外加两者之间的一个折中。The general method is this page’s trick: treat the joint angles as knobs, take "how far the hand is from the target" as the loss, and turn the knobs step by step. The derivative needed is, once again, a Jacobian matrix J: how far the hand moves across and up when each joint turns a little. With it, there are three ways to step: gradient descent and Newton’s method from step ①, plus a compromise between the two.

梯度为什么是 Jᵀe：J 的第 j 列说的是"第 j 个关节转一点，手往哪动"，它和 e 的点积，就是这个关节转一点时 L 涨多少。伪逆为什么是牛顿法：如果手严格按直线规律动，J Δθ = −e 就能一步把差消掉，这正是第 ① 步"沿切线走到交点"；可三个未知数只有两个方程，解有无穷多个，伪逆挑的是三个关节一共转得最少的那个（第 ① 步说过，旋钮一多，牛顿法得先决定挑哪个交点）。它为什么会出事：σmin 是手最难动的那个方向上的灵敏度，伪逆要把手在这个方向上还差的距离除以 σmin。手臂的三节排成一条直线时（比如伸直），每个关节转动，手都只能往垂直于这条线的方向动，沿着这条线一点也动不了，σmin = 0，这叫奇异位形。阻尼最小二乘在分母上加了 λ²，每个方向上，关节转的角度最多是手在这个方向上还差的距离除以 2λ。Why the gradient is Jᵀe: column j of J says "which way the hand moves when joint j turns a little", and its dot product with e is how much L goes up when that joint turns a little. Why the pseudoinverse is Newton’s method: if the hand moved exactly linearly, J Δθ = −e would cancel the miss in one step, which is exactly step ①’s "follow the tangent to the crossing point"; but with three unknowns and only two equations there are infinitely many solutions, and the pseudoinverse picks the one in which the three joints turn least in total (step ① noted that with more knobs, Newton’s method first has to decide which crossing point to pick). Why it goes wrong: σmin is the sensitivity in the direction in which the hand is hardest to move, and the pseudoinverse divides the distance the hand still has to go in that direction by σmin. When the arm’s three links lie in one straight line (fully stretched, for example), turning any joint can only move the hand at right angles to that line, not along it at all, so σmin = 0; this is called a singular configuration. Damped least squares adds λ² to the denominator, so in every direction the joints turn by at most the distance the hand still has to go in that direction divided by 2λ.

Newton’s method takes 4 steps, ending them 8.4 cm, 1.2 cm, 1.3 mm and 0.02 mm away: the miss shrinks faster and faster. Damped least squares takes 7 steps. Put the target out of reach (1.03 m from the base, so at best 6.6 cm short) and, at step 3, Newton’s method turns one joint by 103° in one go, then keeps jumping about; damped least squares settles 6.9 cm short.

这些办法都比深度学习早。Whitney 1969 用雅可比的逆，把"手想往哪动、动多快"换算成"每个关节转多快"；1984 年，Balestrino 等人和 Wolovich、Elliott 各自改用雅可比的转置；伪逆在奇异位形附近会要求关节转得极快，Nakamura 和 Hanafusa 1986、Wampler 1986 各自提出了加阻尼的解法，它和数值优化里的 Levenberg–Marquardt 法是同一个式子。Buss 2004 的讲义把这几种放在一起比较。今天常用的求解器，比如 TRAC-IK（2015），把牛顿法的迭代改进了一下（卡在关节的转动极限上时随机跳开），再和一个通用的非线性优化同时跑，谁先解出来用谁。All of these methods predate deep learning. Whitney 1969 used the inverse of the Jacobian to convert "which way and how fast the hand should move" into "how fast each joint should turn"; in 1984, Balestrino et al. and Wolovich and Elliott each switched to the transpose of the Jacobian; near singular configurations the pseudoinverse asks the joints to turn extremely fast, and Nakamura and Hanafusa 1986 and Wampler 1986 each proposed a damped solution, which is the same formula as the Levenberg–Marquardt method of numerical optimisation. Buss 2004’s notes compare these methods side by side. Solvers in common use today, such as TRAC-IK (2015), improve the Newton iteration (jumping away at random when it gets stuck at the limit of a joint’s travel) and run it alongside a general nonlinear optimiser, taking whichever finds a solution first.

位姿图 SLAM：绕一圈回来，把整条路一起拉直Pose-graph SLAM: after one loop, pull the whole path straight at once

一个扫地机器人不知道自己在哪。它只能数轮子转了几圈，或者看相机画面怎样移动，估计每一步走了多远、转了多少度，这叫里程计。每一步只差一点点，可一步步加起来，误差越积越大：绕一圈回到起点，它以为自己离起点还有一米多。直到它认出眼前就是出发的地方，这叫闭环。这一条信息和里程计对不上：一定有些步子算错了。错在哪几步、每步错了多少？A robot vacuum cleaner does not know where it is. All it can do is count how many times its wheels have turned, or watch how the camera image moves, to estimate how far it went and how many degrees it turned at each step; this is called odometry. Each step is off by only a little, but the errors add up step after step and keep growing: after a loop back to the start, it thinks it is still more than a metre away from where it set off. Then it recognises that what it sees is the place it started from; this is called a loop closure. This one piece of information disagrees with the odometry: some of the steps must have been wrong. Which ones, and by how much?

SLAM（同时定位与建图）分前后两段：前端从传感器数据里得出一条条测量，比如"这一步往前走了 0.5 m"、"又回到了起点"；后端把所有测量合在一起，求出和它们最一致的整条路，地图再沿着这条路拼起来。从 Lu 和 Milios 1997 开始，人们把后端写成一个大的最小二乘问题，叫位姿图：旋钮是机器人走过的每一个位姿（位置加朝向）；每条测量连着两个位姿，说"它们之间应该隔多远、转多少"；损失是所有测量和当前估计之间的差的平方和。要把它压到最小，需要每条差对它连着的位姿的导数，也就是又一张雅可比；然后用高斯–牛顿法，也就是专门对付平方和的牛顿法，一次挪动所有位姿。SLAM (simultaneous localisation and mapping) comes in two parts: the front end turns sensor data into individual measurements, such as "this step went 0.5 m forward" or "I am back at the start"; the back end combines all the measurements and finds the whole path that agrees with them best, and the map is then pieced together along that path. Since Lu and Milios 1997, the back end has been written as one big least-squares problem, called a pose graph: the knobs are all the poses (position plus heading) the robot passed through; each measurement links two poses and says how far apart they should be and how much one is turned relative to the other; the loss is the sum of the squared differences between all the measurements and the current estimate. Minimising it needs the derivative of each difference with respect to the poses it links, which is yet another Jacobian; then the Gauss–Newton method, a version of Newton’s method made for sums of squares, moves all the poses at once.

损失是所有测量的差的平方和，每条测量按它有多可信加权。差 eij 是"按现在估计的位姿 i 和 j，这条测量应该读出什么"，减去"实际读到了什么"。第三行是高斯–牛顿走的一步：解一个线性方程组，得到所有位姿一起要挪的量 Δθ。The loss is the sum of the squared differences over all measurements, each weighted by how far it can be trusted. The difference eij is "what this measurement should read, given the current estimates of poses i and j", minus "what it actually read". The third line is one Gauss–Newton step: solving one system of linear equations gives Δθ, how far all the poses move together.

把每条测量想成一根弹簧，连着两个位姿：估计和测量对不上，弹簧就被拉长或扭转，L 相当于所有弹簧的总能量。Ω 越大，弹簧越硬：闭环那根比里程计的硬得多。第三行是这样来的：把每条差换成它的直线近似 eij + Jij Δθ（第 ① 步的切线），平方和就变成 Δθ 的一个碗；对 Δθ 求导、令它为 0，碗底就是第三行的解。弹簧几乎是线性的时候（朝向只差几度），这个碗底就几乎是真正的答案。Think of each measurement as a spring joining two poses: when the estimate and the measurement disagree, the spring is stretched or twisted, and L amounts to the total energy of all the springs. The larger Ω, the stiffer the spring: the loop-closure spring is much stiffer than the odometry ones. The third line comes from this: replace each difference by its linear approximation eij + Jij Δθ (the tangent of step ①), and the sum of squares becomes a bowl in Δθ; differentiate with respect to Δθ and set the result to 0, and the bottom of the bowl is the solution of the third line. When the springs are almost linear (headings off by only a few degrees), the bottom of this bowl is almost the real answer.

Adding up the odometry, the end is 1.42 m from the start, the path is on average 96 cm from the true one, and the sum of squared errors is 5753. 位姿图的写法来自 Lu 和 Milios 1997；Grisetti 等 2010 的教程从头讲了这套做法。真实的系统有成千上万个位姿，解得动，靠的是稀疏：每条测量只连两个位姿，所以 Σ Jijᵀ Ωij Jij 里绝大多数是 0。这里那个 120 × 120 的矩阵，不是 0 的只有对角线附近窄窄一条；闭环多了，带外才会多出零星几块。g2o（2011）、iSAM2（2012，来了新测量只更新受影响的那一部分，GTSAM 库里有它的实现）、Ceres 这些库就是干这个的：用户写每种测量的差怎么算（有的还要写它的雅可比），库来解稀疏的高斯–牛顿或 Levenberg–Marquardt；Ceres 还能自动求出雅可比。ORB-SLAM 这样的视觉 SLAM 系统，后端就用 g2o。The pose-graph formulation comes from Lu and Milios 1997; the tutorial by Grisetti et al. 2010 sets out the whole approach from the beginning. Real systems have thousands of poses, and what makes them solvable is sparsity: each measurement links only two poses, so almost all of Σ Jijᵀ Ωij Jij is zero. In the 120 × 120 matrix here, the non-zero entries form only a narrow band along the diagonal; with more loop closures, a few scattered blocks appear outside the band. Libraries such as g2o (2011), iSAM2 (2012, which updates only the affected part when a new measurement arrives; the GTSAM library includes an implementation) and Ceres do exactly this: the user writes how the difference is computed for each kind of measurement (with some libraries, its Jacobian too), and the library solves the sparse Gauss–Newton or Levenberg–Marquardt problem; Ceres can also work out the Jacobian automatically. This is the "sluggish direction" from inverse kinematics again: bending the whole path slowly all at once, with each pose moving only a little, barely changes the sum of squared errors, so the gradient barely feels it; but these small amounts add up over 40 steps, and the whole path ends up tens of centimetres off. Gauss–Newton solves for all directions at once, so this does not bother it.

把这一步串起来看：在机器人里，可微大多在为逆问题和控制服务：每个关节该转多少、我在哪、周围长什么样、两份数据怎么对齐、仿真器里的参数是多少、该发什么指令。旋钮千差万别，招式只有一个：预测、比较、把差别反向传回去。和训练神经网络比，有一点不一样：上面两个例子里，旋钮从几个到几万个，问题的结构又清楚，人们要的不只是梯度这一个方向，而是整张雅可比，好走牛顿类的步子，几步就到；神经网络有几百万个权重，每次又只看一小批数据，通常只沿着梯度一点点走。Taken together, this step shows that in robotics, differentiability mostly serves inverse problems and control: how far each joint should turn, where I am, what my surroundings look like, how two sets of data line up, what the parameters in the simulator are, what command to send. The knobs vary enormously, but there is only one trick: predict, compare, and pass the difference back. One thing differs from training a neural network: in the two examples above, with anywhere from a few knobs to tens of thousands and a clear structure to the problem, people want not just the gradient, a single direction, but the whole Jacobian, so that they can take Newton-type steps and get there in a few; a neural network has millions of weights and looks at only a small batch of data at a time, so it usually just inches along the gradient.

"Differentiable" is often not a fact but a design choice.
  • 最常见的坑不是"算不出导数"。按"哪里会断"分，方法最多的是"近似有偏"，其次是"只看得到附近"和"内存和算力"：导数算得出来，可它是对一个近似的、软化的、学出来的东西求的，或者只在附近有用，或者算得起却存不下、等不起。The most common pitfall is not "the derivative cannot be computed". Grouped by "Where it breaks", the largest group is "Biased approximations", followed by "Only sees nearby" and "Memory and compute": the derivative can be computed, but it is the derivative of something approximate, softened or learned, or it is only useful nearby, or it is affordable to compute but too big to store or too slow to wait for.

总结Summary

回到开头：各处说的"可微"，是一回事吗Back to the start: is "differentiable" the same thing everywhere?

核心是一回事，图的东西和难处不是。The core is the same; what it is for and what makes it hard are not.

核心是同一件事：放大看像直线（局部线性），一长串计算的导数等于每一步的导数相乘（链式法则），而反向模式让所有旋钮的导数一共只要几倍于算一遍的代价。三件事合在一起，"每个旋钮往哪拧、拧多少"在几百万、几千亿个旋钮上也算得起；任何零件只要可微，就能接进这条链里，和别的零件被同一个损失一起训练。所以人们到处都想"把它做成可微"。The core is one and the same: zoomed in, the curve looks like a straight line (locally linear); the derivative of a long chain of computations is the product of the derivatives of its steps (the chain rule); and reverse mode gets the derivatives for all the knobs at only a few times the cost of one pass. Together, these three make "which way to turn each knob, and by how much" affordable to compute even with millions or hundreds of billions of knobs; and any part that is differentiable can be plugged into this chain and trained together with the other parts by the same loss. That is why people everywhere want to "make it differentiable".

人们说"X 可微"，通常指三件事中的一件：一是数学上的，X 放大看像直线（旋钮多时像一块斜面）；二是工程上的，X 写进了能自动求导的框架，导数一键就能算出来；三是设计上的，X 被改造过（变软、加噪声、换成替身），算出来的导数才有用。神经网络三条都天然满足；可微渲染、可微仿真，主要在做第三条。When people say "X is differentiable", they usually mean one of three things. The first is mathematical: zoomed in, X looks like a straight line (or, with many knobs, like a tilted plane). The second is a matter of engineering: X is written in a framework that can differentiate automatically, so its derivatives can be computed at the press of a button. The third is a matter of design: X has been reworked (made soft, with noise added, or replaced by a stand-in), and only then are the derivatives you compute useful. Neural networks meet all three by nature; most of the work in differentiable rendering and differentiable simulation goes into the third.

Start with the "Why a gradient" column: there are really only three motives. Learn from data: neural networks, and letting discrete and random parts be trained along with the rest. Infer causes: rendering, localisation and registration, system identification, imaging. Control and design: simulation, world models, inverse kinematics, focusing sound waves. The benefit is the same everywhere: however many knobs there are, one forward pass and one reverse pass tell you which way to turn each one and by how much. What really differs is where the gradient comes from and where it can break, and in this respect differentiability plays three roles. In neural networks, it is the foundation: the parts are chosen to be differentiable in the first place, and without it, training is impossible. In rendering, simulation, the wave equation and robotics, it is a tool that has to be specially honed: the problems were there long before, but the physics is not always smooth (collisions, occlusion), so you have to make it soft or get the jumps right; and even when it is smooth, the gradient only sees what is nearby (local optima). Inverse kinematics and localisation in robotics are its oldest users, and what they want is not just the gradient but the whole Jacobian, so that they can take Newton-type steps. In world models, it is a free gift: the real world gives no derivatives, but a learned stand-in is differentiable by nature, so the gradient comes for free, though it is only as accurate as the stand-in.

其他几个问题Other questions

  • 什么是可微？放大看像一条直线：输入变一点，输出跟着按比例变，比例就是导数（第 0 步）。机器学习里说"可微"，通常还暗含一句：整条计算链上每一步都能求导，所以每个旋钮的导数都能自动算出来。至于算出来的导数有没有用，是另一回事（第 ④ 步）。What is differentiability? Zoomed in, the curve looks like a straight line: change the input a little and the output changes in proportion; the ratio is the derivative (step 0). In machine learning, "differentiable" usually implies one more thing: every step along the whole chain of computation can be differentiated, so the derivative for every knob can be computed automatically. Whether the derivatives you compute are actually useful is another matter (step ④).
  • 为什么这么重要？第一，导数同时给出方向和大小，不用再一次次瞎试（①）。第二，反向模式求所有旋钮的导数，代价只是算一遍的几倍，不会再乘上旋钮的个数，所以几千亿个旋钮也训练得动（②）。第三，可微的零件可以拼起来端到端地训练（③）。Why does it matter so much? First, the derivative gives both the direction and the size, so you no longer have to try blindly again and again (①). Second, reverse mode gets the derivatives for all the knobs at only a few times the cost of one pass, not that cost multiplied by the number of knobs, so even hundreds of billions of knobs can be trained (②). Third, differentiable parts can be put together and trained end to end (③).
  • 可微就够了吗？不够。"几乎处处可导"不等于"梯度有用"：碰撞附近梯度会是 0、会指错方向、会大得没有意义（④）；梯度只看得到附近：初值不好会卡在错的地方，靠近奇异位形时，按直线近似算出的一步会大得没有意义（⑧ 的逆运动学）；对替身求导，替身错的地方梯度也错（⑦）。Is being differentiable enough? No. "Differentiable almost everywhere" is not the same as "the gradient is useful": near a collision, the gradient can be 0, point the wrong way, or be meaninglessly large (④); the gradient only sees what is nearby: with a poor initial guess it gets stuck in the wrong place, and near a singular configuration a step computed from the linear approximation can be meaninglessly large (inverse kinematics in ⑧); and when you differentiate a stand-in, wherever the stand-in is wrong, the gradient is wrong too (⑦).
  • 不可微怎么办？把硬的变软（⑤、⑥），从带噪声的结果里估（④、⑥），学一个可微的替身（⑦），只对一小段求导（④），或者干脆不对环境求导：强化学习（从结果里估梯度）、进化算法、在模型里随机试一批再挑。What if it is not differentiable? Make the hard parts soft (⑤, ⑥), estimate from noisy outcomes (④, ⑥), learn a differentiable stand-in (⑦), differentiate only a short stretch (④), or avoid differentiating the environment altogether: reinforcement learning (estimating the gradient from outcomes), evolutionary algorithms, or trying a random batch in the model and picking the best.
  • 什么时候不该用梯度？旋钮很少、试一次又很便宜时，二分、网格搜索就够了；系统混沌、到处是跳变时，梯度可能比随机试还差；求一次导比多试几次还贵时，也不划算。When should you not use gradients? When there are few knobs and each try is cheap, bisection or a grid search is enough; when the system is chaotic and full of jumps, gradients may do worse than trying at random; and when computing one derivative costs more than a few extra tries, it is not worth it either.

小测验Quiz

有 1000 个旋钮、一个损失。用有限差分和用反向模式，各要算几遍？There are 1 000 knobs and one loss. How many passes do finite differences and reverse mode each need?

有限差分：原样一遍，每个旋钮各改一遍，一共 1001 遍正向。反向模式：一遍正向（把中间值存下来）加一遍反向，计算量一般是一遍正向的 2 到 3 倍，和旋钮个数无关；代价是要存中间值（第 ② 步）。Finite differences: one pass unchanged, plus one with each knob changed, 1 001 forward passes in all. Reverse mode: one forward pass (storing the intermediate values) plus one reverse pass; the work is usually 2 to 3 times that of one forward pass, however many knobs there are; the price is storing the intermediate values (step ②).

球打在近处筐壁的外侧时，局部梯度为什么说"再慢一点"？When the ball hits the outside of the near bin wall, why does the local gradient say "slower still"?

扔得稍慢一点，球打在筐壁上更低的地方，掉下来离筐中心反而近了一点点；局部梯度只看得到这一点点，于是让它更慢，正好背离了筐。它看不见"再快一点就能越过筐沿进筐"，因为那里隔着一个跳变（第 ④ 步）。Throw a little slower and the ball hits the wall lower down, and when it drops it actually ends up a tiny bit closer to the bin centre. The local gradient sees only this tiny bit, so it makes the throw slower still, which leads straight away from the bin. It cannot see "a little faster and it would clear the rim and go in", because a jump lies in between (step ④).

"进没进"对出手速度的导数几乎处处是 0，为什么 REINFORCE 还能估出有用的梯度？If the derivative of "in or not" with respect to the release speed is 0 almost everywhere, why can REINFORCE still estimate a useful gradient?

它根本不对结果求导，而是看结果和抖动的关系：进了的那几次，抖动平均偏向哪边，梯度就指向哪边。它估的其实是"带着抖动的进筐率"这个光滑函数的斜率；抖动要大到能偶尔碰到筐，否则一次都不进，估出来还是 0（第 ④、⑥ 步）。It never differentiates the outcome at all; instead, it looks at how the outcome relates to the jitter: whichever way the jitter leaned on average in the throws that went in, that is the way the gradient points. What it actually estimates is the slope of a smooth function, "the hit rate with jitter"; the jitter has to be large enough to reach the bin now and then, otherwise no throw goes in and the estimate is still 0 (steps ④ and ⑥).

重参数化和 REINFORCE 都能对一个平均值求导，什么时候用哪个？Reparameterisation and REINFORCE can both differentiate an average. When should you use which?

结果对随机变量可导（比如差的平方）时，用重参数化，方差小得多；结果不可导（比如 0/1），或者随机性来自一个你没法求导的环境时，只能用 REINFORCE，最好减一个基线。所以策略梯度一类的强化学习（REINFORCE、PPO）用后者；变分自编码器用前者，能对评论家或世界模型求导的（DDPG、Dreamer）也用前者（第 ⑥、⑦ 步）。When the outcome is differentiable with respect to the random variable (the squared miss, for example), use reparameterisation: its variance is much smaller. When the outcome is not differentiable (a 0/1 result, for example), or the randomness comes from an environment you cannot differentiate, REINFORCE is the only choice, preferably with a baseline subtracted. That is why policy-gradient reinforcement learning (REINFORCE, PPO) uses the latter, while variational autoencoders use the former, as do methods that can differentiate through a critic or a world model, such as DDPG and Dreamer (steps ⑥ and ⑦).

为什么硬边缘的渲染对物体位置的导数几乎处处是 0？有哪几种办法？Why is the derivative of hard-edged rendering with respect to an object’s position 0 almost everywhere? What can be done about it?

每个像素只看像素中心落没落在物体里，物体挪一点点，只要没有边缘跨过像素中心，画面就一个像素都不变；跨过的那一刻又突然变一整格。办法：把边缘画软（Soft Rasterizer），把边缘上的那一项导数单独算对（边缘采样），或者换一种天生就软的表示（NeRF 的体渲染、高斯泼溅）（第 ⑤ 步）。Each pixel only checks whether its centre falls inside the object. Move the object a little, and as long as no edge crosses a pixel centre, not a single pixel of the image changes; the moment one does cross, a whole pixel suddenly changes. The fixes: draw the edges soft (Soft Rasterizer), get the edge term of the derivative right on its own (edge sampling), or switch to a representation that is soft by nature (volume rendering in NeRF, Gaussian splatting) (step ⑤).

三维高斯泼溅里，哪一部分不是由梯度下降来调的？为什么？In 3D Gaussian splatting, which part is not adjusted by gradient descent? Why?

高斯色斑的个数。复制、分裂、删除色斑是离散的操作，没有导数，所以按规则来做：某个色斑的位置梯度长期很大，就复制或分裂它；几乎透明的，就删掉（第 ⑤ 步）。The number of Gaussian blobs. Copying, splitting and deleting blobs are discrete operations with no derivative, so they follow rules: if a blob’s position gradient stays large for a long time, copy or split it; if it is almost transparent, delete it (step ⑤).

学出来的世界模型天生可微，为什么很多方法规划时还是不对它求导？A learned world model is differentiable by nature. Why do many methods still not differentiate through it when planning?

梯度只和模型一样准；穿过模型往前想很多步，误差和第 ④ 步的跳变、混沌问题都会累积。在模型里随机试一批动作再挑最好的，往往更稳（上一篇第 ⑤ 步）。The gradient is only as accurate as the model; when you look many steps ahead through the model, both its errors and the jump and chaos problems of step ④ build up. Trying a random batch of actions in the model and picking the best is often more reliable (step ⑤ of the previous page).

机械臂快伸直时，为什么牛顿法（伪逆）会让关节一下转很多，梯度下降却不会？When a robot arm is nearly straight, why does Newton’s method (the pseudoinverse) make a joint turn a long way in one go, while gradient descent does not?

手臂伸直时，手沿手臂的方向几乎动不了，σmin 接近 0。牛顿法按直线近似直接解"差多少补多少"，要把这个方向上还差的距离除以 σmin，步子就大得没有意义。梯度下降沿 −Jᵀe 走，在这个方向上的推力跟着 σmin 一起变小，所以不会迈飞，只是越走越慢。阻尼最小二乘取中间：每个方向上关节转的角度，最多是还差的距离除以 2λ（第 ⑧ 步）。When the arm is straight, the hand can hardly move along the arm, and σmin is close to 0. Newton’s method solves the linear approximation directly, "make up whatever is missing", so it has to divide the distance still to go in that direction by σmin, and the step becomes meaninglessly large. Gradient descent steps along −Jᵀe, and its push in that direction shrinks along with σmin, so it does not fly off; it just gets slower and slower. Damped least squares sits in between: in each direction, the joints turn by at most the distance still to go divided by 2λ (step ⑧).

位姿图里，梯度下降走了 1000 步，误差平方和已经很小了，为什么整条路还歪着？In the pose graph, after 1000 steps of gradient descent the sum of squared errors is already small; why is the whole path still bent?

让整条路一起慢慢弯过去，每个位姿只挪一点点，误差平方和几乎不变，梯度几乎感觉不到这个方向；可这一点点沿着几十步加起来，整条路就差了几十厘米。高斯–牛顿把所有方向一起解（解一个线性方程组），所以两步就到底（第 ⑧ 步）。Bending the whole path slowly all at once, with each pose moving only a little, barely changes the sum of squared errors, so the gradient barely feels this direction; but these small amounts add up over dozens of steps, and the whole path ends up tens of centimetres off. Gauss–Newton solves for all directions at once (by solving one system of linear equations), so it reaches the bottom in two steps (step ⑧).
