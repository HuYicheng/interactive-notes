每次都偏一点A Little Off, Every Time

厨房机器人头上的相机拆下来擦过一次，再装回去以后，它去抓杯子时夹爪总是落在杯子的同一侧，偏几毫米。每次都偏，而且偏向同一边。这样的误差叫系统误差：多看几眼、多平均几次都去不掉，只能先找出它从哪来、有多大，再把它从模型里减掉。这件事叫标定。这一篇从头讲起：误差分哪两种，它们怎样沿着"像素 → 相机 → 深度 → 相机装在哪 → 手臂 → 夹爪"这条链一段段传下去，为什么说标定其实就是估参数，什么样的数据才估得出来。然后把链上的每一段拆开来标：相机的内参（小孔模型和张正友标定），深度相机，头上的相机在手臂的哪里（手眼标定），手臂自己的运动学，IMU，激光雷达和底盘，各个传感器的时钟，夹爪上的负载和工具。最后讲怎样验证标得好不好、标定什么时候会过期，再给这台机器人排一份标定计划，算一算标完以后够不够准。The kitchen robot’s head camera was taken off once to be cleaned, and since it was put back, whenever the robot goes to grasp a cup, the gripper always lands on the same side of it, a few millimetres off. It is off every time, and always off to the same side. An error like this is called a systematic error: looking a few more times or averaging a few more times will not get rid of it. The only way is to find out first where it comes from and how big it is, and then subtract it from the model. That is called calibration. This page starts from the beginning: what the two kinds of error are, how they are passed along the chain "pixel → camera → depth → where the camera is mounted → arm → gripper" one link at a time, why calibration is really just estimating parameters, and what kind of data it takes to estimate them. Then it takes the chain apart and calibrates each link in turn: the camera’s intrinsics (the pinhole model and Zhang’s method), the depth camera, where the head camera is relative to the arm (hand–eye calibration), the arm’s own kinematics, the IMU, the lidar and the base, the clocks of the various sensors, and the payload and tool on the gripper. Finally it covers how to check whether a calibration is good and when a calibration expires, then draws up a calibration plan for this robot and works out whether it will be accurate enough once calibrated.

从像素到指尖From pixel to fingertip

还是那台厨房机器人：差速底盘，底盘前面一台激光雷达，头上一台 RealSense D435i 深度相机（里面还带一颗 IMU），一条七个关节的 Franka Research 3 手臂，跑的是 ROS 2 Humble（第 15 篇）。它停在灶台前 (4.5, 3.3)，朝北，杯子 A 在底盘中心前方 0.85 m 的台面上，离相机约 0.8 m；夹爪最多张开 80 mm，杯子直径 60 mm，两边各留 10 mm 的余量。上一篇算过：看一眼就抓，夹住的概率是 0.889；底盘往前挪 25 cm、在 0.55 m 外再看一眼，升到 0.974。The same kitchen robot again: a differential-drive base, a lidar at the front of the base, a RealSense D435i depth camera on its head (with an IMU inside it as well) and a seven-joint Franka Research 3 arm, running ROS 2 Humble (page 15). It is parked in front of the hob at (4.5, 3.3), facing north, with cup A on the counter 0.85 m ahead of the centre of the base, about 0.8 m from the camera; the gripper opens to 80 mm at most and the cup is 60 mm in diameter, which leaves a 10 mm margin on each side. The previous page worked out that if the robot grasps after one look, the probability of getting hold of the cup is 0.889; if the base moves 25 cm forward and looks again from 0.55 m, it rises to 0.974.

上周有人把头上的相机拆下来擦了擦，换了根线，又装了回去。从那以后，它抓杯子 A 时常常抓不住，而且夹爪每次都往同一侧偏（往右），没抓住的那几次，都是左边的手指撞上了杯壁。一查，相机的支架装回去时转了 0.5°，还往旁边挪了 1 mm（示意）。机器人不知道这件事，还按原来的位置把相机看到的东西换算到手臂的坐标系里：0.55 m 外，0.5° 的转角带来 550 mm × tan 0.5° ≈ 4.80 mm 的偏差，再加上平移的 1 mm，一共 5.80 mm，每次都往同一边。成功率从 0.974 掉到 0.825：抓 20 次，平均失手的次数从 0.5 次变成 3.5 次（我们算的）。Last week someone took the head camera off, gave it a wipe, changed a cable and put it back on. Since then the robot has often failed to grasp cup A, and the gripper is off to the same side every time (to the right); in the grasps that failed, it was always the left finger that hit the cup wall. A check shows that when the camera’s bracket went back on, it was turned by 0.5° and shifted 1 mm to the side (illustrative). The robot knows nothing about this and still uses the old position to convert what the camera sees into the arm’s frame: at 0.55 m, the 0.5° rotation causes a bias of 550 mm × tan 0.5° ≈ 4.80 mm, and with the 1 mm of translation added, that makes 5.80 mm in all, to the same side every time. The success rate drops from 0.974 to 0.825: in 20 grasps, the average number of misses goes from 0.5 to 3.5 (our arithmetic).

上一篇的办法这时不够了。"再看一眼"靠的是凑近：离得近，随距离变大的误差都变小，这次的偏差也从 0.8 m 外的 7.98 mm 缩到 5.80 mm。可在同一个地方多看几眼再平均，压得下去的只是每次都不一样的那部分误差：检测框中心每帧抖几个像素，深度每帧差一点。这 5.80 mm 每次都一样，看多少眼都在。误差就这样分成两种：每次都不一样、平均起来为 0 的，叫随机误差；每次都一样、或者变得很慢的，叫系统误差，也叫偏差。对付前者靠多看、靠滤波（第 16 篇）；对付后者，得先找出它从哪来、有多大，再在模型里把它改掉。这件事叫标定，是这一篇的主角。The previous page’s remedy is no longer enough. "Look again" works by moving closer: from nearer, every error that grows with distance gets smaller, and this bias, too, shrinks from 7.98 mm at 0.8 m to 5.80 mm. But looking a few more times from the same spot and averaging only pushes down the part of the error that is different every time: the centre of the detection box jitters by a few pixels from frame to frame, and the depth is a little different in every frame. These 5.80 mm are the same every time; however many looks it takes, they are still there. So errors come in two kinds: the kind that is different every time and averages out to 0 is called random error; the kind that is the same every time, or changes only slowly, is called systematic error, or bias. The first is dealt with by looking more and by filtering (page 16); for the second, you first have to find out where it comes from and how big it is, and then correct it in the model. That is called calibration, and it is the main subject of this page.

机器人看到杯子到夹爪合上，中间要过这样一条链，每一段都是一个带参数的模型，参数错了，误差就从这里进来：Between the robot seeing the cup and the gripper closing on it lies a chain like this. Each link is a model with parameters, and if a parameter is wrong, that is where error gets in:

  • 像素：检测程序在彩色图上找到杯子，给出它中心的像素位置 (u, v)。Pixel: the detection program finds the cup in the colour image and gives the pixel position (u, v) of its centre.
  • 相机内参：焦距、主点、镜头畸变，把像素换成一个方向（第 ④ 步）。主点错了 4 个像素，0.8 m 外就差 3.44 mm，0.55 m 外 2.36 mm（我们算的）。Camera intrinsics: the focal length, the principal point and the lens distortion, which turn a pixel into a direction (step ④). With the principal point 4 pixels off, the error is 3.44 mm at 0.8 m and 2.36 mm at 0.55 m (our arithmetic).
  • 深度：深度图给出这一点离相机平面有多远（沿光轴的距离 Z，不是沿视线的距离），和方向一起定出这一点（第 ⑤ 步）。深度的比例错了 1%，0.8 m 外就差 8 mm，不过主要是前后方向。Depth: the depth image gives how far this point is from the camera plane (the distance Z along the optical axis, not the distance along the line of sight), and together with the direction this fixes the point (step ⑤). With the depth scale 1% off, the error is 8 mm at 0.8 m, though mostly in the forward–backward direction.
  • 相机外参：相机在手臂基座坐标系 fr3_link0 里的位姿 Tlink0_cam，把相机里的点换算到手臂的坐标系（第 ⑥ 步）。这次出事的就是这一段。Camera extrinsics: the pose Tlink0_cam of the camera in the arm’s base frame fr3_link0, which converts points in the camera frame into the arm’s frame (step ⑥). This is the link that went wrong this time.
  • 手臂运动学：手臂按自己的模型（每节连杆多长、每个关节的零位在哪）把关节角换算成夹爪的位置（第 ⑦ 步）。第 1 个关节的零位差 0.1°，0.6 m 外就差 1.05 mm；凑近再看一眼以后，杯子离手臂底座只有 0.35 m，是 0.61 mm（我们算的）。Arm kinematics: the arm uses its own model (how long each link is, where each joint’s zero is) to turn joint angles into the gripper’s position (step ⑦). With joint 1’s zero 0.1° off, the error is 1.05 mm at 0.6 m; after moving closer and looking again, the cup is only 0.35 m from the arm’s base, and it is 0.61 mm (our arithmetic).
  • 夹爪：工具中心点 TCP 到底在法兰前面多远，抓着东西时负载多重（第 ⑪ 步）。Gripper: how far in front of the flange the tool centre point (TCP) really is, and how heavy the load is when it is holding something (step ⑪).

链外还有几样东西也要标：IMU 的零偏和标度（第 ⑧ 步），激光雷达装在底盘的哪里、轮子的半径和轮距（第 ⑨ 步），各个传感器的时钟差了多少（第 ⑩ 步）。这一篇要回答九个问题：A few things outside the chain need calibrating too: the IMU’s bias and scale factor (step ⑧); where the lidar is mounted on the base, and the wheel radius and track width (step ⑨); and how far apart the various sensors’ clocks are (step ⑩). 两种误差Two kinds of error

先把"误差"说清楚：一个读数减去它想量的那个真值，就是这个读数的误差。同一件事量很多次，误差有两种表现。一种每次都不一样，有正有负，平均起来为 0：检测框中心这一帧偏左两个像素，下一帧偏右一个；深度这一帧多 1 mm，下一帧少 1 mm。另一种每次都一样，或者在几分钟、几天里才慢慢变：相机支架转了 0.5°，看到的每一个东西都往同一边挪。前一种是随机误差（噪声），后一种是系统误差（偏差）。First, let us be clear about what "error" means: a reading minus the true value it is trying to measure is that reading’s error. Measure the same thing many times and the error shows up in two ways. One kind is different every time, sometimes positive and sometimes negative, and averages out to 0: in one frame the centre of the detection box is two pixels to the left, in the next one pixel to the right; in one frame the depth reads 1 mm long, in the next 1 mm short. The other kind is the same every time, or changes only slowly, over minutes or days: the camera bracket has turned by 0.5°, and everything the camera sees is shifted to the same side. The first is random error (noise); the second is systematic error (bias).

z = x + b + vz̄n = (z1 + … + zn) / n  ⇒  E[z̄n] = x + b,  σ(z̄n) = σ / √n

一个读数等于真值，加上偏差，再加上噪声。把 n 个读数平均起来，平均值的期望是真值加偏差，偏差原封不动；噪声的标准差缩成原来的 1/√n。A reading equals the true value, plus the bias, plus the noise. Average n readings, and the expected value of the average is the true value plus the bias: the bias is left untouched, while the standard deviation of the noise shrinks to 1/√n of what it was.

噪声每次重新"抽"一次，互相独立：n 个独立噪声加起来，方差是 nσ²，除以 n 以后方差变成 σ²/n，标准差就是 σ/√n。偏差在每个读数里都是同一个 b，加起来是 nb，除以 n 还是 b。所以多看、多平均只对付得了噪声；要去掉偏差，得知道它是多少，从读数里减掉，这就是标定。The noise is "drawn" afresh each time, independently: add up n independent noises and the variance is nσ²; divide by n and the variance becomes σ²/n, so the standard deviation is σ/√n. The bias is the same b in every reading: added up it is nb, and divided by n it is still b. So looking more and averaging more can only deal with the noise; to remove the bias, you have to know how big it is and subtract it from the readings, and that is calibration.

同一个误差算哪一种，要看你怎么重复。相机外参差了 0.2°，对这一台机器人是偏差，每次都一样；对一百台机器人，或者对"我们不知道它差了多少"这件事，又可以把它当成 σ = 0.2° 的随机量。第 16 篇的误差预算（第 ⑯ 步）就是这么做的。可平均只压得下"每次重新抽"的那部分。更一般地，n 个读数两两的相关系数都是 ρ 时（ρ 在第 ② 步定义），平均值的方差是 σ²[ρ + (1 − ρ)/n]：ρ = 0 时缩成 σ²/n；ρ = 0.5 时，平均再多次，σ 也只降到 0.707σ；ρ = 1 时一点不降。偏差就是"和自己完全相关"的那部分误差。Which kind an error counts as depends on how you repeat. Camera extrinsics that are 0.2° off are a bias for this one robot, the same every time; but across a hundred robots, or as a way of saying "we don’t know how far off it is", the same error can be treated as a random quantity with σ = 0.2°. The error budget of page 16 (step ⑯) did exactly that. Averaging, though, only beats down the part that is "drawn afresh each time". More generally, if every pair of the n readings has the same correlation coefficient ρ (ρ is defined in step ②), the variance of the average is σ²[ρ + (1 − ρ)/n]: with ρ = 0 it shrinks to σ²/n; with ρ = 0.5, however many times you average, σ only falls to 0.707σ; with ρ = 1 it does not fall at all. Bias is the part of the error that is "perfectly correlated with itself".

两种误差合在一起，衡量"总共差多少"常用均方根误差（RMS）：先平方，再平均，再开方。To measure "how far off in total" with both kinds of error together, the usual choice is the root-mean-square error (RMS): square, then average, then take the square root.

RMS² = E[(z − x)²] = b² + σ²

误差平方的平均值，等于偏差的平方加上噪声的方差。The average of the squared error equals the square of the bias plus the variance of the noise.

把 z − x 写成 b + v：(b + v)² = b² + 2bv + v²。取平均时，v 的平均是 0，交叉项 2bv 没了，v² 的平均就是 σ²。所以总误差里，偏差和散布各占一块，互不抵消；只盯着散布小，偏差大的仪器照样不准。Write z − x as b + v: (b + v)² = b² + 2bv + v². When you take the average, v averages to 0, so the cross term 2bv drops out, and the average of v² is just σ². So bias and scatter each take their own share of the total error, and neither cancels the other: however small its scatter, an instrument with a large bias is still inaccurate.

这就是为什么国际计量学词汇 VIM 把"准"拆成两半：无穷多次重复测量的平均值离参考值多近叫正确度（trueness），它只和系统误差有关，和随机误差无关；重复测量之间彼此多接近叫精密度（precision）；两者都好，才叫准确度（accuracy）高（VIM 2.14、2.15、2.13）。日常说的"准不准"，往往把这两件事混在一起。This is why the International Vocabulary of Metrology, VIM, splits "being accurate" into two halves: how close the average of infinitely many repeated measurements is to a reference value is called trueness, and it depends only on systematic error, not on random error; how close repeated measurements are to one another is called precision; only when both are good is the accuracy high (VIM 2.14, 2.15, 2.13). In everyday speech, "is it accurate?" often lumps these two things together.

机器人手臂也分这两种。工业机器人的性能测试有一个国际标准 ISO 9283：让手臂反复去同一个命令位置，一般 30 次，量出每次实际到的位置，再算两个数（ISO 9283 的说明）：A robot arm’s errors come in the same two kinds. There is an international standard for testing the performance of industrial robots, ISO 9283: send the arm to the same commanded position again and again, usually 30 times, measure where it actually gets to each time, and then compute two numbers (an ISO 9283 explainer):

AP = ‖x̄ − xc‖RP = l̄ + 3 Sl,  lj = ‖xj − x̄‖

绝对定位精度 AP，是 30 次落点的平均位置离命令位置有多远；重复定位精度 RP，是每次落点离平均位置的距离的平均值，再加上这些距离的标准差的 3 倍。Pose accuracy AP is how far the average of the 30 landing points is from the commanded position; pose repeatability RP is the average distance of each landing point from that average position, plus 3 times the standard deviation of those distances.

AP 量的是偏差：平均位置偏离命令多少。RP 量的是散布：每次落点围着平均位置散多开，加上 3 倍标准差，让绝大多数落点都在这个半径以内。两者用的是同样的 30 个落点，回答的是两个不同的问题。AP measures the bias: how far the average position strays from the command. 规格书为什么只写重复精度？工业机器人传统上是"示教"出来的：人用示教器把手臂一点点挪到要去的地方，记下这时的关节角，以后照着这组关节角重放。这样用，模型准不准无所谓，只要每次回得去，所以重复精度最要紧。可这台厨房机器人是"看到哪里、算出关节角、再去那里"，每一步都用模型，要的是绝对精度。Universal Robots 干脆在出厂时给每台手臂单独标定，并在它的 ROS 驱动的文档里提醒：不把这份标定读出来用上，末端的位置"可能差到厘米量级"（UR 驱动文档）。Why does the datasheet give only the repeatability? Industrial robots have traditionally been "taught": a person uses a teach pendant to move the arm, little by little, to where it needs to go, the joint angles at that moment are recorded, and from then on the robot replays that set of joint angles. Used this way, it does not matter whether the model is accurate, only that the arm gets back there every time, so repeatability is what matters most. This kitchen robot, though, works by "see where it is, work out the joint angles, go there": every step uses the model, so what it needs is absolute accuracy. Universal Robots goes as far as calibrating every arm individually at the factory, and the documentation of its ROS driver warns that unless this calibration is read out and used, end-effector positions "might be off in the magnitude of centimeters" (UR driver docs).

偏差还有一个反直觉的地方。偏差小于余量时，散布越小越好：b = 5.8 mm 时，σ 从 6.28 降到 4.49 mm，成功率从 0.742 升到 0.825。可偏差一旦超过余量，只要散布不太大（b = 12 mm 时 σ 小于 10 mm），散布越小反而越糟：b = 12 mm 时，σ 从 6.28 降到 4.49 mm，成功率从 0.375 降到 0.328；σ 降到 1 mm，只剩 0.023（我们算的）。偏得比余量还多时，原来是噪声偶尔把夹爪"推回"杯子上，让它碰巧抓住；看得越清楚，每次就越是稳稳地落在杯子外面。这一页的成功率都按 P = Φ((10 − b)/σ) − Φ((−10 − b)/σ) 算：横向误差是均值 b、标准差 σ 的高斯分布，落在 ±10 mm 以内的概率。There is one more counter-intuitive thing about bias. While the bias is smaller than the margin, the smaller the scatter the better: with b = 5.8 mm, as σ falls from 6.28 to 4.49 mm, the success rate rises from 0.742 to 0.825. But once the bias goes beyond the margin, then as long as the scatter is not too large (for b = 12 mm, σ below 10 mm), a smaller scatter actually makes things worse: with b = 12 mm, as σ falls from 6.28 to 4.49 mm, the success rate drops from 0.375 to 0.328, and with σ down to 1 mm only 0.023 is left (our arithmetic). When the bias is bigger than the margin, it is the noise that now and then "pushes" the gripper back onto the cup, so that it grasps it by luck; the more clearly the robot sees, the more surely it lands outside the cup every time. All success rates on this page are computed as P = Φ((10 − b)/σ) − Φ((−10 − b)/σ): the probability that a lateral error, Gaussian with mean b and standard deviation σ, falls within ±10 mm.

这一步的要点：误差分两种：每次都不一样的随机误差，用 σ 描述，平均 n 次缩成 σ/√n；每次都一样的系统误差（偏差），平均多少次都还在。总误差的平方等于偏差的平方加上方差。手臂的重复精度（RP）和绝对精度（AP）是两回事：FR3 重复得准到 0.1 mm，按名义模型去新地方却可能差一厘米。看到哪里再去哪里的机器人，要的是绝对精度，这就要标定。The point of this step: there are two kinds of error. Random error, different every time, is described by σ, and averaging n times shrinks it to σ/√n; systematic error (bias), the same every time, is still there however many times you average. The square of the total error equals the square of the bias plus the variance. An arm’s repeatability (RP) and its absolute accuracy (AP) are two different things: the FR3 repeats to within 0.1 mm, yet going somewhere new by its nominal model it can be a centimetre off. A robot that goes wherever it sees something needs absolute accuracy, and that takes calibration.

误差怎么传How errors propagate

知道了链上某一段错了多少，下一个问题是：到杯子那里，会差多少？最简单的情形只有一个量。结果 y 由一个量 x 算出来，x 错了一点 δx，y 就跟着错 (dy/dx)·δx。这个 dy/dx 叫灵敏度：x 变一点，y 变几倍。Once you know how far off a link in the chain is, the next question is: how far off will the robot be at the cup? The simplest case has only one quantity. A result y is computed from a quantity x; if x is off by a small δx, then y is off by (dy/dx)·δx in turn. This dy/dx is called the sensitivity: how many times as much y changes when x changes a little.

δy ≈ (dy/dx) · δxδs ≈ d · δθ

结果的误差，约等于灵敏度乘以输入的误差。一个特例最常用：角度错了 δθ（以弧度计），离转动中心 d 远的点就横着挪了 d·δθ。The error in the result is roughly the sensitivity times the error in the input. One special case is used most often: if an angle is off by δθ (in radians), a point at distance d from the centre of rotation moves sideways by d·δθ.

误差很小时，曲线在一小段上近似是直线，斜率就是导数，这是泰勒展开只留第一项。转角的情形，是半径 d 上转过 δθ 弧度对应的弧长 d·δθ；角很小时，弧长、弦长和 d·tan δθ 几乎一样。所以转角的误差要乘上一段距离，这段距离叫杠杆臂：离得越远，同样的角度差得越多。When the error is small, a curve is close to a straight line over a short stretch, and its slope is the derivative: this is a Taylor expansion that keeps only the first-order term. For a rotation, it is the arc length d·δθ covered by turning through δθ radians at radius d; for small angles, the arc length, the chord length and d·tan δθ are almost the same. So a rotation error has to be multiplied by a distance, and that distance is called the lever arm: the farther away, the bigger the miss for the same angle.

结果往往由好几个量一起算出来：杯子的位置由像素位置、内参、深度、相机外参、关节角和手臂模型一起决定。每个量都有自己的灵敏度，叫灵敏度系数。偏差和随机误差这时要分开算：偏差带着正负号，按灵敏度加起来，可能互相抵消，也可能叠在一起；随机误差互不相关时，按方差加起来。A result is usually computed from several quantities together: the cup’s position is set jointly by the pixel position, the intrinsics, the depth, the camera extrinsics, the joint angles and the arm model. Each quantity has its own sensitivity, called its sensitivity coefficient. Biases and random errors now have to be handled separately: biases carry their signs and add up, each scaled by its sensitivity, so they may cancel each other or pile up; random errors, when they are uncorrelated, add in variance.

δy ≈ c1 δp1 + c2 δp2 + … ,  ci = ∂y / ∂piσy² ≈ c1² σ1² + c2² σ2² + …Σy ≈ J Σp JT

几个参数各错了一点，结果的偏差是各自的灵敏度乘以各自的误差，再带着正负号加起来。它们是互不相关的随机误差时，结果的方差是灵敏度的平方乘以各自的方差，再加起来。结果不止一个数（比如杯子的 x、y、z）时，写成矩阵：结果的协方差矩阵等于雅可比矩阵乘以参数的协方差矩阵，再乘以雅可比矩阵的转置。When several parameters are each a little off, the bias in the result is each sensitivity times its own error, added up with their signs. When they are uncorrelated random errors, the variance of the result is each sensitivity squared times its own variance, added up. When the result is more than one number (such as the cup’s x, y and z), write it with matrices: the covariance matrix of the result equals the Jacobian matrix times the covariance matrix of the parameters, times the transpose of the Jacobian.

每个参数都很小时，各自的影响可以叠加（线性化）。偏差是确定的数，所以带符号相加。随机误差相加时，平方展开以后有交叉项 2cicjδpiδpj，它的平均是 2cicj 乘以两者的协方差；只要两两不相关（协方差为 0），交叉项就没了，只剩平方项。不要求独立（独立是更强的条件），也不要求是高斯分布。参数之间有相关时，交叉项留下，矩阵的写法把它们一起装了进去：Σp 非对角线上的就是参数之间的协方差。这就是测量不确定度表示指南 GUM 里的"不确定度传播律"（GUM，JCGM 100:2008）。When each parameter is only a little off, their effects can simply be added up (linearisation). Biases are definite numbers, so they add with their signs. When random errors are added, squaring out the sum gives cross terms 2cicjδpiδpj, whose average is 2cicj times the covariance of the two; as long as every pair is uncorrelated (covariance 0), the cross terms vanish and only the squared terms remain. This needs neither independence (a stronger condition) nor a Gaussian distribution. When the parameters are correlated, the cross terms stay, and the matrix form takes them in as well: the off-diagonal entries of Σp are the covariances between the parameters. This is the "law of propagation of uncertainty" in GUM, the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM, JCGM 100:2008).

加的是平方，所以总误差几乎由最大的一两项决定；把一个小项减半，几乎没用。标定的力气要花在大项上。而偏差带着符号：两个偏差可以在某一点正好抵消，这一点看着很准，换个地方就不一定了。It is the squares that add, so the total error is set almost entirely by the largest one or two terms; halving a small term hardly helps. The calibration effort should go into the big terms. Biases, on the other hand, carry signs: two biases can cancel exactly at one point, which then looks very accurate, but somewhere else that need not hold.

"按方差相加"要的条件，可以画成一幅几何图：The condition behind "adding in variance" can be drawn as a piece of geometry:

σ² = σ1² + σ2² + 2ρ σ1 σ2,  ρ = Cov(e1, e2) / (σ1 σ2)

两项随机误差加在一起，方差等于两者的方差之和，再加上两倍的相关系数乘以两个标准差；相关系数是两者的协方差除以两个标准差。When two random errors are added together, the variance is the sum of their variances plus twice the correlation coefficient times the two standard deviations; the correlation coefficient is their covariance divided by the two standard deviations.

把每一项平均为 0 的误差看成一个向量：两个向量的"内积"取协方差，那么每个向量的长度就是它的 σ，相关系数 ρ 就是两个向量夹角的余弦，总误差是两个向量的和，上面的式子就是余弦定理。不相关（ρ = 0）就是统计意义上的"垂直"，这时退回勾股定理 σ² = σ1² + σ2²，几项也一样：两两垂直，就是 σ² = Σσi²。ρ = 1 时两个向量同向，σ = σ1 + σ2，就是"最坏情况"那样直接相加；ρ = −1 时反向，σ = |σ1 − σ2|，互相抵消。Think of each error term with mean 0 as a vector, and take the covariance as the "inner product" of two vectors. Then the length of each vector is its σ, the correlation coefficient ρ is the cosine of the angle between the two vectors, the total error is the sum of the two vectors, and the formula above is the law of cosines. Uncorrelated (ρ = 0) means "perpendicular" in the statistical sense, and the formula falls back to Pythagoras’ theorem, σ² = σ1² + σ2²; the same goes for several terms: if every pair is perpendicular, σ² = Σσi². With ρ = 1 the two vectors point the same way and σ = σ1 + σ2, a direct sum just like the "worst case"; with ρ = −1 they point opposite ways, σ = |σ1 − σ2|, and they cancel each other.

这里的"垂直"是统计意义上的，和误差朝哪个空间方向无关：那五项都按横向算（深度那一项其实主要是前后方向，第 16 篇把它整个算进横向，偏保守），方向一样，能按平方相加，是因为它们来自互不相干的原因。同一次标定估出来的几个参数常常是相关的：一个大一点、另一个小一点，在标定的那片地方照样对得上（第 ③ 步那个斜着的椭圆）。反过来，空间上互相垂直的两个误差（比如横向的 ex 和前后的 ey），不管相关不相关，总误差长度平方的平均总是 E[ex²] + E[ey²]；相关只改变散布的形状（椭圆斜不斜）。The "perpendicular" here is in the statistical sense and has nothing to do with which way in space the errors point: the five terms are all counted as lateral (the depth term is actually mostly fore–aft; page 16 counted all of it as lateral, which is conservative), so they point the same way, and they can be added in squares because they come from unrelated causes. Several parameters estimated in the same calibration are often correlated: one a little too large and another a little too small still fit just as well over the region where the calibration was done (the tilted ellipse of step ③). Conversely, for two errors that are perpendicular in space (such as the lateral ex and the fore–aft ey), the mean squared length of the total error is always E[ex²] + E[ey²], correlated or not; correlation changes only the shape of the scatter (whether the ellipse is tilted).

线性化有用的前提是误差小、模型在这一小段上近似是直线。角度大、模型弯得厉害时（第 16 篇第 ⑦ 步的"香蕉"），可以改用蒙特卡洛：按各个参数的分布随机抽很多组，每一组都用完整的模型算一遍，看结果散成什么样。Linearisation is only useful if the errors are small and the model is close to a straight line over that short stretch. 这一步的要点：误差沿着链传下去时，乘的是灵敏度：转角乘距离，像素乘"每像素多少毫米"。几个偏差带着符号相加，几个互不相关的随机误差按方差相加（矩阵写法是 J Σ JT），这是统计意义上的勾股定理，相关时要加上交叉项；总误差由最大的一两项决定。同一个错了的参数在不同地方造成的误差不一样，在一个点上对准只修好了那一点；要修好整片地方，得把参数本身找出来。The point of this step: as an error passes down the chain, it is multiplied by a sensitivity: rotation angles by a distance, pixels by "millimetres per pixel". Several biases add with their signs; several uncorrelated random errors add in variance (in matrix form, J Σ JT), which is Pythagoras’ theorem in the statistical sense, and when they are correlated a cross term has to be added; the total error is set by the largest one or two terms. The same wrong parameter causes different errors in different places, so zeroing it at one point fixes only that point; to fix the whole region, you have to find the parameter itself.

第 ③ 步 · 让模型和量到的东西对上Step ③ · Making the model match what you measured

标定就是估参数Calibration is parameter estimation

链上的每一段都是一个模型：从已知的东西和一组参数，算出读数应该是多少。相机的模型从三维点和内参算出像素；深度的模型从真实距离和比例、偏移算出深度读数；手臂的模型从关节角和连杆参数算出夹爪的位置。标定就是：拿一些"真值已知"的东西去量，再找一组参数，让模型算出来的读数和实际量到的读数对得最好。已知的东西叫参照物：格子尺寸量得出来的标定板，用卷尺量过距离的一面墙，地球的重力，或者手臂自己报出来的关节角。Every link in the chain is a model: from things you know and a set of parameters, it works out what the reading should be. The camera model works out pixels from 3D points and the intrinsics; the depth model works out the depth reading from the true distance, a scale and an offset; the arm model works out where the gripper is from the joint angles and the link parameters. Calibration is this: measure some things whose "true value is known", then find the set of parameters for which the readings worked out by the model agree best with the readings actually measured. The known things are called references: a calibration board whose squares can be measured, a wall whose distance has been measured with a tape measure, the Earth’s gravity, or the joint angles the arm reports itself.

zi = h(xi; θ) + viθ̂ = argminθ Σi (zi − h(xi; θ))² / σi²

第 i 个读数等于模型在已知量 xi 和参数 θ 下算出的值，加上噪声。标定出来的参数，是让"读数减模型"的平方和最小的那一组；每一项除以这个读数的方差，让准的读数说话分量重一些。Reading i equals the value the model works out for the known quantity xi and the parameters θ, plus noise. The calibrated parameters are the set that makes the sum of the squares of "reading minus model" smallest; each term is divided by the variance of its reading, so that the more accurate readings get more of a say.

噪声是高斯分布时，让这个加权平方和最小的参数，正好是"最可能产生这些读数"的参数（最大似然）。h 是参数的非线性函数时，没有一步到位的公式，就从一个初值出发反复改：在当前的 θ 附近把 h 线性化，h ≈ h(θ0) + J·Δθ，解一个线性最小二乘得到这一步该改多少，改完再线性化，直到不再变小。这叫高斯–牛顿法；每一步再加一点"别走太远"的阻尼，就是 Levenberg–Marquardt（LM）法，绝大多数标定程序用的都是它。When the noise is Gaussian, the parameters that minimise this weighted sum of squares are exactly the parameters "most likely to have produced these readings" (maximum likelihood). When h is a nonlinear function of the parameters, there is no formula that gets there in one step, so you start from an initial guess and improve it over and over: linearise h around the current θ, h ≈ h(θ0) + J·Δθ, solve a linear least-squares problem to find the change for this step, make the change, linearise again, and repeat until the sum stops getting smaller. This is the Gauss–Newton method; add to each step a little damping that says "don’t go too far", and you have the Levenberg–Marquardt (LM) method, which is what the great majority of calibration programs use.

读数的噪声要是算错了（比如把每个读数都当成一样准），估出来的参数不一定错得离谱，但它的 σ 会算错，后面判断"标得够不够好"就没了依据。If you get the noise of the readings wrong (for example, by treating every reading as equally accurate), the estimated parameters will not necessarily be badly off, but their σ will be wrong, and later on you have nothing to go on when judging whether the calibration is "good enough".

估出来的参数自己也有误差：读数里的噪声，会通过最小二乘传到参数上。线性化以后，可以直接写出参数的协方差，它同时回答了两个问题：每个参数估得多准，以及这些数据到底能不能把参数一个个分开。The estimated parameters have errors of their own: the noise in the readings is carried through the least squares into the parameters. After linearising, you can write down the covariance of the parameters directly, and it answers two questions at once: how accurately each parameter is estimated, and whether these data can actually separate the parameters from one another at all.

参数的协方差矩阵，约等于"雅可比矩阵的转置乘以权重再乘以雅可比矩阵"的逆。用标出来的参数去预测某个新情况 x 下的读数，预测的方差是这一点的灵敏度向量夹着参数的协方差。The covariance matrix of the parameters is approximately the inverse of "the transpose of the Jacobian times the weights times the Jacobian". When you use the calibrated parameters to predict the reading in some new situation x, the variance of the prediction is the parameters’ covariance with the sensitivity vector at that point on either side.

JTWJ 量的是数据里装着多少关于参数的信息：读数越多、越准、对参数越敏感，它越大，逆过来参数的方差就越小。要是两列 J 差不多成比例，也就是两个参数对读数的影响几乎一样，这个矩阵就接近奇异：一个变大、另一个变小，读数几乎不变，数据分不开它们。这时两个参数的相关系数接近 ±1，各自的 σ 很大。能不能分开叫可辨识性；为了分开而让数据铺开、让机器人往不同方向动，叫激励。矩阵最大和最小特征值之比叫条件数，越大越接近分不开；它和参数用什么单位有关，要先把各列缩放到差不多大再算。JTWJ measures how much information about the parameters the data contain: the more readings there are, the more accurate they are and the more sensitive they are to the parameters, the larger it is, and so, once inverted, the smaller the variances of the parameters. If two columns of J are nearly proportional, that is, if two parameters affect the readings in almost the same way, this matrix is close to singular: make one larger and the other smaller and the readings hardly change, so the data cannot separate them. The correlation coefficient of the two parameters is then close to ±1, and each one’s σ is large. Whether they can be separated is called identifiability; spreading the data out, making the robot move in different directions, so that they can be separated, is called excitation. The ratio of the matrix’s largest eigenvalue to its smallest is called the condition number; the larger it is, the closer the parameters are to being impossible to separate. It depends on the units the parameters are in, so first scale the columns to roughly the same size, then compute it.

参数各自不准，不等于预测不准：在标定时量过的地方，它们一个大一个小地互相弥补，预测照样准；离开那片地方，弥补不上了，误差才暴露出来。所以标定数据要覆盖将来要用的那片范围，宁可铺开，不要挤在一处。Inaccurate parameters on their own do not mean inaccurate predictions: where you measured during calibration, one too large and the other too small make up for each other, and the prediction is still accurate; away from there they can no longer make up for each other, and only then does the error show. 不是每次都有现成的参照物。没有已知的东西时，还可以靠一致性：同一个运动，被两个传感器各自量了一遍，它们必须对得上（第 ⑥ 步的手眼标定、第 ⑨ 步的激光雷达）；或者机器人干活时，让滤波器顺便把参数也估出来（第 ⑬ 步）。不管哪一种，底下都是同一件事：一个带参数的模型，一堆数据，一个最小二乘，和"这些数据分得开这些参数吗"这个问题。There is not always a ready-made reference. 相机：一个像素对着哪个方向The camera: which way a pixel points

链的第一段，是把图上的一个像素换成空间里的一个方向。最常用的相机模型是小孔模型：所有光线都穿过一个点（光心），落在它后面的成像平面上。在相机坐标系里（光心是原点，x 向右，y 向下，z 朝前，OpenCV 和 RealSense 都这么定），一个点 (X, Y, Z) 落在图上的位置是：The first link in the chain turns a pixel in the image into a direction in space. The most widely used camera model is the pinhole model: all rays of light pass through one point (the optical centre) and land on the image plane behind it. In the camera frame (origin at the optical centre, x to the right, y down, z forward, as both OpenCV and RealSense define it), a point (X, Y, Z) lands in the image at:

u = fx · X / Z + cx,  v = fy · Y / Z + cyλ [u, v, 1]T = K [X, Y, Z]T,  K = [[fx, 0, cx], [0, fy, cy], [0, 0, 1]]

一个点在图上的横坐标，是它的横向位置除以深度、乘以焦距、再加上主点的横坐标；纵坐标同理。写成矩阵，就是内参矩阵 K 乘以这个点，再除以它的深度。A point’s horizontal coordinate in the image is its sideways position divided by its depth, times the focal length, plus the horizontal coordinate of the principal point; the vertical coordinate works the same way. As a matrix, it is the intrinsic matrix K times the point, divided by its depth.

X/Z 是这个点的方向（相似三角形：离得越远，同样的横向偏移在图上越小）；乘以 f 把它换成像素；cx、cy 是光轴落在图上的位置，因为像素坐标从左上角数起，不从中心数起。反过来，知道 (u, v) 只能知道方向 ((u − cx)/fx, (v − cy)/fy, 1)，距离要靠深度来补（第 ⑤ 步）。X/Z is the point’s direction (similar triangles: the further away it is, the smaller the same sideways offset looks in the image); multiplying by f turns it into pixels; cx and cy are where the optical axis lands in the image, because pixel coordinates are counted from the top-left corner, not from the centre. Going the other way, knowing (u, v) tells you only the direction ((u − cx)/fx, (v − cy)/fy, 1); the distance has to come from depth (step ⑤).

内参是对某一个分辨率说的。图缩小一半，fx、fy、cx、cy 全要减半；换了分辨率还用原来那组，就是一个很大的系统误差。自动对焦和变焦的镜头，每对一次焦内参都会变一点。Intrinsics belong to one particular resolution. Halve the image size, and fx, fy, cx and cy must all be halved too; change the resolution but keep using the old set, and you have a large systematic error. With autofocus and zoom lenses, the intrinsics change a little every time the lens focuses.

真实的镜头不是完美的小孔，会把直线拍弯：离图中心越远弯得越厉害，这叫径向畸变；镜片和传感器没装得完全平行，还会有一点切向畸变。OpenCV 用下面的式子描述它们，先在"归一化坐标"上加畸变，再乘焦距、加主点（OpenCV calib3d）：A real lens is not a perfect pinhole, and it photographs straight lines as curves: the further from the centre of the image, the more they bend. This is called radial distortion; if the lens and the sensor are not mounted perfectly parallel, there is also a little tangential distortion. OpenCV describes them with the formulas below, first adding the distortion in "normalised coordinates", then multiplying by the focal length and adding the principal point (OpenCV calib3d):

xn = X / Z,  yn = Y / Z,  r² = xn² + yn²xd = xn(1 + k1r² + k2r⁴ + k3r⁶) + 2p1xnyn + p2(r² + 2xn²)u = fx xd + cx

先把点的方向写成归一化坐标，算出它离光轴多远（r）；径向畸变按 r 的偶次幂把它往外推或往里拉，切向畸变再加一点不对称的挪动；最后照常乘焦距、加主点。y 方向同理（p1、p2 的位置互换）。First write the point’s direction in normalised coordinates and work out how far it is from the optical axis (r); radial distortion pushes it outwards or pulls it inwards by even powers of r, and tangential distortion adds a small asymmetric shift; finally, multiply by the focal length and add the principal point as usual. The y direction works the same way (with p1 and p2 swapping places).

镜头大致是绕光轴对称的，所以径向的挪动只和 r 有关，又因为对称，只出现 r 的偶次幂。光轴附近 r 很小，畸变几乎为 0；图的边角 r 最大，畸变也最大。这就是为什么标定数据一定要覆盖到图的边角：只在中间拍，k1、k2 几乎没有被"看到"（第 ⑫ 步）。A lens is roughly symmetric about the optical axis, so the radial shift depends only on r, and, again because of the symmetry, only even powers of r appear. Near the optical axis r is small and the distortion is almost 0; at the edges and corners of the image r is largest, and so is the distortion. That is why calibration data must reach the edges and corners of the image: shoot only in the middle and k1 and k2 are hardly "seen" at all (step ⑫).

参数越多，越能贴住标定数据，也越容易在没拍到的地方乱来（第 ⑫ 步）。普通镜头用五个系数通常就够；鱼眼镜头要换专门的模型。The more parameters there are, the more closely the model can hug the calibration data, and the more easily it goes wild where you took no photos (step ⑫). For an ordinary lens, five coefficients are usually enough; a fisheye lens needs a dedicated model.

内参怎么标？1998 年张正友提出的办法今天几乎人人在用：拿一块平的棋盘格，换几个角度对着相机拍几张，就够了，不需要事先知道棋盘格放在哪（Zhang，2000；技术报告）。关键是一个观察：棋盘格是平的，把它自己的坐标系定在板上，所有角点的 Zb = 0。于是投影少了一列，变成一个 3 × 3 的矩阵：How do you calibrate the intrinsics? Almost everyone today uses the method Zhengyou Zhang proposed in 1998: take a flat chessboard, hold it in front of the camera at a few different angles and take a few photos, and that is enough; you do not need to know beforehand where the chessboard is (Zhang, 2000; technical report). The key is one observation: the chessboard is flat, so if you fix its own coordinate frame on the board, every corner has Zb = 0. The projection then loses a column and becomes a 3 × 3 matrix:

λ [u, v, 1]T = K [r1 r2 t] [Xb, Yb, 1]T = H [Xb, Yb, 1]Th1T B h2 = 0,  h1T B h1 = h2T B h2,  B = K−T K−1

一张照片里，棋盘格上的点和它们在图上的位置之间，是一个 3 × 3 的变换 H，叫单应矩阵，它等于 K 乘以"板的两根轴和板的位置"。因为板的两根轴互相垂直、长度都是 1，H 的前两列 h1、h2 满足两个方程，方程里只有一个和 K 有关的矩阵 B。In one photo, the points on the chessboard and their positions in the image are related by a 3 × 3 transformation H, called a homography, which equals K times "the board’s two axes and the board’s position". Because the board’s two axes are perpendicular to each other and both have length 1, the first two columns of H, h1 and h2, satisfy two equations in which K appears only through a single matrix, B.

每张照片的 H 都能从它的角点直接算出来（至少 4 个点），只是只能算到差一个倍数 μ。r1 = K−1h1/μ，r2 = K−1h2/μ，代进 r1Tr2 = 0 和 |r1| = |r2|，μ 正好消掉，就得到上面两个方程。张正友的 K 还多一个斜切项，这时 B 是对称矩阵，6 个数，只能确定到一个比例，相当于 5 个未知数；每张照片给 2 个方程，所以至少要 3 张。像上面那样假设没有斜切，B 少一个数，2 张就够。先用这些方程闭式地解出 B，再分解出 K，再算出每张照片里板的位姿 [R | t]；最后把所有照片、所有角点放在一起，连同畸变系数，用 LM 法最小化重投影误差（第 ③ 步）。Each photo’s H can be computed directly from its corners (at least 4 points), but only up to a factor μ. Substitute r1 = K−1h1/μ and r2 = K−1h2/μ into r1Tr2 = 0 and |r1| = |r2|, and μ cancels out exactly, giving the two equations above. Zhang’s K has one more term, for skew; B is then a symmetric matrix with 6 numbers, determined only up to a scale, which amounts to 5 unknowns; each photo gives 2 equations, so at least 3 photos are needed. Assume no skew, as above, and B has one number fewer, so 2 photos are enough. First solve these equations for B in closed form, then decompose it to get K, then work out the board’s pose [R | t] in each photo; finally put all the photos and all the corners together, along with the distortion coefficients, and minimise the reprojection error with the LM method (step ③).

In the simulations, the more photos, the smaller the errors in the parameters, with the biggest improvement from 2 photos to 3; yet with the real photos the reprojection error was smallest with 2 photos (0.30 px), and 0.39 px with 3, because it is a training error. An angle of around 45° between the board and the image plane worked best; at only 5°, the boards in the 3 photos were almost parallel to one another, and 40% of the trials failed (Zhang’s technical report).

板平移而不转，或者几张照片里的板互相平行，新照片就不带新信息：方程重复了。正对着拍的照片还有一个更隐蔽的问题：板离得远一点，和焦距短一点，在图上看起来几乎一样，f 和距离分不开。斜着拍，同一块板近的那头大、远的那头小，透视的变化才把 f 暴露出来。If the board is moved without being turned, or the boards in several photos are parallel to one another, a new photo brings no new information: the equations just repeat. Photos taken square-on have a subtler problem too: a board a little further away looks almost the same in the image as a focal length a little shorter, so f and the distance cannot be separated. Shoot at an angle, and the near end of the same board looks bigger and the far end smaller; it is this change in perspective that gives f away.

标得好不好，最常看的数是重投影误差：The number people look at most often to judge a calibration is the reprojection error:

RMS = √( (1/N) Σi,j ‖uij − π(K, d, Ri, ti, Xj)‖² )

这是一个训练误差：它只说明模型贴住了这些照片，不说明它在别的地方也准（第 ⑫ 步）。照片都挤在图中间时，它可以很小，图边上的误差却很大。This is a training error: it only shows that the model fits these photos closely, not that it is accurate elsewhere too (step ⑫). When the board sits in the middle of the image in every photo, it can be very small while the error at the edges of the image is large.

所以真正要紧的，是拍什么样的照片。ROS 2 的 camera_calibration 工具在界面上画四根条：X、Y、Size、Skew。X、Y 是板在图上的左右、上下位置铺开了多少；Size 是板在图上最大占到多大（够不够近），Skew 是板最斜的时候有多斜。四根条都满了（变绿），或者收够 40 张，"标定"按钮才会亮（camera_calibration 教程；calibrator.py 源码）。要领就是：板要出现在图的左右上下和四个角上，要有近有远，要往不同方向倾斜。So what really matters is what kind of photos you take. The ROS 2 camera_calibration tool draws four bars in its window: X, Y, Size and Skew. X and Y show how widely the board’s position in the image has been spread from left to right and from top to bottom; Size shows the largest the board has appeared in the image (whether it has been close enough), and Skew how far it has been tilted at its most. Only when all four bars are full (they turn green), or 40 photos have been collected, does the "CALIBRATE" button light up (camera_calibration tutorial; calibrator.py source). The knack is this: the board should appear on the left, right, top and bottom of the image and in all four corners, near and far, and tilted in different directions.

人拍的照片好不好，差别大得惊人。2013 年密歇根大学做过一个实验：16 个人各自用 OpenCV 把同一台相机标 4 次，每次拍 10–16 张；结果焦距的标准差，是用他们的 AprilCal（每一步告诉你下一张该把板放在哪）标出来的 7.5 倍，主点的标准差是 2.4 倍；按最坏的地方算，OpenCV 的标定平均差约 38 px，AprilCal 约 1.6 px，而 AprilCal 通常 6–8 张照片就能把误差压到 1 个像素以下。作者特别提醒：大家报的重投影误差是"训练误差，而不是测试误差"（Richardson、Strom、Olson，2013）。How good people’s photos are varies astonishingly widely. In 2013, researchers at the University of Michigan ran an experiment: 16 people each calibrated the same camera 4 times with OpenCV, taking 10–16 photos each time; the standard deviation of the focal length came out 7.5 times that of calibrations made with the researchers’ own AprilCal (which tells you at each step where to put the board for the next photo), and that of the principal point 2.4 times; measured at the worst place, the OpenCV calibrations were off by about 38 px on average and AprilCal by about 1.6 px, and AprilCal usually gets the error below 1 pixel with 6–8 photos. The authors make a point of warning that the reprojection errors people report are "training error, not test error" (Richardson, Strom and Olson, 2013).

第 ⑤ 步 · 会随距离长的偏差Step ⑤ · A bias that grows with distance

深度：差多少，要看多远Depth: how far off depends on how far away

知道了方向，还要知道多远。D435i 是双目深度相机：左右两个红外相机相距 B = 50 mm（基线），同一个点在两张图上的横坐标差 d 个像素，这个差叫视差。离得越远，视差越小。相似三角形给出深度：Once we know the direction, we still need to know how far. The D435i is a stereo depth camera: its left and right infrared cameras are B = 50 mm apart (the baseline), and the same point’s horizontal position in the two images differs by d pixels. This difference is called the disparity. The farther away the point, the smaller the disparity. Similar triangles give the depth:

z = f B / dσz ≈ z² σd / (f B),  δz ≈ − z² δd / (f B)

深度等于焦距乘基线，除以视差。视差有一点随机误差 σd，深度的随机误差约是距离的平方乘以它，再除以焦距和基线的乘积；视差有一个固定的偏移 δd，深度就有一个同样随距离平方增长的偏差，方向相反（视差读大了，深度就读小了）。Depth equals the focal length times the baseline, divided by the disparity. If the disparity has a little random error σd, the random error in depth is roughly the distance squared times that, divided by the product of the focal length and the baseline. If the disparity has a fixed offset δd, the depth has a bias that likewise grows with the square of the distance, in the opposite direction (read the disparity too large and the depth reads too small).

对 z = fB/d 求导：dz/dd = −fB/d² = −z²/(fB)。远处的点视差小，视差差一点点，对应的距离就差很多，所以深度的误差，不管随机的还是固定的，都随距离的平方涨。基线 B 或焦距 f 本身错了 1%，深度就整体差 1%，那是一个比例误差，随距离线性涨。Differentiate z = fB/d: dz/dd = −fB/d² = −z²/(fB). A distant point has a small disparity, so a tiny difference in disparity means a large difference in distance. That is why depth errors, random or fixed, grow with the square of the distance. If the baseline B or the focal length f is itself 1% wrong, every depth is 1% off: that is a scale error, and it grows linearly with distance.

D400 系列的规格是：2 m 以内，绝对误差（Z-accuracy）和 RMS 误差都不超过距离的 2%，而且只对装好、做过整机标定的模组成立（RealSense 支持文章）。2 m 外的 2% 是 40 mm；要是全来自视差偏移，那是 0.32 px，同一个偏移在 0.8 m 外只差 6.40 mm（0.8%），在 0.55 m 外 3.02 mm（我们算的）。所以"2%"不是一个固定的比例：在近处，按视差偏移的模型，它会小得多。The D400 series spec says: within 2 m, the absolute error (Z-accuracy) and the RMS error are both at most 2% of the distance, and this holds only for modules that have been assembled and calibrated as a whole unit (RealSense support article). 深度为什么会偏？RealSense 自己的说明是：经历过剧烈的冷热循环，或者过大的冲击和振动，标定会变差；内参的误差主要来自镜片位置的微小移动，外参的误差来自固定两个传感器的那根"加强梁"微小的弯曲和扭转（RealSense 自标定说明）。左右两个相机的相对位姿错了一点，视差就整体偏一点，深度的偏差便随距离平方长。Why would depth be off? RealSense’s own explanation: after extreme temperature cycling, or excessive shock and vibration, the calibration degrades; errors in the intrinsics come mainly from microscopic shifts in the lens positions, and errors in the extrinsics from slight bending and twisting of the "stiffener" that holds the two sensors (RealSense self-calibration guide). If the relative pose of the left and right cameras is a little off, every disparity is shifted by a little, and so the depth bias grows with the square of the distance.

检查深度最简单的办法是平面测试：让相机对着一面平整的墙，用卷尺量好墙到深度原点的距离（深度从左红外相机的光心算起，D435i 上它在前盖玻璃后面约 4.2 mm，从玻璃量会凭空多出 4 mm 的"偏差"）。把深度图里墙上的点拟合成一个平面：点离这个平面的 RMS 是噪声，说明深度"平不平"（精密度）；平面到相机的距离和卷尺量的距离之差，是偏差（正确度）。两件事要分开看：一台噪声很小的相机，可能整体偏了 6 mm。The simplest way to check depth is the plane test: point the camera at a flat wall and measure the distance from the depth origin to the wall with a tape measure (depth starts at the optical centre of the left infrared camera, which on the D435i sits about 4.2 mm behind the front cover glass; measure from the glass and you get an extra 4 mm of "bias" out of nowhere). Fit a plane to the wall’s points in the depth image: the RMS distance of the points from this plane is the noise, which tells you how "flat" the depth is (precision); the difference between the plane’s distance from the camera and the tape-measured distance is the bias (trueness). Keep the two apart: a camera with very little noise may still be off by 6 mm across the board.

RealSense 为这两件事各准备了一个工具（RealSense 自标定说明）：RealSense provides one tool for each of the two (RealSense self-calibration guide):

  • 片上自标定（On-Chip Self-Calibration）：在相机里跑 0.7–3 s，改善的是精密度，也就是噪声和平整度。它只用视场中间 20% 的区域，一次只能修内参或外参中的一种。它给出一个"健康值"：绝对值小于 0.25 说明几乎处在最佳状态，大于 0.25 重标能变好，大于 1 基本不能用了。On-Chip Self-Calibration: runs inside the camera in 0.7–3 s and improves precision, that is, the noise and flatness. It uses only the central 20% of the field of view and can correct either the intrinsics or the extrinsics, but only one of them at a time. It gives a "health-check value": an absolute value below 0.25 means the calibration is nearly optimal, above 0.25 a recalibration will improve things, and above 1 the camera is essentially unusable.
  • Tare 标定：对着一个距离已知的平面，修的是绝对距离。说明里写道，修正绝对距离"通常要同时修斜率和偏移"。Tare calibration: with the camera facing a flat target at a known distance, it corrects the absolute distance. The guide notes that correcting absolute distance "usually involves correcting both the slope and offset".

两者算出的新标定只是先生效，不会自动写进相机的存储器：比较过、觉得好，再写进去；出厂标定随时可以恢复。With both, the newly computed calibration takes effect only provisionally and is not written to the camera’s memory automatically: compare first, and write it in only if you are happy with it; the factory calibration can be restored at any time.

这又回到第 ③ 步：一个已知距离，只给一个方程，只能修一个数。比如真实的情况是视差偏移 0.1 px，外加 −0.5% 的比例误差（示意）：不修正时，0.55 m、0.8 m、2.0 m 外分别差 −3.69、−5.99、−22.36 mm。只在 0.8 m 处对一个已知距离、只修视差偏移，0.8 m 处变成 0，0.55 m 处剩 −0.86 mm，2.0 m 处还剩 +15.19 mm，只比不修正时的 −22.36 mm 小了三分之一，方向还反了（我们算的）。在常用的距离上做，就在常用的距离上准；要在整段距离上都准，至少要两个距离，最好是一串。This brings us back to step ③: one known distance gives only one equation, so it can correct only one number. Say the truth is a disparity offset of 0.1 px plus a −0.5% scale error (illustrative). Without correction, the errors at 0.55 m, 0.8 m and 2.0 m are −3.69, −5.99 and −22.36 mm. Calibrate against a single known distance at 0.8 m, correcting only the disparity offset: 0.8 m becomes 0, 0.55 m is left with −0.86 mm, and 2.0 m still has +15.19 mm, only a third smaller than the −22.36 mm without correction, and now off to the other side (our arithmetic). Calibrate at the distances you use most and you will be accurate at those distances; to be accurate over the whole range you need at least two distances, and preferably a whole series.

对这台机器人来说，深度的误差主要沿着视线，也就是杯子的前后和上下，横向几乎不受影响：横向的那 5.80 mm 不是它造成的。可前后差了几毫米，夹爪会合得早一点或晚一点；上下差了，夹爪会抓得高一点或低一点。For this robot, depth errors lie mainly along the line of sight, which at the cup means front–back and up–down; the sideways direction is hardly affected, so the 5.80 mm sideways error is not caused by depth. But a few millimetres off front to back, and the gripper closes a little early or a little late; off up or down, and it grasps a little high or a little low.

手眼：相机在手臂的哪里Hand–eye: where the camera is relative to the arm

相机看到的点在相机坐标系里，手臂要的是手臂基座坐标系 fr3_link0 里的点。中间差一个固定的变换：相机在手臂基座坐标系里的位姿 Tlink0_cam。URDF 里写着设计图上的数，可相机支架装得歪一点，这个数就错了，这一次就是它错了 0.5° 和 1 mm。求这个变换叫手眼标定（"手"是手臂，"眼"是相机）。The points the camera sees are in the camera frame, but the arm wants points in its base frame, fr3_link0. In between is one fixed transform: the camera’s pose in the arm’s base frame, Tlink0_cam. The URDF holds the value from the design drawings, but if the camera bracket is mounted slightly crooked, that value is wrong, and this time that is exactly what went wrong: it is off by 0.5° and 1 mm. Finding this transform is called hand–eye calibration (the "hand" is the arm, the "eye" is the camera).

相机有两种装法。装在手臂末端、跟着手一起动的，叫眼在手上（eye-in-hand），要求的是相机在法兰上的位姿，标定时把标定板固定在桌上。装在别处、不跟着手动的，叫眼在手外（eye-to-hand），要求的是相机在手臂基座里的位姿，标定时把一个标记贴在手上。这台机器人的头部相机固定在底盘的支架上，和手臂基座之间没有关节，属于眼在手外：在夹爪上装一个 ArUco 标记，让手臂摆十几个姿势，每个姿势记下两样东西：There are two ways to mount the camera. Mounted on the end of the arm so that it moves with the hand, it is called eye-in-hand: what you want is the camera’s pose on the flange, and the calibration board is fixed on the table while you calibrate. Mounted somewhere else so that it does not move with the hand, it is called eye-to-hand: what you want is the camera’s pose in the arm’s base frame, and a marker is stuck on the hand while you calibrate. This robot’s head camera is fixed to a bracket on the base, with no joint between it and the arm’s base, so it is eye-to-hand: put an ArUco marker on the gripper, have the arm take a dozen or so poses, and at each pose record two things:

  • 手臂说的：法兰在基座里的位姿 Fi = Tlink0_flange，由关节角和手臂的模型算出来（正运动学）。What the arm says: the flange’s pose in the arm’s base frame, Fi = Tlink0_flange, computed from the joint angles and the arm’s model (forward kinematics).
  • 相机看到的：标记在相机里的位姿 Mi = Tcam_marker，由标记四个角在图上的位置和它已知的尺寸算出来（PnP）。What the camera sees: the marker’s pose in the camera frame, Mi = Tcam_marker, computed from where the marker’s four corners appear in the image and its known size (PnP).

未知的有两个：要求的 X = Tlink0_cam，和标记贴在法兰上的位姿 Y = Tflange_marker（贴的时候没量准，也当成未知）。从基座出发走两条路到标记，结果必须一样：There are two unknowns: the one we want, X = Tlink0_cam, and the marker’s pose on the flange, Y = Tflange_marker (it was not measured precisely when the marker was put on, so it is treated as unknown too). Two routes from the base to the marker must give the same result:

Fi Y = X Mi  ⇒  A X = X BA = Fj Fi−1,  B = Mj Mi−1

基座经过手臂到法兰、再到标记，等于基座经过相机到标记。拿两个姿势 i、j 相比，消掉未知的 Y，剩下一个只含 X 的方程：手臂在两个姿势之间的相对运动 A，乘以 X，等于 X 乘以相机看到的标记的相对运动 B。Going from the base through the arm to the flange and on to the marker is the same as going from the base through the camera to the marker. Compare two poses i and j, eliminate the unknown Y, and you are left with an equation in X alone: the arm’s relative motion between the two poses, A, times X, equals X times the marker’s relative motion as the camera sees it, B.

从第一个式子解出 Y = Fi−1 X Mi，它对每个姿势都一样，所以 Fi−1 X Mi = Fj−1 X Mj，左乘 Fj、右乘 Mi−1 就得到 AX = XB。直观地说：A 和 B 是同一个运动，一个在手臂基座里看，一个在相机里看，X 就是把一个坐标系换成另一个的那个变换。眼在手上的情形，换一下 A、B 的定义，得到同一个方程。这个方程最早由 Shiu 和 Ahmad 写出（1987 年的会议论文，1989 年登在期刊上；Shiu、Ahmad，1989）。Solving the first equation for Y gives Y = Fi−1 X Mi, which is the same for every pose, so Fi−1 X Mi = Fj−1 X Mj; multiplying by Fj on the left and by Mi−1 on the right gives AX = XB. Intuitively: A and B are the same motion, one seen in the arm’s base frame and one seen in the camera frame, and X is the transform that converts one frame into the other. For eye-in-hand, change the definitions of A and B and you get the same equation. The equation was first written down by Shiu and Ahmad (a conference paper in 1987, published in a journal in 1989; Shiu and Ahmad, 1989).

RA RX = RX RB  ⇒  α = RX β(RA − I) tX = RX tB − tA

每一对 A、B 给出一对转轴 α、β，它们之间差一个 RX。一对转轴只能把 RX 定到"还能绕这根轴转"的程度，所以至少要两个转轴不平行的运动，也就是至少 3 个姿势（OpenCV 文档的原话是：至少要两个转轴不平行的运动，强烈建议用多得多的姿势；OpenCV；Horaud、Dornaika，1995）。有了很多对转轴，就像第 ③ 步那样用最小二乘找最合适的 RX（Park 和 Martin 1994 年给出了这个解法；Park、Martin，1994）。平移方程里的 RA − I，沿着转轴方向是 0，在垂直于转轴的方向上，大小是 2 sin(θ/2)。转得越少，这个系数越小，解平移时要除以它，误差就被放大 1/(2 sin(θ/2)) 倍；沿着转轴的那个方向，这次运动对平移一点信息都没有。Each pair A, B gives a pair of axes α, β, which differ by RX. A single pair of axes pins RX down only as far as "it can still turn about this axis", so you need at least two motions whose rotation axes are not parallel, that is, at least 3 poses (in the OpenCV documentation’s own words: a minimum of two motions with non-parallel rotation axes is needed, and it is strongly recommended to use many more poses; OpenCV; Horaud and Dornaika, 1995). With many pairs of axes, find the best-fitting RX by least squares, as in step ③ (Park and Martin gave this solution in 1994; Park and Martin, 1994). In the translation equation, RA − I is 0 along the rotation axis and has magnitude 2 sin(θ/2) perpendicular to it. The less the hand turns, the smaller this coefficient; solving for the translation means dividing by it, so the error is amplified by a factor of 1/(2 sin(θ/2)); and along the rotation axis, this motion tells you nothing at all about the translation.

两个姿势之间只转 5°：平移的误差放大 11.46 倍，相机的 PnP 差 1 mm，X 的平移就可能差 1 cm 多；转 30°，放大 1.93 倍；60°，1.00 倍；90°，0.71 倍（我们算的）。Turn only 5° between two poses and the translation error is amplified 11.46 times: if the camera’s PnP is off by 1 mm, X’s translation can be off by more than 1 cm; turn 30° and it is amplified 1.93 times; 60°, 1.00 times; 90°, 0.71 times (our arithmetic).

所以手眼标定时，手要大幅度地转，而且绕不同的轴转。easy_handeye2（ROS 2 上常用的手眼标定包）的建议几乎就是这几个式子的翻译：末端尽量多转，每根轴都转到 90° 左右、两个方向都转；平移不必要，姿势之间的平移尽量小；标记尽量靠近相机；先把相机内参和手臂标好（easy_handeye2）。So in hand–eye calibration the hand has to turn a lot, and about different axes. The advice of easy_handeye2 (a hand–eye calibration package commonly used on ROS 2) reads almost like a translation of these equations: rotate the end effector as much as possible, to about 90° about each axis and in both directions; translation is not needed, and the translation between poses should be as small as possible; keep the marker as close to the camera as possible; calibrate the camera intrinsics and the arm first (easy_handeye2).

解法有好几种。Tsai 和 Lenz 1989 年的方法先解转动、再解平移（Tsai、Lenz，1989）；Park 和 Martin 用李群的办法解转动；Daniilidis 1999 年用对偶四元数把转动和平移一起解（Daniilidis，1999）；Horaud 和 Dornaika 1995 年给出了一种闭式解和一种非线性优化的解法，后者更准。OpenCV 的 calibrateHandEye 五种都有（TSAI、PARK、HORAUD、ANDREFF、DANIILIDIS）；calibrateRobotWorldHandEye 不先消掉 Y，而是把 X 和 Y 一起解（SHAH、LI 两种方法）（OpenCV）。更彻底的做法是把这些当初值，最后直接最小化标记角点的重投影误差，让相机的测量误差按像素计入。There are several ways to solve it. Tsai and Lenz’s 1989 method solves the rotation first and then the translation (Tsai and Lenz, 1989); Park and Martin solve the rotation with Lie-group methods; Daniilidis in 1999 used dual quaternions to solve the rotation and translation together (Daniilidis, 1999); in 1995 Horaud and Dornaika gave a closed-form solution and a solution by non-linear optimisation, the latter being more accurate. OpenCV’s calibrateHandEye offers five methods (TSAI, PARK, HORAUD, ANDREFF, DANIILIDIS); calibrateRobotWorldHandEye does not eliminate Y first but solves for X and Y together (two methods, SHAH and LI) (OpenCV). A more thorough approach uses these as initial values and finally minimises the reprojection error of the marker corners directly, so that the camera’s measurement error counts in pixels.

手眼标定还有一个要当心的地方：A 用的是手臂的名义模型。手臂本身有几毫米的误差时，标出来的 X 会顺手"吸收"一部分：在标定时手经过的那一片地方，相机加手臂这条链对得很好，离开那一片就不一定了，这和第 ② 步的单点修正是一回事。所以要在将来干活的那片地方做手眼标定，更好的是先把手臂自己标好（第 ⑦ 步）。标完以后，留几个没用来标定的姿势做检验：按 Fk、X、Y 预测标记该出现在哪，和相机实际看到的比（第 ⑫ 步）。Hand–eye calibration has one more thing to watch out for: A uses the arm’s nominal model. When the arm itself has errors of a few millimetres, the calibrated X quietly "absorbs" part of them: in the region the hand passed through during calibration, the camera-plus-arm chain lines up very well, but away from that region it may not, which is the same thing as the one-point correction of step ②. So do the hand–eye calibration in the region where the robot will work, and better still, calibrate the arm itself first (step ⑦). Afterwards, keep a few poses that were not used for calibration as a check: predict from Fk, X and Y where the marker should appear, and compare with what the camera actually sees (step ⑫).

回到故事：重装相机以后重做一次手眼标定。下面的交互里（PnP 的误差按沿视线 1 mm、转角 0.2° 示意），手绕两根轴各转 ±45°、12 个姿势，X 本身的转角误差约 0.20°、平移约 2.19 mm，可在杯子 A 那里（标记就举在那儿）合起来只差 1.50 mm：同一次标定估出的转角和平移误差互相抵着，这正是第 ② 步说的相关；随机 15 个、转到 ±60° 的姿势，杯子 A 处只差 0.83 mm（交互里算的）。所以看手眼标定好不好，要看它在干活的地方差多少，不要只看 X 的转角和平移各自差多少。Back to the story: after the camera is remounted, the hand–eye calibration is redone. 这一步的要点：手眼标定求的是相机和手臂之间那个固定的变换 X。手臂说的位姿和相机看到的位姿，走两条路到同一个标记，消掉标记的位置以后得到 AX = XB。转动部分要两个转轴不平行的运动才定得下来；平移部分要除以 2 sin(θ/2)，转得少，误差被放大，沿转轴的方向根本没有信息。所以要大幅度地、绕不同的轴转，标记靠近相机，先标好内参和手臂，在将来干活的地方标，再拿没用过的姿势检验。The point of this step: Hand–eye calibration finds the fixed transform X between the camera and the arm. The pose the arm reports and the pose the camera sees are two routes to the same marker; once the marker’s position is eliminated, you get AX = XB. The rotation part can only be pinned down by two motions with non-parallel axes; the translation part has to be divided by 2 sin(θ/2), so small turns amplify the error, and along the rotation axis there is no information at all. So turn a lot, about different axes, keep the marker close to the camera, calibrate the intrinsics and the arm first, calibrate where the robot will work, and check with poses that were not used.

真实的手臂和这张表不会完全一样：编码器的零点和关节真正的零位差一点（零位误差），连杆加工和装配差几十微米，关节轴不是严格垂直或平行；连杆和减速器在重力下会弯一点、扭一点；温度变了，铝合金每米每度胀 23 µm 左右，0.855 m 的臂展热 10 °C 就长约 0.20 mm（我们算的）。1987 年 Roth 等人把手臂的标定分成三级：第一级只修关节层面（编码器读数和关节真实角度的关系，比如零位）；第二级修整个运动学模型的几何参数；第三级再加上非几何的因素，比如刚度、柔性和摩擦（Roth、Mooring、Ravani，1987）。A real arm will never match this table exactly: the encoder’s zero is slightly off from the joint’s true zero (zero-offset error), machining and assembly leave the links off by tens of micrometres, and the joint axes are not exactly perpendicular or parallel; the links and gearboxes bend and twist a little under gravity; and when the temperature changes, aluminium alloy expands by about 23 µm per metre per degree, so a 0.855 m reach that warms by 10 °C grows by about 0.20 mm (our arithmetic). In 1987 Roth et al. divided arm calibration into three levels: the first corrects only the joint level (the relation between encoder readings and the true joint angles, such as zero offsets); the second corrects the geometric parameters of the whole kinematic model; the third also adds non-geometric factors such as stiffness, compliance and friction (Roth, Mooring and Ravani, 1987).

Measure many poses, stack them up and solve for the parameter errors by least squares.

参数误差很小时，夹爪的误差和它们是线性关系；这个雅可比矩阵 Jp 不是平常控制里用的那个（对关节角求导），而是对模型参数求导，叫辨识雅可比。它和关节角有关，所以每个姿势给出不同的几行方程，姿势越多、越分散，方程越"互不相同"，参数越分得开。解出来的 δp 加到名义参数上，再线性化、再解，几轮就收敛（第 ③ 步的高斯–牛顿）。When the parameter errors are small, the gripper error is linear in them. This Jacobian Jp is not the one used in everyday control (derivatives with respect to the joint angles) but the derivative with respect to the model parameters, and it is called the identification Jacobian. It depends on the joint angles, so each pose gives a few different rows of equations; the more poses there are and the more spread out they are, the more "different from one another" the equations are, and the better the parameters can be separated. Add the solved δp to the nominal parameters, linearise again, solve again, and it converges in a few rounds (the Gauss–Newton of step ③).

夹爪的位置要用手臂以外的东西来量：激光跟踪仪最准，也最贵；可以用一台标定好的相机；可以让工具尖去碰几个已知位置的点；前面提到的 2025 年那项工作只用一个 3D 打印的、带两个球窝的小工具，在采数据时约束住末端，就把 Franka 的平均绝对误差从 10 mm 量级降到 0.2 mm（Franzese 等，2025）。The gripper’s position has to be measured with something other than the arm: a laser tracker is the most accurate and also the most expensive; you can use a calibrated camera; you can have the tool tip touch a few points at known positions; the 2025 work mentioned earlier used nothing but a small 3D-printed tool with two ball sockets to constrain the end effector while collecting data, and brought the Franka’s mean absolute error down from the order of 10 mm to 0.2 mm (Franzese et al., 2025).

参数不是想估多少就估多少。有些参数做的是同一件事：底座绕竖直轴转一个小角度，和第 1 关节的零位差同样的角度，夹爪在所有姿势下挪得一模一样，数据永远分不开它们，只能估它们的和；把两个都放进最小二乘，矩阵就是奇异的。对一条只有转动关节和移动关节的串联手臂，能独立辨识的几何参数最多是 4r + 2p + 6 个，r、p 是转动和移动关节的数目，6 是基座（或工具）的位姿（Chen、Wang、Lin，2014）。FR3 有 7 个转动关节：4 × 7 + 6 = 34 个（我们算的）。只量位置、不量姿态时，能辨识的还更少。You cannot estimate as many parameters as you like. Some parameters do the same job: turning the base by a small angle about the vertical axis and putting joint 1’s zero off by the same angle move the gripper in exactly the same way in every pose, so the data can never separate them and only their sum can be estimated; put both into the least squares and the matrix is singular. For a serial arm with only revolute and prismatic joints, at most 4r + 2p + 6 geometric parameters can be identified independently, where r and p are the numbers of revolute and prismatic joints and the 6 is the pose of the base (or tool) (Chen, Wang and Lin, 2014). The FR3 has 7 revolute joints: 4 × 7 + 6 = 34 (our arithmetic). If you measure only position and not orientation, even fewer can be identified.

DH 参数还有一个老毛病：相邻两根关节轴接近平行时，其中一个参数会变得极大，模型变得病态，一点点轴的不平行就要用一个巨大的 d 来描述（NASA 的 Barker 1986 年就写过；Barker，1986）。所以标定时常换一种参数化，比如给近乎平行的那对轴多加一个小转角（Hayati 的修改）。FR3 相邻的轴在名义上都互相垂直，可不少手臂有平行的轴：Universal Robots 的第 2、3、4 个关节轴就互相平行。UR 在出厂时给每台手臂单独标定，存在控制器里；它的 ROS 驱动文档说，用这个驱动控制手臂时这一步不是必须的，但强烈建议先把这份标定读出来，生成这台手臂自己的参数文件，否则末端"可能差到厘米量级"（UR 驱动文档）。DH parameters also have an old weakness: when two adjacent joint axes are nearly parallel, one of the parameters becomes extremely large and the model becomes ill-conditioned, so even a slight departure from parallel has to be described by a huge d (NASA’s Barker wrote about this back in 1986; Barker, 1986). So calibration often switches to a different parameterisation, for example adding an extra small rotation angle for the nearly parallel pair of axes (Hayati’s modification). The FR3’s adjacent axes are all nominally perpendicular, but many arms have parallel axes: on Universal Robots arms, the axes of joints 2, 3 and 4 are parallel to one another. UR calibrates each arm individually at the factory and stores the result in the controller; its ROS driver docs say that this step is not necessary to control the arm with the driver, but that it is strongly recommended to read this calibration out first and generate a parameter file for that particular arm, as otherwise end-effector positions "might be off in the magnitude of centimeters" (UR driver docs).

标出来的参数怎么用上？取决于谁在用模型。如果是自己的程序（比如 MoveIt 用 URDF 算逆运动学）算出关节角再发给手臂，那么把 URDF 换成标定后的参数就行：关节角是按真实的手臂算的。如果用的是控制器内部的笛卡尔指令，它用的是控制器自己的模型，就只能在发出去之前把目标"反着挪一下"：先用标定后的模型算出该去的关节角，再按名义模型把它换成控制器能理解的目标。How do you put the calibrated parameters to use? It depends on who is using the model. If your own program (MoveIt computing inverse kinematics from the URDF, say) computes the joint angles and sends them to the arm, just replace the parameters in the URDF with the calibrated ones: the joint angles are then computed for the real arm. If you use the controller’s internal Cartesian commands, they rely on the controller’s own model, so all you can do is "move the target the opposite way" before sending it: first use the calibrated model to compute the joint angles the arm should go to, then use the nominal model to turn them into a target the controller understands.

D435i 里有一颗 Bosch BMI055：三轴陀螺量角速度，三轴加速度计量加速度（静止时读到的是和重力等大反向的量：朝上的那根轴读 +g）。它把三维的运动直接给出来，又快又不怕看不清，可它的误差会被积分放大：角速度积一次分是角度，加速度积两次分是位置。一颗 IMU 的读数通常这样描述：Inside the D435i is a Bosch BMI055: a three-axis gyro measures angular velocity and a three-axis accelerometer measures acceleration (at rest it reads a quantity equal in size to gravity and opposite in direction: the axis pointing up reads +g). It reports 3D motion directly and quickly, and it does not care whether anything can be seen, but integration amplifies its errors: integrate angular velocity once and you get an angle; integrate acceleration twice and you get a position. An IMU’s readings are usually described like this:

ωm = (I + Sg + Mg) ω + bg + ngam = (I + Sa + Ma) a + ba + na

陀螺读到的角速度，等于真实角速度乘以一个接近单位矩阵的矩阵（对角线上差一点是标度误差，非对角线上的一点点是轴之间的串扰），再加上零偏和噪声。加速度计同理。The angular velocity the gyro reads is the true angular velocity multiplied by a matrix close to the identity matrix (small differences on the diagonal are scale-factor errors; tiny entries off the diagonal are crosstalk between the axes), plus a bias and noise. The accelerometer works the same way.

这是第 ① 步 z = x + b + v 的三维版本，只是多了一个乘性的部分。S 是三根轴各自的灵敏度和 1 差多少（标度误差）；M 来自三根轴不完全正交、芯片装得不正，绕 z 转时 x、y 也读到一点（交叉轴灵敏度）；b 是零偏，静止时也有的读数；n 是噪声。前三项都是系统误差，可以标；噪声只能用统计描述。This is the 3D version of step ①’s z = x + b + v, with one extra, multiplicative part. S is how far each axis’s sensitivity is from 1 (the scale-factor error); M comes from the three axes not being perfectly orthogonal and the chip being mounted slightly askew, so that a turn about z also makes x and y read a little (cross-axis sensitivity); b is the bias, a reading that is there even at rest; n is the noise. The first three terms are all systematic errors and can be calibrated; the noise can only be described statistically.

加速度计最好的参照物是重力：它的大小 g 处处一样，方向朝下。把 IMU 某根轴朝上放，这根轴应该读 +g；朝下放，应该读 −g。两次读数一加一减，就把零偏和标度分开了：The accelerometer’s best reference is gravity: its size g is the same everywhere, and it points down. Place the IMU with one axis pointing up and that axis should read +g; with it pointing down, it should read −g. Adding and subtracting the two readings separates the bias from the scale factor:

a+ = k g + b,  a− = − k g + bb = (a+ + a−) / 2,  k = (a+ − a−) / (2 g)

一根轴朝上时的读数是标度乘以 g 加零偏，朝下时是负的标度乘以 g 加零偏。两个读数的平均就是零偏，差的一半除以 g 就是标度。With an axis pointing up, its reading is the scale factor times g plus the bias; pointing down, it is minus the scale factor times g plus the bias. The mean of the two readings is the bias; half their difference, divided by g, is the scale factor.

朝上和朝下时，零偏不变，重力的贡献变号：相加时重力抵消，只剩零偏；相减时零偏抵消，只剩重力。这是第 ③ 步"激励"最干净的例子：一个参数只在一种姿势下看不出来，换一种姿势就分开了。三根轴各做一次，就是六个面。Between up and down, the bias stays the same while gravity’s contribution changes sign: adding cancels gravity and leaves only the bias; subtracting cancels the bias and leaves only gravity. This is the cleanest example of step ③’s "excitation": a parameter that cannot be seen from one pose alone comes apart as soon as you change the pose. Do this once for each of the three axes and you have six faces.

陀螺没有重力那样白给的参照物（地球自转约 15 °/h，也就是 0.004 °/s，比这颗芯片温度每变 1 K 零偏的变化 0.015 °/s 还小，拿来当参照不现实），零偏就靠静止时平均：静止时真实角速度只剩地球自转那 0.004 °/s，对这颗芯片可以当成 0，读数的平均就是零偏（平均到 100 s 时，平均出来的"零偏"里已经含着地球自转在这根轴上的那一份）。平均得越久，噪声的影响越小：平均 1 s 约 0.010 °/s，100 s 约 0.001 °/s（规格书的噪声密度是单边的，平均 T 秒的标准差是 0.014/√(2T)，我们算的）。可也不能无限长地平均：零偏自己在慢慢游走。平均多久最好、噪声和游走各有多大，用 Allan 方差来看：The gyro has no free reference like gravity (the Earth turns at about 15 °/h, or 0.004 °/s, which is less than the 0.015 °/s by which this chip’s bias changes for each 1 K of temperature; using it as a reference is not practical). So its bias comes from averaging at rest: at rest the only true angular velocity left is the Earth’s 0.004 °/s, which for this chip can be taken as 0, so the mean of the readings is the bias (by the time you average for 100 s, the averaged "bias" already includes the part of the Earth’s rotation that falls on this axis). The longer you average, the smaller the effect of the noise: about 0.010 °/s for 1 s, about 0.001 °/s for 100 s (the datasheet’s noise density is one-sided, so the standard deviation of a T-second average is 0.014/√(2T); our arithmetic). But you cannot average forever: the bias itself slowly wanders. How long is best to average, and how big the noise and the wander each are, is what the Allan variance shows:

σ2(τ) = ½ ⟨ (ω̄k+1(τ) − ω̄k(τ))² ⟩σ(τ) ≈ N / √τ,  σmin ≈ 0.664 B,  σ(τ) ≈ K √(τ / 3)

把一长段静止的数据切成每段 τ 秒，算出每段的平均；相邻两段平均之差的平方，取平均再除以 2，就是 τ 处的 Allan 方差，开方叫 Allan 偏差。把 Allan 偏差画成对数坐标的曲线，短的 τ 上它随 1/√τ 下降，由白噪声 N 决定；中间有一段最低的平底，高度约是零偏不稳定性 B 的 0.664 倍；长的 τ 上它随 √τ 上升，由速率随机游走 K 决定。Cut a long stretch of data recorded at rest into clusters τ seconds long and work out the mean of each; square the difference between the means of neighbouring clusters, average it and divide by 2, and that is the Allan variance at τ; its square root is called the Allan deviation. Plot the Allan deviation as a curve on logarithmic axes: at short τ it falls as 1/√τ, set by the white noise N; in the middle there is a lowest, flat floor, whose height is about 0.664 times the bias instability B; at long τ it rises as √τ, set by the rate random walk K.

白噪声平均得越久越小，所以左边往下走，斜率 −½，τ = 1 s 处的值就是 N；零偏慢慢游走，平均得越久，相邻两段之间游走得越多，所以右边往上走，斜率 +½，在 τ = 3 s 那条斜线上读出 K；两者之间那段最平的地方，就是平均时间最合适、零偏最"稳"的地方（MathWorks 的说明）。这个方法原本用来描述原子钟，后来成了陀螺测试标准里的方法（IEEE Std 952）。White noise gets smaller the longer you average, so the left side goes down with a slope of −½, and its value at τ = 1 s is N; the bias slowly wanders, and the longer the averages, the more it wanders between neighbouring clusters, so the right side goes up with a slope of +½, and K is read off that sloping line at τ = 3 s; the flattest stretch between the two is where the averaging time is best and the bias is most "stable" (MathWorks). The method was first used to describe atomic clocks and later became the method used in the test standard for gyros (IEEE Std 952).

这一步的要点：IMU 的读数 = (单位矩阵 + 标度误差 + 轴间串扰) × 真值 + 零偏 + 噪声。加速度计拿重力当参照：一根轴朝上朝下各读一次，相加得零偏，相减得标度，三根轴就是六个面。陀螺的零偏靠静止平均，但平均多久最好、噪声和游走各多大，要看 Allan 偏差（Allan 方差开方）的曲线：左边的斜率 −½ 读白噪声，平底读零偏不稳定性，右边的斜率 +½ 读随机游走。零偏还随温度变，所以除了事先标，运行中也要一直估。The point of this step: An IMU’s reading = (identity matrix + scale-factor error + crosstalk between axes) × true value + bias + noise. The accelerometer uses gravity as its reference: read each axis once pointing up and once pointing down; adding gives the bias, subtracting gives the scale factor, and three axes make six faces. The gyro’s bias comes from averaging at rest, but how long is best to average, and how big the noise and the wander are, has to be read from the curve of the Allan deviation (the square root of the Allan variance): the slope −½ on the left gives the white noise, the floor gives the bias instability, and the slope +½ on the right gives the random walk. The bias also changes with temperature, so as well as calibrating it beforehand you must keep estimating it during operation.

第 ⑨ 步 · 装在哪、转多大Step ⑨ · Where it is mounted, how far it turns

激光雷达、轮子和相机之间Between the lidar, the wheels and the camera

底盘前面的激光雷达，URDF 里写着装在底盘中心前方 0.20 m、朝正前方。它测的每一个点，都要先按这个位姿换算到 base_link，再放进地图。这个位姿错了，错的方式和相机一样，也分转动和平移，只是杠杆臂长得多：激光看得到 10 m 外的墙。安装角差 0.5°，4 m 外的墙就横着挪 34.9 mm，10 m 外 87.3 mm（我们算的）。The URDF says the lidar at the front of the base is mounted 0.20 m ahead of the base centre, facing straight ahead. Every point it measures is first converted into base_link with this pose, and only then put into the map. If this pose is wrong, it is wrong in the same ways as the camera’s, in rotation and in translation, only the lever arm is much longer: the lidar sees walls 10 m away. With the mounting angle 0.5° off, a wall 4 m away shifts sideways by 34.9 mm, and one 10 m away by 87.3 mm (our arithmetic).

平移错了，在原地转身时最明显。底盘绕自己的中心转，激光雷达跟着画一个圆；程序以为它在 0.20 m 处，其实在 0.22 m 处（示意），程序算的"激光雷达转过去的位置"就和真实的差一点：转 90° 后，前后两帧扫描对不上 2 sin 45° × 20 mm ≈ 28.3 mm，转 180° 后差 40 mm（我们算的）。墙在叠起来的扫描里变成了两道，或者一道很粗的线。A wrong translation shows up most clearly when the robot turns on the spot. The base turns about its own centre and the lidar traces a circle with it; the program thinks the lidar is at 0.20 m when it is really at 0.22 m (illustrative), so the program’s idea of "where the lidar has turned to" is a little off from the truth: after a 90° turn, the scans before and after are 2 sin 45° × 20 mm ≈ 28.3 mm out of line, and after 180° they are 40 mm out (our arithmetic). In the stacked scans the walls come out doubled, or as one very thick line.

这正好给了一个不用任何标定板的标定办法：让机器人在原地慢慢转一整圈，把每一帧扫描都按"假设的安装位姿"放进同一个坐标系里，然后调这个位姿，直到墙变得最薄。墙最薄时，激光雷达离转动中心多远、从它自己看转动中心在哪个方向，就都定下来了。可光转身分不开 x、y 和安装角：把整个安装绕底盘中心转一个小角度（位置沿那个圆挪一点，安装角也转同样的角度），叠出来的扫描只是整体转了一下，墙一样薄；0.20 m 的杠杆臂上，0.5° 的安装角和 1.75 mm 的横向偏移在转身时分不开（我们算的）。所以再让机器人直着走一段：激光雷达看到自己朝哪个方向走，安装角就定下来了。它用的参照物，是"墙不会动"和"底盘朝正前方走"这两件事。This happens to give a calibration method that needs no calibration board at all: have the robot turn slowly through a full circle on the spot, put every scan into the same frame using the "assumed mounting pose", and then adjust that pose until the walls are as thin as they can get. When the walls are thinnest, how far the lidar is from the centre of rotation, and in which direction the centre of rotation lies as seen from the lidar itself, are both pinned down. But turning alone cannot separate x, y and the mounting angle: turn the whole mounting through a small angle about the base centre (the position moves a little along that circle, and the mounting angle turns by the same angle) and the stacked scans merely rotate as a whole, with walls just as thin; on the 0.20 m lever arm, a 0.5° mounting angle and a 1.75 mm sideways offset cannot be separated by turning (our arithmetic). So the robot also drives straight for a stretch: the lidar sees which way it is moving, and that fixes the mounting angle. The references it uses are two facts: "the walls do not move" and "the base drives straight ahead".

平地上转身有一个限制：只绕竖直轴转，激光雷达离地多高、有没有一点俯仰和横滚，这些转身时都看不出来。转身加直走，能标的只有 x、y 和安装角；其余的要靠别的数据，比如让它看到地面或一个倾斜的平面。这和手眼标定里"只绕一根轴转，绕这根轴的转角和沿这根轴的平移都定不下来"是同一件事（第 ⑥ 步）。Turning on a flat floor has one limitation: it only turns about the vertical axis, so how high the lidar sits above the floor, and whether it has a little pitch or roll, cannot be seen while turning. Turning plus driving straight can calibrate only x, y and the mounting angle; the rest needs other data, such as letting the lidar see the floor or a tilted plane. This is the same thing as in hand–eye calibration, where "turning about only one axis leaves both the angle about that axis and the translation along it undetermined" (step ⑥).

转身的角度本身也来自轮子。差速底盘由左右两个轮子的转速算出前进的速度和转身的速度：The turning angle itself also comes from the wheels. A differential-drive base works out its forward speed and its turning rate from the speeds of its left and right wheels:

v = r (ωR + ωL) / 2,  ω = r (ωR − ωL) / b

底盘前进的速度，是轮子半径乘以左右轮转速的平均；转身的角速度，是轮子半径乘以左右轮转速之差，再除以轮距。The base’s forward speed is the wheel radius times the mean of the left and right wheel speeds; its turning rate is the wheel radius times the difference between the two wheel speeds, divided by the track width.

两个轮子一样快，底盘直着走；一快一慢，底盘绕着两轮连线上的某一点转（两轮反着转时这一点在两轮之间，同向时在慢的那个轮子外侧），两轮的线速度之差除以轮距就是转得多快。所以轮子的半径 r 错了，走的距离和转的角度都按比例错；轮距 b 错了，只有转的角度按比例错；左右两个轮子的半径不一样，直着走时会走出一条弧。With both wheels equally fast, the base drives straight; with one faster than the other, the base turns about some point on the line through the two wheels (between the wheels when they turn in opposite directions, outside the slower wheel when they turn the same way), and the difference between the two wheels’ linear speeds divided by the track width is how fast it turns. So if the wheel radius r is wrong, both the distance travelled and the angle turned are wrong in proportion; if the track width b is wrong, only the angle turned is wrong in proportion; and if the left and right wheels have different radii, driving straight traces out an arc.

轮胎是橡胶的，载重一压、气压一变、磨损一多，半径和着地点都会变，所以轮子要定期重标。Borenstein 和 Feng 的 UMBmark 让机器人沿 4 m × 4 m 的正方形顺时针、逆时针各走 5 圈，从回到起点时的偏差反推轮径差和轮距误差，修正以后里程计准了一个数量级以上（他们的例子里从 317 mm 降到 21 mm）（Borenstein、Feng，1996）。ROS 2 的 robot_calibration 包里有一个 base_calibration_node，用来估轮径、轮距的比例和陀螺的增益（robot_calibration）。Tyres are made of rubber: when a load presses on them, when the pressure changes, as they wear, the radius and the contact points change, so the wheels need recalibrating regularly. Borenstein and Feng’s UMBmark has the robot drive a 4 m × 4 m square 5 times clockwise and 5 times anticlockwise, and works back from the errors on returning to the start to the difference in wheel diameters and the error in the track width; after correction the odometry was more than an order of magnitude more accurate (from 317 mm down to 21 mm in their example) (Borenstein and Feng, 1996). The ROS 2 robot_calibration package has a base_calibration_node that estimates the scales of the wheel diameter and the track width, and the gyro’s gain (robot_calibration).

相机和激光雷达之间也要知道相对位姿，比如要把激光的点画到图上，或者用相机认出的东西去查激光的距离。一块标定板就能把两者连起来：相机从板上的格子算出板所在的平面（法向量 n 和到相机的距离 d），激光雷达在板上留下一串点，这些点换到相机坐标系里以后，必须落在那个平面上（Zhang、Pless，2004）：The relative pose between the camera and the lidar must be known as well, for example to draw the lidar points onto the image, or to look up the lidar distance of something the camera has recognised. A single calibration board can link the two: from the squares on the board the camera works out the plane the board lies in (its normal n and its distance d from the camera); the lidar leaves a string of points on the board, and once converted into the camera frame these points must lie on that plane (Zhang and Pless, 2004):

nT (R pL + t) = d

激光雷达测到的板上的点，用待求的转动 R 和平移 t 换到相机坐标系里，再投到板的法向量上，必须正好等于板到相机的距离。A point on the board measured by the lidar, converted into the camera frame with the unknown rotation R and translation t and then projected onto the board’s normal, must come out exactly equal to the board’s distance from the camera.

这是"点在平面上"写成的方程：平面上所有的点，在法向量方向上的投影都一样长。板摆一次，每个激光点给一个方程；板只换位置、不换朝向，方程互相重复，所以板要摆成好几个不同的朝向，让 R 和 t 的六个数都被"看到"（又是激励）。先用线性方法解一个初值，再做非线性的精修。This is "the point lies on the plane" written as an equation: every point on a plane has a projection of the same length along the normal. Each placement of the board gives one equation per lidar point; if the board only changes position and not orientation, the equations repeat one another, so the board has to be placed in several different orientations so that all six numbers of R and t are "seen" (excitation again). A linear method first gives an initial value, followed by a non-linear refinement.

第 ⑩ 步 · 时间戳也是一个参数Step ⑩ · The time stamp is a parameter too

时间：读数是什么时候的Time: when the reading was taken

每个读数都带着一个时间戳，说明它是什么时候量的。把两个传感器的读数放在一起用时，靠的就是这个时间戳：相机看到杯子在图上某处，要按"拍这张图那一刻"底盘和手臂的位姿，把它换算到别的坐标系里。时间戳要是比真实的时刻晚了 Δt，用的就是 Δt 以后的位姿；机器人静止时不要紧，一动起来就差：Every reading carries a time stamp that says when it was measured. When readings from two sensors are used together, everything rests on this time stamp: when the camera sees the cup somewhere in the image, it has to convert that into other frames using the poses the base and the arm had "at the moment this image was taken". If the time stamp is Δt later than the true moment, the poses used are the ones from Δt later; while the robot stands still this does not matter, but as soon as it moves, the result is off:

δp ≈ v Δt + ω r Δt

时间差带来的位置误差，约等于平移速度乘以时间差，加上转动的角速度乘以距离再乘以时间差。The position error caused by a time offset is roughly the translational speed times the time offset, plus the angular velocity of the turn times the distance times the time offset.

时间差 Δt 里，底盘往前走了 vΔt；转身时，离转动中心 r 远的点划过了 ωrΔt 的弧长（第 ② 步的杠杆臂）。所以时间误差就是"动得多快"乘以"差了多久"：静止时它是 0，动得越快越大。During the time offset Δt, the base moves forward by vΔt; when it turns, a point r from the rotation centre sweeps an arc of length ωrΔt (the lever arm of step ②). So the timing error is "how fast it is moving" times "how big the time gap is": it is 0 when the robot is still, and grows the faster it moves.

The robot is standing still when it grasps the cup, so this time the story has nothing to do with timing; but when it "looks while driving" or "searches while turning", timing is one of the biggest sources of error.

时间差从哪来？曝光要一段时间，时间戳该打在曝光的中间还是开始；图像从传感器读出来、经 USB 传到电脑、在驱动的队列里排队，各要几毫秒到几十毫秒；时间戳是在采集时打的，还是在电脑收到时才打的；几台电脑的钟本来就不一样快。按从根上解决到事后补救的顺序，办法是：Where does a time offset come from? An exposure takes a while: should the stamp mark its middle or its start? Reading the image out of the sensor, sending it over USB to the computer and queuing in the driver each take from a few milliseconds to tens of milliseconds. Is the stamp applied at capture, or only when the computer receives the image? And the clocks of different computers never run at quite the same rate in the first place. In order from fixing the root cause to patching things up afterwards, the remedies are:

  • 在硬件上打时间戳：D435i 的 IMU 数据包，就是用深度芯片的时钟打的时间戳，IMU 和深度图天然对得齐。能用硬件触发、硬件时间戳的，就别在电脑收到时才打。Stamp in hardware: the D435i’s IMU packets are stamped with the depth chip’s clock, so the IMU data and the depth images line up naturally. Wherever you can use a hardware trigger and hardware time stamps, don’t stamp the data only when the computer receives it.
  • 把几台电脑的钟对齐：局域网里用 chrony 或 PTP（精确时间协议），不对齐的话，一台电脑的"10:00:00.000"在另一台上可能是 10:00:00.030。Synchronise the computers’ clocks: on the local network, use chrony or PTP (Precision Time Protocol); without this, "10:00:00.000" on one computer may be 10:00:00.030 on another.
  • 把剩下的固定时间差当成参数标出来：时间差固定时，它就是一个系统误差，和外参一样可以标。Calibrate the remaining fixed offset as a parameter: when the time offset is fixed, it is a systematic error, and it can be calibrated just like the extrinsics.

怎么标？让同一个运动被两个传感器各量一遍。底盘左右来回转，陀螺量到一条角速度曲线，相机用视觉里程计也算出一条转动速度的曲线；两条曲线形状一样，只是相机那条晚了一点。把它往前挪、往后挪，挪到和陀螺那条对得最齐的位置，挪的量就是时间差：How is it calibrated? Have both sensors measure the same motion. The base turns back and forth, left and right: the gyro measures one angular-velocity curve, and the camera, with visual odometry, works out a curve of rotation rate too. The two curves have the same shape; the camera’s is just a little late. Slide it earlier and later until it lines up best with the gyro’s, and the amount you slid it by is the time offset:

Δt̂ = argmaxτ Σk |ωimu(tk)| · |ωcam(tk + τ)|

估出来的时间差，是让两条角速度曲线"乘起来再加起来"最大的那个挪动量 τ，也就是两条曲线最像的位置。The estimated time offset is the shift τ for which "multiplying the two angular-velocity curves together and adding up" gives the largest result, that is, the position where the two curves are most alike.

这是互相关：两条一样形状的曲线对齐时，正对正、负对负，乘积处处为正，加起来最大；错开时有正有负，加起来变小。角速度只和转动有关，和两个传感器装在哪无关；两者的坐标轴朝向不一样，就拿角速度的大小 |ω| 去对，所以可以先把时间对齐，再去标外参。This is cross-correlation: when two curves of the same shape are lined up, positive meets positive and negative meets negative, so every product is positive and the sum is at its largest; when they are out of step, some products are positive and some negative, and the sum gets smaller. Angular velocity depends only on the rotation, not on where the two sensors are mounted; since their axes point different ways, compare the magnitudes |ω|. That is why the time can be aligned first and the extrinsics calibrated afterwards.

更精细的做法是把时间差和外参放进同一个最小二乘里一起估。Kalibr 用连续时间的样条描述 IMU 的运动，把相机的观测放在"时间戳加 Δt"的时刻上去对；在真实数据上（四种曝光时间，每种十组），估出的时间差随曝光时间变化的斜率是 0.498，和理论值 0.5 很接近，40 组估计离这条拟合直线都不超过 ±0.2 ms，约是 IMU 采样周期（5 ms）的 4%（真实的时间差无从知道，所以这说的是一致性）；同时外参平移的标准差约 0.76–1.6 mm，转动 0.02°–0.06°（Furgale、Rehder、Siegwart，2013；Kalibr）。A more refined approach puts the time offset and the extrinsics into one least-squares problem and estimates them together. Kalibr describes the IMU’s motion with a continuous-time spline and matches each camera observation at the instant "time stamp plus Δt". On real data (four exposure times, ten data sets each), the estimated time offset varied with exposure time with a slope of 0.498, very close to the theoretical 0.5, and all 40 estimates lay within ±0.2 ms of this fitted line, about 4% of the IMU’s sampling period (5 ms) (the true time offset cannot be known, so this is a statement about consistency); at the same time, the standard deviations of the extrinsics were about 0.76–1.6 mm in translation and 0.02°–0.06° in rotation (Furgale, Rehder and Siegwart, 2013; Kalibr).

要有变化才看得出时间差。底盘匀速转圈时，两条曲线都是平的，怎么挪都一样，时间差根本估不出来；匀速直线走也一样。所以标定时要来回转、忽快忽慢（Yang 等，2019）。A time offset only shows when something changes. When the base turns in circles at a constant rate, both curves are flat, every shift looks the same, and the time offset simply cannot be estimated; the same goes for driving in a straight line at constant speed. So when calibrating, turn back and forth, now faster, now slower (Yang et al., 2019).

时间差也可以在机器人干活时，作为一个状态一直估下去：VINS-Mono 这类视觉–惯性系统就把相机和 IMU 之间的时间差当成要估的量，让它随着运行慢慢收敛（Qin、Shen，2018）。在线估计的道理，第 ⑬ 步再讲。The time offset can also be estimated continuously, as a state, while the robot works: visual–inertial systems such as VINS-Mono treat the time offset between the camera and the IMU as one of the quantities to estimate, and let it converge gradually as they run (Qin and Shen, 2018). 这一步的要点：时间戳错了 Δt，动起来时位置就错 vΔt + ωrΔt：转身时 30 ms 就是 12 mm。先在硬件上打时间戳、把电脑的钟对齐，剩下的固定时间差当成参数来标：让同一个运动被两个传感器各量一遍，互相关最大的地方就是时间差。匀速运动时看不出时间差，标定时要来回、忽快忽慢地动。The point of this step: if a time stamp is off by Δt, the position is off by vΔt + ωrΔt as soon as the robot moves: when turning, 30 ms means 12 mm. First stamp in hardware and synchronise the computers’ clocks, then calibrate the remaining fixed offset as a parameter: have both sensors measure the same motion, and the time offset is where the cross-correlation is largest. Motion at constant speed does not reveal a time offset, so when calibrating, move back and forth, now faster, now slower.

负载、末端和工具Payload, end effector and tool

链的最后一段在夹爪上。FR3 的七个关节里都有力矩传感器，手臂用它们来感觉"有没有碰到东西"：量到的关节力矩，减去模型说"托住自己、托住手上的东西、加减速"本该需要的力矩，剩下的就当成外力。libfranka 给出的 tau_ext_hat_filtered 就是这样算出来的外力矩，模型里包括你配置好的末端执行器和负载（libfranka RobotState）。末端执行器（夹爪本身）的质量、质心、惯量和它相对法兰的位姿，在 Franka 的管理界面 Desk 里设；手上额外拿的东西，用 setLoad 告诉它：质量、质心在法兰坐标系里的位置、惯量（libfranka Robot）。The last link in the chain is at the gripper. Each of the FR3’s seven joints has a torque sensor, and the arm uses them to feel "have I touched anything?": from the measured joint torques it subtracts the torques that the model says should be needed "to hold itself up, hold up what is in its hand, and speed up and slow down", and treats what is left as external force. The tau_ext_hat_filtered that libfranka reports is an external torque worked out exactly this way, and the model includes the end effector and the load you have configured (libfranka RobotState). The mass, centre of mass and inertia of the end effector (the gripper itself), and its pose relative to the flange, are set in Desk, Franka’s management interface; anything extra held in the hand you tell it about with setLoad: the mass, the position of the centre of mass in the flange frame, and the inertia (libfranka Robot).

要是没告诉它手里拿着杯子呢？那杯子的重量就被当成了外力。一只装了水的杯子算 0.15 kg（示意），就是 0.15 × 9.81 ≈ 1.47 N 的"假外力"；拿着 0.5 kg 的东西，就是 4.90 N。质心的位置设错 2 cm，0.15 kg 带来 0.029 N·m 的假力矩（我们算的）。放杯子时，程序常用"外力的大小超过某个阈值"来判断杯底碰到了台面，阈值比如 3 N（示意）：手里的杯子没配置，还没碰到台面，假外力已经吃掉了一半的余量；拿得更重，还没碰到就以为碰到了。要是程序只看台面顶回来的那个方向，假外力的方向正好相反，要多压 1.47 N 才发现碰到了。负载的质量和质心，也是要标的参数。And if you don’t tell it that it is holding a cup? Then the cup’s weight is taken for an external force. Say a cup with water in it is 0.15 kg (illustrative): that is a "phantom external force" of 0.15 × 9.81 ≈ 1.47 N; holding something of 0.5 kg, it is 4.90 N. Set the centre of mass 2 cm wrong, and 0.15 kg produces a phantom torque of 0.029 N·m (our arithmetic). When putting a cup down, programs often decide that the bottom of the cup has touched the counter when "the size of the external force exceeds some threshold", say 3 N (illustrative). With the cup in the hand not configured, the phantom external force has already eaten half of the margin before the counter is touched; hold something heavier, and the robot thinks it has touched before it has. If the program only looks at the direction in which the counter pushes back, the phantom force points exactly the opposite way, and the arm has to press 1.47 N harder before it notices the contact. The payload’s mass and centre of mass are parameters to calibrate too.

有些机器人在手腕上装一个六维力/力矩传感器。它的读数里除了外力，还有传感器自己的零偏，和它下面工具的重量；工具的重量在传感器坐标系里的方向，随手腕的姿态变：Some robots have a six-axis force/torque sensor at the wrist. Besides the external force, its readings contain the sensor’s own bias and the weight of the tool mounted below it, and the direction of the tool’s weight in the sensor frame changes with the pose of the wrist:

Fm = F0 + RiT m g + Fextτm = τ0 + c × (RiT m g) + τext

传感器读到的力，等于它的零偏，加上工具的重力换到传感器坐标系里，再加上真正的外力。读到的力矩同理：零偏，加上工具重心到传感器的那段杠杆臂叉乘重力，再加上外力矩。The force the sensor reads equals its bias, plus the tool’s weight expressed in the sensor frame, plus the true external force. The torque it reads works the same way: the bias, plus the lever arm between the tool’s centre of gravity and the sensor crossed with the weight, plus the external torque.

不碰任何东西时外力为 0，读数里只剩零偏和工具的重力。把手腕摆成几个不同的姿态，重力在传感器坐标系里的方向各不相同，零偏却不变，用最小二乘就能把 F0、τ0、m、c 分开。要是只绕竖直轴转手腕，重力在传感器里的方向始终一样，它和零偏就分不开，又是激励的问题。When nothing is being touched, the external force is 0, and only the bias and the tool’s weight are left in the reading. Put the wrist into several different poses: the direction of gravity in the sensor frame is different each time while the bias stays the same, so least squares can separate F0, τ0, m and c. If the wrist only turns about a vertical axis, gravity always points the same way in the sensor and cannot be separated from the bias: once again, a question of excitation.

最后是工具中心点 TCP：工具上"算位置时算的那一点"到底在法兰前面多远。夹爪有设计图，可指尖磨损了、换了一副手指、装了一支笔或一个探针，这一点就变了。最常用的标法叫枢轴法：让工具尖插在一个小坑里不动，手臂换着姿态绕它转；不管姿态怎么换，工具尖在基座里的位置都不变：Last comes the tool centre point, the TCP: how far in front of the flange is the point on the tool that "positions are calculated for"? The gripper has design drawings, but once the fingertips wear, the fingers are replaced with a new pair, or a pen or a probe is fitted, that point moves. The most common way to calibrate it is called pivot calibration: the tool tip stays seated in a small divot while the arm pivots around it through a series of poses; however the pose changes, the tool tip’s position in the base frame stays the same:

Ri p + ti = q  ⇔  [Ri  −I] [p; q] = − ti

第 i 个姿态下，工具尖在法兰坐标系里的位置 p，经过法兰的位姿换到基座里，都等于同一个小坑的位置 q。把每个姿态的方程摞起来，p 和 q 一起用最小二乘解出来。At each pose i, the tool tip’s position p in the flange frame, carried into the base frame by the flange’s pose, equals the same divot position q. Stack the equations from all the poses, and solve for p and q together by least squares.

法兰的位姿 Ri、ti 由手臂报出来，p 和 q 是 6 个未知数，每个姿态给 3 个方程。姿态之间的转角越大，p 越分得清；只绕工具自己的轴转，或者所有姿态都绕同一根轴倾斜，沿那根轴的分量就分不出来（矩阵奇异）。两个姿态之间只有一根转轴，所以至少要三个倾斜方向不同的姿态；工业机器人上常用"四点法"：换四个姿态去碰同一点。The flange poses Ri and ti are reported by the arm; p and q are 6 unknowns, and each pose gives 3 equations. The larger the rotations between the poses, the better p is pinned down; if the tool only turns about its own axis, or all the poses tilt about the same axis, the component along that axis cannot be determined (the matrix is singular). Two poses have only one rotation axis between them, so at least three poses tilted in different directions are needed; on industrial robots the "four-point method" is common: touch the same point from four poses.

第 ⑫ 步 · 训练误差会骗人Step ⑫ · Training error can fool you

怎么知道标好了How to tell the calibration is good

每个标定程序跑完都会报一个数：重投影误差 0.2 px，残差 0.3 mm。这个数小，只说明模型贴住了标定时用的那些数据，第 ④ 步已经说过，它是训练误差。参数越多，模型越"软"，越能贴住数据，训练误差只会越来越小，不管这些参数在别处是不是胡来。真正要问的是：拿一些没用来标定的数据，模型还准不准？那叫测试误差。Every calibration program reports a number when it finishes: a reprojection error of 0.2 px, a residual of 0.3 mm. A small number only says that the model hugs the data used for the calibration; as step ④ said, it is a training error. The more parameters, the "softer" the model and the more closely it can hug the data, so the training error only ever gets smaller, whether or not those parameters are running wild elsewhere. The real question is: on data that was not used for the calibration, is the model still accurate? That is the test error.

镜头畸变是最好的例子。标定照片都挤在图中间时，加更多的畸变系数（比如 OpenCV 的有理模型，从 5 个加到 8 个），训练误差还能再降一点；可图中间的畸变本来就小，多出来的系数只是在拟合噪声，到了没拍到的图边上，它们可以把点推得很离谱。训练误差越来越小、测试误差先降后升，这就是过拟合。下面的交互里，只在图中间（r ≤ 0.5）拍：用 2 项时，训练误差 0.310 px，测试误差 0.497 px；加到 6 项，训练误差降到 0.283 px，没拍到的角上却差出几十万个像素。铺满整幅图再用 6 项，训练 0.283 px，测试 0.308 px，一点事没有（r 按焦距归一化，把 r = 1 当作图的角，示意）。两条对策：数据要覆盖将来要用的范围，参数要和数据配得上。Lens distortion is the best example. When the calibration photos are all crowded into the middle of the image, adding more distortion coefficients (OpenCV’s rational model, say, going from 5 to 8) can bring the training error down a little further; but the distortion in the middle of the image is small to begin with, so the extra coefficients are only fitting noise, and out at the edges of the image, where nothing was photographed, they can push points absurdly far. Training error that keeps falling while test error first falls and then rises: that is overfitting. In the interactive below, photographing only the middle of the image (r ≤ 0.5): with 2 terms, the training error is 0.310 px and the test error 0.497 px; with 6 terms, the training error falls to 0.283 px, yet in the corners that were never photographed the error runs to hundreds of thousands of pixels. Cover the whole image and use 6 terms, and training is 0.283 px, test 0.308 px: no trouble at all (r is normalised by the focal length, with r = 1 taken as the corner of the image; illustrative). Two remedies: the data must cover the range the model will be used over, and the parameters must be ones the data can support.

所以一次标定，至少要看这几样：So for any calibration, check at least these:

  • 残差：总的 RMS，和每一组数据各自的（OpenCV 的 perViewErrors，每张照片一个 RMS）。哪张特别大，多半是检测错了或者那张拍糊了，删掉重标。Residuals: the overall RMS, and that of each data set on its own (OpenCV’s perViewErrors, one RMS per photo). If one stands out as much larger, most likely the detection went wrong or that photo is blurred: delete it and calibrate again.
  • 每个参数的 σ：OpenCV 的 calibrateCamera 可以同时给出每个内参的标准差（OpenCV）。σ 大、参数之间强相关，说明数据没把它们分开（第 ③ 步）。The σ of each parameter: OpenCV’s calibrateCamera can also return the standard deviation of each intrinsic (OpenCV). A large σ, or strong correlation between parameters, means the data has not separated them (step ③).
  • 测试误差：留几张照片、几个姿势不参与标定，标完以后用它们检验。Test error: hold out a few photos and a few poses from the calibration, and check against them once it is done.
  • 残差有没有图案：残差应该像噪声；在图边上一致地朝外、随距离一致地变大，说明模型少了一项。Whether the residuals show a pattern: residuals should look like noise; if near the edges of the image they consistently point outwards, or consistently grow with distance, the model is missing a term.

对这台机器人，最终要的是"夹爪能不能落在杯子上"，所以最好的检验，是直接检验这件事，而且用和标定无关的方法：For this robot, what ultimately counts is "can the gripper land on the cup?", so the best check tests exactly that, directly, and with methods that have nothing to do with the calibration:

  • 摸一下：在台面上贴一个 ArUco 标记，让相机找到它的一个角，再让手臂把指尖（或一根探针）送到那个角上，用尺子或塞尺量差了多少。在台面上换几个地方各做一次，就是一张"整条链"的误差图。The touch test: stick an ArUco marker on the counter, have the camera find one of its corners, then have the arm bring a fingertip (or a probe) to that corner, and measure how far off it is with a ruler or a feeler gauge. Do it once at each of several places on the counter, and you have an error map of "the whole chain".
  • 闭环检查：同一个变换，走两条独立的路算两遍，应该一样。比如手上贴着标记时，"相机看到的标记位姿，换到手臂基座里"和"手臂报的法兰位姿，加上标记在法兰上的位置"应该重合；在没用来标定的姿势上，两者差多少，就是这一段标定真实的误差。Loop-closure check: compute the same transform twice along two independent routes; the two results should agree. For example, with a marker on the hand, "the marker pose the camera sees, converted into the arm’s base frame" and "the flange pose the arm reports, plus the marker’s position on the flange" should coincide; at poses not used for the calibration, how far apart the two are is the true error of that link’s calibration.
  • 和预算比：验收的标准从任务来。这台机器人要在 0.55 m 外夹住杯子，随机的部分（检测和深度）已经有 3.52 mm；标定剩下的偏差要压到 1.5 mm 以内，成功率才能保持在 0.99 以上（第 ⑮ 步）。Compare with the budget: the acceptance criterion comes from the task. This robot has to grasp a cup 0.55 m away, and the random part (detection and depth) is already 3.52 mm; the bias left after calibration has to be kept within 1.5 mm for the success rate to stay above 0.99 (step ⑮).

2013 年的那个实验（第 ④ 步）对训练误差说得很直白：大家报的重投影误差是训练误差，不是测试误差；训练用的照片选得不好时，它可以看着很小，换个地方却差很多。他们因此提出用"最大期望重投影误差"来衡量：从参数的不确定里抽很多组参数，把整幅图上一格格的点投一遍，看最坏的地方平均差多少（Richardson、Strom、Olson，2013）。The 2013 experiment (step ④) is blunt about training error: the reprojection errors people report are training errors, not test errors; when the training photos are poorly chosen, the error can look very small and yet be far off somewhere else. So they proposed measuring with the "maximum expected reprojection error": draw many sets of parameters from the parameters’ uncertainty, project a grid of points across the whole image with each set, and see how far off the worst place is on average (Richardson, Strom and Olson, 2013).

The best check has nothing to do with the calibration and tests the task directly: have the fingertip touch the point the camera sees, or compute the same transform twice along two routes. 第 ⑬ 步 · 漂移、监控和在线标定Step ⑬ · Drift, monitoring and online calibration

标定会过期Calibrations expire

标定是某一天、某个温度下的一张快照。这一篇的故事里，相机就是在标好以后被人拆过一次；还有很多东西会让标定慢慢或者突然过期：A calibration is a snapshot, taken on one day at one temperature. In this page’s story, someone took the camera off once after it had been calibrated. Plenty of other things can make a calibration expire, slowly or suddenly:

  • 温度：零件热胀冷缩；IMU 的零偏随温度变（第 ⑧ 步）；RealSense 说，剧烈的冷热循环会让深度标定变差。Temperature: parts expand and contract; the IMU’s bias changes with temperature (step ⑧); RealSense says that extreme temperature cycling degrades the depth calibration.
  • 冲击和振动：磕一下、运输一趟，支架和镜片会微微挪动（RealSense 的说法是"过大的冲击和振动"）。Shock and vibration: one knock or one trip in transit, and the mounts and lenses shift very slightly (RealSense’s phrase is "excessive shock and vibration").
  • 拆装：这次的故事。换线、擦镜头、换夹爪手指，都可能动到装配。Remounting: the story on this page. Changing a cable, cleaning a lens or swapping the gripper fingers can all disturb the assembly.
  • 磨损：轮胎越磨越小（第 ⑨ 步），减速器有了间隙。Wear: tyres wear down and get smaller (step ⑨), and gearboxes develop backlash.
  • 负载和工具：换了夹爪、装了新工具，质量和 TCP 都变了（第 ⑪ 步）。Payload and tools: with a new gripper or a new tool, both the mass and the TCP change (step ⑪).
  • 软件：有人改了 URDF 里的一个数，驱动升级改了坐标系的约定，相机换了一个分辨率。它们不动一颗螺丝，效果却和拆装一样。Software: someone edits a number in the URDF, a driver update changes a frame convention, the camera switches to a different resolution. Not a single screw moves, yet the effect is the same as a remount.

所以标定不是做一次就完的事，要有监控：So calibration is not a one-off job. It needs monitoring:

  • 每次开机查一个固定的姿势：充电座旁边贴一个标记，机器人每次开机，让手臂把指尖（或手上的标记）放到一个固定的位置，相机看一眼，比较"手臂和相机外参说它在哪"和"相机看到它在哪"。差值超过阈值（比如 2 mm，示意），就报警、停下来重标。这个检查只要几秒钟。Check a fixed pose at every start-up: stick a marker next to the dock. Every time the robot starts up, the arm puts its fingertip (or the marker on its hand) at a fixed position, the camera takes a look, and the robot compares "where the arm and the camera extrinsics say it is" with "where the camera sees it". If the difference is over a threshold (say 2 mm, illustrative), it raises an alarm and stops for recalibration. The check takes only a few seconds.
  • 看设备自己的健康值：RealSense 的片上自标定会给出一个健康值，绝对值超过 0.25，重标能变好；超过 1，不重标基本不能用（第 ⑤ 步）。Read the device’s own health-check value: RealSense On-Chip Self-Calibration reports a health-check value. If its absolute value is above 0.25, recalibrating will improve things; above 1, the camera is essentially unusable until it is recalibrated (step ⑤).
  • 看滤波器的新息：滤波器每次用读数修正时，读数和预测之差（新息）应该像噪声；它一直偏向一边，或者大得超出它自己算的协方差，说明模型里有参数错了（第 16 篇第 ⑭ 步）。Watch the filter’s innovations: each time the filter corrects itself with a reading, the difference between the reading and the prediction (the innovation) should look like noise. If it stays off to one side, or is bigger than the covariance the filter itself computes, some parameter in the model is wrong (step ⑭ of page 16).

更进一步，有些参数可以在机器人干活时一边跑一边估，叫在线标定（也叫自标定）。办法是把参数并进状态里：原来滤波器估的是位姿和速度，现在再加上陀螺的零偏、相机和 IMU 之间的外参、两者的时间差。这些参数的"运动模型"是不变，或者极慢地随机游走；读数的模型里用到了它们，所以每次修正，读数和预测之差都会顺便把它们往对的方向推一点：Going further, some parameters can be estimated on the fly, while the robot works. This is called online calibration (also self-calibration). The idea is to add the parameters to the state: where the filter used to estimate pose and velocity, it now also estimates the gyro bias, the extrinsics between the camera and the IMU, and the time offset between the two. The "motion model" of these parameters is that they stay constant, or random-walk very slowly. The reading model uses them, so at every correction the difference between reading and prediction also nudges them a little in the right direction:

xaug = [x; θ],  θk+1 = θk + wkzk = h(xk; θk) + vk

把要标的参数接在状态后面，一起估。参数每一步几乎不变，只允许它以很小的随机量慢慢变。读数的模型同时依赖状态和参数，所以滤波器在用读数修正状态的同时，也在修正参数。Append the parameters to be calibrated to the state and estimate them together. From one step to the next the parameters hardly change; they are only allowed to drift slowly by a small random amount. The reading model depends on both the state and the parameters, so when the filter corrects the state with a reading, it corrects the parameters too.

这和离线标定是同一个最小二乘，只是数据一边来一边用（卡尔曼滤波就是递推地做最小二乘）。随机游走的那一点 w 让参数可以跟着温度慢慢变；它设得太大，参数会跟着噪声乱跳，设得太小，参数变了它也跟不上。This is the same least squares as offline calibration; the data are simply used as they arrive (a Kalman filter is least squares done recursively). The small random-walk term w lets the parameters follow the temperature slowly. Set it too large and the parameters jump around with the noise; set it too small and the estimate cannot keep up when the parameters do change.

陀螺零偏几乎所有视觉–惯性系统都在线估；Mirzaei 和 Roumeliotis 2008 年让相机对着一块标定板，用扩展卡尔曼滤波递推地估相机和 IMU 之间的外参，并证明了它是可观的（Mirzaei、Roumeliotis，2008）；时间差也可以这样估（第 ⑩ 步）。Almost every visual–inertial system estimates the gyro bias online. In 2008 Mirzaei and Roumeliotis pointed a camera at a calibration board, estimated the extrinsics between the camera and the IMU recursively with an extended Kalman filter, and proved that they are observable (Mirzaei and Roumeliotis, 2008); the time offset can be estimated the same way (step ⑩).

参数只有在"影响读数"时才估得出来，这叫可观。机器人怎么动，决定了哪些参数此刻影响读数。A parameter can be estimated only if it "affects the readings". Such a parameter is called observable. How the robot moves decides which parameters affect the readings at any given moment.

可观这件事，在线标定时比离线更要紧：离线时可以故意摆姿势、来回转，在线时机器人做的是它自己的事。一组研究专门分析过视觉–惯性系统在线标定的"退化运动"：只平移不转，外参的平移估不出来；只绕一根轴转，沿这根轴方向的平移估不出来；角速度和线速度都不变时，时间差估不出来；所以不建议在平面运动下做在线标定（Yang 等，2019）。这台机器人的底盘恰恰只在平地上走、只绕竖直轴转：头上相机和 IMU 之间的平移，竖直方向的那一个分量在干活时永远估不出来。对策是：这一项用离线标定（比如用 Kalibr，手拿着相机绕各个方向晃）的结果固定住，在线只估那些被激励到的参数，比如陀螺零偏。Observability matters more for online calibration than for offline: offline, you can strike poses on purpose and turn back and forth; online, the robot is busy with its own work. One line of research looked specifically at the "degenerate motions" of online calibration in visual–inertial systems. With pure translation and no rotation, the translation of the extrinsics cannot be estimated. With rotation about one axis only, the translation along that axis cannot be estimated. With constant angular and linear velocity, the time offset cannot be estimated. So online calibration under planar motion is not recommended (Yang et al., 2019). And this robot’s base does exactly that: it only drives on a flat floor and only turns about the vertical axis. The vertical component of the translation between the head camera and the IMU can never be estimated while it works. The remedy: fix that term at the value from an offline calibration (for example with Kalibr, holding the camera in your hand and waving it about in every direction), and estimate online only the parameters that are excited, such as the gyro bias.

Likewise, lidar–base needs the turning angle, and the turning angle comes from the wheels, so the wheels go first.

拿到一台陌生的机器人，读它的误差和标定，可以问这几个问题：When you are handed an unfamiliar robot, you can read its errors and its calibration by asking these questions:

  1. 从传感器到执行器，这台机器人的误差链有哪几段？每一段的模型和参数是什么？From the sensors to the actuators, what are the links in this robot’s error chain? What is each link’s model, and what are its parameters?
  2. URDF 和配置文件里的每一个数是从哪来的：设计图、厂家的标定，还是在这台机器上标过？什么时候标的，之后拆装过、撞过、改过软件吗？Where did each number in the URDF and the configuration files come from: the design drawings, the manufacturer’s calibration, or a calibration done on this machine? When was it calibrated, and since then has anything been taken off and put back, has the robot been in a collision, has its software changed?
  3. 规格书写的是重复精度还是绝对精度？任务要的是哪一种？Does the datasheet give the repeatability or the absolute accuracy? Which one does the task need?
  4. 任务的误差预算是多少，哪一项最大？标定的力气花在那一项上了吗？What is the task’s error budget, and which term is the largest? Has the calibration effort gone into that term?
  5. 每一项标定用的数据够不够分散，参数分得开吗？报的是训练误差还是测试误差？Is the data for each calibration spread out enough, and can the parameters be separated? Is the error reported a training error or a test error?
  6. 标定的先后顺序对吗？后面的有没有把前面的错误吸收进去？Are the calibrations done in the right order? Has a later one absorbed the errors of an earlier one?
  7. 各个传感器的时间戳是在哪打的，钟对齐了吗，固定的时间差标过吗？Where is each sensor’s time stamp applied, are the clocks synchronised, and has the fixed time offset been calibrated?
  8. 有没有开机检查和运行中的监控？哪些参数在线估，它们被激励到了吗？Is there a start-up check, and monitoring while the robot runs? Which parameters are estimated online, and are they actually being excited?

答不上来的那几个，就是上手之前要补的：有的要翻配置文件和 URDF 的提交记录，有的要跑一遍检查，有的要真的标一次。The ones you cannot answer are what you need to fill in before getting hands-on: some mean digging through the commit history of the configuration files and the URDF, some mean running a check, and some mean actually doing a calibration.

这一步的要点：标定有先后：内参、深度、IMU，然后相机–IMU；轮子、然后激光–底盘；手臂、然后手眼、然后负载和 TCP；最后验证。前面的错误会被后面的吸收。全标好以后，抓杯子 A 的 σ 约 3.60 mm，成功率 0.99 以上；跳过手眼标定，就回到 0.825。读一台陌生机器人，就按这八个问题去问。The point of this step: Calibration has an order: intrinsics, depth and the IMU, then camera–IMU; the wheels, then lidar–base; the arm, then hand–eye, then the load and the TCP; validation comes last. Errors in the earlier steps get absorbed by the later ones. With everything calibrated, σ for grasping cup A is about 3.60 mm and the success rate is above 0.99; skip the hand–eye calibration and it falls back to 0.825. 总结Summary

两种误差，一个最小二乘，九个回答Two kinds of error, one least-squares fit, nine answers

整篇其实只讲了一件事：机器人对世界的每一次换算都用模型，模型里的参数要和这一台机器对上。误差分两种，随机的靠多看、靠滤波，系统的靠标定；标定就是拿已知的东西去量，用最小二乘把参数找出来，而数据要铺开，参数才分得开。链上每一段都是这个套路，区别只在模型、参照物和要怎么动：The whole page has really said just one thing: every conversion the robot makes about the world goes through a model, and the parameters in the model have to match this particular machine. Errors come in two kinds: random errors are dealt with by looking more and by filtering, systematic ones by calibration. Calibration means measuring something known and finding the parameters by least squares, and only when the data is spread out can the parameters be separated. Every link in the chain follows the same recipe; they differ only in the model, the reference and how things have to move:

回到开头的九个问题：Back to the nine questions from the opening:

  1. 误差有哪两种，为什么规格书上的"0.1 mm"不等于准到 0.1 mm：随机误差每次不同，平均 n 次缩成 σ/√n；系统误差每次一样，平均不掉。总误差的平方是偏差的平方加方差。FR3 的 0.1 mm 是重复精度（RP），按名义模型去新地方的绝对误差在 1 cm 量级（第 ① 步）。What are the two kinds of error, and why does "0.1 mm" on the datasheet not mean accurate to 0.1 mm: random error is different every time, and averaging n times shrinks it to σ/√n; systematic error is the same every time and does not average away. The square of the total error is the square of the bias plus the variance. The FR3’s 0.1 mm is its repeatability (RP); going somewhere new on the nominal model, its absolute error is of the order of 1 cm (step ①).
  2. 一个参数错一点，到杯子那里差多少：乘以灵敏度：转角乘杠杆臂，0.5° 在 0.55 m 外是 4.80 mm。偏差带符号相加，互不相关的随机误差按方差相加（统计意义上的勾股定理，J Σ JT；相关时加上交叉项），总误差由最大的一两项决定；误差在不同地方不一样，在一点上对准只修好那一点（第 ② 步）。If one parameter is a little off, how far off is it at the cup: multiply by the sensitivity: an angle times the lever arm, so 0.5° at 0.55 m is 4.80 mm. Biases add with their signs, while uncorrelated random errors add in variance (Pythagoras’ theorem in the statistical sense, J Σ JT; when they are correlated, add the cross terms), and the total error is set by the largest one or two terms. The error differs from place to place, and zeroing it at one point fixes only that point (step ②).
  3. 标定在做什么，什么样的数据才标得出来：一个带参数的模型，一组在已知条件下量的数据，一个最小二乘；参数的协方差是 (JTWJ)−1。数据挤在一起，参数互相纠缠、分不开，只在量过的地方准（第 ③ 步）。What is calibration doing, and what kind of data does it need: a model with parameters, a set of data measured under known conditions, and a least-squares fit; the covariance of the parameters is (JTWJ)−1. When the data is bunched together, the parameters are entangled and cannot be separated, and the result is accurate only where you measured (step ③).
  4. 相机内参怎么标，棋盘格怎么摆：张正友法：每张照片一个单应矩阵、两个方程，3 张以上（没有斜切时 2 张）闭式求解，再连同畸变一起精修。板要倾斜（约 45° 最好）、要铺到图的边角；重投影误差是训练误差（第 ④ 步）。How do you calibrate the camera intrinsics, and how should the checkerboard be placed: Zhang’s method: each photo gives one homography and two equations; 3 or more photos (2 if there is no skew) are solved in closed form, and the result is then refined together with the distortion. The board should be tilted (about 45° is best) and should reach the corners of the image; the reprojection error is a training error (step ④).
  5. 深度相机的偏差为什么随距离变：z = fB/d，视差上的一点偏移到深度上乘以 z²/(fB)；一个已知距离只能修一个数，要整段都准至少要两个距离（第 ⑤ 步）。Why does a depth camera’s bias change with distance: z = fB/d, so a small offset in the disparity is multiplied by z²/(fB) by the time it reaches the depth; one known distance can fix only one number, and for the whole range to be right you need at least two distances (step ⑤).
  6. 相机在手臂的哪里，为什么要大幅转：两条路到同一个标记，消掉未知的标记位置得到 AX = XB；转动要两个不平行的轴才定得下来，平移要除以 2 sin(θ/2)，转 5° 误差放大 11 倍多（第 ⑥ 步）。Where is the camera relative to the arm, and why must the arm turn so far: two paths lead to the same marker, and eliminating the marker’s unknown position gives AX = XB; the rotation can only be pinned down with two non-parallel axes, and the translation has to be divided by 2 sin(θ/2), so a 5° turn magnifies the error more than 11 times (step ⑥).
  7. 手臂自己有多准，怎样更准：名义模型差 1 cm 量级，运动学标定用外部测量和辨识雅可比解出零位和连杆参数，能独立辨识的最多 4r + 2p + 6 个（FR3 是 34）；标好的参数要用在真正算关节角的模型里（第 ⑦ 步）。How accurate is the arm itself, and how can it be made more accurate: the nominal model is off by the order of 1 cm; kinematic calibration uses external measurements and the identification Jacobian to solve for the zero offsets and the link parameters, of which at most 4r + 2p + 6 can be identified independently (34 for the FR3); the calibrated parameters must go into the model that actually computes the joint angles (step ⑦).
  8. IMU、激光、轮子、时钟、负载各要标什么：IMU 的零偏、标度和串扰（六个面），噪声和随机游走（Allan 方差）；激光在底盘上的 x、y、安装角（转一圈让墙变薄）；轮径和轮距（UMBmark）；固定的时间差（互相关、Kalibr）；负载的质量和质心、工具尖的位置（setLoad、枢轴法）（第 ⑧–⑪ 步）。What needs calibrating on the IMU, the lidar, the wheels, the clocks and the load: the IMU’s bias, scale factor and cross-axis coupling (six positions), and its noise and random walk (Allan variance); the lidar’s x, y and mounting angle on the base (spin once so that the walls get thin); the wheel diameter and the track width (UMBmark); the fixed time offset (cross-correlation, Kalibr); the load’s mass and centre of mass, and the position of the tool tip (setLoad, pivot calibration) (steps ⑧–⑪).
  9. 怎么知道标好了，什么时候该重标：看测试误差而不是训练误差，用和标定无关的方法检验任务（摸一下、闭环）；温度、冲击、拆装、磨损、换工具、改软件都会让标定过期，开机查一个固定姿势，慢变的参数在线估，但只有被激励到的才估得出（第 ⑫、⑬ 步）。How do you know the calibration is good, and when should you recalibrate: look at the test error, not the training error, and check the task with a method that has nothing to do with the calibration (the touch test, loop closure); temperature, shocks, taking parts off and putting them back, wear, changing the tool and changing the software all make a calibration expire. Check one fixed pose at start-up and estimate the slowly changing parameters online, but only the ones that are excited can be estimated (steps ⑫ and ⑬).

回到这几篇一直在追的目标：拿到一台陌生的机器人，能看懂、能上手，看不懂的地方知道差什么。读它的误差和标定，就沿着误差链一段段问：这一段的模型是什么，参数从哪来，什么时候标的，怎么检查的。答不上来的，多半差在这几样上：URDF 里的数是设计图上抄来的，从没在这台机器上标过；规格书上的重复精度被当成了绝对精度；标定数据挤在一处，参数分不开；先后顺序反了，后面的把前面的错误吸收了；时间戳在电脑收到时才打；标完以后没有任何检查，拆过一次就再也没人知道它准不准。Back to the goal these pages have been chasing all along: handed an unfamiliar robot, be able to understand it and get hands-on with it, and where you cannot understand it, know what is missing. To read its errors and calibration, go along the error chain link by link and ask: what is this link’s model, where do its parameters come from, when were they calibrated, and how were they checked? Where you cannot answer, the gap is usually one of these: the numbers in the URDF were copied from the design drawings and have never been calibrated on this machine; the repeatability on the datasheet has been taken for absolute accuracy; the calibration data is bunched in one place, so the parameters cannot be separated; the order is the wrong way round, and a later step has absorbed an earlier one’s errors; the time stamps are applied only when the computer receives the data; after calibration there is no check of any kind, so once the robot has been taken apart, nobody knows any more whether it is accurate.
