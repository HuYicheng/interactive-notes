从噪声到样本From Noise to Samples

GAN、VAE、流模型、自回归、扩散、Flow Matching，名字一个接一个，每一种都像是另起炉灶。这一篇只用一个小例子：平面上一朵由几百个点组成的八瓣"花"。所有生成模型都在回答同一个问题：怎样把一把随机数，变成一个像这朵花里的点、又不是照抄的新点？从最简单的办法讲起，每一步都会撞上前一种方法的毛病，于是逼出下一种。走完一圈，再回答几个横跨所有方法的问题：起点为什么总是高斯噪声，怎样让生成又快又听话，机器人为什么也要"生成"动作；最后把几十种生成模型按不同的问题分组放在一起。GAN, VAE, normalizing flows, autoregressive models, diffusion, flow matching: one name after another, each looking like a fresh start. This page uses a single small example: an eight-petalled "flower" in the plane, made of a few hundred points. Every generative model answers the same question: how do you turn a handful of random numbers into a new point that looks like it belongs to the flower, without simply copying one? We start from the simplest approach; at each step the previous method runs into trouble, and that trouble forces the next one into being. After the full round we answer a few questions that cut across all the methods: why the starting point is always Gaussian noise, how to make generation fast and make it do what it is told, and why robots also need to "generate" their actions. Finally, dozens of generative models are put side by side, grouped by one question at a time.

什么是生成What is generation?

这一篇要回答几个问题：什么是生成？生成模型学的到底是什么，生成的又是什么？GAN、VAE、扩散、Flow Matching 等等，各有什么特点和优劣，彼此是什么关系？它们在机器人里又是怎么用的？This page sets out to answer a few questions. What is generation? What does a generative model actually learn, and what does it generate? GANs, VAEs, diffusion, flow matching and the rest: what are the strengths and weaknesses of each, and how are they related? And how are they used in robotics?

先看一张照片。一张 64 × 64 的彩色小图是 64 × 64 × 3 = 12288 个数。让这 12288 个数随便取值，几乎永远只会得到一片雪花；能叫"照片"的那些组合，只占这个巨大空间里小得不可想象的一部分。而且这一部分有形状：相邻像素的颜色相近，边缘连成线，猫的头长在猫的身子上。生成一张新照片，就是在这一小部分里取一个新的点。Start with a photo. A small 64 × 64 colour image is 64 × 64 × 3 = 12 288 numbers. Let those 12 288 numbers take random values and you will almost always get a screen of static; the combinations that deserve to be called "photos" occupy an unimaginably small part of this huge space. And that small part has a shape: neighbouring pixels have similar colours, edges join up into lines, a cat’s head sits on a cat’s body. Generating a new photo means picking a new point in that small part.

一万多维的空间画不出来，所以全篇用一个二维的替身：平面上一朵八瓣的"花"。每片花瓣是一团拉长的点，所有点合起来就是"数据"。它小到能画出来，又保留了真实数据最麻烦的几个特点：数据只占整个空间的一小部分；分成好几块（八片花瓣），块和块之间是空的；每一块的形状都不是圆的。A space of more than ten thousand dimensions cannot be drawn, so the whole page uses a two-dimensional stand-in: an eight-petalled "flower" in the plane. Each petal is an elongated cloud of points, and all the points together are the "data". It is small enough to draw, yet keeps the most awkward features of real data: the data fill only a small part of the space; they come in several pieces (eight petals) with empty space between them; and no piece is round.

那什么叫"生成"？从数据的分布里，抽一个新的样本。"分布"说的是哪里的点多、哪里的点少、哪里根本没有。一个好的生成结果，要同时做到三件事：So what does "generate" mean? Drawing a new sample from the data’s distribution. "Distribution" describes where points are plentiful, where they are scarce, and where there are none at all. A good generated result has to do three things at once:

  • 像：落在花瓣上，而不是花瓣之间的空白里。对照片来说，就是看起来像一张真照片。Look right: land on a petal, not in the empty space between petals. For photos, it means looking like a real photo.
  • 多样：八片花瓣都要有，比例也要对，不能只会生成其中一片。Be varied: all eight petals must appear, in the right proportions; producing only one of them is not enough.
  • 新：不是训练数据里某个点的复制品。Be new: not a copy of some point in the training data.

只做到其中两件，很容易：照抄训练数据，又像又多样，可是不新；在方框里乱撒，又新又多样，可是不像。把所有训练点取个平均更糟：只有一个答案，落在花的正中间，那里一个点也没有，三件里只剩下"新"。下面的交互可以比较这几种"生成"。Doing just two of these is easy. Copying the training data looks right and is varied, but is not new. Scattering points at random in the box is new and varied, but does not look right. Averaging all the training points is worse still: it gives a single answer, right in the middle of the flower, where there is not a single point, so of the three only "new" is left. "取平均"这个结果值得记住。很多机器学习模型学的就是平均：给定输入，预测输出的平均值，用均方误差训练的回归都是这样。只有一个正确答案的时候，这很好；有好几个正确答案的时候，平均值往往落在它们中间的空白里，一个都不是。图片发糊、机器人的动作撞上障碍物，都是这个毛病，后面会一再遇到。The "Take the average" result is worth remembering. Many machine-learning models learn exactly an average: given an input, they predict the average output, as every regression trained with mean squared error does. When there is only one right answer that is fine; when there are several, the average often falls in the empty space between them and is none of them. Blurry images and robot motions that run into obstacles both come from this flaw, and it will keep turning up.

用高斯拼出这朵花Building the flower from Gaussians

最简单的分布是高斯分布（也叫正态分布）：有一个中心，越往外越稀，等密度线是椭圆。它只要两样东西：中心 μ，和协方差 Σ（决定椭圆的大小、扁不扁、朝哪边）。从高斯里抽样也很容易，计算机产生的随机数经过几步运算就行；第 ⑨ 步会细说，为什么大家都这么爱用它。The simplest distribution is the Gaussian (also called the normal distribution): it has a centre, thins out further away, and its contours of equal density are ellipses. It needs only two things: the centre μ, and the covariance Σ (which sets the ellipse’s size, how flat it is and which way it points). Drawing from a Gaussian is easy too: a few operations on the computer’s random numbers will do. Step ⑨ explains in detail why everyone loves it so much.

拿一个高斯去拟合这朵花，结果是一个大圆团：它把八片花瓣和中间的空白一视同仁地盖住，抽出来的点有一大半落在花瓣之间。一个高斯只有一个峰，这朵花却有八个。最自然的补救是多用几个，这就是混合高斯（GMM）：先按比例掷骰子选一个高斯，再从这个高斯里抽一个点。Fit a single Gaussian to the flower and you get one big round blob: it covers the eight petals and the empty space between them alike, and more than half of the points drawn from it land between petals. One Gaussian has one peak; the flower has eight. The natural fix is to use several, which is a Gaussian mixture (GMM): first roll a die, weighted by the proportions, to pick a Gaussian, then draw a point from that Gaussian.

p(x) = Σk=1…K πk · N(x; μk, Σk)

x 处的概率密度，等于每个高斯在 x 处的密度，按各自的权重加起来。The probability density at x is the sum of every Gaussian’s density at x, each multiplied by its weight.

一个高斯只有一个峰；加起来，就能有很多个峰，每个峰的位置、形状、高低都可以不一样。只要高斯够多，几乎任何形状的分布都能这样拼出来。One Gaussian has one peak; add several and you get many peaks, each with its own position, shape and height. With enough Gaussians almost any shape of distribution can be assembled this way.

混合高斯在二维里很好用，在图像那种上万维的空间里却几乎没法用：拿第 0 步那张 64 × 64 的小图来说，一个高斯的协方差就有 12288² ≈ 1.5 亿个数，而要拼出"照片"这种形状，需要的高斯多到数不过来。这正是后面要请出神经网络的原因。Gaussian mixtures work well in two dimensions but are nearly useless in the ten-thousand-plus dimensions of images: for the 64 × 64 picture of step 0, a single Gaussian’s covariance alone has 12288² ≈ 150 million numbers, and assembling a shape like "photos" would take more Gaussians than anyone could count. This is exactly why neural networks are called in later.

参数怎么定？最常用的原则叫最大似然：在所有可能的参数里，挑一组，让训练数据在这个模型下"看起来最可能"。How are the parameters chosen? The most common principle is maximum likelihood: of all possible parameters, pick the set under which the training data "look most likely".

θ* = argmaxθ  (1/N) Σi=1…N log pθ(xi)

最好的参数，是让所有训练点的平均对数概率密度最大的那一组。The best parameters are the ones that maximise the average log probability density of all the training points.

一个模型如果给真实出现的数据很高的概率，它多半抓住了数据的形状。关键在于概率的总量是固定的：整个空间上积分必须等于 1。给这里多分一点，别处就得少分一点，模型没法处处都给高分，只能把概率堆到数据真正在的地方。取对数，是把"所有点的概率相乘"变成"相加"，算起来更稳，而最大值的位置不变。If a model gives high probability to data that actually occurred, it has probably caught the shape of the data. The key is that the total amount of probability is fixed: it must integrate to 1 over the whole space. Give more here and you must give less elsewhere, so the model cannot give high scores everywhere; it can only pile probability where the data really are. Taking the log turns "multiply the probabilities of all the points" into "add them up", which is numerically steadier and leaves the location of the maximum unchanged.

最大似然等价于让模型的分布和数据的分布尽量接近（用第 ③ 步要讲的 KL 散度来量）。后面的方法大多在用某种办法近似它：流模型精确地最大化它，VAE 最大化它的一个下界，扩散的训练目标也可以看成它的一个下界的加权版本；离它最远的是 GAN，完全不碰似然。Maximum likelihood is equivalent to making the model’s distribution as close as possible to the data’s (measured by the KL divergence of step ③). Most of the later methods approximate it one way or another: flows maximise it exactly, VAEs maximise a lower bound on it, and diffusion’s training objective can also be seen as a weighted version of a lower bound on it; the furthest from it is the GAN, which does not touch the likelihood at all.

混合高斯的参数没法一步算出来，标准办法是 EM 算法（Dempster 等 1977）：先随便放好 K 个高斯，然后反复做两件事：按现在的高斯，算每个点有多大可能属于哪个高斯；再按这个分法，重新算每个高斯的中心、形状和权重。每做一轮，似然都不会变小。A Gaussian mixture’s parameters cannot be computed in one go; the standard method is the EM algorithm (Dempster et al. 1977): place K Gaussians anywhere, then repeat two things. Using the current Gaussians, work out how likely each point is to belong to each Gaussian; then, using that split, recompute every Gaussian’s centre, shape and weight. No round ever lowers the likelihood.

"背下来"不算生成Memorising is not generating

如果只看训练集，似然可以想多大有多大：在每个训练点上放一个很窄的小高斯（这叫核密度估计，KDE），宽度越窄，训练点上的密度越高，宽度趋于 0 时，训练集上的似然趋于无穷大。从这个模型抽样，就是挑一个训练点再抖一抖：它把数据背了下来。Judged on the training set alone, the likelihood can be made as large as you like: put a very narrow little Gaussian on every training point (this is kernel density estimation, KDE). The narrower they are, the higher the density at the training points; as the width goes to 0, the likelihood of the training set goes to infinity. Sampling from this model means picking a training point and jiggling it: it has memorised the data.

要看出这个毛病，得留一部分数据不参与拟合，叫测试集，看模型给这些没见过的点打多少分。背下来的模型在测试集上很差：测试点落在训练点之间的空隙里，那里的密度几乎是 0。好的生成模型要学的是数据背后的分布，而不是手里那几百个点。To expose this flaw, hold back part of the data from the fitting, the test set, and see what scores the model gives to these unseen points. A memorising model does badly on the test set: test points land in the gaps between training points, where the density is almost 0. A good generative model has to learn the distribution behind the data, not the few hundred points in hand.

混合高斯在这里好用，是因为我们知道花就是 8 个高斯拼的。真实数据的形状没人知道，维度又高得多，没法再靠手工摆几个高斯。于是换一个思路：不再手工拼概率的公式，而是学一个函数，把简单的随机数变成数据。最直接的做法，是一个可以倒回去的变形：能倒回去，概率还能顺带精确地算出来。The Gaussian mixture works here because we know the flower is made of 8 Gaussians. Nobody knows the shape of real data, and its dimension is far higher, so placing a few Gaussians by hand is no longer an option. Hence a different idea: instead of assembling a formula for the probability by hand, learn a function that turns simple random numbers into data. The most direct version is a deformation that can be run backwards; and because it can be run backwards, the probability can be computed exactly as a bonus.

第 ② 步 · 把噪声变成数据Step ② · Turning noise into data

一层层可逆的变形Invertible deformations, layer by layer

从高斯里抽样很容易。如果有一个函数 f，能把高斯里抽出的点 z 变成像数据的点 x = f(z)，生成就解决了：抽一个 z，算一下 f(z) 就行。问题变成：这个 f 怎么找？Drawing from a Gaussian is easy. If some function f turns a point z drawn from a Gaussian into a data-like point x = f(z), generation is solved: draw a z and compute f(z). The question becomes: how do we find this f?

先看一维。把花投影到横轴上，得到一个有三个峰的一维分布：两片竖着的花瓣投影到一起，成了中间那个尖峰；左右两边各有三片花瓣叠在一起，成了两个宽峰。一维里，这样的 f 一定存在，而且能直接写出来：按排名一一对应。噪声 z 在所有噪声里排在第百分之几，就把它变成数据里同样排在第百分之几的那个 x。"排在第百分之几"就是累积分布函数：数据的记作 F(x)，标准高斯的记作 Φ(z)，于是 x = F⁻¹(Φ(z))。这是从一维分布里抽样的一个基本办法，叫逆变换抽样（高斯另有更快的办法，见第 ⑨ 步的 Box–Muller）。Start in one dimension. Project the flower onto the horizontal axis and you get a one-dimensional distribution with three peaks: the two upright petals project onto the same spot and form the spike in the middle, and on each side three petals overlap into one broad peak. In one dimension such an f always exists and can be written down directly: match by rank. If a noise value z sits at some percentile among all noise values, map it to the x at the same percentile of the data. "Which percentile" is the cumulative distribution function: F(x) for the data and Φ(z) for the standard Gaussian, so x = F⁻¹(Φ(z)). This is a basic way of sampling from a one-dimensional distribution, called inverse transform sampling (for the Gaussian there is a faster way: the Box–Muller transform of step ⑨).

要紧的是看密度怎么跟着变：What matters is how the density changes along with it:

pX(x) = pZ(z) ÷ |dx/dz|

变形之后 x 处的密度，等于原来 z 处的密度，除以这里被拉伸的倍数。After the deformation, the density at x equals the density at z divided by how much the space was stretched there.

概率不会凭空多出来，也不会凭空少掉：z 附近一小段里的概率，变形之后全部落在 x 附近对应的那一小段里。这一段被拉长了几倍，同样多的概率摊在更长的一段上，密度就稀了几倍；被压短了，密度就变高。Probability is neither created nor lost: the probability in a small stretch around z all ends up in the corresponding small stretch around x. If that stretch has been lengthened several-fold, the same amount of probability is spread over a longer stretch and the density drops by the same factor; if it has been shortened, the density rises.

二维以上，"拉伸倍数"换成面积（或体积）的放大倍数，就是雅可比矩阵 ∂x/∂z 的行列式：pX(x) = pZ(z) ÷ |det ∂x/∂z|。用这个公式有两个前提：f 可逆（每个 x 只能来自一个 z），行列式算得出来。流模型的全部约束和全部麻烦，都来自这两条。In two or more dimensions, the "stretch" becomes the factor by which area (or volume) is enlarged: the determinant of the Jacobian matrix ∂x/∂z, so pX(x) = pZ(z) ÷ |det ∂x/∂z|. The formula needs two things: f must be invertible (each x can come from only one z), and the determinant must be computable. All of a flow model’s constraints, and all of its troubles, come from these two.

二维以上，没有"排名"这么简单的对应办法，但思路一样：找一个可逆的变形，把高斯捏成花。复杂的变形不好直接设计，就用很多个简单的叠起来，每一个都可逆、拉伸倍数都好算；叠起来以后，总的拉伸倍数就是各层的乘积。这叫标准化流（normalizing flow；"标准化"是说反过来，它能把数据变回标准高斯，Rezende & Mohamed 2015）。最常用的简单变形叫耦合层（NICE 2014、RealNVP 2016）：In two or more dimensions there is no simple rank matching, but the idea is the same: find an invertible deformation that squeezes the Gaussian into the flower. A complicated deformation is hard to design directly, so stack many simple ones, each invertible and each with an easy stretch; once they are stacked, the total stretch is the product of the layers’. This is a normalizing flow ("normalizing" because, run backwards, it turns data back into a standard Gaussian; Rezende & Mohamed 2015). The most common simple deformation is the coupling layer (NICE 2014, RealNVP 2016):

x1 = z1，  x2 = z2 · es(z1) + t(z1)x1 = z1, x2 = z2 · es(z1) + t(z1)

第一个坐标原样不动；第二个坐标先乘一个倍数、再加一个偏移，倍数和偏移都由第一个坐标决定。The first coordinate stays as it is; the second is first multiplied by a factor and then shifted, and both the factor and the shift are set by the first coordinate.

这样设计，可逆和拉伸倍数都是白送的。倒回去时，z1 = x1 直接就知道，于是 s 和 t 都能算出来，z2 = (x2 − t) · e−s；面积的拉伸倍数就是 es(z1)，一个数而已。而 s 和 t 可以是任意复杂的神经网络，它们自己并不需要可逆。一层只能"看着第一个坐标去推第二个"，所以层与层之间要换着推：这里每一层后面，再把整个平面转一个固定的角度。With this design, invertibility and the stretch come for free. Going backwards, z1 = x1 is known at once, so s and t can be computed, and z2 = (x2 − t) · e−s; the area stretch is just es(z1), a single number. Yet s and t can be neural networks as complicated as you like, and they do not themselves have to be invertible. A layer can only "push the second coordinate according to the first", so the layers must take turns: here every layer is followed by turning the whole plane through a fixed angle.

因为能精确算出 log p(x)，流模型可以直接用最大似然训练，也能回答"这个点有多大可能"。代价有两个。一是网络被"可逆"绑住了：每层输入多少维，输出就得多少维，不能先压缩再展开，每层的结构也受限制，同样多的参数，表达力不如不受约束的网络。二是连续又可逆的变形不能把平面撕开：一整块高斯只能被捏细，不能被剪成八块，花瓣之间总会留下细细的"桥"。Because log p(x) can be computed exactly, a flow can be trained directly by maximum likelihood and can also answer "how likely is this point?". The price comes in two parts. First, the network is tied down by invertibility: every layer must output as many dimensions as it takes in, so it cannot compress and then expand, and each layer’s structure is constrained; for the same number of parameters it is less expressive than an unconstrained network. Second, a continuous, invertible deformation cannot tear the plane: one Gaussian blob can be pinched thin but never cut into eight pieces, so thin "bridges" always remain between the petals.

把这条路走得最远的是 Glow（Kingma & Dhariwal 2018），能生成相当清晰的人脸。另一个方向是把"一层层"换成连续的时间：让点沿着一个由神经网络给出的速度场流动，解一个常微分方程（ODE），就能从噪声走到数据（Neural ODE 2018、FFJORD 2018）。这样就不再需要特殊的耦合结构，可训练时要反复解这个方程，非常慢。第 ⑧ 步的 Flow Matching 正是它的后代：保留"沿着速度场流动"，换掉了训练的办法。The flow that went furthest down this road is Glow (Kingma & Dhariwal 2018), which can generate fairly sharp faces. Another direction replaces the "layers" with continuous time: let the points flow along a velocity field given by a neural network and solve an ordinary differential equation (ODE) to get from noise to data (Neural ODE 2018, FFJORD 2018). That removes the need for a special coupling structure, but training has to solve the equation again and again and is very slow. The flow matching of step ⑧ is its descendant: it keeps "flowing along a velocity field" and replaces the way of training.

这里先记下一个问题：起点为什么总是标准高斯？为什么不是别的分布，甚至干脆什么都不要？这个问题横跨所有方法，第 ⑨ 步专门回答。Let us note one question here: why is the starting point always a standard Gaussian? Why not some other distribution, or nothing at all? The question cuts across every method, and step ⑨ is devoted to it.

流模型的毛病都出在"可逆"上。如果放开这一条，允许网络先把数据压缩成更短的编码，再从编码还原呢？这就是 VAE。All of a flow’s troubles come from invertibility. What if we drop that requirement and let the network first compress the data into a shorter code and then reconstruct it from the code? That is the VAE.

第 ③ 步 · VAEStep ③ · VAE

先压缩，再还原Compress, then reconstruct

先看一个更朴素的东西：自编码器。一个网络（编码器）把数据压成一段很短的编码 z（也叫隐变量），另一个网络（解码器）再从 z 还原出数据，训练目标就是还原得像。一张照片能压成几百个数、再还原得八九不离十，说明这几百个数抓住了照片的要点。Start with something plainer: an autoencoder. One network (the encoder) squeezes the data into a short code z (also called a latent variable), another (the decoder) reconstructs the data from z, and the training goal is simply a faithful reconstruction. That a photo can be squeezed into a few hundred numbers and reconstructed almost exactly shows that those numbers capture what matters in it.

能不能拿它来生成：随便造一个 z，交给解码器？不行。普通自编码器的编码散得毫无规律，训练样本的编码之间是大片没人去过的空隙，随手造的 z 多半落在空隙里，解码出来的东西没有意义。Can it be used to generate, by making up a z and handing it to the decoder? No. An ordinary autoencoder’s codes are scattered without any pattern; between the codes of the training samples lie large gaps nobody has visited, so a made-up z usually lands in a gap and decodes to something meaningless.

变分自编码器（VAE，Kingma & Welling 2013；Rezende 等 2014）做了两处改动。一是编码器不再给出一个点，而是给出一小团云：一个有中心、有宽度的高斯，训练时从云里随机取一个 z 去还原。这逼着解码器对 z 附近的一片都给出合理的结果，而不只是那一个点。二是把每一团云都往标准高斯 N(0, I) 那边拉：中心往原点拉，宽度往 1 拉。还原要云小、彼此分开，这一条要云大、都挤到原点；两头一拉扯，结果是一团团不大的云挤在原点附近，合起来大致铺满一个标准高斯。这样，生成就有了现成的办法：从标准高斯里抽一个 z，交给解码器。The variational autoencoder (VAE; Kingma & Welling 2013; Rezende et al. 2014) makes two changes. First, the encoder no longer gives a point but a small cloud: a Gaussian with a centre and a width, and training reconstructs from a z drawn at random from the cloud. This forces the decoder to give sensible results for a whole patch around z, not just one point. Second, every cloud is pulled towards the standard Gaussian N(0, I): its centre towards the origin, its width towards 1. Reconstruction wants the clouds small and apart; this pull wants them large and piled at the origin. The tug-of-war leaves many modest clouds crowded around the origin, which together roughly fill a standard Gaussian. Generation then has a ready-made recipe: draw a z from the standard Gaussian and hand it to the decoder.

log pθ(x) ≥ Ez∼q(z|x)[ log pθ(x | z) ] − KL( q(z|x) ‖ N(0, I) )

一个点的对数概率，至少是"从它的编码云里取一个 z，能把它还原得多好"，减去"这团云离标准高斯有多远"。A point’s log probability is at least "how well it can be reconstructed from a z drawn from its code cloud" minus "how far that cloud is from the standard Gaussian".

本来想直接做最大似然，可 pθ(x) 要对所有可能的 z 求积分，算不出来。好在只要编码器给出一团云 q(z|x)，就能证明右边这一项一定不大于 log pθ(x)（证明只用到一次 Jensen 不等式），所以叫证据下界（ELBO；"证据"说的就是 log pθ(x) 本身，"变分"说的是拿一个可以调的分布 q 去近似算不出来的东西）。把右边往上推，就是在把 log pθ(x) 往上推。右边两项正好在拉扯：第一项要每个点的编码又准、又彼此分得开，第二项要每一团云都尽量变成标准高斯本身。We would like to do maximum likelihood directly, but pθ(x) requires integrating over every possible z, which cannot be done. Fortunately, as long as the encoder provides a cloud q(z|x), one can prove that the right-hand side is never larger than log pθ(x) (the proof needs just one use of Jensen’s inequality), hence the name evidence lower bound (ELBO; the "evidence" is log pθ(x) itself, and "variational" refers to using an adjustable distribution q to approximate something that cannot be computed). Pushing the right-hand side up pushes log pθ(x) up. Its two terms pull against each other: the first wants each point’s code to be precise and well separated from the others, the second wants every cloud to become the standard Gaussian itself, as far as it can.

编码要准，又不能太"个性"：每一团云都被往标准高斯那边拉，结果大家挤在一起，不留空洞。The codes must be precise, but not too "individual": every cloud is pulled towards the standard Gaussian, and as a result they all crowd together without leaving holes.

解码器是宽度 σ 的高斯时，第一项就是 −|x − x̂|² ÷ (2σ²) 再加一个常数，所以训练时最小化的是"均方误差 + β × KL"，其中 β = 2σ²。交互里 β = 0.3 时，KL 约 2.5，测试集上的均方重建误差约 0.23；β = 10 时，KL 降到 0，重建误差约 4.1，和直接猜"所有数据的平均"差不多（花的点到花心的平均平方距离约 4.2）。When the decoder is a Gaussian of width σ, the first term is just −|x − x̂|² ÷ (2σ²) plus a constant, so training minimises "mean squared error + β × KL", with β = 2σ². In the interactive, at β = 0.3 the KL is about 2.5 and the mean squared reconstruction error on the test set about 0.23; at β = 10 the KL drops to 0 and the reconstruction error is about 4.1, about as bad as simply guessing "the average of all the data" (the mean squared distance of the flower’s points from its centre is about 4.2).

为什么 VAE 的样本总是发糊？解码器用均方误差训练，它学到的是"给定 z，x 的平均"。几个不同的 x 被编到相近的 z 时，解码器只能输出它们的平均：在这里，就是落在两片花瓣之间；在图片里，就是几张清晰的图叠在一起，糊了。把 KL 的权重调小，编码更准、重叠更少，可编码之间的空隙又多了起来。VAE 一直在这两头之间找平衡。Why are VAE samples always blurry? The decoder is trained with mean squared error, so what it learns is "the average x given z". When several different x are encoded to nearby z, the decoder can only output their average: here, a point between two petals; in images, several sharp pictures stacked on top of each other, which is a blur. Lower the KL weight and the codes become more precise and overlap less, but the gaps between codes multiply again. A VAE is always balancing between these two ends.

第 ④ 步 · GANStep ④ · GAN

两个网络对着练Two networks trained against each other

生成对抗网络（GAN，Goodfellow 等 2014）不再写出任何概率，也不再拿生成的样本和某个真值去比距离。它请来第二个网络当评委：判别器 D 看一个点，打一个 0 到 1 之间的分，表示"这是真数据"的把握。生成器 G 把噪声 z 变成样本 G(z)，它的目标只有一个：让判别器把它的样本判成真的。两个网络轮流训练：The generative adversarial network (GAN; Goodfellow et al. 2014) writes down no probability at all, and no longer measures how far a generated sample is from some true value. Instead it brings in a second network as judge: the discriminator D looks at a point and gives a score between 0 and 1, its confidence that "this is real data". The generator G turns noise z into a sample G(z), and it has a single goal: to get the discriminator to call its samples real. The two networks are trained in turns:

minG maxD  Ex[ log D(x) ] + Ez[ log(1 − D(G(z))) ]

判别器想把真数据判成真、把生成的样本判成假；生成器想让判别器把它的样本判成真。The discriminator wants to call real data real and generated samples fake; the generator wants the discriminator to call its samples real.

生成器从来看不到真数据，它只看得到判别器的意见：判别器的打分对样本位置求导，告诉每个样本往哪挪会显得更真（交互里的小箭头）。判别器学得越好，这个方向就越准。可以证明，对上面这个 min-max 的写法，判别器每次都训练到最好时，生成器其实在缩小生成分布和数据分布之间的一种距离（JS 散度）；两个分布完全一样时，判别器只能五五开地瞎猜，打分处处是 0.5。The generator never sees real data; all it sees is the discriminator’s opinion. The derivative of the discriminator’s score with respect to a sample’s position tells each sample which way to move to look more real (the little arrows in the interactive). The better the discriminator, the more accurate that direction. One can prove that, for the min-max form above, when the discriminator is trained to its best every time, the generator is in effect shrinking a particular distance (the JS divergence) between the generated distribution and the data distribution; when the two are identical, the discriminator can only guess fifty-fifty and scores 0.5 everywhere.

一个造假的，一个鉴定的。鉴定的越厉害，造假的就被逼得越像。A forger and an appraiser. The sharper the appraiser, the better the forger is forced to become.

实际训练时，生成器通常不去最小化 log(1 − D(G(z)))，而是最大化 log D(G(z))。原因是刚开始时生成的样本很假，判别器一眼就能看穿，D(G(z)) 接近 0，前一种写法在那里几乎是平的，梯度太小，生成器学不动；后一种写法在那里最陡。交互里用的就是后一种。In practice the generator usually does not minimise log(1 − D(G(z))) but maximises log D(G(z)). The reason is that at the start its samples are obviously fake, the discriminator sees through them at once, and D(G(z)) is close to 0, where the first form is almost flat: the gradient is too small for the generator to learn. The second form is steepest there. The interactive uses the second form.

损失里没有"拿样本和某个真值比距离"这一项，生成器不会被逼着输出几个答案的平均，所以样本可以很锐利，不会糊在花瓣之间。可也没有任何东西强迫生成器覆盖所有花瓣：它只要让当下的判别器满意就行。于是会出现模式坍塌：所有噪声都被映射到判别器眼下最喜欢的一小块；判别器学会拒绝那里，生成器就整体跳到另一块……两个网络可能就这样绕圈追逐，不一定收敛到一个平衡点。The loss has no term that "compares a sample with some true value by distance", so the generator is not pushed into outputting the average of several answers, and samples can be sharp rather than smeared between petals. But nothing forces the generator to cover every petal either: it only has to satisfy the current discriminator. Hence mode collapse: all the noise is mapped to the one small patch the discriminator likes best at the moment; the discriminator learns to reject that patch, and the generator jumps wholesale to another… The two networks may chase each other in circles like this and need not converge to an equilibrium.

GAN 的毛病，大部分出在"两个网络互相追"上：没有一个固定的目标，训练不一定收敛；坍塌了也不容易发现，因为损失曲线本身说明不了样本好不好。这些年的补救大致有三类：Most of a GAN’s troubles come from "two networks chasing each other": there is no fixed target, so training need not converge, and collapse is hard to spot because the loss curve by itself says little about sample quality. Over the years the remedies have fallen into roughly three kinds:

  • 换一种距离。WGAN（Arjovsky 等 2017）让判别器估计两个分布之间的"推土机距离"（把一堆土推成另一堆的最小功），即使两个分布毫不重叠，它也能给出有意义的方向；为了让这个估计成立，判别器要足够平滑，于是有了梯度惩罚（WGAN-GP）。Use a different distance. WGAN (Arjovsky et al. 2017) has the discriminator estimate the "earth mover’s distance" between the two distributions (the least work needed to push one pile of earth into the shape of the other), which still gives meaningful directions even when the two distributions do not overlap at all. For that estimate to hold the discriminator must be smooth enough, hence the gradient penalty (WGAN-GP).
  • 让判别器看一批而不是一个。它若能同时看到好几个样本，就能发现"它们都一样"，交互里的"看两个"就是 PacGAN（Lin 等 2018）的做法。Let the discriminator look at a batch, not a single sample. If it sees several samples at once, it can notice that "they are all the same"; the "sees pairs" setting in the interactive is the approach of PacGAN (Lin et al. 2018).
  • 大量工程上的稳定技巧：网络结构（DCGAN）、规范化、渐进地加大分辨率，再加上大模型、大批量（BigGAN）。StyleGAN（Karras 等 2019）生成的人脸，一度真假难辨。Many engineering tricks for stability: the network architecture (DCGAN), normalisation, growing the resolution gradually, plus big models and big batches (BigGAN). The faces generated by StyleGAN (Karras et al. 2019) were for a while hard to tell from real photos.

GAN 一步就能生成，样本锐利；但训练不稳，会漏掉一部分数据，也算不出概率。2021 年以后，图像生成的主角换成了扩散模型（Dhariwal & Nichol 2021 的标题就是"扩散模型在图像生成上打败了 GAN"）。可"对抗"这个想法一直没退场：机器人里用判别器当奖励来模仿示范（第 ⑪ 步的 GAIL 模仿整个行为，AMP 模仿动作的风格），把扩散压缩成一步时也常常借助一个判别器（第 ⑩ 步）。A GAN generates in one step and its samples are sharp, but training is unstable, it drops part of the data, and it cannot compute probabilities. After 2021, diffusion models took over as the leading approach to image generation (the title of Dhariwal & Nichol 2021 is literally "Diffusion models beat GANs on image synthesis"). But the adversarial idea never left the stage: in robotics a discriminator serves as a reward for imitating demonstrations (in step ⑪, GAIL imitates the whole behaviour and AMP the style of motion), and squeezing diffusion into a single step often relies on a discriminator too (step ⑩).

到这里，流、VAE、GAN 都是一步就把整个样本变出来，可各有各的毛病：流被"可逆"绑住，VAE 发糊，GAN 锐利却不稳、会漏，还算不出概率。有没有一种办法，概率能精确地算，训练只是普通的分类，几个峰都画得出来？有：把一个样本拆成一串数，一次只生成一个。So far, flows, VAEs and GANs all produce a whole sample in one step, and each has its own problem: flows are bound by invertibility, VAEs are blurry, GANs are sharp but unstable, drop part of the data and cannot compute probabilities. Is there a way to compute probabilities exactly, train with nothing but ordinary classification, and still draw several peaks? There is: split a sample into a string of numbers and generate them one at a time.

第 ⑤ 步 · 自回归Step ⑤ · Autoregression

一次只生成一个数One number at a time

一张图是一长串像素，一句话是一长串词，机器人的一段动作是一长串关节角度。任何一串数一起出现的概率，都可以拆成一连串"已知前面的，下一个是什么"的概率相乘：An image is a long string of pixels, a sentence a long string of words, a stretch of robot motion a long string of joint angles. The probability of any string of numbers occurring together can be split into a chain of "given what came before, what comes next" probabilities multiplied together:

p(x1, x2, …, xn) = p(x1) · p(x2 | x1) · p(x3 | x1, x2) ⋯ p(xn | x1, …, xn−1)

一串数一起出现的概率，等于第一个数的概率，乘以已知第一个时第二个的概率，一直乘到已知前面所有数时最后一个的概率。The probability of a string of numbers occurring together equals the probability of the first, times the probability of the second given the first, and so on up to the probability of the last given all the ones before it.

这是概率的乘法规则，不是近似，对任何分布都成立。它把一个很难的问题（一下子写出整串数的分布）拆成了很多个容易的问题：每次只预测一个数。而"预测一个数的分布"正是分类器擅长的事：把这个数可能的取值切成格子，网络给每个格子一个概率。这样的分类器能表示任何形状的分布，有几个峰都行。This is the product rule of probability: not an approximation, it holds for every distribution. It turns one very hard problem (writing down the distribution of the whole string at once) into many easy ones: predict one number at a time. And "predict the distribution of one number" is exactly what a classifier is good at: cut the number’s possible values into bins and let the network give each bin a probability. Such a classifier can represent a distribution of any shape, with as many peaks as needed.

In the interactive, 96% of the first kind’s samples land on a petal and only about 45% of the second’s.

训练很简单：每个位置都是一个分类问题，用交叉熵训练（这正是最大似然），而且所有位置可以并行地一起算，因为前文都是现成的真实数据。生成却只能一个一个来：n 个数要走 n 步。随机性藏在每一步的"抽签"里。抽之前还可以调一个温度 T：把每个格子的概率先取 1/T 次方，再归一化。温度越低越保守；温度为 0 时，每一步都选最可能的那个，结果就完全确定了。Training is simple: every position is a classification problem trained with cross-entropy (which is exactly maximum likelihood), and all positions can be computed in parallel because the preceding text is real data that is already there. Generation, though, goes one at a time: n numbers take n steps. The randomness lies in the "draw" at each step. Before drawing, a temperature T can be applied: raise each bin’s probability to the power 1/T, then renormalise. The lower the temperature, the more conservative; at temperature 0 every step picks the most likely option and the result is completely determined.

大语言模型就是这样工作的：把文字切成词元（token），每次给出下一个词元的概率，抽一个接在后面，再预测下一个（GPT-3）。预测下一个的网络通常是 Transformer（Vaswani 等 2017），它能同时看到前面所有的词元。This is how large language models work: cut text into tokens, give the probability of the next token, draw one and append it, then predict the next (GPT-3). The network that predicts the next token is usually a Transformer (Vaswani et al. 2017), which can see all the earlier tokens at once.

图像也能这么生成。最早是逐个像素来（PixelRNN、PixelCNN 2016），声音也有逐个采样点生成的 WaveNet。可一张图有几十万个数，逐个生成太慢，后来大多先用第 ③ 步的 VQ-VAE 把图压成几百个整数（码本里的编号），再自回归地生成这些整数；也有按"从粗到细"的顺序，一次生成一整层分辨率的（VAR 2024）。Images can be generated this way too. The first attempts went pixel by pixel (PixelRNN, PixelCNN 2016), and sound got WaveNet, which generates one sample at a time. But an image has hundreds of thousands of numbers, too many to generate one by one, so later models mostly first use step ③’s VQ-VAE to compress an image into a few hundred integers (entries in a codebook) and then generate those integers autoregressively; others go "coarse to fine", generating a whole level of resolution at a time (VAR 2024).

机器人也一样。RT-2（Brohan 等 2023）把每个动作维度切成 256 个格子，当成 256 个"词"，让一个视觉–语言大模型像说话一样，把动作一个词一个词地"说"出来。FAST（Pertsch 等 2025）先用和 JPEG 类似的频域压缩，把一整段动作压成少得多的词元，再自回归地生成它们。Robots too. RT-2 (Brohan et al. 2023) cuts every action dimension into 256 bins, treats them as 256 "words", and has a vision–language model "speak" the action word by word as if it were talking. FAST (Pertsch et al. 2025) first uses JPEG-like frequency-domain compression to squeeze a whole stretch of actions into far fewer tokens, then generates them autoregressively.

自回归的代价也很清楚：一个一个生成，慢；前面抽错了，后面只能将错就错；连续的量要切成格子，切粗了不准，切细了格子太多。另外，"先 x1 后 x2"这个顺序是人定的，对文字很自然，对图片和动作并不天然。The costs of autoregression are just as clear: generating one at a time is slow; a mistake early on has to be lived with afterwards; and continuous quantities have to be cut into bins, which is imprecise if they are coarse and makes for too many bins if they are fine. Besides, the order "x1 first, then x2" is chosen by people: natural for text, not for images or actions.

到这里，模型学的要么是概率本身（混合高斯、流、自回归），要么是一个从噪声到数据的函数（VAE、GAN）。学概率本身的那几种，为了让概率加起来正好等于 1，处处受限：只能拼高斯，只能用可逆的变形，还得规定顺序、把连续的量切成格子。能不能干脆不管这个"等于 1"？这就是第三条路：不学概率是多少，只学概率往哪边升高。So far, a model has learned either the probability itself (Gaussian mixtures, flows, autoregression) or a function from noise to data (VAEs, GANs). The ones that learn the probability itself are hemmed in everywhere by the need for it to add up to exactly 1: only sums of Gaussians, only invertible deformations, a fixed order and continuous values cut into bins. Could we simply ignore this "adds up to 1"? That is the third road: learn not what the probability is, but only which way it increases.

第 ⑥ 步 · 能量和分数Step ⑥ · Energy and score

只学"往哪走更像数据"Learning only "which way looks more like data"

写出一个概率分布，最难的是"总和等于 1"这个要求。混合高斯、流、自回归都靠特殊结构来保证它：每个高斯自己积分为 1；可逆变形不改变概率的总量；乘法规则里每个因子都已经归一化。代价是这些结构限制了模型能长成什么样。能量模型（EBM）干脆放开这一条：网络只给每个点打一个分 E(x)，叫"能量"，能量越低越像数据，然后The hardest part of writing down a probability distribution is the requirement that it "sums to 1". Gaussian mixtures, flows and autoregressive models all guarantee it through special structure: each Gaussian integrates to 1 by itself; an invertible deformation keeps the total amount of probability; each factor in the product rule is already normalised. The price is that these structures limit what the model can look like. An energy-based model (EBM) simply drops the requirement: the network only gives each point a rating E(x), its "energy", with lower energy meaning more like the data, and then

p(x) = e−E(x) ÷ Z，  Z = ∫ e−E(x) dxp(x) = e−E(x) ÷ Z, Z = ∫ e−E(x) dx

一个点的概率，正比于 e 的负能量次方；Z 是把所有点的 e−E 加起来，好让总和等于 1。A point’s probability is proportional to e raised to minus its energy; Z adds up e−E over all points so that the total is 1.

e−E 永远是正数，不管网络输出什么，它都是一个合法的（只是没归一化的）密度，所以 E 可以是任意形状的网络。物理里的玻尔兹曼分布就是这个样子：能量越低的状态越常见。e−E is always positive, so whatever the network outputs, it is a legitimate (just unnormalised) density, and E can be a network of any shape. The Boltzmann distribution of physics looks exactly like this: the lower a state’s energy, the more common it is.

E(x)
能量：网络给 x 打的分，越低越像数据The energy: the network’s rating of x, lower meaning more like the data
Z
归一化常数：所有点的 e−E 之和（对整个空间积分）The normalising constant: the sum of e−E over all points (an integral over the whole space)

网络只管说"这里像不像"，不管总分是不是 1。The network only says "does this look right here", without caring whether the total comes to 1.

混合高斯也能写成这个样子：E(x) = −log p(x)，Z = 1。可对一个随便的网络，Z 是对整个空间的积分，在一万多维的空间里根本算不出来。A Gaussian mixture can also be written this way: E(x) = −log p(x), Z = 1. But for an arbitrary network, Z is an integral over the whole space and simply cannot be computed in more than ten thousand dimensions.

算不出 Z，就算不出 log p(x)，最大似然没法直接用，抽样也没有现成的办法。能量模型几十年来时起时落，卡的都是这个 Z（Du & Mordatch 2019 靠迭代采样训练出了相当不错的能量模型）。Without Z there is no log p(x), so maximum likelihood cannot be used directly, and there is no ready-made way to sample either. Energy-based models have risen and fallen over the decades, always stuck on this Z (Du & Mordatch 2019 trained quite good energy models with iterative sampling).

好在有一个量和 Z 无关：log p(x) 对位置 x 的梯度。Fortunately one quantity has nothing to do with Z: the gradient of log p(x) with respect to the position x.

s(x) = ∇log p(x) = −∇E(x)

分数，就是对数密度上升最快的方向，也就是能量下降最快的方向。The score is the direction in which the log density rises fastest, which is also the direction in which the energy falls fastest.

log p(x) = −E(x) − log Z，而 log Z 是一个常数，一求梯度就消失了。所以只需要知道每个点"往哪走能量更低"，完全不需要 Z。log p(x) = −E(x) − log Z, and log Z is a constant that vanishes as soon as you take the gradient. So all you need is "which way is lower energy" at each point; Z is not needed at all.

分数（score）：x 处的那个向量。它是一个方向，和前面"打分""测试分"里的那个数不是一回事Score: the vector at x. It is a direction, not the same thing as the single number in the "scores" given to points or the "test score" earlier

分数像一个指南针：站在任何地方，它都指向"更像数据"的方向。The score is like a compass: wherever you stand, it points towards "more like the data".

对一个中心为 μ、协方差为 σ²I 的高斯，s(x) = −(x − μ) ÷ σ²：永远指向中心，离得越远越用力；σ 越小越陡。For a Gaussian with centre μ and covariance σ²I, s(x) = −(x − μ) ÷ σ²: it always points to the centre, pulling harder the further away you are, and the smaller σ, the steeper it is.

分数不是概率，可光有它就能抽样（下面的朗之万），也能直接从数据里学（再下面的去噪分数匹配）。The score is not a probability, yet it alone is enough to sample (Langevin dynamics, below) and it can be learned directly from data (denoising score matching, further below).

有了分数，怎么抽样？顺着它往上爬，同时随机抖一抖：With the score in hand, how do we sample? Climb along it while jiggling at random:

x ← x + (η/2) · ∇log p(x) + √η · ξ

每一步：顺着分数走一小步，再加一点随机的抖动。Each step: move a little along the score, then add a little random jiggle.

只往上爬，所有点最后都会挤到峰顶，那是在找"最可能的点"，而不是抽样。加上随机抖动，点就会在峰附近按密度散开：密的地方待得久，稀的地方很快离开。步子足够小、走得足够久，点的分布就正好是 p(x)。这叫朗之万动力学，它原本描述的是悬浮在水里的小颗粒怎样被分子撞来撞去。Only climbing would crowd every point onto the peaks, which finds "the most likely points" rather than sampling. With random jiggles added, the points spread out around the peaks according to the density: they linger where it is dense and leave quickly where it is sparse. With small enough steps taken for long enough, the points are distributed exactly as p(x). This is Langevin dynamics, which originally described how small particles suspended in water get knocked about by molecules.

"走得足够久"是个大问题。花瓣之间的空地，对一个小步抖动的点来说就是一堵墙：进了一片花瓣，几乎永远出不来。于是每片花瓣最后分到多少点，取决于点从哪里出发，而不是数据里的比例。"Long enough" is a big problem. To a point taking small, jiggly steps, the empty ground between petals is a wall: once inside a petal, it almost never gets out. So how many points each petal ends up with depends on where the points started, not on the proportions in the data.

分数又怎么学？数据只是一堆点，没人告诉我们每个点的 ∇log p。办法叫去噪分数匹配（Vincent 2011）：给数据点加一点高斯噪声，训练网络在加了噪声的位置上指回原来的点。And how is the score learned? The data are just a pile of points; nobody tells us ∇log p at each one. The method is denoising score matching (Vincent 2011): add a little Gaussian noise to the data points and train the network to point from each noisy position back to the original point.

L(θ) = Ex, ε ‖ sθ(x + σε) + ε/σ ‖²

一个加了噪声的点，可能来自好几个原来的点，网络不知道是哪一个，用平方误差训练，它就会输出所有可能方向的平均。这个平均恰好就是"被噪声糊过的数据"的分数 ∇log pσ。这一次，"取平均"不是毛病，正是我们要的东西。A noisy point could have come from several original points; the network cannot know which, so trained with squared error it outputs the average of all the possible directions. That average is exactly the score ∇log pσ of "the data blurred by the noise". This time, "taking the average" is not a flaw but exactly what we want.

一个数据点加了 σ = 0.1 的噪声，被推开 0.1 左右：训练目标 −ε/σ 指回原来的点，长度约 |ε|/σ ≈ 10。σ 越小，分数越陡。A data point with noise σ = 0.1 added gets pushed about 0.1 away: the training target −ε/σ points back to the original point with length about |ε|/σ ≈ 10. The smaller σ, the steeper the score.

网络只在加了噪声的点上受过训练。σ 很小时，这些点紧贴着花瓣，离花瓣远的地方网络从没见过，那里的分数全靠"猜"；在图像那种一万多维的空间里，数据只占极薄的一层，这个问题严重得多。The network is trained only at noisy points. With small σ these points hug the petals, and far from the petals the network has never seen anything, so its score there is pure "guesswork"; in the ten-thousand-plus dimensions of images, where the data occupy an extremely thin layer, the problem is far worse.

Song 和 Ermon 的办法（NCSN，噪声条件分数网络，Song & Ermon 2019）是同时用很多个噪声级别，把 σ 也当成网络的输入：大噪声把数据糊成一大团，整个空间都有训练样本，分数又简单又可靠；小噪声保留细节。采样时从最大的噪声开始跑朗之万，跑一阵就把 σ 降一级，一直降到最小。这叫退火朗之万。The remedy of Song and Ermon (NCSN, noise-conditional score network, Song & Ermon 2019) is to use many noise levels at once, with σ as an extra input to the network: large noise blurs the data into one big blob, so there are training samples all over the space and the score is simple and reliable; small noise keeps the detail. Sampling starts Langevin dynamics at the largest noise, runs it for a while, lowers σ one level, and so on down to the smallest. This is annealed Langevin dynamics.

退火一下子解决了两件事：大噪声时，花瓣之间的空地被填平了，点可以自由地在花瓣之间走动，比例自然就对了；小噪声时只做局部的修整。可它要走很多步，每一级跑多少步、步长多大，全靠手调。扩散模型从另一个方向走到了同一个地方，而且把每一步该怎么走都算得清清楚楚。Annealing solves two things at once: with large noise the empty ground between petals is filled in, so points move freely between petals and the proportions come out right; with small noise it only tidies up locally. 先加噪，再一步步去噪Add noise, then remove it step by step

扩散模型的想法其实比 NCSN 还早（Sohl-Dickstein 等 2015），到 DDPM（Ho 等 2020）才真正好用。它也是从大噪声一级级走到小噪声，只是把整件事做成了一个干净的框架：先规定一个"加噪"的过程，让数据一点点变成纯噪声；再训练一个网络，学会把这个过程倒回去。加噪的规则非常简单：The idea of diffusion models is actually older than NCSN (Sohl-Dickstein et al. 2015), but it only became really useful with DDPM (Ho et al. 2020). It too goes from large noise to small, level by level, but it turns the whole thing into a clean framework: first fix a "noising" process that turns data, bit by bit, into pure noise; then train a network to run that process backwards. The noising rule is very simple:

xσ = α · x + σ · ε，  α² + σ² = 1xσ = α · x + σ · ε, α² + σ² = 1

加了噪声的点，等于原来的点乘一个缩小系数 α，再加上 σ 倍的标准高斯噪声。A noisy point equals the original point multiplied by a shrink factor α, plus σ times standard Gaussian noise.

高斯加高斯还是高斯，所以不管要加多少噪声，都能一步直接加好，不用一点点去模拟。α 和 σ 的平方和等于 1，是为了让大小保持住：数据事先标准化到方差 1 时，加噪前后方差一直是 1（所以这种写法叫"保方差"）；这里的花没有标准化，加噪时会从半径 2 左右缩到 1 出头。σ 从 0 涨到 1，数据从原样一点点变成纯粹的标准高斯，到那时，原来的数据一点痕迹也不剩。A Gaussian plus a Gaussian is still a Gaussian, so however much noise is to be added, it can be added in one go without simulating it bit by bit. α and σ have squares adding to 1 to keep the size in check: if the data are first standardised to variance 1, the variance stays 1 throughout the noising (hence the name "variance preserving"); the flower here is not standardised, so noising shrinks it from a radius of about 2 to a little over 1. As σ rises from 0 to 1, the data change bit by bit from their original form into a pure standard Gaussian, and by then not a trace of the original data is left.

网络要学的是倒回去：看到一个加了噪声的点 xσ，猜原来的点 x。The network has to learn to go backwards: given a noisy point xσ, guess the original point x.

加噪，再去噪：一个最普通的回归问题，没有对手，也不用算积分。Add noise, then remove it: a perfectly ordinary regression problem, with no opponent and no integral to compute.

网络可以直接猜原始点 x，也可以猜加进去的噪声 ε（DDPM 原文的做法），两者差一个换算：猜到 x̂，噪声就是 ε̂ = (xσ − α x̂) ÷ σ。The network can guess the original point x directly, or the noise ε that was added (the original DDPM approach); one converts directly into the other: having guessed x̂, the noise is ε̂ = (xσ − α x̂) ÷ σ.

训练稳定，是扩散模型最大的优点之一：它只是一个回归，没有 GAN 那样两个网络互相追。而且每个噪声级别的训练都要照顾到所有数据，所以不容易漏掉哪片花瓣。Stable training is one of diffusion’s biggest advantages: it is just a regression, with no GAN-style pair of networks chasing each other. And training at every noise level has to account for all the data, so petals are unlikely to be left out.

噪声只会把数据摊开，从密的地方摊向稀的地方。倒回去的最好办法，就是往密度更高的方向挪，挪多远和噪声的方差 σ² 成正比。这个关系叫 Tweedie 公式（来历见 Efron 2011），只要噪声是高斯的，它就严格成立。Noise only spreads the data out, from dense places towards sparse ones. 如果猜得准，这一步正好落在"噪声为 σ′ 的数据"上；猜得不准也不要紧，下一步接着修。每一步噪声只降一点，猜错的代价就小。这是 DDIM（Song 等 2020）的做法，整条路线是确定的；DDPM 原本的做法是每一步再加一点新的噪声，路线是随机的。If the guess is right, this step lands exactly on "data with noise σ′"; if not, no matter, the next step keeps correcting. The noise only drops a little at each step, so a wrong guess costs little. This is DDIM’s approach (Song et al. 2020), and the whole route is deterministic; the original DDPM adds a little fresh noise at every step, so its route is random.

扩散模型为什么在 2021 年前后取代了 GAN？训练只是回归，稳定；每个噪声级别都看所有数据，不容易漏掉花瓣；网络越大、数据越多，效果越好（Dhariwal & Nichol 2021；EDM，Karras 等 2022 把各种设计上的选择梳理得很清楚）。代价是生成要走很多步。Why did diffusion models take over from GANs around 2021? Training is just regression and stable; every noise level looks at all the data, so petals are unlikely to be left out; and the bigger the network and the more data, the better the results (Dhariwal & Nichol 2021; EDM, Karras et al. 2022 laid out the various design choices very clearly). The price is that generation takes many steps.

第 ⑧ 步 · Flow MatchingStep ⑧ · Flow matching

每一对都走直线A straight line for every pair

第 ② 步末尾提到过另一种思路：让点沿着一个速度场"流"过去，从噪声出发，解一个常微分方程（ODE），一路走到数据。难处在于这个速度场怎么训练。2022 年，Flow Matching（Lipman 等）、Rectified Flow（Liu 等）和随机插值（Albergo & Vanden-Eijnden）几乎同时给出了一个简单得出奇的办法：随手配一对，一个噪声 z、一个数据点 x，连一条直线，让网络学这条直线上的速度。The end of step ② mentioned another idea: let points "flow" along a velocity field, starting from noise and solving an ordinary differential equation (ODE) all the way to data. The difficulty is how to train that velocity field. In 2022, flow matching (Lipman et al.), rectified flow (Liu et al.) and stochastic interpolants (Albergo & Vanden-Eijnden) arrived almost at once at a surprisingly simple method: pair up a noise point z and a data point x at random, join them with a straight line, and have the network learn the velocity along that line.

xt = (1 − t) · z + t · xL(θ) = Ez, x, t ‖ vθ(xt, t) − (x − z) ‖²

在噪声 z 和数据 x 之间的直线上随便取一点 xt，让网络在这个位置、这个时刻，输出这条直线的速度 x − z。Take any point xt on the straight line between noise z and data x, and have the network output, at that position and time, the line’s velocity x − z.

沿着直线匀速走，速度处处都是 x − z，这是一个再简单不过的回归目标。可同一个位置会有很多条直线穿过，网络不知道是哪一条，平方误差又一次让它学到平均：v(xt, t) = E[x − z | xt]。妙就妙在，这个平均速度场恰好能把一整团噪声一点不差地变成数据的分布（论文里证明了这一点）；而且同一时刻两个点不会挤到同一个位置（常微分方程的解是唯一的），所以整个过程是一个可逆的变形，正是第 ② 步末尾说的那种连续的流。Moving at constant speed along a straight line, the velocity is x − z everywhere: as simple a regression target as there could be. But many lines pass through the same position and the network cannot tell which one it is on, so squared error once again makes it learn the average: v(xt, t) = E[x − z | xt]. The beauty is that this average velocity field turns the whole cloud of noise into exactly the data distribution (the papers prove this); and two points can never be at the same position at the same moment (solutions of an ordinary differential equation are unique), so the whole process is an invertible deformation: exactly the kind of continuous flow mentioned at the end of step ②.

随手把一个噪声点和一个数据点用直线连起来；网络学的是"站在这里，大家平均往哪走"。Pair a noise point with a data point at random and join them with a straight line; the network learns "standing here, which way does everyone go on average".

生成时从 z 出发，按 x ← x + Δt · vθ(x, t) 一小步一小步走到 t = 1。只走一步（Δt = 1）：t = 0 时的平均速度是"数据的平均 − z"，于是所有点都走到花心。和扩散一模一样。To generate, start at z and step to t = 1 a little at a time by x ← x + Δt · vθ(x, t). With a single step (Δt = 1): at t = 0 the average velocity is "the average of the data − z", so every point goes to the centre of the flower. Exactly as with diffusion.

"直线"说的只是每一对。很多对的直线互相交叉，交叉处的平均速度会拐弯，所以真正走出来的路是弯的。交互里，路程平均是起点到终点直线距离的 1.5 倍左右。叠上扩散的路线比一比：扩散的路程是直线距离的 1.18 倍，Flow Matching 反而是 1.54 倍。两者走的是同一族路径（下面就会看到），只是比例尺和时间表不同；单靠 Flow Matching 本身，路并不比扩散直。真正把路拉直的，是后面的 Reflow。"Straight" applies only to each pair. The lines of many pairs cross one another, and where they cross the average velocity bends, so the paths actually taken are curved. In the interactive, the distance travelled is on average about 1.5 times the straight-line distance from start to end. Overlay the diffusion routes to compare: diffusion travels 1.18 times the straight-line distance, while flow matching travels 1.54 times. The two follow the same family of paths (as we will see below), just with a different scale and timetable; flow matching on its own does not make the paths any straighter than diffusion. What really straightens them is reflow, further down.

把这条直线换个写法，就能看出它和扩散是一家：xt ÷ t = x + ((1 − t) ÷ t) · z。除以 t 以后，正是第 ⑥ 步那种"数据加 σ 倍噪声"，σ = (1 − t) ÷ t。也就是说，Flow Matching 的路径就是一条扩散的路径，只是换了比例尺和时间表。网络学速度、学去噪、学分数还是学噪声，互相之间都只差一行换算，比如 v = (D − xt) ÷ (1 − t)，D 是去噪器。再进一步：精确地解方程时，同一个噪声在扩散（DDIM）和 Flow Matching 里会变成同一个样本，只是半路上走的路线不同（Gao 等 2024 讲得很清楚）。Rewrite the line and you can see it belongs to the same family as diffusion: xt ÷ t = x + ((1 − t) ÷ t) · z. After dividing by t, it is exactly step ⑥’s "data plus σ times noise", with σ = (1 − t) ÷ t. In other words, the flow-matching path is a diffusion path with a different scale and timetable. Whether the network learns a velocity, a denoised guess, a score or the noise, they differ by a single line of conversion, for example v = (D − xt) ÷ (1 − t), where D is the denoiser. Going further: when the equations are solved exactly, the same noise becomes the same sample under diffusion (DDIM) and flow matching; only the route along the way differs (Gao et al. 2024 explain this very clearly).

那它们差在哪？一是网络学的是哪个量、训练时各个噪声级别各占多大分量，这会影响学出来的网络好不好；二是生成时步子怎么切。在这些选择上，Flow Matching 的写法最省事：目标就是一个速度，时间就是 0 到 1，步子均匀地切。So where do they differ? First, in which quantity the network learns and how much weight each noise level gets during training, which affects how good the learned network is; second, in how the generation steps are spaced. On these choices flow matching is the simplest to write: the target is just a velocity, time just runs from 0 to 1, and the steps are spaced evenly.

真正让路变直的，是换一种配对。Reflow（Liu 等）的做法是：用训练好的模型把每个噪声 z 送到它的终点，再拿这些配好的对（z 和它的终点）重新训练一遍。这一次直线几乎不交叉，速度沿着每条线几乎不变，一两步就够了：交互里拉直以后只走 1 步，就有约四分之三的样本落在花瓣上，而原版走 1 步是 0。代价是要多训一轮，而新网络只能近似老模型，误差会累积：拉直以后走 64 步也只有 86% 落在花瓣上，原版是 94%；大模型上通常还要再蒸馏。另一种办法是训练时就在一小批数据里按"谁离谁近"来配对（最优传输配对，Tong 等 2023），让直线少交叉。What really straightens the road is a different pairing. Flow Matching 还有一个好处：起点不必是高斯，任何能抽样的分布都行。那为什么大家几乎总是从高斯出发？Flow matching has one more advantage: the starting point need not be Gaussian; any distribution that can be sampled will do. So why does almost everyone still start from a Gaussian?

第 ⑨ 步 · 为什么总从高斯出发Step ⑨ · Why always start from a Gaussian

起点为什么是高斯噪声Why the starting point is Gaussian noise

回头看前面的方法，大多数都从高斯出发：流模型从高斯出发；VAE 从高斯里抽编码；GAN 的生成器通常吃进一个高斯噪声；扩散和 Flow Matching 也都从高斯出发。为什么总是它？它是唯一的选择吗？是最好的选择吗？干脆不要噪声，"凭空"生成行不行？Look back at the methods so far: most of them start from a Gaussian. Flows start from a Gaussian; VAEs draw their codes from a Gaussian; a GAN’s generator usually takes in Gaussian noise; diffusion and flow matching also start from a Gaussian. Why always the Gaussian? Is it the only choice? Is it the best one? Could we do without noise altogether and generate "from nothing"?

为什么非要随机Why randomness is unavoidable

先回答最后一个问题。生成器是一个函数：同样的输入，永远给出同样的输出。如果输入是固定的，它只能生成同一个东西；而为了离所有数据都近一些，训练出来的这个东西往往就是平均。多样性必须从某个地方的随机来。The last question first. A generator is a function: the same input always gives the same output. If the input is fixed, it can only ever produce one thing, and since training pulls that one thing towards being close to all the data, it usually ends up being the average. Variety has to come from randomness somewhere.

随机可以在起点一次给足：流模型、GAN，还有扩散和 Flow Matching 的确定性采样，都是"抽一个噪声，再用一个确定的函数变过去"。也可以在路上一点点加：自回归每一步都抽一次签（第 ⑤ 步里温度为 0 时就没有随机了，永远是同一个点）；扩散的 DDPM 每一步都加新噪声；掩码式的扩散从一整段"空白"出发，每一步随机地填上几个位置（D3PM 2021、LLaDA 2025）。所以"从一个点出发"其实可以，只要路上一直加随机。从一个点出发、靠沿途的布朗运动走到数据分布的办法有很多；其中相对纯布朗运动"改动最少"的那一个，叫 Föllmer 过程。交互里的"一个点，路上加随机"就是它：对每个终点来说，走的都是一座布朗桥（起点和终点都定死、中间随机抖动的布朗运动）。The randomness can be supplied all at once at the start: flows, GANs and the deterministic samplers of diffusion and flow matching all "draw some noise, then transform it with a fixed function". Or it can be added bit by bit along the way: autoregression draws lots at every step (at temperature 0 in step ⑤ there is no randomness left, and it is always the same point); diffusion’s DDPM adds fresh noise at every step; masked diffusion starts from a whole stretch of "blanks" and fills in a few positions at random at each step (D3PM 2021, LLaDA 2025). So "starting from a single point" does work, as long as randomness keeps being added along the way. There are many ways to start from one point and reach the data distribution by means of Brownian motion along the way; the one that departs "least" from plain Brownian motion is called the Föllmer process. The interactive’s "One point, randomness on the way" is exactly this: for each end point, the path is a Brownian bridge (Brownian motion with both its start and its end pinned down, jiggling at random in between).

为什么偏偏是高斯Why the Gaussian in particular

不是因为它最好，而是因为它最省事，而且省事的地方恰好处处用得上：Not because it is the best, but because it is the most convenient, and its conveniences happen to be useful everywhere:

  • 好抽，好算。任何电脑都能抽：两个均匀随机数做几步运算就行（Box–Muller 变换）。概率密度只有一行公式，第 ② 步的流模型算似然就要用它。Easy to draw, easy to compute. Any computer can draw it: a few operations on two uniform random numbers will do (the Box–Muller transform). Its probability density is a one-line formula, which step ②’s flows need to compute likelihoods.
  • 处处都有，各个方向一样。高斯在整个空间上都大于 0，哪里都可能抽到点，所以只要目标也处处大于 0，原则上就有可逆的变形能把它变过去；目标被空白分成几块时，只能无限逼近（第 ② 步花瓣之间的"桥"）；它又是旋转对称的，不偏向任何方向，所有的结构都留给网络去学。在方差一定的分布里，它的熵（分布有多"散"、多不确定）最大，是"假设最少"的那一个。Everywhere, and the same in every direction. A Gaussian is greater than 0 over the whole space, so a point can be drawn anywhere; as long as the target is also greater than 0 everywhere, there is in principle an invertible deformation that turns one into the other; when the target is split by empty space into several pieces, it can only be approached ever more closely (the "bridges" between petals in step ②). It is also rotationally symmetric, favouring no direction and leaving all the structure for the network to learn. Among distributions with a given variance it has the largest entropy (how "spread out" and uncertain a distribution is): it is the one that "assumes the least".
  • 加噪声的终点就是它。不管数据长什么样，一直往上加高斯噪声（同时把数据缩小），最后都会变成高斯；就算加的不是高斯噪声，很多个小的随机加在一起，结果也趋于高斯（中心极限定理）。所以扩散模型知道该从哪里开始倒着走，而不用关心数据本来的样子。It is where adding noise ends up. Whatever the data look like, keep adding Gaussian noise (while shrinking the data) and you end up with a Gaussian; and even if the noise added is not Gaussian, many small random contributions added together still tend to a Gaussian (the central limit theorem). So a diffusion model knows where to start walking backwards, without caring what the data originally looked like.
  • 高斯加高斯还是高斯。所以扩散训练时能一步跳到任意的噪声级别（第 ⑦ 步的 xσ = αx + σε），不用一步步模拟。A Gaussian plus a Gaussian is still a Gaussian. So diffusion training can jump straight to any noise level (step ⑦’s xσ = αx + σε) without simulating step by step.
  • 去噪就是估分数。加的是高斯噪声时，最好的去噪猜测和分数之间有一个干净的等式（第 ⑦ 步的 Tweedie 公式），扩散的整套理论就搭在它上面。Denoising is estimating the score. When the noise added is Gaussian, the best denoising guess and the score are linked by a clean equation (step ⑦’s Tweedie formula), and the whole theory of diffusion rests on it.

还有一个有点反直觉的事实：高维的标准高斯像一个肥皂泡。在 d 维空间里，样本到中心的距离几乎都在 √d 附近：d = 10000 时，样本离中心都是 100 左右，上下一般只差 0.7 左右，99% 以上在 98 到 102 之间，中心附近反而几乎抽不到。中心的密度虽然最高，可中心附近的体积小得可怜；越往外，同样厚的一层球壳，体积按 rd−1 暴涨，两者一抵消，概率就都挤在半径 √d 的那一层。所以图像生成用的"噪声"，其实是在一个巨大的球面上随手取一点：高维里，"从标准高斯抽"和"在半径 √d 的球面上均匀取一点"几乎是一回事。There is also a somewhat counter-intuitive fact: a high-dimensional standard Gaussian is like a soap bubble. In d dimensions, almost every sample lies at a distance of about √d from the centre: with d = 10 000, samples are about 100 from the centre, typically give or take about 0.7, with over 99% between 98 and 102, and the region near the centre is almost never drawn. The density is highest at the centre, but the volume near the centre is tiny; further out, a shell of the same thickness has a volume that grows like rd−1, and the two balance so that the probability piles up in the shell of radius √d. So the "noise" used in image generation is really a point picked at random on a huge sphere: in high dimensions, "draw from a standard Gaussian" and "pick a point uniformly on the sphere of radius √d" are almost the same thing.

是唯一的选择吗？Is it the only choice?

不是。No.

  • 均匀分布也行。早期的 GAN（比如 DCGAN）用的就是均匀分布的噪声；交互里换成均匀的方块，Flow Matching 的结果和高斯差不多。A uniform distribution works too. Early GANs (such as DCGAN) used uniformly distributed noise; in the interactive, switching to the uniform square gives flow-matching results about as good as the Gaussian’s.
  • 离散的数据常常不用高斯。掩码扩散从一整段 [MASK]（空白）出发，随机性在"先填哪个位置、填什么"里。Discrete data often do without Gaussians. Masked diffusion starts from a whole stretch of [MASK] (blanks), and the randomness lies in "which position to fill first, and with what".
  • 数据有"重尾"时得小心。重尾是说偶尔会出现特别大的值。拉伸倍数处处有上限的变形（数学上叫 Lipschitz），从高斯出发变不出重尾，而常见的流模型大多就是这种变形（第 ② 步的 F⁻¹(Φ(z)) 在远处能无限拉伸，不受这个限制）。所以要么让变形在远处越拉越狠，要么换一个本身就有重尾的起点（Jaini 等 2020）。Heavy-tailed data need care. "Heavy tails" means that very large values turn up now and then. A deformation whose stretch is bounded everywhere (mathematicians call it Lipschitz) cannot turn a Gaussian into something heavy-tailed, and most common flow models are deformations of this kind (the F⁻¹(Φ(z)) of step ② can stretch without limit far out, so it is not bound by this). So either the deformation must stretch harder and harder far out, or the starting point must itself be heavy-tailed (Jaini et al. 2020).
  • 起点也可以学。VAE 的先验可以不是标准高斯，而是学出来的，让先验贴合编码真正的分布，生成时不容易从空洞里抽 z（VampPrior 2018）。The starting point can be learned. A VAE’s prior need not be a standard Gaussian; it can be learned, to match the actual distribution of the codes, so that generation is less likely to draw a z from a hole (VampPrior 2018).
  • 起点甚至可以是另一组数据。Flow Matching 和随机插值允许从任何能抽样的分布出发：从草图到照片、从模糊到清晰，都可以看成从一个分布"流"到另一个（Albergo & Vanden-Eijnden 2022；I²SB 2023）。The starting point can even be another dataset. Flow matching and stochastic interpolants can start from any distribution that can be sampled: going from sketch to photo or from blurred to sharp can be seen as "flowing" from one distribution to another (Albergo & Vanden-Eijnden 2022; I²SB 2023).
  • 加的噪声也不一定是高斯。Cold Diffusion 用模糊、遮挡这类确定的"破坏"来代替加噪（Bansal 等 2022）；也有用重尾噪声做扩散的（Pandey 等 2024）。代价是失去 Tweedie 公式那样干净的等式，理论和训练都得另想办法。The noise added need not be Gaussian either. Cold Diffusion replaces adding noise with deterministic "damage" such as blurring or masking (Bansal et al. 2022); there is also diffusion with heavy-tailed noise (Pandey et al. 2024). The price is losing a clean identity like Tweedie’s formula, so both the theory and the training have to be worked out afresh.

是最好的选择吗？Is it the best choice?

也不是。交互里选"一个粗略的答案"：起点已经大致在它要去的花瓣附近，路短得多，1 步就有约三分之二、2 步就有近九成落在花瓣上；从高斯出发，要 8 步左右才到这个水平。起点离数据越近，路越短越直，步子越少。最好的起点是数据本身，可那正是我们抽不出来的东西。Not that either. Choose "A rough answer" in the interactive: each start is already roughly near the petal it is heading for, the road is much shorter, and about two thirds land on a petal after 1 step and nearly nine in ten after 2; starting from a Gaussian, it takes about 8 steps to reach that level. The closer the start is to the data, the shorter and straighter the road and the fewer the steps. The best start of all would be the data themselves, but that is exactly what we cannot sample.

所以选起点是一个权衡：要好抽；要覆盖整个空间，否则有些数据根本到不了；还要离数据近。高斯在前两点上几乎无敌，在第三点上谈不上好坏：它不知道数据在哪，也不偏向错的地方，所以成了默认值。手里有好的先验时，人们就会换掉它：修复一张模糊的图，可以从这张模糊的图出发；机器人里，有的工作从一个粗糙的先验策略给出的动作出发，再把它修细，而不是每次都从纯噪声开始（BRIDGeR，Chen 等 2024）。So choosing the start is a trade-off: it must be easy to sample; it must cover the whole space, or some data can never be reached; and it should be close to the data. The Gaussian is nearly unbeatable on the first two and neither good nor bad on the third: it knows nothing about where the data are, but it does not favour the wrong places either, which is why it became the default. When there is a good prior at hand, people replace it: to restore a blurred image you can start from the blurred image itself; in robotics, some work starts from the actions given by a rough prior policy and refines them, instead of starting from pure noise every time (BRIDGeR, Chen et al. 2024).

第 ⑩ 步 · 又快又听话Step ⑩ · Fast, and on request

少走几步，按要求生成Fewer steps, generation on request

扩散和 Flow Matching 质量好、训练稳，可生成一个样本要走几十步，每一步都要把整个网络跑一遍。而且光会"随便生成一个"还不够，我们要的往往是"按要求生成"：按一句话画一张图，按机器人此刻看到的画面给出动作。这一步看三件事：怎样少走几步，怎样在压缩过的空间里生成，怎样让生成听话。Diffusion and flow matching give good quality and stable training, but a single sample takes tens of steps, each running the whole network. And "generate something at random" is not enough: what we usually want is "generate what was asked for": an image from a sentence, actions from what the robot sees right now. This step looks at three things: how to take fewer steps, how to generate in a compressed space, and how to make generation do what it is told.

少走几步Fewer steps

第 ⑦、⑧ 步已经看到，一步生成的根本困难是"平均"：从纯噪声一步跳到数据，最好的猜测只能是平均。绕开它的办法大致有四类：Steps ⑦ and ⑧ already showed that the fundamental difficulty of one-step generation is the "average": jumping from pure noise to data in one step, the best guess can only be the average. The ways round it fall roughly into four kinds:

  • 更聪明地解方程。路是弯的，就用更好的求解器：迈步之前多看几个点，估计路怎么拐。DPM-Solver，还有 EDM 用的 Heun 方法（先试走一步，看看那里的速度，再用两头速度的平均重走这一步），能把步数降到 10 到 20 步。Solve the equation more cleverly. The road is curved, so use a better solver: look at a few more points before each step to estimate how the road bends. DPM-Solver, and the Heun method used by EDM (take a trial step, look at the velocity there, then redo the step with the average of the velocities at both ends), bring the step count down to 10–20.
  • 把路拉直。第 ⑧ 步的 Reflow，或者训练时就用更好的配对。Straighten the road. Step ⑧’s reflow, or better pairing during training.
  • 蒸馏：让学生直接学老师走很多步的结果。渐进蒸馏每一轮把步数减半（Salimans & Ho 2022）；一致性模型让网络从路上的任何一点直接跳到终点（Song 等 2023）；对抗蒸馏再请一个判别器来把关，让一到四步的结果也足够锐利，SDXL Turbo 就是这么做的（Sauer 等 2023）。GAN 的想法在这里又回来了。Distillation: a student learns directly what the teacher reaches after many steps. Progressive distillation halves the step count in each round (Salimans & Ho 2022); consistency models let the network jump from any point on the road straight to its end (Song et al. 2023); adversarial distillation also brings in a discriminator as a check, so that even one to four steps give sharp results, which is how SDXL Turbo works (Sauer et al. 2023). The GAN idea is back.
  • 学一整段的平均速度。普通的 Flow Matching 学的是"此刻的速度"，步子一大就拐不过弯；如果网络直接学"从这一刻到那一刻，这一整段的平均速度"，一步就能跨过整段（shortcut 模型，Frans 等 2024；MeanFlow，Geng 等 2025）。Learn the average velocity over a whole stretch. Ordinary flow matching learns the "velocity at this moment", and with big steps it cannot make the turns; if the network instead learns "the average velocity over the whole stretch from this moment to that one", a single step can cross the whole stretch (shortcut models, Frans et al. 2024; MeanFlow, Geng et al. 2025).

第 ⑨ 步的"粗略的答案"也是一条路：起点离数据越近，要走的步子越少。Step ⑨’s "rough answer" is another route: the closer the start is to the data, the fewer steps are needed.

在压缩过的空间里生成Generating in a compressed space

一张 512 × 512 的彩色图有 78 万多个数，直接在上面做扩散，每一步都很贵，而且大部分算力花在了人眼几乎看不出的细节上。隐空间扩散（LDM，Rombach 等 2022）先训练一个自编码器（第 ③ 步），把图压成 64 × 64 × 4 的"隐变量"，扩散只在这 1.6 万个数上做，最后再用解码器还原成图。这就是 Stable Diffusion。后来把去噪网络从卷积网络换成 Transformer（DiT，Peebles & Xie 2023），模型越大，效果越好。A 512 × 512 colour image has more than 780 000 numbers; running diffusion directly on them makes every step expensive, and most of the compute goes on details the eye can barely see. Latent diffusion (LDM, Rombach et al. 2022) first trains an autoencoder (step ③) to compress the image into a 64 × 64 × 4 "latent", runs diffusion only on those 16 000-odd numbers, and finally restores the image with the decoder. That is Stable Diffusion. Later the denoising network was switched from a convolutional network to a Transformer (DiT, Peebles & Xie 2023), and the bigger the model, the better the results.

让生成听话Making generation do what it is told

要"按要求生成"，最直接的办法是把条件 c（一个类别、一句话、一张画面）也喂给网络：去噪器变成 D(xσ, σ, c)，速度场变成 v(x, t, c)，学到的就是条件分布 p(x | c)。可实践中，这样生成的样本常常不够贴题。早期的办法是另训一个分类器，用它的梯度把样本推向"更像 c"的方向（分类器引导，Dhariwal & Nichol 2021）；后来发现，连分类器都不用另训：To "generate on request", the most direct way is to feed the condition c (a class, a sentence, an image) to the network as well: the denoiser becomes D(xσ, σ, c) and the velocity field v(x, t, c), and what is learned is the conditional distribution p(x | c). In practice, though, samples generated this way often do not follow the request closely enough. The early fix was to train a separate classifier and use its gradient to push samples towards "more like c" (classifier guidance, Dhariwal & Nichol 2021); later it turned out that not even a separate classifier is needed:

vw(x, t, c) = v(x, t) + w · ( v(x, t, c) − v(x, t) )

引导后的速度，等于不看条件的速度，加上 w 倍的"看了条件以后多出来的那一部分"。The guided velocity equals the velocity that ignores the condition, plus w times "the extra part that appears once the condition is taken into account".

v(x, t, c) − v(x, t) 指向"更像 c、更不像别的"那个方向。w = 1 时就是条件模型本身；w > 1 时，沿着这个方向多推一点，样本更贴题。注意这里混合的是速度，不是分布：0 < w < 1 并不等于"一部分要 c、一部分不要"，样本会有很多落在花瓣之间。训练时随机把一部分条件扔掉（换成"空"），同一个网络就同时学会了有条件和无条件两种速度，所以叫无分类器引导（CFG，Ho & Salimans 2022）。v(x, t, c) − v(x, t) points towards "more like c, less like anything else". At w = 1 this is just the conditional model; with w > 1 it pushes a little further in that direction and the samples follow the request more closely. Note that what is mixed here is the velocity, not the distribution: 0 < w < 1 does not mean "some samples heed c and the rest do not", and many samples land between the petals. If the condition is dropped (replaced by "empty") for a random fraction of the training examples, one network learns both the conditional and the unconditional velocity at once, hence the name classifier-free guidance (CFG, Ho & Salimans 2022).

Image generation often wants exactly this touch of "unreality" in exchange for images that follow the prompt noticeably better: Stable Diffusion’s default guidance strength is 7.5.

机器人里，条件就是机器人此刻看到的画面、听到的指令，要生成的是它接下来的一串动作。可为什么机器人的动作也要"生成"，而不是直接"预测"一个？In robotics, the condition is what the robot sees and hears right now, and what is to be generated is its next sequence of actions. But why should a robot’s actions be "generated" at all, rather than just "predicted"?

第 ⑪ 步 · 机器人里怎么用Step ⑪ · Use in robotics

动作也要"生成"Actions have to be "generated" too

让机器人学会一件事，最直接的办法是模仿：人遥控机器人示范几十上百遍，记下每一刻它看到了什么（观测 o）、做了什么（动作 a），再训练一个网络，从 o 预测 a。这叫行为克隆。最简单的做法是回归：用均方误差训练，网络输出"在这个画面下，动作的平均"。The most direct way to teach a robot a task is imitation: a person teleoperates the robot through tens or hundreds of demonstrations, recording at every moment what it saw (the observation o) and what it did (the action a), and then a network is trained to predict a from o. This is behavioural cloning. The simplest version is regression: trained with mean squared error, the network outputs "the average action for this view".

问题在于，同一个画面下，人的做法常常不止一种：绕障碍物可以走左边，也可以走右边；抓杯子可以抓杯身，也可以抓把手；同一个动作，快一点慢一点都行。均方误差学到的是这些做法的平均，而平均往往哪一种都不是。这就是第 0 步那个"取平均"的毛病，放在机器人身上，后果是一头撞上去。The trouble is that, for the same view, people often have more than one way of doing things: an obstacle can be passed on the left or on the right; a cup can be grasped by its body or by its handle; the same motion can be done a little faster or slower. Mean squared error learns the average of these ways, and the average is often none of them. This is step 0’s "Take the average" flaw again, and in a robot its consequence is driving straight into things.

所以这几年，机器人学习越来越多地把策略写成一个条件生成模型：So in recent years robot learning has increasingly written the policy as a conditional generative model:

at : t+H ∼ πθ( · | ot )

看到当前的观测 ot，从策略给出的分布里，抽出接下来 H 个时刻的动作。Given the current observation ot, draw the actions for the next H time steps from the distribution the policy gives.

策略不再是"一个画面对应一个动作"，而是"一个画面对应一个动作的分布"。网络学的是整个分布，抽样时自然只会选其中一种做法。一次生成一整段动作（叫动作块），而不是一步，是因为人示范时的"决定"往往要延续一阵子：一旦决定从左边绕，接下来一整段都往左；每一步都重新抽，就可能这一步往左、下一步往右。The policy is no longer "one view, one action" but "one view, a distribution of actions". The network learns the whole distribution, and sampling naturally picks just one way of doing things. A whole stretch of actions is generated at once (an action chunk) rather than a single step, because a demonstrator’s "decision" usually lasts a while: once you decide to go round on the left, the whole next stretch goes left, whereas drawing afresh at every step might go left now and right the next moment.

At about the same time came ACT (Zhao et al. 2023): trained as a CVAE, it outputs a chunk of actions at a time; but in use it fixes z at 0 and so gives only one way of doing things, the CVAE mainly helping it digest the several ways in the demonstrations during training.

为什么扩散和 Flow Matching 成了主流Why diffusion and flow matching became the mainstream

用前面几步的眼光回头看：回归会取平均（第 0 步）；混合高斯要事先定好有几种做法（第 ① 步）；VAE 的样本会糊（第 ③ 步）；GAN 训练不稳、还会漏掉做法（第 ④ 步）；自回归要把动作切成格子，精度和速度都得权衡（第 ⑤ 步）。扩散和 Flow Matching 的训练只是回归，稳定；能表示任意多种做法；动作是连续的、精确的；和画面、语言这些条件也很容易结合（第 ⑩ 步）。Look back in the light of the earlier steps: regression averages (step 0); a Gaussian mixture has to fix in advance how many ways of doing the task there are (step ①); VAE samples come out blurry (step ③); GANs train unstably and drop some of the ways (step ④); autoregression has to cut actions into bins, trading precision against speed (step ⑤). Training diffusion and flow matching is just regression, so it is stable; they can represent any number of ways of doing the task; the actions are continuous and precise; and they combine easily with conditions such as images and language (step ⑩).

它们的短处是要走好几步，对每次只有几十毫秒的机器人，这是个真问题。所以机器人里普遍用 Flow Matching 只走很少几步（π0 走 10 步，GR00T N1 走 4 步），或者蒸馏成一到三步（Consistency Policy，Prasad 等 2024），或者让下一段动作的生成和这一段动作的执行同时进行（Black 等 2025）。Their weakness is that they need several steps, and for a robot that has only a few dozen milliseconds each time, that is a real problem. So robotics commonly runs flow matching with only a few steps (π0 takes 10, GR00T N1 takes 4), or distils it into one to three steps (Consistency Policy, Prasad et al. 2024), or generates the next chunk of actions while the current one is still being executed (Black et al. 2025).

第 ⑫ 步 · 光谱Step ⑫ · The spectrum

几十种生成模型，放在一起看Dozens of generative models, side by side

到这里已经见过几十种生成模型。它们看上去各有一套，其实都在回答同一个问题：怎样用一把随机数，按数据的分布抽出一个新样本。差别在于几个选择：By now we have met dozens of generative models. Each seems to have its own system, but they are all answering the same question: how to use a handful of random numbers to draw a new sample from the distribution of the data. The differences come down to a few choices:

  • 学的是什么。概率本身（混合高斯、流、自回归）；一个从噪声到数据的函数（VAE 的解码器、GAN 的生成器、一步模型）；一个场，告诉每个位置该往哪走（分数、去噪、速度：扩散和 Flow Matching）；或者一个没有归一化的打分（能量、判别器）。What is learned. The probability itself (Gaussian mixtures, flows, autoregression); a function from noise to data (the VAE’s decoder, the GAN’s generator, one-step models); a field that tells every position which way to go (score, denoised guess, velocity: diffusion and flow matching); or an unnormalised rating (energy, discriminator).
  • 能不能算出概率。能精确算的，可以直接用最大似然训练，也能回答"这个点有多大可能"；扩散和 Flow Matching 也能算，但要把它们当成连续的流，沿着方程积分一遍，很贵；只能算下界的，退而求其次；算不出的，只能抽样。Whether the probability can be computed. Models that compute it exactly can be trained directly by maximum likelihood, and can also answer "how likely is this point?"; diffusion and flow matching can compute it too, but only by treating them as continuous flows and integrating along an equation, which is expensive; those that only compute a lower bound settle for second best; those that cannot compute it can only sample.
  • 生成要几步。一步、几步、几十到上千步，或者序列多长就走多少步。步数和质量常常此消彼长。How many steps generation takes. One step, a few steps, tens to thousands of steps, or as many steps as the sequence is long. Steps and quality often trade off against each other.
  • 怎么训练。最大似然、变分下界、对抗、回归、蒸馏。用回归（均方误差）训练，学到的就是平均；扩散和 Flow Matching 的办法是在每个噪声级别上学平均，再一步步走，平均就成了指路的箭头。How it is trained. Maximum likelihood, a variational lower bound, adversarial training, regression, distillation. Train by regression (mean squared error) and what you learn is the average; the trick of diffusion and flow matching is to learn the average at every noise level and then walk step by step, so that the average becomes an arrow pointing the way.

总结Summary

回到开头：生成模型到底学了什么Back to the start: what does a generative model actually learn?

生成模型到底学了什么？数据的分布：哪里的点多、哪里的点少、哪里根本没有。分布本身没法直接交给网络，所以每一类方法换了一种说法来表示它。混合高斯、流、自回归直接写出概率（第 ①、②、⑤ 步）；VAE 和 GAN 学一个把噪声变成样本的函数（③、④）；能量模型学一个"像不像"的打分（⑥）；分数模型、扩散和 Flow Matching 学一个场，告诉每个位置、每个噪声级别"往哪走更像数据"（⑥、⑦、⑧）。这几种说法在数学上是相通的：分数是对数概率的梯度，最好的去噪猜测就是分数换算过来的（Tweedie 公式），速度又是去噪结果的一行换算。What does a generative model actually learn? The distribution of the data: where points are dense, where they are sparse and where there are none at all. A distribution cannot be handed to a network as it is, so each family of methods expresses it in its own way. Gaussian mixtures, flows and autoregression write the probability down directly (steps ①, ② and ⑤); VAEs and GANs learn a function that turns noise into samples (③, ④); energy-based models learn a rating of "how much like the data" (⑥); score models, diffusion and flow matching learn a field that tells every position, at every noise level, "which way looks more like the data" (⑥, ⑦, ⑧). Mathematically these descriptions are connected: the score is the gradient of the log probability, the best denoising guess is a conversion of the score (Tweedie’s formula), and the velocity is in turn a one-line conversion of the denoising guess.

其他几个问题Other questions

  • 什么是生成？生成的是什么？用随机数，按学到的分布抽一个新样本。生成出来的，是和训练数据同类的一个新实例：一张图、一句话、一段动作、一条轨迹、一个抓取的位置和朝向、几帧未来的画面（第 ⑪ 步的表）。它要像（落在数据的形状里），要多样（每一块都有，比例也对），要新（不是训练数据的复制品）。照抄做不到新，乱撒做不到像，取平均既不像、也不多样（第 0 步）。What is generation? What is generated? Using random numbers, draw a new sample from the learned distribution. What comes out is a new instance of the same kind as the training data: an image, a sentence, a stretch of actions, a trajectory, a grasp position and orientation, a few frames of the future (the table in step ⑪). It should look right (fall within the shape of the data), be varied (every part covered, in the right proportions) and be new (not a copy of the training data). Copying fails to be new, scattering at random fails to look right, and averaging neither looks right nor is varied (step 0).
  • 为什么"平均"一再出现？用均方误差训练，学到的就是平均。回归策略撞上障碍物、VAE 的样本发糊、一步去噪落在花心，都是它（0、③、⑦、⑪）。扩散把它变成了优点：在每个噪声级别上学平均，平均就成了指路的方向。Why does "the average" keep coming up? Train with mean squared error and what you learn is the average. The regression policy crashing into the obstacle, blurry VAE samples, one-step denoising landing in the centre of the flower: all of these are the average at work (0, ③, ⑦, ⑪). Diffusion turns it into a strength: learn the average at every noise level, and the average becomes a direction pointing the way.
  • 它们各自的特点和优劣？见上面的表。一句话：流精确，但被"可逆"绑住；VAE 稳，但样本发糊；GAN 锐利，但不稳、会漏；自回归精确，但一个一个生成，慢；能量和分数结构自由，但要迭代采样；扩散和 Flow Matching 稳、全、质量高，但要走好多步。What are their characteristics, strengths and weaknesses? See the table above. In one sentence: flows are exact but bound by invertibility; VAEs are stable but blurry; GANs are sharp but unstable and drop data; autoregression is exact but generates one piece at a time, which is slow; energy and score models leave the network free but need iterative sampling; diffusion and flow matching are stable, thorough and high in quality, but take many steps.
  • 它们之间是什么关系？扩散和分数模型是同一件事的两种说法（⑦）；Flow Matching 和扩散是一族，精确求解时同一个噪声变成同一个样本（⑧）；Flow Matching 训练出来的，就是第 ② 步末尾那种连续的流（⑧）；扩散也可以看成一个编码器固定、层数很多的 VAE，所以它的训练目标是证据下界的一个加权版本（①、③、⑦）；每一步输出一个高斯的自回归模型，本身就是一个流（地图里的 MAF），输出格子概率的就不是；VAE 给扩散提供压缩过的空间（⑩）；GAN 的判别器回到了一步蒸馏和机器人的奖励里（⑩、⑪）。How are they related? Diffusion and score models are two descriptions of the same thing (⑦); flow matching and diffusion are one family, and when the equations are solved exactly the same noise turns into the same sample (⑧); what flow matching trains is exactly the kind of continuous flow mentioned at the end of step ② (⑧); diffusion can also be seen as a VAE with a fixed encoder and very many layers, which is why its training objective is a weighted version of the evidence lower bound (①, ③, ⑦); an autoregressive model that outputs a Gaussian at each step is itself a flow (MAF in the map), though one that outputs bin probabilities is not; VAEs give diffusion a compressed space to work in (⑩); the GAN discriminator has come back in one-step distillation and in robot rewards (⑩, ⑪).
  • 为什么总从高斯出发？随机必须从某处来，否则只能得到平均；高斯最省事：好抽、处处都有、是一直加噪声的终点、高斯加高斯还是高斯、去噪就是估分数。它不是唯一的选择（均匀、掩码、另一组数据都行），也不是最好的（离数据越近的起点越省步数），只是在"好抽、覆盖全、离数据近"之间最均衡的默认值（⑨）。Why always start from a Gaussian? The randomness has to come from somewhere, or all you get is the average; the Gaussian is the least trouble: easy to sample, non-zero everywhere, the end point of adding noise for ever, a Gaussian plus a Gaussian is still a Gaussian, and denoising amounts to estimating the score. It is not the only choice (uniform noise, masks or another dataset all work), nor the best (the closer the starting point is to the data, the fewer steps are needed); it is simply the best-balanced default between "easy to sample", "covers everything" and "close to the data" (⑨).
  • 怎样又快又听话？更好的求解器、把路拉直、蒸馏、学一整段的平均速度；在压缩过的空间里生成；用条件和引导（⑩）。How do you make it fast and make it do what you ask? Better solvers, straightening the paths, distillation, learning the average velocity over a whole stretch; generating in a compressed space; conditions and guidance (⑩).
  • 在机器人里怎么用？同一个画面下，动作往往有好几种对的做法，回归会取平均，所以策略越来越多地写成以观测为条件的生成模型，一次生成一段动作：扩散策略、π0 和 GR00T N1 的 Flow Matching、RT-2 的动作词元；ACT 也用 CVAE 生成动作块，只是使用时只出一种做法。此外还有用判别器当模仿的奖励（GAIL、AMP）、用扩散规划轨迹和生成抓取、用流给出逆运动学的多个解、用视频生成模型当世界模型和造训练数据（⑪）。How are they used in robotics? For the same scene there are often several correct ways to act, and regression averages them, so policies are increasingly written as generative models that are conditioned on the observation and generate a chunk of actions at a time: Diffusion Policy, the flow matching of π0 and GR00T N1, RT-2’s action tokens; ACT also generates action chunks with a CVAE, but in use gives only one way of doing things. Beyond that, discriminators serve as imitation rewards (GAIL, AMP), diffusion plans trajectories and generates grasps, flows give several solutions to inverse kinematics, and video generation models serve as world models and produce training data (⑪).

小测验Quiz

用 8 个高斯拟合这朵花，测试分接近真分布；每个训练点放一个宽度 0.01 的小高斯，训练分高得多。为什么后者反而是更差的生成模型？Fitting this flower with 8 Gaussians gives a test score close to that of the true distribution; putting a small Gaussian of width 0.01 on every training point gives a far higher training score. Why is the latter nonetheless the worse generative model?

它把训练点背了下来：训练点上的密度极高，可没见过的点都落在训练点之间的空隙里，测试分一落千丈（交互里从 −2 左右掉到 −80 以下）。从它抽样，只是给训练点加一点抖动，生成不出新的东西。好的生成模型要学的是数据背后的分布，所以要看测试集上的分数。It has memorised the training points: the density on them is extremely high, but unseen points all fall into the gaps between training points, and the test score collapses (in the interactive, from about −2 to below −80). Sampling from it only adds a little jitter to training points; it cannot generate anything new. A good generative model has to learn the distribution behind the data, which is why we judge it by its score on the test set.

一个标准化流能不能把一个高斯，精确地变成两团完全分开的点？Can a normalizing flow turn a Gaussian exactly into two completely separate clusters of points?

不能。连续又可逆的变形不能把一整块撕成两块，最多把中间捏得很细：两团之间总会连着一条密度很低、但不是 0 的"桥"，桥上也会抽到一些样本（第 ② 步交互里花瓣之间的细线）。No. A continuous, invertible deformation cannot tear one piece into two; at most it can pinch the middle very thin. The two clusters always stay joined by a "bridge" whose density is very low but not zero, and some samples land on the bridge too (the thin lines between petals in the interactive of step ②).

把 VAE 的 KL 权重 β 调得很大，样本会变成什么样？为什么？If you turn the VAE’s KL weight β up very high, what do the samples look like? Why?

都挤在花心。KL 项压倒了重建项，最省事的办法是让每个点的编码都等于标准高斯本身，编码里不再有关于 x 的任何信息（后验坍塌）；解码器只好对所有 z 都输出同一个答案，也就是所有数据的平均。They all pile up in the centre of the flower. The KL term overwhelms the reconstruction term, and the easiest way out is to make every point’s code equal to the standard Gaussian itself, so the code no longer carries any information about x (posterior collapse); the decoder can then only give the same answer for every z, namely the average of all the data.

GAN 的生成器学得比判别器快很多，会发生什么？What happens if a GAN’s generator learns much faster than its discriminator?

模式坍塌和来回跳：判别器刚觉得哪里"像真的"，生成器就把几乎所有样本一起搬过去；判别器学会拒绝那里，生成器又整体跳到别处。交互里的前几千步，两个网络就这样绕圈追逐，每段时间只覆盖一两片花瓣（这个小例子练久了还能铺开，大模型常常没这么幸运）。Mode collapse and hopping about: as soon as the discriminator thinks somewhere "looks real", the generator moves almost all its samples there at once; when the discriminator learns to reject that spot, the generator jumps somewhere else en masse. For the first few thousand steps in the interactive, the two networks chase each other in circles like this, and at any one time only one or two petals are covered (this small example does spread out if trained long enough; large models are often less lucky).

自回归模型的温度设为 0，生成 100 次，会得到几个不同的结果？Set an autoregressive model’s temperature to 0 and generate 100 times. How many different results do you get?

一个。每一步都选最可能的那个，整个过程没有任何随机，同样的开头永远得到同样的结果。生成离不开随机。One. Every step picks the most likely option, the whole process has no randomness at all, and the same start always gives the same result. Generation cannot do without randomness.

扩散模型只走一步，从纯噪声直接去噪，会得到什么？What do you get if a diffusion model takes just one step, denoising pure noise directly?

数据的平均（这里是花心）。纯噪声里没有任何关于"来自哪里"的信息，最好的去噪猜测就是所有数据的平均。用普通方法训练的去噪器，就得分很多步，让每一步只确定一点细节；想一两步生成，得另外训练会"跳"的网络（第 ⑩ 步的蒸馏、一致性模型、MeanFlow）。The average of the data (here, the centre of the flower). Pure noise carries no information about "where it came from", so the best denoising guess is the average of all the data. With a denoiser trained the ordinary way, the work has to be split into many steps, each settling only a little detail; to generate in one or two steps you need a separately trained network that can "jump" (the distillation, consistency models and MeanFlow of step ⑩).

Flow Matching 训练时每一对噪声和数据之间都是直线，为什么生成时走的路还是弯的？怎样让它变直？In flow matching training every pair of noise and data is joined by a straight line. Why are the paths taken during generation still curved? How can they be straightened?

很多对的直线互相交叉，网络在交叉处只能学到平均速度，平均速度会拐弯，所以整体的路线是弯的（交互里路程约是直线距离的 1.5 倍）。让它变直要换配对：用训练好的模型重新配对再训练一次（Reflow），或者训练时就按"谁离谁近"配对（最优传输配对）。The straight lines of many pairs cross one another, and where they cross the network can only learn the average velocity; the average velocity bends, so the overall routes are curved (in the interactive, the path is about 1.5 times the straight-line distance). Straightening them means changing the pairing: re-pair using the trained model and train again (reflow), or pair by "who is close to whom" from the start (optimal-transport pairing).

为什么不能"凭空"生成？如果一定要从一个固定的点出发，怎么办？Why can’t you generate "out of thin air"? If you must start from one fixed point, what can you do?

从固定的输入出发、路上又不加随机，就是一个确定的函数，只能给出一个结果，而且往往是平均。要从一个点出发，就得在路上不停地加随机（比如第 ⑨ 步的 Föllmer 过程），或者像自回归那样每一步都抽签。Starting from a fixed input with no randomness added along the way is just a deterministic function: it can only give one result, and usually the average. To start from a single point you have to keep adding randomness along the way (for example the Föllmer process of step ⑨), or draw lots at every step as autoregression does.

无分类器引导的强度 w 从 1 调到 5，样本会怎样？As the strength w of classifier-free guidance goes from 1 to 5, what happens to the samples?

越来越"典型"、越来越单调：样本被推离别的类别，挤到这一类最有代表性的地方，横向的变化几乎消失；w 太大会推过头，落到真实数据里没有的地方（图像里就是颜色过饱和、细节夸张）。They become ever more "typical" and ever more monotonous: samples are pushed away from the other classes and crowd into the most representative part of this class, and the sideways variation all but disappears; with w too large they are pushed too far and land where there is no real data (in images: oversaturated colours and exaggerated details).

示范里一半从左边绕开障碍物、一半从右边绕。用均方误差训练的策略会怎么走？扩散策略呢？In the demonstrations, half go round the obstacle on the left and half on the right. How will a policy trained with mean squared error move? What about a diffusion policy?

均方误差学到的是两种动作的平均：起点在正中间时，平均动作是笔直向前，于是撞上障碍物。扩散策略学到的是动作的分布，抽样时只会选其中一边；再一次生成一整段动作，就不会在左右之间来回犹豫。Mean squared error learns the average of the two actions: starting from dead centre, the average action is straight ahead, so the robot crashes into the obstacle. A diffusion policy learns the distribution of actions, so each sample picks just one side; and because it generates a whole chunk of actions at once, it does not dither back and forth between left and right.
