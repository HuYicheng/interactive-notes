从噪声到样本From Noise to Samples

生成模型的全景：混合高斯、流、VAE、GAN、自回归、能量和分数、扩散、Flow Matching。它们都在回答同一个问题：怎样把一把随机数，变成一个像数据、又不是照抄的新样本；生成模型到底学了什么，为什么总从高斯出发，怎样又快又听话，机器人怎么用它们出动作；一张 64 种方法的地图。配了十五个交互。The whole spectrum of generative models: Gaussian mixtures, flows, VAEs, GANs, autoregression, energy and score, diffusion and flow matching. They all answer the same question: how to turn a handful of random numbers into a new sample that looks like the data without copying it. What a generative model actually learns, why it starts from a Gaussian, how to make it fast and controllable, and how robots use it to produce actions; a map of 64 methods. With fifteen interactives.
