ℝ 和 ℝ³：数轴和三维空间ℝ and ℝ³: the number line and 3D space

ℝ 是全体实数（−2、0、3.14 这样的数），可以想成一条无限长的数轴。ℝ³ 是"三个实数一组"的全体，写成 (x, y, z)。它就是我们熟悉的三维空间：一组数既可以表示一个点，也可以表示一根从原点出发的箭头，也就是向量。ℝ is the set of all real numbers (numbers like −2, 0, 3.14); think of it as an infinitely long number line. ℝ³ is the set of all “groups of three real numbers”, written (x, y, z). It is the familiar three-dimensional space: one triple can stand for a point, or for an arrow starting at the origin, which is a vector.

(1, 2, 3) + (0, 1, 0) = (1, 3, 3)2 · (1, 2, 3) = (2, 4, 6)

向量相加就是各分量分别相加，乘一个数就是每个分量都乘它。ℝ³ 是"平直"的：怎么加、怎么缩放，结果都还在 ℝ³ 里，走多远也不会绕回原处。后面会看到，旋转恰恰不能这样加。Adding vectors means adding them component by component; multiplying by a number multiplies every component. ℝ³ is “flat”: however you add or scale, the result stays in ℝ³, and no matter how far you go you never loop back. As we will see, rotations cannot be added like this.

为什么说旋转住在"弯曲"的空间里Why rotations live in a “curved” space

先看平面里的旋转。它只有一个参数：转角。但转 10° 和转 370° 是同一个旋转。把所有平面旋转摆出来，得到的不是一条无限长的直线，而是一个圆圈：沿着走一圈会回到起点。这个圆就是 SO(2)。Start with rotations in the plane. They have a single parameter, the angle. But turning by 10° and turning by 370° are the same rotation. Lay out all planar rotations and you do not get an infinite straight line but a circle: go once around and you are back where you started. This circle is SO(2).

直线 ℝ 和圆 SO(2) 在很小的局部看起来一样，都是"一维的"，但圆是弯的，会绕回来。在圆上，你找不到一个处处光滑、又一一对应的数字当坐标：用角度的话，总有一个地方会从 359° 跳回 0°。Up close, the line ℝ and the circle SO(2) look the same, both “one-dimensional”, but the circle is curved and loops back. On a circle you cannot find a single number that works as a coordinate everywhere, smoothly and one-to-one: with angles, there is always a place where 359° jumps back to 0°.

三维旋转也是这样，只是没法画在纸上：它有 3 个自由度，所有三维旋转构成的 SO(3) 是一个三维的"弯曲空间"。ℝ³ 是平直的三维空间，SO(3) 是弯曲的三维空间，这就是"旋转不是普通向量"的根本原因。Rotations in 3D are the same, just impossible to draw on paper: they have 3 degrees of freedom, and the set SO(3) of all 3D rotations is a three-dimensional “curved space”. ℝ³ is flat 3D space, SO(3) is curved 3D space, and that is the root reason why “a rotation is not an ordinary vector”.

弯曲空间有一个好用的工具：切线。在圆上"不转"的那一点（记作 I）画一条切线，切线是直的，可以随便加减。把切线上长度为 φ 的一段像绳子一样缠到圆上，就得到转角为 φ 的旋转。"缠上去"这个操作叫 exp，反过来"从圆上读回切线"叫 log。这个圆叫李群，这条切线叫李代数，"李群与李代数"一节会把它推广到三维。先在平面上玩一下。Curved spaces come with a handy tool: the tangent line. At the “no rotation” point of the circle (call it I), draw the tangent line. The tangent line is straight, so you can add and subtract on it freely. Take a piece of length φ on the tangent line and wrap it onto the circle like a string, and you get the rotation by angle φ. The “wrapping” step is called exp, and reading it back from the circle onto the tangent line is called log. The circle is called a Lie group and the tangent line a Lie algebra; the “Lie groups and Lie algebras” section extends this to 3D. First, play with it in the plane.

核心逻辑Core idea

三句话看懂所有表示法Three sentences that explain every representation

1. 旋转有 3 个自由度，但它不是普通向量1. A rotation has 3 degrees of freedom, but it is not an ordinary vector

确定一个三维姿态需要 3 个数，所以说它有 3 个自由度。但所有姿态构成的 SO(3) 是弯曲的，不是平直的 ℝ³。所以旋转不能直接相加，也不满足交换律。Pinning down a 3D orientation takes 3 numbers, so it has 3 degrees of freedom. But the set SO(3) of all orientations is curved, not the flat ℝ³. So rotations cannot simply be added, and they do not commute.

2. 每种表示法都只是一套"坐标"，而 3 个数的坐标一定有坏点2. Every representation is just a set of “coordinates”, and any 3-number coordinates must have bad points

矩阵、欧拉角、轴角、四元数，都是给 SO(3) 里的点起名字的方式。就像地球上的一个地点，可以用经纬度描述，也可以用三维坐标描述。数学上有个定理（Stuelpnagel, 1964）：只用 3 个数，不可能既覆盖所有旋转，又处处没有奇异点。Matrices, Euler angles, axis-angle and quaternions are all ways of naming points of SO(3), just as a place on Earth can be described by latitude and longitude or by 3D coordinates. There is a theorem (Stuelpnagel, 1964): with only 3 numbers, you cannot both cover every rotation and avoid singularities everywhere.

"奇异点"就是坐标失灵的地方，表现为三种症状之一：一个姿态对应无穷多组数；姿态稍微一动，数字就突然跳变；算变化率时要除以 0。经纬度就是现成的例子：两个数描述球面，到了北极点，经度可以是任何值，这一维失灵了。A “singularity” is a place where the coordinates break down, showing one of three symptoms: one orientation corresponds to infinitely many sets of numbers; a tiny change in orientation makes the numbers jump; or computing rates of change requires dividing by 0. Latitude and longitude are a ready-made example: two numbers describe a sphere, and at the North Pole the longitude can be anything, so that coordinate fails.

3. 所以只有两条路3. So there are only two routes

约束constraint
数字之间必须满足的关系，比如"四个数的平方和等于 1"a relation the numbers must satisfy, for example “the four squares add up to 1”

真正能独立变化的方向有几个degrees of freedom: how many independent directions things can actually change in

第一条路：只用 3 个数，没有约束，但一定有奇异点。欧拉角、轴角/旋转向量走这条路。第二条路：多用几个数、加上约束，换来全局光滑。四元数是 4 个数减 1 个约束，旋转矩阵是 9 个数减 6 个约束，自由度都是 3。代价是要时刻维护约束，四元数还会给每个姿态起两个名字。Route one: use only 3 numbers with no constraints, and accept singularities. Euler angles and axis-angle/rotation vectors take this route. Route two: use more numbers plus constraints, and get global smoothness in return. A quaternion is 4 numbers minus 1 constraint, a rotation matrix 9 numbers minus 6 constraints; both have 3 degrees of freedom. The price is keeping the constraints satisfied, and quaternions also give every orientation two names.

现代机器人软件两条路一起用：用矩阵或四元数存储和复合姿态，用 3 个数的"小增量"做求导和优化，欧拉角只在给人看的时候用。后面每种表示的优缺点，基本都能从这三句话推出来。Modern robotics software uses both routes together: matrices or quaternions to store and compose orientations, 3-number “small increments” for derivatives and optimization, and Euler angles only for showing things to people. The pros and cons of each representation follow almost entirely from these three sentences.

单位四元数Unit quaternions

先回忆复数：平面上的点 (x, y) 可以写成复数 x + iy，乘以 cos θ + i sin θ，就把它转了 θ。四元数是哈密顿（Hamilton）把这个想法推广到三维的结果：一个四元数有 4 个分量，写成 q = w + x i + y j + z k，规则是 i² = j² = k² = ijk = −1。由此能推出 ij = k、ji = −k，乘法不能交换顺序，和三维旋转一样。Recall complex numbers: a point (x, y) in the plane can be written as the complex number x + iy, and multiplying by cos θ + i sin θ turns it by θ. Quaternions are Hamilton’s extension of this idea to 3D: a quaternion has 4 components, written q = w + x i + y j + z k, with the rules i² = j² = k² = ijk = −1. From these it follows that ij = k but ji = −k, so multiplication does not commute, just like 3D rotations.

这里的 i、j、k 只是四元数的三个"虚数单位"，和转轴 k 无关。下面一律把四元数写成 (w, x, y, z) 四个数，或者写成 (w, u)：w 是实部，一个数；u = (x, y, z) 是虚部，一个三维向量。The i, j, k here are just the quaternion’s three “imaginary units” and have nothing to do with the rotation axis k. From here on a quaternion is written as four numbers (w, x, y, z), or as (w, u): w is the real part, a number; u = (x, y, z) is the imaginary part, a 3D vector.

转换关系Conversions

它们之间怎么互相换算How to convert between them

规律只有一条：任何表示 → 矩阵或四元数，都是光滑的闭式公式，永远能算；反过来，矩阵或四元数 → 3 个数的表示，才会碰到奇异（万向锁、θ = 0 或 180°）和多解（±q、欧拉角的等价解）。所以工程上把矩阵或四元数当"枢纽"，欧拉角和轴角之间不直接换算，都经过它们中转。There is just one rule: going from any representation → matrix or quaternion is a smooth closed-form formula that always works; going back from matrix or quaternion → a 3-number representation is where you hit singularities (gimbal lock, θ = 0 or 180°) and multiple solutions (±q, equivalent Euler triples). So in practice the matrix or quaternion is the “hub”: Euler angles and axis-angle are never converted directly into each other, but always via the hub.

At t = 0.5 both weights are sin 22.5° / sin 45° = 0.541, and the result is 0.541 × (1.707, 0, 0, 0.707) = (0.924, 0, 0, 0.383), exactly 45° about z: precisely half way.

为什么要先检查符号：q₁ 和 −q₁ 是同一个姿态，但在 4 维球面上一个离 q₀ 近、一个远。点积小于 0 说明 q₁ 在"远的那一侧"，不翻过来，SLERP 就会转 360° 减去最短夹角的远路。Why check the sign first: q₁ and −q₁ are the same orientation, but on the 4D sphere one is near q₀ and the other far. A negative dot product means q₁ is on the “far side”; unless you flip it, SLERP takes the long way round, 360° minus the shortest angle.

R(t) = R₀ · exp( t · log( R₀ᵀ R₁ ) )

从里往外读：R₀ᵀR₁ 是"从起点到终点还差的那个旋转"；log 把它变成旋转向量（转轴和转角）；乘以 t 表示只转其中一部分；exp 再变回旋转；最后接在起点 R₀ 后面。这条路径就是弯曲空间里的"直线"（测地线），和 SLERP 是同一条。Read it from the inside out: R₀ᵀR₁ is “the rotation still needed to get from start to end”; log turns it into a rotation vector (axis and angle); multiplying by t means turning only part of the way; exp turns it back into a rotation; finally, it is applied after the start R₀. This path is the “straight line” of the curved space (a geodesic), and it is the same path as SLERP.

实际怎么选How to choose in practice

  • 存姿态、做复合：四元数（定期归一化）或矩阵。Storing and composing orientations: quaternions (normalized now and then) or matrices.
  • 变换点、串坐标系：齐次矩阵 T。Transforming points, chaining frames: homogeneous matrices T.
  • 优化、滤波、求雅可比：李代数里的 3 维扰动 δ。雅可比就是"输出对输入的导数"排成的矩阵，优化器靠它决定往哪走。Optimization, filtering, Jacobians: 3D perturbations δ in the Lie algebra. A Jacobian is the matrix of “derivatives of outputs with respect to inputs”; the optimizer uses it to decide which way to go.
  • 显示和人工输入：欧拉角，只在接口处用，并写清轴序、内旋还是外旋、度还是弧度。Display and manual input: Euler angles, only at the interface, and always state the axis order, intrinsic or extrinsic, and degrees or radians.
  • 轨迹插值：SLERP，先保证 q₀ · q₁ ≥ 0。Trajectory interpolation: SLERP, after making sure q₀ · q₁ ≥ 0.
  • 报角度误差：‖log(Restᵀ Rgt)‖，等价于 2 · acos(|q₁ · q₂|)。est 是估计值，gt 是真值（ground truth）。Reporting angular error: ‖log(Restᵀ Rgt)‖, equivalent to 2 · acos(|q₁ · q₂|). est is the estimate, gt the ground truth.
  • 点云配准：Kabsch / Umeyama 算法（给两组一一对应的点，用 SVD 一步算出最优旋转）直接给出 R，注意 det = −1 的镜像解要修正；ICP（迭代最近点：反复"找最近点、求变换"）每次迭代在旋转向量上线性化求增量。Point-cloud registration: the Kabsch / Umeyama algorithm (given two sets of corresponding points, it finds the best rotation in one step with SVD) gives R directly, but correct the mirrored solution with det = −1; ICP (iterative closest point: repeatedly “match closest points, solve for the transform”) linearizes in the rotation vector at each iteration to find the increment.
  • 神经网络回归姿态：用 6D 表示，即网络输出 R 的前两列，再用 Gram–Schmidt 正交化补出完整矩阵（把第二根向量里沿第一根的分量减掉，各自归一化，第三列用叉积得到）。四元数和欧拉角都有不连续点，不适合直接回归。Regressing orientation with neural networks: use the 6D representation: the network outputs the first two columns of R, and Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization completes the matrix (remove from the second vector its component along the first, normalize both, and get the third column with a cross product). Quaternions and Euler angles both have discontinuities and are poor targets for direct regression.

常见坑Common pitfalls

  1. Rab 到底是哪个方向？是"b 系在 a 系里的姿态"，还是"把 a 系坐标变到 b 系"？两者差一个转置。先定义清楚，再写代码。Which way does Rab go? Is it “the orientation of frame b in frame a”, or “converts frame-a coordinates into frame b”? The two differ by a transpose. Define it clearly before writing code.
  2. 四元数的顺序和约定：wxyz 还是 xyzw，Hamilton 还是 JPL。Quaternion order and convention: wxyz or xyzw, Hamilton or JPL.
  3. 欧拉角的约定：轴序、内旋还是外旋、度还是弧度。Euler angle conventions: axis order, intrinsic or extrinsic, degrees or radians.
  4. 反三角函数的精度：acos、asin 的参数先截断到 [−1, 1]。算轴角的 θ 时，用 atan2(‖(R − Rᵀ)∨‖ / 2, (tr R − 1) / 2) 代替 acos，在 0° 和 180° 附近精确得多。这里 (R − Rᵀ)∨ 就是 (r32 − r23, r13 − r31, r21 − r12)，它的长度的一半正好是 sin θ。Precision of inverse trig functions: clamp the arguments of acos and asin to [−1, 1] first. When computing the axis-angle θ, use atan2(‖(R − Rᵀ)∨‖ / 2, (tr R − 1) / 2) instead of acos; it is far more accurate near 0° and 180°. Here (R − Rᵀ)∨ is (r32 − r23, r13 − r31, r21 − r12), and half its length is exactly sin θ.
  5. 求多个旋转的平均：不要直接平均欧拉角或矩阵元素。四元数先统一到同一半球，再相加、归一化，姿态彼此接近时够用；严格做法是取 Σ qiqiᵀ 这个 4×4 矩阵最大特征值对应的特征向量（Markley 方法）。Averaging several rotations: do not average Euler angles or matrix entries directly. Put the quaternions on the same half of the sphere first, then add them up and normalize, which is good enough when the orientations are close together; the rigorous way is to take the eigenvector of the 4×4 matrix Σ qiqiᵀ with the largest eigenvalue (Markley’s method).

检验题Check question

对姿态做卡尔曼滤波时，如果状态里存的是四元数，协方差矩阵应该是 4×4 还是 3×3？为什么？When running a Kalman filter on orientation with a quaternion in the state, should the covariance matrix be 4×4 or 3×3? Why?

提示Hint

四元数有几个数？有几个自由度？在"长度必须为 1"的那个方向上，会有不确定性吗？再回头看看"优化：在切空间里走一小步"。How many numbers does a quaternion have? How many degrees of freedom? Can there be any uncertainty along the direction where “the length must be 1”? Look back at “Optimization: take a small step in the tangent space”.

参考答案Answer

3×3。四元数虽然有 4 个数，但只有 3 个自由度；沿"长度"那个方向不允许有任何变化，4×4 协方差在这个方向上没有意义，矩阵会奇异或病态。常用做法是误差状态卡尔曼滤波：状态里存四元数，误差用切空间里的 3 维小旋转 δθ 表示，q真 = q估 ⊗ (1, δθ/2)，协方差描述的是 δθ，所以是 3×3。每次更新后，把 δθ 用 exp 贴回四元数，再把误差清零。3×3. A quaternion has 4 numbers but only 3 degrees of freedom; nothing is allowed to change along the “length” direction, so a 4×4 covariance is meaningless in that direction and the matrix becomes singular or ill-conditioned. The usual approach is the error-state Kalman filter: the state stores the quaternion, and the error is a small 3D rotation δθ in the tangent space, qtrue = qest ⊗ (1, δθ/2). The covariance describes δθ, so it is 3×3. After each update, δθ is folded back into the quaternion with exp, and the error is reset to zero.
