# Checks behind page 12 · 第 12 篇里"你会看到"那些数的核对

- `check.js` → `check.txt`: opens the page in a headless browser, operates every interactive the way a reader would
  (following each "先玩 / Try first" prompt literally: pressing buttons, moving sliders, and dragging the poles in the
  s-plane and z-plane with the mouse) and compares the readouts with the numbers quoted in the "你会看到 / What you
  will see" lines and the figure captions. Where the text does a small sum itself, the script redoes it: the length of
  the sum of two arrows, decibels of ratios, the gain from averaging, the aliased frequency, bins of the DFT, the
  dips of a comb, the Butterworth roll-off, decay times from pole positions, z = e^(sT), PSNR. The level of the
  valleys between harmonics under the rectangular and the Hann window is computed with the page's own functions
  (`TB.makeRec`, `TB.spectrum`). The thirteen answers of the quiz at the end of the page are recomputed as well.
  Run: `node check.js /absolute/path/to/signals/index.html` (needs Node and Playwright; about 3 minutes).

What the script cannot check is what a reader hears. Where a "What you will see" line says how something sounds, it
repeats what the page or the interactive's own status line says at that setting, or states what follows from the
measured numbers (a doubled frequency is an octave; three low harmonics with 8% of the power sound quiet and dull).
The recording itself is synthetic: every part of it (clap, voice, hum, hiss, room) is generated by the code in the
page, so each number can be recomputed from that code.

`check.js` 打开页面，按每一步"先玩"里写的顺序去点、去拖（s 平面和 z 平面上的极点是用鼠标拖的），把读数和正文里"你会看到"、
图注里写的数逐个对一遍；正文里自己做的小算术也重算一遍（两根箭头之和的长度、比值的分贝数、平均带来的增益、混叠以后的频率、
DFT 的频点、梳状滤波的坑、巴特沃斯的滚降、由极点位置算衰减时间、z = e^(sT)、PSNR）。矩形窗和汉宁窗下谐波之间低谷的电平，
是用页面自己的函数（`TB.makeRec`、`TB.spectrum`）算的。页面最后小测验的十三个答案也都重算。
脚本核对不了"听起来怎么样"：正文里这类话，要么是页面或交互自己在那个设置下的状态栏写的，要么是从量出来的数推出来的
（频率翻倍就是高八度；只有三个低的谐波、功率只剩 8%，听起来就又轻又闷）。
这段录音是合成的，拍手、人声、嗡嗡声、沙沙声和房间的回声都由页面里的代码生成，所以每个数都能从代码重算。
