两秒钟的录音Two Seconds of Sound

在厨房里用手机录了两秒：先拍一下手，再说一声"啊——"。录下来的不只是这两样：还有电源带来的嗡嗡声、一层沙沙的底噪、厨房墙壁的回声。这两秒在手机里是一串数，每秒 16000 个。信号与系统里那一堆词，都是在回答关于这串数的问题：它有多响（分贝）？干不干净（信噪比）？里面有哪些频率（傅里叶）、占了多宽（带宽）？怎样把它放到电台的载波上传出去（调制）？每秒记多少个数才不丢东西（采样）？厨房把声音变成了什么样（卷积）？怎么把嗡嗡声去掉（滤波器）？拉普拉斯变换和 z 变换，是分析和设计这些"处理"的语言：前一个用在连续的世界里，后一个用在数字的世界里。这一篇只用这一段录音，把这些词一个个讲清楚：每个定义配一个物理直觉，每个公式都代入这段录音的数。最后换成手机顺手拍的一张厨房照片：同样的概念在图像里怎么出现，哪里不一样，背后共同的东西是什么。Two seconds recorded on a phone in a kitchen: first a clap, then a voice saying "ah—". But the recording holds more than these two: there is also a hum from the mains, a layer of background hiss, and the echoes off the kitchen walls. Inside the phone, the two seconds are a string of numbers, 16000 of them every second. All the jargon of signals and systems is there to answer questions about this string of numbers. How loud is it (decibels)? How clean is it (signal-to-noise ratio)? Which frequencies are in it (Fourier), and how wide a band does it take up (bandwidth)? How do you put it on a radio station’s carrier and send it out (modulation)? How many numbers a second must you record so that nothing is lost (sampling)? What has the kitchen done to the sound (convolution)? How do you get rid of the hum (filters)? The Laplace transform and the z-transform are the languages for analysing and designing this kind of "processing": the first for the continuous world, the second for the digital one. This page uses only this one recording to explain these terms one at a time: every definition comes with a physical intuition, and every formula is filled in with this recording’s numbers. At the end we switch to a quick phone snap of the kitchen: how the same ideas appear in images, where they differ, and what they share underneath.

两秒钟里有什么What’s in the two seconds

整篇只用这一段录音，先把它说清楚。手机放在厨房的台面上，你站在一米开外。录音从 0 秒开始，一共 2 秒：The whole page uses just this one recording, so first let’s describe it properly. The phone lies on the kitchen worktop and you stand a metre away. The recording starts at 0 seconds and lasts 2 seconds in all:

  • 0.25 秒，你拍了一下手。拍手只有几毫秒，是整段里最响的一下。At 0.25 seconds you clap once. A clap lasts only a few milliseconds, and this one is the loudest moment in the whole recording.
  • 0.80 到 1.80 秒，你说了一声"啊——"，音高平平的，每秒振动 220 次。From 0.80 to 1.80 seconds you say "ah—" on a flat, steady pitch, vibrating 220 times a second.
  • 从头到尾都有两样东西：冰箱、灯这些电器跟着 50 Hz 的交流电振出来的嗡嗡声（50 Hz 和它的整数倍 100、150 Hz）；抽油烟机的风扇和话筒自己的电路带来的沙沙声，各个频率都有一点。From start to finish two things are always there: the hum of appliances such as the fridge and the lights, which vibrate in step with the 50 Hz mains (50 Hz and its whole-number multiples, 100 and 150 Hz); and the hiss from the cooker hood’s fan and the microphone’s own circuitry, a little at every frequency.
  • 厨房的墙、台面、柜门又硬又平，拍手和人声都会被它们来回反射：话筒先收到直接传来的声音，1.9 毫秒后收到第一次反射，然后越来越密、越来越弱，大约半秒才完全消失。这就是回声（混响）。The kitchen’s walls, worktop and cupboard doors are hard and flat, so the clap and the voice both bounce back and forth between them. The microphone first picks up the sound that comes straight to it, then the first reflection 1.9 milliseconds later, then more and more reflections, ever denser and weaker, until they die away completely after about half a second. This is the echo (reverberation).

手机每秒记 16000 个数（采样率 16 kHz），两秒一共 32000 个，每个都在 −1 和 1 之间。这段录音是用程序合成的：真的录音里每样东西都缠在一起，没法单独拿出来；合成的录音每一样都单独知道，后面每一步都能把它们拆开看，每个数也都算得清楚。The phone records 16000 numbers a second (a sample rate of 16 kHz), 32000 in the two seconds, each between −1 and 1. This recording was made by a program: in a real recording everything is tangled together and nothing can be taken out on its own; in a synthetic one every part is known separately, so each later step can pull them apart, and every number can be worked out exactly.

先玩：先把音量调小，听一遍。再关掉人声和拍手，只听剩下的两样：嗡嗡声低，沙沙声高，你分得出来吗？看第 ③ 帧："啊"的波形每隔多久重复一次？关掉回声，再看第 ② 帧：拍手后面那一串小尖峰去哪了？Try first: turn the volume down and listen once. 这几帧里已经藏着这一篇所有的问题。第 ③ 帧里的形状每 4.55 毫秒重复一次：重复得多快，就是频率；一个周期里的形状，由哪些更快的振动叠出来，要靠傅里叶去拆。四样东西有大有小：人声的一个起伏能到 0.2，嗡嗡声只有它的十分之一，沙沙声更小，这些大小之比用分贝说最方便，人声比噪声大多少，就是信噪比。第 ② 帧里，一下拍手变成了一长串回声：厨房对每一下声音都做同样的事，这件事叫卷积。最后，想把嗡嗡声和沙沙声去掉、把录音发出去，就要设计滤波器，决定采样率和带宽。These frames already hide every question on this page. The shape in frame ③ repeats every 4.55 milliseconds: how fast it repeats is its frequency; to find which faster vibrations add up to the shape within one period, you need Fourier to take it apart. The four parts come in very different sizes: one swing of the voice reaches 0.2, the hum is only a tenth of that, and the hiss is smaller still. Ratios of sizes like these are easiest to talk about in decibels, and how much bigger the voice is than the noise is the signal-to-noise ratio. In frame ②, a single clap turns into a long string of echoes: the kitchen does the same thing to every sound, and this is called convolution. Finally, to remove the hum and the hiss and to send the recording out, you have to design filters and settle on a sample rate and a bandwidth.

两种看法：沿着时间，沿着频率Two views: along time, along frequency

同一段录音有两种看法。一种是上面这样，沿着时间看：每一刻的值是多少，这叫时域。另一种是把它拆成许多不同频率的正弦，看每个频率有多少，这叫频域。嗡嗡声在时域里是一条慢慢起伏的波，在频域里只是 50、100、150 Hz 三根细线；沙沙声在时域里是一片乱糟糟的毛刺，在频域里是铺满所有频率的一层薄薄的底。很多事情在一个域里很难，换到另一个域里就很简单，这一篇大半的工具，都是在这两个域之间来回换。There are two ways to look at the same recording. One is the way above, along time: what the value is at each moment. This is the time domain. The other is to split the recording into many sines of different frequencies and see how much there is at each frequency. This is the frequency domain. In the time domain the hum is a slowly rising and falling wave; in the frequency domain it is just three thin lines at 50, 100 and 150 Hz. In the time domain the hiss is a messy fuzz of spikes; in the frequency domain it is a thin floor spread across every frequency. Many things that are hard in one domain become simple in the other, and most of the tools on this page are about switching back and forth between the two.

第 ① 步 · 频率、幅度、相位Step ① · Frequency, amplitude, phase

最简单的信号：一个正弦The simplest signal: a sine

第 ③ 帧里的"啊"每 4.55 毫秒重复一次，可一个周期里的形状弯弯曲曲。先看最简单的一种重复：一个光滑的正弦。音叉敲一下发出的声音、挂在弹簧上的重物上下颠，都是正弦：它们离开平衡位置越远，被拉回来的力越大，而且两者成正比，满足这一条的东西，一振起来就是正弦。交流电的电压也是正弦，因为发电机里的线圈在磁场里匀速地转，下面马上会看到，匀速转动正是正弦的来历。The "ah" in frame ③ repeats every 4.55 milliseconds, but the shape within one period is full of wiggles. Start with the simplest kind of repetition: a smooth sine. The sound of a struck tuning fork and a weight bobbing up and down on a spring are both sines. The further they move from their resting position, the harder they are pulled back, in exact proportion, and anything that obeys this rule moves as a sine once it starts to vibrate. AC voltage is a sine too, because the coil in a generator turns at a steady rate in a magnetic field; as you will see in a moment, steady turning is exactly where the sine comes from.

x(t) = A cos(2π f t + φ) = A cos(ω t + φ)

t 时刻的值，等于幅度 A 乘以一个角度的余弦；这个角度从 φ 出发，每秒转 f 圈。The value at time t equals the amplitude A times the cosine of an angle; the angle starts at φ and turns f full circles every second.

把一个点放在半径为 A 的圆上匀速转，它在水平线上的影子就这样来回走：转到最右是 +A，最左是 −A。所以三个数就把一个正弦说完了：圆多大（幅度 A）、转多快（频率 f）、从哪里出发（相位 φ）。转一圈是 2π 弧度，每秒转 f 圈，就是每秒转 2πf 弧度，这个数叫角频率 ω。Put a point on a circle of radius A and turn it at a steady speed: its shadow on a horizontal line moves back and forth in exactly this way, reaching +A at the far right and −A at the far left. So three numbers say everything about a sine: how big the circle is (amplitude A), how fast it turns (frequency f) and where it starts (phase φ). One turn is 2π radians, so f turns a second is 2πf radians a second; this number is called the angular frequency ω.

The lowest part of the hum is at 50 Hz, with a period of 20 ms: that is the slow wave in frame ①. Sound travels 343 m through air every second, and the distance it covers in one period is the wavelength λ = c/f: 1.56 m at 220 Hz and 6.86 m at 50 Hz. Across the 20 Hz to 20 kHz that human ears can hear, wavelengths run from 17 m down to 1.7 cm.

频率和幅度是信号自己的；相位却要先说好"从哪一刻算起"。把计时的起点挪一下，φ 就变了，可声音一点没变。所以单独一个声音的相位意义不大，两个声音之间的相位差才有用。Frequency and amplitude belong to the signal itself, but phase only makes sense once you have agreed "which moment to count from". Move the starting point of the clock and φ changes, yet the sound has not changed at all. So the phase of a single sound means little; what is useful is the phase difference between two sounds.

相位就是时间差Phase is a time difference

同一个声音晚到 τ 秒，波形整体往后挪 τ：x(t − τ) = A cos(2πf t + φ − 2πf τ)。也就是说，晚 τ 秒等于相位少了 2πfτ。晚到的时间一样，频率越高，落后的角度越多。你离手机远 0.5 m，声音就晚到 0.5 / 343 = 1.46 ms：对 220 Hz 是落后 115°，对 1 kHz 是落后 525°，也就是一圈多 165°。If the same sound arrives τ seconds later, the whole waveform shifts later by τ: x(t − τ) = A cos(2πf t + φ − 2πf τ). In other words, arriving τ seconds late is the same as losing 2πfτ of phase. For the same delay, the higher the frequency, the bigger the angle it falls behind. Stand 0.5 m further from the phone and the sound arrives 0.5 / 343 = 1.46 ms later: at 220 Hz that is 115° behind, and at 1 kHz it is 525° behind, which is one full turn plus 165°.

耳朵每天都在用相位差：左右两只耳朵相距十几厘米，从一侧来的声音到两只耳朵差零点几毫秒，低频的声音，大脑主要靠这个时间差（也就是相位差）判断方向。Your ears use phase differences every day. They sit between ten and twenty centimetres apart, so a sound from one side reaches the two ears a few tenths of a millisecond apart; for low-frequency sounds, the brain judges direction mainly from this time difference (that is, from the phase difference).

反相抵消，就是降噪耳机的道理：耳机外面的话筒听到噪声，耳机里的喇叭放出一个和它反相的声音，两个在耳朵里抵消。这个想法 1933 年就有人申请了专利（Lueg 1936）。可它对高频很难做好：抵消要求两个声音的相位差正好是 180°，晚到同样的时间，频率越高，相位错得越多。1 kHz 时差 0.1 ms 就错了 36°，于是降噪耳机主要压低频的轰鸣，人声和高频的噪声还得靠耳罩去挡。Cancelling in anti-phase is how noise-cancelling headphones work: a microphone on the outside of the headphones picks up the noise, and the speaker inside plays a sound in anti-phase with it, so the two cancel in your ear. Someone applied for a patent on this idea as early as 1933 (Lueg 1936). But it is hard to do well at high frequencies: cancelling needs the two sounds to be exactly 180° apart, and for the same delay, the higher the frequency, the further the phase is off. At 1 kHz, being 0.1 ms off already puts the phase out by 36°. So noise-cancelling headphones mainly suppress low-frequency rumble, and voices and high-frequency noise still have to be blocked by the ear cups.

两个频率略有不同的声音叠在一起，两根箭头转得不一样快，一会儿同向、一会儿反向，和的大小就一胀一缩：220 Hz 和 222 Hz 一起响，每秒胀缩 2 次，这叫拍。调吉他时，两根弦的音越接近，拍越慢，直到听不出来。When two sounds of slightly different frequencies are added, the two arrows turn at different speeds, now pointing the same way, now opposite ways, so the size of the sum swells and shrinks: 220 Hz and 222 Hz sounding together swell and shrink 2 times a second. These are called beats. When you tune a guitar, the closer two strings get in pitch, the slower the beats, until you can no longer hear them.

这一步的要点：正弦由三个数决定：幅度 A、频率 f（或周期 T = 1/f）、相位 φ。把它看成一根转动的箭头的影子，晚到 τ 秒就是相位少 2πfτ；同频率的两个声音相加，就是两根箭头相加，同相时加强，反相时抵消。The point of this step: a sine is set by three numbers: amplitude A, frequency f (or period T = 1/f) and phase φ. Think of it as the shadow of a turning arrow: arriving τ seconds late means losing 2πfτ of phase, and adding two sounds of the same frequency means adding two arrows, which reinforce each other in phase and cancel in anti-phase.

第 ② 步 · 分贝Step ② · Decibels

有多响：用对数说大小How loud: describing size with logarithms

录音里四样东西有大有小。正弦有幅度，可人声、沙沙声这种乱七八糟的波形，"幅度"说不清。更好的量法是功率：把每个数平方，再在一段时间里取平均，记作 P。平方以后正负都算数，而且对很多物理量，平方正比于能量：声压的平方正比于声音带的能量，电压的平方正比于电阻上发的热。功率开个平方根，叫有效值（RMS），它和原来的数同一个单位。一个幅度为 A 的正弦，有效值是 A/√2 ≈ 0.707A。The four parts of the recording differ a lot in size. A sine has an amplitude, but for messy waveforms like the voice or the hiss, "amplitude" is hard to pin down. A better measure is power: square every value, then average over a stretch of time, and call the result P. Once squared, negative values count as well as positive ones, and for many physical quantities the square is proportional to energy: the square of the sound pressure is proportional to the energy the sound carries, and the square of a voltage is proportional to the heat it produces in a resistor. The square root of the power is called the root mean square (RMS) value, and it has the same unit as the original values. A sine of amplitude A has an RMS value of A/√2 ≈ 0.707A.

The voice’s RMS value is 10 times the hum’s, and its power 100 times; it is 31.6 times the hiss’s, and its power 1000 times. With only three things that is still manageable, but the range of sound levels is astonishing: between a sound you can only just hear and one that hurts your ears, the sound pressure differs by a factor of a million and the power by a factor of a million million. Signals also often pass through one stage after another: microphone, amplifier, filter, loudspeaker, each multiplying them by some factor, so in the end you have to multiply all the factors together. Logarithms make both of these simple: a huge range is squeezed into small numbers, and a chain of multiplications becomes a chain of additions.

The "bel" is a factor of 10 in power, which is too coarse, so a tenth of it is normally used: the decibel (dB). 1 dB is roughly the smallest change in loudness the ear can notice. The name comes from Bell, the inventor of the telephone: in the 1920s the Bell Telephone System first used this unit to measure losses on telephone lines, calling it the "transmission unit"; in 1929 it was renamed the decibel, and ten decibels make one bel (Martin 1929).

The other way round, −10 dB leaves 1/10 of the power, −20 dB leaves 1/10 of the amplitude, and −60 dB leaves a thousandth of the amplitude: the kitchen’s reverberation time RT60 = 0.5 s means that after a sound stops, it takes half a second for the echo’s amplitude to fall to a thousandth.

分贝不能直接相加。两个一样大、互不相关的声音（两台同样的风扇）叠在一起，是功率相加，变成 2 倍，只多 3 dB；两个一模一样、同相的声音叠在一起，是幅度相加，变成 2 倍，多 6 dB。一个比另一个小 10 dB 的不相关声音，只让总量多 0.41 dB：嗡嗡声 −40.0 dB 加上沙沙声 −50.0 dB，一共 −39.6 dB。Decibels cannot simply be added. When two equally loud, uncorrelated sounds (two identical fans) are heard together, their powers add: the power becomes 2 times as large, which is only 3 dB more. When two identical sounds in phase are added, their amplitudes add: the amplitude becomes 2 times as large, which is 6 dB more. An uncorrelated sound 10 dB quieter than another raises the total by only 0.41 dB: the hum at −40.0 dB plus the hiss at −50.0 dB comes to −39.6 dB.

第 ③ 步 · 信噪比Step ③ · Signal-to-noise ratio

有多干净：信号比噪声大多少How clean: how much bigger the signal is than the noise

录音里我们要的是人声，嗡嗡声和沙沙声都是不想要的，统称噪声。录音干不干净，看的不是噪声本身有多大，而是它和人声比起来有多大：同样的沙沙声，盖在大声说的话上听不太出来，盖在悄悄话上就很吵。这个比就是信噪比。What we want from the recording is the voice; the hum and the hiss are both unwanted, and together they are called noise. How clean a recording is depends not on how big the noise is in itself, but on how big it is compared with the voice: the same hiss is barely noticeable over loud speech, but very intrusive over a whisper. This ratio is the signal-to-noise ratio.

两个都是功率，比一比就知道谁压过谁；取对数以后，除法变成减法，所以在分贝的尺子上，信噪比就是两个标记之间的距离。噪声的功率要在"只有噪声"的地方量：这段录音的开头 0.25 秒还没人出声，正好拿来量。Both are powers, so comparing them tells you which one dominates; taking logarithms turns division into subtraction, so on a decibel ruler the SNR is simply the distance between two marks. The noise power has to be measured where there is "only noise": in the first 0.25 s of this recording nobody has made a sound yet, which makes it just right for the job.

什么算"噪声"，取决于你要什么。想听人声，嗡嗡声是噪声；想测厨房的回声，拍手才是信号。回声也不是噪声：它不是另外加上去的一样东西，而是人声自己被厨房改变了的样子，要用别的办法对付（第 ⑩ 步）。What counts as "noise" depends on what you are after. If you want to hear the voice, the hum is noise; if you want to measure the kitchen’s echo, it is the clap that is the signal. The echo is not noise either: it is not something separate added on top, but the voice itself as changed by the kitchen, and it has to be dealt with in other ways (step ⑩).

N 次录音里，人声每次都一样，相加是幅度相加，变成 N 倍，功率变成 N² 倍；噪声每次都不一样、互不相关，相加只是功率相加，变成 N 倍（上一步：两个不相关的声音只多 3 dB）。除以 N 取平均不改变两者之比，所以信噪比赚了 N² / N = N 倍。In the N recordings the voice is the same every time, so adding them adds amplitudes: N times the amplitude, N² times the power. The noise is different every time and uncorrelated, so adding only adds powers: N times the power (previous step: two uncorrelated sounds give only 3 dB more). Dividing by N to take the average does not change the ratio between them, so the SNR gains a factor of N² / N = N.

平均 4 次多 6.0 dB，16 次多 12.0 dB，64 次多 18.1 dB，100 次多 20 dB。想多赚 10 dB，就要多录 10 倍的次数。Averaging 4 times gains 6.0 dB, 16 times 12.0 dB, 64 times 18.1 dB, 100 times 20 dB. To gain another 10 dB, you need 10 times as many recordings.

前提是噪声每次独立，而且信号每次都一模一样、对得齐。嗡嗡声每次录都在，可它和人声的相位关系每次不一样，平均也能把它压下去；可要是噪声和信号同步（比如每次都是同一下回声），平均就一点用也没有。This assumes the noise is independent each time, and that the signal is exactly the same each time and lines up. The hum is there in every take, but its phase relative to the voice is different each time, so averaging pushes it down too; but if the noise is in step with the signal (say, the same echo every time), averaging does no good at all.

这个办法用得很广：测脑电时，一次刺激引起的脑电反应埋在背景脑电里，要把几十上百次对齐平均才看得出来；核磁共振、雷达、超声也常常把同一次测量重复多次再平均，用测量时间换信噪比。This trick is used widely. When brain waves are recorded (EEG), the brain’s response to a single stimulus is buried in the background activity, and only becomes visible after dozens or hundreds of trials are lined up and averaged; MRI, radar and ultrasound also often repeat the same measurement many times and average the results, trading measurement time for SNR.

还有一个省事的办法：只保留信号所在的那些频率。沙沙声这种白噪声，功率平均分在 0 到 8 kHz 上，每 1 Hz 只分到 −89 dB。只留 0 到 4 kHz，噪声的功率就少了一半，信噪比多 3 dB；而人声的能量几乎全在 4 kHz 以下，一点不少。所以白噪声的功率和你留了多宽的频率成正比，这也是电子电路里热噪声的规律：电阻里电子热运动带来的噪声功率，正比于温度和带宽（Johnson 1928，Nyquist 1928）。怎么只留一段频率，是第 ⑥ 步和第 ⑪ 步的事。There is an easier way too: keep only the frequencies where the signal is. White noise such as the hiss has its power spread evenly over 0 to 8 kHz, so each 1 Hz gets only −89 dB. Keep only 0 to 4 kHz and the noise power halves, so the SNR rises by 3 dB, while the voice, with almost all its energy below 4 kHz, loses nothing. So the power of white noise is proportional to how wide a band of frequencies you keep, and this is also the rule for thermal noise in electronic circuits: the noise power from the thermal motion of electrons in a resistor is proportional to temperature and bandwidth (Johnson 1928, Nyquist 1928). How to keep just one band of frequencies is the subject of steps ⑥ and ⑪.

这一步的要点：信噪比是信号功率比噪声功率，用分贝说就是两者的分贝数之差：这段录音是 19.6 dB（只算沙沙声是 30.0 dB）。N 次独立的录音平均，信噪比多 10 log10N dB；只留信号所在的频率，也能把噪声去掉一部分。The point of this step: the signal-to-noise ratio is the signal power divided by the noise power; in decibels, it is the difference between their levels: 19.6 dB for this recording (30.0 dB counting only the hiss). Averaging N independent recordings adds 10 log10N dB to the SNR; keeping only the frequencies where the signal is also removes part of the noise.

第 ④ 步 · 傅里叶级数Step ④ · Fourier series

一个音，拆成一串谐波One tone, split into a string of harmonics

"啊"每 4.545 毫秒重复一次，可一个周期里的形状不是正弦：一个大的起伏上骑着更快的小起伏。傅里叶在研究热传导时提出：任何周期性的形状，都能拆成一串正弦之和，而且这些正弦的频率都是重复频率的整数倍（Fourier 1822，1807 年先在巴黎科学院宣读过）。这一步只看人声，而且先把厨房的回声关掉（合成的录音可以这么做），看声音刚从嘴里出来的样子。The "ah" repeats every 4.545 milliseconds, but within one period the shape is not a sine: smaller, faster wiggles ride on a big swell. While studying heat conduction, Fourier claimed that any periodic shape can be split into a sum of sines, and that the frequencies of these sines are all whole multiples of the repetition frequency (Fourier 1822, first read at the Paris Academy of Sciences in 1807). This step looks at the voice only, and first switches off the kitchen’s echo (a synthetic recording lets us do that), to see the sound as it comes out of the mouth.

x(t) = A0 + Σk=1∞ Ak cos(2π k f0 t + φk)

周期为 T0 的信号，等于一个常数加上一串正弦；第 k 个正弦的频率是 k f0（f0 = 1/T0），有自己的幅度 Ak 和相位 φk。A signal with period T0 equals a constant plus a string of sines; the kth sine has frequency k f0 (f0 = 1/T0) and its own amplitude Ak and phase φk.

为什么只有整数倍？要和整个信号一样每 T0 重复一次，每个成分在 T0 里都得正好走完整数个周期：f0 走 1 个，2f0 走 2 个……别的频率走到 T0 时停在半路，下一个周期就对不上了。为什么什么形状都拼得出来？这是傅里叶的大胆断言，二十多年后狄利克雷才给出严格的条件：形状只要跳变有限、起伏（极大极小）也有限，项数越来越多时，和就越来越接近它，跳变处收敛到跳变的中点（Dirichlet 1829）。Why only whole multiples? To repeat every T0 like the signal as a whole, each component has to complete exactly a whole number of cycles in T0: f0 completes 1, 2f0 completes 2, and so on. Any other frequency would be stopped halfway at T0, and the next period would not line up. Why can every shape be built this way? That was Fourier’s bold claim; only more than twenty years later did Dirichlet give rigorous conditions: as long as the shape has a finite number of jumps and also a finite number of ups and downs (maxima and minima), the sum gets closer and closer to it as more terms are added, and at a jump it converges to the midpoint of the jump (Dirichlet 1829).

So the average "recognises" only the kth harmonic and filters out all the others. Mathematically, the harmonics are orthogonal to one another.

谐波的幅度连起来是一条有峰有谷的曲线：峰在 850、1220、2810 Hz 附近。这几个峰来自嗓子到嘴唇这一段管道（声道）的共振，叫共振峰，它决定了这是"啊"而不是"咿"。换一个音高，谐波整体挪位置，包络不变，听起来还是"啊"；换一个元音，谐波的位置不变，包络变了。所以音高看谐波的间隔，音色看谐波的幅度怎么分布。第 ⑫ 步会把共振峰写成极点。Joined up, the harmonic amplitudes form a curve with peaks and valleys: the peaks are near 850, 1220 and 2810 Hz. These peaks come from resonances of the tube running from the throat to the lips (the vocal tract), and they are called formants; they are what makes this an "ah" and not an "ee". Change the pitch and the harmonics all shift, but the envelope stays the same, so it still sounds like "ah"; change the vowel and the harmonics stay where they are, but the envelope changes. So pitch depends on the spacing of the harmonics, and timbre on how their amplitudes are distributed. Step ⑫ will write the formants as poles.

相位呢？打乱以后波形面目全非，耳朵听着却几乎一样。欧姆（就是欧姆定律的那个欧姆）1843 年提出，耳朵会把一个周期性的声音拆成一个个正弦来听（Ohm 1843）；亥姆霍兹接着做实验，发现对稳定的声音，音色只看各个谐波的大小，不看它们的相位（Helmholtz 1863）。这不是绝对的：很低的音、挤在一起的高次谐波，相位会改变听到的"粗糙"程度。可大体上，耳朵像一台频谱仪，看的是幅度。What about phase? After scrambling, the waveform is unrecognisable, yet to the ear it sounds almost the same. In 1843 Ohm (the Ohm of Ohm’s law) proposed that the ear hears a periodic sound by splitting it into separate sines (Ohm 1843); Helmholtz followed this up with experiments and found that for steady sounds, timbre depends only on the sizes of the harmonics, not on their phases (Helmholtz 1863). This is not absolute: for very low notes, and for high harmonics crowded close together, phase changes how "rough" the sound seems. But broadly, the ear is like a spectrum analyser: it looks at amplitudes.

有跳变的形状最难拼。方波在跳变的地方，不管加多少项，部分和总会冲过头大约跳变高度的 9%，项数越多，冲过头的地方越窄，可高度不变。这叫吉布斯现象：威尔布拉罕 1848 年就发现了，后来以吉布斯 1899 年的信命名（Wilbraham 1848，Gibbs 1899）。图像压缩、截掉高频的滤波器边上出现的"振铃"，就是它。Shapes with jumps are the hardest to build. At the jumps of a square wave, however many terms you add, the partial sum always overshoots by about 9% of the height of the jump; with more terms the overshoot gets narrower, but its height stays the same. This is the Gibbs phenomenon: Wilbraham had already found it in 1848, but it was later named after a letter Gibbs wrote in 1899 (Wilbraham 1848, Gibbs 1899). The "ringing" that appears beside edges after image compression, or after a filter that cuts off the high frequencies, is exactly this.

这一步的要点：周期为 T0 的信号是一串谐波之和，频率是 f0 = 1/T0 的整数倍。第 k 个谐波的幅度和相位，用"乘上倒着转的箭头、在一个周期里取平均"量出来。音高看谐波的间隔，音色看谐波幅度的分布；稳定的声音，耳朵几乎不管相位。The point of this step: a signal with period T0 is a sum of harmonics whose frequencies are whole multiples of f0 = 1/T0. The amplitude and phase of the kth harmonic are measured by "multiplying by a backward-turning arrow and averaging over one period". Pitch depends on the spacing of the harmonics, timbre on how their amplitudes are distributed; for steady sounds, the ear hardly cares about phase.

第 ⑤ 步 · 傅里叶变换和频谱Step ⑤ · Fourier transform and spectrum

不重复的声音，也能拆成频率Sounds that don’t repeat can be split into frequencies too

傅里叶级数要求信号一遍遍重复。可拍手只有一下，整段录音也不重复：嗡嗡声、人声、沙沙声各走各的。办法是把"周期"想成无穷长：周期 T0 越长，谐波之间的间隔 1/T0 越小，谐波越挤，最后挤成一条连续的曲线。这条曲线叫频谱，算它的办法叫傅里叶变换。A Fourier series needs the signal to repeat over and over. But the clap happens only once, and the recording as a whole does not repeat either: the hum, the voice and the hiss each go their own way. The trick is to think of the "period" as infinitely long: the longer the period T0, the smaller the spacing 1/T0 between harmonics, and the more crowded they get, until they merge into a continuous curve. This curve is called the spectrum, and the way to calculate it is called the Fourier transform.

第一条就是第 ① 步的"相位就是时间差"，对每个频率各用一次：同样晚 τ，频率越高落后的相位越多，相位随频率直线下降。第二条：把录音快放一倍，每个振动都快一倍，所有频率翻倍，声音变尖。第三条叫帕塞瓦尔定理（对积分形式的严格证明是 Plancherel 1910）：频谱不但告诉你有哪些频率，|X(f)|² 还告诉你能量在频率上怎么分布，所以"某一段频率里有多少能量"有意义。The first is the "phase is a time difference" of step ①, applied to each frequency in turn: for the same delay τ, the higher the frequency, the more phase it loses, so the phase falls in a straight line with frequency. The second: play the recording at double speed and every vibration is twice as fast, so all frequencies double and the sound becomes shriller. The third is called Parseval’s theorem (the rigorous proof for the integral form is Plancherel 1910): the spectrum does not just tell you which frequencies are present; |X(f)|² also tells you how the energy is spread over frequency, so "how much energy lies in a given band of frequencies" makes sense.

第 ⑥ 步 · 带宽Step ⑥ · Bandwidth

占了多宽的频率How wide a band of frequencies

带宽说的是一段频率有多宽，单位是 Hz。它有两种用法。一种说信号：它的能量铺在多宽的一段频率上，"啊"的能量主要在 1.3 kHz 以下，拍手铺到好几 kHz。另一种说系统：它能让多宽的一段频率通过，电话线只让 300 到 3400 Hz 通过，带宽约 3.1 kHz；人耳能听到 20 Hz 到 20 kHz。不管哪种，频谱的边都不是一刀切的，而是慢慢降下去，所以"宽度"要先说好在哪里量。Bandwidth is how wide a band of frequencies is, measured in Hz. It is used in two ways. One is about a signal: how wide a band its energy is spread over; the energy of the "ah" lies mostly below 1.3 kHz, while the clap spreads over several kHz. The other is about a system: how wide a band it lets through; a telephone line passes only 300 to 3400 Hz, a bandwidth of about 3.1 kHz, and the human ear hears from 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Either way, the edges of a spectrum are not cut off sharply but slope away gradually, so for a "width" you first have to agree on where to measure it.

So although the fundamental of a man’s voice is often below the telephone band, you can still hear its pitch on the phone. What matters more is what the telephone loses above 3.4 kHz: that is where most of the energy of consonants such as s and f lies, so on the phone s and f are easily confused, and people spelling out letters say words like Sierra and Foxtrot.

σt · σf ≥ 1/(4π)Δt · Δf = 2 ln 2 / π ≈ 0.44

一个声音持续得越短，它占的频率越宽。按均方根宽度量，时长乘带宽有一个严格的下限 1/(4π)，包络是高斯曲线的短音正好达到它。第二个式子用更直观的量法：高斯短音功率在一半以上的时长，乘以它的 −3 dB 带宽，等于 0.44。The shorter a sound lasts, the wider the band of frequencies it occupies. Measured by RMS widths, duration times bandwidth has a strict lower limit, 1/(4π), which a tone burst with a Gaussian envelope reaches exactly. The second formula uses a more intuitive measure: for a Gaussian tone burst, the time for which the power is above half, times its −3 dB bandwidth, equals 0.44.

上一步的伸缩性质：时间压缩 a 倍，频率拉宽 a 倍，乘积不变。直觉上，要确定一个声音的频率，就得数它振了几个周期；只给你 1 毫秒，1 kHz 的音只振一下，它是 900 Hz 还是 1100 Hz 根本分不清。Gabor 把这件事写成了和量子力学里一样的不等式（Gabor 1946）。This is the scaling property from the previous step: squeeze time by a factor of a and frequency stretches by a factor of a, so the product stays the same. Intuitively, to pin down the frequency of a sound you have to count how many cycles it goes through; give a 1 kHz tone only 1 millisecond and it vibrates just once, so there is no telling whether it is 900 Hz or 1100 Hz. Gabor wrote this down as the same inequality as in quantum mechanics (Gabor 1946).

带宽和比特率Bandwidth and bit rate

C = B log2(1 + SNR)

一条带宽为 B、信噪比为 SNR 的信道，每秒最多能可靠地传 C 比特。A channel with bandwidth B and signal-to-noise ratio SNR can reliably carry at most C bits per second.

带宽决定每秒能放进多少个互相独立的数：带宽 B 的信号，每秒最多 2B 个（Nyquist 1928，第 ⑧ 步的采样定理是同一件事）。信噪比决定每个数能分出多少档：噪声越小，能区分的档越多，每个数装的比特越多。香农把两者合在一起，证明了这是上限，而且可以无限接近（Shannon 1948，Shannon 1949）。Bandwidth decides how many independent numbers fit into each second: a signal of bandwidth B carries at most 2B per second (Nyquist 1928; the sampling theorem of step ⑧ is the same idea). The SNR decides how many levels each number can be split into: the less noise, the more levels you can tell apart, and the more bits each number holds. Shannon put the two together and proved that this is an upper limit, and one you can get as close to as you like (Shannon 1948, Shannon 1949).

It can be measured in several ways, such as −3 dB and 99%, so say which one you mean. The shorter the sound, the wider its band; bandwidth and SNR together decide how many bits per second a line can carry.

快的振动，慢的轮廓Fast oscillation, slow outline

回到开场的录音。放大看第 ③ 帧，"啊"每 4.55 毫秒振一次；退远了看整段，它在 0.80 秒后的 60 毫秒里慢慢变响，平稳一阵，最后 120 毫秒慢慢变轻。同一个信号里有两种速度：里面是快的振动，外面是慢慢起落的轮廓。这个轮廓叫包络。我们听到的"忽大忽小"，说的都是包络：第 ① 步里 220 Hz 和 222 Hz 叠出来的拍，是一个每秒胀缩两次的包络；第 ④ 帧里回声慢慢沉下去，也是一个包络。Back to the opening recording. Zoom in on frame ③ and the "ah" oscillates once every 4.55 milliseconds; step back and look at the whole recording, and it slowly gets louder over the 60 milliseconds after 0.80 seconds, holds steady for a while, then slowly fades over its last 120 milliseconds. One signal moves at two speeds: inside, a fast oscillation; outside, a slowly rising and falling outline. This outline is called the envelope. Whenever we hear something "get louder and quieter", what we are hearing is the envelope: the beats that 220 Hz and 222 Hz made together in step ① are an envelope that swells and shrinks twice a second; the echo slowly dying away in frame ④ is an envelope too.

x(t) = a(t) cos(2π fc t + φ(t))a(t) = | x(t) + j ℋ{x}(t) |, fi(t) = fc + (1/2π) dφ/dt

一个窄带的信号，可以写成一个慢慢变化的幅度 a(t) 乘上一个快的余弦；a(t) 就是包络。余弦的角度也可以慢慢晃，让频率时高时低；此刻的频率叫瞬时频率 fi。A narrowband signal can be written as a slowly changing amplitude a(t) times a fast cosine; a(t) is the envelope. The cosine’s angle can also wobble slowly, making the frequency now higher, now lower; the frequency at any given moment is called the instantaneous frequency fi.

第 ① 步说过，余弦是一根转动的箭头的影子。箭头的长度要是慢慢变，影子摆的幅度就跟着变：箭头的长度就是包络。可我们手里只有影子，怎么把箭头找回来？把信号的每个频率都挪 90°，得到另一个方向上的影子，记作 ℋ{x}（希尔伯特变换）；两个影子合起来 x + jℋ{x}，就是那根箭头，它的长度是包络，它转得多快就是瞬时频率。Gabor 1946 年把这根箭头叫作"复信号"（Gabor 1946），后来通称解析信号；用它给包络下一个简洁的定义，是 Dugundji 1958 年的工作（Dugundji 1958）。在频谱里做最简单：把负频率去掉、正频率乘 2，再变回来。Step ① showed that a cosine is the shadow of a turning arrow. If the arrow’s length changes slowly, the swing of its shadow changes with it: the arrow’s length is the envelope. But all we have is the shadow, so how do we get the arrow back? Shift every frequency component of the signal by 90° to get the shadow in the other direction, written ℋ{x} (the Hilbert transform); put the two shadows together, x + jℋ{x}, and you have the arrow. Its length is the envelope, and how fast it turns is the instantaneous frequency. In 1946 Gabor called this arrow the "complex signal" (Gabor 1946); it later became generally known as the analytic signal. Using it to give the envelope a neat definition was the work of Dugundji in 1958 (Dugundji 1958). It is easiest to do in the spectrum: remove the negative frequencies, multiply the positive ones by 2, and transform back.

调幅：让幅度跟着声音变Amplitude modulation: making the amplitude follow the sound

要把这段录音用电台播出去，不能直接把 220 Hz 的电信号送上天线：天线的长度要和波长相当，1 kHz 的电磁波波长 300 km，四分之一波长的天线也要 75 km；而且所有电台都挤在同样的几千赫兹里，收音机分不开。办法是找一个高频的正弦当载波，让声音去改变它：改它的幅度，就是调幅（AM）；改它的频率，就是调频（FM）。每个电台用不同的载波频率，收音机里的调谐就是一个带通滤波器，只放过一个电台。To broadcast this recording by radio, you cannot just feed the 220 Hz electrical signal to an antenna: an antenna has to be comparable in length to the wavelength, and a 1 kHz electromagnetic wave has a wavelength of 300 km, so even a quarter-wave antenna would be 75 km long; and every station would be crammed into the same few kilohertz, where a radio could not tell them apart. The answer is to take a high-frequency sine wave as a carrier and let the sound change it: change its amplitude and that is amplitude modulation (AM); change its frequency and that is frequency modulation (FM). Each station uses a different carrier frequency, and the tuning in a radio is a band-pass filter that lets just one station through.

m 不能超过 1：超过以后 1 + m s(t) 会变成负数，包络在零点处折回来，包络检波取出来的就不再是原来的声音，这叫过调制。调幅还有一个弱点：路上的噪声直接加在幅度上，收音机分不出哪些起伏是声音、哪些是噪声，所以中波广播总带着沙沙声。m must not go above 1: beyond that, 1 + m s(t) becomes negative, the envelope folds back at zero, and what envelope detection recovers is no longer the original sound. 调频：让频率跟着声音变Frequency modulation: making the frequency follow the sound

x(t) = cos(2π fc t + β sin(2π fm t)), β = Δf / fmfi(t) = fc + Δf cos(2π fm t), BFM ≈ 2 (Δf + fm)

幅度不变，瞬时频率在 fc 上下 ±Δf 之间来回摆，每秒摆 fm 次。频谱上出现一串间隔 fm 的边带，大部分功率落在宽 2(Δf + fm) 的一段里。The amplitude stays the same, while the instantaneous frequency swings back and forth by ±Δf around fc, fm times a second. The spectrum shows a row of sidebands spaced fm apart, and most of the power falls within a band 2(Δf + fm) wide.

频率是相位变化的快慢，所以要让频率跟着 cos 摆，相位就得跟着它的积分 sin 摆。相位按 sin(2πfmt) 周期地晃，信号每隔 1/fm 秒就把它的"晃法"重复一遍，第 ④ 步说过，这样的东西只有间隔 fm 的谱线；每根的大小由 β 决定（是贝塞尔函数 Jk(β)）。带宽的估计很直观：频率要扫过 ±Δf，再加上每次摆动只持续约 1/fm（第 ⑥ 步的"短就宽"），两边再各多出 fm，这叫卡森法则。Carson 和 Fry 1937 年算出了它的两头：β 很大时频谱铺满 fc ± Δf，β 很小时只剩 fc 和 fc ± fm（Carson & Fry 1937），2(Δf + fm) 是把两头接起来的经验公式。Frequency is how fast the phase changes, so to make the frequency swing like a cos, the phase has to swing like its integral, a sin. The phase wobbles periodically, following sin(2πfmt), so every 1/fm seconds the signal repeats its "wobble" once more; as step ④ showed, something like that has only spectral lines spaced fm apart, and the size of each line is set by β (it is the Bessel function Jk(β)). The bandwidth estimate is intuitive: the frequency sweeps across ±Δf, and because each swing lasts only about 1/fm (step ⑥’s "short means wide"), another fm is added on each side. This is called Carson’s rule. In 1937 Carson and Fry worked out the two extremes: when β is large the spectrum fills fc ± Δf, and when β is small only fc and fc ± fm are left (Carson & Fry 1937); 2(Δf + fm) is a rule of thumb that joins the two.

唱歌的颤音就是调频：歌剧演员的音高平均每秒上下晃 6 次，上下各偏约 0.7 个半音，也就是约 4%（Prame 1994，1997）。"啊"的 220 Hz 要是这样晃，就是 ±9 Hz；它的第 4 个谐波 880 Hz 跟着晃 ±35 Hz，越高的谐波晃得越多。调频广播：频偏 ±75 kHz（ITU-R BS.450），声音到 15 kHz，卡森法则给出 2 × (75 + 15) = 180 kHz；欧洲在 87.5 到 108 MHz 之间按 100 kHz 排台，美国的每个频道宽 200 kHz。The vibrato in singing is FM: an opera singer’s pitch wobbles up and down 6 times a second on average, by about 0.7 of a semitone each way, or about 4% (Prame 1994, 1997). If the 220 Hz of the "ah" wobbled like that, it would swing ±9 Hz; its 4th harmonic, 880 Hz, would swing ±35 Hz along with it, so the higher the harmonic, the more it wobbles. FM radio: deviation ±75 kHz (ITU-R BS.450), sound up to 15 kHz, and Carson’s rule gives 2 × (75 + 15) = 180 kHz; Europe places its stations 100 kHz apart between 87.5 and 108 MHz, and in the US each channel is 200 kHz wide.

调频用宽得多的带宽，换来了抗噪声。路上的噪声既让幅度起伏，也让相位抖动：收音机先把幅度削平，幅度上的噪声和噼啪声就没了；剩下的只是相位的一点抖动，而声音是用 ±Δf 的大摆动写进去的，频偏越大，这点抖动比起声音就越小，输出的信噪比随带宽的平方提高（Armstrong 1936）。所以宽是调频干净的原因，不只是代价；频偏很小的窄带调频，并不比调幅好多少。早先人们以为调频能比调幅更省带宽，Carson 1922 年就证明了这不可能（Carson 1922）。FM pays for its resistance to noise with a much wider bandwidth. Noise picked up on the way both makes the amplitude rise and fall and makes the phase jitter: the radio first clips the amplitude flat, and the noise and crackle on the amplitude are gone; what is left is only a slight jitter in the phase, while the sound was written in as big swings of ±Δf, so the larger the deviation, the smaller this jitter is compared with the sound, and the output SNR improves with the square of the bandwidth (Armstrong 1936). So the wide band is the reason FM is clean, not just its price; narrowband FM with a very small deviation is not much better than AM. People once thought FM could use less bandwidth than AM; Carson proved as early as 1922 that it cannot (Carson 1922).

第 ⑧ 步 · 采样、混叠和量化Step ⑧ · Sampling, aliasing and quantisation

每秒记多少个数，每个数用几位How many numbers a second, and how many bits each

空气里的声压是连续变化的，手机只能记一串数。它做了两件事：每隔 1/16000 秒量一次，这叫采样；每次量到的值，只能存成有限的几个档之一，这叫量化。两件事都会丢东西。问题是：怎样才能不丢，或者丢得不要紧？The sound pressure in the air changes continuously, but a phone can only record a string of numbers. It does two things: it measures once every 1/16000 of a second, which is called sampling; and each value it measures can only be stored as one of a limited number of levels, which is called quantisation. Both lose something. The question is: how can we lose nothing, or lose only what doesn’t matter?

x[n] = x(n Ts), Ts = 1 / fsfs > 2 B ⇒ x(t) = Σn x[n] sinc((t − n Ts) / Ts)

第 n 个样本，就是 n Ts 时刻的值。如果信号里没有 B 以上的频率，而采样率高于 2B，那么这串样本就完全决定了原来的连续信号：把每个样本换成一个 sinc 形状的小波浪，加起来就恢复了每一时刻的值。The nth sample is the value at time n Ts. If the signal has no frequencies above B and the sample rate is higher than 2B, then the samples completely determine the original continuous signal: replace each sample with a small sinc-shaped ripple, add them all up, and you get back the value at every moment.

一个正弦，每个周期至少要采到两个点以上，才分得清它是哪个频率。频率超过 fs/2 的正弦，样本会和一个更低频率的正弦的样本一模一样，这串数根本分不出它们（下面的混叠）。反过来，只要所有成分都低于 fs/2，每个样本串就只对应一个连续信号。这就是采样定理：E. T. Whittaker 1915 年研究了用一串等间隔的值和 sinc 函数拼出来的"基数函数"，指出它里面的频率有上限；Nyquist 1928 年算出带宽 B 的线路每秒最多能送 2B 个独立的脉冲；Kotelnikov、Shannon 先后从通信的角度把采样定理说清楚（Whittaker 1915，Kotelnikov 1933，Shannon 1949）。fs/2 叫奈奎斯特频率。To tell which frequency a sine wave has, you need more than two samples in each cycle. A sine wave above fs/2 gives exactly the same samples as a sine wave of some lower frequency, and the numbers alone cannot tell them apart (aliasing, below). Conversely, as long as every component is below fs/2, each string of samples belongs to only one continuous signal. This is the sampling theorem: in 1915 E. T. Whittaker studied the "cardinal function" pieced together from a string of evenly spaced values and sinc functions, and pointed out that the frequencies in it have an upper limit; in 1928 Nyquist worked out that a line of bandwidth B can carry at most 2B independent pulses a second; Kotelnikov and Shannon, one after the other, set out the sampling theorem clearly from the point of view of communication (Whittaker 1915, Kotelnikov 1933, Shannon 1949). fs/2 is called the Nyquist frequency.

在样本时刻 n Ts 上，cos(2πf n Ts) 和 cos(2π(f − k fs) n Ts) 只差 2πkn，也就是整数圈，完全相同。所以 f 和 f − k fs 在样本上分不出来，看起来总像落在 0 到 fs/2 之间的那一个。频率轴像一把折尺，在 fs/2、fs、3fs/2……处来回折，这叫混叠。At the sample times n Ts, cos(2πf n Ts) and cos(2π(f − k fs) n Ts) differ only by 2πkn, a whole number of turns, so they are exactly the same. So f and f − k fs cannot be told apart from the samples, and always look like whichever of them falls between 0 and fs/2. The frequency axis is like a folding ruler, folded back and forth at fs/2, fs, 3fs/2, …; this is called aliasing.

误差在 −Δ/2 和 +Δ/2 之间，信号足够复杂时，它在这段里哪儿都差不多，平方的平均就是 Δ²/12（Bennett 1948）。每多一位，档数翻倍，Δ 减半，噪声的幅度减半：信噪比多 6.02 dB。满量程正弦的功率是 1/2，代进去就是那个 1.76。The error lies between −Δ/2 and +Δ/2, and when the signal is complicated enough it is about equally likely to be anywhere in that range, so the mean of its square is Δ²/12 (Bennett 1948). 第 ⑨ 步 · 离散傅里叶变换、窗和语谱图Step ⑨ · Discrete Fourier transform, windows and spectrograms

电脑怎么算频谱How a computer works out a spectrum

第 ⑤ 步的傅里叶变换要对所有时刻积分，电脑手里却只有一段有限长的样本。它算的是离散傅里叶变换（DFT）：取 N 个样本，算出 N 个复数，每个对应一个频率。手机上的频谱仪、语音识别、音乐软件，算的都是它。The Fourier transform of step ⑤ integrates over all time, but a computer only has a finite segment of samples. What it computes is the discrete Fourier transform (DFT): take N samples and compute N complex numbers, each belonging to one frequency. The spectrum analysers on phones, speech recognition and music software all compute it.

X[k] = Σn=0N−1 x[n] e−j2π k n / N, fk = k fs / N

第 k 个值：把 N 个样本分别乘上一根倒着转的箭头（在 N 个样本里正好转 k 圈），加起来。它对应的频率是 k fs/N。The kth value: multiply each of the N samples by an arrow turning backwards (exactly k turns over the N samples), and add them up. Its frequency is k fs/N.

还是"乘上倒着转的箭头再加起来"，只是积分换成了求和、时间换成了样本序号。N 个样本里正好转整数圈的箭头只有 N 种，所以只算 N 个频率，间隔 fs/N。这个间隔只取决于这一段有多长：fs/N = 1/(N Ts)，也就是 1 除以这一段的时长。想把两个很近的频率分开，就得取很长的一段。It is still "multiply by an arrow turning backwards and add up", only the integral has become a sum and time has become the sample number. Over N samples there are only N different arrows that make a whole number of turns, so only N frequencies are computed, spaced fs/N apart. This spacing depends only on how long the segment is: fs/N = 1/(N Ts), which is 1 divided by the segment’s duration. To separate two frequencies that are very close, you need a very long segment.

X[k] 的计算方式是：每个频点要把 N 个样本各乘一次，N 个频点一共 N² 次乘法。快速傅里叶变换（FFT）把它降到大约 N log2N 次：N = 1024 时，从一百万次降到一万次（Cooley & Tukey 1965）。高斯 1805 年左右就用过同样的办法算小行星的轨道，只是生前没有发表，1866 年才印进他的全集（Heideman 等 1984）。FFT 算出来的数和 DFT 一模一样，只是快。Computing X[k] as written means multiplying each of the N samples once for every bin, so N bins take N² multiplications in all. The fast Fourier transform (FFT) cuts this to about N log2N: for N = 1024, from a million down to ten thousand (Cooley & Tukey 1965). Gauss had used the same method around 1805 to work out the orbits of asteroids, but did not publish it in his lifetime; it was only printed in his collected works in 1866 (Heideman et al. 1984). The numbers the FFT gives are exactly the same as the DFT’s; it is just faster.

窗和频谱泄漏Windows and spectral leakage

DFT 默默做了一个假设：这 N 个样本首尾相接，一遍遍重复。如果一个正弦在这一段里正好振了整数个周期，首尾接得上，它就落在一个频点上；可 512 个样本里，220 Hz 振了 7.04 个周期，首尾接不上，接口处的跳变带来一串别的频率，能量从它该在的频点漏到邻近的频点去，这叫频谱泄漏。第 ④ 步说过：平均必须正好取整数个周期。真实的信号里有各种频率，不可能每个都正好。The DFT quietly makes an assumption: that the N samples are joined end to end and repeat over and over. If a sine wave goes through exactly a whole number of cycles in the segment, the ends join up and it lands on a single bin; but in 512 samples, 220 Hz goes through 7.04 cycles, the ends do not join up, and the jump at the join brings in a string of other frequencies: energy leaks from the bin where it belongs into the neighbouring bins. This is called spectral leakage. Step ④ said that an average has to be taken over exactly a whole number of cycles. A real signal contains all kinds of frequencies, and they cannot all fit exactly.

办法是给这一段乘上一个两头为 0、中间为 1 的窗，让首尾平滑地降到 0，接口处就没有跳变了。最常用的是汉宁窗（Hann，形状是一段升起又落下的余弦）。代价是：每根谱线变宽了，相隔很近的两个频率更难分开。不加窗等于用矩形窗，泄漏的第一个旁瓣只比主峰低 13 dB；汉宁窗低 31 dB，主峰却宽了一倍（Harris 1978）。The fix is to multiply the segment by a window that is 0 at both ends and 1 in the middle, so the ends fall smoothly to 0 and there is no jump at the join. The most widely used is the Hann window (shaped like a stretch of cosine that rises and then falls). The price: every spectral line gets wider, and two frequencies close together are harder to separate. Using no window is the same as using a rectangular window, whose first side lobe of leakage is only 13 dB below the main peak; the Hann window’s is 31 dB below, but its main peak is twice as wide (Harris 1978).

语谱图：一段一段地看Spectrograms: one segment at a time

整段录音的频谱只告诉你有哪些频率，不告诉你它们什么时候出现。把录音切成一小段一小段（常常互相重叠），每段加窗、做 DFT，再把结果按时间排起来，横轴是时间、纵轴是频率、颜色是大小，就是语谱图。这个办法叫短时傅里叶变换（STFT）。1946 年贝尔实验室造出了专门画它的机器，叫声谱仪（Koenig, Dunn & Lacy 1946）。The spectrum of the whole recording only tells you which frequencies are present, not when they occur. Cut the recording into short segments (often overlapping), window each one and take its DFT, then line up the results in time order, with time along the horizontal axis, frequency up the vertical axis and size as colour, and you have a spectrogram. The method is called the short-time Fourier transform (STFT). In 1946 Bell Labs built a machine just for drawing them, called the sound spectrograph (Koenig, Dunn & Lacy 1946).

补零（在一段的后面补一串 0 再做 DFT）能让频点变密，曲线画得更圆滑，但分不开本来分不开的两个频率：分辨率只看真正的时长。Zero padding (adding a run of 0s to the end of a segment before the DFT) makes the bins denser and the curve smoother, but it cannot separate two frequencies that could not be separated before: the resolution depends only on the true duration.

这一步的要点：DFT 把 N 个样本变成 N 个频点，间隔 fs/N，也就是 1/时长；FFT 是它的快算法。截一段等于默认它首尾相接，接不上就会泄漏，加窗能压住泄漏、代价是谱线变宽。语谱图是一段段的频谱排起来，段长决定时间和频率谁看得清。The point of this step: The DFT turns N samples into N bins spaced fs/N apart, that is, 1/duration; the FFT is a fast way of computing it. Cutting out a segment quietly assumes its ends join up, and if they don’t, energy leaks; a window holds the leakage down at the cost of wider spectral lines. A spectrogram is the spectra of successive segments lined up side by side, and the segment length decides whether time or frequency is seen clearly.

第 ⑩ 步 · 冲激响应和卷积Step ⑩ · Impulse response and convolution

厨房对声音做了什么What the kitchen does to sound

前几步看的是信号本身。从这一步起看"处理"：厨房、话筒、电话线、滤波器，都是把一个信号变成另一个信号的系统。厨房做的事，第 ② 帧里看得最清楚：一下拍手进去，出来是一长串回声。The steps so far looked at signals themselves. From this step on we look at "processing": a kitchen, a microphone, a phone line and a filter are all systems that turn one signal into another. What the kitchen does is clearest in frame ②: one clap goes in, and a long train of echoes comes out.

大多数这类系统有两个性质。线性：输入大一倍，输出也大一倍；两个声音一起进去，出来的是它们各自输出的和。时不变：今天拍手和明天拍手，厨房的反应一样，只是晚了一天。两条都满足的叫线性时不变系统（LTI）。只要厨房里没人走动、声音也不大到让东西变形，它就是一个 LTI 系统。Most systems of this kind have two properties. Linear: double the input and the output doubles too; put two sounds in together and what comes out is the sum of their separate outputs. Time-invariant: clap today or clap tomorrow, and the kitchen responds in the same way, just a day later. A system with both properties is called a linear time-invariant system (LTI). As long as nobody moves about in the kitchen and no sound is loud enough to make things distort, the kitchen is an LTI system.

LTI 系统有一个了不起的性质：只要知道它对一下极短的冲击（冲激）的反应，就知道它对任何声音的反应。这个反应叫冲激响应，记作 h。拍手只有几毫秒，比厨房半秒的回声短得多，几乎就是一个冲激，所以录下来的拍手，几乎就是厨房的冲激响应。声学里测一个房间，真就是在房间里放一声发令枪、扎破一个气球，或者放一段扫频再换算。LTI systems have a remarkable property: once you know how one responds to a single, very short kick (an impulse), you know how it responds to any sound. This response is called the impulse response, written h. A clap lasts only a few milliseconds, far shorter than the kitchen’s half-second of echoes, so it is very nearly an impulse, and the recorded clap is very nearly the kitchen’s impulse response. In acoustics, measuring a room really does mean firing a starting pistol in it, popping a balloon, or playing a frequency sweep and converting the result.

输出的第 n 个数：输入的每一个样本 x[m]，都放出一份从 m 开始、乘了 x[m] 的冲激响应；把所有这些份在 n 时刻的值加起来。这个运算叫卷积，记作 ∗。Output number n: every input sample x[m] launches a copy of the impulse response that starts at m and is multiplied by x[m]; add up the values of all these copies at time n. This operation is called convolution, written ∗.

任何输入都能看成一串一个挨一个的冲激，第 m 个高 x[m]。时不变，所以每个冲激的反应都是同一个 h，只是挪到 m 开始；线性，所以高 x[m] 的冲激得到 x[m] 倍的 h，而且各份可以直接相加。所以知道了 h，就知道了一切。换个角度算同一件事：要输出的第 n 个数，就把 h 左右翻过来、挪到 n，和 x 对齐逐个相乘再加起来，这是"翻转再滑动"。Any input can be seen as a row of impulses one after another, the one at m being x[m] high. The system is time-invariant, so every impulse gets the same response h, only shifted to start at m; it is linear, so an impulse x[m] high gets x[m] times h, and the copies can simply be added. So once you know h, you know everything. The same sum can be worked out from another angle: for output number n, flip h left to right, slide it to n, line it up with x, multiply sample by sample and add up. This is "flip and slide".

Y(f) = X(f) · H(f)

时域里的卷积，在频域里是相乘：输出的频谱，等于输入的频谱乘上冲激响应的频谱。用分贝说，就是相加。Convolution in the time domain is multiplication in the frequency domain: the spectrum of the output equals the spectrum of the input times the spectrum of the impulse response. In decibels, that means adding.

一个频率为 f 的正弦进入 LTI 系统，每一份 h 都是它挪了一点再加起来，挪动只改相位（第 ⑤ 步），所以出来的还是频率 f 的正弦，只是大小乘了 |H(f)|、相位挪了 ∠H(f)。每个频率各管各的，于是整个频谱就是逐个频率相乘。H(f) 是 h 的傅里叶变换，叫频率响应（第 ⑪ 步）。When a sine at frequency f enters an LTI system, each copy of h amounts to the sine shifted a little, and these are all added up. A shift changes only the phase (step ⑤), so what comes out is still a sine at frequency f; only its size is multiplied by |H(f)| and its phase moved by ∠H(f). Each frequency is handled on its own, so the whole spectrum is multiplied frequency by frequency. H(f) is the Fourier transform of h and is called the frequency response (step ⑪).

反过来，在频域相乘，常常比在时域卷积省事得多：两秒录音和半秒的冲激响应直接卷积，要两亿多次乘加；用 FFT 换到频域，相乘，再换回来，只要几百万次（Stockham 1966）。The other way round, multiplying in the frequency domain is often far less work than convolving in the time domain: convolving the two-second recording directly with a half-second impulse response takes over two hundred million multiply-adds; going to the frequency domain with the FFT, multiplying and coming back takes only a few million (Stockham 1966).

同样的式子，不翻转 h 就是相关：Σ x[n] y[n + ℓ] 量的是 y 和挪了 ℓ 的 x 有多像。拿录音和它自己做相关，挪到 4.55 ms 时第一次又变得几乎一模一样（挪 9.1、13.6 ms……也一样），那正是"啊"的周期，很多测音高的办法就靠它（Rabiner 1977）；拿已知的脉冲去和接收到的信号做相关，能在很大的噪声里找到它在哪，这叫匹配滤波，雷达和超声都靠它（North 1943）。卷积神经网络里的"卷积"，严格说也是相关：核不翻转，反正它是学出来的。The same formula without flipping h is correlation: Σ x[n] y[n + ℓ] measures how much y looks like x shifted by ℓ. Correlate the recording with itself: at a shift of 4.55 ms it first becomes almost identical again (and likewise at 9.1, 13.6 ms and so on), and that is exactly the period of the "ah"; many ways of measuring pitch rely on this (Rabiner 1977). Correlate a known pulse with a received signal and you can find where the pulse is even in heavy noise; this is called matched filtering, and radar and ultrasound both rely on it (North 1943). The "convolution" in a convolutional neural network is, strictly speaking, also correlation: the kernel is not flipped, since it is learned anyway.

能不能把回声去掉？Y = X·H，那就 X = Y / H。可 H 在梳子的坑里很小，一除就把那里的噪声放大很多倍；房间的 h 又长又复杂，还会随着你挪一步而变。所以去混响至今是难题，只对付一次回声倒容易，第 ⑬ 步会看到。Can the echo be removed? Y = X·H, so X = Y / H. But H is very small in the dips of the comb, and dividing by it amplifies the noise there many times over; and a room’s h is long and complicated, and it changes as soon as you take a step. So removing reverberation is still a hard problem today, though dealing with just one echo is easy, as step ⑬ will show.

这一步的要点：线性时不变系统完全由冲激响应 h 决定：输出 = 输入卷积 h，每个输入样本放出一份缩放、挪位的 h，全部加起来。在频域里卷积变成相乘：Y = X·H。拍手几乎是冲激，所以录下来的拍手几乎就是厨房的 h。The point of this step: a linear time-invariant system is completely determined by its impulse response h: output = input convolved with h; each input sample launches a scaled, shifted copy of h, and all the copies are added up. In the frequency domain convolution becomes multiplication: Y = X·H. A clap is nearly an impulse, so the recorded clap is nearly the kitchen’s h.

第 ⑪ 步 · 频率响应和滤波器Step ⑪ · Frequency response and filters

把嗡嗡声去掉Getting rid of the hum

上一步看到：一个正弦进入 LTI 系统，出来还是同频率的正弦，只是大小和相位变了。这件事对别的波形都不成立，方波进去，出来就不是方波了。正弦是 LTI 系统唯一"认得出"的形状，这正是把信号拆成正弦的根本原因：拆开以后，每个频率各走各的，系统对每个频率只做两件事，乘一个倍数、挪一个相位。把这两件事对所有频率列出来，就是频率响应。The last step showed that a sine that goes into an LTI system comes out as a sine of the same frequency, with only its size and phase changed. This holds for no other waveform: put a square wave in and what comes out is no longer a square wave. The sine is the only shape an LTI system "recognises", and that is the real reason for breaking signals into sines: once they are broken up, each frequency goes its own way, and the system does just two things to each one: multiply it by a factor and shift its phase. List those two things for every frequency and you have the frequency response.

滤波器就是故意设计出某种频率响应的系统：低通放过低频、挡住高频；高通反过来；带通只放过一段；陷波只挖掉很窄的一段。放过和挡住之间的分界叫截止频率 fc，按惯例定在功率降到一半的地方，也就是 −3 dB：又是那个"一半的能量"。A filter is a system designed on purpose to have a particular frequency response: a low-pass lets low frequencies through and blocks high ones; a high-pass does the opposite; a band-pass lets through only one band; a notch cuts out only a very narrow band. 相位：滤波器会让声音晚到Phase: filters make sound arrive late

滤波器不只改大小，还改相位，而且常常不同的频率改得不一样。相位挪了 ∠H（负的就是落后），相当于晚到 −∠H/(2πf) 秒；更有用的是群时延：τg = −(1/2π) d∠H/df，一小群挨在一起的频率（比如一个音的包络）整体晚到多久。上面的高通，在 220 Hz 群时延是 1.5 ms，在截止频率 140 Hz 是 4.2 ms：越靠近截止频率，晚得越多。如果所有频率晚到的时间一样（相位随频率成直线），波形只是整体挪后，形状不变，这叫线性相位；对称的 FIR 滤波器（第 ⑬ 步）能做到，巴特沃斯这类滤波器做不到。对一段已经录好的声音，还可以正着滤一遍、倒着再滤一遍，两次的相位正好抵消。A filter changes not only size but also phase, and often by different amounts at different frequencies. A phase shift of ∠H (negative means a lag) amounts to arriving −∠H/(2πf) seconds late. More useful is the group delay, τg = −(1/2π) d∠H/df: how late a small group of neighbouring frequencies (such as a note’s envelope) arrives as a whole. For the high-pass above, the group delay is 1.5 ms at 220 Hz and 4.2 ms at the 140 Hz cutoff: the closer to the cutoff, the later things arrive. If every frequency is delayed by the same time (the phase is a straight line against frequency), the waveform is simply moved later as a whole and keeps its shape; this is called linear phase. Symmetric FIR filters (step ⑬) can do this; filters like the Butterworth cannot. For a sound that is already recorded, you can also filter it once forwards and once backwards, and the two phase shifts cancel exactly.

这一步的要点：LTI 系统对每个频率只做两件事：乘 |H(f)|、挪 ∠H(f)。滤波器的截止频率定在 −3 dB（功率减半）；阶数 m 越高，过了截止频率掉得越陡（每 10 倍频率 20m dB）。去嗡嗡声用高通或陷波，去沙沙声用低通，可人声频带里面的噪声，固定的滤波器去不掉。The point of this step: an LTI system does only two things to each frequency: multiply by |H(f)| and shift by ∠H(f). A filter’s cutoff is set at −3 dB (half the power); the higher the order m, the more steeply it falls past the cutoff (20m dB per factor of 10 in frequency). For hum use a high-pass or a notch, for hiss a low-pass, but noise inside the voice’s band cannot be removed by a fixed filter.

第 ⑫ 步 · 拉普拉斯变换和 s 平面Step ⑫ · The Laplace transform and the s-plane

极点：一个系统怎么响、响多久Poles: how a system rings, and for how long

声音变成数字之前，走的都是连续的世界：你的声道、话筒的振膜、手机里的放大器和抗混叠滤波器。描述它们的是微分方程。最简单的一个：一个电阻 R 和一个电容 C 串起来，在电容两端取输出，就是一个低通滤波器（RC 低通）。电容充电要时间，输入变得太快，电容跟不上，快的起伏就过不去。写成方程是 RC dy/dt + y = x。Before a sound becomes numbers, everything it goes through is continuous: your vocal tract, the microphone’s diaphragm, the amplifier and the anti-aliasing filter in the phone. These are described by differential equations. The simplest one: a resistor R and a capacitor C in series, with the output taken across the capacitor, form a low-pass filter (an RC low-pass). The capacitor needs time to charge; if the input changes too quickly, the capacitor can’t keep up, so fast wiggles don’t get through. As an equation, RC dy/dt + y = x.

傅里叶变换能分析它，可有两件事它做不好：一是会增长的信号（一个不稳定的系统），傅里叶积分根本算不出来；二是开始的那一下（打开开关时电容上已经有电），它不好处理。拉普拉斯变换把探测用的"转动的箭头"换成"一边转、一边伸缩的螺旋"，两件事都解决了，还顺手把微分方程变成了代数方程。The Fourier transform can analyse it, but there are two things it does badly. One is signals that grow (an unstable system): the Fourier integral cannot even be computed. The other is the moment things start (the capacitor is already charged when the switch is turned on), which it handles awkwardly. The Laplace transform replaces the "turning arrow" used as the probe with "a spiral that turns while it stretches or shrinks"; that solves both problems and, along the way, turns differential equations into algebraic ones.

X(s) = ∫0∞ x(t) e−st dt, s = σ + jωdx/dt ↔ s X(s) − x(0)

把信号乘上 e−st，从 0 时刻积分到无穷。s 是一个复数：虚部 ω 管转多快，实部 σ 管每秒衰减或增长多少。求一次导数，在 s 的世界里就是乘一个 s（再减去开始时的值）。Multiply the signal by e−st and integrate from time 0 to infinity. s is a complex number: its imaginary part ω sets how fast it turns, and its real part σ how much it decays or grows each second. Taking a derivative is, in the world of s, just multiplying by s (and subtracting the starting value).

With the extra decay factor e−σt, even a growing signal integrates to a finite number. 数字滤波器的语言The language of digital filters

手机里的降噪不碰电压，它对付的是一串数：每来一个新样本 x[n]，就用它和前面几个样本、前面几个输出，算出新的输出 y[n]。这样的规则叫差分方程。比如四点移动平均 y[n] = (x[n] + x[n−1] + x[n−2] + x[n−3]) / 4，就是把最近 4 个数平均一下。拉普拉斯变换是微分方程的语言，z 变换就是差分方程的语言。The noise reduction in a phone never touches a voltage; it works on a string of numbers. Each time a new sample x[n] arrives, it combines it with a few earlier samples and a few earlier outputs to compute the new output y[n]. A rule like this is called a difference equation. For example, the four-point moving average y[n] = (x[n] + x[n−1] + x[n−2] + x[n−3]) / 4 simply averages the latest 4 numbers. The Laplace transform is the language of differential equations; the z-transform is the language of difference equations.

X(z) = Σn x[n] z−nx[n − 1] ↔ z−1 X(z)

把样本串写成 z 的幂级数：第 n 个样本做 z−n 的系数。晚一个样本，就是乘一个 z−1。Write the string of samples as a power series in z: sample n becomes the coefficient of z−n. One sample later means multiplying by z−1.

z−n 像一个标签，记着这个数排在第几个。整串往后挪一格，每个标签的次数都多 1，相当于整体乘 z−1。于是差分方程里的"前一个样本"变成了乘法，整个方程又变成了代数方程。它和拉普拉斯变换的关系是 z = esTs：晚一个样本就是晚 Ts 秒，而在 s 的世界里晚 Ts 秒是乘 e−sTs。这个办法是二战前后为分析雷达跟踪这类"隔一会儿才测一次"的系统发展出来的，"z 变换"这个名字是 1952 年起的（Hurewicz 1947，Ragazzini & Zadeh 1952）。z−n is like a label recording where this number sits in the line. Shift the whole string back one place and every label’s degree goes up by 1, which is the same as multiplying everything by z−1. So "the previous sample" in a difference equation becomes a multiplication, and the whole equation becomes algebraic again. Its link to the Laplace transform is z = esTs: one sample later is Ts seconds later, and in the world of s, Ts seconds later means multiplying by e−sTs. The method was developed around the Second World War to analyse systems such as radar tracking that "only measure every so often", and the name "z-transform" was coined in 1952 (Hurewicz 1947, Ragazzini & Zadeh 1952).

The magnitude at a given frequency equals the product of the distances from that point on the circle to each zero, divided by the product of its distances to each pole.

频率为 f 的正弦，相邻两个样本之间转了 θ = 2πf/fs，所以"晚一个样本"对它来说就是乘 e−jθ，也就是把 z 放在单位圆上角度 θ 的地方。在 s 平面里，频率在虚轴上，从 0 一直到无穷；在 z 平面里，它被卷到单位圆上，转一圈正好是 fs：f 和 f + fs 落在同一点，这就是第 ⑧ 步的混叠，换成了几何。把 H 写成因式，每个零点、极点各贡献一个"距离"：圆上的点走到零点旁边，距离很小，出现坑；走到极点旁边，分母很小，出现峰。A sine at frequency f turns through θ = 2πf/fs between neighbouring samples, so for it "one sample later" means multiplying by e−jθ, which is to say putting z on the unit circle at angle θ. 录完音，你顺手用手机拍了一张厨房的墙：一面方瓷砖，左下角挂着一块细条纹的抹布，右边一个杯子。头顶的灯接在同一个 50 Hz 的电网上。照片也是一串数，只是排成了一个方阵：256 × 256 个像素，每个像素一个亮度，0 是黑，255 是白（这里先只看灰度）。录音是"时间 → 声压"，照片是"位置 → 亮度"：信号从一个变量变成了两个变量。前面讲的每个概念，在这张照片里都有对应：After recording, you also snap the kitchen wall with your phone: a wall of square tiles, a finely striped towel hanging at the bottom left, and a mug on the right. The ceiling light runs off the same 50 Hz mains. A photo is also a sequence of numbers, only arranged in a square: 256 × 256 pixels, one brightness per pixel, 0 for black and 255 for white (for now we look only at grey levels). A recording is "time → sound pressure"; a photo is "position → brightness": the signal goes from one variable to two. Every concept so far has a counterpart in this photo:

A phone’s sensor does not capture the whole picture at once; it reads it out row by row from top to bottom (a rolling shutter). 二维傅里叶变换：对每一对频率 (u, v)，把照片乘上一片以这个频率、这个方向起伏的"波纹"，全部加起来。The 2D Fourier transform: for each pair of frequencies (u, v), multiply the photo by a sheet of "ripples" rising and falling at that frequency, in that direction, and add it all up.

一维里，探测用的是一根转动的箭头；二维里，ej2π(um + vn) 是一片平行的条纹，条纹垂直于向量 (u, v)，间距是 1/√(u² + v²) 个像素。所以二维的频率是一个向量：长度说条纹多密，方向说条纹朝哪。计算上它可以拆开：先对每一行做一维 FFT，再对每一列做一遍。In one dimension, the probe is a turning arrow; in two, ej2π(um + vn) is a sheet of parallel stripes, perpendicular to the vector (u, v) and 1/√(u² + v²) pixels apart. So a 2D frequency is a vector: its length says how dense the stripes are, and its direction says which way they face. It can be computed in parts: first a 1D FFT along every row, then another along every column.

二维频谱里方向很重要：一条竖直的边，左右突变、上下不变，它的能量落在横轴上。频谱里每一个亮点，都对应照片里某个方向上的一种条纹。Direction matters in a 2D spectrum: a vertical edge changes abruptly from left to right but not at all from top to bottom, so its energy lands on the horizontal axis. Every bright dot in the spectrum corresponds to one kind of stripe in the photo, running in one particular direction.

镜头和传感器把一个点光源拍成一小团，这一小团就是这套系统的冲激响应，叫点扩散函数（PSF）。只要镜头在画面各处的表现一样（平移不变），整张照片就是每个点各自摊开的一小团加起来，和厨房里每个样本放出一份回声完全一样。H(u, v) 的大小叫调制传递函数（MTF），说的是每种密度的条纹还能剩下多少对比度：越密的条纹剩得越少，这就是"清晰度"。The lens and sensor turn a point of light into a small blob; that blob is the system’s impulse response, called the point spread function (PSF). As long as the lens behaves the same everywhere in the frame (shift-invariant), the whole photo is the sum of the small blobs that each point spreads into, exactly like each sample sending out its own copy of the echo in the kitchen. The magnitude of H(u, v) is called the modulation transfer function (MTF): it says how much contrast stripes of each density keep. The denser the stripes, the less they keep, and that is "sharpness".

区别在哪里Where they differ

工具是同一套，可两种信号有几处根本的不同：The tools are the same, but the two kinds of signal differ in a few fundamental ways:

  • 维数和方向。声音只有一个方向（时间往前），频率是一个数；照片有两个方向，频率是一个带方向的向量。旋转照片，频谱跟着转；"竖条纹"和"横条纹"是两个不同的频率。Dimensions and direction. Sound has only one direction (time moving forward), and a frequency is a single number; a photo has two directions, and a frequency is a vector with a direction. Rotate the photo and the spectrum rotates with it; "vertical stripes" and "horizontal stripes" are two different frequencies.
  • 有没有先后。时间有方向：实时处理声音时只能用已经发生的样本，滤波器必须是因果的，输出总比输入晚；拉普拉斯和 z 变换里极点、稳定、相位延迟那一套，很大程度上就是为这件事准备的。照片一下子全都在，左右、上下没有先后，滤波器可以左右对称、完全不带相位延迟，所以图像处理里大多用 FIR，很少需要谈"稳定"。Whether there is a before and after. Time has a direction: when sound is processed in real time, only samples that have already happened can be used, so filters must be causal and the output always lags behind the input; the whole apparatus of poles, stability and phase delay in the Laplace and z-transforms exists, to a large extent, for this. A photo is all there at once, with no before or after from left to right or top to bottom, so filters can be symmetric and have no phase delay at all; that is why image processing mostly uses FIR filters and rarely needs to talk about "stability".
  • 相位的分量。第 ④ 步说过，耳朵听稳定的声音，几乎不管各个谐波的相位。眼睛正相反：把两张照片的幅度和相位互换，看起来像的是提供相位的那一张（Oppenheim & Lim 1981）。照片里最重要的是边缘和它们在哪，而"在哪"写在相位里：一条边要靠许多频率在同一个位置同时对齐才形成。声音里的拍手、辅音这些短促的东西也一样依赖相位，只是持续的元音不在乎。The importance of phase. As step ④ said, for steady sounds the ear hardly cares about the phases of the harmonics. The eye is the opposite: swap the magnitudes and phases of two photos, and each result looks like the one that supplied the phase (Oppenheim & Lim 1981). What matters most in a photo is its edges and where they are, and "where" is written in the phase: an edge forms only when many frequencies line up at the same place at once. Short sounds such as claps and consonants depend on phase in the same way; it is only sustained vowels that do not care.
  • 尺度。声音的频率是绝对的：220 Hz 就是那个音高。照片里同一个杯子，离远一倍，所有空间频率翻倍；自然照片的幅度谱大致按 1/f 下降，每个倍频程里的能量差不多，没有一个特殊的尺度（Field 1987），所以图像处理特别爱用金字塔和小波这种"每个尺度都看一遍"的工具。Scale. Frequency in sound is absolute: 220 Hz is that particular pitch. In a photo, move the same mug twice as far away and all its spatial frequencies double; the magnitude spectrum of natural photos falls roughly as 1/f, with about the same energy in every octave and no special scale (Field 1987), which is why image processing is so fond of tools such as pyramids and wavelets that "look at every scale in turn".
  • 谁来看。耳蜗本身就是一排带通滤波器，把声音按频率分开；视网膜做的是局部的带通滤波（中心和周围相减），视觉皮层再分出方向，对每度视角几个周期的条纹最敏感（Campbell & Robson 1968）。JPEG 把细节压得狠、把粗的保留，正是照着眼睛的敏感度来的（Wallace 1991）。Who is looking. The cochlea is itself a bank of band-pass filters that separates sound by frequency; the retina does local band-pass filtering (centre minus surround), the visual cortex then separates out orientations, and the visual system is most sensitive to stripes of a few cycles per degree of visual angle (Campbell & Robson 1968). JPEG compresses fine detail hard and keeps the coarse structure, precisely following the eye’s sensitivity (Wallace 1991).
  • 噪声和采样的细节。照片的主要噪声来自光子数的随机起伏：越暗的地方，光子越少，信噪比越低，这和一直不变的沙沙声不同。手机拍暗处时连拍好几张、对齐了再平均，和第 ③ 步平均录音是同一件事（Hasinoff 等 2016）。像素也不是一个点，而是一小块面积上的平均，本身就带一点低通；彩色传感器每个像素只采一种颜色，又会带来彩色的摩尔纹（Bayer 1976）。Details of noise and sampling. The main noise in a photo comes from random fluctuations in the number of photons: the darker the area, the fewer the photons and the lower the SNR, unlike the hiss, which never changes. When a phone shoots in the dark, it takes several shots in a burst, aligns them and averages them, which is the same thing as averaging the recordings in step ③ (Hasinoff et al. 2016). Nor is a pixel a point: it is an average over a small area, which is itself slightly low-pass; and a colour sensor samples only one colour at each pixel, which brings coloured moiré as well (Bayer 1976).

本质是什么What it comes down to

把两种信号放在一起看，共同的东西就露出来了：一个信号是定义在某个"域"上的函数。录音的域是时间轴，照片的域是平面。这一篇的工具，几乎全都来自两条假设：系统是线性的；它对域上的平移一视同仁（时间上晚一点、画面上挪一点，反应都一样）。Put the two kinds of signal side by side and what they share comes into view: a signal is a function defined on some "domain". The recording’s domain is the time axis; the photo’s is the plane. Almost all the tools on this page come from two assumptions: the system is linear, and it treats every shift across the domain alike (a little later in time or a little further across the picture, the response is the same).

x(t) → x(t − τ): ej2πft → e−j2πfτ · ej2πft

把一个"转动的箭头"在时间上平移 τ，它的形状不变，只是整体乘上一个复数 e−j2πfτ。Shift a "turning arrow" by τ in time and its shape does not change; it is only multiplied as a whole by one complex number, e−j2πfτ.

别的波形一平移就变了样，只有这种指数函数平移以后还是它自己，只差一个倍数，所以它们是"平移"这个操作的特征函数。一个对平移一视同仁的线性系统（就是第 ⑩ 步的卷积）和平移可以交换次序，于是它也只能把每个这样的波乘一个倍数：这个倍数就是频率响应。频率、卷积、频率响应、"卷积变相乘"，全是从这一条推出来的。二维里把 ft 换成 um + vn，一模一样。Any other waveform changes shape when shifted; only these exponentials are still themselves after a shift, differing only by a factor, so they are the eigenfunctions of the operation "shift". A linear system that treats every shift alike (that is, the convolution of step ⑩) can swap order with a shift, so it too can only multiply each such wave by a factor: that factor is the frequency response. Frequency, convolution, the frequency response and "convolution becomes multiplication" all follow from this one point. In 2D, replace ft with um + vn and it works exactly the same.

一个圆周上的信号（转一圈回到原处，也就是周期信号），特征函数自己也得转一圈正好接上，只剩 k f0 这些频率：这就是第 ④ 步的傅里叶级数。规则的网格上（每秒 16000 个样本、每像素一个），只能平移整数个格子，频率 f 和 f + fs 被乘上同一个倍数，分不出来，于是频率在 fs/2 或每像素 0.5 周期处折回来：这就是混叠。For a signal on a circle (go once round and you are back where you started, which makes it a periodic signal), the eigenfunctions themselves must also join up exactly after one turn, which leaves only the frequencies k f0: this is the Fourier series of step ④. On a regular grid (16000 samples a second, or one per pixel), you can only shift by a whole number of grid steps, which means the frequencies f and f + fs are multiplied by the same factor and cannot be told apart; so frequencies fold back at fs/2 or at 0.5 cycles per pixel: this is aliasing.

所以"频率"不是声音特有的东西，也不是图像特有的，它是"域上能平移"的副产品。换一个域，就换一套"频率"：球面上（全景照片、地球的引力场、环境光照）是球谐函数（Ramamoorthi & Hanrahan 2001）；一张图（社交网络、传感器网络、三维网格）上没有平移，可以用图的拉普拉斯矩阵的特征向量当"频率"，在上面做滤波和卷积（Shuman 等 2013），图卷积网络就是从这里来的（Kipf & Welling 2017）。So "frequency" is not special to sound, nor to images; it is a by-product of "being able to shift across the domain". Change the domain and you change the set of "frequencies": on a sphere (panoramic photos, the Earth’s gravity field, environment lighting) they are spherical harmonics (Ramamoorthi & Hanrahan 2001); a graph (a social network, a sensor network, a 3D mesh) has no shifts, but the eigenvectors of the graph’s Laplacian matrix can serve as "frequencies" for filtering and convolution on it (Shuman et al. 2013), and that is where graph convolutional networks come from (Kipf & Welling 2017).

不同的模态，变的是这几样：域是几维的（声音 1 维，照片 2 维，视频 3 维）、有没有先后（时间有，空间没有）、是不是规则的网格（像素是，激光雷达的点云不是）、每个点上的值是什么（一个数，三种颜色，一个复数）、最后给谁看（耳朵、眼睛、还是一个算法）。不变的是：只要能平移、系统是线性的，就有频率、卷积、采样和混叠。What changes from one modality to another is this: how many dimensions the domain has (1 for sound, 2 for a photo, 3 for video), whether there is a before and after (time has one, space does not), whether it is a regular grid (pixels are, a lidar point cloud is not), what the value at each point is (one number, three colours, a complex number), and who looks at it in the end (an ear, an eye, or an algorithm). What stays the same: as long as you can shift and the system is linear, there are frequency, convolution, sampling and aliasing.

别的模态Other modalities

  • 视频：域是 (x, y, t)，空间和时间的概念都在：每秒 24 或 30 帧是时间上的采样率，车轮倒转就是时间上的混叠（第 ⑧ 步）。Video: the domain is (x, y, t), and the concepts of both space and time apply: 24 or 30 frames per second is the sample rate in time, and wheels that seem to turn backwards are aliasing in time (step ⑧).
  • 核磁共振：机器根本不直接拍图像，它测的就是人体切面的二维（或三维）傅里叶变换，叫"k 空间"，图像是做一次逆 FFT 得来的（第一张核磁共振像用的还是一个个方向的投影：Lauterbur 1973；"k 空间"的看法：Twieg 1983）。k 空间采得太稀，图像上就出现重影，这是混叠；随机地少采一些，再利用图像的稀疏性恢复，可以明显缩短扫描时间（Lustig 等 2007）。MRI: the scanner does not take a picture directly at all; what it measures is the 2D (or 3D) Fourier transform of a slice through the body, called "k-space", and the image comes from one inverse FFT (the first MRI image was still built from projections taken one direction at a time: Lauterbur 1973; the "k-space" view: Twieg 1983). Sample k-space too sparsely and ghosts appear in the image: that is aliasing. Sampling less, at random, and then recovering the image using its sparsity can shorten the scan considerably (Lustig et al. 2007).
  • 深度图和点云：深度相机给出规则网格上的距离，前面的工具照用；激光雷达的点云是不规则的，没有现成的 FFT，要么先放进体素网格，要么用图的办法。Depth maps and point clouds: a depth camera gives distances on a regular grid, so the tools above apply unchanged; a lidar point cloud is irregular and has no ready-made FFT, so you either put it into a voxel grid first or use graph methods.
  • 机器人身上的传感器：关节编码器、力传感器、惯性测量单元，都是一维的时间信号，采样、滤波、延迟的问题和这段录音一样，滤波器带来的相位延迟还会直接影响控制回路能不能稳（第 6 篇）。Sensors on a robot: joint encoders, force sensors and inertial measurement units all produce one-dimensional signals in time, with the same issues of sampling, filtering and delay as this recording; and the phase delay that a filter adds directly affects whether a control loop can stay stable (page 6).
  • 文字：句子是一串词，位置是规则的一维网格，却没有"频率"天然的意义；Transformer 还是给每个位置加上一组不同频率的正弦，好让网络知道"谁在谁前面多远"（Vaswani 等 2017）。Text: a sentence is a string of words, and its positions form a regular one-dimensional grid, yet "frequency" has no natural meaning there; even so, the Transformer adds a set of sines of different frequencies to each position, so that the network can tell "who is how far ahead of whom" (Vaswani et al. 2017).

这一步的要点：图像是两个变量的信号，频率变成了带方向的向量；谐波、混叠（摩尔纹）、卷积（模糊）、滤波、噪声都有一一对应的样子。不同在于：空间没有先后，滤波不必因果；眼睛靠相位看结构，耳朵听稳定的声音几乎不管相位。本质是：线性加上对平移一视同仁，就必然有频率、卷积和采样定理；换一个域，"频率"就换成那个域上的特征函数。The point of this step: an image is a signal of two variables, and frequency becomes a vector with a direction; harmonics, aliasing (moiré), convolution (blur), filtering and noise all have direct counterparts. The differences: space has no before and after, so filters need not be causal; the eye reads structure from phase, while the ear hardly cares about phase in steady sounds. At heart: linearity plus treating every shift alike inevitably brings frequency, convolution and the sampling theorem; change the domain, and "frequency" becomes the eigenfunctions on that domain.

信号处理在今天的神经网络里Signal processing in today’s neural networks

最近几年最直接的一个例子，是用"线性时不变系统"当神经网络的一层。S4 这一类状态空间模型，每一层就是第 ⑫ 步那样的连续系统，用第 ⑬ 步的双线性变换离散化，再按第 ⑩ 步的办法，把整层写成一个很长的卷积，用 FFT 算（Gu 等 2022）：The most direct example of recent years is using a "linear time-invariant system" as a layer of a neural network. In state-space models of the S4 kind, each layer is a continuous system like those of step ⑫, discretised with the bilinear transform of step ⑬; then, as in step ⑩, the whole layer is written as one very long convolution and computed with the FFT (Gu et al. 2022):

ẋ(t) = A x(t) + B u(t), y(t) = C x(t)y = K ∗ u, K = (C B̄, C Ā B̄, C Ā² B̄, …)

一层是一个线性系统：输入 u 推动一个内部状态 x，输出 y 从状态读出来。离散化以后，整层等于输入和一个很长的冲激响应 K 做卷积。A layer is a linear system: the input u drives an internal state x, and the output y is read off the state. Once discretised, the whole layer amounts to convolving the input with a very long impulse response K.

它就是第 ⑫ 步的传递函数，只是写成了矩阵的形式：A 的特征值就是极点，决定这一层"记得多久"、对哪些频率敏感。线性时不变，所以冲激响应 K 完全刻画它，训练时可以一次用 FFT 把整段序列卷完；推理时又能一个样本一个样本地递推，像 IIR 滤波器那样。It is simply the transfer function of step ⑫, written in matrix form: the eigenvalues of A are the poles, which decide how long the layer "remembers" and which frequencies it is sensitive to. Being linear and time-invariant, it is completely described by its impulse response K, so in training the whole sequence can be convolved in one go with the FFT; at inference it can run recursively, one sample at a time, like an IIR filter.

这一页的"啊"其实也可以这样看：三个共振峰是三对极点，声带的脉冲 u 进去，声音 y 出来。S4 只是把极点的数目加到几十上百，让网络自己去学它们放在哪。The "ah" on this page can be seen this way too: its three formants are three pairs of poles; pulses u from the vocal folds go in, and the sound y comes out. S4 simply raises the number of poles to dozens or hundreds and lets the network learn where to put them.

后来的 Mamba 让 B、C 和离散化的步长随输入变化（于是 Ā 也跟着变），能力更强，可也就不再是时不变的系统，不能再写成一个卷积（Gu & Dao 2023）。"时不变"换来的是 FFT 的速度，代价是不能看着输入改变自己。The later Mamba lets B, C and the discretisation step size vary with the input (so Ā varies too). It is more capable, but it is then no longer a time-invariant system and can no longer be written as one convolution (Gu & Dao 2023). What "time-invariant" buys is the speed of the FFT; the price is that the layer cannot change itself in response to the input.

别处也一样：卷积网络的每一层是一组学出来的 FIR 滤波器；它的下采样如果不先低通，就会混叠，网络对平移一像素都不稳（Zhang 2019）；神经辐射场和高斯泼溅放大缩小时的锯齿和闪烁，也是混叠，修法同样是先按采样率低通（Barron 等 2021，Yu 等 2024）；语音识别模型 Whisper 的输入，就是第 ⑨ 步的语谱图，只是频率轴换成了梅尔刻度（Radford 等 2023）。It is the same elsewhere: each layer of a convolutional network is a set of learned FIR filters; if its downsampling is not preceded by a low-pass filter, it aliases, and the network is not stable even under a one-pixel shift (Zhang 2019); the jagged edges and flicker that neural radiance fields and Gaussian splatting show when you zoom in and out are aliasing too, and the fix, again, is to low-pass according to the sample rate first (Barron et al. 2021, Yu et al. 2024); and the input to the speech recognition model Whisper is the spectrogram of step ⑨, only with the frequency axis changed to the mel scale (Radford et al. 2023).

这一步的要点：这些方法沿着几条线长出来：换更合适的基本波形（变换）、每秒要多少个数（采样和信息）、怎样从噪声里挑出信号（估计）、怎样造出想要的频率响应（滤波器），再搬到声音、图像和别的域上。今天的神经网络里，它们以学出来的滤波器、状态空间模型和防混叠的形式又出现了一遍。The point of this step: these methods grew along a few lines: switching to better-suited basic waveforms (transforms), how many numbers per second are needed (sampling and information), how to pick a signal out of noise (estimation), and how to build the frequency response you want (filters), and were then carried over to sound, images and other domains. In today’s neural networks they appear once more, as learned filters, state-space models and anti-aliasing.

其他几个问题A few other questions

  • 为什么偏偏拆成正弦，不拆成方波？因为线性时不变系统对正弦只做两件事：乘一个倍数、挪一个相位，出来还是同频率的正弦（⑪）。拆成正弦以后，每个频率各走各的，卷积变成逐个频率相乘；拆成方波就没有这个好处。Why break signals into sines in particular, and not square waves? Because a linear time-invariant system does only two things to a sine: it multiplies it by a factor and shifts its phase, and what comes out is still a sine of the same frequency (⑪). Once a signal is split into sines, each frequency goes its own way, and convolution becomes multiplication, frequency by frequency; split it into square waves and you get none of this.
  • 傅里叶、拉普拉斯、z 变换到底什么关系？拉普拉斯变换在虚轴上的值就是傅里叶变换；z 变换在单位圆上的值就是样本串的频谱；两者通过 z = esTs 连起来，s 平面的左半边映到单位圆里面（⑫、⑬）。How exactly are the Fourier, Laplace and z-transforms related? The Laplace transform on the imaginary axis is the Fourier transform; the z-transform on the unit circle is the spectrum of the sample sequence; the two are linked by z = esTs, which maps the left half of the s-plane to the inside of the unit circle (⑫, ⑬).
  • 分贝为什么有时乘 10、有时乘 20？乘 10 是对功率之比，乘 20 是对幅度之比；功率正比于幅度的平方，两种算出来是同一个数（②）。Why do decibels sometimes multiply by 10 and sometimes by 20? Multiplying by 10 is for a ratio of powers, by 20 for a ratio of amplitudes; power is proportional to amplitude squared, so both give the same number (②).
  • "一半的能量"为什么是 −3 dB，不是 −6 dB？功率减半是 10 log10(1/2) = −3.01 dB；这时幅度只降到 0.707。幅度减半才是 −6 dB，那时功率只剩四分之一（②）。Why is "half the energy" −3 dB and not −6 dB? Halving the power is 10 log10(1/2) = −3.01 dB; the amplitude then falls only to 0.707. Halving the amplitude is −6 dB, and by then only a quarter of the power is left (②).
  • "带宽"到底指哪个数？要看说的是信号还是系统，以及怎么量：−3 dB、−6 dB、99% 能量……同一个东西，不同的量法可以差好几倍。网络里的"带宽"是比特率：信噪比不变时，它正比于真正的带宽（⑥）。Which number does "bandwidth" actually mean? It depends on whether you mean a signal or a system, and on how you measure it: −3 dB, −6 dB, 99% of the energy and so on. For the same thing, different measures can differ several times over. In networking, "bandwidth" is a bit rate: at a fixed SNR, it is proportional to the real bandwidth (⑥).
  • 图像和声音，到底是不是一回事？工具是同一套：只要信号定义在一个能"平移"的域上、系统是线性的，就有频率、卷积、采样和混叠。不同在域：照片是二维的，频率有方向；空间没有先后，滤波不必因果；眼睛靠相位看边缘，耳朵听稳定的声音几乎不管相位（⑭）。Are images and sound really the same thing? The tools are the same: as long as the signal is defined on a domain where you can "shift" it, and the system is linear, you have frequencies, convolution, sampling and aliasing. The difference is in the domain: a photo is two-dimensional, so its frequencies have directions; space has no before and after, so filtering need not be causal; the eye relies on phase to see edges, while the ear, listening to steady sounds, hardly cares about phase (⑭).
  • 调频为什么比调幅干净？调幅把声音放在幅度里，噪声一加上来，声音就跟着变。调频把声音放在频率里，收音机先把幅度削平，幅度上的噪声和噼啪声就去掉了；剩下的噪声只让相位抖一点，频偏越大，这点抖动比起声音就越小。所以调频是拿带宽换干净：调频广播一个台约 180 kHz，中波调幅只有 9 kHz（⑦）。Why is FM cleaner than AM? AM puts the sound in the amplitude, so as soon as noise is added, the sound changes with it. FM puts the sound in the frequency: the radio first clips the amplitude flat, which removes the noise and crackle on the amplitude; the noise that is left only makes the phase jitter a little, and the larger the deviation, the smaller this jitter is compared with the sound. So FM trades bandwidth for a clean sound: an FM broadcast station takes about 180 kHz, a medium-wave AM station only 9 kHz (⑦).
  • 采样率为什么常是 44.1 kHz 或 48 kHz？人耳听到 20 kHz，按采样定理至少要 40 kHz，再给抗混叠滤波器留一点余地（⑧）。Why is the sample rate so often 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz? The ear hears up to 20 kHz, so by the sampling theorem you need at least 40 kHz, plus a little room for the anti-aliasing filter (⑧).

"啊"每 4.55 ms 重复一次。它的基频是多少？第 5 个谐波在哪？The "ah" repeats every 4.55 ms. What is its fundamental frequency? Where is the 5th harmonic?

f0 = 1/0.00455 s ≈ 220 Hz；第 5 个谐波在 5 × 220 = 1100 Hz（④）。f0 = 1/0.00455 s ≈ 220 Hz; the 5th harmonic is at 5 × 220 = 1100 Hz (④).

人声的有效值是嗡嗡声的 10 倍。相差多少 dB？功率差多少倍？The voice’s RMS value is 10 times the hum’s. How many dB apart are they? By what factor do their powers differ?

20 log1010 = 20 dB；功率差 10² = 100 倍（②）。20 log1010 = 20 dB; the powers differ by a factor of 10² = 100 (②).

两台一样的风扇一起开，比一台响多少 dB？两个一模一样的音同相叠在一起呢？Two identical fans running together: how many dB louder than one? And two identical tones added in phase?

风扇的噪声互不相关，功率相加，翻倍，多 3 dB；同相的两个音幅度相加，翻倍，多 6 dB（②）。The fans’ noises are uncorrelated, so their powers add: twice the power, 3 dB more. For two tones in phase the amplitudes add: twice the amplitude, 6 dB more (②).

同一句话录了 16 次，对齐了取平均，信噪比多多少？The same sentence is recorded 16 times, and the recordings are lined up and averaged. How much does the SNR improve?

人声每次一样，噪声每次不同：信噪比变成 16 倍，多 10 log1016 = 12 dB（③）。The voice is the same every time and the noise is different each time: the SNR becomes 16 times larger, a gain of 10 log1016 = 12 dB (③).

一个 1 kHz 的短音只持续 2 ms（−3 dB 时长），它的 −3 dB 带宽大约多宽？A 1 kHz tone burst lasts only 2 ms (−3 dB duration). Roughly how wide is its −3 dB bandwidth?

高斯形状时 Δt · Δf ≈ 0.44，Δf ≈ 0.44 / 0.002 s = 220 Hz（⑥）。For a Gaussian shape Δt · Δf ≈ 0.44, so Δf ≈ 0.44 / 0.002 s = 220 Hz (⑥).

一个 1000 Hz 的载波，用 100 Hz 的音去调幅，频谱上有哪几根线？改成调频、频偏 ±500 Hz，大约要多宽？A 1000 Hz carrier is amplitude-modulated by a 100 Hz tone. Which lines are in the spectrum? Switched to FM with a deviation of ±500 Hz, roughly how wide does it get?

调幅：1000 Hz 的载波，加上 900 Hz 和 1100 Hz 两根边带。调频：β = 500 / 100 = 5，边带每隔 100 Hz 一根；卡森法则给出 B ≈ 2 × (500 + 100) = 1200 Hz，大约从 400 Hz 到 1600 Hz（⑦）。AM: the 1000 Hz carrier, plus two sidebands at 900 Hz and 1100 Hz. FM: β = 500 / 100 = 5, with a sideband every 100 Hz; Carson’s rule gives B ≈ 2 × (500 + 100) = 1200 Hz, roughly from 400 Hz to 1600 Hz (⑦).

用 8 kHz 采样，一个 5 kHz 的音看起来是多少 Hz？Sampled at 8 kHz, what frequency does a 5 kHz tone appear to have?

fa = |5000 − 8000| = 3000 Hz（⑧）。fa = |5000 − 8000| = 3000 Hz (⑧).

DFT 取 1024 个样本（fs = 16 kHz），频点间隔多少？要把 50 Hz 和 100 Hz 的嗡嗡声分开够不够？A DFT takes 1024 samples (fs = 16 kHz). How far apart are the bins? Is that enough to separate the 50 Hz and 100 Hz hum?

16000 / 1024 = 15.6 Hz，相隔 50 Hz 的两根线隔了 3 个频点，够；加汉宁窗以后谱线变宽，勉强分得开（⑨）。16000 / 1024 = 15.6 Hz, so two lines 50 Hz apart are 3 bins apart: enough. With a Hann window the lines get wider, and they can only just be told apart (⑨).

只有一次回声：直达声加上 2 ms 后 0.5 倍的自己。频率响应的坑在哪里，间隔多少？Just one echo: the direct sound plus itself at 0.5 times, 2 ms later. Where are the dips in the frequency response, and how far apart are they?

H(f) = 1 + 0.5 e−j2πf × 0.002；回声反相的频率是 250、750、1250 Hz……，间隔 1/0.002 = 500 Hz；坑里剩 0.5 倍（−6 dB）（⑩）。H(f) = 1 + 0.5 e−j2πf × 0.002; the echo is in anti-phase at 250, 750, 1250 Hz and so on, 1/0.002 = 500 Hz apart; at the bottom of each dip 0.5 is left (−6 dB) (⑩).

4 阶巴特沃斯高通，截止频率 140 Hz：在 140 Hz 衰减多少？在 14 Hz 呢？A 4th-order Butterworth high-pass with its cutoff at 140 Hz: how much does it attenuate at 140 Hz? And at 14 Hz?

在截止频率处总是 −3 dB；14 Hz 低了 10 倍，4 阶每 10 倍 80 dB，所以约 −80 dB（⑪）。At the cutoff frequency it is always −3 dB; 14 Hz is 10 times lower, and 4th order means 80 dB per factor of 10, so about −80 dB (⑪).

一对极点在 s = −100 ± j2π·440。它敲一下以后以什么频率振？多久衰减到 1/e？稳定吗？A pair of poles sits at s = −100 ± j2π·440. Struck once, at what frequency does it ring? How long until it decays to 1/e? Is it stable?

以 440 Hz 振；时间常数 1/100 s = 10 ms；实部为负，在左半平面，稳定。它的 −3 dB 带宽是 100/π ≈ 32 Hz，Q ≈ 14（⑫）。It rings at 440 Hz; the time constant is 1/100 s = 10 ms; the real part is negative, in the left half-plane, so it is stable. Its −3 dB bandwidth is 100/π ≈ 32 Hz, and Q ≈ 14 (⑫).

z 平面上，一对零点放在单位圆上 θ = ±90° 的地方（fs = 16 kHz）。哪个频率被完全挡掉？In the z-plane, a pair of zeros sits on the unit circle at θ = ±90° (fs = 16 kHz). Which frequency is blocked completely?

θ = 2πf/fs = π/2，f = fs/4 = 4 kHz（⑬）。θ = 2πf/fs = π/2, so f = fs/4 = 4 kHz (⑬).

照片里的瓷砖每 32 像素重复一次。它在二维频谱上留下的点，间隔多少？把照片隔一个像素取一个（降采样 2 倍），新的奈奎斯特频率是多少（按原来的像素算）？The tiles in the photo repeat every 32 pixels. How far apart are the dots they leave in the two-dimensional spectrum? If you keep every other pixel of the photo (downsampling by 2), what is the new Nyquist frequency (counted in the original pixels)?

间隔 1/32 ≈ 0.031 周期/像素，横竖都是；降采样以后每 2 个原像素一个样本，奈奎斯特频率从 0.5 降到 0.25 周期/像素，比它密的条纹就会折回来：抹布的 u = 0.375 折成 0.375 − 0.5 = −0.125，2.6 像素一道的细条纹变成约 7.2 像素一道、斜向也反过来的假条纹（⑭）。1/32 ≈ 0.031 cycles per pixel apart, both horizontally and vertically. After downsampling there is one sample for every 2 original pixels, so the Nyquist frequency drops from 0.5 to 0.25 cycles per pixel, and any stripes finer than that fold back: the towel’s u = 0.375 folds to 0.375 − 0.5 = −0.125, and the fine stripes, one every 2.6 pixels, turn into fake stripes about one every 7.2 pixels, slanting the other way (⑭).
