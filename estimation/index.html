机器人的估计 · 从一个读数到整条路 · 从下往上Robot estimation · From one reading to the whole path · From the bottom up

我在哪，有多确定Where Am I, and How Sure?

上一篇把厨房机器人整个搭了起来。现在它停在岛台旁边，有人叫它去灶台前把那只小杯子拿过来。听起来简单，可它得一直回答三个问题：我在厨房的哪里，朝着哪边？手臂的七个关节各转到了哪，有没有什么东西在推它？那只杯子到底在哪？没有一个传感器会直接说出答案。轮子只知道自己转了几圈；IMU 只知道自己转得多快、加速多大；激光雷达只知道 1081 个方向上离障碍物多远；相机只看得见一个个像素。每个读数都带着误差，有的每秒来 200 次，有的每秒 10 次，有的晚到几十毫秒。把这些不完整、不准、不同步的读数，变成一句"我在这儿，误差大概这么大"，就是估计。这一篇从一个读数的误差讲起：先看误差长什么样，只靠自己推算为什么越走越偏，两个读数怎么合成一个；再从贝叶斯的"预测、修正"讲到卡尔曼滤波、扩展卡尔曼和粒子滤波；然后处理真实机器人上的麻烦：频率和延迟各不相同的传感器、没有地图时的 SLAM、把整条路一起估的因子图、跟踪好几只杯子、估计力和偏差这些不是位置的东西；最后看估计会怎样出错，把所有方法放到一张图上，再给这台机器人搭出它的估计层，算一笔"杯子到底准不准"的账。The previous page put the whole kitchen robot together. Now it is parked beside the island, and someone tells it to go over to the hob and fetch that small cup. It sounds simple, but the whole time it has to keep answering three questions: Where in the kitchen am I, and which way am I facing? What angle is each of the arm’s seven joints at, and is anything pushing on it? And where exactly is that cup? No sensor gives the answer directly. The wheels know only how many times they have turned; the IMU knows only how fast it is turning and how hard it is accelerating; the lidar knows only how far away the obstacles are in 1081 directions; the camera sees nothing but pixels. Every reading carries an error; some come 200 times a second, some 10 times a second, and some arrive tens of milliseconds late. Turning these incomplete, inaccurate, unsynchronised readings into one sentence, "I am here, give or take this much", is estimation. This page starts from the error in a single reading. First we see what errors look like, why a robot that relies only on its own reckoning drifts further off the further it goes, and how two readings become one. Then we go from the Bayesian "predict, correct" to the Kalman filter, the extended Kalman filter and the particle filter. After that come the troubles of a real robot: sensors with different rates and latencies, SLAM when there is no map, factor graphs that estimate the whole path at once, tracking several cups, and estimating things that are not positions, such as forces and biases. Finally we see how estimation goes wrong, put all the methods on one chart, and build this robot its estimation layer, doing the sums on "just how accurately do we know where the cup is".

厨房里的一圈A lap of the kitchen

还是上一篇那台厨房机器人，零件都是真的产品。和"它在哪"有关的是这几样：It is still the kitchen robot from the previous page, and every part is a real product. The ones that matter for "where is it" are these:

  • 轮子：底盘是两个轮子的差速底盘，每个轮子的电机上有编码器，数着轮子转过的角度；单片机每秒约 50 次把它们换算成"走了多远、转了多少"，发出 /odom（频率是估的）。Wheels: the base is a differential-drive base with two wheels, and each wheel’s motor has an encoder that counts the angle the wheel has turned through; about 50 times a second the microcontroller converts these counts into "how far it has gone and how much it has turned" and publishes /odom (the rate is an estimate).
  • IMU：在头部相机 D435i 里，是一颗 Bosch BMI055，陀螺每秒 200 次（可选 400 次），加速度计每秒 63 次（可选 250 次），每个数据包都用相机深度芯片的时钟打上时间戳（RealSense 说明）。IMU: a Bosch BMI055 inside the D435i head camera; the gyro reports 200 times a second (optionally 400) and the accelerometer 63 times a second (optionally 250), and every data packet is time-stamped by the clock of the camera’s depth chip (RealSense guide).
  • 激光雷达：Hokuyo UST-10LX，装在底盘前面，每秒扫 40 圈，每圈 270°、1081 个方向，量程 0.06–10 m，规格书上的精度是 ±40 mm，重复性 σ < 30 mm（规格书）。Lidar: a Hokuyo UST-10LX at the front of the base; it scans 40 times a second, each scan covering 270° in 1081 directions, with a range of 0.06–10 m; the datasheet gives its accuracy as ±40 mm and its repeatability as σ < 30 mm (datasheet).
  • 深度相机：D435i 的深度图 1280 × 720、每秒 30 帧，两只红外镜头相距 50 mm，像人的两只眼睛一样从视差算深度（第 2 篇第 ⑤ 步）。Depth camera: the D435i’s depth images are 1280 × 720 at 30 frames per second; its two infrared lenses sit 50 mm apart and, like a pair of human eyes, it works out depth from the disparity (step ⑤ of page 2).
  • 手臂：Franka Research 3，七个关节里都有编码器和力矩传感器，每 1 毫秒报一次关节角、速度和力矩（规格书）。Arm: a Franka Research 3; each of its seven joints has an encoder and a torque sensor, and it reports joint angles, velocities and torques every 1 ms (datasheet).

厨房是 6 m × 4.5 m 的长方形：中间一个 1.8 m × 0.9 m 的岛台，北墙是一排台面（西头是水槽，东头是灶台），西北角一台冰箱比台面凸出 0.3 m，台面中间一个嵌着烤箱的高柜凸出 0.2 m，南墙也是一排台面，东西墙上各有一扇门。机器人停在岛台西南边的充电座，要绕到岛台北边，停在灶台前，把台面上的杯子 A 拿起来；它旁边 17 cm 还放着一只一样的杯子 B。The kitchen is a 6 m × 4.5 m rectangle. In the middle is a 1.8 m × 0.9 m island. Along the north wall runs a counter, with the sink towards its west end and the hob towards its east end; in the north-west corner a fridge sticks out 0.3 m past the counter, and halfway along the counter a tall cabinet with a built-in oven sticks out 0.2 m. The south wall has a counter too, and the east and west walls each have a door. The robot is parked at the dock south-west of the island. It has to go round to the north side of the island, stop in front of the hob and pick up cup A from the counter; 17 cm from it stands an identical cup, B.

一路上它得一直知道三件事：底盘的位姿：在厨房地图里的 x、y 和朝向 θ，导航每秒 20 次要用；手臂的状态：七个关节角和速度，控制器每 1 毫秒要用，还要知道是不是有人或东西在推它；杯子的位置：在底盘坐标系里准到几毫米，夹爪才夹得住。可每一个传感器都只告诉它一部分，而且各有各的毛病：轮子不知道自己在厨房的哪里，只能把每一步加起来，越加越偏；IMU 只能积分，偏得更快；激光知道离墙多远，可要和地图比对才知道自己在哪，在长得一样的地方会认错；相机看得见杯子，可看到的是几十毫秒以前的画面（比如 80 毫秒，示意）。这一篇要回答八个问题：All the way there, it has to know three things at every moment. The pose of the base: x, y and heading θ in the kitchen map, which navigation needs 20 times a second. The state of the arm: the seven joint angles and velocities, which the controller needs every 1 ms, and also whether someone or something is pushing on it. The position of the cup: in the base frame, to within a few millimetres, or the gripper cannot get hold of it. But each sensor tells it only part of this, and each has its own flaws: the wheels do not know where in the kitchen they are and can only add up every step, getting further off with each one; the IMU can only integrate, and goes off even faster; the lidar knows how far away the walls are, but has to compare with the map to know where it is, and in places that look alike it can mistake one for another; the camera can see the cup, but what it sees is a picture from tens of milliseconds ago (80 ms, for example; illustrative). 读数等于真值，加上一个每次都差不多的偏差，再加上一个每次都不一样的噪声。把 n 个读数平均起来，噪声的标准差变成原来的 1/√n，偏差一点不变。A reading is the true value, plus a bias that is about the same every time, plus noise that is different every time. Average n readings and the standard deviation of the noise shrinks to 1/√n of what it was, while the bias does not change at all.

噪声和偏差来源不同，对付的办法也不同。噪声来自电路、光子这些每次都重新抽一遍的随机因素，正负会互相抵消，所以多读几次取平均就能压下去（n 个独立读数的和，方差是 nσ2，除以 n 以后方差是 σ2/n）。偏差来自装歪了、没标定好、温度变了这些"一直都在"的原因，平均多少次都还在，只能靠标定，或者靠另一个传感器发现它。Noise and bias have different sources, and you deal with them in different ways. Noise comes from random causes, such as the electronics and photons, that are drawn afresh every time; positive and negative errors cancel out, so averaging a few readings beats it down (the sum of n independent readings has variance nσ2; divided by n, the variance is σ2/n). Bias comes from causes that are "always there", such as a crooked mounting, a poor calibration or a change in temperature; it stays however many readings you average, and can only be found by calibration, or by another sensor.

两个数一起偏：协方差Two numbers off together: covariance

底盘的位置有 x、y 两个数，它们的误差常常连在一起：比如斜着往东北开，朝向错了一点，偏出去的方向也是斜的，x 和 y 就会一起错。只给 x、y 各一个 σ 不够，还要说它们一起偏的程度。The base’s position is two numbers, x and y, and their errors are often linked: say it drives diagonally to the north-east with its heading slightly off; then it strays off diagonally too, and x and y go wrong together. One σ each for x and y is not enough; you also have to say how much they are off together.

Σ = [ σx2 , ρ σx σy ; ρ σx σy , σy2 ]

两个数的不确定，用一张 2 × 2 的表来写：对角线是各自的方差，另外两格是它们一起偏的程度，叫协方差。The uncertainty of two numbers is written as a 2 × 2 table: the diagonal holds each one’s own variance, and the other two cells say how much they are off together; this is called the covariance.

只看对角线，等于假设 x 偏和 y 偏毫无关系；可实际的误差常常是"x 偏大时 y 也偏大"。ρ 把这种关系量出来：ρ = 0 时两者无关，ρ 接近 1 时几乎总是一起偏，接近 −1 时总是一个大一个小。把这张表画成图，就是一个椭圆：所有"一样可能"的点连起来的那条线。椭圆的两条轴沿着这个矩阵的两个特征向量；1σ 椭圆的两个半轴，长短是对应特征值的平方根。Looking only at the diagonal amounts to assuming that x being off and y being off have nothing to do with each other; but real errors are often "when x is too large, y is too large as well". ρ puts a number on that relationship: at ρ = 0 the two are unrelated, with ρ near 1 they are almost always off together, and near −1 one is always too large when the other is too small. Draw this table as a picture and you get an ellipse: the line joining all the points that are "equally likely". The ellipse’s two axes lie along the matrix’s two eigenvectors; the 1σ ellipse’s semi-axes are as long as the square roots of the corresponding eigenvalues.

第 ② 步 · 航迹推算Step ② · Dead reckoning

只靠自己算，越走越偏Relying only on itself, it drifts further and further

最省事的办法是不看外面，只算自己。底盘的单片机每隔一小段时间读一次两个轮子的编码器：左轮转了 ΔφL，右轮转了 ΔφR（弧度）。轮子半径 r，两个轮子相距 L（轮距），这一小段里底盘往前走了 Δs = r (ΔφR + ΔφL) / 2，转了 Δθ = r (ΔφR − ΔφL) / L；按当时的朝向把 Δs 分到 x 和 y 上，一段一段加起来，就是轮式里程计。IMU 也能这样算：陀螺的角速度积一次分是朝向，加速度计的加速度（去掉重力）积两次分是位置。这类办法叫航迹推算：从上一刻的位置，加上这一刻的运动，推出现在的位置。它平滑、频率高、从不跳，可每一步的小误差都会被加进去，而且再也减不掉。The easiest approach is to ignore the outside world and just keep track of yourself. Every short interval, the base’s microcontroller reads the encoders of the two wheels: the left wheel has turned ΔφL and the right wheel ΔφR (in radians). With wheel radius r and the two wheels L apart (the wheelbase), in that short interval the base has moved forward Δs = r (ΔφR + ΔφL) / 2 and turned Δθ = r (ΔφR − ΔφL) / L; split Δs into x and y using the heading at the time, add the pieces up one after another, and that is wheel odometry. An IMU can do the same: integrate the gyro’s angular rate once to get the heading, and the accelerometer’s acceleration (with gravity removed) twice to get the position. Methods like these are called dead reckoning: take the position a moment ago, add the motion since then, and you have the position now. It is smooth, high-rate and never jumps, but every step’s small error is added in and can never be taken out again.

误差从哪来，Borenstein 和 Feng 在 1996 年专门量过（Borenstein、Feng，1996）。轮式里程计有两种系统误差占大头：两个轮子直径不一样（橡胶轮胎做不到一模一样，载重一压还会变），以及实际的轮距和标称值不一样（"在一些商用机器人上可达 1% 左右"）。轮径差只在走直线时起作用，会让机器人走出一条弧；轮距误差只在转弯时起作用，每转一圈多算或少算一点角度。他们设计了一个叫 UMBmark 的测试：让机器人沿 4 m × 4 m 的正方形顺时针、逆时针各走 5 遍，从回到起点时的偏差反推这两个误差，修正以后里程计准了至少一个数量级（例子里从 317 mm 降到 21 mm）。另一类是非系统误差：打滑、地面不平、压到东西，它们没法事先标定。In 1996 Borenstein and Feng set out to measure where the errors come from (Borenstein and Feng, 1996). Two systematic errors make up most of it in wheel odometry: the two wheels having different diameters (rubber tyres cannot be made exactly alike, and they also change under load), and the actual wheelbase differing from the nominal value ("up to about 1% on some commercial robots"). The diameter mismatch only acts when driving straight, and makes the robot drive an arc; the wheelbase error only acts when turning, counting a little too much or too little angle on every full turn. They designed a test called UMBmark: the robot drives a 4 m × 4 m square 5 times clockwise and 5 times anticlockwise, and both errors are worked out backwards from how far off it is when it gets back to the start; after correction, the odometry became at least an order of magnitude more accurate (in their example, from 317 mm down to 21 mm). The other kind is non-systematic errors: slip, uneven floors, running over things; these cannot be calibrated in advance.

陀螺有一点零偏，朝向误差随时间成正比地长；加速度计有一点零偏，位置误差随时间的平方长；朝向错了一个小角度，再往前开 d 米，横向就差 d 乘这个角度。A small gyro bias makes the heading error grow in proportion to time; a small accelerometer bias makes the position error grow with the square of time; with the heading off by a small angle, driving on another d metres leaves you off to the side by d times that angle.

航迹推算是积分。速度上一个固定的小误差，积一次分，就变成随时间线性增长的误差；加速度上一个固定的小误差，要积两次分才变成位置，所以长得像 t2。朝向的误差最麻烦：它不只是自己错，还会让后面每一步都往偏的方向走，所以横向误差跟着走过的路一起长。Dead reckoning is integration. A small constant error in a velocity, integrated once, becomes an error that grows linearly with time; a small constant error in an acceleration has to be integrated twice to become position, so it grows like t2. Heading errors are the most troublesome: the heading is not only wrong in itself, it also sends every later step off in the wrong direction, so the lateral error grows along with the distance travelled.

只靠积分的方法，误差没有上限。IMU 单独算位置，几秒钟就不能用了；它在机器人上主要用来算朝向（只积一次分），以及在两次外部修正之间补上短时间的运动。A method that relies only on integration has no upper limit on its error. An IMU working out position on its own is useless within a few seconds; on a robot it is mainly used for heading (integrated only once), and to fill in short stretches of motion between two external corrections.

所以实际的机器人常把两样凑在一起：距离听轮子的，朝向主要听陀螺的。轮距不准和转弯时的打滑都只影响轮子算出来的角度，陀螺量的是机身真正转了多少，不受它们影响；而陀螺的零偏，可以在机器人停着时估出来。Nav2 的教程里，robot_localization 从轮式里程计取前进速度、侧向速度和转动的角速度，从 IMU 只取绕竖直轴的角速度，两个角速度按各自的方差加权合在一起（Nav2 教程；怎么加权见第 ③ 步）。可这样仍然是航迹推算，只是漂得慢一些。要让误差有个上限，必须和外面的东西比一比：激光对地图，相机对认得出的东西。那些读数不会越走越偏，却有噪声、会认错、会晚到。下面几步讲怎么把这两类读数合起来。So real robots often combine the two: trust the wheels for distance, and mostly the gyro for heading. An inaccurate wheelbase and slip while turning only affect the angle the wheels work out; the gyro measures how far the body really turned and is not affected by either; and the gyro’s bias can be estimated while the robot is standing still. In the Nav2 tutorial, robot_localization takes the forward velocity, sideways velocity and turn rate from the wheel odometry, and only the rate of turn about the vertical axis from the IMU, and combines the two turn rates weighted by their variances (Nav2 tutorial; for how the weighting works, see step ③). But this is still dead reckoning; it just drifts more slowly. To give the error an upper limit, you have to compare against something outside: the lidar against the map, the camera against things it can recognise. Those readings do not drift further and further off, but they are noisy, they can mistake one thing for another, and they can arrive late. 第 ③ 步 · 合并Step ③ · Fusing

两个读数，信谁Two readings: which one to trust?

机器人在厨房西头，朝北对着冰箱开过去，想知道自己离冰箱门还有多远。激光正前方那束光说 1.52 m，σ = 30 mm；轮式里程计从上一次知道的位置推过来，说 1.45 m，走了一段路，它的 σ 已经长到 80 mm（示意）。两个说法差了 7 cm，信谁？The robot is at the west end of the kitchen, driving north towards the fridge, and wants to know how far it still is from the fridge door. The lidar beam pointing straight ahead says 1.52 m, σ = 30 mm. The wheel odometry, reckoning forward from the last position it knew, says 1.45 m; after driving some distance, its σ has already grown to 80 mm (illustrative). The two answers are 7 cm apart. Which one do you trust?

都信，按各自有多准来分配。直觉是对的：准的那个多听一点，不准的那个少听一点。问题是"多听多少"。答案出奇地简单：权重和方差成反比。Trust both, and divide the trust according to how accurate each one is. The intuition is right: listen a bit more to the accurate one and a bit less to the inaccurate one. The question is "how much more". The answer is surprisingly simple: the weight is inversely proportional to the variance.

x̂ = ( z1 / σ12 + z2 / σ22 ) / ( 1 / σ12 + 1 / σ22 )1 / σ2 = 1 / σ12 + 1 / σ22

合起来的值，是两个读数的加权平均，每个读数的权重是它方差的倒数；合起来的方差的倒数，等于两个方差的倒数相加。The combined value is a weighted average of the two readings, with each reading weighted by the inverse of its variance; the inverse of the combined variance equals the sum of the inverses of the two variances.

在所有的加权平均里，这样配权重得到的结果方差最小（前提是两个读数的误差互相独立、都没有偏差）。把方差的倒数叫作信息：越准的读数信息越多；独立的信息可以直接相加，所以合起来比任何一个单独的读数都准。两个读数的误差都是高斯分布时，这正好是把两条高斯曲线相乘再归一化的结果，第 ④ 步会看到为什么是"相乘"。Of all weighted averages, weighting this way gives the result with the smallest variance (provided the errors of the two readings are independent of each other and neither has a bias). Call the inverse of the variance information: the more accurate a reading, the more information it carries; independent information simply adds up, so the combination is more accurate than any single reading. When the errors of both readings are Gaussian, this is exactly what you get by multiplying the two Gaussian curves together and normalising; step ④ will show why we "multiply".

两个前提一个也不能少。误差要独立：同一份信息算两遍（比如把同一个激光读数当成两个读数），公式会说 σ 缩小了 √2 倍，其实一点新信息都没有，这叫太自信，第 ⑭ 步会看到它的后果。读数要没有偏差：两个读数之差超过好几个 √(σ12 + σ22)，说明至少一个坏了，这时取平均只会得到一个两边都不对的数。Neither assumption can be dropped. The errors must be independent: count the same information twice (for example, by treating one lidar reading as two readings) and the formula says σ has shrunk by a factor of √2, when in fact there is no new information at all. This is called being over-confident, and step ⑭ will show what it leads to. The readings must have no bias: if two readings differ by more than several times √(σ12 + σ22), at least one of them is broken, and averaging them then only gives a number that is wrong either way.

这个加权平均是整页的地基。后面每一个滤波器，"修正"那一步做的都是同一件事：把"我原来以为的"和"刚读到的"合起来，越准的听得越多；两者都是高斯时，正好就是这个按信息量的加权平均（一般的情况是乘上似然，第 ④ 步）。区别只在于原来以为的是什么形状（一个高斯、一堆样本，还是一整条路）。This weighted average is the foundation of the whole page. The only difference is the shape of what it believed before (a single Gaussian, a cloud of samples, or a whole path).

第 ④ 步 · 贝叶斯Step ④ · Bayes

先验乘似然：预测，再修正Prior times likelihood: predict, then correct

第 ③ 步默认机器人大概知道自己在哪，只是不太准。可要是它完全不知道呢？比如刚开机，或者被人推着挪了个地方。先把问题缩成一维：机器人沿北边的通道往东开（坐标从厨房西南角量起，x 朝东、y 朝北；通道在 y = 3.3 m），激光朝左（朝北）的那一束，量到正对着的家具表面的距离。这一路上，它会先后对着冰箱（0.3 m）、台面（0.6 m）、烤箱柜（0.4 m）、又一段台面（0.6 m）。它读到"0.6 m"：可能在第一段台面前，也可能在第二段台面前，两段一样可能。一个数装不下"可能在这儿，也可能在那儿"，得记下一整个分布：在每个位置的可能性各是多少。这个分布叫置信。Step ③ assumed the robot roughly knows where it is, just not very accurately. But what if it has no idea at all? Say it has just been switched on, or someone has pushed it somewhere else. First shrink the problem to one dimension: the robot drives east along the north corridor (coordinates are measured from the kitchen’s south-west corner, with x pointing east and y north; the corridor is at y = 3.3 m), and the lidar beam pointing left (north) measures the distance to the furniture surface directly opposite. Along the way it faces, in turn, the fridge (0.3 m), a counter (0.6 m), the oven cabinet (0.4 m) and another stretch of counter (0.6 m). It reads "0.6 m": it could be in front of the first counter or in front of the second, both equally likely. A single number cannot hold "maybe here, maybe there"; it has to keep a whole distribution: how likely it is to be at each position. This distribution is called the belief.

把通道切成 5 cm 一格，从 0.3 m 到 5.7 m 一共 108 格，每格记一个概率。机器人每做一件事，就更新一次这 108 个数：Cut the corridor into 5 cm bins: from 0.3 m to 5.7 m that makes 108 bins, each holding one probability. Every time the robot does something, it updates these 108 numbers once:

p−(x′) = Σx p(x′ | x, u) · p(x)p(x) ← η · p(z | x) · p−(x)

预测：走了一步 u 以后，在 x′ 的可能性，等于从每一个旧位置 x 走到 x′ 的可能性，按旧的置信加权加起来。修正：读到 z 以后，把每个位置的可能性乘上"在这个位置会读到 z 的可能性"，再整体放大或缩小，让总和回到 1。Predict: after a move u, the probability of being at x′ is the probability of getting to x′ from each old position x, weighted by the old belief and added up. Correct: after reading z, multiply the probability of each position by "the probability of reading z at this position", then scale the whole thing up or down so that the total is back to 1.

这就是贝叶斯公式用在一个随时间变化的量上。修正那一行是"后验 ∝ 似然 × 先验"：先验是走完这一步以后我以为在哪，似然是这个读数在各个位置合不合理。预测那一行把整个分布往前挪 u，同时因为每一步都有误差而变模糊。能这样一步步算，靠的是马尔可夫假设：现在的置信已经包含了过去所有有用的信息，新的读数只和现在的位置有关，和怎么来的无关。第 ③ 步的加权平均，就是这个乘法在两条高斯曲线上的结果。This is Bayes’ rule applied to a quantity that changes over time. The correction line is "posterior ∝ likelihood × prior": the prior is where I think I am after this move, and the likelihood is how plausible this reading is at each position. The prediction line shifts the whole distribution forward by u and, because every move has an error, blurs it at the same time. What lets us compute it step by step like this is the Markov assumption: the current belief already contains all the useful information from the past, and a new reading depends only on where the robot is now, not on how it got there. The weighted average of step ③ is exactly what this multiplication gives for two Gaussian curves.

Then it reads 0.6 m (σ = 3 cm). For the bins in front of the fridge, the likelihood is exp(−0.32 / (2 × 0.032)) = e−50; in front of the oven cabinet it is e−22; both are practically 0. The 84 bins in front of the two counters all have the same likelihood, so after normalising each holds 1/84. The belief has become two plateaus of equal height. Now all it can do is move on and read again. With every move east, both plateaus get narrower: the part of the western one that shifts in front of the oven cabinet does not fit the 0.6 m reading and is cut off; the eastern one is pushed up against the end of the corridor, and the part that would leave it is impossible and is dropped too. Only when the robot really reaches the oven cabinet and reads 0.4 m does the eastern plateau collapse completely. Conversely, if it is actually in front of the eastern counter, it reads nothing but 0.6 m all the way east, and the two plateaus narrow together and stay equally high: with this one left-pointing beam alone, it cannot tell which counter it is at.

这一步的要点：估计的对象不是一个数，而是一个分布（置信）。每一步做两件事：预测，按运动把置信往前挪、变模糊；修正，乘上读数的似然再归一化。置信可以有好几个峰，表示"可能在这儿，也可能在那儿"。后面所有的滤波器都是这个循环的近似，区别只在用什么来记这个分布。The point of this step: What we estimate is not a number but a distribution (the belief). Each step does two things: predict, which shifts the belief forward by the motion and blurs it; and correct, which multiplies it by the likelihood of the reading and normalises. The belief can have several peaks, meaning "maybe here, maybe there". Every filter that follows is an approximation of this predict-and-correct loop; they differ only in what they use to hold the distribution.

第 ⑤ 步 · 卡尔曼滤波Step ⑤ · Kalman filter

只记平均值和方差：卡尔曼滤波Keep only the mean and variance: the Kalman filter

第 ④ 步的格子有个特殊情况，好算得多：如果置信是高斯分布，运动是线性的、误差是高斯的，读数也是线性的、误差也是高斯的，那么预测以后它还是高斯，修正以后也还是高斯。高斯只要两个数就能写全：平均值和方差。于是那 108 格缩成两个数，每一步只更新这两个数（第 ④ 步那 389 万格的 (x, y, θ)，也只要 9 个数：3 个平均值，加上 3 × 3 协方差里的 6 个）。这就是卡尔曼滤波。The grid of step ④ has a special case that is much easier to compute: if the belief is a Gaussian distribution, the motion is linear with Gaussian errors, and the reading is linear with Gaussian errors too, then the belief is still a Gaussian after the prediction, and still a Gaussian after the correction. A Gaussian is fully described by just two numbers: its mean and its variance. So those 108 bins shrink to two numbers, and each step updates only these two (the (x, y, θ) that needed 3.89 million cells in step ④ also needs only 9 numbers: 3 means, plus 6 from the 3 × 3 covariance). This is the Kalman filter.

它的来历和登月有关。Rudolf Kálmán 在 1960 年 3 月发表了这个方法（Kalman，1960）。当时 NASA Ames 研究中心动力学分析部门的负责人 Stanley Schmidt 正在为阿波罗飞船往返月球的轨道估计找办法，他认为这个理论就是答案；导航方程是非线性的，他们把这个方法用到了非线性的方程上，后来装进阿波罗 11 号的这个版本叫"扩展"卡尔曼滤波（Aerospace America 的回顾；McGee、Schmidt，1985）。第 6 篇第 ③ 步也用过它：从带噪声的编码器读数里估出关节的角速度。Its history is tied up with the Moon landings. Rudolf Kálmán published the method in March 1960 (Kalman, 1960). At the time, Stanley Schmidt, head of the dynamics analysis branch at NASA Ames Research Center, was looking for a way to estimate the trajectory of the Apollo spacecraft to the Moon and back, and he saw this theory as the answer. The navigation equations were nonlinear, so they applied the method to nonlinear equations; the version that later went into Apollo 11 is called the "extended" Kalman filter (Aerospace America’s retrospective; McGee and Schmidt, 1985). Step ③ of page 6 used it too, to estimate a joint’s angular velocity from noisy encoder readings.

回到厨房。底盘以 0.3 m/s 沿直线往前开（为了简单，这一步只看沿着前进方向的那一个数）。每 0.1 s，轮子报告"又走了 0.03 m"，这一步的误差 σ = 3 mm；激光扫描和地图对一对，换算成沿前进方向的位置，误差 σ = 30 mm。Back to the kitchen. The base drives straight ahead at 0.3 m/s (to keep it simple, this step looks only at the one number along the direction of travel). Every 0.1 s the wheels report "another 0.03 m", with an error of σ = 3 mm for that step; matching the lidar scan against the map and converting it into a position along the direction of travel gives an error of σ = 30 mm.

预测：估计往前挪 u，方差加上 Q。修正：算出一个 0 到 1 之间的增益 K，把"读数减预测"的 K 倍加到预测上，方差缩成原来的 1 − K 倍。每 0.1 s 做一遍。Predict: move the estimate forward by u and add Q to the variance. Correct: work out a gain K between 0 and 1, add K times "reading minus prediction" to the prediction, and shrink the variance to 1 − K times what it was. Do this every 0.1 s.

修正这一半就是第 ③ 步的加权平均：预测是一个"读数"，方差 P−；激光是另一个，方差 R。测量的权重是 R 的倒数除以总信息，化简出来正好是 K = P− / (P− + R)；而 (1 − K) P− 就是 1/P = 1/P− + 1/R 的另一种写法。预测这一半是第 ④ 步"往前挪、变模糊"在高斯上的样子：挪 u，方差加 Q（两个独立误差相加，方差相加）。The correction half is just the weighted average of step ③: the prediction is one "reading", with variance P−; the lidar is the other, with variance R. The measurement’s weight is the inverse of R divided by the total information, which simplifies to exactly K = P− / (P− + R); and (1 − K) P− is just another way of writing 1/P = 1/P− + 1/R. The prediction half is what step ④’s "shift forward and blur" looks like for a Gaussian: shift by u and add Q to the variance (when two independent errors add, their variances add).

Q 和 R 是两个"信任"旋钮，稳态的增益只取决于它们的比值。Q 设得太小，滤波器太信轮子，打滑时要很久才跟上；R 设得太小，太信激光，估计跟着激光的噪声一起抖。它们应该是真实误差的大小，而不是随手调出来的。Q and R are two "trust" knobs, and the steady-state gain depends only on their ratio. Set Q too small and the filter trusts the wheels too much, so after a slip it takes a long time to catch up; set R too small and it trusts the lidar too much, so the estimate jitters along with the lidar’s noise. They should be the size of the real errors, not values tuned on a whim.

卡尔曼滤波在"运动和读数都是线性的、误差都是高斯的"这个前提下是最优的：在所有估计方法里，它的误差方差最小。就算误差不是高斯的，它也是所有线性估计方法里最好的。它还非常便宜：每一步只有几次乘法，单片机上每秒跑几千次都不费力。它的局限都写在前提里：模型要是线性的（下面第 ⑦ 步处理"不线性"），置信要是一个峰（第 ⑧ 步处理"好几个峰"），Q 和 R 要是对的（第 ⑭ 步看"不对"会怎样）。Under the assumption that "motion and readings are both linear and the errors are all Gaussian", the Kalman filter is optimal: of all estimation methods, it has the smallest error variance. Even when the errors are not Gaussian, it is still the best of all linear estimation methods. It is also very cheap: each step takes only a few multiplications, and a microcontroller can run it a few thousand times a second without effort. Its limits are all in the assumptions: the model has to be linear (step ⑦ below deals with "not linear"), the belief has to have a single peak (step ⑧ deals with "several peaks"), and Q and R have to be right (step ⑭ looks at what happens when they are "wrong").

这一步的要点：运动和读数都是线性的、误差都是高斯的时候，贝叶斯滤波只需要记平均值和方差：这就是卡尔曼滤波。预测时估计往前挪、方差加 Q；修正时按增益 K = P⁻/(P⁻ + R) 把新息加进来，方差缩小。K 由"预测有多不确定"和"读数有多不确定"之比决定；Q 和 R 应该是真实误差的大小。The point of this step: When motion and readings are both linear and all the errors are Gaussian, the Bayes filter only needs to keep the mean and the variance: that is the Kalman filter. To predict, move the estimate forward and add Q to the variance; to correct, add in the innovation with gain K = P⁻/(P⁻ + R), and the variance shrinks. K is set by the ratio of "how uncertain the prediction is" to "how uncertain the reading is"; Q and R should be the size of the real errors.

第 ⑥ 步 · 向量Step ⑥ · Vectors

只量位置，速度也能估出来Measure only position, and velocity can be estimated too

导航的控制器不只要知道底盘在哪，还要知道它开多快。可激光只量位置，不量速度。办法是把位置和速度放进同一个状态向量 [位置, 速度]，让滤波器同时估这两个数。不确定也从一个方差变成一个 2 × 2 的协方差矩阵 P，画出来是一个椭圆（第 ① 步）。The navigation controller needs to know not only where the base is but also how fast it is moving. But the lidar measures only position, not velocity. The answer is to put position and velocity into one state vector [position, velocity] and let the filter estimate both numbers at once. The uncertainty, too, changes from a single variance into a 2 × 2 covariance matrix P, which you can draw as an ellipse (step ①).

这样做的妙处在两半循环里看得最清楚。预测：位置要加上"速度 × 0.1 s"，所以速度上的不确定会漏进位置里，椭圆被斜着拉长：位置和速度开始一起偏，出现了相关。修正：激光只把椭圆在"位置"方向上压扁；可因为椭圆是斜的，压扁位置的同时，速度方向也被带着变窄了。一遍遍量位置，速度就被估了出来。The clever part shows most clearly in the two halves of the loop. Predict: position has "velocity × 0.1 s" added to it, so the uncertainty in velocity leaks into position and the ellipse is stretched diagonally: position and velocity start to go wrong together, and a correlation appears. Correct: the lidar squashes the ellipse only along the "position" direction; but because the ellipse is tilted, squashing position also narrows it along velocity. Measure position again and again, and velocity gets estimated.

F P FT 是协方差经过线性变换以后的样子：椭圆上的每个点都被 F 搬了一下。K = P− HT S−1 仍然是"预测的不确定"除以"总的不确定"，只是写成了矩阵。关键在 P− 的非对角元：位置和速度的协方差不为零，P− HT 的第二行就不为零，一个位置的读数就会顺带修正速度。F P FT is what the covariance looks like after a linear transformation: every point on the ellipse has been moved by F. K = P− HT S−1 is still "the predicted uncertainty" divided by "the total uncertainty", only written as matrices. The key is the off-diagonal entries of P−: if the covariance between position and velocity is not zero, the second row of P− HT is not zero, and a position reading corrects velocity along the way.

一个量要能通过模型在读数里留下自己的痕迹，而且这个痕迹别的量凑不出来，才估得出来，这叫可观。反过来，没有任何读数能区分的量，读数压不小它在 P 里的方差；只要它有过程噪声，方差就会一直长下去（第 ⑬、⑭ 步）。A quantity can be estimated only if, through the model, it leaves its own mark in the readings, a mark that no other quantity can fake; such a quantity is called observable. Conversely, if no reading can tell a quantity apart, the readings cannot shrink its variance in P; as long as it has process noise, that variance just keeps growing (steps ⑬ and ⑭).

That biases can be put into the state and estimated along with it is also thanks to "observability": as long as there are other readings to check against, a bias shows up in the innovation.

第 ⑦ 步 · 非线性Step ⑦ · Nonlinearity

拐弯以后：扩展卡尔曼和香蕉Once it turns: the extended Kalman filter and the banana

前两步的底盘只沿一条直线走。真正的底盘有朝向 θ，往前走的那一步要按朝向分到 x 和 y 上：x 加上 vΔt·cos θ，y 加上 vΔt·sin θ。cos 和 sin 不是线性的。高斯分布经过线性变换还是高斯，经过非线性变换就不是了，卡尔曼滤波的前提塌了一半。In the last two steps the base moved only along a straight line. A real base has a heading θ, and each step forward has to be split between x and y according to the heading: x gains vΔt·cos θ and y gains vΔt·sin θ. Neither cos nor sin is linear. A Gaussian distribution stays Gaussian through a linear transformation but not through a nonlinear one, and half of the Kalman filter’s premise collapses.

阿波罗的工程师用的办法沿用至今：在当前的估计附近，把非线性函数当成一条直线（一次泰勒展开），直线的斜率就是雅可比矩阵（第 11 篇的 J）。平均值照样用原来的非线性函数往前推、算预测的读数；只在协方差和增益里，用雅可比代替第 ⑥ 步的 F 和 H。这就是扩展卡尔曼滤波（EKF）。The method the Apollo engineers used is still in use today: near the current estimate, treat the nonlinear function as a straight line (a first-order Taylor expansion); the slope of that line is the Jacobian (the J of page 11). The mean is still pushed forward, and the predicted reading computed, with the original nonlinear function; only in the covariance and the gain does the Jacobian take the place of the F and H of step ⑥. This is the extended Kalman filter (EKF).

x′ = x + v Δt cos θy′ = y + v Δt sin θθ′ = θ + ω Δt∂x′ / ∂θ = − v Δt sin θ0∂y′ / ∂θ = v Δt cos θ0

底盘沿着现在的朝向往前走 vΔt，再转 ωΔt。EKF 在当前估计的朝向 θ0 处，把这个关系拉成一个线性的近似；它的斜率组成雅可比矩阵 F，其中最要紧的是这两格：朝向变一点点，x′ 和 y′ 各变多少（F 的其余部分是单位矩阵）。The base moves forward vΔt along its current heading, then turns by ωΔt. At the currently estimated heading θ0, the EKF straightens this relation into a linear approximation; its slopes make up the Jacobian F, and the two entries that matter most are these: how much x′ and y′ each change when the heading changes a tiny bit (the rest of F is the identity matrix).

只有线性变换能把高斯变成高斯。EKF 赌的是：在估计附近那一小片里，函数几乎是直的。这两格说的就是朝向的误差怎样漏进位置：朝东走（θ0 = 0）时，朝向差一点，y′ 就差 vΔt 乘这一点，x′ 几乎不变。不确定小的时候这个赌很安全；不确定大了，"那一小片"就不再小，函数的弯曲开始起作用。Only a linear transformation turns a Gaussian into a Gaussian. The EKF’s bet is that in the small patch around the estimate the function is almost straight. These two entries say how heading error leaks into position: driving east (θ0 = 0), a small heading error puts y′ off by vΔt times that error, while x′ hardly changes. When the uncertainty is small this bet is safe; when it is large, "the small patch" is no longer small, and the function’s curvature starts to matter.

EKF 的线性化出问题，多半是在不确定大的时候：开机还不知道朝向、很久没有修正、被人挪了地方。这时要么换能表示弯曲分布的方法，要么先用别的办法把不确定压小，再交给 EKF。When the EKF’s linearisation goes wrong, it is mostly when the uncertainty is large: at start-up before the heading is known, after a long time without a correction, or after someone has moved the robot. Then either switch to a method that can represent a curved distribution, or first shrink the uncertainty some other way and then hand over to the EKF.

换法之一是无迹卡尔曼滤波（UKF，Julier、Uhlmann 等人 1995 年最早发表，Julier、Uhlmann，2004 是后来的综述）：不求导，而是挑几个有代表性的点（n 维时 2n + 1 个，叫 sigma 点），让它们穿过真正的非线性函数，再用穿过去的点拟合一个新的高斯。它对弯曲看得准一些，也不用写雅可比；robot_localization 同时提供 ekf_node 和 ukf_node，Stanley 用的就是 UKF。另一个麻烦是三维的姿态：旋转不住在平坦的空间里（第 1 篇的李群），直接把四元数当成向量做加法会出错。现在的做法是误差状态滤波：滤波器估的是"真实姿态和名义姿态之间的一个小转动"，它住在切空间里，是平的，修正完再贴回旋转上（Solà，2017）。还有一种是迭代 EKF：修正时把线性化反复做几遍，每次都在新的估计处重新求雅可比，激光惯性里程计 FAST-LIO2 用的就是它。One alternative is the unscented Kalman filter (UKF; first published by Julier, Uhlmann and colleagues in 1995, with Julier and Uhlmann, 2004 as the later review): instead of taking derivatives, it picks a few representative points (2n + 1 of them in n dimensions, called sigma points), passes them through the true nonlinear function, and fits a new Gaussian to the points that come out. It sees curvature somewhat more accurately, and there are no Jacobians to write; robot_localization offers both ekf_node and ukf_node, and a UKF is what Stanley used. Another problem is 3D attitude: rotations do not live in a flat space (the Lie groups of page 1), and adding quaternions as if they were vectors goes wrong. Today’s approach is error-state filtering: the filter estimates "a small rotation between the true attitude and the nominal attitude", which lives in the tangent space and is flat, and after the correction it is put back onto the rotation (Solà, 2017). Yet another is the iterated EKF: during the correction it repeats the linearisation several times, recomputing the Jacobian at the new estimate each time; the lidar–inertial odometry FAST-LIO2 is built on it.

The particle filter takes a different approach: instead of storing the whole distribution, it scatters a large handful of "guesses". Modern particle filtering is usually dated from the 1993 paper by Gordon et al. (Gordon et al., 1993). In 1999 it was applied to robot localisation as Monte Carlo localisation (MCL); the paper says it can localise without knowing the starting point, and is faster, more accurate and uses less memory than the grid-based methods of the time (Dellaert et al., 1999).

每一轮还是预测和修正，外加一步重采样：Each round is still predict and correct, plus one extra step, resampling:

  1. 预测：按轮式里程计报告的运动，把每个粒子往前挪，并给每个粒子加上不一样的随机误差（运动模型），于是粒子云会慢慢散开。Predict: move every particle forward by the motion that the wheel odometry reports, and give each particle a different random error (the motion model), so the particle cloud slowly spreads out.

频率不同、延迟不同、坐标系不同Different rates, different latencies, different frames

到这里，每一步的滤波器都假设读数整整齐齐地按拍子到来。真实的机器人上，读数乱糟糟地涌进来：陀螺每秒 200 个，轮式里程计每秒约 50 个，AMCL 每走 0.25 m 才给一次修正（0.3 m/s 时大约每 0.8 s 一次），检测程序每秒约 10 次报告杯子的位置（估），而且每张图都是 80 毫秒以前拍的（示意）。把它们放进同一个滤波器，要守三条规矩。Up to now, the filter in every step has assumed that readings arrive neatly, on the beat. On a real robot they come pouring in, all jumbled: the gyro sends 200 a second, the wheel odometry about 50 a second, AMCL gives a correction only once every 0.25 m travelled (about once every 0.8 s at 0.3 m/s), and the detector reports the cup’s position about 10 times a second (est.), each time from an image taken 80 ms earlier (illustrative). Putting them all into one filter means keeping three rules.

第一条：按时间戳，不按到达的时刻。ROS 的每条传感器消息头上都有一个时间戳（header.stamp），记的是读数产生的时刻，不是它到达的时刻。滤波器收到一个读数，先把状态预测到这个时间戳，再修正。时间戳打得越靠近硬件越好：D435i 的 IMU 数据包，就是用相机深度芯片的时钟打的时间戳，所以 IMU 和深度图能对齐（RealSense 说明）。跨电脑时，几台电脑的钟还得对齐（局域网里常用 chrony 或 PTP），否则一台电脑的"10:00:00.000"在另一台上是 10:00:00.030，第 5 篇第 ⑩ 步讲过，两台电脑的钟差 50 ppm，十分钟就差 30 毫秒；那一篇总结里的检验题算过，30 毫秒的钟差让一辆 15 m/s 的车错开 0.45 m。Rule one: go by the time stamp, not by the time of arrival. Every ROS sensor message has a time stamp in its header (header.stamp) that records the moment the reading was taken, not the moment it arrived. When the filter receives a reading, it first predicts the state up to that time stamp, then corrects it. The closer to the hardware a time stamp is applied, the better: the D435i’s IMU packets are stamped with the clock of the camera’s depth chip, which is why the IMU data and the depth images line up (RealSense guide). Across computers, their clocks must also agree (on a local network, usually with chrony or PTP); otherwise "10:00:00.000" on one computer is 10:00:00.030 on another. As step ⑩ of page 5 explained, two computers whose clocks differ by 50 ppm are 30 ms apart after just ten minutes; and a check question in that page’s summary worked out that a 30 ms clock offset shifts a car moving at 15 m/s by 0.45 m.

第二条：迟到的读数要退回去处理。一个读数到的时候，滤波器可能已经用更新的读数往前走过了。三种办法：当成"现在"的读数直接用（错）；扔掉（浪费）；退回到它的时间戳，从那里把后面的读数重新处理一遍（对，但要存一段历史）。robot_localization 的第三种办法要打开 smooth_lagged_data，并用 history_length 规定存多少秒的历史，它的文档说这"对那些要花很多 CPU 才算出位姿的节点（比如激光扫描匹配）特别有用"，因为它们给出的结果常常迟到（robot_localization 文档）。默认 smooth_lagged_data 是 false、history_length 是 0（我们在 robot_localization 源码里读到的）：这时迟到的读数被直接拿来修正现在的状态，也就是第一种办法。Rule two: late readings must be handled by rewinding. By the time a reading arrives, the filter may already have moved on using newer readings. There are three options: use it directly as a reading of "now" (wrong); throw it away (wasteful); or rewind to its time stamp and reprocess all the later readings from there (right, but a stretch of history must be kept). In robot_localization, the third option means turning on smooth_lagged_data and using history_length to set how many seconds of history to keep; its documentation says this is "especially useful for measurements that come from nodes that require heavy CPU usage to generate pose estimates (e.g., laser scan matchers)", because their results are often late (robot_localization docs). By default smooth_lagged_data is false and history_length is 0 (we read this in the robot_localization source code): a late reading is then used straight away to correct the current state, which is the first option.

δ ≈ ( v + ω · D ) · Δt

时间差了 Δt，机器人以速度 v 往前开、以角速度 ω 转，离转动中心 D 远的东西，最多会被放错 (v + ωD)Δt。If the time is off by Δt while the robot drives forward at speed v and turns at angular velocity ω, something at distance D from the centre of rotation is misplaced by up to (v + ωD)Δt.

用错了时刻的位姿去换算一个读数，等于把物体交给了"Δt 之前（或之后）的那个机器人"：平移的部分差 vΔt；转动的部分让方向差 ωΔt 弧度，在 D 远的地方变成 ωΔt·D 的弧长（小角度近似）。两部分方向不一定相同，这里取最坏的情况相加。Transforming a reading with the pose of the wrong moment is like handing the object to "the robot at a moment Δt earlier (or later)": the translation is off by vΔt; the rotation puts the direction off by ωΔt radians, which at distance D becomes an arc of length ωΔt·D (small-angle approximation). The two parts need not point the same way; here we take the worst case and add them.

One lidar revolution takes 25 ms, and the 270° field of view takes up 18.75 ms of it, so the first and the last beam are not fired at the same moment: at 0.5 m/s the two ends of the scan are 9.4 mm apart, and when turning at 0.5 rad/s the scan is twisted by 0.54° from end to end, so a wall 5 m away is shifted by 4.7 cm (our arithmetic).

这个误差不随噪声平均掉：同样的延迟，每次都把东西往同一个方向放错，它是偏差。机器人动得越快、东西越远，越要认真对待时间。This error does not average out with the noise: the same delay misplaces things in the same direction every time, so it is a bias. The faster the robot moves and the farther away things are, the more seriously you have to take time.

第三条：坐标系要对，同一份信息只用一次。REP 103 规定了 ROS 里的单位和坐标轴：长度用米、角度用弧度；机身坐标系 x 朝前、y 朝左、z 朝上；相机的光学坐标系 z 朝前、x 朝右、y 朝下（REP 103）。REP 105 规定了两级位姿（第 15 篇第 ⑩ 步）：odom → base_link 必须连续、不跳，由航迹推算给出；map → odom 由定位程序给出，可以跳。这台机器人上，前者是 robot_localization 的 ekf_node 融合轮子和陀螺的结果，后者是 AMCL。为什么要分两级？导航的控制器每秒 20 次算速度，最怕位置突然跳一下；全局规划要的是在地图上准，跳一下也不要紧。robot_localization 的配置文件里也写着：融合连续的数据（轮式里程计、视觉里程计、IMU）时，world_frame 设成 odom；融合 GPS 这类全局的绝对位置时设成 map，并且"一定要有别的什么在发 odom → base_link"，而且那一个不该再融合全局数据（ekf.yaml）。Rule three: get the frames right, and use the same information only once. REP 103 specifies the units and axes used in ROS: lengths in metres, angles in radians; the body frame has x forward, y left and z up; a camera’s optical frame has z forward, x right and y down (REP 103). REP 105 specifies two levels of pose (step ⑩ of page 15): odom → base_link must be continuous, never jumping, and comes from dead reckoning; map → odom comes from the localisation program and may jump. On this robot, the first is the output of robot_localization’s ekf_node fusing the wheels and the gyro, and the second is AMCL. Why two levels? The navigation controller computes a velocity 20 times a second, and what it fears most is a sudden jump in position; global planning needs accuracy on the map, and a jump does it no harm. The robot_localization configuration file says the same: when fusing continuous data (wheel odometry, visual odometry, IMU), set world_frame to odom; when fusing global absolute positions such as GPS, set it to map, and "MAKE SURE something else is generating the odom → base_link transform", and that one should not fuse the global data as well (ekf.yaml).

robot_localization 的文档还专门提醒：只用轮子编码器的里程计，它报告的位置、朝向和速度全都来自同一组编码器，要是都喂进滤波器，就是把同一份信息重复喂了好几遍，"最好只用速度"（第 ③ 步的"同一份信息只能算一次"）；两个 IMU 都说自己的朝向方差是 0.1，可它们的读数差得比这还大，滤波器的输出就会在两者之间来回摆。读数消息里带的协方差，必须说真话。The robot_localization documentation also makes a point of warning: with odometry from wheel encoders alone, the position, heading and velocity it reports all come from the same set of encoders, so feeding them all into the filter feeds it the same information several times over, and "it’s best to just use the velocities" (step ③’s "the same information can be counted only once"); and if two IMUs each say their orientation variance is 0.1 but their readings differ by more than that, the filter’s output will swing back and forth between them. The covariance carried in a reading’s message must tell the truth.

A time difference Δt brings an error of about (v + ωD)Δt; it is a bias and does not average out. 第 ⑩ 步 · SLAMStep ⑩ · SLAM

没有地图：一边建图一边定位No map: mapping while localising

AMCL 要一张地图才能比对。这张地图是团队事先做的：机器人刚到厨房那天，有人用手柄推着它绕厨房走了几圈，它一边走一边跑 slam_toolbox，最后把地图存下来；以后每次开机，地图服务器把它读进来交给 AMCL。可这里有个先有鸡还是先有蛋的问题：画地图要知道每一圈扫描是在哪里扫的，知道在哪里又要有地图。同时解决这两件事，就叫 SLAM（同时定位与建图）。AMCL needs a map to compare against. The team made this map in advance: on the robot’s first day in the kitchen, someone drove it a few laps around the kitchen with a joystick while it ran slam_toolbox, and at the end the map was saved; since then, every time the robot starts up, the map server loads it and hands it to AMCL. But there is a chicken-and-egg problem here: to draw the map you need to know where each scan was taken, and to know where you are you need a map. Solving both at once is called SLAM (simultaneous localisation and mapping).

这个问题起源于 1986 年旧金山的 IEEE 机器人与自动化会议，几位研究者在会上把好些桌布和餐巾纸写满了讨论；SLAM 这个缩写在 1995 年的一次研讨会上才第一次出现。早期大家以为地图上各个路标的误差会越积越大，后来才明白，路标之间的相关正是关键：一旦重新看到一个旧路标，所有和它相关的路标都会一起变准（Durrant-Whyte、Bailey，2006）。最早的做法是把机器人和所有路标的位置放进一个 EKF（第 ⑦ 步），可协方差矩阵随路标数平方地长，每看到一次都要整个更新，路标一多就算不动。The problem goes back to the 1986 IEEE Robotics and Automation Conference in San Francisco, where a few researchers covered a good many tablecloths and napkins with their discussions; the acronym SLAM first appeared only in 1995, at a symposium. At first people thought the errors of the landmarks on a map would just keep growing; only later did they realise that the correlations between landmarks are the key: as soon as an old landmark is seen again, every landmark correlated with it becomes more accurate too (Durrant-Whyte and Bailey, 2006). The earliest approach put the robot and the positions of all the landmarks into one EKF (step ⑦), but the covariance matrix grows with the square of the number of landmarks and has to be updated in full at every sighting, so with many landmarks the computation cannot keep up.

室内机器人最常用的地图是占据栅格（Moravec 和 Elfes 1985 年提出，Moravec、Elfes，1985）：把地面切成小格子，每个格子记一个"被占据的可能性"。一束激光从机器人出发，打到 3.2 m 外停下：落点那一格多半有东西，路过的那些格子多半是空的。The map indoor robots use most is the occupancy grid (proposed by Moravec and Elfes in 1985, Moravec and Elfes, 1985): cut the floor into small cells and let each cell record a "chance of being occupied". A laser beam leaves the robot and stops 3.2 m away: the cell where it lands probably holds something, and the cells it passed through are probably empty.

每个格子记一个数 l：被激光打中一次，就加上"打中时的对数几率"（比如 +0.85）；被激光穿过一次，就加上"穿过时的对数几率"（比如 −0.41）。要用占据的概率时，再用第二行换回去。Each cell keeps one number, l: each time a beam hits it, add the "log-odds for a hit" (say +0.85); each time a beam passes through it, add the "log-odds for a pass-through" (say −0.41). When you need the probability that the cell is occupied, convert back with the second line.

这是第 ④ 步的贝叶斯修正，换成对数几率 log(p/(1 − p)) 以后的样子：乘法变成了加法，好算，也不会因为乘了很多个小于 1 的数而丢精度。它假设格子和格子之间互相独立，每个格子各算各的，所以几百万个格子也算得动。This is the Bayes correction of step ④, rewritten in log-odds, log(p/(1 − p)): multiplication becomes addition, which is easy to compute and loses no precision from multiplying many numbers smaller than 1. It assumes the cells are independent of one another, so each cell is worked out on its own, and even several million cells are no problem.

一个格子被打中 3 次：l = 3 × 0.847 = 2.54，Pocc = 0.927；接着又被穿过一次：l = 2.54 − 0.41 = 2.13，Pocc = 0.894（我们算的）。门开着时，那几格被反复穿过，l 一路往下掉，地图上就成了空地。这是教科书的做法；slam_toolbox 沿用 Karto，更简单：每格数被扫到几次、其中打中几次，扫到超过 2 次、打中的比例超过 0.1 就算占据（我们在源码里读到的）。A cell hit 3 times: l = 3 × 0.847 = 2.54, Pocc = 0.927; then passed through once: l = 2.54 − 0.41 = 2.13, Pocc = 0.894 (our arithmetic). While a door stands open, beams pass through those few cells again and again, l keeps falling, and the map shows free space there. That is the textbook method; slam_toolbox follows Karto and does something simpler: each cell keeps a count of how many times it has been scanned and how many of those were hits, and a cell scanned more than 2 times with a hit fraction above 0.1 counts as occupied (we read this in the source code).

格子里的数是画在"估计出来的位姿"上的。位姿错了，墙就画到错的地方：绕一圈回来，里程计漂了 20 cm，同一面墙在地图上就成了两道，相距 20 cm。The numbers in the cells are drawn at the "estimated pose". slam_toolbox 是 ROS 2 默认的 SLAM 包：2020 年 1 月，导航工作组决定让它随 ROS 2 和 Navigation2 一起发布（ROS Discourse，2020），取代了 GMapping（Macenski、Jambrecic，2021）。它分两层：前端拿每一圈新扫描和附近的旧扫描做匹配（继承自 SRI 的 Open Karto），算出相邻位姿之间的相对运动，比轮子准得多；后端把这些位姿和它们之间的约束存成一张位姿图，用 Google 的 Ceres 求解（第 9 篇第 ⑧ 步做过这件事）。我们在它的默认配置和源码里读到：地图格子 5 cm；底盘走过约 0.5 m 才加一个新节点（配置里还写着"转 0.5 rad"，可源码默认只看走了多远，原地转身不加节点）；回到旧路 3 m 以内时去找回环：那里要有一串至少 10 个前后相连的旧节点，新扫描和这一串拼成的局部地图匹配得足够好，才算数；每 0.02 s 发一次 map → odom。论文说它能实时地画出 24,000 m2 的地方。slam_toolbox is the default SLAM package in ROS 2: in January 2020 the navigation working group decided to ship it with ROS 2 and Navigation2 (ROS Discourse, 2020), replacing GMapping (Macenski and Jambrecic, 2021). 只估现在，还是把整条路一起估Estimate only the present, or the whole path at once

第 ⑤ 到 ⑨ 步的滤波器只记"现在"：一个新读数到了，修正完现在的状态，过去的状态就不要了。过去的信息并没有丢，它被"折进"了现在的协方差里；可折进去的时候，用的是当时的线性化，以后再也改不了。第 ⑩ 步的位姿图换了一个思路：把过去的位姿也当成未知数留着，把每一个读数都当成一条约束，最后把所有未知数一起解出来。这叫平滑。把未知数画成圆圈、约束画成小方块、方块连到它牵涉的那几个圆圈上，就是因子图（Dellaert、Kaess，2017）：The filters of steps ⑤ to ⑨ remember only "now": when a new reading arrives, they correct the current state and discard the past states. The past information is not lost; it has been "folded into" the current covariance. But it was folded in with the linearisation of that moment, and that can never be changed afterwards. The pose graph of step ⑩ took another approach: keep the past poses as unknowns too, treat every reading as a constraint, and in the end solve for all the unknowns together. This is called smoothing. Draw the unknowns as circles and the constraints as small squares, connect each square to the circles it involves, and you have a factor graph (Dellaert and Kaess, 2017):

每个因子是一条高斯约束：残差 rk 越大，这组变量越不可能，可能性正比于 exp(−½‖rk‖2Σk)。所有因子的可能性相乘，取对数，乘法就变成平方和；"最可能的那一组变量"（最大后验）就是这个最小二乘问题的解。用 Σ−1 加权，就是第 ③ 步"按信息量加权"的推广：准的约束说话分量重。解法是高斯–牛顿：在当前的解附近把残差线性化，解一个线性方程组，挪一步，再重新线性化，重复几次。Each factor is a Gaussian constraint: the larger the residual rk, the less probable this set of variables, with a probability proportional to exp(−½‖rk‖2Σk). Multiply the probabilities of all the factors and take the logarithm, and the product becomes a sum of squares; the "most probable set of variables" (maximum a posteriori, MAP) is the solution of this least-squares problem. Weighting by Σ−1 generalises the "weighting by information" of step ③: accurate constraints get a bigger say. It is solved with Gauss–Newton: linearise the residuals around the current solution, solve a linear system, take a step, linearise again, and repeat a few times.

滤波和平滑的关系，在因子图上一眼就能看清：滤波就是把旧变量一个个边缘化掉的平滑。边缘化一个变量，它的信息不会消失，而是变成一条新的约束，连在它所有的邻居之间。所以每去掉一个旧位姿，它的邻居之间就多出一个非零块（叫填充）。把所有过去的位姿都去掉，只留"现在的位姿 + 所有路标"，剩下的矩阵就几乎全是非零的：这正是第 ⑩ 步说的 EKF-SLAM 为什么随路标数平方地变慢。滤波还有第二个代价：被边缘化的变量，线性化的点固定在当时，以后发现估错了也改不回来；平滑可以随时重新线性化。On a factor graph the relation between filtering and smoothing is plain to see: filtering is smoothing that marginalises the old variables one by one. When you marginalise a variable, its information does not vanish; it becomes a new constraint linking all its neighbours. So each time an old pose is removed, an extra non-zero block appears between its neighbours (this is called fill-in). Remove all the past poses, keeping only "the current pose + all the landmarks", and the remaining matrix is almost entirely non-zero: this is exactly why, as step ⑩ said, EKF-SLAM slows down with the square of the number of landmarks. Filtering has a second cost: a marginalised variable keeps the linearisation point of its moment, and if the estimate later turns out to be wrong, that cannot be undone; a smoother can relinearise at any time.

中间的做法是滑动窗口：只留最近的十来个位姿，更早的边缘化掉。视觉惯性里程计 VINS-Mono 就是这样，窗口里留 10 个关键帧（Qin 等，2018）。另一条路是增量平滑：新读数只牵动图的一小部分，iSAM2 用一种叫贝叶斯树的结构，只重新算受影响的那部分，也只在需要时重新线性化，不用定期整个重算（Kaess 等，2012）。常用的库有 GTSAM、Ceres 和 g2o。ROS 里有一个按因子图做多传感器融合的框架 fuse，不过它只为 Jazzy 及以后的发行版发布了二进制包，没有 Humble 的（ROS 软件包索引）；这台跑 Humble 的机器人用不上现成的包，融合还是交给 robot_localization。A middle way is a sliding window: keep only the last ten or so poses and marginalise the older ones. The camera is fixed to the base, so this part does not change over time; while the base is still moving, the point must first be taken into odom using the odom → base_link of the moment the image was taken (step ⑩ of page 15; the time stamps of step ⑨). Every detection has an error: the depth has σ ≈ 1.6 mm at 0.8 m (step ①), and the centre of the box is not necessarily the centre of the cup (σ about 5 mm, illustrative); sometimes a frame misses a cup, sometimes something else is taken for a cup. And on the counter there are two identical cups, A and B, 17 cm apart.

要从一帧帧检测里得到"杯子 A 在哪"，需要一个跟踪器：给每只杯子开一条轨迹，每条轨迹一个小卡尔曼滤波器（杯子大多不动，用"静止"模型；有人在挪它时，用"匀速"模型）。每来一帧，先把每条轨迹预测到这一帧的时间，再回答一个新问题：这一帧的每个检测，属于哪条轨迹？这叫数据关联。To get "where cup A is" out of frame after frame of detections, you need a tracker: it opens a track for each cup, and each track has its own small Kalman filter (cups mostly stay put, so a "static" model; while someone is moving one, a "constant-velocity" model). With each new frame, it first predicts every track forward to the time of that frame, then answers a new question: which track does each detection in this frame belong to? This is called data association.

ν = z − H x̂−S = H P− HT + Rd2 = νT S−1 ν < γ

一个检测和一条轨迹的预测之差叫新息 ν；把它按"本来该有多不确定"（S）缩放以后的平方，小于门限 γ，才认为这个检测可能属于这条轨迹。The difference between a detection and a track’s prediction is called the innovation ν. Scale it by "how uncertain it ought to be" (S) and square it: only if the result is below the gate γ is the detection taken as possibly belonging to this track.

同样差 2 cm，对一条已经跟稳的轨迹来说是很远（S 里差不多只剩检测的 5 mm，4 个 σ），对一条刚被挡住一秒、不确定已经长到 2 cm 的轨迹来说却很近（S 的 σ 约 2.1 cm，不到 1 个 σ）。马氏距离把"差多少"除以"该有多不确定"，各个方向分别算，所以门限画出来是一个椭圆，而不是圆。如果滤波器的 S 是对的，d2 服从自由度为 2 的 χ2 分布：真检测有 99% 落在 γ = 9.21 以内。The same 2 cm gap is a long way for a track that has settled (S holds little more than the detection’s 5 mm, so that is 4 σ), but close for a track that has just been hidden for a second and whose uncertainty has grown to 2 cm (S’s σ is about 2.1 cm, so that is less than 1 σ). The Mahalanobis distance divides "how far off" by "how uncertain it should be", direction by direction, so the gate comes out as an ellipse rather than a circle. If the filter’s S is right, d2 follows a χ2 distribution with 2 degrees of freedom: 99% of true detections fall within γ = 9.21.

门限太紧，真的检测会被扔掉，轨迹断掉；太松，误检和旁边的杯子会被吸进来，两条轨迹的编号互换（ID 交换）。If the gate is too tight, real detections are thrown away and the track breaks off; if it is too loose, false positives and the neighbouring cup get pulled in, and two tracks swap their IDs (an ID switch).

门限挑出"可能的"配对以后，还要决定"到底配谁"。最简单的是最近邻：每条轨迹拿离它最近的那个检测，一条一条来；问题是先挑的轨迹可能抢走了别人的检测。全局最优的做法是把所有轨迹和所有检测排成一张代价表（代价就是 d2），一次求出总代价最小的配对（匈牙利算法）。另外还要管轨迹的生死：一个没人认领的检测，在最近几帧里反复出现（比如 5 帧里有 3 帧），才开一条新轨迹；一条轨迹连续很多帧（比如 15 帧）都没收到检测，就删掉（这些数是示意）。Once the gate has picked out the "possible" pairings, the tracker still has to decide "which actually goes with which". The simplest way is nearest neighbour: each track takes the detection closest to it, one track at a time; the trouble is that a track that picks first may grab another track’s detection. The global optimum approach sets out all the tracks and all the detections in one cost table (the cost is d2) and finds, in one go, the pairing with the smallest total cost (the Hungarian algorithm). The tracker also has to manage the birth and death of tracks: an unclaimed detection opens a new track only if it keeps turning up over the last few frames (say in 3 out of 5); a track that receives no detection for many frames in a row (say 15) is deleted (these numbers are illustrative).

最难的时刻是遮挡。手臂伸向杯子时，自己就挡住了相机。这一两秒里没有检测，轨迹只能靠预测往前走：静止的杯子没关系，不确定慢慢长；可要是这时有人把 B 挪了过去，等手臂让开，跟踪器看到的就是两个"位置有点不对"的检测，配错的可能大大增加。两只杯子挨得比门限还近时，光靠位置是分不清的，要加上外观（颜色、花纹；可 A 和 B 一模一样，外观也帮不上，只能靠运动的连续，或者等它们分开再认），或者用更强的位姿估计：FoundationPose 这类模型给它一个 CAD 模型或者几张参考照片，不用重新训练，就能给出新物体的 6D 位姿并一帧帧跟下去（Wen 等，2024）。The hardest moment is occlusion. When the arm reaches for a cup, it blocks the robot’s own camera. For a second or two there are no detections, and the tracks can only move on by prediction: for a cup standing still that does no harm: its uncertainty just grows slowly; but if someone moves B across in the meantime, then once the arm is out of the way the tracker sees two detections "in slightly the wrong places", and the chance of a wrong pairing rises sharply. When two cups are closer together than the gate, position alone cannot tell them apart: you have to add appearance (colour, pattern; but A and B are identical, so appearance does not help either, leaving only the continuity of motion, or waiting until they separate and telling them apart again), or use a stronger pose estimate: give a model such as FoundationPose a CAD model or a few reference photos and, without retraining, it gives the 6D pose of a new object and follows it frame by frame (Wen et al., 2024).

估计的不只是位置Estimating more than position

到这里估的都是"在哪"。可只要一个量会通过某个模型影响到读数，它就能被放进状态里估出来，不管它是不是位置：So far we have only estimated "where". But as long as a quantity affects the readings through some model, it can go into the state and be estimated, whether or not it is a position:

  • 速度：第 ⑥ 步从位置估出了速度；第 6 篇第 ③ 步从编码器估出了关节角速度。Velocity: step ⑥ estimated velocity from position; step ③ of page 6 estimated joint angular velocity from the encoders.
  • 传感器的偏差：陀螺和加速度计的零偏会慢慢变（比如随温度），Stanley 把它们直接放进了 15 个数的状态里（第 ⑥ 步）。只要还有别的读数校着朝向和位置，零偏就会在新息里露出来。Sensor biases: gyro and accelerometer biases drift slowly (with temperature, for example), and Stanley put them straight into its 15-number state (step ⑥). As long as other readings keep a check on the heading and position, the biases show up in the innovation.
  • 机器人自己的参数：轮子的半径、轮距（第 ② 步的系统误差），可以在走路时和激光比着慢慢估。The robot’s own parameters: the wheel radius and the wheelbase (the systematic errors of step ②) can be estimated little by little while it drives, by comparing against the lidar.
  • 标定参数和时间差：相机和 IMU 之间的外参，甚至两者时间戳之间的固定偏差，都可以当成未知数，和位姿、路标一起优化（Qin、Shen，2018：时间戳"受触发和传输延迟影响"，这个偏差"严重影响融合的效果"）。Calibration parameters and time offsets: the extrinsics between the camera and the IMU, and even a fixed offset between their time stamps, can be treated as unknowns and optimised together with the poses and landmarks (Qin and Shen, 2018: time stamps "suffer from triggering and transmission delays", and this offset "dramatically influences the performance of sensor fusion").
  • 力：有没有东西在推手臂，推得多大。Force: whether something is pushing the arm, and how hard.

最后一种值得细看。Franka 的七个关节里都有力矩传感器，libfranka 每 1 毫秒给出 tau_J（量到的关节力矩）和 tau_ext_hat_filtered：量到的力矩减去模型预测的力矩（重力、惯性、末端负载），再低通滤波，剩下的就是"外面的东西施加的力矩"；还有换算到末端的外力 O_F_ext_hat_K（靠近奇异位形时它会变成 0），以及接触和碰撞的标志（libfranka 文档）。没有关节力矩传感器的机器人，只能从电机的电流去推，还得扣掉减速器里很难建准的摩擦。不管用哪一种力矩，直接的办法都是把运动方程倒过来：外力矩 = 惯量 × 角加速度 + … − 关节力矩；可角加速度要把编码器读数微分两次，噪声被放大得一塌糊涂（第 6 篇第 ③ 步看过微分一次就有多糟）。动量观测器绕开了微分；有力矩传感器时，把式子里的电机力矩换成量到的 tau_J，电机那一侧的摩擦就进不来了。The last one deserves a closer look. All seven of Franka’s joints have torque sensors, and every 1 ms libfranka reports tau_J (the measured joint torque) and tau_ext_hat_filtered: the measured torque minus the torque the model predicts (gravity, inertia, the end-effector load), then low-pass filtered, which leaves "the torque applied by something outside". There is also the external force converted to the end effector, O_F_ext_hat_K (which becomes 0 near singular configurations), as well as contact and collision flags (libfranka docs). A robot without joint torque sensors has to infer the torque from the motor currents, and then subtract the friction in the gearbox, which is hard to model accurately. Whichever torque you use, the direct approach is to run the equation of motion backwards: external torque = inertia × angular acceleration + … − joint torque. But the angular acceleration means differentiating the encoder readings twice, which amplifies the noise into a hopeless mess (step ③ of page 6 showed how bad a single differentiation already is). The momentum observer sidesteps the differentiation; with torque sensors, the motor torque in its equations is replaced by the measured tau_J, so friction on the motor side never gets in.

p = M(q) · q̇dτ̂ / dt = KO · ( τext − τ̂ )

关节的广义动量等于惯量矩阵乘以关节速度。观测器给出的外力矩估计 τ̂，会以 KO 的速率追向真实的外力矩：两者之差按指数缩小，每过 1/KO 秒只剩原来的约 37%。The joints’ generalised momentum is the inertia matrix times the joint velocity. The observer’s estimate of the external torque, τ̂, chases the true external torque at a rate KO: the gap between them shrinks exponentially, and after each 1/KO seconds only about 37% of it is left.

观测器按模型、关节力矩和它自己当前的估计 τ̂ 一起积分，算出一份"应该有的动量"，和用 M(q)·q̇ 算出的"实际的动量"比：差多少，就说明还有多少外力矩没被 τ̂ 算进去，把这个差乘上 KO 当成新的估计。正因为 τ̂ 自己也被积了进去，推下来 τ̂ 的动态正好是第二行：真实的外力矩经过一个时间常数为 1/KO 的一阶低通（要是不把 τ̂ 放进积分，差值就只是外力矩的积分，会一直涨下去）。它只用到 q、q̇、电机力矩和模型，不需要角加速度，也不需要求惯量矩阵的逆，每个关节各自独立（Haddadin 等，2017）。这套方法后来进了 KUKA 的 LBR 系列和 Franka 的产品。The observer integrates the model, the joint torque and its own current estimate τ̂ together, giving the momentum the joint "should have", and compares it with the "actual momentum" computed from M(q)·q̇. However large the difference, that is how much external torque τ̂ has not yet accounted for; multiply the difference by KO and it becomes the new estimate. Precisely because τ̂ itself goes into the integral, working it through gives τ̂ exactly the dynamics of the second line: the true external torque passed through a first-order low-pass filter with time constant 1/KO (if τ̂ were left out of the integral, the difference would just be the integral of the external torque and would keep growing). It uses only q, q̇, the motor torque and the model; it needs no angular acceleration and no inverse of the inertia matrix, and each joint is independent of the others (Haddadin et al., 2017). The method later found its way into KUKA’s LBR series and Franka’s products.

越快越吵：KO 大，反应快，可模型的误差和噪声也一起进来。门槛要高于"模型不准"本身造成的偏差：比如夹爪里拿着杯子，却没在负载里写上它的重量，这份重力就会被当成一个一直存在的外力。Faster means noisier: a large KO reacts quickly, but model errors and noise come in with it. The threshold has to be above the bias that a "wrong model" causes on its own: if the gripper is holding a cup whose weight was never entered in the load, that weight is taken for an external force that is always there.

能估什么、不能估什么，最后都落在可观上（第 ⑥ 步）：只有会影响读数的量才估得出来。陀螺零偏要有别的东西校着才看得出：停着不动时，"角速度是 0"本身就是一个读数（第 ② 步）；走起来以后，要靠激光、相机校着朝向；轮子半径要边走边和外界比才看得出；机器人停着不动时，外参里的某些方向根本看不出来。往状态里加东西之前，先问一句：哪个读数会因为它而变？What can and cannot be estimated comes down in the end to observability (step ⑥): only quantities that affect the readings can be estimated. A gyro bias only shows up when something else keeps a check on it: while the robot stands still, "the angular velocity is 0" is itself a reading (step ②); once it moves, the lidar and the camera have to keep a check on the heading. The wheel radius only shows up if the robot compares itself with the outside world as it drives; and while the robot stands still, some directions of the extrinsics cannot be seen at all. Before adding anything to the state, ask: which reading will change because of it?

第 ⑭ 步 · 出错Step ⑭ · Going wrong

估计什么时候会错，怎么发现When estimation goes wrong, and how to find out

估计器出错时，最危险的不是"误差大"，而是"误差大，它自己却以为很准"：下游的导航、抓取都会照着一个错的位置放心地干活。常见的错法可以归成四类：When an estimator goes wrong, the most dangerous case is not "the error is large" but "the error is large, and it thinks it is accurate": navigation, grasping and everything else downstream will confidently get on with the job from a wrong position. The common ways of going wrong fall into four kinds:

  1. 看不见（退化、不可观）：长走廊里，激光只看到两侧平行的墙，横向定得很准，沿着走廊的方向却一点约束都没有，估计会沿着走廊滑，协方差椭圆被拉成细长条。基于优化的方法可以分析出这些"退化方向"，只在约束充分的方向上求解（Zhang 等，2016）。相机遇到白墙、暗处、反光，也是同一回事。Cannot see (degeneracy, unobservability): in a long corridor the lidar sees only the parallel walls on either side, so the sideways position is pinned down accurately, but along the corridor there is no constraint at all; the estimate slides along the corridor, and the covariance ellipse is stretched into a long, thin strip. Optimisation-based methods can work out these "degenerate directions" and solve only in the directions that are well constrained (Zhang et al., 2016). A camera facing a white wall, darkness or reflections has the same problem.
  2. 太自信：Q、R 设得太小；同一份信息用了两遍（第 ③、⑨ 步）；把 61 束激光当成独立的读数相乘（第 ⑧ 步）；有离群的坏读数。太自信的滤波器协方差太小，门限也跟着太紧（第 ⑫ 步），于是真正的读数被当成离群值扔掉，估计再也回不来。Over-confident: Q and R set too small; the same information counted twice (steps ③ and ⑨); the 61 lidar beams multiplied together as if they were independent readings (step ⑧); bad readings that are outliers. An over-confident filter has too small a covariance, so its gate is too tight as well (step ⑫); genuine readings are then thrown away as outliers, and the estimate never comes back.
  3. 认错：把检测配给了错的物体（第 ⑫ 步）；在长得一样的地方定位到了错的那一处（第 ⑧ 步）；被绑架。Mistaken identity: a detection paired with the wrong object (step ⑫); localising at the wrong one of several places that look alike (step ⑧); being kidnapped.
  4. 时间和坐标系错了：延迟没处理、钟没对齐（第 ⑨ 步）；外参不准：激光装歪了 2°，5 m 外的墙就错开 5 × tan 2° ≈ 17 cm；消息里写错了坐标系的名字。Wrong time or wrong frame: latency not handled, clocks not synchronised (step ⑨); inaccurate extrinsics: with the lidar mounted 2° askew, a wall 5 m away is shifted by 5 × tan 2° ≈ 17 cm; the wrong frame name written in a message.

估计器看不到真值，怎么知道自己错了？靠新息。每次修正时，读数和预测之差 ν 和它的协方差 S 都是现成的；如果滤波器对自己的不确定估得对，新息的大小应该"刚好"和 S 相称。An estimator cannot see the truth, so how can it know it is wrong? From the innovation. At every correction, the difference between the reading and the prediction, ν, and its covariance S are already to hand; if the filter has its own uncertainty right, the innovations should be "just" as large as S says they should be.

d2 = νT S−1 νE[ d2 ] = m

每次修正都算一个归一化新息平方 d2（NIS）；滤波器诚实的话，它的期望（长期的平均值）等于读数的维数 m，一段时间里的平均应该约等于 m，而且只有约 5% 的时候超过 95% 的那条线。At every correction, compute the normalised innovation squared d2 (NIS). If the filter is honest, its expectation (the long-run average) equals the dimension m of the reading: averaged over a stretch of time it should come out at about m, and it should be above the 95% line only about 5% of the time.

把新息按 S 白化以后，就是 m 个互相独立、标准差为 1 的高斯数，它们的平方和服从自由度为 m 的 χ2 分布，平均值是 m。平均值明显大于 m，说明滤波器以为自己比实际更准（太自信，或者模型、时间、外参有偏差）；明显小于 m，说明它太保守：R 设大了，白白浪费了读数；Q 设大了，白白浪费了模型，估计比该有的更抖。第 ⑫ 步拿 d2 决定一个读数收不收，这里拿它的平均值检查滤波器本身。Once the innovation is whitened with S, it is m independent Gaussian numbers, each with a standard deviation of 1; the sum of their squares follows a χ2 distribution with m degrees of freedom, whose mean is m. An average clearly above m means the filter believes it is more accurate than it really is (over-confident, or a bias in the model, the timing or the extrinsics); clearly below m means it is too cautious: an R set too large wastes the readings, and a Q set too large wastes the model and makes the estimate jitter more than it should. Step ⑫ used d2 to decide whether to accept a reading; here its average checks the filter itself.

m = 2：健康的滤波器 d2 平均约 2，95% 的那条线是 5.99。要是把轮式里程计的位置和速度都喂进去（同一份信息用了两遍），P 小得不真实，d2 会长期远大于 2；被绑架的那一刻，d2 会一下子冲到几百。m = 2: for a healthy filter d2 averages about 2, and the 95% line is at 5.99. Feed in both the position and the velocity from the wheel odometry (the same information counted twice) and P becomes unrealistically small, so d2 stays far above 2 for long stretches; at the moment of a kidnapping, d2 shoots up to a few hundred all at once.

这是估计器少有的"自检"：不知道真值，也能从新息看出它诚不诚实。把这些数发到诊断话题上，任务管理就能在定位不可信时让机器人停下来（第 15 篇第 ⑭ 步）。This is one of the few "self-checks" an estimator has: without knowing the truth, you can still tell from the innovations whether it is honest. Publish these numbers on a diagnostics topic, and the task manager can stop the robot when its localisation cannot be trusted (step ⑭ of page 15).

发现以后怎么办？短暂的离群读数，门限会挡住；持续的不一致，就要重新定位：Nav2 里可以调用 AMCL 的 reinitialize_global_localization 把粒子重新撒满全图，或者让人给一个初始位姿；VIO 之类的系统检测到跟丢了，会重新初始化。更根本的办法是换一个不会同时失效的传感器：长走廊里激光退化，轮子和陀螺还在；白墙前相机退化，激光还在。这也是为什么机器人总要装好几种传感器。What do you do once a problem is found? Brief outlier readings are stopped by the gate; a persistent inconsistency calls for relocalisation: in Nav2 you can call AMCL’s reinitialize_global_localization to scatter the particles over the whole map again, or have someone give an initial pose; systems such as VIO reinitialise when they detect that they have lost track. The more fundamental remedy is another sensor that does not fail at the same time: in a long corridor the lidar degenerates, but the wheels and the gyro still work; in front of a white wall the camera degenerates, but the lidar still works. This is also why robots always carry several kinds of sensor.

学习方法改变的主要是前端：从像素里找对应、算深度、认出物体和它的位姿。后端那套"预测、修正、按信息量加权、最小二乘"大多没变：DROID-SLAM 的网络算出对应关系，后面接的仍然是一个可微的位姿和深度优化；MASt3R-SLAM 用一个三维重建模型（第 10 篇）当先验；FoundationPose 能给出杯子的 6D 位姿并一帧帧跟下去，可它不报告不确定，也不管哪只是哪只：把结果放进 base_link、和别的读数合起来、分清 A 和 B，还是要按时间戳、按马氏距离来做（第 ⑨、⑫ 步）。另一条路是不显式地估：学出来的策略把最近一段观测和动作一起输入，自己从中"推断"出需要的状态（第 3 篇第 6 步说的"边做边看"）。可底盘在地图上的位置、手臂有没有撞到东西这些要拿去做安全判断的量，今天的机器人仍然用显式的、能报告自己不确定的估计。算力也在变：NVIDIA 的 cuVSLAM 把视觉 SLAM 放上 GPU，支持 1 到 32 个相机，在 Jetson AGX Orin 上双目模式每次跟踪约 1.8 ms（768 × 480 的图；Korovko 等，2025），ROS 2 里通过 Isaac ROS 使用。What learning has mainly changed is the front end: finding correspondences in the pixels, computing depth, recognising objects and their poses. The back end’s "predict, correct, weight by information, least squares" has mostly stayed the same: DROID-SLAM’s network computes the correspondences, but behind it there is still a differentiable optimisation of poses and depths; MASt3R-SLAM uses a 3D reconstruction model (page 10) as its prior; FoundationPose can give a cup’s 6D pose and follow it frame by frame, but it does not report its uncertainty, nor does it care which cup is which: putting the result into base_link, combining it with other readings and telling A from B still have to be done by time stamp and by Mahalanobis distance (steps ⑨ and ⑫). The other route is not to estimate explicitly at all: a learned policy takes the recent observations and actions together as input and "infers" the state it needs from them on its own (what step 6 of page 3 called "watch as it goes"). But for the quantities that go into safety decisions, such as where the base is on the map or whether the arm has hit something, today’s robots still use explicit estimates that can report their own uncertainty. Computing power is changing too: NVIDIA’s cuVSLAM puts visual SLAM on the GPU and supports from 1 to 32 cameras; in stereo mode on a Jetson AGX Orin, each tracking call takes about 1.8 ms (768 × 480 images; Korovko et al., 2025), and in ROS 2 it is used through Isaac ROS.

这是第 ③ 步反过来：合并读数时，信息（方差的倒数）相加，越合越准；误差一段段串起来时，方差相加，越串越大。因为加的是平方，总误差几乎由最大的那一两项决定：把一个小项减半，几乎没有用。This is step ③ in reverse: when readings are combined, information (the inverse of the variance) adds up, and the more you combine, the more accurate the result; when errors are chained together one stage after another, variances add up, and the longer the chain, the larger the error. 一个循环，几种近似，八个回答One loop, a few approximations, eight answers

开头的八个问题：The eight questions from the opening:

  1. 它怎么知道自己在哪：没有一个传感器直接测"我在哪"。它把会积分的读数（轮子、IMU）和与外界比对的读数（激光对地图、相机对物体）合起来：前者给出平滑的短期运动，后者把长期的漂移拉回来；结果是一个位置，加上它的不确定（第 0、②、⑤ 步）。How does it know where it is? No sensor measures "where am I" directly. It combines readings that integrate (wheels, IMU) with readings that compare against the outside world (lidar against the map, camera against objects): the former give smooth short-term motion, the latter pull the long-term drift back. The result is a position plus its uncertainty (steps 0, ② and ⑤).
  2. 为什么绕一圈就不对了：轮式里程计是航迹推算，每一步的误差都被加进去、再也减不掉。轮距差 1%，每转一圈朝向就错 3.6°，朝向一错，横向误差又随走过的路长；IMU 更快，陀螺零偏让朝向误差随时间线性长，加速度计零偏让位置误差随时间平方长（第 ② 步）。Why is it off after one lap? Wheel odometry is dead reckoning: every step’s error is added in and can never be taken out again. With the wheelbase 1% off, the heading is 3.6° out after every full turn, and once the heading is off, the lateral error grows with the distance driven. An IMU drifts faster still: a gyro bias makes the heading error grow linearly with time, and an accelerometer bias makes the position error grow with the square of time (step ②).
  3. 两个传感器说法不一样，信谁：都信，按方差的倒数加权：激光 1.52 m（σ 30 mm）和轮子 1.45 m（σ 80 mm）合成 1.511 m（σ 28.1 mm），比两个都准。前提是误差独立、没有偏差；差得远超它们的 σ 时，先找哪个坏了（第 ③ 步）。Two sensors disagree: which one to believe? Both, weighted by the inverse of their variances: the lidar’s 1.52 m (σ 30 mm) and the wheels’ 1.45 m (σ 80 mm) combine into 1.511 m (σ 28.1 mm), more accurate than either. This assumes independent errors and no bias; when they differ by far more than their σ, first find out which one is broken (step ③).
  4. 卡尔曼滤波在算什么：贝叶斯的"预测、修正"循环在高斯、线性时的精确解：只记平均值和方差，预测时方差加 Q，修正时按增益 K = P⁻/(P⁻ + R) 加入新息。它是加权平均的递推版，又快，在它的前提下又最优，所以从阿波罗飞船到今天的机器人，到处都有它（第 ④–⑥ 步）。What is the Kalman filter computing? The exact solution of the Bayesian "predict-and-correct" loop when everything is Gaussian and linear: it keeps only a mean and a variance, adds Q to the variance when predicting, and adds in the innovation with gain K = P⁻/(P⁻ + R) when correcting. It is a recursive weighted average, fast, and optimal under its assumptions, which is why it is everywhere, from the Apollo spacecraft to today’s robots (steps ④–⑥).
  5. EKF、粒子滤波、因子图各为了什么：EKF 为了非线性（用雅可比把模型拉直），不确定大时会错；粒子滤波为了好几个峰和不知道起点（AMCL 就是它）；因子图为了能回头改过去、处理回环，靠稀疏又快；滤波就是把旧变量边缘化掉的平滑（第 ⑦、⑧、⑪ 步）。What are the EKF, the particle filter and the factor graph each for? The EKF is for nonlinearity (it straightens the model with a Jacobian) and goes wrong when the uncertainty is large; the particle filter is for several peaks and an unknown start (AMCL is one); the factor graph is for going back to revise the past and handling loop closures, and sparsity keeps it fast. Filtering is simply smoothing with the old variables marginalised away (steps ⑦, ⑧ and ⑪).
  6. 不同频率、延迟、位置的传感器怎么放到一起：按时间戳处理，迟到的读数退回去重算；时间差 Δt 带来约 (v + ωD)Δt 的偏差；电脑之间要对时；坐标系照 REP 103 和 REP 105，odom → base_link 连续，map → odom 可以跳；同一份信息只用一次，协方差要说真话（第 ⑨ 步）。How do sensors with different rates, latencies and positions fit together? Go by time stamp, and rewind and replay for late readings; a time offset Δt causes a bias of about (v + ωD)Δt; the computers’ clocks must be synchronised; frames follow REP 103 and REP 105, with odom → base_link continuous and map → odom allowed to jump; use each piece of information only once, and make the covariances tell the truth (step ⑨).
  7. 没有地图怎么办：SLAM：一边建图一边定位。占据栅格用对数几率累加证据，扫描匹配做前端，位姿图优化做后端；回到老地方的回环约束把一圈的误差分摊开（第 ⑩、⑪ 步）。What if there is no map? SLAM: build the map while localising in it. The occupancy grid adds up evidence as log-odds, scan matching is the front end and pose-graph optimisation the back end; the loop-closure constraint from coming back to a familiar place spreads a lap’s error around the loop (steps ⑩ and ⑪).
  8. 什么时候会错，怎么发现：看不见（退化）、太自信、认错、时间和坐标系不对。用新息自检：归一化新息平方的平均应该约等于读数的维数；不对就报警、挡离群值、重新定位，并且靠几种不会同时失效的传感器互相兜底（第 ⑭ 步）。When does it go wrong, and how do you find out? When it cannot see (degeneracy), is over-confident, mistakes one thing for another, or has the time or the frames wrong. Self-check with the innovation: the average normalised innovation squared should be about the dimension of the reading; if it is not, raise an alarm, block outliers, relocalise, and let several sensors that do not fail at the same time back each other up (step ⑭).
