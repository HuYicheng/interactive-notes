第 1 步 · 针孔模型Step 1 · Pinhole model

针孔相机：三维点怎么变成像素The pinhole camera: how a 3D point becomes a pixel

最常用的相机模型是针孔模型。想象一个不透光的盒子，前面开一个小孔，光线穿过小孔打在后面的感光片上。物体上每个点只有一条光线能穿过小孔，所以感光片上会留下一个清晰但倒过来的像。真正的相机用镜头代替小孔，几何关系是一样的。为了不处理"倒过来"，通常把成像面画在小孔前面同样远的地方（虚拟成像面），像就是正的。The most common camera model is the pinhole model. Picture a light-tight box with a tiny hole in the front: light passes through the hole and lands on the film at the back. Only one ray from each point on an object can pass through the hole, so the film records a sharp but upside-down image. A real camera uses a lens instead of a hole, but the geometry is the same. To avoid dealing with the image being upside down, the image plane is usually drawn the same distance in front of the hole (a virtual image plane), where the image is the right way up.

相似三角形：同样的横向偏移 X，离相机越远（Z 越大），在成像面上越小。这就是"近大远小"，也是透视的来源。"除以 Z"是整个相机模型里唯一不是矩阵乘法的一步。Similar triangles: the same sideways offset X looks smaller on the image plane the farther away it is (the larger Z). This is why near things look big and far things look small, and it is where perspective comes from. “Divide by Z” is the only step in the whole camera model that is not a matrix multiplication.

点在相机前 0.5 m、偏右 5 cm：x′ = 0.05 ÷ 0.5 = 0.1。挪到 1 m 远，x′ 只剩 0.05，在图上离中心近了一半。A point 0.5 m in front of the camera and 5 cm to the right: x′ = 0.05 ÷ 0.5 = 0.1. Move it to 1 m away and x′ is only 0.05, half as far from the centre of the image.

第 2 步 · 内参Step 2 · Intrinsics

内参 K：相机自己的属性Intrinsics K: properties of the camera itself

内参描述"光线进了相机以后，怎么落到哪个像素上"。它只和相机本身（镜头、传感器）有关，和相机放在哪儿无关：把相机搬来搬去、转来转去，内参都不变。The intrinsics describe “once light enters the camera, which pixel it lands on”. They depend only on the camera itself (lens and sensor), not on where the camera is: move it around or turn it however you like, and the intrinsics stay the same.

K 里常用的只有 4 个数：两个焦距 fx、fy 管"放大多少"，两个主点坐标 cx、cy 管"往哪平移"；第 5 个数斜切 s 几乎总是 0。下面的网格画的是归一化坐标，也就是上一节"离小孔 1 个单位远"那张成像面上的坐标；K 负责把它画到传感器的像素上。Only four numbers in K matter in practice: the two focal lengths fx, fy say “how much to scale”, and the two principal point coordinates cx, cy say “where to shift”; the fifth number, the skew s, is almost always 0. K 做的事很简单：先放大（乘焦距，把"每米多少"换成"每像素多少"），再平移（加主点，把原点从图像中心挪到左上角，因为像素是从左上角开始数的）。所以 K 的左上 2×2 管缩放，第三列管平移，最后一行永远是 0 0 1。K does something simple: first it scales (multiplying by the focal length turns “per metre” into “per pixel”), then it shifts (adding the principal point moves the origin from the image centre to the top-left corner, because pixels are counted from the top left). So the top-left 2×2 block of K handles scaling, the third column handles shifting, and the last row is always 0 0 1.

内参是对某一个分辨率标定的。图像缩小一半，fx、fy、cx、cy 都要跟着减半，否则算出来的点云会变形、错位。Intrinsics are calibrated for one particular resolution. If you halve the image size, fx, fy, cx and cy must all be halved too, or the resulting point cloud will be distorted and shifted.

第 3 步 · 外参Step 3 · Extrinsics

外参 [R | t]：相机放在哪、朝哪Extrinsics [R | t]: where the camera is and which way it faces

外参描述世界坐标系和相机坐标系之间的关系，也就是相机在世界里的位置和朝向。它和相机本身无关：同一台相机换个地方放，内参不变，外参变。The extrinsics describe the relationship between the world frame and the camera frame: where the camera is in the world and which way it points. They have nothing to do with the camera itself: move the same camera somewhere else and the intrinsics stay the same while the extrinsics change.

理解外参最直接的办法，是把同一个场景画两遍：一遍画在世界的坐标纸上，一遍画在相机的坐标纸上（相机永远在原点、朝正前方）。外参 [R | t] 就是这两张坐标纸之间的换算：R 管转，t 管挪。The most direct way to understand extrinsics is to draw the same scene twice: once on the world’s grid paper and once on the camera’s grid paper (where the camera always sits at the origin, looking straight ahead). The extrinsics [R | t] are the conversion between the two sheets: R handles the turning, t handles the shifting.

第 5 步 · 双目测深度Step 5 · Stereo depth

深度、双目和立体匹配Depth, stereo and stereo matching

普通照片的每个像素记的是颜色，深度图的每个像素记的是"那里离相机多远"。很多深度相机输出的就是这样一张图：每个像素一个整数，单位是毫米，0 表示没测到。Each pixel of an ordinary photo records a colour; each pixel of a depth image records “how far away that is from the camera”. Many depth cameras output exactly such an image: one integer per pixel, in millimetres, with 0 meaning “not measured”.

这里的"多远"指的是沿相机正前方（光轴方向）的距离 Z，也就是相机坐标里的第三个数，不是镜头到那个点的直线距离。比如相机正对一面墙，墙上每一点的深度都一样，虽然墙角离镜头的直线距离更远。“How far” here means the distance Z straight ahead of the camera (along the optical axis), the third number of the camera coordinates, not the straight-line distance from the lens to the point. For example, if the camera faces a wall head-on, every point on the wall has the same depth, even though the corners are farther from the lens in a straight line.

测深度主要有三种办法：There are three main ways to measure depth:

  • 双目：两台相机，像人的两只眼睛，靠"同一个点在两张图里差了多少"算距离；stereo: two cameras, like a pair of eyes, measuring distance from “how far apart the same point is in the two images”;
  • 结构光：投一个已知的图案，看它落在物体上怎么变形；structured light: project a known pattern and see how it deforms on the object;
  • 飞行时间（ToF）：测光打出去再弹回来用了多久。time of flight (ToF): measure how long light takes to go out and bounce back.

平移只负责把两团点的重心对上，所以先把重心都减掉，只剩下"朝向"的差别。H 把这种差别压缩进一个 3×3 矩阵，SVD 再从里面读出最贴合的那个旋转。点很少、共面或者噪声很大时，SVD 可能给出一个镜像解（det = −1），D 就是用来把它掰回真正的旋转。最后转好了，再用 t 把重心对上。Translation only has to line up the two centroids, so subtract them first and only the difference in “orientation” remains. H compresses that difference into a 3×3 matrix, and the SVD reads off the best-fitting rotation from it. With few points, coplanar points or heavy noise, the SVD may return a reflection (det = −1), and D bends it back into a proper rotation. Once the rotation is right, t lines up the centroids.

二维的情况更直观，最佳转角 θ = atan2(Σ p′ × q′, Σ p′ · q′)，p′、q′ 是减过重心的点。源点 (1, 0)、(−1, 0)，目标点 (0, 1)、(0, −1)，重心都是原点。叉积之和 = 1×1 − 0×0 + (−1)×(−1) − 0×0 = 2，点积之和 = 0，θ = atan2(2, 0) = 90°：确实是把横线转成竖线。t = q̄ − R p̄ = 0。The 2D case is more intuitive: the best angle is θ = atan2(Σ p′ × q′, Σ p′ · q′), where p′ and q′ are the centroid-subtracted points. Source points (1, 0), (−1, 0); target points (0, 1), (0, −1); both centroids are at the origin. Sum of cross products = 1×1 − 0×0 + (−1)×(−1) − 0×0 = 2, sum of dot products = 0, θ = atan2(2, 0) = 90°: indeed it turns a horizontal line into a vertical one. t = q̄ − R p̄ = 0.

需要至少 3 个不共线的点。临床上用标记点、或者手动点选鼻根、耳屏这类解剖标志，走的就是这条路，它常用来给 ICP 一个初始位置。You need at least 3 points that are not on one line. Clinically, markers or manually picked anatomical landmarks (nasion, tragus) follow exactly this route, and it is often used to give ICP its starting position.

麻烦在于：相机点云和 MRI 表面上的点没有编号，不知道谁对应谁。这就要靠 ICP。The catch: the points in the camera cloud and on the MRI surface are not numbered, so we don’t know which corresponds to which. That is where ICP comes in.

第 8 步 · ICPStep 8 · ICP

ICP：边猜对应，边求位姿ICP: guess the correspondences, solve the pose, repeat

ICP 是 Iterative Closest Point（迭代最近点）的缩写。这是个先有鸡还是先有蛋的问题：知道位姿，就能找到谁对应谁；知道谁对应谁，就能用上面的方法算出位姿。ICP 的办法是两步轮流来：ICP stands for Iterative Closest Point. It is a chicken-and-egg problem: if you knew the pose, you could find the correspondences; if you knew the correspondences, you could compute the pose with the method above. ICP alternates between the two:

  1. 找最近点：对源点云里的每个点，在目标表面上找离它最近的点，先假装它们就是对应点。Find the closest points: for each point of the source cloud, find the nearest point on the target surface and pretend that is its correspondence.
  2. 求 R、t：按这批"假装的对应"，用上面的方法一步算出最好的 R、t，把整个源点云挪过去。Solve R and t: using these “pretend correspondences”, compute the best R and t in one step with the method above, and move the whole source cloud.
  3. 回到第 1 步。挪过以后最近点会变，对应关系一般也更准了。一直重复，直到误差不再下降。Go back to step 1. After the move the closest points change, and the correspondences are usually better. Repeat until the error stops decreasing.

几个关键性质：Key properties:

  • 标准的点到点 ICP，每一轮误差只会变小或不变，所以一定会停下来。但它停在"附近最低的坑"（局部最优），不保证是正确答案；初始位置偏得太远，它会很笃定地停在错的地方。所以实际流程是"粗配准 + 精配准"：先用标记点、特征匹配或者机械臂自己的位姿给一个大概位置，再用 ICP 精修。In standard point-to-point ICP the error never increases from one round to the next, so it always stops. But it stops in “the nearest low spot” (a local minimum), which is not guaranteed to be the right answer; start too far off and it will stop confidently in the wrong place. That is why the real workflow is “coarse registration + fine registration”: markers, feature matching or the robot arm’s own pose give a rough position first, then ICP refines it.
  • 对应关系不要求一一对应：好几个源点完全可以对到同一个目标点。Correspondences need not be one-to-one: several source points may well match the same target point.
  • 离群点会把结果拉偏。头发、衣领、背景这些 MRI 头皮上没有的点，也会硬找一个"最近点"。常见做法是把距离明显偏大的配对扔掉（trimmed ICP）。Outliers pull the result off. Hair, collars, background, anything not on the MRI scalp, still grabs a “closest point”. The usual fix is to throw away pairs whose distance is clearly too large (trimmed ICP).
  • 方向要选对。相机只拍到一小块头皮，MRI 有整个头，应该让"一小块"到整个头上找最近点，也就是小的当 source。反过来的话，MRI 上没被拍到的部分会被硬拉去配对。Get the direction right. The camera sees only a small patch of scalp, while the MRI has the whole head, so the small patch should look for closest points on the whole head, i.e. the small one is the source. Do it the other way round and the parts of the MRI the camera never saw get forced into pairs.
  • 光滑的弧面会"滑"。如果只拍到一小块光滑、接近球面的头皮，整块点云沿着球面转一点，到 MRI 表面的距离几乎不变。这时误差可以非常小，位置却是错的。下面用矩阵解释为什么。Smooth curved surfaces “slide”. If the camera only sees a small, smooth, nearly spherical patch of scalp, rotating the whole patch a little along the sphere barely changes its distance to the MRI surface. The error can then be tiny while the position is wrong. The matrices below explain why.

点到面用"点到目标表面切平面的距离"，允许点沿着表面滑，通常几轮就收敛，比点到点快很多。每一轮假设这一步转得很小，把旋转近似成 I + [ω]×，残差就变成 6 个未知数的线性函数，最小二乘一步解出来，再用 exp 把 ω 贴回真正的旋转（上一篇"李群"一节的套路）。Point-to-plane uses “the distance from the point to the tangent plane of the target surface”, which lets points slide along the surface; it usually converges in a few rounds, much faster than point-to-point. Each round assumes the step is a small rotation and approximates it as I + [ω]×, so the residuals become linear in 6 unknowns, solved in one least-squares step; then exp turns ω back into a true rotation (the recipe from the “Lie groups” section of the previous page).

AᵀA 的特征值告诉你哪些方向约束得住。如果某个特征值接近 0，对应的特征向量就是一种"挪了也不改变任何残差"的运动，也就是会滑的方向。比如一块完美的球面：每个点的法向量都穿过球心，绕球心转一点，所有点到球面的距离都不变，AᵀA 就有 3 个接近 0 的特征值。实际系统里可以算最小和最大特征值之比，太小就说明这次配准靠不住，需要拍到鼻子、眉骨、耳朵这类有起伏的区域，或者用别的信息（机械臂位姿、多个视角）补上。The eigenvalues of AᵀA tell you which directions are pinned down. If one eigenvalue is close to 0, its eigenvector is a motion that “moves without changing any residual”, a direction the cloud can slide in. For a perfect sphere, for example, every normal passes through the centre, so a small rotation about the centre leaves every point’s distance to the sphere unchanged, and AᵀA has 3 eigenvalues near 0. In a real system you can compute the ratio of the smallest to the largest eigenvalue: if it is too small, the registration cannot be trusted, and you need to capture contoured areas such as the nose, brow ridge or ears, or bring in other information (the arm’s pose, multiple viewpoints).

常见坑Common pitfalls

  1. 外参的方向。OpenCV 的 rvec、tvec 是"世界 → 相机"（Tcam_world），机器人里说的相机位姿通常是反过来的 Tworld_cam。tvec 不是相机的位置，相机位置是 C = −Rᵀt。Which way the extrinsics go. OpenCV’s rvec and tvec are “world → camera” (Tcam_world), while the camera pose in robotics is usually the reverse, Tworld_cam. tvec is not the camera’s position; the camera position is C = −Rᵀt.
  2. 两套相机坐标系。光学坐标系是 x 向右、y 向下、z 朝前（OpenCV、RealSense 的点云用这个）；ROS 的机体坐标系是 x 朝前、y 向左、z 向上。RealSense 的 ROS 驱动两套都发布（camera_link 和 *_optical_frame），点云在 optical frame 里，混用会差一个固定的旋转。Two camera frame conventions. The optical frame has x right, y down, z forward (OpenCV and RealSense point clouds use it); the ROS body frame has x forward, y left, z up. The RealSense ROS driver publishes both (camera_link and *_optical_frame), and point clouds live in the optical frame; mixing them up costs you a fixed rotation.
  3. 内参和分辨率绑定。换了分辨率要用对应的内参，或者按比例缩放 fx、fy、cx、cy。Intrinsics belong to a resolution. When the resolution changes, use the matching intrinsics or scale fx, fy, cx, cy proportionally.
  4. 深度和彩色不是同一台相机。它们各有内参，之间还有一组外参。要让深度和彩色逐像素对应，先用 SDK 的对齐（align）功能，把深度图变到彩色相机的视角。Depth and colour come from different cameras. Each has its own intrinsics, with a set of extrinsics between them. To match depth and colour pixel by pixel, first use the SDK’s align function to warp the depth image into the colour camera’s view.
  5. 单位。深度原始值要乘 depth scale（常见 0.001）才是米。ICP 里毫米和米混用，剔除阈值和收敛判据会全部失效。Units. Raw depth values must be multiplied by the depth scale (often 0.001) to get metres. Mixing millimetres and metres in ICP breaks every rejection threshold and convergence test.
  6. 畸变。反投影之前先看畸变系数：不是 0 就要先去畸变。Distortion. Check the distortion coefficients before back-projecting: if they are not 0, undistort first.

检验题Check question

如果相机的焦距标定错了 5%，配准时 ICP 能不能把这个误差一起"对"掉？为什么？If the camera’s focal length is calibrated 5% wrong, can ICP “align away” that error during registration? Why or why not?

提示Hint

回到点云那个交互，把焦距调错，看点云的形状变了没有。再想想上一篇讲的：旋转和平移能改变什么、不能改变什么。Go back to the point cloud interactive, set the wrong focal length and see whether the shape of the cloud changes. Then recall the previous page: what can rotation and translation change, and what can’t they?

参考答案Answer

不能。焦距错了，反投影出来的 X、Y 被整体缩放，Z 却不变，所以点云本身变了形：点和点之间的距离变了。旋转和平移不会改变任何两点之间的距离（这正是"什么是旋转"那三个条件里的一条），所以无论 R、t 取什么，都没法把变形的点云和 MRI 完全贴合。ICP 只会找一个"平均最好"的位置：残差变大，位姿也会被带偏。办法是把内参标定准；允许缩放的配准（相似变换）只能补偿均匀缩放，补偿不了"只缩 X、Y、不缩 Z"这种变形。No. With the wrong focal length, the back-projected X and Y are scaled while Z is not, so the point cloud itself is deformed: the distances between points change. Rotation and translation never change the distance between two points (one of the three conditions in “what is a rotation”), so no choice of R and t can make the deformed cloud fit the MRI exactly. ICP will only find the position that fits “best on average”: the residuals grow and the pose is biased. The fix is accurate intrinsic calibration; registration that allows scaling (a similarity transform) can only compensate a uniform scale, not a deformation that “scales X and Y but not Z”.
